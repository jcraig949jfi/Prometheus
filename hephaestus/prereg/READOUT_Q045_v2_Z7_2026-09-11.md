## 2. The defect the kill found (v1's shift column was ill-posed)

v1 drew shift inputs with entries 0..6 and evaluated them under Z6. The entry 6 is outside Z6. Ops
that reduce mod 6 (`p02`..`p05`, `p07`..`p09`, and B's `eadd`/`emul`/`einc`) fold 6 to 0; ops that
only permute (`p00` rotate, `p01` reverse, B's `rot`/`rev`) keep it as 6. So a target computed by
`p00` carried a 6 where an equivalent candidate carried a 0, and v1 marked a program that is
identical to the target on every element of Z6^4 as "not robust". Verified directly: on x = (6,1,2,3),
`p00(x)` = (1,2,3,6) while `emul(econst1(X), rot(X))` = (1,2,3,0) under MOD 6 and (1,2,3,6) under
MOD 7. Under Z7 the shifted alphabet IS the ring, every op has defined semantics on it, and the
column is well-posed. The four "gains" are therefore v1 false negatives, not v2 errors.

Scope of the defect: witness-level `robust` flags in v1. Target-level statements survive: v2 has
C robust 20/20 on LOST (every target keeps at least one robust C witness, and its certified program
is always one of them) and A0 robust 10/10 on CONTROL. The v1 readout is annotated, not rewritten
(operator ruling: do not rewrite or replace the frozen result).

## 3. Characterization: what the losses are (V2-P3)

Under Z7, 43 arm-level decreases (86 witness-level losses) across arms A0/A2/B/C, plus the 4 B-arm
increases of section 2 (all listed in the JSON; lost witnesses carry "not robust"). Mechanically, every
one of the 86 lost witnesses is exhaustive-equal to its target on all of Z6^4 (that is what made it
mechanism-bearing) and differs from it on the Z7 shift points, so each relies on an identity that
holds in Z6 and not in Z7. Reading the 47 changed witness lists, the identities are of two families
(two examples of each verified by direct evaluation; the family assignment of every one of the 86
was made by inspection, not by a mechanical prover):
- **x^3 = x (mod 6)**, hence x^4 = x^2: e.g. `p05(p05(X,X), p05(X,X))` for target `p05(X,X)`;
  `emul(emul(X,X), emul(X,X))`; `p03(p03(p03(X)))` = 8x = 2x (mod 6) for target `p03(X)` = 2x.
  Checked: x^4 = x^2 holds for all x in Z6 and fails in Z7.
- **6x = 0 (mod 6)**: e.g. `eadd(eadd(eadd(X,X), eadd(X,X)), eadd(eadd(X,X), rot(X)))` = 6x + rot(x)
  for target `rot(X)`; `p04(p03(p03(X)), p04(p03(X), p05(X,X)))` = 4x + 2x + x^2 = x^2 (mod 6).
  Checked: 6x = 0 for all x in Z6 and fails in Z7.
