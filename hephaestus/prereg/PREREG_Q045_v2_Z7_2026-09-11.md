# PREREGISTRATION -- Q045 v2: instrument characterization under a TRUE ring change (Z7 shift column)

**Filed:** 2026-09-11, before any run, in its own commit. **Author:** Hephaestus (conflicted: author of
the gauntlet, v1 and this file). **Governing:** operator rulings of 2026-09-11 (ruling 1: reruns are
instrument-characterization experiments, never replacements; ruling 4: preregister eligible
denominator, interval treatment and boundary semantics; ruling 5: exact tuple equality stays the
COERCE). **Status of v1:** `hephaestus/closure_results/q045_lost_class.json` is FROZEN and is not
written by this experiment.

## 0. What changes, and what cannot change

One thing changes: the 300 shift points (entries 0..6, seed 20260901, the same points as v1) are
evaluated under Z7 arithmetic for both the target's certified program and every candidate
(`SpecZ7.shift_context`, via the gauntlet's new spec-declared `shift_context` hook, default no-op).
v1 evaluated those points under Z6, which folds entry 6 onto 0, so v1's shift tested alphabet
extension, not ring dependence.

By construction the search column (six probes, Z6) and the exhaustive column (1,290 vectors, Z6)
are untouched, so `mechanism_bearing`, `n_mech`, `n_alias`, every witness expression and every
classification MUST be identical to v1. Only `robust` (and therefore `n_robust`, `C_robust`,
`robust_A0`) may differ.

## 1. Eligible denominator and boundary semantics (ruling 4)

- Eligible LOST rows for the robustness question: exactly the rows with a C mechanism-bearing
  witness in v1 = **20** (v1 `C witness 20/20`). Eligible CONTROL rows: the 10 with an A0 witness.
- Unit of observation: the target (a witness list is one observation per target), n = 20 and 10.
- No numeric gate is set on the count of robust targets; this is a characterization, and a gate
  chosen now would be chosen with no attainable-range information for Z7. Every count is reported
  with its denominator; nothing here is read as "TRUE" or "FALSE" about the substrate.
- Boundary semantics: a target counts as robust-under-Z7 iff at least one of its (up to 5 listed)
  mechanism-bearing witnesses passes the Z7 shift column. A target whose ONLY listed witnesses fail
  is "not robust under Z7"; the untested equivalents beyond the five listed are NOT inferred either
  way (the list cap is a known limitation, reported).

## 2. Predictions (falsifiable; tooling kills first)

- **V2-P1 (identity, tooling).** For all 30 targets and all four arms, the witness expression
  lists, alias counts and classifications are identical to v1. Any difference = tooling defect;
  STOP and report; no other prediction is read.
- **V2-P2 (identity of certified programs).** Every witness that is textually identical to the
  target's certified full-inventory program (or is that program under commutativity of `p04`/`p05`
  alone) is robust under Z7. A failure here = tooling defect (the same program cannot disagree with
  itself under any ring).
- **V2-P3 (characterization).** Some v1-robust witnesses lose robustness under Z7. Each loss must
  be explainable by a Z6 identity that fails in Z7; the readout lists every lost witness with the
  identity it relied on. A loss with NO such explanation = INDETERMINATE (instrument), reported as
  such, never as a substrate claim.
- **V2-P4 (direction).** Robust counts can only go DOWN or stay: Z7 is a strictly stronger test on
  these points because v1's Z6 evaluation identified entry 6 with 0. Any target robust in v2 but not
  in v1 = tooling defect.

## 3. What this experiment can and cannot say

Can: characterize how much of v1's `robust` column depended on Z6 coincidences; name the identities.
Cannot: change v1's classification of any target; change P1-P5 of v1; say anything about specimens
1-2 (their `shift_context` is the default no-op and their results are byte-identical under the hook,
to be re-verified before this run is read); widen arm B (ruling 1).
