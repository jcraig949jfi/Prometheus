SOPON can instantiate many competing hypotheses as overlapping reaction‑diffusion patterns. Failed hypotheses are suppressed by inhibitory morphogen fields, pruning the search space without explicit back‑propagation.  This yields a **fast, parallel, self‑pruning hypothesis‑testing loop** that exploits both spatial competition and temporal binding.
