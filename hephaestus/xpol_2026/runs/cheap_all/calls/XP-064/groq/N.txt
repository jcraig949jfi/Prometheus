The free‑energy objective couples prediction errors to a KL‑divergence term, yielding an update rule identical to **variational auto‑encoder** (VAE) inference but constrained by gauge equivariance and spectral consistency.

**Advantage for self‑hypothesis testing**  
Because predictions are invariant under local gauge transformations, the system can generate *equivalence classes* of hypotheses that differ only by symmetry operations. Spectral decomposition isolates low‑frequency (global) vs. high‑frequency (detail) prediction errors, letting the agent first test coarse‑grained hypotheses before committing resources to fine‑grained ones. A rise in free energy signals a mismatch that is *decomposed* into gauge‑mode and spectral‑mode components, pinpointing whether the failure stems from an incorrect symmetry assumption or from missing high‑frequency structure—thus sharpening metacognitive monitoring.

The proposal therefore constitutes a novel interdisciplinary synthesis rather than a re‑branding of an existing field.
