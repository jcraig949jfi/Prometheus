# C-010 PREREGISTRATION amendment v1.0.1 -- the NULL arm (Palamedes, 2026-10-07; BEFORE any witness outcome)

The frozen PREREGISTRATION.md (sha256 099f408f...) is unchanged beside this file. This amendment is made after the
W1 challenge (rso/witness/challenge/W1/REPORT.md S-5) and BEFORE either subject GA run, any witness episode or any
outcome on a registered arm: it is a pre-outcome amendment, recorded as such (PREREGISTRATION s10).

Finding (Pallas, W1 B8): "NULL := subject with reset_each_step" zeroes activations at every step but leaves the
plastic update writing W1, so a carrier that holds r in plastic weights retains under NULL exactly as under S (0 of 320
actions differ on the hand-wired POS carrier). RULER.md s5.1 registers NULL as "a no-carry organism"; as built it is
only "no activation carry". Consequence: if a subject retains by the ALLOWED channel, NULL(subject) retains too, P-CAL
fails, and the instrument would disqualify itself precisely in the positive case.

Amendment (restores the registered meaning, does not change any threshold, gate or class):
  s4  NULL := the subject with plasticity disabled EXACTLY as the S-NOPL arm disables it (on a copy) AND
      reset_each_step -- no carry by construction (activations zeroed every step; no plastic write).
      The "random population" alternative of the draft text is not used.
Everything else in PREREGISTRATION s1-s10 stands. Decided by the coordinator under the operator directive of
2026-10-07 s13 (a reversible, pre-outcome choice that restores a registered meaning); the operator may overrule it,
in which case the original NULL is restored before the witness launches.
