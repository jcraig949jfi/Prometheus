# Primary Source Ledger

Every claim in this programme carries a tag for how it was obtained. The tags are load-bearing:
the directive demands *recovered* mathematics, and recalled mathematics is not recovered.

    RECOVERED    read from full text fetched into sources/ and quoted
    DERIVED      my analysis OF that recovered text
    RECALLED     from model knowledge, NOT verified against a primary source
    SPECULATIVE  proposed here, with no source claim at all

**Consequence, stated plainly.** `WATSON_MVG_MECHANISM_COMPARISON.md` and
`TOUSSAINT_WATSON_COMMON_OBJECT.md` rest on RECALLED descriptions of N1 and N2. They are
written at the mechanism level as the directive permits, and they are **not evidence**. Every
conclusion depending on N1 or N2 is marked UNVERIFIED and must not be promoted until those
sources are recovered. This is the same discipline the cartography gate enforced: a claim whose
source was not read is a claim about my memory, not about the literature.
