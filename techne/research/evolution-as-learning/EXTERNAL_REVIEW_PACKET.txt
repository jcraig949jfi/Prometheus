1. THE RECOVERED MATHEMATICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Watson et al. 2014, Equation 1, verbatim:

    P(t+1) = P(t) + tau_1 * sigma( B . P(t) ) - tau_2 * P(t)
    P(0) = G ,  tau_1 = 1 ,  tau_2 = 0.2 ,  sigma(x) = tanh(x) elementwise

Degenerate case the paper explicitly contrasts itself against (T=1, sigma=id):

    P = G + tau_1 (B.G) - tau_2 G      ==>   P = B . G
    identified in-source as Lipson/Pollack/Suh 2002 and Kashtan et al. 2009

The selection result:

    selection coefficient = (dP* . S) / (1 + P* . S)
    delta b_ij = r * s_i * s_j        (r > 0)      == Hebb rule

2. THE THIRTEEN QUESTIONS, ANSWERED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1  IS "EVOLUTION LEARNS" MATHEMATICALLY LITERAL?
    PARTLY, and the qualifier is load-bearing. The Hebbian result is DERIVED in
    the source, not asserted -- but it is derived under a stated condition
    ("selection sufficiently efficacious that phenotypic characters at least
    have the same sign as the direction of selection") and it is derived in the
    SINGLE-STEP LINEAR case, where one mutation touches one trait. In the
    recurrent non-linear regime the source itself states a mutation "may affect
    many phenotypic characters", so the outer-product form is an approximation
    whose error is never characterised.
    THE EQUIVALENCE IS CLEANEST WHERE THE INTERESTING BEHAVIOUR IS ABSENT AND
    APPROXIMATE WHERE IT LIVES. Everything beyond that is framing.

 2  WHAT OBJECT STORES THE HISTORICAL INFORMATION?
    The interaction matrix B. Not the genotype G.

 3  WHAT MECHANISM CHANGES IT?
    Selection on interaction coefficients, following delta b_ij = r s_i s_j.

 4  DOES THAT CHANGE ALTER FUTURE VARIATION?
    YES BY CONSTRUCTION. Adult phenotype is a deterministic function of (G,B);
    mutation perturbs G; so the reachable phenotype distribution is a function
    of B. This is a property of the equations, not an experimental finding.

 5  DOES ALTERED VARIATION IMPROVE SUBSEQUENT ACQUISITION?
    YES within the trained structural family (S1), and in unseen environments
    of the SAME regularity (S3). UNTESTED for orthogonal regularities.

 6  IS IT DEMONSTRATED LONGITUDINALLY?
    Partially -- B is shown changing over evolutionary time in simulation.

 7  IS IT CAUSAL?
    NOT ESTABLISHED. No recovered source runs the same-probe counterfactual:
    match adult phenotype across histories, apply an identical mutation
    operator, compare offspring distributions. This is the central gap.

 8  DOES IT GENERALISE OUTSIDE THE HISTORICAL STRUCTURAL DISTRIBUTION?
    NO EVIDENCE. Strongest demonstrated level is G3 -- novel states within the
    learned family. The source calls its own result "extrapolative" for
    producing 0, 2 and 4 loops after training on 1 and 3, but that is unseen
    VALUES on a SEEN axis. G4 is neither demonstrated nor claimed.

 9  SMALLEST MOTIF REPRODUCING THE EFFECT?
    A recurrent saturating map whose parameters accumulate the outer product of
    externally selected states. Four ingredients: recurrence, saturation,
    correlational parameter accumulation, timescale separation. Only the
    non-linearity is RECOVERED as necessary; the other three are DERIVED and
    are the subject of experiment E3.

10  DOES TOUSSAINT OPERATE ON THE SAME ABSTRACT OBJECT?
    UNVERIFIED -- source not recovered. Stated as a well-posed candidate with a
    named failure condition, not as a result.

11  DO KASHTAN/ALON AND KOUVARIS REPRESENT OTHER ROUTES?
    KOUVARIS: yes, and RECOVERED -- it adds the CONTROLS (noise, connection
    cost) rather than a new mechanism.
    KASHTAN/ALON: UNVERIFIED, two live branches, one discriminating test named.

12  SECOND-ORDER MECHANISM?
    NO. Recursion depth in the recovered lineage is ONE. The Hebbian update is a
    fixed law, not an evolved object. Any meta-evolvability claim drawn from
    this lineage is unsupported.

13  HIGHEST-VALUE UNRESOLVED EXPERIMENT?
    E1, the same-probe counterfactual. Section 5.

5. MINIMUM JUSTIFIED NEXT EXPERIMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E1 -- SAME-PROBE COUNTERFACTUAL

    Evolve two lineages under different historical target sets. Select genotypes
    whose ADULT PHENOTYPE is matched across lineages. Apply the IDENTICAL
    mutation operator. Compare the offspring phenotype DISTRIBUTIONS.

    Pre-register: same mean, different shape.
    If the distributions are indistinguishable, K2 fires and developmental
    memory is a current-phenotype effect only.

    Why this and nothing else first: every claim in this lineage rests on
    "history modifies the generator". In the equations that is true BY
    CONSTRUCTION. A construction is not a result, and no recovered source has
    measured it.
