# SFE Experiment Candidates

Ranked by information value per unit cost. All are small.

## E1  SAME-PROBE COUNTERFACTUAL           [rank 1]

    question    Do two systems with the SAME current phenotype generate DIFFERENT phenotype
                distributions under identical perturbation, as a function of history alone?
    why first   The whole programme rests on "history modifies the generator". In the S1
                equations this is true BY CONSTRUCTION but has never been MEASURED. A
                construction is not a result.
    substrate   Eq. 1 directly. N traits, B evolved under two different target sets.
    protocol    match adult phenotype across arms; apply identical mutation operator;
                compare offspring distributions by shape, not mean.
    verdict on  K2. If null, developmental memory is a current-phenotype effect only.
    cost        small -- pure simulation, no model calls

## E2  ORTHOGONAL-REGULARITY TRANSFER      [rank 2]

    question    Does stored memory HELP or HURT on a structural regularity orthogonal to the
                trained one?
    why         S3 establishes transfer within a regularity. The ceiling is untested, and the
                lock-in analysis predicts a negative.
    control     naive-B lineage with no historical training
    verdict on  the scope of every evolvability claim in this lineage
    cost        small

## E3  NON-LINEARITY ABLATION              [rank 3]

    question    Which of the four motif ingredients is load-bearing?
    why         MINIMAL_MEMORY_MOTIF.md asserts all four are essential; S1 RECOVERS only that
                non-linearity matters. Recurrence, correlational accumulation and timescale
                separation are DERIVED, not demonstrated.
    protocol    four arms, each removing one ingredient, on the same task
    verdict on  K5 and K6 -- and it is the test that could show a simpler mechanism suffices
    cost        small
