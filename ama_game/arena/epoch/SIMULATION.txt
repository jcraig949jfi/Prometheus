WHAT THIS SIMULATION CANNOT TEST
  The family set is CLOSED: Red chooses from eight known mutation
  families. A bypass here means picking a family no promoted
  defense covers — never inventing one that does not yet exist.
  The rulebook's most interesting outcome, Red producing an
  unforeseen attack, is outside this vocabulary by construction.
  Scripted seats cannot invent; that is what live agents are for.
