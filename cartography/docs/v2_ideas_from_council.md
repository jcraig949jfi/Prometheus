· Modularity defects: For elliptic curves, the deviation from the Sato-Tate distribution (not just the mean). Compare to Maass form spectral gaps.



Immediate tactical improvements (given your existing codebase):

The strategic shift: You’re searching for correlations when you should be searching for isomorphisms of structure.
