# PRIMARY SOURCE LEDGER -- Kashtan / Alon modularly varying goals (MVG) lineage

Prepared 2026-09-03 by Elenchus (adversarial historical forensics, Historical Collider).
Every load-bearing claim in this deep-dive is traceable to a file in `sources/` that was
downloaded, hashed, converted to text, and grepped. No load-bearing claim rests on model
recall. Where a claim could NOT be verified against retrieved bytes it is tagged

## Retrieval discipline

Two independent extraction passes were run on the 2008 paper: an automated page-reader,
and a direct download + text conversion + grep. The two DISAGREED twice, and in both cases
the automated reader overstated the result. This is recorded because it bears on how much
weight any single-pass literature summary deserves:

  CORRECTION 1. Automated reader: "adaptation of MVG-organisms toward a randomly picked
  goal results in evolution that is as slow, or even slower, than FG-organisms."
  Retrieved text: "MVG adaptation toward non-modular goals was not significantly
  different from FG's (Figure 6E)." -- NOT slower. Null, not reversal.

  CORRECTION 2. Automated reader: "No deliberate structural-but-non-evolved control is
  reported." Retrieved text: Figure 9B/9C carry exactly that control -- "a random class
  of inverse-fold genomes (genomes generated by an algorithm to yield a desired fold)"
  and "circuits which achieve the goal but were generated by an optimization algorithm
  rather than by an evolutionary process (see Text S1 section 3.1)."

Both corrections are in the direction of the retrieved primary source. All numbers quoted
downstream come from the retrieved text.

## Consequence for this review

The experimental physics is reconstructible at the level of ALGORITHM and GOALS, and NOT
at the level of EXACT GENOME ENCODING for the 2005 circuits. Any Prometheus
reimplementation is therefore a RE-SPECIFICATION, not a replication: the same
genotype-phenotype map cannot be restored from what survives in the open literature.
That is a positive finding for SFE calibration purposes (the effect must be robust to
encoding if it is to be a calibration target at all) and a negative finding for anyone
wanting a bit-exact historical rerun.
