1. CLAIM UNDER REVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Assigned question: did the Kashtan/Alon programme causally demonstrate that
exposure to structured environmental variation changes an evolving computational
representation in a way that reduces the cost of acquiring future adaptive
functionality -- and did they MEASURE the change in the local adjacent possible
that mediates it, or only infer it from modularity and faster adaptation?

The second half was expected to be the easy no. It is a yes.

4. THE ACCESSIBILITY MEASUREMENT -- THE CENTRAL FINDING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Verbatim, 2008: "we considered the phenotypic neighborhood [37]-[39], defined as
the set of phenotypes that are accessible from a given genotype by a single point
mutation." And: "The genetic neighborhood is defined as the set of all genomes
different in one position from the wild type genomes."

This is the adjacent possible, measured under the system's own variation
operator, in phenotype space. Not an analogy to what HC-T01 proposes. The same
measurement -- and EXHAUSTIVE rather than sampled, because B = 104 bits.

It is longitudinal: "Mean FV measure (+-SE) vs. generations of 500 best-fitness
circuits in each population is shown. Statistics are for 30 independent
experiments."

Ladder position: NOT_MEASURED and SELECTED_INDIVIDUALS_ONLY and
POPULATION_SNAPSHOT are all exceeded. LONGITUDINAL is met. LINKED_TO_ACQUISITION
is met AT ARM LEVEL. CAUSALLY_PERTURBED is met only on the cause, never on the
mediator.

5. D-LEVEL ADJUDICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Kashtan & Alon 2005 ............................. D0
  Kashtan, Noor & Alon 2007 ....................... D1-adjacent
  Parter, Kashtan & Alon 2008 ..................... D4 (weak form)
  PROGRAMME MAXIMUM ............................... D4 (weak form)

D4 is weak because accessibility and acquisition are joined at the treatment arm,
not within runs: nobody asked whether the runs with the steepest facilitated-
variation rise are the runs that later adapt fastest.

D5 fails for one reason only. The "genetic triggers" -- genomic positions found by
mutual information with the environment -- are never knocked out, frozen,
transplanted or randomised. The one perturbation present runs the wrong way: make
the environment constant and the property decays. That manipulates the cause and
measures the mediator. D5 needs the reverse.

THE MISSING EXPERIMENT, stated so it can be run: take MVG-evolved genomes,
disable the trigger positions while HOLDING fitness on the current goal and the
modularity measure CONSTANT, then present a novel-module goal and measure
adaptation time. If accessibility is the mediator, the advantage collapses. If
modularity is, it survives. B = 104 bits, populations of 5000, triggers "readily
detected for all MVG cases tested". It was affordable in 2008 and was not run.

7. THE TWO DISSOCIATIONS THAT BREAK THE STANDARD STORY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1. SPEED WITHOUT MODULARITY. 2005 reports randomly varying goals produce no
modular structure. 2007 reports they produce 45x, 160x, 10x and 3x speedups in
four of five models. In feed-forward circuits, alternating with NO SELECTION AT
ALL (VG_0) gives 190x against MVG's 265x. Modularity is not necessary for the
acceleration, and most of the acceleration is generic plateau escape: RVG_v
"seems to help by pushing the population in a random direction, thereby rescuing
it from fitness plateaus or local maxima."

D2. THE NBVG CONTROL, recovered from Text S1 and absent from the main text. The
authors built goal pairs that are explicitly NOT decomposable but whose NEUTRAL
NETWORKS COME CLOSE in genotype space. Verbatim: "the evolutionary dynamics of
NBVG is very similar to that of MVG, with respect to the rapid adaptation when
environment changes. The design and the mechanisms that underlie this rapid
adaptation are equivalent to that of MVG and include the location of genomes at
the border of the neutral networks and the evolution of small number of genetic
triggers."

But MVG and NBVG DIFFER on neighbourhood composition and on the facilitated-
variation measure (Figure S4). The resulting ordering is NON-MONOTONIC in
environmental variability:
  MVG (structured variation) ..... HIGH facilitated variation
  FG  (no variation) ............. MEDIUM (from robustness)
  unstructured variation ......... VERY LOW
Unstructured change is worse than no change. That is the strongest evidence in
the corpus that STRUCTURE, not CHANGE, builds the biased neighbourhood.

THE TWO-LAYER READING THIS FORCES:
  layer 1  speed and memory of seen goals <- successive goals having adjacent
           solution sets. Modularity NOT required.
  layer 2  biased neighbourhood and in-family generalisation <- the shared
           structure being modular specifically.
Anyone saying "modularly varying goals speed evolution because they make
organisms modular" is wrong on layer 1 by the authors' own control.

7b. THE FAILED REPLICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Clune, Beckmann, McKinley & Ofria 2010 (GECCO '10, 635-642) attempted Kashtan &
Alon's RETINA problem WITH A DIRECT-ENCODING CONTROL, and the sign reversed:

  "the MVG regimes performed worse than the FG-AND regime, which was the opposite
   of what occurred in Kashtan and Alon's study. Our result raises questions as to
   the generality of Kashtan and Alon's discovery ... the differences are
   relatively small and should not preclude such a seemingly general result."

They closed the obvious confounds: both switching rates including Kashtan's own
E = 20, faster and slower rates reported as not qualitatively different, 20 runs
per treatment, population 500, extended to 30,000 generations.

Their diagnosis converges with Kashtan's own hidden NBVG control from the opposite
direction. They attribute the discrepancy to DISCRETENESS -- binary inputs, step
activations with three thresholds, few discrete weights -- because "Mutations
between a few discrete values may have a larger effect on the network output,
making it more likely that single mutations can switch between a solution to FG-OR
and FG-AND." That is the same statement as "goals whose neutral networks come
close". Two groups, one trying to confirm and one trying to refute, land on
genotype-space proximity as the operative condition.

They also record, in 2010, that "the description of their model is not complete" --
an external confirmation that the 2005 paper cannot be exactly reproduced from its
own text, which matches this pass's finding that the genotype-phenotype map is
UNSPECIFIED and no code was ever released.

SCOPE, AND IT IS THE CRITICAL CAVEAT OF THIS PACKET: this is the retina arm only.
*** THE NAND LOGIC-CIRCUIT ARM -- WHICH CARRIES EVERY ACCESSIBILITY RESULT IN THIS
REVIEW -- HAS NEVER BEEN INDEPENDENTLY REPLICATED OR REFUTED BY ANYONE. ***

Further, from the same pass: the lineage's own descendant Kouvaris et al. 2017
reports "We find no rate of environmental variation capable of causing evolution by
natural selection to evolve a developmental organisation that produces the entire
class" -- generalisation required added noise and regularisation. Friedlander et al.
2013, from the Alon lab itself, reports that with sum-rule mutations -- Kashtan's own
rule -- modular goals "generally do not" produce modularity. Espinosa-Soto 2018
independently confirms the decay: "Once fluctuations stop, modularity drops
abruptly."

NOT challenges, and explicitly withdrawn as such: Hintze & Adami 2008 (their
environments vary RANDOMLY, the condition Kashtan & Alon predicted would fail; the
authors disclaim it themselves) and Crombach & Hogeweg 2008 (no sub-goal
decomposition; zero occurrences of "modular" in the body).

8. THE BOUNDARY -- THE SPECIMEN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Verbatim: "We find that MVG's outperformance occurred only toward goals within
the modularity language. MVG adaptation toward non-modular goals was not
significantly different from FG's (Figure 6E)." And: "In experiments toward
randomly chosen goals, populations had equal chance to be taken over by either FG
or MVG genomes."

The out-of-family goals were difficulty-matched by an explicit procedure: the
entire genotype space of a smaller 38-bit circuit model was enumerated (~1e11
genotypes) and goal hardness estimated by number of solutions. The obvious
loophole was closed by the authors themselves.

Authored family size, canonical circuit case: g,h in {XOR,EQ}, f in {AND,OR} gives
8 goals, of which six were studied. The comparison space of 4-input 1-output truth
tables has 65,536 members. The advantage exists on roughly 1e-4 of the phenotype
space -- the part the experimenter wrote down.

The input partition ({x,y} vs {w,z}) is fixed for every run in every condition and
is never varied, discovered or tested.

9. THE TWELVE REQUIRED ANSWERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Did MVG causally increase future acquisition efficiency?
    YES for in-family targets, with replication and controls. Causal in the sense
    that the environment was manipulated and the outcome moved; NOT causal at the
    level of the proposed mediator, which was never perturbed.

 2. Did it change the local phenotypic adjacent possible, or is that inferred?
    MEASURED. Exhaustively, over all single-point mutants, in phenotype space.
    Not inferred.

 3. Did the investigators measure that local change longitudinally?
    YES. Facilitated variation vs generations, 500 best-fitness circuits per
    population, 30-40 independent runs, over 1e5 generations, per arm.

 4. Was the future target genuinely novel or a new combination of known modules?
    BOTH WERE TESTED. new-comb (recombination) succeeded in both substrates
    (~68% RNA, ~75% circuits takeover). novel-module (a previously UNSEEN
    sub-function) succeeded in LOGIC CIRCUITS ONLY (~70% takeover) and was NULL
    in RNA. Genuinely new CONTENT is acquired faster; a genuinely new SCHEME was
    never presented.

 5. How much of the benefit came from human-authored structure in the goal family?
    All of the generalisation benefit. The advantage is switched off by leaving the
    authored language. The composition scheme, arity, module count and input
    partition are authored and invariant; only the slot fillers vary.

 6. Is modularity itself causal, or merely correlated with another change?
    BOTH, at different layers. Not causal for the speed effect (NBVG and RVG
    dissociate it). Causal for the neighbourhood bias (MVG and NBVG differ on
    exactly that). The hidden property underneath layer 1 is neutral-network-border
    positioning plus localized triggers.

 7. Broad evolvability, or narrow evolvability along the historical decomposition?
    NARROW. But note carefully: narrow WITHOUT a measured penalty. Outside the
    family the result is PARITY, not inversion. No ceiling was demonstrated. The
    mechanically-predicted cost (reduced pleiotropy should hurt targets needing
    cross-module coordination) was never tested, because random goals are not
    adversarial against the partition.

 8. What transfers to a synthetic world with unrelated physics?
    The DETECTOR and the CONTROLS, not the result. Portable: exhaustive 1-mutant
    phenotypic neighbourhood; the non-evolved phenotype-matched control; the
    three-arm environment design; the switching-period band (below the objective's
    reconfiguration time, above the unaided solution time -- three orders of
    magnitude wide here); the neutral-network-proximity recipe for constructing
    related-but-non-modular objectives. NOT portable: the facilitated-variation
    measure's usefulness predicate, which is defined by the authored decomposition
    and would be tautological if imported unchanged.

 9. What does Toussaint measure that MVG does not?
    An EVOLVABLE VARIATION OPERATOR. In MVG the operator is a fixed constant of
    the experiment; what moves is the genotype-phenotype map. Toussaint's
    second-type mutations make the representation itself mutable with a direct
    control parameter, and permit a phenotype-preserving intervention -- "same
    phenotype, same fitness, different reachable futures". MVG has no analogue.

10. What does MVG establish that Toussaint does not?
    A NON-TAUTOLOGICAL INTERVENTION. HC-T01's TRAP 1 is declared live: its ablated
    parameter directly parameterises the variation operator that induces its own
    detector. MVG intervenes on the GOAL SCHEDULE, causally remote from the
    mutation operator, which is never touched. Also: held-out target families with
    a difficulty-matched negative; 30-40 replicates with reported uncertainty
    against a Toussaint record with no noise floor at all; and a three-arm
    environmental design with a non-monotonic outcome.

11. Does this lineage threaten, complement, or preempt HC-T01?
    It PREEMPTS THE INSTRUMENT and COMPLEMENTS THE QUESTION. The local,
    longitudinal, population-level accessibility cell has been occupied since 2008.
    Any Collider document asserting that cell is empty should be corrected, date-
    stamped 2026-09-03. It does not preempt the phenomenon Prometheus is chasing,
    because the historical effect is bounded to the authored family.
    It also offers HC-T01 a concrete repair. *** SEE THE CORRECTION IN SECTION 14.
    The premise of what follows was overtaken by a commit that landed after this
    packet was frozen, and the reasoning below is retained only in weakened form. ***
    HC-T01 executed and its kill condition K7 fired -- its detector predicts
    acquisition no better than current fitness does. The MVG detector is not a
    breadth statistic; it is content-addressed -- is THIS specific useful phenotype
    one mutation away. Text S1 explicitly reports that organisms under unstructured
    variation vary MORE broadly and score LOWER. A breadth-like detector has no
    reason to beat fitness. Measuring WHICH phenotypes are reachable relative to a
    named target set, rather than HOW MANY, is inheritable and costs only a
    redefinition over the same samples.

12. Is there an untested composition between Toussaint, MVG and Kouvaris?
    *** SUPERSEDED. SEE SECTION 15. A systematic citation-intersection sweep found
    27 papers citing both lines, and at least two that compose them in experiment.
    The answer below overstated the emptiness and is corrected there. ***
    YES, and it is better than untested -- it is ASSERTED IN PRINT AND NEVER RUN.
    Fernando, Szathmary & Husbands 2012 (Front Comput Neurosci 6:24) state the
    composition directly: the Parter/Kashtan/Alon effect "occurs if there is
    non-trivial neutrality as described by Toussaint (2003)", citing the same PhD
    thesis the house Toussaint specimen is built on -- and the demonstration they
    offer is a different paper on a different substrate. Nobody ran it on
    Kashtan/Alon's systems. Reisinger & Miikkulainen 2006 is a near-miss that
    generalises Kashtan's varying fitness function but never engages Toussaint's
    neutrality line in its prose. Kouvaris 2017 and Watson & Szathmary 2016 both
    cite Toussaint only inside bulk background brackets, with zero occurrences of
    his name in body text: co-citation without composition.
    The reason it matters: the historical mechanism is a MIRROR, not a ratchet --
    the property decays when the environment stops supplying structure (2008
    Fig 9D; 2005 Fig 3; independently confirmed by Espinosa-Soto 2018). It cannot
    compound, because nothing carries the alignment forward except the pressure
    that created it. Toussaint supplies a heritable, self-adapting variation
    operator, which is a candidate for what could hold the reflection.
    Residual uncertainty: a full forward-citation sweep of the thesis was blocked
    by HTTP 429, so a composition could hide in an unindexed conference paper.

  refuted. Third-party reimplementations of the circuit arm exist and report
  supporting results, but none is peer-reviewed AND both are materially incomplete

- CONSEQUENCE FOR CALIBRATION: house calibration particle cal-08 (Kashtan-Alon,
  fam-161) cannot be used to pass or fail a Prometheus detector as things stand.

  use. SFE_MVG_CALIBRATION_PROPOSAL.md carries the amended scoring.
- The Kouvaris genealogy in this packet is established STRUCTURALLY, from two

- STILL UNDETERMINED: Kashtan's PhD thesis. Absent from DBLP and from his Google

- Hoverstad 2011 was identified but its full text is genuinely paywalled with zero

  benchmarks against Kashtan & Alon is unknown.
- Text S1's typeset algebra did not survive OLE2 text extraction, so the exact
  facilitated-variation formula and the mutational-modularity measure are
  reconstructed from prose and tagged INFERRED.
- The 2005 genotype-phenotype map is UNSPECIFIED (supplement HTTP 403).
- The 2007 per-model parameters are UNSPECIFIED (SI Appendix not retrieved).
- CONFOUND NOT CLOSED BY THE SOURCES: the FG arm in the 2008 facilitated-variation

- THE PROGRAMME'S BLIND SPOT, inherited by this review: every transfer test in the
  corpus is single-step. Evolve, then present ONE new goal. There is no second hop.
  A lineage that adapts fast to goal 2 and is crippled for goal 3 would be
  invisible to every experiment in this lineage. No claim about compounding can be
  sourced here in either direction.

13. REVIEWER'S BOTTOM LINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The one question to confirm or contest: given that the local, longitudinal,
population-level accessibility measurement has existed since 2008 with a cleaner
intervention than HC-T01 -- and that the effect it detects is switched off by
leaving the experimenter's own goal language -- is the Historical Collider still
looking for a missing measurement, or is it now looking for a world whose
decomposition nobody wrote down?

And the operational one, which is cheaper to settle: the NAND circuit arm that
every finding here rests on has never been independently reproduced by anyone, in
eighteen years. Runnable third-party implementations exist. Is Prometheus willing
to be the first independent test of it, or should this specimen be held at
SINGLE-LABORATORY and kept out of the calibration set until someone is?

14. CORRECTION FILED AGAINST THIS PACKET AFTER FREEZE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Filed 2026-09-03 by the same seat, against its own section 9 answer 11. Left in
place rather than edited away, per the same discipline that produced the three
corrections in section 3.

WHAT THIS PACKET SAID: HC-T01's kill condition K7 fired, its detector predicts
acquisition no better than current fitness does, and the reason is that a
BREADTH-like detector has no reason to beat fitness -- so the repair is to make
the detector content-addressed.

WHAT LANDED AFTERWARDS: commit 62f7a1a1e (Lexis HC-T01 adjudication) and commit
9c1badfba (RA-1 preregistration), both after this packet was frozen. Lexis's
finding, verbatim from the commit record:

  "HC-T01's acquisition outcome is gain toward a bounded optimum, so gain is
   capped by (ceiling - current fitness) and current fitness is the sufficient
   statistic for that cap. Any predictor correlated with fitness inherits the
   correlation, and HC-T01 reported MARGINAL Spearman only, never partial. This
   rescues the hypothesis from its strongest-sounding disconfirmation and convicts
   the design, because a kill condition that cannot be passed is not a test."

The RA-1 data audit puts numbers on it: ceiling saturation is "severe, early and
ASYMMETRIC across exactly the arms HC-T01 compared", with 63 percent of one arm at
the optimum by generation 80 and zero percent of the ablated arm there through 175;
and at one horizon the treated arm has ONE distinct gain value across 30 runs,
where "a rank statistic there is undefined".

WHY THIS IS A DEFECT IN MY PACKET, NOT MERELY NEW INFORMATION: I offered a
MECHANISM explanation for K7 without first eliminating a MEASUREMENT explanation.
If gain is capped by (ceiling minus current fitness), then current fitness is the
sufficient statistic for the cap and ANY predictor correlated with fitness loses by
construction -- no property of the detector is required to explain the outcome.
That is the simplest explanation and it was available to me from the structure of
the outcome variable alone. Testing the simplest explanation before asserting a
mechanism is the doctrine this deep-dive invokes against its own specimen four
times, including at section 7. I did not apply it to my own repair proposal.

WHAT SURVIVES: the recommendation itself, at reduced strength and on different
grounds. Text S1's finding that unstructured variation produces BROADER and LESS
USEFUL variation than static environments is verified and unaffected. It remains a
good reason to prefer a content-addressed accessibility statistic over a breadth
statistic. It is no longer evidence about why K7 fired, because K7's firing is now
better explained by a bounded outcome variable than by anything about detectors.

WHAT IS WITHDRAWN: the inference "K7 fired, therefore the detector is inadequate".
That inference is unsafe and should not be quoted from this packet.

CONFIRMED, AND MORE SHARPLY THAN THE CORRECTION ABOVE CLAIMED. Commit d51d1fa82
(RA-1/RA-2 zero-compute adjudication over the frozen HC-T01 rows, preregistration
committed beforehand) measures the hazard exactly: at the original K7 window the
Spearman between current fitness and subsequent gain is "EXACTLY -1.0000 and
1-r^2 is 0.00000. Once a run reaches the optimum, gain equals minus current
fitness identically, so gain is a deterministic function of the conditioner. The
partial correlation does not exist there. K7 was computed at a point where it
could not have failed to fire."

Note what that does and does not settle. It explains K7's firing completely and
removes it as evidence about detectors. It does NOT rescue the accessibility
claim: the corrected conditional test has no eligible points in the arm that
matters, and the adjudication records INDETERMINATE without collapsing it into
NO_SIGNAL. The question is unanswered in that substrate, not answered favourably.

A SECOND FINDING FROM THE SAME PASS BEARS ON MY OWN D-LEVEL AWARD, and I record it
against myself rather than leaving it in another seat's ledger. RA-1's reverse-
precedence control (NC1) reports that accessibility measured AFTER the outcome
window tracks the outcome three to seven times better than accessibility measured
BEFORE it -- "the detector behaves like a readout of where a run currently is, not
a leading indicator of where it will go."

That is a precedence failure, and it is a hazard for any D3/D4 award anywhere,
including the D4-weak I gave Parter 2008 in section 5. My stated reason for the
"weak" qualifier was that accessibility and acquisition are joined at the treatment
arm rather than within runs. NC1 supplies the empirical reason that qualifier
matters: in a related substrate, an accessibility detector that looked longitudinal
turned out to be concurrent. The 2008 paper never ran a reverse-precedence control,
so nothing in this deep-dive establishes that its facilitated-variation trajectory
LEADS acquisition rather than tracking it. Any reproduction should run NC1 first;
it is free, being a re-ordering of data already collected.

A THIRD, weaker cross-reference. RA-2 finds that in that substrate the detector's
response is "largely reading whether the genome contains at least one production
rule" -- a STEP, not a gradient, with the first operator moving it by 2.39 and every
subsequent operator together by 0.44. The analogous untested question for this
specimen is whether the facilitated-variation measure is likewise dominated by the
presence or absence of a single structural feature rather than by graded
organisation. Nobody has checked. It is cheap to check in a reproduction and it is
registered here as a check, not as a finding.

ONE MORE CROSS-REFERENCE, not a correction. Lexis independently reports that the
frontier for this question is in Avida rather than in the GRN lineage, naming
Misevic, Ofria & Lenski 2006 and Kumawat, Lalejini, Acosta & Zaman 2024 (PNAS),
with the latter supplying an acquisition outcome that avoids the ceiling trap
(time-to-re-adapt rather than gain-to-ceiling) plus a reciprocal transplant
control. Section 5 of this packet states that D5 is unoccupied "in this lineage
and in Toussaint's and in Kouvaris's alike". That scoping remains correct as
written, and readers should not extend it to the Avida literature, which this
deep-dive did not survey and which may contain a stronger perturbation design.
