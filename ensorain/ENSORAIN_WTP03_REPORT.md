## 2. What the collider established

1. **Survival and exposure were achieved (auth s5-s6).** Every admitted carrier lived >= 75% of its
   lifetime, with >= 200 updates and replay consolidation.
2. **The constant, marginal, lookup and simple-substrate nulls lose where they should.**
   - V2: on an additive field, the best structured XC is -1.6.
   - V3: the constant has XC <= 0 on the WTP-02 #10 world.
   - Every promoted specimen beats all of N0-N5 on cells the stream never touched, including the
     pair-block recombination sets where lookup is useless.
3. **Structure dependence is exact.** The surrogate preserves the mean, every per-mode marginal and
   the variance to machine precision. Every promoted specimen collapses on it.
4. **Interventions are honest.** Every Wave E intervention recorded support. A reskin-invariant
   "specimen" would have failed (none was promoted without losing on reskin).
5. **Substrate phase structure did NOT replicate.**
   - 358 Wave B crossovers; 0 REPLICATED of 12 tested.
   - Budget and exposure crossovers between substrates are seed noise at this resolution.
   - The single bracketed boundary is a capacity threshold (TT rank becomes affordable), i.e. known.
6. **No superadditive hybrid survived the chain.**
   - The one STRUCTURE-DEPENDENT X4 (q18) has no carrier and no reskin loss.
   - Its hybrid is marginals + low-rank in a pairwise field: additive plus interaction.
7. **No conversion was ever necessary.** Distilling a trained substrate into another never beat
   training the target directly.

## 3. Instrument weaknesses found (for WTP-04)

W1 **TRANSFERS IS NEAR-TAUTOLOGICAL for field models.**
   - The "related world" keeps the SAME field (same seed; only sensing noise and policy change), so
     any competent field model transfers.
   - 9 of 12 specimens "transferred".
   - Discounting TRANSFERS entirely, the full chain still holds in 3 lineages (via RECOMBINES and
     PHASE_BOUNDARY): q0-q3, q11, q12.
   - Required: transfer to a DIFFERENT field from the same generator family, where only
     structure-level (not value-level) knowledge can carry.
W2 **ADMISSION PRE-SELECTS THE PHENOMENON.**
   - G5 requires an unbounded same-class planted fit to beat the ladder, so admitted worlds are
     exactly those where completion works.
   - The collider then confirms completion. That is correct as a positive control, but it cannot
     find anything completion does not.
   - Required: admit on information demand measured by a class-agnostic learner (e.g. held-out
     predictability by any member of a DIFFERENT class than the specimen), or run the collider on a
     G5-agnostic population.
W3 **FOUNDER CONCENTRATION.**
   - 174 of 181 admitted worlds are mutants of 13 founders; 145 more passed but hit the lineage cap.
   - The cap worked, but the effective sample is 13, not 181.
W4 **SAME-CLASS NULL MISSING.**
   - The ladder has no "batch fit of the specimen's own class" rung.
   - The post-data N6 check (s3a) beats every promoted specimen.
   - Required: an N6 = the best unbounded batch completion of any class. A candidate
     "extraordinary" specimen must beat N6, or do something N6 cannot (e.g. amortise, transfer
     across fields, recombine where N6 is unidentifiable).

## 4. Findings about the world grammar

- **Inhabitable learning worlds are rare in the WTP grammar.** 0 of 2,000 random dev candidates, and
  the first campaign founder only after ~6,000 candidates. Unconditionally about 0.5% of candidates
  are admissible, almost all found by climbing near-misses.
- **The binding constraint is in-life learning speed, not information.**
  - Bounded learners rarely out-earn trivial behaviour (44 of 181 admitted worlds).
  - Offline, the same stream supports strong completion.
  - The ratio of learning time to lifetime is the latent control ratio that decides admission
    (auth s14).
- **Weird physics generally destroys learnability.** 3 of 181 admitted worlds are "wild"; transform
  chains, credit corruption and graph-cell maps almost always make the stream uninformative.
  Keeping the search weird (s19) and requiring relationships to matter (s4) pull hard against each
  other in this grammar.
