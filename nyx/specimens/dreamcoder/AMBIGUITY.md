# DreamCoder -- alternative cuts, preserved (charter VII)

Currency: 2026-09-11. Nyx's cut is A. B, C and D are plausible cuts not
taken, recorded for a second Chopper or a consumer. Nothing is merged;
the operator's direction of 2026-09-11 forbids resolving cuts by
argument.

## Cut A (Nyx, v0): three organs at three scales, two pressures

    organ.dreamcoder.library_compression.v0             MECHANISM
    organ.dreamcoder.wake_sleep_alternation.v0          SUBSYSTEM
    organ.dreamcoder.recognition_guided_enumeration.v0  MECHANISM
    pressure.dreamcoder.recurring_structure.v0          (delivered)
    pressure.dreamcoder.budget_below_the_space.v0       (held)

Reason for the cut: the OPERATIONAL evidence. One piece (compression)
runs here as a standalone descendant in 70 ms; one piece (the
enumerator + recogniser) cannot be built here at all; the loop is
neither. The specimen's own lineage made the first cut (Stitch), which
is evidence the compression organ is separable, and the blockers make
the second cut visible (the enumerator is a process boundary). Where
humans and toolchains have already cut, Nyx cut along the same lines --
and that is a bias worth naming for the self-comparison (NYX-23): Nyx
cut where the seams were ALREADY visible, not where the mechanism might
be.

## Cut B: the loop is the only organ

Compression's objective is the enumerator's prior (the grammar cost);
the recogniser's training data is the compressor's output plus samples
from the grammar it produced. Under B the three phases are not
separately meaningful because each phase's output format exists only
as the next phase's input format, and nothing outside the loop consumes
any of them. The organ is "amortised search over a self-revised
representation" and it is a SUBSYSTEM; asking for smaller organs
destroys the property (charter III's stopping rule).

Where B disagrees with A: A says compression has an external consumer
(H0's frozen-library cells; Techne's export interface accepted by the
Vivarium loader), so it is separately meaningful. B replies that an
empty library was the only thing ever exported, so the external
consumer has consumed nothing yet and A's evidence is a plan.

## Cut C: the typed grammar with a prior is the organ

Everything else is an operation on it: compression adds productions,
recognition reweights them, enumeration evaluates them in order. Under
C the transferable substrate is the REPRESENTATION (a grammar that can
grow and be reweighted), and a soup organism that carries such a thing
could acquire any of the three operations by selection.

Where C disagrees with A: A treats the grammar as STATE of each organ;
C says the state is the organ and A's organs are its verbs. C predicts
that giving a soup a growable, reweightable representation and NO
operations yields the operations under recurring_structure pressure;
A predicts it does not without at least the compressor. This is a
MIXED-vs-MINIMAL condition in the same world and is the pairwise
experiment Nyx would request.

## Cut D (not developed): the replay buffer is the organ

The set of solved programs is what both sleeps consume; the loop is a
way of spending it. Recorded because it makes the MAP-Elites archive
and the DreamCoder replay buffer the same kind of thing (a retained set
consumed by later operations), which is a cross-specimen cut a second
Chopper might take and Nyx did not.

## Open ambiguities

1. Is the library STATE or ORGAN (A vs C)? Not resolvable by argument;
   resolvable by the MIXED/MINIMAL comparison above.
2. Does "recurring structure" mean syntactic sharing (what Stitch
   measures) or semantic sharing (what a world could reward)? The
   pressure is written for the second; the only local measurement is
   of the first. If Vivarium builds the world with a semantic notion,
   the organ Nyx delivered may not exploit it -- which is the charter X
   return "known organ does not actually exploit it", anticipated.
3. Is the enumerator's type system part of the enumerator or part of
   the grammar (A puts it in the enumerator as a PRIMITIVE; C in the
   grammar)? Untested either way; no local enumerator was examined.
