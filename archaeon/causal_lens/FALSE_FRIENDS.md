# FALSE_FRIENDS ledger (PORTABILITY-01 s11)

Quantities that share a name across Prometheus engines but mean different causal things. Every row cites the defining code.
Canonical terms come from CAUSAL_LINEAGE_CONTRACT v0.1. "Conversion" says whether the native quantity can be turned into the
canonical one, and with what evidence.
