## 3. Coordinate definition and leakage audit

Decision-state myopia, exact: delta(s, g) = Q*(s, g) - V*(s) > 0. Primary coordinate kappa_H(s, g): the chosen probe is dominated by
a probe no worse on ER and strictly better on outcome entropy (cheap, from the fossils and the probe alphabet). Secondary: kappa_tie,
kappa_proxy, pool_suboptimal, root coordinates. Leakage: no producer or coordinate has a target/outcome/winner parameter (tested);
coordinates are computed before the score is revealed and carry the evidence snapshot id. Defect found and fixed between runs: v0
seed_inputs carried target_index (see 4); v1 seeds are (lane, L, state_id, arm, step).

## 9. Ruling on G / W / M

* W and M bought nothing: never better than G at the bar in any of 233 states except 1 and 5 respectively, worse than G in 10-14,
  at 3-9x G's compute. M's design (two-step from W's one-step top-6) cannot see a far-sighted probe when the whole one-step class is
  greedy (exact, section 5). Both are retained as measured controls only; neither is a production candidate. G stays incumbent.
* The deeper policy O beats G by ~2.7 % of probes in ~40 % of these tiny worlds and is never worse; the loss is in the endgame
  (5..16 feasible targets, depth 2-3) and is a tie-break loss of a collision objective, not a lookahead loss. The cheap fix GH
  (entropy tie-break) recovers a third to a half of it at G's cost but is worse than G in 10-18 states -- not licensed either.
* kappa_H is a real but weak coordinate (precision up to 0.71, recall <= 0.35, 7-10 % of decisions); routing on it recovers half
  of O's advantage at 7-10 % of O calls, below the preregistered routing bar.
