## Reading, bounded by the construction fact

direct reach <= 8 and permuted reach <= 12 are ANALYTIC (the four high
bits of the direct genome are inert; a permutation cannot create more
than 12 distinct neighbours). Every number above sits at or just under
its bound, so this readout carries NO evidence about learned
evolvability; it is the H5 instrument CALIBRATED on the live map: the
executor reproduces the published equivalence classes exactly, and the
producer-side arithmetic collapses to those classes as designed. That is
what the alpha was for. The evidence question (does an evolved encoding
exceed the permutation bound in class-reach?) needs the human-issued
comparison with a learned decoder, which does not exist yet.

## Provenance note on the queue rows (Vivarium #181 item 4)

Every row of cs-h5-1 and cs-h5-1-r1 was submitted as its own set-of-one
under a SHARED candidate_set_id, so the consumer bound each in SFE as
one-chosen-over-N (selected=1, alternatives=255 / 23). Those SFE
selection families are a misrepresentation of the design: all members
executed; nothing was chosen over anything. The rows are left as they
are (history); the readout does not use the selection binding. Fixed
for every future issue: vivqueue.submit refuses a reused
candidate_set_id (CandidateSetReused), and campaigns carry their
grouping in source_evidence.campaign_set. See QUEUE_RELATION_CONTRACT.md
correction of 2026-09-11.
