Preregistration: `S4_PREREG_2026-09-12.json` at commit 70e01cc41 (on
origin/main before the batch). Producers frozen at PRODUCER_VERSION
(sha256 of `archaeon/producer/s4_producers.py`) on every proposal.

## What ran, and what did not

The full batch (`S4_RUNNER_2026-09-12.py`) completed 240 worlds: L 8, 12,
16, 24 x n0 1, 3 x 30 seeds, arms U/G/W (+M at L <= 16). The L 32 tail
was STOPPED: its first world had not completed after ~30 minutes against
the preregistered 60 s per-selection bound (the bound as implemented is
checked after a selection returns, so a single slow selection is not cut
short -- a defect in the bound's implementation, recorded). L 32 is
COMPUTATIONALLY_INTRACTABLE for G, W and M at this code; U is trivially
tractable. Because the runner writes its JSON only at the end, the
PRIMARY analysis is computed from the runner's per-world log
(`S4_BATCH_LOG_2026-09-12.txt` -> `S4_PRIMARY_FROM_LOG_2026-09-12.json`,
240 worlds), and the SECONDARY analyses (trajectories, complementarity,
convergence, cost, redundancy) come from a labelled SUBSET rerun of the
same preregistered design with 8 seeds per cell
(`S4_RESULTS_SUBSET_2026-09-12.json`). Nothing was re-selected: the
subset's seeds are the first 8 of each cell.

Reading: G and W almost never propose the same probe while uncertainty
is high (0 percent at log2|T| > 10) and converge as it collapses (27-59
percent at log2|T| <= 4); 20-50 percent of states are ones where every
producer's proposal gains the same (late, small sets). When G does
poorly, W is modestly better on average (by 0.05-0.22 bits) but beats G
outright on only 6-19 percent of those states, and U on 0-25 percent:
G's poor states are mostly poor for everyone. Producer errors are only
weakly complementary in this world; failure is NOT predictable from the
observable regime coordinates at a level that would license a
meta-selector, and none is proposed.

## Diversity (from the S3 pool correlations, unchanged) and the L 24 myopia

The S3 finding stands: distance is a partial proxy (rho 0.34-0.60). In
S4 the widened pool did not cure the S3 L 24 early lag at the bar: W is
better than G at L 24 n0=3 by 0.013 (17/30 best-or-tied vs 7/30) and
worse at L 24 n0=1 by 0.006 -- neither clears 0.02. The myopia is in the
one-step objective, not only in the pool.

Preserved as evidence: evidence-consuming producers dominate blind
production in every cell (0.024-0.091 nAUC, sign tests p < 0.05); the
three information producers are interchangeable at this bar on this
world; their best-or-tied counts drift with regime (G at one fossil,
W/M at three) below the bar; cost differs materially (G cheapest, W 4-25 s
at L 24, M intractable above 16); complementarity is weak; the
preregistered bound was implemented post-hoc per selection and let L 32
run past it -- a defect to fix before any season that includes L 32.
