0. SUMMARY FOR THE HURRIED REVIEWER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VERDICT: in this search (Proteus v0 VM, CMP3 GA), an honest signalling system does NOT evolve or re-establish.
- It fails from random gen 0, and from one instruction or one word away from full communication.
- It fails under both individual and shared (group-mean) reward.
- It fails in a world built so that signalling is free for the signaller and valuable for the group.

MECHANISM (measured, not assumed):
- the selection coefficient of a restored signaller is s ~ .02-.05;
- a clean one-word restore arises in ~1e-4 of offspring;
- the unused listener half decays by neutral drift.
Expected establishments per run << 1. The barrier is QUANTITATIVE (supply x selection vs decay), not principled.

KILLED along the way:
- a zero-sum world as a test bed (B92/B93);
- 'dilution by a rotating scout' as the mechanism (B96b: fixing the scout only concentrates s, it does not raise it);
- 'decay alone' (B96: listener immigration does not help).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. WHAT WAS LEARNED (shapes)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Whether information pays depends on whether the resource is RIVAL. In regeneration-limited worlds a signal only
   redistributes.
2. A two-sided cooperative trait (sender + receiver) is much harder for this search than a single-individual
   structure. The keyed precursor two edits away is restored 4/8 because every part is selected in one genome. The
   signal one word away is restored 0/24, because its benefit lands on others and its receiver half is unselected
   when absent.
3. The barrier is quantitative. Raising s, raising supply, or protecting the receiver half are the levers, each
   tested alone here. None was sufficient at these magnitudes.
