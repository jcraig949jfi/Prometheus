ARCHAEON P2B SFE BETA -- REVIEW PACKET: FINDING 2 OPEN QUESTION 3 (REWARD SEMANTICS)
Branch archaeon/p2b-sfe-2026-10-06. Written 2026-10-17. For a reviewer who has not followed the campaign.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CLAIM (tier: PROBABLE within one world family; second family B119 queued)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Start from a working forager that ignores which pools are harmful. Train it on worlds where each pool is toxic with
p = 1/2 (a toxic harvest costs 2x its value) and a type word says which. In about half the runs, foragers evolve that
READ the type word and AVOID toxic pools. Matched controls trained without toxicity never do.
It is NOT reached from a random gen 0, nor when the type words sit far from the pool words.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHY THE QUESTION WAS OPEN
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In the Finding 2 world family the observed pool magnitude IS the payoff (runtime.py: gain = pools[i]). So the
transferable rule 'harvest the non-empty pool' cannot be wrong about meaning, and transfer had only been tested over
dynamics, not semantics.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. WORLD AND READOUTS (code: b118_toxic.py, b118c_interleaved.py, b118d_seeded_forager.py, b118e_type_info.py)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Toxic wrapper: per episode, each pool is independently toxic. A toxic harvest subtracts cost x gain. The type word
  (1 = toxic) is shown only when the pool is local, like the amount.
- Interleaved layout: channel 0 = [p0, t0, p1, t1, p2, t2, rest...].
- Hand controls: GEN(3) harvests the first non-empty pool and ignores types; CHK(3) also skips toxic pools.
- PLAIN arm: the same layout with p = 0 (type words always 0).
- Training: a fresh echo-free family world (R = 3) every generation; CMP3 N = 200, E = 8, G = 300.
- Held-out: 12 worlds never used in training, lift vs the best constant program.
- PRIMARY READOUT (B118f/g): constant-twin type INFO = held-out lift minus the best lift with EVERY type word fixed to
  0 or to 1. A fixed word carries no information about which pool is toxic. Semantic = info > .01.
  (Zeroing alone measures dependence, not information: the B115 lesson from the communication lane.)

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. CONTROLS THAT CAN KILL THE CLAIM
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Hand GEN3 info 0, CHK3 info .095 on the same held-out set: the readout discriminates.
- Training-side value check: CHK3 - GEN3 +.089, positive on 8/8 training worlds.
- PLAIN controls the layout, the seed, the search and the compute; it differs only in toxicity.
- const1 (every pool labelled toxic) lowers every reader's lift, and const0 (every pool safe) equals the blind
  readout. So readers act on the label, not on the word's presence.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. KNOWN WEAKNESSES (mine) -- please attack these
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
a. B118d's prereg bar failed; the constant-twin readout was defined after seeing two of its cells. It was then
   FIXED in advance for B118f/g. B118d is reported, not counted.
b. Seed defect: my hand manifests used tape_words 100. The GA's halve/double mutation produced an invalid 50 and
   crashed B118f TOXIC 11833. That cell was rerun on a tape-128 seed and became the strongest of the series. B118g
   reran the whole design on the tape-128 seed (clean). The tape-128 seed may itself reach readers more easily
   (B118g 4/8 vs B118f's tape-100 cells 3/7); this is unexamined.
c. One world family, one layout, R = 3 only. Ensemble invariance is untested until B119 (P-boom lineage).
d. Effect sizes are small in absolute terms (lift .03-.08 vs CHK3 .092). Readers are partial (10-16% toxic harvests).
e. The 'distance' interpretation of B118b's failure (types at the end) is a hypothesis; B118b also differed from
   B118c-g in starting from random gen 0. Distance alone was never isolated with a seeded forager.
