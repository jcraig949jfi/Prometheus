## E. Recombination: confounded by construction

Grammar constraint: ['recombination only with EXTERNAL (PAIR_EXECUTION is the endogenous form)']. `grammar.matched_controls: flip_r = ENDOGENOUS_COPY for an EXTERNAL treatment; pressures explicit_fitness and recombination are dropped (-> implicit_survival if empty)`.

Exact pairing, with each control recomputed from its treatment's preserved SPEC: 1829 recombination treatments; 1823 of their reproduction controls ran; controls that keep recombination = **0**; controls that are ENDOGENOUS_COPY = 1829; treatments that also lost `explicit_fitness` in the control = 550 (controls keeping it = 0). Every moat_advantage on a recombination row therefore compares EXTERNAL+recombination(+selection) against endogenous copying without recombination or selection. Three factors change at once. **No causal recombination effect can be read from these rows.**
