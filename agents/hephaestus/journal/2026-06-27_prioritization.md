> "Tool forging is never complete imho. We could easily discover a new approach or

## Resolution

- **Forging is a standing, never-closed capability.** Do NOT declare the forge phase
  "complete" / retire it (an earlier Harmonia framing — withdrawn). A new approach or
  build format could add real value at any time; keep the capability warm.
- **But forging is not the priority thrust.** Fire it *opportunistically* — when a new
  approach or problem-FORMAT appears (diversity/seed forges proved format-change unlocks
  new mechanisms). Do not grind the exhausted Nous-monolithic queue for throughput
  (that's costume-production / source-novelty Goodhart).
- **Priority = exploit what we already have:** (1) the +11/+32pp failure-mining →
  organism ablation card, (2) the NL parsing bottleneck, (3) Apollo `forward_chain`
  integration, (4) metabolization gate (knockout + behavioral-NCD as the gate), (5)
  Harmonia B ladder as oracle. See `STATUS.md` §5.

## Why this is the right call (epistemics)

A generative search cannot be honestly declared "complete" — that was an over-prune.
Over-retiring a useful capability is its own failure mode. The correct posture is
exploit-first with forging held open, not forge-first or forge-closed.
