3. V1-B METABOLIZATION EXPERIMENT (H2) -- PRIMARY, AND A CLEAN NULL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8 frozen design tasks x {control, wiki}; 16 isolated designer agents.
Control: full repo search, wiki never mentioned. Wiki: same + mandatory
structured consultation (queries logged server-side). Frozen deterministic
checklist: D duplication, K known-failure walk-in, R reuse-signature as a
concrete design element, F numeric falsifiers + stopping rule.

Result: ALL 16 ARMS SCORED 4/4. Ties on all 8 tasks; W - L = 0 against a
preregistered demonstration threshold of >= 5.

  G7 = METABOLIZATION_NOT_DEMONSTRATED

Diagnosis (stated, not softened): the instrument SATURATED. Every control
agent found every trap through ordinary repo search -- this repo is dense
with verdict documents and the designers were strong. This is a valid null
about this instrument at this designer strength and documentation density,
not a demonstration of equivalence. V2 discriminating design is specified
(priors outside verdict docs; time-boxed/weaker designers; graded scoring
with demonstrated attainable range; non-author scorer).

Secondary, exploratory, non-gating:
  - G18 traceability: 8/8 wiki proposals map claim ids to design decisions,
    verifiable against ew.read_log; 0/8 controls are machine-resolvable.
  - G8 PASS as phenomenon: concrete changed decisions traceable to
    retrieved negative evidence (e.g. T2-wiki: "rows with finish_reason !=
    stop are QUARANTINED" from the truncation-confound finding) -- but the
    differential vs control was not shown.
  - T3-wiki spontaneously consumed registered gap H-bac36ae694a2 and

6. KNOWN LIMITATIONS & CAVEATS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Annotators/designers are isolated contexts of ONE base-model family;
  the 0.956 agreement partly reflects shared priors. G3's design (labels
  from one agent set, pairs from another) reduces but does not remove this.
  Human or different-model annotation is the V2 strengthening.
- Mnemosyne scored V1-B against frozen checklists and authored the wiki;
  quotes shipped; the result was a null AGAINST the wiki.
- The V1-B null generalizes only to strong designers on a well-documented
  repo; weaker designers or thinner documentation may show a differential.
- G14 cross-host leg and G11 prospective verdict remain open by nature.
- 18/60 condition-C normalizations were flagged low-confidence.
- Consensus ingestion means curator-community label disagreements resolved
  toward the community; policy reversible via HUMAN_RULING (audit packet).
