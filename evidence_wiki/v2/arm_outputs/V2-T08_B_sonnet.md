# PROPOSAL V2-T08 (arm B)

## Hypothesis
A LoRA fine-tune of a small local instruct model on our archive of solved/failed attempt records will NOT produce a source-general success/failure judge if trained the way the prior identical-shape experiment (X-02d362ae90bd, "greedy-LoRA") was trained — because that experiment already measured the gain as format-following > False/kill class-prior > per-source template memorization > genuine reasoning, with zero cross-domain transfer (C-01f913ae81af). This arm's hypothesis is therefore narrower and adversarial to the easy win:

H1 (primary): even after (a) filtering the archive for known provenance defects, (b) balancing the True/False label prior per source, and (c) de-templating source-identifying surface features, a rank-16 LoRA judge fine-tuned on solved/failed attempt records will STILL fail to beat a non-reasoning structural heuristic floor on a leave-one-source-out (LOSO) holdout. I.e., the calibrating kill generalizes past its original confounds and is a property of the archive/objective, not an artifact of the first experiment's hygiene gaps.

H0 (the interesting alternative, not the default expectation): with those three fixes, LOSO judgment clears both chance and the heuristic floor by a preregistered margin — meaning the earlier null was confound-driven, not structural, and genuine cross-source reasoning is extractable from this archive after all.

This is designed so that EITHER outcome is informative: H1 confirmed closes the LLM-judge-from-raw-text approach on this archive with a cleaner methodology than the first kill; H0 confirmed reopens it with a concrete recipe (label-balance + de-templating + LOSO-as-primary-gate).

## Motivating evidence
The exact task shape here — fine-tune a small model on solved/failed attempt records to judge new-attempt success — has already been run once end-to-end (Ergon, X-02d362ae90bd, Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B-Instruct, rank-16 LoRA, greedy substrate scrape, 1,200-item gold set + 494-claim computable-gold OOD set) and REFUTED as a calibrating kill (C-01f913ae81af): "adding more failure data of the same shape will not grow the reasoning share." Leave-one-source-out ablation on that run showed the model's apparent skill is almost entirely per-source memorization: dropping theseus collapsed 0.86->0.25, dropping hephaestus 1.0->0.29, dropping pollux 1.0->0.395 (E-7f5f77d8be35). The OOD computable-gold judgement (494 claims, domains disjoint from training) was 0.55 vs 0.50 chance — and that thin margin sits on top of a 0.20/0.90 True/False split, i.e. a label-prior artifact, not evidence of judgment. A diverse-gold transfer probe (n=600) moved +0.027, ~1.3 sigma, not significant.

A companion positive precedent exists but is NOT the same mechanism: Incubation v1's guard learner recovered an exact trap boundary from failure evidence alone with 0.9975-1.0 out-of-sample failure prediction (C-f1d363ff01e4) — but this was explicit symbolic-predicate search over a small enumerable state space, not free-text LLM fine-tuning, and it is bounded (cannot repair task-level hostility). Separately, warm-start collaborative filtering over a mined-failure solve matrix beat a popularity baseline (COLLAB AUC 0.829 vs POP 0.754, C-b2cebe551f3b) — a structured, behavioral-feature approach, again not an LLM judge, and explicitly in-distribution only. Cold-start feature routing from concept/field metadata was NULL against the same popularity baseline (C-94fc12c3e6af). A related transfer claim on a different archive (14-cell invariant structure) was RETRACTED as anti-transfer/memorization, with both models at or below chance out-of-domain (C-e5e726a050c1) — a second, independent instance of the same failure mode this design must survive.

Two archive-fitness defects bear directly on training-data hygiene: failed API/transport calls were being rendered as if they were real prior-attempt residue, at up to 24.4% contamination in one block (C-55004a4674b8, DEFECT CONFIRMED); and a large corpus (theseus) was found to carry NO usable (state, action, outcome) triple across any of its 8 generators, each failing for a distinct structural reason (C-dc484d4cf977) — meaning "the archive" is not uniformly minable and per-source fitness must be checked before inclusion, not assumed. Finally, a baseline-calibration finding shows a trivial one-line non-LLM heuristic can clear 0.52 on a task family where a real solver only reaches 0.48 (C-82fe472469ca) — establishing that "beats chance" is the wrong floor; a fine-tuned judge must beat the best cheap non-reasoning heuristic.

## Prospective predictions
- If H1 (structural null) holds: LOSO accuracy/AUC for the fine-tuned judge will sit within measurement error of the best cheap heuristic (features: source tag absent, input length, presence of surface lint like error-code tokens or template markers) on every held-out source, even after label-balancing and de-templating.
- If H0 (confound-driven null) holds: on at least 3 of 4 held-out sources, the fine-tuned judge's LOSO accuracy clears the heuristic floor by >=5pp with the confidence interval excluding zero, AND the shuffled-label control on the same split stays at chance (ruling out a residual prior leak).
- Either way, per-source ablation deltas should shrink relative to the original run (0.86->0.25 etc.) once class-balancing and de-templating are applied, because a large share of the original collapse was traceable to source-identifying surface tokens, not source-specific reasoning content — this sub-prediction is falsifiable independently of the headline.

## Experiment
1. **Archive fitness pass (gate, not optional):** for each candidate source (the same substrate families as X-02d362ae90bd plus any new ones), verify a usable (attempt_content, outcome) pair exists and is not sourced from a transport/http_error row (per C-55004a4674b8's defect signature) and is not a corpus already closed for navigation for lacking state/action/outcome triples (per C-dc484d4cf977's per-generator failure list). Sources failing this pass are excluded before any split is drawn.
2. **Label-balance correction:** resample or reweight each source's True/False (succeed/fail) ratio to a fixed target (e.g., 40/60, never worse than 30/70) so no source can supply a False/kill prior strong enough to explain a 0.55-vs-0.50 margin by itself; record the pre- and post-balance ratio per source.
3. **De-templating pass:** strip or randomize source-identifying formatting (field ordering, boilerplate headers, error-code vocabulary specific to one substrate) so the surface template-class signal the original ablation implicated cannot trivially leak source identity into the input.
4. **Splits:** (a) entity-disjoint within-source holdout (replicating C-d1ce433f289a's clean methodology), (b) leave-one-source-out (LOSO) across all fit sources — PRIMARY gate — (c) a computable/verifiable-gold OOD slice disjoint in domain from training, matching the original design's strongest control.
5. **Model/training:** Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B-Instruct (or equivalent <=3-4B instruct model per local VRAM ceiling), rank-16 LoRA, single epoch first pass, same hyperparameters as X-02d362ae90bd where not otherwise changed, so any effect is attributable to the three hygiene interventions above rather than to a new training recipe.
6. **Arms:** (i) full LoRA judge on hygiene-fixed data; (ii) shuffled-label control (same data, labels permuted); (iii) heuristic-floor baseline (cheap structural features, no LLM); (iv) base model, no fine-tune (zero-shot judge) — reproducing the 0.228->0.908-style headline check but on hygiene-fixed data, to confirm the fix didn't kill the in-distribution signal outright.
7. **Metrics:** per-source LOSO accuracy/AUC with bootstrap CI, entity-disjoint within-source accuracy, OOD computable-gold accuracy, and per-source ablation deltas (replicating the original leave-one-out table) for direct before/after comparison against C-01f913ae81af's numbers.

## Controls
- Shuffled-label control on every split (arm ii above) — must sit at chance; a shuffled-label result above chance invalidates the whole run (residual leak).
- Heuristic-floor control (arm iii) — cheap non-reasoning features only; the fine-tuned judge is graded against this floor, not against 0.50 chance, per C-82fe472469ca.
- Entity-disjoint AND source-disjoint (LOSO) splits, not just entity-disjoint — the original run's collapse was invisible to entity-disjointness alone and only surfaced under source ablation.
- Class-balance audit reported per source pre/post-correction, so any residual True/False skew is visible next to the accuracy number, not hidden inside it.
- Archive-fitness manifest listing every excluded source and the exclusion reason (transport contamination / no usable triple), so silent inclusion of unfit data cannot inflate the headline.

## Confound defenses
- **Format/template leak (primary confound from the prior kill):** addressed by the de-templating pass (step 3) and by making LOSO the PRIMARY, not secondary, gate — the original design ran LOSO only as a follow-up ablation after the headline was already reported.
- **False/kill class-prior:** addressed by explicit label-balancing per source (step 2) with the pre/post ratio reported; a judge that only recovers the label prior will fail the shuffled-label control at the same rate it "succeeds," making the confound visible rather than hidden inside a 0.55-vs-0.50 margin.
- **Fabricated/contaminated residue rows:** addressed by the archive-fitness pass (step 1) using the exact defect signature already confirmed in C-55004a4674b8 (transport/http_error rows rendered as legitimate prior-attempt residue).
- **Corpus unfit for the (state,action,outcome) objective:** addressed by excluding any source matching one of the 8 documented failure patterns in C-dc484d4cf977 rather than assuming every archived "attempt record" contains a usable triple.
- **Wrong floor (chance instead of heuristic):** addressed by carrying the heuristic-floor arm as a first-class comparator, not a footnote, per C-82fe472469ca's finding that a trivial heuristic can beat a real solver.
- **Selection-relation control breaking:** LOSO and entity-disjoint splits are constructed so no held-out row shares an entity or source-template with any training row (control must break the selection relation, not just resemble a held-out set).

## Preregistered falsifiers (numeric thresholds)
1. **LOSO primary gate:** the fine-tuned judge (arm i) must beat the heuristic floor (arm iii) by >=5pp accuracy/AUC on at least 3 of 4 (or however many, N>=4) held-out sources, each with a bootstrap CI excluding zero, to count as ANY positive signal. Failing on <3 sources => H1 (structural null) stands, disposition KILL for the raw-text LLM-judge approach on this archive.
2. **Shuffled-label ceiling:** the shuffled-label control (arm ii) must land within 2pp of chance on every split. If it exceeds chance by >5pp anywhere, the run is INVALIDATED (leak), not merely disconfirmed — fix and rerun before interpreting arm i.
3. **OOD computable-gold bar:** to count as generalization (not the original 0.55-vs-0.50 margin), the hygiene-fixed judge must clear 0.60 accuracy on the computable-gold OOD slice (n>=494, matching the original set size) with the 95% CI lower bound above 0.52 (the documented heuristic floor from C-82fe472469ca), not merely above 0.50 chance.
4. **Per-source ablation improvement:** at least 2 of the 3 originally-collapsing sources (theseus, hephaestus, pollux) must show a leave-one-out drop of <=0.30 absolute (vs. the original 0.61, 0.71, 0.605 drops) after de-templating, to support the sub-claim that surface-template leakage — not source-specific reasoning — drove the original collapse.
5. Any result landing between falsifiers 1 and 3 (e.g., LOSO gate passes but OOD-gold bar fails, or vice versa) is reported as NOT_ESTABLISHED with both numbers shown side by side — no verdict is asserted from a single passing metric.

## Stopping rule
Run the archive-fitness pass and label-balance/de-templating pipeline first and freeze the resulting dataset manifest (source list, per-source N, pre/post label ratio) BEFORE any training run. Train and evaluate all four arms in one pass on the frozen manifest. Do not iterate on hyperparameters, prompt format, or source inclusion after seeing arm-i LOSO numbers — a second pass with knowledge of the first pass's per-source results would reintroduce exactly the per-template overfitting this design exists to detect. If falsifier 2 (shuffled-label leak) fires, one repair-and-rerun cycle is permitted (fix the identified leak only); a second leak on the repair run stops the experiment and is reported as an instrumentation failure, not a judgment result.

## Expected failure modes
- Most likely (given two independent priors, C-01f913ae81af and C-e5e726a050c1, both showing anti-transfer/memorization on small-model fine-tunes over archived attempt records): H1 holds — LOSO stays at or below the heuristic floor even after hygiene fixes, because the archive's signal-bearing content per source is genuinely too narrow/idiosyncratic for a 1.5-3B model to extract a source-general judgment rule from raw text, regardless of label balance.
- De-templating may fail to fully remove source identity if content-level vocabulary (not just formatting) is itself source-specific (e.g., theseus content is intrinsically about knot invariants) — in that case LOSO collapse would persist for a content reason indistinguishable from a template reason, and this design cannot fully separate the two; flag this as a residual confound in the writeup rather than claiming full de-templating.
- The archive-fitness pass may exclude so many sources that N is too small for a meaningful LOSO gate (fewer than ~4 fit sources) — if so, downgrade to entity-disjoint-only reporting and mark the LOSO falsifier NOT APPLICABLE rather than force a low-N LOSO claim.
- Label-balancing by resampling may itself shrink the training set below what the original run used, confounding a "did balancing help" comparison with "did less data hurt" — mitigate by reporting raw source N before/after resampling.

## Compute estimate
Single small local model (<=3-4B parameters, e.g., Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B-Instruct), rank-16 LoRA, single epoch, matching X-02d362ae90bd's original recipe scale — that run fit within the documented local VRAM ceiling (3-4B comfortably, 7B OOMs on the 17GB card). Expect a single-GPU run on the order of the original experiment's wall-clock (low single-digit GPU-hours for training across ~1,200-2,000 archive rows post-fitness-filtering, plus inference-only passes for the heuristic-floor and base-model arms, which are near-zero additional compute). No multi-node or multi-seed requirement for this pass; if falsifier 1 is marginal (2 of 4 sources pass), a second-seed replication is the natural follow-on, not part of this run's committed budget.

## Unresolved uncertainty
Whether content-level (not merely formatting-level) source specificity can be separated from genuine cross-source reasoning at all with this archive is not resolved by this design — de-templating attacks surface template leakage but cannot distinguish "this source's math content is intrinsically narrow" from "the model failed to generalize a general rule." Also unresolved: whether a structured/behavioral-feature judge (in the style of the collaborative-filtering positive, C-b2cebe551f3b) would outperform the raw-text LLM judge on the same archive — that comparison is out of scope for this arm (it fine-tunes a small LLM per the task spec) but is flagged as the more promising next design if H1 holds here, since the one clean positive precedent in this evidence set (C-f1d363ff01e4) and the one clean in-distribution positive (C-b2cebe551f3b) both used structured/symbolic rather than free-text-LLM judgment.
