0. SUMMARY FOR THE HURRIED REVIEWER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

HYPOTHESIS UNDER ATTACK (H): Useful new computational capabilities are more
readily accumulated through composition, encapsulation and reuse of previously
viable behaviors than through undirected local mutation alone.

VERDICT: H IS NOT SUPPORTED IN THIS ARENA, and the reason is specific and
measured rather than a null: composition pays a large, quantified interference
cost and buys almost no discovery in return.

  WHAT DIED
  * Direct composition (arm C) acquired the target capability 0 times out of 36
    (arm, world) runs. Compose-then-refine (arm E) acquired it once in 36.
  * At real composition joins, the child LOST a capability that a parent had in
    78.3 percent (C) and 82.6 percent (E) of 14,400 joins each.
  * Composition produced a capability that NEITHER parent had in 0 of 14,400
    joins (C) and 0 of 14,400 (E). Encapsulation (D) managed 402 of 14,400
    = 2.8 percent.
  * Capability RETENTION across a single modification: local mutation 1.000
    (95% CI 0.9997-1.0), structured mutation 0.991, versus composition arms
    0.577 / 0.608 / 0.680. Composition is roughly as destructive as random
    replacement (0.527).
  * Causal ablation of every composition crossing: 3,395 crossings, composition
    was causal for 20 of them (0.59 percent). In 3,379 of 3,395 (99.5 percent)
    the capability ALREADY EXISTED IN A PARENT. This is retrospective
    storytelling caught mechanically.
  * Nothing resisted interpretation. Of 15,768 promoted mechanism readings,
    13,257 were behaviorally equivalent to a single known operation and 2,511
    were novel compositions of known operations. ZERO resisted compression.

  WHAT SURVIVED, AND IT IS NOT WHAT I EXPECTED
  * The ONLY reuse mechanism with a causal signal is ENCAPSULATION (arm D), not
    composition: 19 of the 20 causally-verified compositions came from D, and 19
    of 20 were in W1 -- the world I DECLARED composition-favourable in advance,
    before any measurement. That is a positive control firing, not evidence.
  * The single significant between-arm result in the campaign was STRUCTURED
    MUTATION beating everything in W2 (10/12 vs A_LOCAL 3/12, Fisher p=0.0123;
    vs random control p=0.0006). Under radius matching it collapses to 1/12.
    So the one strong effect I found is EXPLAINED BY EDIT RADIUS -- the same
    confound the pre-registration named for composition, firing on the winner.

  DEFECTS I FOUND IN MY OWN CAMPAIGN AND REPORT HERE RATHER THAN SMOOTH OVER
  * Arm C is OPERATOR-DEGENERATE: 20,000 evaluations produced only 24 distinct
    behaviors (B_STRUCT: 1,177; E_COMP_REF: 1,297). Arm C's zero is therefore
    NOT evidence about composition. Arm E, with matched exploration diversity,
    is the fair test and it still loses 1/36 to 14/36.
  * My E2 goal metric for W3 was WRONG: it took the union of capabilities over a
    whole run instead of requiring ONE candidate to hold all three at once. The
    corrected W3 result is 0/12 for EVERY arm; no candidate in 13,982 adjudicated
    W3 claims ever held all three simultaneously.
  * My E4 accumulation field is INVALID (its first-N-by-id rule sampled only
    bootstrap-phase genomes; identical 51/0 for all six arms gives it away). It
    is recomputed from Court rows in section 9 and the original is discarded.
  * My first E3 run was invalid twice over -- once because the radius statistic
    pooled the shared bootstrap (making local mutation read 6.46 when its true
    radius is 0.89), once because the radius cap was applied during the shared
    bootstrap and destroyed the archive. Both fixed; both disclosed.

  THE LIMIT THAT MATTERS MOST (section 10): in this arena the primitives are
  2-to-5 instruction motifs. They are CHEAPER TO REDISCOVER THAN TO SPLICE. That
  is a regime in which reuse has nothing to buy, and it may be the whole story.
  This campaign cannot distinguish "composition does not help" from "composition
  does not help WHEN COMPONENTS ARE CHEAP." I regard that as the single most
  likely way this result is misleading.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The pre-registration named eight competing explanations before any measurement,
each with the mechanism intended to exclude it. Outcome of each:

    -> DID NOT ARISE (composition never won). Tested anyway, and it fired on the
       WINNER instead: structured mutation's advantage is radius-explained.
 X2 composition wins via more compute
    -> DID NOT ARISE. Compute is reported both ways (section 6). Encapsulation

    -> CONTROLLED by declaring W1 composition-favourable IN ADVANCE. 19 of the 20
       causal compositions are in W1. Reported as a control reading, not evidence.
 X4 brute-force expressivity (bigger programs)
    -> Length cap identical (48) for all arms; expressed length reported. Arm E
       ran the SHORTEST programs (7.99) and arm B the longest (24.78).
 X5 novelty is unseen syntax, not competence
    -> EXCLUDED BY CONSTRUCTION: capability is exact match against a reference

 X6 the capability already existed in an ancestor
    -> THE DOMINANT EXPLANATION. 3,379 of 3,395 composition crossings (99.5%),

 X7 duplicate programs counted as distinct discoveries
    -> Canonicalised by behavioral signature. 1,064,029 duplicate claims were

 X8 human interpretation contaminated promotion
    -> The Court is deterministic code. No LLM is in the promotion path anywhere

2. WORLD AND TASK CONSTRUCTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SUBSTRATE ("SlotVM"). 8 registers over Z_256. R0-R1 inputs, R2-R4 scratch,
R5-R7 outputs (3 capability slots). Straight-line programs only: no loops, no
branches. Every program is therefore a TOTAL deterministic function, so
capability is decidable by exact equality -- no halting problem, no timeouts, no
"novelty" that is really a crash. Registers are shared and inputs are not
write-protected, which is what makes interference REAL rather than assumed:
concatenating two viable programs genuinely clobbers state.

12 opcodes, each instruction a triple (op, a, b):
  0 ADD  a b  R[a]=R[a]+R[b]        6 NOT  a .  R[a]=~R[a]
  1 SUB  a b  R[a]=R[a]-R[b]        7 SHL  a k  R[a]=R[a]<<(k&7)
  2 MUL  a b  R[a]=R[a]*R[b]        8 SHR  a k  R[a]=R[a]>>(k&7)
  3 XOR  a b  R[a]=R[a]^R[b]        9 MOV  a b  R[a]=R[b]
  4 AND  a b  R[a]=R[a]&R[b]       10 SETC a k  R[a]=k*17
  5 OR   a b  R[a]=R[a]|R[b]       11 SWAP a b  swap R[a],R[b]
All arithmetic mod 256. Expressed length cap 48, identical for every arm.

THE THREE WORLDS SHARE THE SAME PRIMITIVES and differ ONLY in the rule combining
them, so any between-world difference is a property of the combination rule and
not of primitive difficulty:
  A = bit7(x0)    B = bit7(x1)    C = bit3(x0)

  W1_PIPELINE     slots [A, B, T=A+B].   Goal: T.
                  DECLARED COMPOSITION-FAVOURABLE IN ADVANCE. T is correlated
                  with each primitive, so partial solutions earn partial credit:
                  there is both a gradient AND an obvious composition route. A
                  win here is a POSITIVE CONTROL READING, not evidence for H.
  W2_DECEPTIVE    slots [A, B, T=A XOR B]. Goal: T.
                  T is statistically INDEPENDENT of A and of B, so holding either
                  primitive scores exactly chance on the target. There is no
                  gradient into the optimum. The routes are a lucky structural
                  jump or crossing the valley by composing two viable behaviors.
                  THIS IS THE LOAD-BEARING WORLD: it is where H makes a real,
                  non-trivial, falsifiable claim.
  W3_INTERFERENCE slots [A, B, C]. Goal: hold ALL THREE SIMULTANEOUSLY.
                  Shared scratch and adjacent output registers. Composition is
                  PREDICTED TO LOSE here and is allowed to.

PRE-REGISTERED DIRECTIONAL PREDICTIONS (written to allow embarrassment):
  W1: C,D,E > A.   W2: no arm separates from B.   W3: C < E, and C may lose to A.
OUTCOME: W1 partially fired (D>A, but C and E did not). W2 fired in the sense
that no composition arm separated -- but B separated from everything, which I
did not predict. W3 fired: C lost, and everything lost.

3. CONTAMINATION CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 (a) HELD-OUT PROBES ARE NEVER VISIBLE TO ANY ARM. TRAIN (16 probes) drives
     fitness; HELDOUT (64) is drawn from a disjoint RNG stream and used only by
     the measuring instrument. Verified disjoint: overlap 0.
 (b) THE TARGET IS HELD OUT OF THE BOOTSTRAP. Bootstrap fitness scores PRIMITIVE
     slots only. Measured goal contamination at bootstrap: 0 of 12 seeds, in
     every arm and every world.
 (c) NO LABEL TELLS THE SEARCH WHICH PRIMITIVES BELONG TOGETHER. The archive is
     keyed by capability SET, which is behavioral, and the operators never see a
     world name, a slot name, or a target.
 (d) LEAKAGE CONTROLS (kill condition K2). Constant-output programs (all 16
     constants x 3 worlds), 4,000 input-ignoring programs per world (scratch
     registers only), and the empty program: NONE held any capability.
     K2 PASS, zero violations.
 (e) ARENA-TOO-EASY CONTROL (kill condition K1). Random search acquired the W1
     goal in 0 of 12 seeds (threshold was >=20%). K1 PASS.
 (f) CHANCE FLOORS MEASURED, NOT ASSUMED. Majority-class rate per slot 0.4375 to
     0.625. Probability a random program holds a given capability, over 20,000
     random programs: 0.0004 (slot 0), 0.0006 (slot 1), 0.0000 (slot 2).
 (g) BOOTSTRAP PARITY. All six arms execute an identical operator on an RNG
     stream keyed to the seed alone for the first 20,000 evaluations. Verified
     byte-identical archives across all six arms in all three worlds.
 (h) DETERMINISM. Two independent executions of the same (arm, world, seed)
     produced identical summaries. Court replay failures: 0 of 14,690.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. EXACT DEFINITION OF CAPABILITY (FROZEN BEFORE SEARCH)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Candidate P HOLDS capability k in world W if and only if:

    for EVERY probe x in HELDOUT(W):  run(P,x)[5+k] == f_k(x)     (all 64, exact)

  * A previously-unseen output hash is NOT a capability. Novel output is not
    useful capability. This is enforced structurally: the criterion is agreement
    with an externally specified reference function, never surprise.
  * PERTURBATION: a second 64-probe stream from a DIFFERENT input distribution
    (low-entropy, byte-aligned, adversarial constants). Reported separately.
  * EXHAUSTIVE: the Court additionally checks all 65,536 input pairs. This is a
    Class I machine check, not a sample.
  * TRANSFER: the candidate is run with its two input registers swapped while the
    reference is evaluated on the original inputs. Recorded; NOT a promotion
    blocker, because these references are deliberately position-specific.
  * CAPABILITY SET K(P) is the set of slots held. Accumulation is measured on K.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. GENERATIVE ARMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

One engine, one code path. The arms differ ONLY in the variation operator;
initial population, selection, archive-write rule, budget and RNG discipline are
identical by construction.

  A_LOCAL     point mutation: one instruction's opcode OR one operand
  B_STRUCT    segment mutation: insert / delete / duplicate / move a block
  C_COMPOSE   graft of two DISTINCT viable archive members, no follow-up mutation
  D_ENCAPS    archive members become callable macros; mutation over the
              macro-extended alphabet (one CALL token expands to a whole body)
  E_COMP_REF  arm C followed by one arm-A mutation
  F_RANDOM    uniform random program of matched expressed length (control)

FAIRNESS RULES: all arms seeded from the same initial population and the same
post-bootstrap archive; all arms WRITE to the archive under the same rule, only
C/D/E READ it; budget matched on evaluations; fallback rate recorded (measured
0.000 everywhere -- no composition arm was ever running as a disguised mutation
arm).

SELECTION: replace-worst-if-not-worse, with behavioral-duplicate rejection.

BUDGET: 40,000 evaluations per run = 20,000 shared bootstrap + 20,000 arm phase.
12 seeds per (arm, world), fixed in advance. No optional stopping. 216 runs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. ANCESTRY REPRESENTATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Provenance is WRITTEN AS IT HAPPENS, never inferred afterwards. Every candidate
gets a row: (id, parent_a, parent_b, operator, evaluation index, exact-slot
count, graded fitness, viability flag, behavioral signature). Genomes are
persisted for every node on a recorded lineage, which is what makes exact replay
possible; replay was verified and failed 0 times in 14,690 Court adjudications.
Reused components are recorded by operator and by parent id, so "which archive
members went into this child" is a stored fact rather than a reconstruction.

DISCLOSED LIMIT: non-viable dead-end candidates are counted but their genomes are
not persisted (4.3M candidates across the campaign would not fit). Every node on
every reported lineage is stored.

9. CAPABILITY ACQUISITION AND RETENTION, SEPARATED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

DISCOVERY (can an arm produce a candidate exhibiting a new capability?)
  Composition: NO, essentially never. C 0/36 runs, E 1/36. Encapsulation 6/36,
  concentrated 5/6 in the world declared favourable in advance.
  Structured mutation 14/36 -- and radius-explained.

RETENTION (can descendants preserve previous capabilities while acquiring more?)
  Mutation: YES, near-perfectly (0.991-1.000).
  Composition: NO (0.577-0.680), statistically indistinguishable in kind from
  random replacement's 0.527 and separated from mutation by more than 30
  percentage points with non-overlapping exact intervals at n=14,400.

  THIS IS THE CLEANEST RESULT IN THE CAMPAIGN and it runs directly against the
  part of H that the assignment flagged as mattering more. A process that
  composes viable behaviors in this substrate does not accumulate; it churns.

10. NEGATIVE RESULTS, DEFECTS, AND WHAT WOULD MAKE THIS WRONG
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

DEFECTS IN MY OWN CAMPAIGN, ALL SELF-CAUGHT, ALL DISCLOSED:
 D1 Arm C is operator-degenerate: 24 distinct behaviors from 20,000 evaluations.
    Its reachable set is |archive|^2 x cut-points, and the archive holds 3
    members. C's 0/36 IS NOT EVIDENCE ABOUT COMPOSITION. Arm E, with 1,297
    distinct behaviors against B_STRUCT's 1,177, is the fair test.
 D2 E2's W3 goal metric was wrong (union over a run instead of one candidate
    holding all three). Corrected to 0/12 for every arm.
 D3 E4's accumulation field is invalid (sampled bootstrap genomes only).
    Recomputed from Court rows.
 D4 E3 was invalid twice: radius pooled across the shared bootstrap, then the
    radius cap applied during the bootstrap and destroyed the archive. Both
    fixed; the corrected control is the one reported.
 D5 The exact-CI implementation overflowed at n in the thousands and was replaced
    with a continued-fraction incomplete beta.

THE LIMITATION THAT MOST THREATENS THE CONCLUSION:
  The primitives here are 2-to-5 instruction motifs. Random sampling finds them
  12 times out of 12. In such a regime a component is CHEAPER TO REDISCOVER THAN
  TO SPLICE, and reuse has nothing to buy -- it can only pay interference costs.
  H is a claim about accumulating DEEP capability, and this arena has no depth.
  I could not distinguish "composition does not help" from "composition does not
  help when components are cheap," and I regard the second as the more likely
  reading. THE SINGLE HIGHEST-VALUE FOLLOW-UP IS TO RAISE COMPONENT COST until
  rediscovery is expensive, and re-run this identical harness unchanged.

OTHER THREATS, STATED PLAINLY:
  * 12 seeds. Several cells are 0/12 with an upper bound of 0.265 -- consistent
    with a real rate as high as one in four.
  * The archive keys on capability set, admitting at most 2^3 = 8 niches, so the
    composition arms had at most 3 things to compose. A richer niche space might
    change the interference/production balance.
  * Arm D's compute advantage (2.3x arm E) is unresolved and it is the arm with
    the positive result.
  * Straight-line programs only. No control flow means no subroutine can be
    conditionally invoked, which is a real restriction on what encapsulation can
    mean.
  * All three worlds share two primitives. That was deliberate (it isolates the
    combination rule) but it means the campaign tested three combination rules,
    not three independent domains.

13. STRONGEST CONCLUSION WARRANTED / NOT WARRANTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WARRANTED:
  In a substrate where viable components are cheap to rediscover, composition and
  reuse of previously viable behaviors do NOT improve capability acquisition over
  mutation, and they measurably DEGRADE capability retention. Of every apparent
  compositional acquisition, 99.5 percent is the capability arriving intact from
  one parent -- exactly the retrospective-storytelling failure the campaign was
  built to detect, and it is detectable ONLY by causal ablation. Where reuse does
  help, the mechanism that helps is ENCAPSULATION THROUGH A CALL SITE, not
  splicing, and the difference between them is interference at the interface.

NOT WARRANTED:
  * "Composition is not useful for capability acquisition." The arena has no
    depth; components cost 2-5 instructions. This tests one regime.
  * "Structured mutation is the better mechanism." Its only significant win is
    radius-explained.
  * Any claim from arm C. It is operator-degenerate and its zeros are artifacts.
  * "Encapsulation improves accumulation." It leads on that measure (0.237 versus
    0.097) but it also used 2.3x the compute of arm E, and I did not separate
    those. PROVISIONAL, one falsification family only.
  * Any generalisation from W1. I declared it composition-favourable before
    measuring precisely so that a win there could not be counted as evidence, and
    19 of the 20 causal compositions are in it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
14. IMPLICATIONS FOR THE NEXT PROMETHEUS / FOUNDRY ITERATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 1. RAISE COMPONENT COST. The decisive experiment is this identical harness with
    primitives expensive enough that rediscovery is not free. Until then no
    composition result from this program means much in either direction.
 2. MEASURE RETENTION, NOT JUST DISCOVERY. Retention separated the arms by 30-40
    points at n=14,400 while discovery separated them by a few counts out of 12.
    It is the higher-powered instrument and this program has not been using it.
 3. ABLATE EVERY COMPOSITION CLAIM. 99.5 percent of them dissolved. Any pipeline
    that reports "composition produced X" without the remove-a-parent test is
    reporting inheritance.
 4. PREFER CALL-SITE REUSE TO SPLICING. This is the one actionable design
    difference the campaign found, and it is a mechanism, not a preference.
 5. RADIUS-MATCH BEFORE BELIEVING ANY OPERATOR COMPARISON. The single significant
    effect here evaporated under matching, and it would have been published
    without the control.
