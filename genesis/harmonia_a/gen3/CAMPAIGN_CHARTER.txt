0. BINDING RULES (frozen before any experiment)

  R1  AT MOST eight experiments, 3A..3H, run IN ORDER.
  R2  An experiment may be SKIPPED only when its prerequisite
      (section 2) has been falsified upstream; the skip is recorded
      with the falsifying verdict cited.
  R3  NO-RESCUE: a later experiment may not be interpreted as
      rescuing a hypothesis killed earlier. If a premise dies,
      subsequent experiments either stop or explicitly open a NEW
      hypothesis class under a fresh freeze.
  R4  NO GEN-3I. No ninth experiment may be invented, regardless of
      how any verdict reads.
  R5  Each experiment gets its own freeze (question, endpoints,
      gates, verdict strings, analysis code hash) issued before its
      evidence exists; one experiment at a time.
  R6  All outcomes are measured under the Gen-2b ruler R_VEC2
      (classes NEUTRAL/SMALL/LARGE/DESTRUCTION[/FAULT]; components
      d, surv_sym, minmass') or a substrate-specific instance
      re-derived from the Gen-2 AXIOMS (never by carrying Boolean
      thresholds across substrates -- relevant from 3G on).
  R7  StackVM artifacts remain untouched until 3H, and 3H runs
      only if a structural hypothesis survives to it.

1. THE EIGHT EXPERIMENTS (questions as authorized)

  3A  Re-adjudicate Gen-1 under R_VEC2. Did the Gen-1 negative
      survive fixing the ruler?
      Verdicts: NO_TRANSFER_STRUCTURE / TRANSFER_STRUCTURE_
      RECOVERED / INDETERMINATE.
  3B  Held-out-parent transport. Does any predictive structure
      transfer to unseen objects? Random mutation-level splits
      FORBIDDEN; entire parents withheld.
  3C  Geometry kill-pair search. Do surviving coordinates survive
      constructed exchange pairs (near-equal coordinate distance,
      sharply different consequence; and converse)?
  3D  Mathematical-type tournament. What KIND of object describes
      mutation -> consequence: scalar coordinate, stratified/
      piecewise, graph neighborhood, partial order, operator
      family, parent-conditioned charts, or null?
  3E  Parent-condition dependence. Global geometry vs atlas of
      parent-local charts vs object-specific response surfaces;
      do local charts admit mappings between one another?
  3F  Representation invariance. Behaviorally equivalent objects,
      different encodings, analogous mutations: does discovered
      structure track behavior or representation?
      (Failure label: REPRESENTATION_SPECIFIC.)
  3G  Validity boundary. Smallest validity-constrained substrate;
      FAULT goes live; do faults/destruction/large transitions
      organize around distance-to-validity-boundary?
  3H  Cross-substrate transport of surviving hypotheses, ruler
      re-derived from axioms. Boolean artifact, representation-
      class phenomenon, or portable?
