## CAMPAIGN VERDICT (charter §3 mapping — strongest claim surviving every experiment that tested it)

**TRANSFERABLE_GEOMETRY** — with the qualification the funnel itself proved:
the transferable object is NOT a metric or scalar coordinate (3C killed
that) but a **richer-than-scalar, operator-family-conditioned, global,
behavior-tracking predictive structure** over (site influence, edit kind,
site position, object covariates), valid within the Boolean behavior-space
substrate family, across unseen parents (3B) and across encodings (3F-b).

SMALL/LARGE distinction) does not exist on the arithmetic substrate.

and it aligns with the per-site influence spectrum:
graded spectrum (circuits, 7.6% middle mass) → local band exists;
gapped spectrum (Z₉₇, 0.0% middle mass) → local band empty.
