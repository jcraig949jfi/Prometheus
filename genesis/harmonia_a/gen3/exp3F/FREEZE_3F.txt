Date: 2026-08-31. Campaign rules R1-R7. Prerequisite satisfied:
surviving structural claim from 3D/3E (global operator-family-

QUESTION. Does the discovered structure track BEHAVIOR or
REPRESENTATION? Construct behaviorally equivalent executables with
deliberately different internal encodings, apply the analogous
mutation class, and test the FROZEN model (fitted on original
encodings, train seeds only) on the re-encoded held-out parents.

ENCODINGS (deterministic; behavior equality asserted at build,
abort on violation):
  E-ORIG   the Gen-1 24-gate circuits (baseline).
  E-RELAB  input-relabeled circuits (positive control: the edit
           space is isomorphic; expected invariant).
  E-NAND   gate-by-gate NAND expansion (AND -> 2 NANDs, OR -> 3,
           XOR -> 4, NAND -> 1), 24 gates -> up to 96; same
           4-op alphabet, same single-gate edit class; different
           granularity, positions, influence profile.
EDITS: K = 128 per re-encoded object, uniform over its own
enumerated op/rewire space (streams (seed, 105|106, level, obj),
stat seed 20260907). Features as 3D/3E: inf_target measured IN THE
NEW ENCODING (influence is behavioral per site, but the SITES are
representation objects -- that is exactly what makes this a real
test), kind, gpos = gate_index / G_encoding, parent behavioral
covariates (unchanged by construction).

MODEL UNDER TEST (frozen): the 3E GLOBAL model, per-kind
rank-linear, fitted per LOSO fold on E-ORIG train-seed edits only;
evaluated on the held-out seed's E-NAND (and E-RELAB) edits.
Non-neutral edits only (neutral exclusion declared forced, as
before).

GATES (frozen):
  G_CONTROL   E-RELAB AUC within 0.02 of the E-ORIG held-out AUC
              per fold (else HARNESS_SUSPECT -- the isomorphic
              encoding must be invariant).
  VERDICT on E-NAND AUC per fold:
    BEHAVIOR_TRACKING          >= 0.65 in 5/5 folds
    REPRESENTATION_SPECIFIC    <= 0.55 in any fold
    PARTIAL_INVARIANCE         otherwise
  (A structure labeled REPRESENTATION_SPECIFIC is not "reasoning
  geometry" -- charter/brief wording -- and the campaign verdict
  ceiling drops accordingly.)

CLAIM CEILING: invariance tested across THESE encodings (relabel,
NAND-granularity). Passing does not establish invariance across
substrate families -- that is 3H. Failing here binds the claim to
the encoding class immediately.
