4. SITE DEFINITION (inherited)

  A site is one position in the native serialized genotype
  sequence returned by /v0/artifacts/{id}/genotype -- the unit the
  native point mutation (stackvm.point_indel.v1, p_point=1.0,
  max_point_sites=1, inserts/deletes/dup off) perturbs. Site
  identity of a mutation is recovered by exact positional diff of
  parent vs child genotypes (equal lengths required; guaranteed by
  the operator config, verified per pair).

6. CANONICAL INTERVENTION AND SELECTION RULE (frozen)

  The canonical single-site intervention at site s of parent P is
  the native single-point mutation that D-13's I-L_strong family
  itself produces at s (exactly one site perturbed; execution
  proceeds normally; consequence measured by the inherited
  comparator over the inherited battery -- all server-side).
  Because the native family draws the replacement value from its
  own RNG, several samples may hit the same site. FROZEN
  DETERMINISTIC SELECTION RULE: influence(P, s) = the displacement
  of the FIRST pair, in server-returned pair order within the
  lowest-seed call, whose diff is exactly site s of parent P and
  which is ELIGIBLE under section 7. All other samples of (P, s)
  are preserved raw and may appear only in post-hoc descriptive
  analysis. No averaging enters the primary statistic.

7. ELIGIBILITY, EXCLUSIONS, DENOMINATOR (frozen)

  A pair is ELIGIBLE iff: genotype lengths equal AND positional
  diff count == 1 AND parent clean AND child clean (both_clean --
  the D-13 kpos025 channel's own convention). Exclusions, each
  counted and reported: diff==0 (value-identical redraw; also
  serves as the no-op control class), diff>1 (locality violation
  -- must be ~0 given max_point_sites=1; any nonzero rate is
  investigated before the verdict), child faulted (FAULT-class
  intervention: influence undefined under the inherited clean-pair
  convention; rate reported).
  DENOMINATOR (explicit): all measured sites = distinct (parent_id,
  site) pairs with at least one ELIGIBLE sample. Sites never hit
  by the native sampler are unmeasured and outside the
  denominator; per-parent coverage (measured sites / genotype
  length) is reported. Site sampling follows the NATIVE operator's
  site distribution; its observed position histogram is reported.
