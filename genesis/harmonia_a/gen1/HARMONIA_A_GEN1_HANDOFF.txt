3. THE FINDING OF RECORD: THE RULER HAS A SEMANTIC DEFECT

  Exploratory, labeled, and the most transportable output: the
  Hamming LOCAL band conflates "small change" with "proportional
  destruction of an unbalanced object". For 145/298 objects,
  min(bal, 1-bal) <= 0.25 -- TOTAL DESTRUCTION of the function
  (collapse to a constant) lands inside the LOCAL band. q90 of
  nonzero displacement tracks min-mass at 0.596. The balance_dev ->
  local_share association is therefore substantially ruler-mediated
  (residual rho 0.467 survives in the stratum where destruction
  cannot be local, so not a pure tautology). ANY consequence ruler
  built on raw normalized Hamming inherits this defect, including
  D-13's K_positive@0.25 channel: a system whose objects are
  behaviorally unbalanced can score "local non-neutral movement" by
  dying proportionally. A successor ruler should separate the two,
  e.g. report d alongside distance-to-nearest-constant, or gate
  "local" on preservation of non-trivial behavior.
