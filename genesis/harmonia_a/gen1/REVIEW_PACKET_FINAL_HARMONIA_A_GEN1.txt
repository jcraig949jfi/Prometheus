0. SUMMARY FOR THE HURRIED REVIEWER
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Gen-1 asked: within one frozen substrate, does a controlled
mechanical property of an executable object -- primary candidate:
causal liveness / influence density -- predict the SHAPE of
consequence-space movement produced by a fixed native edit family?

    FINAL VERDICT: NO_LOW_DIMENSIONAL_TRANSFER_COORDINATE

The answer has three layers, and the layering is the product:

LAYER 0 (FORCED, declared as a theorem in the freeze BEFORE any
run): for every single-gate edit at gate g, displacement
d <= influence(g). Consequence: P(neutral) carries an arithmetic
floor. Measured: corr(P(neutral), forced_floor) = 0.928. The
liveness ladder's spectacular association with neutral mass
(eta2 = 0.714) is this theorem restated. A design that had made
P(neutral) the primary endpoint -- the naive reading of the Gen-0
exploratory gradient that motivated this generation -- would today
be announcing a strong "transfer coordinate" that is arithmetic.
The freeze was built to prevent exactly that promotion, and did.

LAYER 1 (EMPIRICAL): liveness predicts ACTIVITY, not SHAPE. On the
non-forced primary endpoint LOCAL_SHARE = P(d <= 0.25 | d > 0),
the ladder is nearly flat: eta2 = 0.044, permutation p = 0.015
(gate 0.01), per-seed eta2 4/5 below the 0.15 reliability bar with
one seed carrying triple the others -- the exact one-stratum
pathology the frozen gate exists to reject.

LAYER 2 (WHERE SHAPE SIGNAL ACTUALLY LIVES): the dominant
object-level predictor of consequence shape is TRUTH-TABLE BALANCE
DEVIATION (partial rho +0.52 controlling both proposed axes), and
a labeled exploratory follow-up shows this is substantially a
defect OF THE CONSEQUENCE RULER: for 145 of 298 objects, total
destruction of the function (collapse to a constant) lands INSIDE
the "local" band. The Hamming local band conflates "small change"
with "proportional destruction of an unbalanced object". This
defect transports: D-13's K_positive@0.25 channel inherits it.

Also measured and reported plainly: structural liveness and
behavioral influence are near-collinear in this population
(corr = 0.935) -- the two axes the brief required kept distinct
barely exist as separate coordinates here; and the uniform
generator CANNOT reach structural liveness above 0.5, so the
saturated regime is not identifiable by rejection sampling. The
stop condition is answered NO: no D-14 is recommended.

3. DESIGN AS EXECUTED
---------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTROLLED (identical to Gen-0, bit-for-bit): substrate (Boolean
f: {0,1}^10 as 24-gate straight-line circuits over
{AND,OR,XOR,NAND}); the uniform GENERATOR (the ladder is built by
rejection only -- no wiring knob exists that could condition the
population); the EDIT FAMILY (the 1056-member single-gate op/rewire
space, size and per-gate weights identical for every object by
construction, so the edit process cannot adapt to liveness); the
RULER (exact full-domain Hamming displacement, d = Hamming/1024).
STRATIFIED (the axis): structural liveness s_live = live gates/24.
MATCHED (band only): truth-table balance in [0.05, 0.95].
MEASURED ONLY (analysis controls, never acceptance criteria):
b_live, inf_density, full 24-gate influence profile, balance, live
input variables, ANF support, cone depth, and the two forced
quantities. The freeze declared this CONTROLLED/MATCHED/STRATIFIED/
MEASURED split before outcome generation.

THE TWO COORDINATES, KEPT DISTINCT:
  s_live: fraction of gates on a dependency path to the output
    (graph-only, no evaluation).
  inf(g): exact fraction of the 1024 inputs on which negating gate
    g's wire (intervention, propagated downstream) flips the
    output; b_live = fraction of gates with inf > 0; inf_density =
    mean influence.

LADDER RULE (frozen; bins are an outcome of the rule, not a
choice): pooled graph-only census of 300,000 uniform circuits
(60,000 x 5 master seeds 11,22,33,44,55); span = [P1,P99] of
s_live; 5 equal-width bins; every bin requires natural mass
>= 0.5%; one span-shrink retry; failure =
AXIS_NOT_IDENTIFIABLE_UNDER_CURRENT_GENERATOR and no assay.
OBJECTS: 12 per (seed, level) = 300, by rejection from the
unchanged generator, accept iff s_live in bin AND balance in band.
ASSAY: K = 128 edits/object (NAT); SHAM arm (input-relabeled
circuit, same edit class) at the two extreme levels as the
machine-checked harness positive control. Unit of independence:
the OBJECT (n = 300); mutations are repeated observations. Bands
NEUTRAL d=0 / LOCAL 0<d<=0.25 / FAR d>0.25 for D-13 comparability,
AND complete per-object distributions shipped (dyadic + 64-bin
linear histograms + quantiles) because a threshold can hide the
phenomenon -- which section 6 shows it partially did.

6. THE FINDING OF RECORD: THE CONSEQUENCE RULER HAS A SEMANTIC
   DEFECT (exploratory, labeled, and the generation's most
   transportable output)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The dominant shape "coordinate" (balance_dev, partial +0.52) is
substantially a property of the RULER, not the object:

  - For 145 of 298 objects, min(balance, 1-balance) <= 0.25, so
    TOTAL DESTRUCTION of the function -- collapse to a constant --
    produces displacement inside the LOCAL band.
  - The 90th percentile of nonzero displacement tracks min-mass at
    corr = 0.596: displacement CEILINGS scale with balance.
  - The association survives at rho = 0.467 (n = 153) in the
    stratum where destruction cannot land local (min-mass > 0.25),
    so it is not a pure tautology -- but the clean interpretation
    of "local = small meaningful change" is unavailable for
    roughly half the population.

TRANSPORT: any consequence channel built on raw normalized Hamming
displacement inherits this, including D-13's K_positive@0.25. A
system whose objects are behaviorally unbalanced can score "local
non-neutral movement" by dying proportionally. PRESCRIPTION for
any successor ruler: report displacement alongside
distance-to-nearest-constant, or gate "local" on preservation of
non-trivial behavior. This ruler repair precedes any further
coordinate hunt.

8. WHAT THIS GENERATION ACTUALLY LEARNED
---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. LIVENESS PREDICTS ACTIVITY, NOT SHAPE. How often an edit does
   anything is (provably) governed by the influence profile; the
   local/far character of what it does is not organized by the
   liveness ladder (eta2 0.044). The Gen-0 exploratory gradient
   that motivated this generation was the forced layer in disguise.
2. DECLARING THE FORCED LAYER BEFORE THE RUN IS WHAT MAKES A
   MEASUREMENT PROGRAM TRUSTWORTHY. eta2 = 0.714 with p = 0.001,
   replicated 5/5 seeds, is exactly the kind of number that
   headlines a result -- and it is arithmetic. It was caught
   because the theorem and its per-object quantities were frozen
   as confounds before any outcome existed.
3. THE RULER LESSON (section 6): raw Hamming displacement bands
   conflate small change with proportional destruction for
   unbalanced objects. This is the single most transportable
   output, applies upstream to D-13's channel definitions, and
   must be repaired before any Gen-2 coordinate hunt.
4. THE AXES COLLAPSE: s_live and b_live correlate 0.935 under this
   generator; a population where they separate would need to be
   constructed deliberately -- and the construction would change
   the population (the section-13 trade, now measured).
5. A STACKVM REFORMULATION, RECORDED WITHOUT RECOMMENDATION AND
   WITHOUT TOUCHING STACKVM: the forced layer implies the D-13
   signature (neutral 0.915, kpos025 = 0, 505/505) is equivalent
   to a GAPPED per-site influence profile -- every byte position
   has influence either 0 or > 0.25 under its operator family,
   with no graded middle -- whereas these circuits show a graded
   profile. That is a falsifiable, mechanistic, out-of-sample
   claim available to whoever designs D-14. This generation does
   not recommend D-14 (section 9).
