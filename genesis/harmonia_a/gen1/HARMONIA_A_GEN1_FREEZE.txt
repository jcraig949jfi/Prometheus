0. QUESTION UNDER TEST

  Within one frozen substrate family, does a controlled mechanical
  property of an executable object predict the SHAPE of consequence-
  space movement produced by a fixed native edit family?
  Primary candidate coordinate: causal liveness / influence density.
  This is a HYPOTHESIS inherited from a Gen-0 exploratory
  stratification, not a finding. Any of H0-H4 (brief section 8) is
  an acceptable answer. StackVM is HELD OUT: no StackVM artifact is
  read, measured, or tuned against during this generation.

1. WHAT IS CONTROLLED, MATCHED, STRATIFIED, MEASURED (declared now)

  CONTROLLED (identical to Gen-0, bit-for-bit semantics):
    substrate (f: {0,1}^10 via 24-gate straight-line circuits over
    {AND,OR,XOR,NAND}); the GENERATOR (uniform Gen-0 generator --
    the ladder is built by rejection only, never by a new wiring
    rule); the EDIT FAMILY (the 1056-member single-gate op/rewire
    space, whose size and per-gate weights are the same for every
    24-gate circuit by construction -- the edit process cannot adapt
    to liveness); the CONSEQUENCE RULER (exact full-domain Hamming
    displacement d = Hamming(f,f')/1024).
  STRATIFIED (the axis): structural liveness s_live = live gates/24,
    5 ladder levels chosen by the frozen census rule of section 3.
  MATCHED (band only): truth-table balance in [0.05, 0.95] (Gen-0
    census band, applied identically at every level).
  MEASURED ONLY (recorded per object, used as analysis controls,
    never as acceptance criteria): b_live, inf_density, full 24-gate
    influence profile, balance, live input variables, ANF support,
    output-cone depth, forced_neutral_floor, forced_local_bound.
  Rationale against over-matching (brief section 4): matching on
  behavioral covariates could condition on the endpoint's causes;
  they are measured and adjusted for in E4 instead, with residual
  per-level imbalance disclosed in the census report.

2. THE TWO COORDINATES, KEPT DISTINCT (brief section 2)

  A. STRUCTURAL LIVENESS s_live: fraction of gates on a dependency
     path to the output. Graph-only; no evaluation.
  B. BEHAVIORAL INFLUENCE: per gate g, inf(g) = exact fraction of
     the 1024 inputs on which negating g's wire (intervention,
     propagated downstream) flips the output. Derived scalars:
     b_live = fraction of gates with inf > 0; inf_density = mean
     inf over all 24 gates.
  Their empirical relationship is itself a reported quantity.

3. THE FORCED LAYER, DECLARED BEFORE ANY OUTCOME (attack J)

  THEOREM-LEVEL BOUND: for every single-gate edit at gate g,
  d <= inf(g). Proof sketch: the edit changes g's output on some
  input set A; the final output changes only on {x in A : the
  negation intervention at g flips f(x)}, a subset of g's influence
  set. Consequences, all declared FORCED, none claimable as
  discovery:
    - an edit at an influence-0 gate is neutral with probability 1;
    - P(neutral) has the forced floor forced_neutral_floor =
      edit-weighted share of influence-0 gates (weights
      w_g = (3 + 2*(9+g))/1056, fixed for all objects);
    - edits at gates with 0 < inf <= 0.25 can only be neutral or
      local, never far (forced_local_bound = their weight share).
  THEREFORE the primary endpoint is CONDITIONAL:
      LOCAL_SHARE = P(d <= 0.25 | d > 0)
  -- the shape of REALIZED non-neutral movement, which is where the
  D-13 stackvm signature lived (zero local GIVEN non-neutral), and
  which the bound does not fix (mass may fall anywhere below the
  per-gate ceilings). forced_neutral_floor and forced_local_bound
  enter the E4 confound set: if the "coordinate" is the influence
  profile restated, the partials must expose that and the verdict
  must carry the label.

4. LADDER CONSTRUCTION (frozen RULE; bins are an outcome of the
   rule, not a choice)

  Graph-only census: 60,000 uniform circuits per master seed
  (11,22,33,44,55; stream key (seed,100)), pooled n = 300,000.
  Rule: span = [P1, P99] of pooled s_live; 5 equal-width bins over
  the span; every bin requires natural mass >= 0.5%. If any bin
  fails: one retry with span [P2.5, P97.5]. If still failing:
      AXIS_NOT_IDENTIFIABLE_UNDER_CURRENT_GENERATOR
  and the primary assay is NOT run.
  Objects: 12 per (seed, level) cell = 300 total, by rejection from
  the unchanged generator (stream (seed,101,level)); accept iff
  s_live in bin AND balance in band; cap 400,000 draws per cell,
  cap breach = the same axis-not-identifiable verdict. The census
  report ships: natural distribution, bin edges/masses, per-cell
  acceptance rates, per-level covariate means/sds (residual
  imbalance, disclosed before outcome rows exist).

5. ASSAY

  NAT arm: K = 128 edits per object, uniform with replacement over
  the object's 1056-edit space (stream (seed,102,level,obj)); per
  row: displacement d, target gate, edit kind.
  SHAM arm (harness positive control, machine-checked): at the two
  extreme levels only, input-relabeled circuits + same edit class
  (streams (seed,103/104,level,obj)); analytic expectation:
  distribution identical to NAT. Same-object assertions abort on
  violation.
  Bands for comparability: NEUTRAL d=0; LOCAL 0<d<=0.25; FAR
  d>0.25 -- AND the complete distribution ships per object (dyadic
  histogram + 64-bin linear histogram + quantiles), because a
  threshold can hide the phenomenon (brief section 5).

6. ENDPOINTS, GATES, VERDICTS (implemented verbatim in analyze1.py)

  Unit of independence: the OBJECT (n = 300). Mutations are
  repeated observations within object. All statistics at object
  level. Permutation/bootstrap seed 20260901.

  E1 (PRIMARY): eta-squared of ladder level on object-level
    LOCAL_SHARE. LOCAL_SHARE defined only for objects with >= 5
    nonzero-d edits of 128; SUPPORT RULE: every level must retain
    >= 60% of its objects, else the primary falls back to E2 (this
    fallback chain is frozen here; no other reassignment allowed).
    eta-squared is direction-free BY DESIGN: monotone, U, threshold
    and discontinuous shapes all register (brief section 7); the
    shape itself is reported descriptively via level means and the
    full histograms.
  E2: eta-squared of level on P(neutral) (forced component declared
    in section 3; reported net of nothing, interpreted with it).
  E3: eta-squared of level on median nonzero displacement.
  Gate for a supported coordinate, applied to the primary endpoint:
    pooled eta2 >= 0.25, per-seed eta2 >= 0.15 in 5/5 seeds,
    within-seed permutation p <= 0.01 (1000 permutations of level
    labels within seed). Bootstrap 95% CI (5000 resamples over
    objects) shipped.
  E4 (H1/H2/H3/H4 adjudication): object-level Spearman and partial
    Spearman. Axis candidates: s_live (H1), inf_density (H2).
    Partials control {other axis, balance_dev, live_vars,
    anf_support, depth}, and a second partial additionally controls
    the forced quantities. Confound dominance: each of {b_live,
    balance_dev, live_vars, anf_support, depth,
    forced_neutral_floor, forced_local_bound} partialled against
    both axes. Thresholds: an axis KEEPS signal at |partial| >=
    0.30 and LOSES it at |partial| < 0.15.

  VERDICT FAMILY (frozen strings):
    TRANSFER_COORDINATE_SUPPORTED
      gate passes AND s_live keeps partial signal (mechanism field:
      STRUCTURAL if inf_density loses, BOTH if both keep,
      UNRESOLVED_COLLINEAR if neither keeps individually).
    BEHAVIORAL_INFLUENCE_DOMINATES_LIVENESS
      gate passes AND inf_density keeps while s_live loses.
    ALTERNATE_COORDINATE_DOMINATES
      gate passes AND both axes lose while a named confound keeps
      (mechanism field CONFOUND:<name>).
    NO_LOW_DIMENSIONAL_TRANSFER_COORDINATE
      gate fails with census and harness clean.
    AXIS_NOT_IDENTIFIABLE_UNDER_CURRENT_GENERATOR
      census rule fails (assay not run).
    HARNESS_SUSPECT
      sham check or ladder-realization check fails.
  H4 evidence (no scalar adequate) is reported through the E4 table
  and within-level spread regardless of verdict.
