3. THE AXIOMS (frozen; encoding the brief's required distinctions)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Rulers are functions of BEHAVIOR PAIRS (f, f') on {0,1}^10 only
(representation-blindness is definitional and verified by identity
cells). Classes: NEUTRAL, SMALL, LARGE, DESTRUCTION, plus FAULT --
declared but unexercisable in this substrate (no invalid objects);
carried because Gen-3 substrates with validity constraints will
exercise it. NOVELTY is a reported component, explicitly
UNADJUDICATED (baseline-relative; these axioms cannot bind it).

  AX1  NEUTRAL iff f' == f (biconditional; AX1c: nothing with
       d > 0 may be NEUTRAL).
  AX2  Collapse to a constant is DESTRUCTION at EVERY parent
       balance (the Gen-1 defect cell class).
  AX3  k-flip modification of a surviving object is SMALL
       (k <= 8 unbalanced; k <= 60 balanced).
  AX4  Severity flag: any collapse classified SMALL is the named
       Gen-1 pathology (counted per ruler).
  AX5  Representation-blindness.
  AX6  Flip-ladder monotonicity.
  AX7  A transform to a HEALTHY function (minority mass >= 0.05)
       is never DESTRUCTION.
  AX8  A transform removing >= 3/4 of the parent's minority
       support is never SMALL (licensed verbatim by the brief's
       definition of small meaningful movement).
  AX9  A transform with d >= 0.45 and < 10% support overlap is
       never SMALL.
Triviality threshold eps_triv = 0.025 = half the object-population
balance-band floor -- JUSTIFIED against the population, not chosen
(the bootstrap's instrument lesson applied; worst-case margin:
an 8-flip child of a band-floor object keeps minority mass 0.042).

4. NECESSITY THEOREMS (forced; the mathematical core)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
N1: no sound ruler is a function of displacement d alone.
    Exchange pair at IDENTICAL d = m: (a) m*1024 flips of a
    balanced object -- support intact, healthy, required SMALL;
    (b) collapse of an object with minority mass m in
    [0.055, 0.0625] -- required DESTRUCTION by AX2. Same d,
    different required class. QED.
N2: no sound ruler is a function of the child f' alone.
    Exchange pair at IDENTICAL f' = g: (a) parent one flip from g
    -- required SMALL; (b) unrelated balanced parent mapped to g --
    required not-SMALL (AX9), not-NEUTRAL (AX1c), not-DESTRUCTION
    (AX7). QED.
Scope: >= 2 informationally independent inputs are necessary for
ANY ruler satisfying the axioms. The surviving ruler uses 3
components; MINIMALITY OF 3 IS NOT CLAIMED.
