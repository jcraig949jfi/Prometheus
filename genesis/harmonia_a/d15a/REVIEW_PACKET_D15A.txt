---------------------------------------------------------------------
THE QUESTION. When a reasoning system's dynamics are broken and the
fix is UNDERIDENTIFIED (several distinct repairs are consistent with
what it has seen), can the system ACTIVELY CHOOSE observations that
reduce uncertainty over the missing repair -- and does that
reduction predict when synthesizing a new operator is warranted?
The claim under test is NOT "more search sees more states." It is
"intelligently chosen information is disproportionately useful for
distinguishing repair hypotheses." This is the synthetic analogue of
designing an experiment.

2. THE SCIENTIFIC OBJECT: REPAIR IDENTIFIABILITY
---------------------------------------------------------------------
A world (state space Z_6 x Z_6 = 36 states, fully enumerable) has
true dynamics using an operator set the agent PARTLY lacks: a hidden
operator h. With its known operators T the agent cannot reach the
target G (a DYNAMICS failure, not mere search failure). It must
synthesize a repair from a candidate pool. It may PROBE: reveal the
true dynamics h(s) at a chosen state s (an intervention). Probes
constrain which repairs remain consistent.
Three nested equivalence relations (the v1 generator conflated the
first with the object; the v2 redesign fixes this):
  E1 extensional operator equality (exact function identity).
  E2 GOAL-RELEVANT REPAIR EQUIVALENCE (PRIMARY): two repairs are the
     same iff they make the same targets reachable AND preserve the
     same soundness (taboo) status -- collapsing all differences
     OFF the goal-relevant region.
  E3 decision equivalence (same warrant endpoint; secondary).
IDENTIFIABILITY = the size of the version space over E2 CLASSES
consistent with the probes so far. UNDERIDENTIFIED = more than one
E2 class possible; IDENTIFIED = one. The whole science is: can
actively chosen probes collapse that version space faster than
passive exploration, and does the collapse predict synthesis
warrant?

4. TRACK B -- GENERATOR REDESIGN
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Only the world mathematics was touched (the engine, firewall,
replay, and ordering were left alone -- they had passed).

FIXED AND EXACTLY VERIFIED (per-world, by exhaustive BFS, not
sampling):
  - E2 now collapses operator multiplicity: E1/E2 class-count ratio
    median 3.0-5.0 across every rung. The v1 defect is gone.
  - EQUIV rung real (I5): median E1/E2 = 5.0, one E2 class from many
    syntactic candidates.
  - ZERO-INFO pole real (I0): probing the ENTIRE observable universe
    shrinks the version space in 0% of cases (zero_info_frac = 1.00);
    underidentified forever by construction.
  - IDENTIFIED pole real (I5): identified_frac = 1.00.
  - THE ACTIVE-INFORMATION OBJECT IS REAL AND SHARP (I1):
    active_advantage_frac = 1.00. In every I1 world, PASSIVE
    (navigation-reachable) probing CANNOT identify the repair
    (cost = infinite) while a SINGLE active teleport probe DOES
    (cost = 1). This is a stronger contrast than the graded rung the
    design asked for.
  - No master-key repair (max hidden-op frequency <= 0.15 over an
    enlarged 26-operator family).

WHAT IS STILL NOT READY:
  - I2 (the GRADED middle rung: both passive and active can identify,
    but active with strictly fewer probes) is NON-CONSTRUCTIBLE in
    Z_6 x Z_6. In a 36-state world, informative states are almost
    always already navigation-reachable, so passive and active costs
    are equal; the strict per-world filter rejected all 12,000 seeds.
    A substrate-size limit, not a logic error.
  - Two open measurement items: the info-only/goal-only/mixed/null
    probe factorial and the matched-difficulty stratification (the
    guarantee that identifiability varies INDEPENDENTLY of ordinary
    search difficulty) are only partially instantiated and must be
    re-measured on the populated rungs.
  - Frozen census criterion "reject if a rung disappears" therefore
    fires: GENERATOR_NOT_READY (5/6 rungs), INSTRUMENT_NOT_READY.
    Honest, and correct.

5. TWO REMEDIES (operator decision required)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
R-A ENLARGE THE SUBSTRATE to Z_8 x Z_8 (64 states) or Z_6^3 (216).
   A larger navigation/observable gap makes graded passive>active
   costs generic, so I2 becomes constructible. Exact enumeration
   stays cheap. Keeps the full 6-rung ladder.
R-B RESCOPE TO A 5-RUNG LADDER in which I1 (passive-impossible /
   active-trivial) IS the active-advantage rung. Scientifically this
   is arguably STRONGER: the D15-A claim is sharpest when passive
   literally cannot disambiguate and active trivially can. The graded
   I2 becomes an optional dose-response secondary. This CHANGES the
   preregistered ladder and therefore needs sign-off.
Harmonia's recommendation: R-B for the confirmatory ladder (cleaner,
sharper contrast); R-A in parallel if the graded gradient is wanted
as a secondary dose-response.

6. QUESTIONS FOR THE REVIEWER
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1 Is the sharp I1 contrast (passive-impossible vs active-trivial)
   an acceptable, or preferable, primary form of the active-
   information claim -- or does the science require the GRADED I2
   dose-response to avoid a knife-edge result?
Q2 E2 makes SOUNDNESS (taboo preservation) a meaningful repair
   distinction but treats repair COST as only an E3 distinction. Is
   that the right scientific commitment, or should cost enter the
   primary identifiability object?
Q3 The matched-difficulty stratification (identifiability must vary
   independently of reach-size / target-count / solver-density) is
   the guard against regressing to "hard world -> high entropy." What
   is the minimum stratification a reviewer would require before
   accepting a positive confirmatory result?
Q4 Epistemic replay is now a hard gate (A3): every warrant decision
   must be bit-reconstructable from ledger + KnowledgeSet. Is
   bit-identity the right bar, or is a declared-tolerance replay
   acceptable for deterministic-but-floating-point feature pipelines
   in later campaigns?
Q5 For the confirmatory forks-as-counterfactuals design, ENG-2 means
   fork branches must rebuild state from the inherited event ledger,
   not from artifact content. Does the reviewer want ENG-1/ENG-2
   fixed and requalified FIRST (deeper forks, readable inheritance),
   or is depth-1 ledger-reconstruction sufficient for D15-A?
Q6 Topology-2 (a separate ecology experiment) remains frozen at 6/90
   cells under the PRE-upgrade engine pin. Restart clean under the
