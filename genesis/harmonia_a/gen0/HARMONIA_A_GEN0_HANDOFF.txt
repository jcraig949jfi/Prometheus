1. WHAT OBJECT WAS STUDIED

  60 Boolean functions f: {0,1}^10 -> {0,1}, each generated as a
  random 24-gate straight-line circuit over {AND,OR,XOR,NAND},
  5 master seeds x 12 objects, census-screened for balance.
  Population characterization (measured, post-census): these
  objects are LOW-COMPLEXITY -- median 4 live variables (range
  1-8), median ANF support 4 monomials, median TT max-rank 2.
  Random straight-line circuits have small live cones. This bounds
  the claim ceiling (section 6).

2. WHICH REPRESENTATIONS WERE LEGITIMATE

  Ran and harness-verified: NAT (circuit, 1-gate edits), SHAM
  (relabeled circuit -- control), ANF (F2 polynomial, 1-monomial
  toggle), ANF_SCR (bijection control), VT (truth table --
  triviality ceiling), TT / TT_SCR (exact numpy TT-SVD, 1-core-entry
  nudge, eps {0.125, 0.5, 2.0}, primary 0.5).
  TOOL_NOT_APPLICABLE: tensorly, quimb, discopy, egglog -- no
  Techne donor handoff exists in this tree and none is importable
  here. Exact numpy TT-SVD substituted for the one donor surface
  the design needed; no claim depends on a donor library.

3. DID ALTERNATE REPRESENTATION CHANGE REACHABILITY: NO.

  FROZEN VERDICT (gate G1): NO_REPRESENTATIONAL_REACHABILITY_EFFECT

  The phenomenon this bench hunted -- native small moves
  neutral/far/invalid while an alternate representation's small
  moves open a non-neutral local band -- DOES NOT EXIST in this
  tuple, because its premise fails: the native circuit tuple
  already has substantial local mass.

5. FAILURE CASES / SHAPES (what the distributions actually say)

  a. The D-13 stackvm gap DID NOT TRANSPLANT. Native single-gate
     edits give a graded profile (83.6% neutral / 11.9% local /
     4.5% far), not stackvm's empty local band (kpos025 = 0.000,
     505/505, verified against D:\ZeusE\d12\analysis\
     p1_results.json before this bench froze). The locality wall
     is a property of stackvm's byte-to-behavior map, not of
     program-shaped representations generally.
  b. EXPLORATORY (post-hoc, labeled): native local mass RISES with
     object complexity -- ANF support 1-2: 0.074; 3-5: 0.118;
     6-10: 0.165; 11+: 0.155. The gradient runs AGAINST the
     "degenerate population hides the gap" rescue: denser objects
     reach the local band MORE easily natively, within this family.
  c. TT is decoder-smoothing-dominated at small doses: eps=0.125
     is 100.0% absorbed by sign-rounding; the primary eps=0.5 is
     99.4% absorbed. Only eps=2.0 moves behavior, and then spills
     25% of moves into FAR. The TT small-move regime on exact
     low-rank cores of these objects is essentially inert-or-blunt.
  d. G2 structure observation (descriptive): at eps=2.0 the
     SCRAMBLED TT has MORE local mass (0.332 vs 0.285) and 4.7x
     LESS far mass than the structure-aligned TT. Alignment did
     not help local reachability; full-rank scrambled cores spread
     a fixed-rms nudge more diffusely. Cost is where alignment
     shows: TT median 60 params vs TT_SCR 2,728 (45x), ANF median
     4 vs ANF_SCR 512 (128x). REPRESENTATION STRUCTURE BOUGHT
     COMPRESSION, NOT REACHABILITY, in this tuple.

6. CLAIM CEILING

  All claims conditional on: n=10, G=24 random straight-line
  circuits (low-liveness population, median 4 live vars), the
  frozen edit families, sign-rounding TT decode, the frozen eps
  grid, and the full-domain Hamming ruler. Nothing here asserts
  that representation cannot be a reachability lever elsewhere --
  D-13's stackvm wall is real and verified; what died here is the
  specific transplant "program-shaped native reps generically
  lack a local consequence band." Nothing here evaluates search
  either: this is a neighborhood-measurement bench, not a
  search-improvement result.

7. NEXT EXPERIMENT (one, concrete)

  The live question D-13 + Gen 0 jointly sharpen: WHAT PROPERTY of
  a native representation/operator tuple predicts an empty local
  band? stackvm has one; circuits do not; circuit local mass grows
  with liveness. Gen 1 should make LIVENESS/DENSITY the controlled
  axis inside ONE substrate: circuits at matched size with forced
  full-liveness (every gate on the output path, e.g. output = XOR
  fold) vs natural dead-cone circuits, native band profile
  measured under the same frozen edit family. If the local band
  empties as liveness saturates, the D-13 wall becomes an instance
  of a measurable one-parameter family -- and THAT parameter, not
  representation swapping, is the diagnostic to feed back to the
  Foundry as an M1 requirement. No M1 requirement is filed from
  Gen 0 (escalation unjustified: effect absent).
