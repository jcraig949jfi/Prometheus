0. SUMMARY FOR THE HURRIED REVIEWER
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The bench asked D-13's successor question: can an ALTERNATE
REPRESENTATION of the same underlying object open a small-move
consequence neighborhood (the non-neutral local band) that the
native representation lacks?

    FINAL VERDICT: NO_REPRESENTATIONAL_REACHABILITY_EFFECT
                   (frozen gate G1, failed at its FIRST clause)

The phenomenon's PREMISE is what died: the native circuit tuple does
not have an empty local band. Single-gate edits on random 24-gate
circuits over {0,1}^10 produce a graded profile -- 83.6% neutral,
11.9% local (0 < d <= 0.25), 4.5% far -- where D-13's stackvm
produced kpos025 = 0.000 over 505/505 clean pairs. The stackvm
locality wall is therefore a property of THAT byte-to-behavior map,
not of program-shaped representations generally. This NARROWS the
D-13 representational claim rather than extending it.

Three shapes survive as the actual product:
  (1) the D-13 gap does not transplant to circuit-native tuples;
  (2) representation structure bought COMPRESSION, NOT REACHABILITY
      -- the structure-aligned tensor-train rep is 45x smaller than
      its scrambled control and no more locally reachable at any
      dose (the scrambled control actually has MORE local mass);
  (3) tensor-train small moves are decoder-smoothing-dominated:
      99.4-100% of primary-dose perturbations are absorbed by
      sign-rounding; the surviving dose spills 25% of moves FAR.

An exploratory (labeled) attack on the null's weakest point -- the
low-complexity object population -- found the gradient runs the
WRONG WAY for a rescue: native local mass RISES with object
complexity (0.074 -> 0.165). Every harness gate passed, including a
machine-implemented sham check, empirical reproduction of all
analytically forced cells, and a dose-knob non-inertness gate. All
five D-13 review lessons (B1, B4, B5, F1, G-1) were inherited
STRUCTURALLY, not procedurally -- see section 6.

3. DESIGN AS EXECUTED
---------------------------------------------------------------------
OBJECT. f: {0,1}^10 -> {0,1}, identified with its 1024-entry truth
table. 60 objects = 5 master seeds (11,22,33,44,55) x 12, each a
random 24-gate straight-line circuit over {AND,OR,XOR,NAND},
census-screened for balance (resample cap 50; max attempts used: 2).
Same-object invariant: every representation must decode to exactly
f; violated assertions abort the run (none fired).

CONSEQUENCE RULER. d(f,f') = Hamming(f,f')/1024 by FULL ENUMERATION.
Exact, deterministic, defined with no reference to any edit metric.
ONE battery serves census, channels, and claims -- the D-13 F1
defect (channels measured on sibling terrains) cannot arise. Faults
do not exist in this substrate; TT rounding absorption is measured
AS the TT neutral rate rather than hidden (decoder-smoothing is a
reported channel, not a confound).

BANDS (frozen; aligned with D-13's K_positive@0.25 channel):
  NEUTRAL d = 0;  LOCAL 0 < d <= 0.25;  FAR d > 0.25;
  plus full dyadic histograms shipped per arm.

ARMS. K = 128 edits/object/arm, uniform with replacement, all
randomness from named SeedSequence spawns of the master seeds.
  NAT      circuit; enumerated edit space (1-gate op change OR
           1-input rewire; min 1056 edits/object), sampled.
  SHAM     input-relabeled circuit, same edit class. Analytic
           expectation: identical to NAT. Harness positive control,
           MACHINE-CHECKED at analysis (D-13 B4 inherited).
  ANF      exact F2 polynomial (Moebius); toggle 1 of 1024 monomial
           coefficients. DECLARED FORCED in the freeze: d = 2^-deg,
           LOCAL mass = 1 - 11/1024 = 0.9893 by arithmetic. Not a
           discovery; a harness check.
  ANF_SCR  ANF after a seeded input-space bijection. Analytic:
           identical to ANF (displacement is bijection-invariant).
  VT       truth table; flip 1 row. The TRIVIALITY CEILING, run as
           an arm: local reachability per se is trivially available
           at representation size 2^n, so NO claim may rest on
           locality existing SOMEWHERE. Pre-registers the "cheap
           deterministic algorithm" attack against the bench itself.
  TT       exact TT-SVD cores of F = (-1)^f; add s*eps*rms(core) to
           one uniformly chosen core entry, s in {+1,-1}; decode by
           full contraction; sign-round. eps grid {0.125, 0.5, 2.0}
           ALL RUN, primary eps = 0.5 named in the freeze before any
           row (a dose-response report, not a selection pool).
           EMPIRICAL CELL.
  TT_SCR   TT after a seeded permutation of the 1024 tensor entries
           (destroys variable-order structure, identical mechanics).
           EMPIRICAL CELL.
HONESTY DECLARATION (freeze section 4, verbatim intent): the band
masses of ANF, ANF_SCR, VT, SHAM are forced by arithmetic. The only
empirical unknowns were the NAT shape and the TT/TT_SCR shapes. The
freeze says so, so no forced cell can be presented as a finding.

8. WHAT THIS GENERATION ACTUALLY LEARNED
---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE D-13 WALL IS NARROWER THAN ITS TEMPTING READING. "Program-
   shaped representations lack a local consequence band" is FALSE
   as a generalization: circuit tuples have a graded band (11.9%
   local). The wall belongs to stackvm's byte-to-behavior map. The
   productive question is no longer "which alternate representation
   fixes it" but "WHAT PROPERTY of a native tuple predicts an empty
   band" -- a one-parameter diagnostic, not a representation swap.
2. STRUCTURE BOUGHT COMPRESSION, NOT REACHABILITY. Aligned reps are
   45-128x smaller than scrambled controls (that is real, and it is
   the storage story); their local reachability is equal or worse.
   Independently, Ergon's Gen-0 (commit 503dd553) found the D-5
   slot consumes CONTENT, not abstractions. Two seats, two
   substrates, one shape: representational structure paying rent in
   storage while the behavioral ledger stays empty.
3. EXACT LOW-RANK TENSOR SMALL MOVES ARE INERT-OR-BLUNT. Sign-
   rounding absorbs 99.4-100% of small perturbations; the first
   dose that moves behavior spills 25% of moves FAR. If a successor
   wants tensor reps as a search neighborhood, the lever is the
   decode margin, not the core nudge size.
4. FORCED CELLS, DECLARED AS FORCED, ARE WHAT MAKE A NULL LEGIBLE.
   Four of seven arms had arithmetically forced outcomes; declaring
   that in the freeze converted them into harness verification
   (all reproduced to <= 0.002) instead of fake findings.

matched-size circuits with forced full liveness (every gate on the
output path) vs natural dead-cone circuits, same frozen edit family,
same ruler. If the local band empties as liveness saturates, the
D-13 wall becomes one point on a measurable one-parameter family --
and THAT parameter, not representation swapping, is what returns to
the Foundry as an M1 requirement. Secondary: tensorly is now a

decode-margin-aware TT move operator is the identified successor arm.
