0. THE QUESTION
---------------------------------------------------------------------
What measurable properties distinguish a computational substrate that
supports navigable accumulation of useful behavioural change from one
that does not -- and can Prometheus decide this CHEAPLY and
PROSPECTIVELY, before spending serious compute searching?

This campaign does NOT ask whether continuous representations are
better, does not attempt to rescue StackVM, and does not assume the
pathological-geometry diagnosis is correct. Its job is to make the
competing explanations -- substrate, mutation operator, their
interaction, behavioural measurement, task structure -- compete under
one ruler.

3. OPERATIONAL DEFINITIONS (fixed before any result is seen)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
PHENOTYPE SPACE. Every substrate maps a genotype to a Boolean
  function on {0,1}^10, held as a 1024-entry truth table computed by
  FULL ENUMERATION. This is the design's load-bearing choice: it makes
  d exactly comparable across substrates, so a cross-substrate
  comparison compares genotype->phenotype MAPS and not two different
  behaviour spaces wearing one name.

d(f,g)            = Hamming(f,g)/1024. Exact.
LOCAL             = exactly ONE elementary intervention in the
                    substrate's own native coordinates. Budget B=1 for
                    all geometry; B>1 only in the search stage, where
                    it is stated.
BEHAVIOURAL       = d(f,f') > 0. Nothing weaker counts as variation,
  VARIATION         and neutrality is never called a small change.
CONSEQUENCE CLASS = R_VEC2, verbatim from A (NEUTRAL / DESTRUCTION /
                    SMALL / LARGE; EPS_TRIV=0.025, LOCAL_BAND=0.25).
USEFUL            = STRICTLY REDUCES DISTANCE TO A FROZEN TARGET:
                    d(f',T) < d(f,T). Target-relative, deterministic,
                    and defined with no reference to any search
                    trajectory, any operator, or any substrate.
VIABLE INTERMEDIATE = non-NEUTRAL and non-DESTRUCTION and USEFUL.
REACHABLE@B       = attainable by at most B elementary interventions.
COMPUTE           = number of phenotype evaluations. This is the only
                    currency every substrate shares, and it is the
                    matched budget wherever a comparison is claimed.

FROZEN TARGET BATTERY (chosen before any substrate was profiled, from
  standard Boolean-complexity families, plus the two control-owned
  targets so that every substrate faces one identical battery):
    T1 PARITY5       parity of x0..x4
    T2 MAJORITY10    at least 5 of 10 bits set
    T3 THRESH7       at least 7 of 10 bits set
    T4 MUX           x0,x1 address, select among x2..x5
    T5 CMP           (x0..x4 as int) > (x5..x9 as int)
    T6 RUN3          three consecutive set bits anywhere
    T7 RANDBAL       fixed pseudorandom balanced function, seed 4242
    T8 INTERVAL      (x as int) in [300, 800)
    T9 P1_TARGET     the positive control's own target (declared)
    T10 N1_TARGET    the N1 control's own target (declared)
  Every target is evaluated against every substrate. Per-target
  results are reported; aggregation never hides a per-target row.

4. THE NAVIGABILITY VECTOR Q  (precommitted; NOT scalarised)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Q is measured per (SUBSTRATE x OPERATOR) cell, and its target-relative
coordinates additionally per TARGET. Scalarisation is forbidden as a
primary: section 8 tests whether any low-dimensional summary of Q
transfers, and pre-collapsing Q would destroy the very information
that test is about.

GEOMETRY (target-free)
  q1  neutral_rate            P(d = 0)
  q2  destruction_rate        P(class = DESTRUCTION)
  q3  band_rate               P(class = SMALL)
  q4  middle_mass             P(0 < d <= 0.25)     [A's D-14 statistic]
  q5  median_nonzero_d        median d | d > 0
  q6  gapped                  1 iff q4 <= 0.05     [A's GAPPED rule]
TARGET-RELATIVE
  q7  improving_rate          P(d(f',T) < d(f,T))
  q8  improve_given_nonneutral q7 / (1 - q1)
  q9  mean_improve_magnitude  E[max(0, d(f,T) - d(f',T))]
  q10 reach_improve@1         P(the single-edit neighbourhood contains
                              at least one improving edit)
                              -- this is the prompt's E(S)
  q11 drift                   P(improve) - P(worsen), among non-neutral
STRUCTURE
  q12 neutral_option_gain     among neutral neighbours, the mean
                              fraction of their non-neutral
                              consequence set that was NOT reachable
                              from the parent. Does neutral drift buy
                              new options?
  q13 baseline_d              mean d(f,T) at the start. Normaliser;
                              never a navigability claim on its own.

5. THE ARENA: SUBSTRATE x OPERATOR MATRIX
---------------------------------------------------------------------
SUBSTRATES (all -> the same Boolean phenotype space)
  S1 CIRCUIT     24 straight-line gates over {AND,OR,XOR,NAND}.
                 A's native representation, ported verbatim. Serves as
                 the CALIBRATION ANCHOR (section 7, E1).
  S2 BYTEVM      byte-addressed stack machine, 48 bytes. An ANALOG of
                 a byte-level genotype map -- NOT StackVM (C-2).
                 Underflow is DEFINED (reads all-false), so the
                 substrate has no execution-fault channel at all and
                 fault rate cannot masquerade as consequence geometry.
  S3 DNF         6-term DNF over 10 variables. Grammar-constrained:
                 every genotype is well-formed by construction, so
                 there is no invalid-program class.
  S4 RELAX(tau)  continuous genotype, soft gates at temperature tau,
                 phenotype = threshold of the soft output at 0.5.
                 tau -> 0 recovers a discrete circuit. The continuous
                 arm, present to be TESTED, not to be endorsed.
  OMITTED, with reasons: graph-rewrite and explicit module/composition
  substrates are omitted as disproportionate to the measurement
  question -- both need a rewrite engine and a composition semantics
  before a single number is produced, and neither adds a cell the
  factorial cannot already estimate (typed/grammar structure is
  carried by S3, operator-family conditioning by S1's op/wire split).
  A probabilistic/distributional instruction representation is
  omitted because S4 already varies exactly the property it would
  contribute (soft mixing over instructions).

OPERATORS (defined SEPARATELY from substrates and crossed with them --
  this separation is mandatory and is the campaign's third mandate)
  M-UNIFORM    uniform site, uniform alternative. Defined identically
               for every discrete substrate. This is what makes the
               substrate main effect estimable at all.
  M-RAWBYTE    replace one byte with a uniform random other byte
               (BYTEVM). Knows nothing about instruction boundaries.
  M-INSTR      same substrate, same genotype, semantics-respecting:
               change EITHER opcode OR operand, never both.
  M-OPONLY     circuit gate ops only; topology frozen.
  M-WIREONLY   circuit topology only; ops frozen.
  M-GAUSS[s]   one continuous coordinate, Gaussian step s (RELAX).
  SWEEP-ALL    not an operator: the full single-edit neighbourhood,
               used where the estimand is the SITE POPULATION rather
               than an operator-weighted draw. The two are reported
               separately and NEVER interchanged -- this is external
               review #1's Q4 distinction, adopted at design time.

CONTROLS (DECLARED CONSTRUCTIONS; they carry no empirical content
  about real substrates, exactly as A declared the FAULT class
  definitional in Gen-3G. Their only job is to qualify the assay.)
  P1-BLOCKS    SMOOTH + NAVIGABLE. 16 blocks; each bit XORs one block
               of the target. Every edit moves d by exactly 1/16;
               hill-climbing reaches d=0 in <= 16 strictly monotone
               steps. Spectrum: a single spike at 0.0625.
  N1-SMOOTH-UNREACHABLE  SMOOTH + NON-NAVIGABLE. The SAME 16-block
               geometry, so its consequence spectrum is a spike at
               0.0625 TOO -- verified identical to P1's, mechanically.
               Its target disagrees with the anchor on exactly half of
               every block, so flipping any block exchanges the
               agreeing and disagreeing halves and leaves d(f,T)
               EXACTLY 0.5 for all 2^16 reachable phenotypes.
               VERIFIED: 400 sampled genotypes, d(f,T) = 0.5 with no
               other value observed; edit-d unique value 0.0625 for
               both P1 and N1.
  N2-HASH      DISCONTINUOUS + NON-NAVIGABLE. sha256 counter-mode:
               every genotype is an independent random truth table.
               The pathological floor.
  DISCONTINUOUS + NAVIGABLE is not constructed; it is an EMPIRICAL
  CELL, and S1/S3 are its candidates. If no arena cell occupies it,
  that is reported as an unoccupied quadrant, not manufactured.

  THE CONTROL PAIR IS THE CAMPAIGN'S SHARPEST INSTRUMENT. P1 and N1
  have provably IDENTICAL local consequence geometry and OPPOSITE
  navigability. Any statistic that ranks them equally is measuring
  smoothness, not navigability. Section 6's E4 is that test.

6. PRECOMMITTED HYPOTHESES  (falsifiable, with disclosure)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
PC-1  ASSAY QUALIFICATION. The Q vector separates P1 from N2 on
      target-relative coordinates (q7-q11), AND separates P1 from N1
      on q7-q11 while NOT separating them on q3-q5. Falsified if the
      controls are not separated: the assay is then unqualified and
      the campaign HALTS at E1 with no substrate ranking.
PC-2  OPERATOR MOVES DESTRUCTION, SUBSTRATE OWNS THE BAND. On BYTEVM,
      swapping M-RAWBYTE for M-INSTR moves q2 (destruction) by
      >= 0.02 absolute, but leaves q3 (band) below 0.05 under BOTH
      operators.
      DISCLOSURE: this pattern was OBSERVED in the labelled bootstrap
      (n=2400/operator: destruction .090 -> .048, band .0046 ->
      .0071). It is frozen here as a prediction to be re-tested at
      scale on DISJOINT seeds, and its evidential novelty is reduced
      accordingly. Recorded, not hidden.
PC-3  MIDDLE MASS DOES NOT SUFFICE. Across the FULL arena including
      controls, q4 (A's D-14 spectrum statistic) will NOT rank cells
      by subsequent search improvement (|rho| < 0.5). This is forced
      by construction once N1 is present -- N1 has maximal middle
      mass and provably zero navigability -- so the full-arena clause
      is a CONSISTENCY CHECK, not a discovery, and is labelled as
      such. THE RISKY CLAUSE is the second: restricted to the REAL
      substrates only (S1-S4, controls excluded), q4 will still fail
      to reach |rho| >= 0.5, while q10 (reach_improve@1) will reach
      |rho| >= 0.7. If q4 predicts search on the real substrates,
      PC-3's risky clause is FALSIFIED and that is a result for the
      spectrum programme, not against it.
PC-4  SEARCH RANK. A frozen pre-search ranking of cells by q10 will
      correlate with compute-matched search improvement at Spearman
      rho >= 0.7 across the arena. Falsified below that: the ruler
      characterises neighbourhoods but does not predict search, and
      the campaign says so (verdict CURRENT_RULER_DOES_NOT_PREDICT_
      SEARCH).
PC-5  RELAXATION SMOOTHS WITHOUT NAVIGATING. Increasing RELAX's
      temperature or step size raises q3/q4 (more graded consequence)
      WITHOUT a proportional rise in q10/q7. Falsified if q10 rises
      with q4 at comparable rate.
PC-6  NO FORCED UNIVERSAL. It is an admissible and expected outcome
      that no low-dimensional summary of Q transfers across
      materially different substrates. NO_TRANSFERABLE_NAVIGABILITY_
      COORDINATE is a first-class verdict, not a failure.

7. THE EXPERIMENT FUNNEL  (at most EIGHT, in order, no ninth)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Each experiment is frozen in its own FREEZE_E*.txt, issued and hashed
into JOURNAL.jsonl BEFORE its evidence exists.

E1  ASSAY QUALIFICATION AND CALIBRATION
    HYPOTHESIS      the assay can measure a millimetre.
    NULL            it cannot; any later ranking is then noise.
    INTERVENTION    measure Q on P1, N1, N2 and on S1 CIRCUIT.
    FROZEN METRIC   PC-1 clauses; plus the CALIBRATION ANCHOR:
                    S1's P(0 < d <= 0.25) under SWEEP-ALL must land
                    within +/- 0.03 of A's published 0.119.
    CONTROL         P1 (positive), N2 (pathological), N1 (the sharp
                    one).
    KILL            any clause fails -> HALT the campaign, report an
                    unqualified assay, run no ranking.
E2  ARENA CHARACTERISATION
    Measure Q on every (substrate x operator) cell. Descriptive; the
    only verdict available is that a cell is or is not measurable.
E3  SUBSTRATE vs OPERATOR DECOMPOSITION
    HYPOTHESIS      brittleness is substrate-owned.
    NULL            it is operator-owned, or interaction-owned.
    METRIC          eta^2 of substrate, operator, and interaction on
                    each Q coordinate, over matched intervention
                    budgets. PC-2 adjudicated here.
E4  SMOOTHNESS IS NOT NAVIGABILITY
    HYPOTHESIS      a smoothness statistic fails to separate P1/N1.
    METRIC          the 2x2 occupancy table and the P1/N1 contrast on
                    q3-q5 versus q7-q11.
    KILL            if smoothness DOES separate them, PC-1's premise
                    is wrong and E5-E8 are re-scoped, not rescued.
E5  FROZEN PREDICTION, THEN COMPUTE-MATCHED SHORT-HORIZON SEARCH
    The pre-search ranking by q10 is FROZEN AND JOURNALED BEFORE any
    search runs. Then (1+lambda) hill-climbing per cell, per target,
    matched by phenotype-evaluation count. PC-4 adjudicated here.
E6  CROSS-SUBSTRATE PREDICTOR TRANSFER
    Leave-one-substrate-out: fit a predictor of search improvement on
    Q from k-1 substrates, predict the held-out one. PC-6's test.
E7  PROSPECTIVE CHEAPNESS
    How small an assay budget still predicts? Predictive power as a
    function of edits sampled. Answers "cheaply and prospectively".
E8  RESERVED. Allocated after E1-E7, to the sharpest surviving
    question. If none survives, E8 is NOT run and that is recorded.

9. THE BOOTSTRAP, AND WHAT I ALREADY SAW
---------------------------------------------------------------------

(genesis/harmonia_b/bootstrap/). It answered engineering questions
only: do the substrates run, are they non-degenerate, are they fast.

      this freeze; the repair is recorded in the substrate docstring
      and the identical-spectrum claim is now mechanically verified.
