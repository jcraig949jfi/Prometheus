HARMONIA B GEN-3B -- FREEZE E8 (THE RESERVED SLOT): DOES THE
GEOMETRY DECOMPOSITION SURVIVE ON SEARCH?
Issued 2026-09-01, BEFORE any E8 evidence exists. This is the eighth
and FINAL experiment. There is no ninth.
=====================================================================

WHY THIS QUESTION GOT THE RESERVED SLOT
  The charter allocated E8 after E1-E7 to the sharpest surviving
  question. E3 and E5 disagree, and the disagreement is the sharpest
  thing this campaign found:
    E3 (GEOMETRY):  on the crossed 3x3, eta2 on the band rate is
                    substrate 0.9752, operator 0.0002. The operator is
                    ~5000x smaller. Substrate owns the geometry.
    E5 (SEARCH):    CIRCUIT|M-UNIFORM improves 0.339 while
                    CIRCUIT|M-OPONLY improves 0.193. SAME substrate,
                    1.76x, from an operator E3 says barely moves the
                    band.
  Either the operator matters much more for search than for geometry,
  or one of the two decompositions is an artefact of its own scale. E8
  runs the SAME two-way decomposition on SEARCH OUTCOME over the
  IDENTICAL crossed design, so the two are directly comparable
  numbers rather than two anecdotes.

HYPOTHESIS
  The substrate/operator decomposition is NOT invariant to what is
  measured: eta2_operator is materially larger on search outcome than
  on the geometry coordinates, on the same cells.

NULL / COMPETING EXPLANATIONS
  N-a  It IS invariant: eta2_operator stays negligible on search too,
       and the CIRCUIT contrast in E5 was a single-substrate accident
       or a different-operator-set artefact (E5 used M-OPONLY, which
       is substrate-typed and NOT in the crossed design).
  N-b  Scale artefact: both decompositions are dominated by the
       between-substrate spread and eta2 is simply the wrong summary.
       Tested by reporting, alongside eta2, the WITHIN-SUBSTRATE
       operator range for both geometry and search -- the same
       diagnostic already journaled against my own E3 headline.

INTERVENTION
  Exactly the E3 crossed design: {CIRCUIT, BYTEVM, DNF} x
  {M-UNIFORM, M-UNIFORM2, M-TAILSITE}, the three SUBSTRATE-AGNOSTIC
  operators. Compute-matched (1+1) search, 800 phenotype evaluations,
  10 targets, 15 seeds, both acceptance modes. Same budget and same
  harness as E5, so E5's controls carry over.
  NOTE ON BUDGET FAIRNESS, declared: M-UNIFORM2 spends TWO elementary
  edits per evaluation while the other two spend one. Evaluations are
  matched, elementary edits are not, and that asymmetry is intrinsic
  to a budget arm. Both accountings are reported; neither is hidden.

FROZEN METRIC
  eta2_substrate / eta2_operator / eta2_interaction on mean normalised
  improvement, per acceptance mode, computed by the identical two-way
  routine E3 used, with the seed as the replication unit.
  Reported beside E3's geometry eta2 for the same cells, and beside
  the within-substrate operator RANGE for both.

GATES
  E8-G1  DISSOCIATION CONFIRMED iff eta2_operator on search exceeds
         eta2_operator on the band rate by a factor >= 10 in at least
         one acceptance mode.
  E8-G2  The within-substrate operator range on search must exceed
         0.05 absolute normalised improvement in at least one
         substrate, or the dissociation is a ratio between two small
         numbers and is reported as such.

KILL CONDITION
  If eta2_operator on search is also negligible (< 0.01) then N-a
  holds, the E5 CIRCUIT contrast was operator-set-specific, and the
  campaign reports the substrate framing as SURVIVING on both
  measurements -- which would strengthen A's programme, not mine.

WHAT THE RESULT CHANGES
  It decides which sentence goes in the final packet: whether
  "substrate geometry" is a sufficient object of study for
  navigability, or whether the operator has to be carried in every
  claim. It is the last experiment; whatever it says, the campaign
  closes on it.
