HYPOTHESIS
  Some property measured BEFORE search, on k-1 substrate families,
  predicts search outcome on a HELD-OUT family it was never fitted to.
  This is the campaign's strongest possible positive result and it is
  deliberately the hardest thing here to obtain.

NULL / COMPETING EXPLANATIONS
  N-a  NO_TRANSFERABLE_NAVIGABILITY_COORDINATE. Nothing transfers; each
       family needs its own ruler. A FIRST-CLASS outcome, not a failure.
  N-b  Transfer is really baseline-difficulty transfer: q13 (starting
       distance) does the work and the geometry adds nothing. Tested by
       including q13 as a competing single predictor and by requiring
       any winner to beat it.
  N-c  n is too small and any apparent transfer is noise. Tested by a
       label-permutation null.

UNIT OF ANALYSIS -- and why it changed from the charter's wording
  The charter said "leave one substrate out" over cells. With nine real
  cells that is a 9-point regression and it is not adjudicable. The
  assay already computes the target-relative coordinates PER TARGET, and
  E5 already produced outcomes PER (cell, target). So the unit here is
  the (cell x target) pair: 9 real cells x 10 targets = 90 rows, with
  folds still cut BY SUBSTRATE FAMILY so that no row of a held-out
  family is ever in train. This is a resolution change, not a bar
  change, and it is declared before any E6 number exists.

INTERVENTION
  Families: CIRCUIT (3 cells), BYTEVM (2), DNF (1), RELAX (3).
  Leave-one-FAMILY-out, 4 folds. Train on all rows of the other three
  families; predict held-out rows. Both acceptance modes, separately.
  PREDICTORS, each evaluated ALONE (no multivariate fit at this n):
     q1, q2, q3, q4, q5, q10(target), q11(target), q13(target)
  and the frozen composite  C = q4 * (1 - q2)   declared here, before
  looking: graded consequence discounted by destruction. It is the
  simplest two-term combination the campaign's own results motivate and
  it is fixed now so it cannot be tuned later.

FROZEN METRIC AND GATES
  E6-G1  TRANSFER iff some single predictor achieves held-out Spearman
         rho >= 0.50 in ALL FOUR folds, in at least one acceptance mode.
  E6-G2  It must BEAT the difficulty null: that predictor's mean
         held-out rho must exceed q13's by >= 0.10.
  E6-G3  It must beat a label-permutation null: mean held-out rho above
         the 99th percentile of 200 within-train label permutations.
  All three -> TRANSFERABLE. Any failure -> NO_TRANSFERABLE_
  NAVIGABILITY_COORDINATE, reported as a first-class result with the
  achieved numbers.

CONTROLS
  The three designed controls are EXCLUDED from E6 entirely. They are
  constructions, they are not a substrate family, and including them
  would let a designed landscape decide a transfer claim. Their effect
  on transfer is instead reported separately as a sensitivity note.

KILL CONDITION
  DNF is a single-cell family; its fold predicts 10 rows from one cell
  and is UNDERPOWERED BY CONSTRUCTION. If the verdict turns on the DNF
  fold alone, E6 reports INSUFFICIENTLY_ADJUDICATED for that fold rather
  than counting it as a pass or a fail.

WHAT THE RESULT CHANGES
  A pass licenses a SUBSTRATE QUALIFICATION GATE recommendation with a
  named coordinate. A failure means the final packet recommends against
  any prospective gate that assumes one ruler crosses families.
