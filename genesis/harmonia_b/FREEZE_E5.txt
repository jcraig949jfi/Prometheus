INTERVENTION
  (1+1) hill-climbing from a random genotype of the cell's own
  substrate, minimising d(f, T), under a FIXED BUDGET of 800 phenotype
  evaluations per run -- the only currency every substrate shares.
  12 cells x 10 targets x 15 seeds x 2 acceptance modes.
  ACCEPTANCE MODES, both run because the difference is itself a result:
     STRICT      accept only strict improvement
     NEUTRAL-OK  accept improvement OR equality (permits neutral drift)
  Wall-clock differs by up to ~5x across substrates (RELAX evaluates
  slowest); that is recorded and is NOT the matched quantity. The
  matched quantity is evaluations.

FROZEN METRIC
  outcome = (d_start - d_best) / d_start, per run; cell score = mean
  over targets and seeds. Also recorded: absolute d_best, exact-hit
  rate (d_best = 0), and accepted-step count.
