## 1. Executive thesis

**Build an instrument for different causal machines, not a common cognitive machine with interchangeable agents.** The Observatory should standardize experimental custody, declared information access, interventions, resource reporting and evidence. It should not standardize what a thought, memory, objective, object, step or individual must be.

This portfolio contains 36 frontier sketches and 12 deep packets. They are hypotheses about useful mechanisms, not claims of novelty or likely intelligence. Seven of the twelve use a primary carrier of adaptation that does not map cleanly onto a standard modern ML architecture. Others deliberately expose familiar relations to compression, program search and internal simulation. Familiarity does not disqualify a mechanism; disguising it does.

The central separation is between **investigator powers** and **organism powers**. An investigator may inspect a complete state or restart a run. An organism does not thereby receive an omniscient state vector, checkpoint API, simulator, semantic memory service, truth oracle or stable episode identity. If it constructs any of these abilities, the construction, information source and cost must appear in its history.

The smallest adequate Observatory is a **federation of native runtimes under explicit experimental contracts**. A common synchronous VM would simplify replay while excluding or altering the developmental, ecological and material candidates. A universal byte interpreter could simulate many of them, but computational expressibility is weaker than preserving information, causality, costs and intervention meaning.

Three outcomes are valuable: a surviving bounded mechanism claim; a diagnosed counterfeit; or an architecture whose requirements expose an Observatory limitation. None requires claiming intelligence. Strong recursive sagacity remains detection-unqualified; packet A12 proposes a narrower discovery-rule experiment, not a shortcut around that ceiling.

**Scope and source boundary.** The conceptual baseline is the v0.3 hardened design and v0.4 synthesis delivered at base commit `3bd02f393a2a2b7f39958e0550cfc92f8027602d`. Their thin-federation, reset-closure, claim-specific qualification and comparator principles are retained. This is not a conformance audit of all current repository runtimes. The Evidence Wiki query was unavailable because of a client import failure; no new wiki evidence or empirical result is claimed. No literature survey drives the proposals. Resource quantities below bound prospective designs, not authorized runs.

## 2. Search-space map

Architectures are separated by **what changes, where the change lives, what preserves it, and what causal removal should erase its benefit**. Different metaphors with the same answers do not earn separate slots.

| Dimension | Deliberately represented | Remaining blind spot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adaptation carrier | Rules, topology, chemical flux, irreversible bonds, simulated residues, paths, addresses, object boundaries, constitutions, experiments, defects, generators | Mechanisms requiring unknown physical processes |
| Unit of cognition | Single process, growing body, interacting population, material domain | Very large societies and institutions |
| Time | Event-driven, asynchronous, multirate, irreversible, developmental | Unbounded real-time deployment |
| Memory | Descriptive, executable, relational, circulatory, environmental, structural | Biological molecular detail |
| Search | Local edit, branching model, revisitation, coalition formation, experiment selection, changing proposal grammar | Complete search over unrestricted programs |
| Persistence | Chosen retention, reproduction, repair, stable circulation, commitment | Literal indefinite survival |
| Objective | Compression, viability constraints, service exchange, endogenous preservation criteria | Objective-free agency: not assumed coherent |
| Ontology | Fixed symbols through invented addresses to newly constructed object types | Uninterpreted behavior without any testable output |
| Reversibility | Restorable digital states through costly commitments to non-clonable material states | Perfect physical reversal |

Anti-gravity test: could the mechanism be derived from finite resources, state transformations, locality and selective persistence without knowing today's ML categories? Each sketch answers through its mechanism, not through a product analogy.

**H** means hard to map cleanly to a standard modern ML architecture; **K** means a recognizable connection exists and is acknowledged. H is not a historical novelty claim. There are 20 H and 16 K sketches; Fable should challenge that classification. Recategorization is preferable to inventing novelty.

Controls, outside the frontier count: constants; running means and sufficient statistics; nearest-neighbor and lookup tables; fixed and exhaustive program libraries; uniform search and archive search; ordinary adaptive policies; recurrent/numeric learners; and, only where interfaces warrant them, conventional neural/RL/language-model agents. Give comparators the same lawful information and a serious tuning allowance. A strong conventional win is an allocation result, not an embarrassment.

## 3. Thirty-six frontier architecture sketches

### S01. Rewrite Quarry [K; deep A01]
An organism factors repeated transformation traces into executable rules and retains a rule only when its description, invocation and verification cost repay construction. It might acquire reusable operators rather than answers. Adaptation lives in a changing rewrite language, not just stored examples. A dictionary compressor or supplied parser may explain everything. Observatory pressure: distinguish discovered executable structure from privileged factorization supplied by the adapter.

### S02. Developmental Relay [H; deep A02]
A tiny local rule set grows and prunes a communication body in response to traffic, congestion and resource gradients. Mature geometry routes transformations that the original kernel could not perform directly. Construction history, not a fixed controller, is the candidate memory. Mere path shortening or a seeded circuit is the impostor. Required capability: component birth, death and local development without global coordinates.

### S03. Catalytic Treaty Ecology [H; deep A03]
Locally interacting compartments exchange resources and transformation products; mutually enabling cycles preserve a collective capability that no member contains. Membership changes as service-producing compartments reproduce. The interest is compositional population computation, not cooperation as a metaphor. A central allocator or an abundant single species could explain success. Observatory pressure: causal accounting across organisms, extracellular state and resources without appointing a master agent.

### S04. Commitment Fuse Garden [H; deep A04]
An organism can spend scarce material to make selected transitions difficult to reverse, stabilizing a partial computation while keeping alternatives mutable. The learned variable is where and when to surrender optionality. This could protect useful abstractions against interference. Ordinary hysteresis or simply having extra memory is the impostor. Observatory pressure: asymmetric transition costs without investigator rollback becoming free organism repair.

### S05. Counterfactual Residue Workshop [K; deep A05]
The organism builds fallible local simulators, branches them and stores only the discrepancies between branch predictions and later observations. Reusable error-correction procedures, not complete imagined histories, persist. It may learn when a hypothetical computation is unreliable. A host-provided perfect world simulator would counterfeit the entire effect. Observatory pressure: provenance and metering of organism-built simulations separately from laboratory forks.

### S06. Return-Path Loom [K; deep A06]
The organism compresses action sequences into conditional routes back to previously useful states, then explores outward from those states. Return is enacted through the world, not granted by restoration. This might cross search bottlenecks while preserving reachable options. A teleporting archive or externally stable landmark IDs is the impostor. Observatory pressure: keep physical revisitation distinct from checkpoint replay.

### S07. Addressless Memory Weave [H; deep A07]
Records initially lack stable names; local probes create linking and discrimination procedures that become the organism's own addresses. Writing and retrieval co-develop, including collision repair and retirement of stale names. Interesting behavior is portable constructed addressing. A hash map secretly provided by the host would do the work. Observatory pressure: raw local contact without a free global memory namespace.

### S08. Boundary-Inventing Fabric [H; deep A08]
A graph of interacting events proposes and tests cuts that become new internal objects with their own permissible transformations. Object identity is an earned predictive/interventional convenience, not input segmentation. This could produce new representational units. A privileged parser or external object tracker is the impostor. Observatory pressure: changing internal schemas without changing the world's hidden ontology to match them.

### S09. Viability Constitution Collective [H; deep A09]
Internal processes compete over limited resources but also edit local rules determining what must be preserved. Constraints on multiple reserves create pressure without a scalar reward. Acquired preservation priorities may organize future search. Fixed homeostatic thresholds may suffice. Observatory pressure: distinguish endogenous priorities from designer-imposed viability and record policy changes without revealing evaluator preferences.

### S10. Experimental Obligation Ledger [K; deep A10]
Competing explanations attach explicit obligations to predictions; scarce probes are selected to make incompatible obligations disagree. Confirmed procedures for discrimination are retained and reused. This may produce reusable experiment operators instead of confident narratives. A lookup of test outcomes or a supplied disagreement oracle could counterfeit it. Observatory pressure: meter information acquisition and separate internal explanations from external scientific truth.

### S11. Traveling-Defect Computer [H; deep A11]
Local excitations in a spatial medium carry stable defect patterns; collisions, motion and boundary changes transform those patterns. Adaptation changes guides and pinning sites so a sequence of local events becomes a reusable computation. Simple resonance or an optimized external decoder could explain apparent reasoning. Observatory pressure: distributed, nonmodular state and honest limits on cloning, tracing and exact reversal.

### S12. Discovery-Rule Foundry [K; deep A12]
An organism modifies the rules that propose, test and package new operators, rather than only extending its operator collection. The proposed gain is cheaper discovery of further useful operators after learned answers are removed. Fixed search bias or hidden operator cargo is the impostor. Observatory pressure: versioned executable self-change with an external evaluator the organism cannot edit.

### S13. Cancellation Calculus [H]
Paired local processes encode unresolved obligations as opposing flows; compatible evidence cancels a pair, while remaining imbalance directs further computation. It might implement distributed constraint propagation without a central agenda. The distinctive state is conservation of unresolved work, not beliefs assigned to nodes. Ordinary diffusion is the main alternative. Observatory need: conserved-flow accounting and matched disruptions of cancellation versus transport.

### S14. Delay-Line Grammar [H]
An organism changes transmission delays so recurring temporal patterns trigger reusable coincidences before their raw streams disappear. Memory resides in transport dynamics rather than accessible records. Temporal compositional recognition is the target; a fixed filter bank is the impostor. Unlike A11, timing relations rather than defect topology carry adaptation. Observatory need: native timing with jitter interventions and no hidden shared clock.

### S15. Negotiated Codec Commons [H]
Processes repeatedly translate each other's incompatible message systems, retaining bilateral translations that enable new joint transformations. A shared language need never emerge. The interest is productive communication across independently developed representations. An aligned seed dictionary would counterfeit agreement. Observatory need: independently randomized namespaces, paid translation, and transplant tests that retain or break only negotiated mappings.

### S16. Quorum of Incompatibilities [H]
Components retain mutually incompatible local descriptions and prevent unsafe actions only when enough conflicts become causally connected. Persistent disagreement, rather than consensus, maintains useful alternatives. It may preserve distinctions until a resolving observation arrives. Redundant voting is the impostor. Observatory need: selective channel cuts that separate conflict propagation from simple replication of one global estimate.

### S17. Void-Trace Memory [H]
A process writes memory by selectively removing material or disabling reactions; later activity is shaped by the resulting absences. The target is useful relational negative space, not raw capacity. A binary mask can emulate a small instance, so the claim must concern construction, costs or interference. Observatory need: provenance of deletions and nulls preserving total missing material while scrambling its arrangement.

### S18. Repair-Taught Circuit [H]
Repeated injury drives local repair rules to construct alternate causal routes; the repair repertoire itself becomes reusable on new disruptions. Cognitive interest lies in acquiring restoration procedures, not merely being robust. Redundancy or a fixed backup circuit is the impostor. Observatory need: adversarial damage with equal initial impairment and separation of repaired function from reconstructed original bytes.

### S19. Dissipative Proof Medium [H]
Candidate transformations can propagate only while consuming local inconsistency; dead ends leave inert residue and successful chains reopen resource access. A solution is a physically supported chain, not a global numerical score. Gradient descent on a supplied energy landscape is the obvious alternative. Observatory need: audit who encoded the constraints and whether the medium merely executes a planted solver.

### S20. Time-Division Organism [H]
Processes negotiate temporary access to shared channels, and acquired temporal allocation patterns determine which computations can compose. The learned object is a schedule with local enforcement, not a task policy. Fixed time slots or a central scheduler may suffice. Observatory need: scheduling as endogenous state, distinguished from the host's execution schedule and its side channels.

### S21. Migratory Workspace [H]
A bounded workspace moves between sites, leaving compact traces but requiring computation to relocate to information. Routing the workspace changes which distant constraints can interact. It may discover economical orders of reasoning under severe locality. A global agent with free random access is the impostor. Observatory need: charge movement and preserve the distinction between information transport and observer inspection.

### S22. Consumable-Certificate Network [H]
Local transformations require expiring, single-use permissions produced by other transformations. The organism learns permission cycles that allow new compositions without duplicating scarce resources. It may acquire causal dependency structure. An externally authored workflow engine is the impostor. Observatory need: linear resource semantics and ancestry of permission creation, not merely authenticated labels.

### S23. Refuge Mosaic [H]
Spatial niches preserve incompatible mechanisms under different local stresses; migration occasionally combines machinery that could not coexist in one compartment. The candidate effect is diversity-maintained combinational discovery. More search budget or a global population archive is the impostor. Observatory need: coupled niche histories and migration interventions without averaging the population into a single agent state.

### S24. Obligation Succession [H]
Processes transfer unfinished dependencies, rather than complete programs or examples, to descendants. Each successor must reconstruct enough machinery to discharge what it inherits. Accumulating constructive obligations could produce lineage-level cognition. A copied answer hidden in the obligation encoding is the impostor. Observatory need: transgenerational custody and exact accounting of inherited information plus reconstruction work.

### S25. Bottleneck Morphology [H]
The organism actively narrows or widens contact surfaces between regions. Selective information loss forces local summaries to emerge before distant computations can coordinate. It might invent useful interfaces. A hand-designed bottleneck representation could explain the result. Observatory need: resource-matched geometry interventions; its distinction from A02 is learned bandwidth and abstraction rather than grown routing.

### S26. Exception Loom [K]
A compact default transformation accumulates executable exceptions; recurring exception patterns are promoted into new defaults while obsolete patches dissolve. The target is acquisition of structured generality from repair. Ordinary decision lists are a close relative and a required comparator. Observatory need: log rule promotion and test transfer on exceptions whose surface forms never occurred during development.

### S27. Symmetry Mint [K]
The organism searches for transformations under which its own outputs remain invariant and constructs quotient descriptions to avoid repeated work. It may discover reusable equivalence classes. Supplied canonicalization or augmentation is the impostor. Observatory need: keep world symmetries hidden and distinguish learned invariance from information erased by preprocessing.

### S28. Translation Triangle [K]
Three independently learned representations check one another through round-trip translations; persistent disagreement identifies missing distinctions and drives new operators. The candidate is productive representational repair. Agreement on a shared wrong code is the impostor. Observatory need: grounded interventions outside the translation cycle and ancestry checks preventing three copies of one decoder.

### S29. Executable Fossil Succession [K]
Dead lineages leave executable fragments that later organisms must inspect, sandbox, adapt and pay to reuse. It might accumulate machinery beyond any one lifetime. A benevolent curator selecting ready-made solutions is the impostor. Observatory need: separate fossil deposition, exposure, compatibility repair and performance, including harmful and irrelevant fossils.

### S30. Adversarial Self-Partition [K]
A system maintains competing decompositions of its own computation and promotes the one whose targeted edits yield useful local improvements. The interesting variable is controllable modularity, not a supplied module boundary. Generic architecture search is a close relative. Observatory need: interventions on candidate partitions without assuming each is already a valid causal component.

### S31. Reversible Derivation Stack [K]
The organism favors transformations with learned inverses, retaining compact undo procedures rather than full previous states. It may explore combinatorial spaces with economical backtracking. A standard reversible interpreter is the impostor if inverses were built in. Observatory need: charge inverse discovery and distinguish an organism's undo from investigator restore.

### S32. Coalition Auction of Proofs [K]
Specialized procedures bid scarce execution time using locally checkable promises and exchange partial derivations. Useful coalitions persist only if their composed proof obligations close. This may create compositional specialization. A fixed mixture or task router is the impostor. Observatory need: costs of coordination and tests that distinguish newly negotiated composition from preassigned roles.

### S33. Sensor Constructor [K]
An organism spends material to create observation procedures, testing which measurements preserve distinctions needed downstream. The possible gain is an acquired measurement vocabulary. Active feature selection is a close relative; a supplied menu may account for all success. Observatory need: distinguish synthesis of a new sensor transformation from choosing a privileged hidden-state channel.

### S34. Forgetting Compiler [K]
The organism learns executable deletion procedures that preserve selected future distinctions while removing costly history. The target is task-relative sufficient forgetting, not memory growth. A running statistic is the first comparator. Observatory need: delayed counterfactual tasks that reveal what was lost, and cost accounting for both deletion and subsequent reconstruction.

### S35. Rival Constructor Tournament [K]
Multiple constructors propose ways to build a solver from identical raw material; a winner leaves a construction procedure rather than the built solver. The target is portable developmental economy. Ordinary program search may fully explain it. Observatory need: fresh material layouts and cross-tests separating constructor transfer from transfer of a prebuilt solution.

### S36. Abstraction Bankruptcy [K]
Internal representations accrue maintenance debt when they create prediction or action exceptions. Bankruptcy dissolves a representation and redistributes its components to competitors. It might escape obsolete ontologies through selective destruction. Thresholded model replacement is the impostor. Observatory need: record destruction and reallocation, with nulls matching the frequency and resource release of bankruptcy.

## 5. Twelve full architecture packets

### Common experimental contract

The following applies to every packet, with explicit exceptions below.

- **Boundary.** Host paths, addresses, object IDs, episode numbers, evaluator scores, random seeds and investigator logs are not observations unless declared. Internal counters are allowed if constructed from accessible events; they are not the host clock. World-side resource changes are observable only through specified sensors.
- **Persistence.** A reset is an intervention on a declared causal closure: organism, world/niche, messages in flight, scheduler state, random sources, adapters, inherited artifacts and measurement feedback. Mark each retained, erased, resampled or inaccessible. A developmental restart is not a content reset. A lawful retained-state twin detects over-erasure. Joint/ecological memory is legitimate if named; it is not organism-only memory.
- **Assay custody.** Freeze generator, comparator budgets, mutation/lesion rules and expected signature before confirmation. Hold out latent structures, not merely input strings or seeds. Keep training, developmental selection, assay calibration and confirmation distinct. Do not select only successful founders for the primary statistic.
- **Proposed bounded screen.** Unless overridden, use 24 independent founder/world blocks, up to twelve prespecified arms, 4,096 native events per arm including allocated development and all branches, and no more than 64 active components and 64 KiB organism state. Shared ancestors belong to the same block. An arm is one complete condition; repeated phases within it share its event cap. Before execution, enumerate every factorial cell, comparator, sham and rescue in an arm ledger. The ceiling is 1,179,648 native events per packet screen; A12 explicitly needs two screens. Charge shared development to each arm's lifecycle comparison, but record actual shared expenditure once in the campaign ledger. Count construction and failed attempts. Qualification runs and external verification need separately approved caps; they are not free work within this ceiling. If the planned controls or known positive do not fit, revise the design before running rather than silently dropping controls or extending limits. These bounds are design choices, not power calculations or execution approval.
- **Decision.** A packet defines a primary directional signature. For the small deterministic screen, require it in at least 20/24 blocks, with at most 4/24 for the principal negative and at least 20/24 for the designed positive. Randomize arm allocation/order where meaningful. Report all differences, not only this threshold. Fix practical effect margins per packet before execution; for cost claims, propose at least 20% lower nominated native cost at equal correctness. A fresh confirmatory protocol must justify uncertainty, effect size and any multiplicity treatment; this screen does not estimate a general error rate or rank all twelve statistically.
- **Damage and rescue.** Each targeted lesion needs an equal-cost, equal-damage sham and a rescue where feasible. Preserve outputs on a calibration set of unaffected operations. Failure of these checks makes the intervention unqualified, not evidence that the architecture failed.
- **Truth.** A hand-built positive calibrates detection of a narrow mechanism; it does not demonstrate endogenous acquisition. An exact tiny world can certify finite functional equivalence. No hash, trace explanation or independent reader is a truth oracle for open-ended cognition.
- **Logging.** Record input access, transition-law version, causal event parents, pre/post state or reconstructible deltas, births/deaths, external draws, resource transfers, interventions and failures. Record actual dependencies, including joint parents and world artifacts. A provenance edge means recorded dependence, not proven causal necessity. Log precision, omissions and observer interference. No complete event reconstruction is promised outside the modeled boundary.
- **Parking.** Invalid positive controls or custody produce UNQUALIFIED/BLOCKED results. A calibrated negative parks the stated mechanism/domain at the stated budget, not all possible descendants. Permit one prespecified diagnostic repair; a new domain or larger budget is a new proposal, not rescue by rhetoric.

### A01. Rewrite Quarry

**Identity and reason for selection.** A finite machine invents reusable executable rewrites by compressing its own successful transformations. Family: compression/program transformation; explicitly a familiar relation. Selected to separate operator acquisition from mere recollection.

**Causal mechanism.** State consists of raw transformation traces T, a rule graph L, applicability guards G, and construction debt D. Primitive symbol edits are supplied, but no task-specific macros. After a local trace repeats, enumerate bounded substitutions of repeated subtraces by a candidate rule. Execute old and proposed forms on accessible examples. Retain a rule only if `saved execution cost > description + test + maintenance cost` over the declared reuse horizon and no checked behavior changes. A work queue schedules use, verification and pruning; rule dependencies form a DAG. Stochasticity is candidate order. Execution is fast; promotion is slower; amortization spans tasks.

**Information boundaries.** The proposer sees its traces and purchased feedback, not hidden canonical solutions, test identities or the world's generative grammar. Global access exists only inside its bounded trace store. Across tasks, retain rules and their guards; discard task answers and work queues. Rebuild from primitive edits for a founder reset.

**Learning/change.** Parameter counts and debts change; traces accumulate; rules and operators are created, composed and deleted. No reproduction or topology outside the rule graph is required. **Memory:** rule bodies and evidence are stored as explicit terms; internally allocated tokens address them; guard matching retrieves them. Unpaid rules expire. Bodies and dependencies can be transplanted; the primitive interpreter is fixed and charged.

**Search dynamics and economy.** Exploration proposes new factorizations and compositions, not only random task actions. Candidate evaluation can be undone internally; external actions cannot. Retained rules form an archive, but archive lookup and unsuccessful candidates cost resources. Savings, not an unexplained reward, fund retention.

**Smallest phenomenon and cheap impostor.** One acquired rewrite reduces primitive work on structurally new inputs. A dictionary containing old input/output pairs, or a parser that already recognizes the desired abstraction, is cheaper evidence against the grander story.

**Bounded falsification assay.** Use short strings generated from withheld compositions of primitive edits. Cross acquired rule bodies with either old traces or erased traces, giving four primary arms; add rule scrambling, an equal-byte dictionary, a fixed generic macro library and a known composed-rule positive. Primary signature: the acquired-rule/no-old-trace arm computes correct new compositions at least 20% more cheaply than the dictionary and rule-scrambled arm; restoring the rule rescues cost without restoring examples. Enumerate all admissible strings up to the registered small length for correctness. A dictionary allowed to store that entire finite domain can win; include its full storage/construction cost and restrict the claim to the named resource regime.

**Observability/interventions/provenance.** Trace each rule to primitive edits, observations, rejected candidates and its callers. Support rule extraction, substitution, freezing, guarded replay and dependency-aware transplant. Supplied primitives, search grammar and verification examples are ancestral structure, not discovered sagacity.

**Minimal implementation / scaling / kill.** Eight primitive edits, at most 16 rules, strings of length at most eight. Next increase: a new composition grammar and independently encoded interpreter. Park if savings vanish against fixed macros at matched correctness and lifecycle cost, or if retained examples rather than executable rules explain transfer.

### A02. Developmental Relay

**Identity and reason for selection.** A small local constructor grows a body whose topology performs useful transformations. Family: developmental distributed computation. Selected because the acquired architecture is not present at birth.

**Causal mechanism.** Each cell has a local symbol state, bounded ports, a material reserve and a small shared construction program. On a message arrival it applies a local rewrite, consumes material, and may create, connect, differentiate or dissolve an adjacent cell. Congestion and local concentration affect construction; no cell sees the full graph. Repeated traffic stabilizes some paths through a local maintenance rule, while unused paths decay. The execution graph is also the grown body. Random local proposals and asynchronous arrival order supply variability. Construction and pruning are slower than message transformation.

**Information boundaries.** Cells observe incident messages, adjacent contacts and their reserve. They receive no coordinates, global topology, developmental age or desired circuit. The initial kernel is inherited; mature body persists across probes. Resetting a probe clears mobile messages, not legitimate grown topology; a birth reset rebuilds from the kernel and fresh material.

**Learning/change.** Local parameters, graph topology, cell roles and material distribution change through development. No task-specific program injection occurs. **Memory:** acquired connectivity and differentiated rules; writes are construction, retrieval is traversal, decay is loss of maintenance. Transplant a whole connected body with ports, or a declared compatible region, not an arbitrary list of nodes. Body organization itself changes.

**Search/economy.** Growth opens new routes; pruning recovers material. Escaping a bad morphology requires local dismantling and regrowth, not free checkpoint return. Competing growth fronts consume the same finite reserve. There need be no explicit task reward; useful traffic and physical maintenance define the designed selection pressure, which must be disclosed.

**Smallest phenomenon / cheap impostor.** A developed topology implements a reusable two-stage transformation on new traffic. Shorter routes, higher bandwidth or a seeded construction recipe could explain the score without cognitive structure.

**Bounded falsification assay.** In tiny symbol-routing worlds, cross identical kernels with two mature topologies and fresh material layouts. Compare a sham-rewired body, a degree/length/bandwidth-matched scrambled topology, and a hand-built relay positive. All worlds randomize exterior port labels. Primary signature: transfer of the mature topology transfers the held-out transformation, whereas matched scrambling removes it while preserving basic packet delivery; reconstructing the relevant motif rescues it. Distinguish body capacity from endogenous construction by logging the complete growth trajectory and retaining all founders.

**Observability/interventions/provenance.** Record parentage of every cell and edge, enabling message, material source and local program version. Support event-cut snapshots including in-flight messages, topology transplants, growth freezing, link lesions and material clamps. A global developmental organizer would be part of the organism, not invisible scaffolding.

**Minimal implementation / scaling / kill.** One kernel, at most 32 cells, two symbol operations, no lineage selection. Next: recombine independently developed regions under independently permuted port systems. Park if a fixed equal-resource relay or simple shortest-path growth reproduces the alleged transformation, or if exterior labels supply the mapping.

### A03. Catalytic Treaty Ecology

**Identity and reason for selection.** Computation is maintained by reciprocal transformations across compartments and their medium. Family: population chemistry. Selected to challenge the assumption that one organism contains the cognitive mechanism.

**Causal mechanism.** A compartment contains reaction rules, local inventories and a membrane permeability pattern. Encounters convert bounded tokens and release products; some products enable reactions in other compartments. Replication consumes material and copies rules with rare edits. Production cycles sustain members; unused compartments dissolve. Update order follows local reaction hazards, not a global round. Fast reactions, slower membrane changes and still slower reproduction create distinct timescales. Conservation laws and transport limit growth.

**Information boundaries.** Each member sees its interior and contacting extracellular material only. No species receives global fitness, task labels or the complete population count. The assay output is sampled through fixed world-side product sensors. Population and medium may persist together; wash-medium, replace-members and full-ecosystem resets are different operations.

**Learning/change and memory.** Rule mutation, membrane modification, births, deaths and coalition composition change. Memory may be in inherited rules, stable composition, circulating intermediates or altered medium. Retrieval is encounter-mediated reaction, not a database call. Decay follows dilution and material loss. Transplants separately transfer members, medium, or both; a claim of collective memory must specify which.

**Search/economy.** New reaction chains arise through local variation and migration. Persistence follows productive resource closure under supplied physics, not a central reproduction score. Cooperative cycles may trap the ecology; finite migration introduces alternatives at an explicit cost. There is no organism-accessible restoration.

**Smallest phenomenon / cheap impostor.** A discovered multi-compartment chain performs a new input-to-output transformation without any one member doing the whole job. A dominant specialist, leaked intermediate answer, or central replenishment schedule may counterfeit collective cognition.

**Bounded falsification assay.** Use a small token chemistry with independently checked reaction semantics. Cross intact members with fresh/conditioned medium; sever one nominated exchange while replacing its maintenance contribution using non-enabling tokens matched under the declared resource law. Specify that law and the matched resource vector before execution: equal token counts alone do not establish equal maintenance or reaction potential. Compare a transport sham, a restored exchange, isolated members, a constant product emitter and a hand-built two-member positive. Primary signature: the transformation needs the nominated cross-member dependency, survives namespace changes and returns on selective rescue, while general survival and the nominated resource vector remain matched. Stop calling it collective if a single equal-budget compartment performs the whole transformation through the same accessible reactions.

**Observability/interventions/provenance.** Record molecule/token origins, reactions, diffusion, replication, rules and material transfers, including unobserved sampling limits. Support member/medium factorial transplants, selective permeability edits and flux clamps. Founders, reaction laws and external supply are explicit ancestry; the assay cannot infer absolute chemical origin.

**Minimal implementation / scaling / kill.** Four compartments, six token types, twelve local reactions; designed positive before unseeded search. Next: independent lineage mixing with incompatible protocols. Park if output depends only on total resource, one species, supplied labels or evaluator-maintained population proportions. If flux matching cannot preserve viability, the severing assay is unqualified.

### A04. Commitment Fuse Garden

**Identity and reason for selection.** Adaptation decides which alternatives to make costly to reopen. Family: selective irreversibility. Selected to test whether useful cognition can depend on controlled loss of reversibility.

**Causal mechanism.** State is a graph of provisional transformations, finite fuse material and committed edges. A local rule may explore alternative continuations, erase a provisional edge cheaply, or bind it using material. Unbinding a committed edge requires a fixed costly reconstruction path; it is not logically impossible in every realization. Local conflict and reuse traces influence binding proposals. Fast exploration precedes slower commitment and rare repair. The commitment selector can change through a bounded rule-edit process; random proposals are the only stochastic input besides world noise.

**Information boundaries.** The selector sees local conflict/reuse history and material, never future regime changes, evaluator approval or task IDs. Commitments persist across interruptions. Probe reset clears temporary proposals, not bound structure; founder reset removes both. Investigator restoration is forbidden as an organism action.

**Learning/change and memory.** Graph edges, commitment rules and their thresholds change; irreversible bonds write memory, traversals retrieve it, costly reconstruction deletes it. The graph may be transplanted with its bound-state and material debt. There is no extra hidden copy of discarded alternatives. Commitment timing is itself retained policy, not a hand-authored schedule.

**Search/economy.** Branching uses mutable regions; commitment reduces branching and interference but creates traps. Reserve material allows occasional escape through dismantling. The candidate must pay for lost options as well as saved maintenance. No cost advantage is assumed from merely labeling an operation irreversible.

**Smallest phenomenon / cheap impostor.** Acquired commitment timing protects a reusable transformation through interruption without preventing later repair. Hysteresis, a fixed save interval, ordinary read-only memory or simply fewer writes may be enough.

**Bounded falsification assay.** Present alternating stable and changed local laws with no boundary marker. Compare acquired commitment, reversible storage with matched capacity/costs, fixed thresholds, and temporally shuffled commitments matched for number and locations; use a hand-designed selective-commitment positive and always/never-commit negatives. Primary signature: learned placement/timing preserves the nominated transformation during stable interference and repairs it after real change, beyond matched fixed/shuffled policies. Include identical-data histories in which only interruption order changes. If benefit exists only because the world grants an arbitrary discount to fused storage, report a storage-economics effect rather than superior reasoning.

**Observability/interventions/provenance.** Record every surrendered option, fuse expenditure, trigger evidence and repair path. Support intervention on selector rules separately from committed contents, equal-damage shams, debt-preserving transplant and world-side interruption replay. Initial bond templates are imported structure.

**Minimal implementation / scaling / kill.** Sixteen graph edges, four fuses, two hidden regime changes. Next: distributed commitment with conflicts between regions. Park if fixed hysteresis matches the full stability/repair frontier, if extra storage explains the benefit, or if rescue requires a free hidden backup.

### A05. Counterfactual Residue Workshop

**Identity and reason for selection.** A machine learns reusable corrections to its own imagined trajectories. Family: internal simulation; familiar, but the simulator is neither perfect nor supplied. Selected to distinguish organism counterfactuals from laboratory forks.

**Causal mechanism.** State contains a locally constructed transition model M, a bounded branch workspace B, discrepancy records E and correction programs R. From observed transitions, propose small model rules. Copy M and local represented state into B, alter one accessible assumption, run a bounded branch, then compare committed predictions with later observations. Compress recurring discrepancies into correction programs and retire harmful ones. Branch scheduling and model edits are stochastic; real interaction, simulated events and correction promotion have separate counters and costs.

**Information boundaries.** Only ordinary observations train M. No branch queries the real world's hidden state, seed or transition function. Model coordinates are private inventions. Across tasks retain M/R only as declared; erase imagined task answers and branch buffers. Forks of the laboratory world remain investigator-only. Creating B copies only organism-accessible represented state through ordinary charged reads/writes in the minimal realization; account for all bytes copied and branch execution. This is not access to investigator capture or an unmetered semantic clone service.

**Learning/change and memory.** Numeric estimates, model rules, branch topology and executable corrections can change. R is memory: written from discrepancies, addressed by internally learned failure conditions, retrieved during simulation, pruned after harmful use. Transplant R separately from M and histories; portability requires declared model interfaces, with adaptation work counted.

**Search/economy.** Exploration can buy real probes or run fallible branches. A corrected model may open new branches but never makes an unreachable real state reachable. Predictions made before observations determine whether a correction saves future cost; internal confidence alone cannot reward it.

**Smallest phenomenon / cheap impostor.** One acquired correction prevents the same class of simulation error on a new layout. More simulation, a perfect host model, a running residual mean or replay of known trajectories are primary alternatives.

**Bounded falsification assay.** Use small worlds where a locally visible mechanism changes the result of certain compositions. Cross M with learned/erased R and matched/unmatched histories; compare equal-compute extra rollouts, constant residual correction, scrambled R and a planted corrector. Commit branch predictions before the real transition. Primary signature: R transfers a selective improvement on held-out compositions, deletion recreates the predicted error, and rescue restores the correction without importing world states. Test an adversarial reversal where the old correction must be rejected, not blindly applied.

**Observability/interventions/provenance.** Log every branch's parent model, assumptions, prediction time, later observation and correction edit; record unsuccessful branches. Support model/corrector swaps and clamping of branch counts. Trace all model knowledge to allowed observations or disclosed initial priors.

**Minimal implementation / scaling / kill.** Eight-state learned models, at most four simultaneous branches and four correction programs. Next: transferring correction constructors across model representations, not merely across seeds. Park if extra compute or constant residuals reproduce the result, if correction is layout memorization, or if any simulated branch reads laboratory truth.

### A06. Return-Path Loom

**Identity and reason for selection.** A searcher acquires executable routes for returning to useful states and branching again. Family: search with learned return. Selected because a useful archive is not the same as the ability to reach its contents.

**Causal mechanism.** State consists of partial local landmarks, action traces, return programs and uncertainty estimates. Upon reaching a potentially reusable configuration, the machine compresses its path into a condition-checked return procedure. It attempts physical returns, repairing failed subpaths from new observations. Exploration chooses an affordable return and a novel continuation. Topology is an organism-built landmark graph, not the true world graph. Random exploration competes with planned revisitation; route repair is slower than action execution.

**Information boundaries.** Landmarks derive from local observation histories; no unique world-state IDs, coordinates or hidden map. The world can change while a route is stored. Across tasks retain reusable procedures only if the boundary permits them; specific world answers and locations are erased for transfer. Environmental marks count as external memory and must be separately controlled.

**Learning/change and memory.** Programs, landmark distinctions and route graph change. Writes compress executed paths; retrieval matches fallible local conditions; unsuccessful routes decay. Programs can be transplanted, but world-specific landmarks may not align. No reproduction is required; no host snapshot is callable.

**Search/economy.** A return consumes actions and can fail. Archives preserve procedures, not reachable states by fiat. Reachability traps are escaped only when a lawful return path exists; irreversible worlds may defeat the architecture. Prices include exploration used to acquire the path, storage and every failed return.

**Smallest phenomenon / cheap impostor.** Learned return enables discovery beyond a bottleneck under a fixed action budget. Free teleportation, repeated seeds, externally named landmarks or an ordinary breadth-first archive could explain apparent advance.

**Bounded falsification assay.** Construct finite worlds with aliased local views, bottlenecks, and matched variants with/without a physical return route. Compare route deletion while preserving landmark/archive bytes, scrambled procedures of equal length, simple backtracking, equal-budget archive search and a known route positive. Primary signature: acquired routes yield more verified new reachable states per total action in the return-possible worlds, lose that gain after route severing, and correctly fail rather than teleport in return-impossible worlds. Independently enumerate reachability only for the investigator.

**Observability/interventions/provenance.** Log actual paths, all return failures, acquired landmark discriminators and environmental markings. Support route/landmark factorial swaps, physical edge lesions, and investigator replay with the organism's archive held fixed. World graph and certification procedure remain inaccessible.

**Minimal implementation / scaling / kill.** Thirty-two world states, four route slots, aliased observations. Next: changing worlds where route repair must transfer. Park if benefits disappear when return costs and landmark aliasing are included, or if simple equal-resource backtracking matches discovery.

### A07. Addressless Memory Weave

**Identity and reason for selection.** A machine constructs the addressing scheme by which its memories become usable. Family: relational/executable memory. Selected to reject a free fixed memory API as a neutral laboratory assumption.

**Causal mechanism.** Records occupy an initially unlabelled contact graph. Each record holds payload, local links and bounded rewrite rules for propagating a probe. A probe may split, accumulate a local discriminator and return through traversed links. Repeated collisions cause local relinking or invention of a longer discriminator; useful paths compress into retrieval procedures. No service accepts a global key. Random contact proposals provide exploration; retrieval events are fast, index construction and decay slower.

**Information boundaries.** A process sees contacted records and returned probe outcomes, not host addresses or all contents. The host can identify objects for logging, but those IDs never enter probes. Payload, links and retrieval rules are separate retention facets. A payload-only reset and an index-only reset answer different questions.

**Learning/change and memory.** Records are created/deleted; links, discriminator programs and aliases change. Writing requires locating or constructing a place; reading requires paid traversal. Decay removes unmaintained links and records. Transplantation may carry the retrieval machinery with or without contents. The organization itself is learned; stable virtual pointers are not silently inherited.

**Search/economy.** Exploration proposes contacts, discriminator tests and new link paths. A failed query costs the traversed work. Capacity pressure selects economical retrieval, but a complete linear scan remains a lawful, serious baseline. Investigator restore is unavailable to queries.

**Smallest phenomenon / cheap impostor.** An acquired addressing procedure remains useful after record relocation and partial namespace change. Exact hashing, a hidden object ID, host nearest-neighbor search or a fixed associative memory could supply the whole effect.

**Bounded falsification assay.** Store 32 records under independently generated local encodings. Cross learned links/procedures with original/permuted contents; relocate physical nodes while preserving the declared contact relation. Compare equal-capacity linear scan, a disclosed fixed content-addressed algorithm, random links and a hand-built distributed index. Primary signature: transfer of constructed retrieval machinery preserves correct queries at at least 20% lower total query-plus-construction cost than the named fixed comparators, at matched correctness over a registered horizon; semantic misalignment selectively breaks it, whereas pure host-address permutation does not. Query withheld relations, not just exact repeated payloads. If a fixed index dominates, report acquired indexing but no architectural advantage.

**Observability/interventions/provenance.** Record contacts, probe paths, collisions, creation of discriminators and their developmental inputs. Support independent payload/link/procedure scrambles and host-address renaming. Mark any imported tokenizer or comparison primitive as inherited structure.

**Minimal implementation / scaling / kill.** Thirty-two records, degree at most four, probes of at most sixteen hops. Next: independent organisms exchanging records without shared initial namespaces. Park if host renaming changes results, if cost savings ignore index construction, or if stable supplied IDs explain transfer.

### A08. Boundary-Inventing Fabric

**Identity and reason for selection.** A machine invents internal objects by constructing boundaries that support reusable interventions. Family: changing ontology. Selected because fixed object types would predetermine its claimed discovery.

**Causal mechanism.** The organism starts with a local event/contact graph, not parsed objects. It proposes a cut, a compression of the cut's interior and a provisional interface. It then predicts consequences of accessible perturbations inside/outside that cut. A boundary survives only if its interface predicts the registered effects at lower total description/intervention cost. Merge, split and interface-rewrite operations create new internal types. Boundary proposals are stochastic; prediction is faster than ontology revision. Multiple incompatible cuts may coexist within a finite budget.

**Information boundaries.** Only local events and paid perturbation outcomes are visible. The evaluator's true objects, factorization and category labels remain hidden. Host objects used to implement the world are not organism objects. Retain constructors and interfaces across transfer; remove source-instance segmentations and named entities.

**Learning/change and memory.** Graphs, executable interfaces, object constructors and permissible compositions change. Memory is the procedure that rebuilds a boundary and uses its interface, not just a cluster label. Writes promote tested cuts; retrieval reruns the constructor; contradictory interventions trigger deletion or splitting. Transplant constructors into raw new worlds; no analyst-created alignment is free.

**Search/economy.** Explore different coarse-grainings and pay to distinguish them experimentally. Boundary archives preserve rival descriptions, but finding a useful new cut requires search. Compression alone is insufficient: arbitrary cuts may compress while failing intervention predictions.

**Smallest phenomenon / cheap impostor.** An acquired object-construction rule supports a new correct compositional intervention prediction. A fixed segmentation, correlation cluster, supplied parser or stable location cue could manufacture object discovery.

**Bounded falsification assay.** Use small coupled mechanisms with visually/statistically similar traces but different intervention boundaries. Transfer the constructor with source labels erased. Compare fixed partitions, correlation clustering, random cuts matched for size/connectivity, and a planted correct boundary constructor. Primary signature: the acquired constructor identifies a portable interventional unit and predicts a held-out composition; cutting the inferred interface selectively breaks that prediction, with a matched unrelated cut leaving it intact. Surface-symbol and geometry permutations must preserve the result; change the actual coupling and the boundary should change. If two ontologies are interventionally equivalent, retain both rather than award the evaluator's preferred name.

**Observability/interventions/provenance.** Record proposed/rejected cuts, evidence for each interface, versioned constructors and every schema migration. Support safe executable-interface changes and tests across old/new schemas. Record imported parsing/comparison machinery. External syntax validation cannot certify semantic truth.

**Minimal implementation / scaling / kill.** Twelve primitive processes and at most four invented composite types. Next: nested objects that require inventing a new operator on an invented type. Park if a fixed partition explains all transfer or if an evaluator-provided alignment is necessary. An unidentifiable ontology claim is narrowed, not resolved by prettier labels.

### A09. Viability Constitution Collective [future pressure]

**Identity and reason for selection.** A population of internal processes changes the rules deciding what the system preserves. Family: endogenous priorities under explicit external viability. Selected to avoid equating every objective with a scalar reward, while admitting designer influence.

**Causal mechanism.** Processes share several non-substitutable reserves through locally enforced permits. Each process carries a preservation proposal: a condition, protected resource/channel, expiry rule and bounded challenge procedure. Local shortfalls trigger proposals; interacting processes may ratify or revoke permits through an explicit threshold protocol. Ratification rules can themselves be edited within a fixed safety envelope. Reserve dynamics constrain which constitutions remain viable. Random encounter order and proposal mutation matter; resource use is fast, constitutional change slow. No host optimization of an internal utility is performed.

**Information boundaries.** Processes see their reserve contacts and received permits, not total future survival or an evaluator ranking. External death/resource laws are fixed and disclosed as designed pressure. Across probes preserve constitutions but refill reserves identically to separate rule from stock. Full reset recreates founders and erases permits in flight.

**Learning/change and memory.** Preservation criteria, allocation rules, message protocols and coalition composition change; optional reproduction copies them. Memory consists of executable permits and constitutional dependencies. It is written by ratification, retrieved when resources are requested, and removed by expiry/revocation. A transplant includes validation rules, not merely a string saying "preserve X."

**Search/economy.** Competing constitutions allocate scarce attempts to different activities. There is no guarantee their endogenous priorities are useful or aligned with a task; parasitic preservation is a possible result. Returning to an old constitution requires retained copies and paid migration. No claim is made that all external selection has vanished.

**Smallest phenomenon / cheap impostor.** Experience changes a persistent internal preservation rule that causally changes allocation in a new resource regime. Fixed homeostasis, a hidden scalar score or starting reserve differences may explain apparent endogenous objectives.

**Bounded falsification assay.** Cross constitutions from two developmental histories with equalized reserves in a third regime. Compare fixed thresholds, an ordinary adaptive allocator with equal information, random permit changes and a planted history-dependent constitution. Primary signature: allocation priorities follow the transplanted rules rather than donor reserve levels, survive superficial resource renaming and adapt when formerly useful protection becomes harmful. Clamp constitutional edits while allowing routine allocation to distinguish rule change from state dynamics. This detects acquired priorities, not free will or goals without designer ancestry.

**Observability/interventions/provenance.** Log every ratification, challenge, rejected proposal, reserve transfer and rule version with local evidence. Support rule/stock factorial transplantation and protocol clamps. Treat the safety envelope, original ratification law and death criteria as imported objectives or constraints.

**Minimal implementation / scaling / kill.** Four processes, three reserves, two editable preservation clauses; no unrestricted governance rewrite. Next: independently developed protocols negotiating scarce resources. Park the frontier claim if a fixed or conventional adaptive allocator explains the changes, if only reserve state transfers, or if defining success requires an undeclared evaluator reward.

### A10. Experimental Obligation Ledger

**Identity and reason for selection.** A machine acquires reusable procedures for choosing informative interventions. Family: internal science/programmed experiment design. Selected to distinguish knowing facts from improving how facts are obtained.

**Causal mechanism.** Candidate explanations are executable small programs. Each registers a prediction and a condition under which it would be wrong. An obligation graph connects incompatible predictions. The organism constructs candidate probes using legal actions, simulates their predicted outcomes under its own explanations, and buys probes expected to separate live alternatives. Actual outcomes remove or revise programs. Repeated successful probe constructions become reusable experiment operators. Stochasticity enters proposal order; observation, explanation revision and operator promotion occur at different timescales.

**Information boundaries.** The graph contains the organism's own predictions, not a host-provided disagreement or truth score. Explanations may all be wrong. Sensors expose only purchased outcomes. Across worlds retain probe constructors, not hidden-law identities or answer tables; no evaluator correctness feedback appears until lawful world observations provide it.

**Learning/change and memory.** Explanations, obligations and experiment programs are created, recombined and deleted. Memory is an executable procedure for constructing a discriminating action plus its domain conditions. Internal tokens address it; matching is paid. Expired procedures are removed. Transplant experiment operators separately from accumulated factual beliefs.

**Search/economy.** Exploration changes what can be known, not just the candidate answer. Every failed or redundant probe is charged. A finite hypothesis list is an initial inductive bias, not a discovered ontology. An expanded version may construct new hypotheses, but that is not assumed in the minimal test.

**Smallest phenomenon / cheap impostor.** An acquired probe constructor distinguishes a new pair of hidden mechanisms with fewer observations. A hardcoded diagnostic sequence, uncertainty heuristic, exhaustive enumeration or leaked law ID may suffice.

**Bounded falsification assay.** Use tiny causal circuits with observationally equivalent but interventionally different members. Cross transferred probe constructors with blank/copied factual ledgers. Compare random probes, a fixed optimal sequence for the training family, a generic information-gain policy and a planted useful constructor. Primary signature: transferred constructor/blank facts identifies a held-out circuit distinction with at least 20% fewer paid probes than the named fixed/generic comparators at matched correctness, while deleting it removes the gain and restoring facts alone does not. Charge development probes across the registered reuse horizon and report compute separately. Adversarial worlds where its usual probe is nondiagnostic test whether it checks its own applicability. Investigator enumeration certifies the tiny contrast, never feeds the organism.

**Observability/interventions/provenance.** Log predictions before probes, rejected hypotheses, all costs, constructor origins and exposure. Support facts/procedure swaps, sensor clamps, sham deletions and exact finite replay. Supplied hypothesis grammar and intervention vocabulary are counted priors.

**Minimal implementation / scaling / kill.** Six binary components, at most eight hypotheses and four experiment procedures. Next: construct a new intervention operator rather than select from a fixed menu. Park if copied facts or a conventional information-gain policy explains the effect at equal cost, or if confirmation law identities leak.

### A11. Traveling-Defect Computer [future pressure]

**Identity and reason for selection.** Useful computation is carried by moving defects and their interaction with an acquired material guide structure. Family: spatial dynamical computation. Selected to resist both fixed memory modules and universal exact replay.

**Causal mechanism.** A bounded lattice/medium has local phase-like state, coupling strengths and slowly changing pinning sites. Local dynamics propagate excitations; winding/defect patterns can survive after raw inputs pass. Collisions or boundary encounters alter subsequent propagation. Slow local plasticity changes a coupling only after a specified local flux pattern and consumes material. The native update law, boundary conditions, noise and numerical approximation are explicit. Fast propagation and slow guide formation are different clocks; the organism receives neither a global phase reference nor decoded defect labels.

**Information boundaries.** Inputs act through local boundary contacts; outputs are fixed local sensors, not a global reconstruction fitted after success. Hidden medium state is investigator-visible only. Guides can persist across probes; mobile excitations may be washed out or intentionally retained. Those resets cannot be conflated. External real-time timing, if sensed, is a declared channel.

**Learning/change and memory.** Couplings, pinning topology and defect distribution change. Memory is a causally persistent material configuration, written by local flux and retrieved by later propagation. It decays through noise, relaxation or resource loss. Transplant may require moving an entire region and its boundary preparation; detachable symbolic memory is not assumed. No program reproduction is required.

**Search/economy.** Input-driven local remodeling explores guide configurations. It cannot inspect all possibilities or restore a prior physical medium. Pinning can trap dynamics; controlled resource pulses permit local dissolution. Compare energy/material/volume and native time separately; do not turn them into invented "equivalent tokens."

**Smallest phenomenon / cheap impostor.** A trained guide mediates reuse of an input-order distinction under new local pulse sequences. Fixed resonance, elapsed-time detection, bulk heating, extra energy or a powerful readout may explain the effect; useful material memory is not automatically reasoning.

**Bounded falsification assay.** First use a finite local model with an independently checked defect diagnostic. Before execution, specify the transition law, defect invariant, input work/energy functional and a feasible matched-perturbation construction. Their existence is an open design obligation, not an established property of an unspecified lattice. Compare trained, sham-trained and fixed-resonator media; relocate or annihilate the nominated defect while matching injected work under that declared functional and preserving basic transmission. Include a work-matched bulk perturbation, a designed defect-mediated positive and a readout-only baseline. If the model has no defensible energy interpretation, match and report its declared pulse/amplitude/update resource vector and narrow the claim; do not call equal pulse counts equal physical energy. Primary signature: the held-out sequence distinction follows the nominated guide/defect intervention and selective rescue, not resource injection or elapsed time. Randomize input timing within the declared tolerance. An external defect label must never become an organism observation.

**Observability/interventions/provenance.** Record boundaries, field resolution, solver/version, tolerances, injected work and measured traces. A simulated matched twin is not a physical clone. For noisy physical realizations replace exact replay with registered distributional tests; those require new power/qualification work and are outside this first screen. Support boundary-preserving regional perturbations and parameter preparation, not fictitious surgery on a named memory variable.

**Minimal implementation / scaling / kill.** A 16 by 16 local discrete medium, two boundary input sites and one fixed sensor; the common 64-component cap is replaced by 256 cells, with the same 4,096-event cap. First demonstrate the designed positive and diagnostic before unguided remodeling. Next: unlike numerical and physical realizations. Park if the effect disappears under fixed readout/timing controls or if it cannot be separated from heating/resonance. Inability to intervene selectively blocks the mechanistic claim, not the medium's existence.

### A12. Discovery-Rule Foundry [future pressure]

**Identity and reason for selection.** A machine changes the procedure by which it discovers and packages further operators. Family: executable meta-search. Selected as a bounded approach toward recursive sagacity, not evidence that strong recursion has been solved.

**Causal mechanism.** State comprises operator library L, candidate generator P, internal trial selector T, packaging rule K and versioned modification proposals. P generates bounded transformations; T buys internal tests; K decides what to retain with applicability conditions. An edit can change P/T/K. A sandbox compares the edited discovery procedure with the old one on organism-accessible development problems, charging all trials. An external immutable evaluator later tests new operators on concealed tasks. Promotion inside the organism can be wrong; the laboratory does not repair it silently. Program execution is fast, operator search slower, discovery-rule revision slowest.

**Information boundaries.** Editable code cannot read confirmation tasks, external expected answers, custodian state or evaluator code. Development outcomes are lawful inputs; selection on them is acknowledged. Across transfer, move the proposed discovery-rule delta while replacing L with the same baseline library and resetting random/search workspaces. Any literal, table or executable dependency inside the delta remains potential answer cargo.

**Learning/change and memory.** Programs, proposal distributions, testing rules and packaging conventions change; new operators are created and recombined. Version history is executable memory, addressed internally and deleted subject to paid rollback constraints. Reproduction is optional. Transplants must close executable dependencies and charge the whole transferred description, not just a short patch.

**Search/economy.** Branching occurs over both candidate operators and discovery procedures. An organism may retain paid backups; investigator restore is not its rollback service. Persisting procedures are those it predicts will improve useful discovery per total cost. Count failed searches, meta-development, verification and library maintenance. Self-reported progress is not a scientific metric.

**Smallest phenomenon / cheap impostor.** A developmental change to P/T/K improves discovery of new useful operators after old operator cargo is removed. A fixed better enumerator, more compute, trained family priors, a concealed library or compiler specialization is a plausible explanation. A learned search prior may be a genuine narrow result; it is not thereby strong recursive improvement.

**Bounded falsification assay.** Cross old/new discovery procedure with baseline/developed libraries; use independent initial search streams, semantic literal scrambling where valid, equal-description fixed proposers and an independently authored hand-improved proposer positive. Retain library-only transfer as an explicit negative for changed discovery. Freeze all code before withheld families. Primary signature: the new-procedure/baseline-library arm discovers externally verified new operators at least 20% more cheaply at matched correctness than old procedure and the best named fixed comparator; swapping back P/T/K removes that gain. Repeat on a second fresh family without allowing confirmation feedback into promotion. Each family receives its own 24-block screen, up to twelve arms and 4,096 events per arm; require the common decision gates independently in both, without pooling passes. Thus A12's two-screen ceiling is 2,359,296 native events, excluding separately capped qualification/verification. A finite grammar can be exhaustively checked for the permitted cargo class; no universal semantic cargo detector is claimed.

**Observability/interventions/provenance.** Log complete program ancestry, candidate failures, internal/external test separation, dependency closure and budget transfers. Support executable version freezing, selective swaps and evaluator isolation. A flattened program preserving interventions and costs is an equivalent realization, not a counterfeit solely because it lacks nested labels.

**Minimal implementation / scaling / kill.** One tiny terminating rewrite language, eight baseline operators, one editable proposal/test rule and one modification round; keep each arm within the common budget. If that cannot admit the known positive, redesign the assay before search. Next: two successive acquired changes improving further discovery under fresh custody. Even that supports bounded iterative discovery improvement, not an unbounded RSE. Park if library cargo, a named fixed enumerator or total lifecycle costs explain the result; retain DETECTION_UNQUALIFIED for strong recursion.

## 7. Minimal architecture-neutral Observatory substrate

### 7.1 What the substrate actually represents

Represent a **declared native experimental system**, not necessarily `(world, agent)` with a fixed partition. An ownership/access relation partitions or overlaps native state among participants, environment and joint structures. The investigator may know more than any participant, but that knowledge has no feedback path by default.

Each runtime declares state domains, transition/scheduling semantics, random inputs, resource units, lawful channels and supported interventions. The shared layer need not understand what a native state means; however, an opaque blob without a capture-completeness argument is insufficient for claims that rely on reset or replay. Semantic claims require qualified native instruments.

The minimal *common* core is: artifact identity and capability declarations; experiment/exposure registration; access/custody enforcement; resource and attempt accounting; provenance and observations; intervention requests with fidelity receipts; and claim-specific evidence dependencies. Capture, physical preparation, arbitrary transplants, endogenous code change and precision models are negotiated native capabilities, not falsely universal operations.

### 7.2 Primitive-by-primitive neutrality audit

| Proposed primitive | Substrate-level justification | How it could manufacture a phenomenon | Removal/generalization decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| State/boundary declaration | Needed to say what changed and who could know it | Privileged split supplies objects or memory compartments | Use native domains plus access relations; no mandatory agent/world anatomy |
| Bounded channels | Needed for causal information claims | A typed message schema supplies the language to be discovered | Enforce transport limits; organism semantics stay native and can change |
| Transition/scheduler contract | Needed to interpret events and replay | Global tick gives synchrony, age or race-order answers | Native partial order; hidden bookkeeping time is not an observation |
| Randomness/exogenous-input custody | Needed to distinguish noise, replay and leakage | Shared seed encodes task identity or aligns processes | Separate registered sources; couple streams only for a justified assay |
| Unit-typed resource account | Needed for honest comparisons | Ledger supplies global energy or reward to the organism | One-way metering; sensing balances is an explicit paid channel |
| Event/provenance record | Needed for traceable ancestry and interventions | Queryable logs become perfect episodic memory | Write-only to investigator; delayed/offline recording and observer checks |
| Capture/fork/restart | Needed by some digital contrasts | Gives return paths, perfect imagination or erasure for free | Investigator-only optional capability; validate native continuation |
| Perturbation/transplant interface | Needed for mechanism tests | An adapter repairs mismatches or gives semantic alignment | Versioned native operations, shams, costs and unsupported responses |
| Measurement and adjudication | Needed to connect events to a claim | Readout computes the answer or trains the organism | Fixed qualified instruments; no feedback unless declared; keep raw evidence |
| Executable confinement | Needed for safely hosting structural change | A whitelist bans new operators; an evaluator approves semantics for free | Restrict external effects/resources, not scientific categories; semantics stay untrusted |
| Lifecycle/preparation contract | Needed for creation, death and lawful persistence | Automatic cleanup becomes a perfect reset; episode marker leaks | Explicit retain/erase/resample operations; continuous systems allowed |
| Capability/claim negotiation | Needed to avoid promises unsupported by a runtime | Admission requires our favorite anatomy or positive result | Reject an inference when unsupported, not automatically the architecture |

Every row is justified by a measurement or custody need, not by supplying an internal cognitive faculty. There is no substrate `learn`, `retrieve`, `plan`, `invent_object`, `choose_goal` or `improve_self` call.

### 7.3 Operational conformance, not checkbox compliance

1. **Access conformance:** inject reachable canaries into forbidden stores, IDs, queues and paired channels. Verify that lawful outputs do not acquire forbidden dependence under the registered finite/noisy criterion. A passed set is bounded evidence, not proof of all physical noninterference.
2. **Continuation conformance:** capture at a causally closed cut, including in-flight messages and external-state dependencies. Test exact continuation where justified; otherwise test registered observable distributions. Do not silently substitute final-score agreement for trace fidelity.
3. **Reset conformance:** distinguish restoration of the old past from erasure of forbidden past. A clean twin with allowed state retained must preserve useful behavior. Include delayed and split-channel leakage.
4. **Intervention conformance:** check locality, off-target damage, resource effects and sham fidelity. Invalid surgery cannot falsify the machine. Replacing a region and preparing a comparable region are different procedures.
5. **Observer conformance:** compare observer off/minimal/full under native traces, scheduler effects, RNG usage and backpressure. Full capture may be impossible without disturbance; disclose that ceiling.
6. **Ancestry conformance:** account for founders, imported primitives, world generators, search grammars, development, rejected variants, decoder training and experimenter selection. Dependency records do not prove necessity; targeted contrasts do.
7. **Resource conformance:** no free copy, undeclared parallel search or uncounted host solver. Report native actions, compute, memory, communication, construction and evaluation as a vector. Register exchange rates only for a particular comparison.
8. **Assay conformance:** a known narrow positive must be admitted and the named impostor refused without looking at candidate outcomes. Unsupported primitives cap affected claims; no positive result is required to host an architecture.

**The twelve are a conformance suite in the following sense:** each demands at least one representable positive, counterfeit, sham, intervention and reset contrast with the stated information boundaries. They do not require twelve successful learning discoveries before the Observatory may operate. A hardcoded imitation of the assay output is not conformance.

### 7.4 What neutrality cannot mean

No computational substrate is assumption-free. Locality, precision, instruction sets and costs favor some mechanisms. The defensible claim is **documented bias with successful counterexamples on a stated panel**, not universal neutrality. If simulating A11 in a digital runtime changes its economics, report separate native and simulation costs. If exact A03/A09 ownership is contested, run alternate boundaries; do not force a single agent definition to simplify accounting.

## 8. Dangerous abstractions

| Accidental framework | Interface decision causing it | More neutral alternative and falsification probe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RL | Mandatory observation/action/reward/terminal loop | Native events and optional feedback; demonstrate A03 without scalar reward |
| Neural network | State is a tensor and learning is an optimizer step | Native graph/program/field domains; transfer A01 rule bodies without weights |
| LLM agent | Text transcript, tools and central orchestration are universal | Typed or raw local transport with no compulsory controller; A02 has no transcript |
| Fixed memory | Every organism receives key/value get/put | Native state and declared contacts; A07 must invent usable addressing |
| Synchronous CA | Every component updates once per global tick | Local scheduling and causal order; perturb A03 encounter order lawfully |
| Episodic task | Reset and `done` define every lifetime | Explicit intervention boundaries, continuous worlds; test A09 without terminal feedback |
| Global clock | Host timestamps/episode IDs enter observations | Hidden audit time plus declared local timing; rename/rephase without leaking A06 landmarks |
| Reward-centric | Persistence or reproduction follows a shared fitness scalar | Native resource/viability laws; compare A09 rules without giving evaluator utility |
| Single-agent | Only one private state and one actuator set | Overlapping ownership and population interfaces; A03 requires joint-state accounting |
| Fixed topology | Component/port schema must be frozen at launch | Resource-bounded lifecycle and versioned interfaces; A02 growth must remain legal |

Additional dangers: automatic replay caches, host canonicalization, free hash lookup, a universal symbolic planner, automatic module discovery, a single scalar intelligence leaderboard, a human decoder fitted after outcomes, and retrospective deletion of failed founders. A convenient API can be the entire mechanism being claimed.

Do not solve neutrality by hiding these services behind generic names such as "adapter" or "physics." Their information, costs and ancestry remain part of the experiment. An external world law may legitimately do computation; whether the claim is organism cognition, joint cognition or world assistance then has to change.

## 10. Future-pressure architectures

Three deep candidates deliberately exceed near-term convenience:

- **A09:** separately developing processes may invent incompatible preservation languages. A laboratory that requires a fixed goal schema cannot express the claim. The minimal test restricts two clauses for tractability; that restriction must not become a universal contract.
- **A11:** native material history can be distributed, noisy and impossible to clone exactly. Requiring detachable memory and bitwise replay would reject the mechanism before testing it. Approximate replay is not automatically qualified; the unresolved instrumentation problem is part of the result.
- **A12:** new executable discovery rules can change their own internal object types, search grammar and validation procedures. The safety boundary must constrain external effects and resource use without giving their internal correctness to a trusted host. The final scientific evaluator remains outside that boundary.

A02/A03/A08 also exert future pressure through development, asynchronous collective causality and ontology change. Extensions S15/S24/S29 add independently invented protocols, inherited obligations and executable fossils. Open-ended lineage is a data-model requirement, not a promise of infinite computation: run finite prefixes, preserve extinct ancestry, and report horizon-dependent censoring.

Do not reserve an "alien architecture" escape hatch that avoids all tests. Even strange candidates owe a finite observable proposition, a lawful input/output boundary, a counterfeit and an intervention with known limitations. Conversely, lack of our favorite intervention cannot be turned into proof that the mechanism is absent.

## 11. Recommended first implementations

Choose **four small native realizations**, sequentially gated; not a common new framework and not an authorized campaign. Their purpose is to maximize distinct information about instruments and mechanisms.

| Order | Candidate and smallest question | Cost character | Observatory fact learned even from a negative |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A07: does constructed addressing survive host renaming and paid transfer? | Small discrete graph; cheap exact controls | Whether the memory interface already performs retrieval |
| 2 | A04: does commitment timing matter beyond fixed hysteresis and matched storage? | Small finite state/cost model | Whether restoration and resource accounting erase irreversibility |
| 3 | A02: can a grown topology transfer a transformation rather than just throughput? | Small asynchronous event graph; moderate intervention work | Whether dynamic lifecycle and message closure are expressible |
| 4 | A10: does an acquired experiment procedure transfer separately from factual answers? | Small finite causal world; moderate independent-oracle work | Whether task truth, information acquisition and internal testing are separated |

This set covers constructed memory, selective irreversibility, development/locality and internal experimentation. A01 is a useful calibration/control bench but not automatically a fifth frontier build: first check whether it contributes information beyond existing rewrite experiments. A03 is the next alternative if independent review finds A02 insufficient to test collective state. A11 needs an instrumentation feasibility study before search. A12 waits until information custody and executable-transfer assays admit their narrow positives and reject cargo.

**Prospective stop/go sequence:** independently specify the tiny world and named impostor; implement known positive and negative; qualify observer/reset/intervention behavior; freeze the candidate's development rule and confirmation split; run the bounded screen; preserve every attempted founder; publish the entire claim/cost vector. No candidate receives a larger budget because it looks complex. Resource caps and reviewer ownership require operator approval before execution.

**Value of a negative:** A07 matching a fixed index warns against attributing cognition to storage organization; A04 matching hysteresis retires an unnecessary story; A02 failing under topology-preserving controls exposes a throughput artifact; A10 matching ordinary information gain narrows the need for novel internal science. These are useful conclusions, not failures to produce a demonstration.
