1. EXECUTIVE VERDICT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The observatory is the right discipline: shared worlds, shared rulers,
claims tied to a registered cell, nulls that carry a type. Keep that.

As a build specification v0.1 is not ready, for two reasons.

(a) Its section-19 protocol has no arm that separates reuse of what was
    built from improvement of the builder, and it leaves five choices
    open. I registered three settings of those choices and ran designed
    organisms in each. In each, an organism with fixed inherited
    machinery passed: a selector over three procedures (as written, read
    the easy way); a library learner (targets built from parts it had
    just learned); a selector over 32 inherited search orders (targets
    sharing no part with its history; a weaker threshold and weaker
    controls). In two stricter settings none of the organisms I ran
    passes, and nobody has a positive to show the steps would pass
    anything there.
    The portfolio says a ruler that passes library accumulation "is
    insufficient". (Section 11.)

(b) v0.1 rightly withholds the word substrate-neutral until two unlike
    realizations operate correctly, and its own rules imply a designed
    positive per substrate. But three of its definitions depend on the
    organism's shape, and the portfolio's description of the second
    substrate does not give it an organism with a known answer. Without
    one, the test can show that a ruler does not fit. It cannot show
    that a ruler which runs is wrong. (Sections 2, 3.)

3. MAJOR REVISIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REV 1. ONE ENTRY RULE, TWO DOORS. Any physics may be run at any time
    under world-side rulers. Class exclusion needs no model of the
    organism; its harness needs a leak test in each physics. But before
    a null is read, before a physics-specific ruler is trusted, and
    before a search campaign is funded, the physics needs a known
    answer. Door one: a designed positive with a matched impostor. Door
    two: a positive found by blind search and certified by class
    exclusion. Door two matters. With door one alone the rule would
    admit only physics a designer finds legible, which is the bias the
    program wants to escape. A reviewer made that point against my
    draft and it is right.

REV 2. TWO WORLD-SIDE AXES IN PLACE OF SHAPE-DEPENDENT DEFINITIONS.
    ORDER      which regularity is exploited, defined by how the WORLD is
               nested: within a task (1), across tasks of a family (2),
               across families (3), and so on.
    RETENTION  what information survives a declared boundary or
               perturbation and is used afterwards: a curve, and where
               the world has a random key, a number of bits acquired at
               each order.
    ORDER needs nothing from inside the organism. RETENTION under a wait
    or a distraction needs nothing either; under a quench it needs the
    physics to declare what is zeroed. The six names (HOLD to RECURSE)
    stay as shorthand. Mechanism work becomes a separate, optional
    layer. A third question, where a capability came from (chosen among
    inherited alternatives, built by a fixed builder, or built by a
    builder that was itself built), has no behavioural test I can
    defend. I proposed one in a draft and withdrew it (section 11).

REV 3. A CEILING FOR CLAIMS WITHOUT AN EXACT BOUND. 01 section 4.2 asks
    for "exact policy bounds where possible" and allows a fallback to
    "progressively stronger adversarial baseline classes where exact
    enumeration is impossible". The fallback is the weaker half. My
    salvage found no component that computes what a restricted class can
    score in a world where an organism acts, and the record holds both
    faults: baselines omitted, and a "shortcut search over a hand-built
    list of cheap policies" standing in for a bound (fixture F-22). Give
    the fallback a ceiling: with no exact bound a claim may be qualified
    and may not be called robust (C1 on v0.1's ladder; L1 on the one I
    propose in RESPONSE_2 section 4.4).
    This does not confine claims to toy worlds. A planted random key
    gives an exact bound in a world of any richness, because the bound
    is about the key. What it confines, today, is the KIND of
    capability: exact bounds exist for retaining random content across
    a boundary. For structure (compression, composition) the best
    available is a designed reference class, which is weaker. That is
    the main open problem on the measurement side. My plan does not
    solve it, and until it is solved what this tunnel can certify is
    memory.

REV 4. SEARCH IS HALF OF EVERY ARCHITECTURE. The cell has a search
    coordinate, and 01 section 13 speaks of substrate/search
    combinations. The portfolio does not carry that through: only R0
    names a way of producing organisms. Make every entry a pair
    (physics, search regime), with at least three regimes per physics
    and gradient wherever the physics admits it. (Section 8.)

REV 5. W1 WITH EXACT VALUES; W2 DEFERRED. (Section 9.)

REV 6. BOUNDARY FIXED AND CERTIFIED FOR EARLY SCIENCE. (Section 10.)

REV 7. MEASUREMENT INTERFERENCE (Q10). The two planes are sound and
    cheap, and they address the easy part. In a simulation, reading
    state does not disturb it. The observer effects that are real:
    - instrumentation that changes timing, rounding or random streams.
      A twin run with and without instruments catches it. Keep that.
    - selection on ruler outputs. Organisms adapt to the instrument. No
      ruler output may reach the search unless registered as a scaffold.
    - leaks from harness to organism: resets, clocks, counters (the
      Crius block counter that is a clock).
    For chemistry, asynchronous and continuous systems the hard part is
    intervention, not observation:
    - a lesion or transplant in continuous or chaotic dynamics leaves the
      manifold. Every one needs a sham of matched size and an ensemble.
      One trajectory proves nothing.
    - where material is not individuated there is no lineage graph. Use
      passive tracers and twin-run influence maps (the Aether one-bit
      twin assay is the precedent).
    - 01 already names deterministic scheduling for class E2 and
      sensitivity to perturbation and precision for E3. I would make the
      first the only route to E2 for now, and the second a certificate
      per claim: the claim survives a finer step and higher precision.
    - cost: one native currency per physics plus one common currency
      (host instructions or joules), and a flag when a cross-physics
      conclusion flips between them.
    Still too digital: a canonical event stream; lineage reconstruction;
    hooks, if they are implemented as component addresses.

REV 9. THE CLAIM LADDER MIXES STRENGTH OF EVIDENCE WITH KIND OF CLAIM.
    In the file I had its seven rungs have no rule for moving up, and
    two of them ("Developmental/reasoning primitive", "Architectural
    principle") name kinds of thing. Use one ladder for strength
    (observed; qualified; robust; reproduced by another author;
    predicted in advance) and record the kind of claim beside it
    (effect, transfer, mechanism, law). Then a transfer shown from
    behaviour can be robust with no mechanism claimed, and a mechanism
    claim stays what it is. Add an independence level to every claim,
    and these types: PHYSICS_SPECIFIC_RULER, PRICE_DEPENDENT,
    WORLD_UNCLIMBABLE, ADAPTER_DEPENDENT.

REV 10. A WIND TUNNEL NEEDS SIMILARITY. A real tunnel works because
    theory says when a small model predicts the full-size car. The RSO
    has no such theory yet, and 01 itself warns that a tiny exact world
    may make deep development irrational. So report transitions as
    dimensionless ratios (information the world offers per lifetime over
    inherited capacity; lifetime over volatility time; search budget
    over expected hitting time), and schedule one check: a relation
    measured at one scale, predicted for a keyed world ten times larger,
    then run there (section 13, days 60-90).

6. MISSING ARCHITECTURE FAMILIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SELF-REFERENTIAL CONVENTIONAL LEARNER. A network that rewrites its own
   update rule (Schmidhuber 1993; Kirsch and Schmidhuber 2022). It is the
   conventional candidate for nested improvement and belongs inside R0.
2. EXTERNAL-MEMORY LEARNER TRAINED BY GRADIENT. The same physics as R1,
   reached by a different search. It says whether R1's trouble is the
   physics or blind search.
3. CONTENT-ADDRESSABLE MEMORY MACHINE (sparse distributed memory, vector
   symbolic, modern Hopfield). Neither program nor trained network. Its
   capacity theory is known, so capacity certificates are cheap, and it
   degrades gracefully under noise. Demand stays a property of the
   world. It imports human mathematics into the substrate, as R7 does.
   Unscheduled.
4. PHYSICAL LEARNING NETWORK. A flow or resistor network under a
   conservation law, whose conductances change with use (Stern and
   Murugan 2023; Dillavou et al. 2022; Kramar and Alim 2021 for the
   slime-mould case). Memory is the structure of the transport network;
   there is no separate store. Coordination comes from the conservation
   law, with no addressing. Its learning rule is a relative of
   contrastive Hebbian learning, so it is not outside the corpus either.
5. SELECTIONIST LEARNER. Lifetime learning by variation and selection of
   internal replicators (Szilagyi et al. 2016). Development and search
   become one process.
6. REACTIVE AGENT PLUS WRITABLE WORLD. Both as a candidate and as the
   impostor for every claim of internal construction.
7. FIXED MEDIUM PLUS TRAINED READOUT (reservoir). The control for R4 and
   R5: if a random fixed medium with a trained readout matches a
   designed or evolved one, the design or the evolution added nothing.
8. UNRELIABLE COMPONENTS, as a regime applied to any candidate.
   Persistence then has to be built and maintained. For noisy stores,
   channel capacity gives the bound. For noisy processing the theory
   (von Neumann 1956 and later) gives thresholds, not exact bounds. One
   fossil has noise dials (Ananke); no document makes noise a property
   of every component.

Q8, DIRECTLY. The question is about mechanisms that designers would not
invent, not about architectures they would not name. Old genres can
still yield such mechanisms. My own blind search in the most
software-like machine found a builder that works in a way I did not
design: it uses registers that are never written as free constants. So
what makes an unanticipated mechanism likely is less the genre than
three other things: blind variation at scale; physics with constraints
designers reason about badly (locality, conservation, noise,
dissipation); and rulers that certify without requiring the mechanism to
be legible. Is Track B enough? As physics, plausibly. As planned, no: a
small shadow with no search behind it cannot reveal anything. The
architecture I would add for this purpose is number 4: it has laboratory
positives, an exact and cheap equilibrium (a linear solve), and nothing
in it that resembles a program. And the portfolio's foundry is the right
instrument, if what it samples is unnamed local physics and what scores
it is a ruler that reads only behaviour. That is experiment X5, and it
is the second door of the entry rule.

8. SEARCH / REACHABILITY CRITIQUE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Is Scaffold Free-Fall sufficient to diagnose a reachability desert? No.
As I reconstruct it (01 section 14), it starts from one designed
organism, removes parts and measures rediscovery. 01 already asks for
several search operators and for the selectability of intermediates.
What it still measures is the local basin of one plant, approached
backwards. It lacks ascent from typical starts, more than one plant, and
any ground truth for its own estimates.

Evidence from my prototype (prototype/p1_slice, RECEIPT_reach.json): an
8-instruction builder, 24 lineages per cell, 200,000 proposals each.

    recovered of 24      d=1    d=2    d=3    d=8 (empty program)
    margin rule           24     0      0      0
    strict rule            9     1      0      0
    neutral rule           1     0      1      1

  - With ONE instruction missing, recovery was 24, 9 or 1 of 24
    depending only on the acceptance rule. That is the solid finding.
  - The margin rule, best near the plant, recovered nothing further out.
    Descent under it alone would have predicted no reach from scratch.
    One neutral-rule lineage did build from an empty program. One is
    one: 0 and 1 of 24 cannot be told apart.
  - That organism used a different mechanism from the designed one.
    Distance to MY plant was the wrong ruler.
  - 0 of 2,000,000 random programs reached 0.9.
These are small counts on one target (level I1). A desert is a property
of space x operator x acceptance x budget, and one designed plant is not
the space.

Before a substrate null can be read, measure:
  1. Hitting times, not distances. From registered start distributions,
     under a registered search, report how many independent lineages
     reach a certified hit within the budget. With zero of n, the
     per-lineage success rate at that budget is below 3/n at 95% (0 of
     24: below 11.7%). A bound on hitting time follows only if the
     hazard is constant, so say which is claimed.
  2. Both directions: descent from plants and ascent from random and
     empty starts.
  3. At least three acceptance regimes and one population method.
  4. At least three unlike plants per capability.
  5. Ground truth: a machine small enough for a census (1e8 to 1e10
     programs) on the simplest retention task. Exact solution count,
     exact basins under the strict and margin rules, exact neutral
     components. The sampled curves are then calibrated against it.
     Without this, the geometry statistics of 01 section 13 are
     estimators with no known error.
  6. A positive control for the search itself: a planted target of the
     same density with a known hitting time.
  7. Climbability of the world: some search on some substrate must reach
     the capability in that world from scratch. Otherwise the null
     belongs to the world (WORLD_UNCLIMBABLE).
  8. Lifetime learning on and off at equal evaluations. Whether
     development smooths the search landscape (Hinton and Nowlan 1987) is
     the central reach question of the program, and descent in genome
     space alone cannot see it.
  9. Pathology type: lethal, plateau, deceptive or disconnected. Each
     has a different remedy.
The only null worth writing: REACH-BOUNDED, with the bound, next to the
planted target that the same search did find.

9. WORLD-PROGRAM CRITIQUE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
W0. Right in purpose. Add: the isomer panel lives here; and every
coordinate later claimed in W1 needs its known positive here first. The
strong sense of RECURSE has none.

W1. Can it identify when development becomes economically useful? Only
under four conditions.
  1. Exact values on both sides at every dial setting: the best policy
     that uses inherited information only, and the best learner. The
     transition surface is then COMPUTED from the world. The experiment
     asks whether search finds organisms on the right side of it and at
     what cost. Without this a missing developer may be unreached, not
     uneconomical.
  2. The break-even price as the observable. Where the surface lies
     depends on the price list for storage, computation and lifetime,
     and the price list is the experimenter's. The price at which two
     strategies tie is a property of the world. A small example from my
     RETAIN world, where the choice is to keep a mapping in the
     persistent store or not: one stored mapping is worth 0.74 correct
     probes per life (18,854 probes in 2,400 lives of 8 stimuli; three
     quarters gained per probe). Above that rent, storing does not pay.
     The number exists before any search.
  3. The inherited-information budget, made mandatory. 01 lists it among
     parameters W1 "may include". With unlimited inheritance,
     hard-wiring wins wherever the regularity is the same in every life.
     So a map needs either that dial or regularities drawn fresh per
     life.
  4. Three reasons for development kept apart, because they are
     different physics: the world holds more than inheritance can carry
     (capacity); it varies between lifetimes but is regular within one
     (variability; Stephens 1991); learned solutions are easier to FIND
     than hard-wired ones (reach). 01 mixes them in one parameter list.
     The third is the one Phase 3 depends on, and an economic reading of
     "pays" cannot see it.

W2. In the file I had it is a list of twelve ingredients. It says cheap
policy classes "can still be bounded aggressively" and does not say how.
Without a method it resembles the class my salvage retired: worlds with
no computed optimum (W-14). It keeps interpretability only if (i)
certified modules compose with a derived bound; (ii) planted random keys
are embedded, so that a counting bound holds however rich the rest is;
(iii) W0 and W1 profiles predict W2 performance. Condition (ii) is the
cheapest, and the scale check of REV 10 is its first use.

Adversarial demand generator (01 section 12). Useful idea, wrong order.
Its target, a world where "the current cheap baseline family fails", is
relative to a list, so it will find worlds that beat the list for dull
reasons. The known positive it protects is designed by the same hands.
01 already asks for sealed third-party world families. Add one rule: the
generator proposes and an exact solver certifies. Not before exact W1
maps exist.

Better families:
  KEY WORLDS         hidden random keys per life, family or epoch; value
                     needs the key after a gap; exact counting bounds;
                     capacity dial. RETAIN is the first; counterfeit/
                     keys.py is one order up.
  KEYED COMPOSITION  parts recur in new combinations; exact cost of
                     relearning each combination against learning parts.
  NOISY PERSISTENCE  the organism's own stores flip bits at rate eps;
                     capacity bounds; demand for built error correction,
                     with no authored task regularity at all.
  LAW TURNOVER       exact change-point posterior, so "warranted revision
                     latency" has an exact reference.
  VARYING GOALS      goals switch among recombinations of shared
                     subgoals (Kashtan and Alon 2005): a family with a
                     published effect on modularity and, in later work
                     by the same group, on search speed.
  MINED WORLDS       properties of sampled finite systems, exact by
                     enumeration, with no author intent (Hecate, W-10).

10. COGNITIVE-BOUNDARY CRITIQUE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A real axis, and second-order for the first 90 days. 01 asks the right
question: where does useful organization choose to live under different
cost structures. Seven boundary conditions (I, E, S and their sums) is
more design than that question needs.

The boundary has one first-order consequence NOW, and it is about
measurement. Every W0 world must certify how much persistent state the
environment lets the organism write: zero unless declared. Otherwise an
organism that parks information in the world passes BUILD with nothing
built. The impostor that does exactly this joins the kit.

So: fix the boundary for the first 90 days and certify it. After that,
one experiment that treats the boundary as an OUTCOME: a price for
internal storage, a price for external storage, exact optima for
internal-only and external-only classes, and the carrier located by
interchange on each store. The external store needs physics of its own
(local, decaying, shared, left behind when the organism moves). If it is
just a second free store, the finding that organisms use it is true by
construction and teaches nothing. A prediction worth registering: what
must travel with the organism is internalised; what is tied to a place
is not. The social channel waits.

13. REVISED 90-DAY PLAN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Not an edit of 05 (not provided). Built to raise uncertainty resolved per
unit of operator attention, which is the scarce input. A reviewer counted
a draft of this plan at twenty work packages behind one gate and called
it too much for one operator. This version is shorter and says what
slips.

DAYS 0-30
  Runner v0: refuses to start unless the preregistration is an ancestor
  commit, the verdict table is total and reachable, and exact power is at
  least 0.99. Receipts carry tokens and energy. Sealed worlds live
  outside the working tree.
  W0: RETAIN-1 (one hidden bit across a gap) and RETAIN, with exact
  bounds for the class that carries nothing across the gap.
  X1 in three physics: the register machine (exists), a recurrent latch,
  a small circulating-packet latch modelled on the Ananke in-flight
  organism.
  Rulers: class exclusion, retention curve, interchange with matched
  shams; one presumption sheet per ruler.
  R0: the ideal observer as the bound, and the in-context learner. R0
  needs no designed positive: its known answer is the ideal observer's
  exact value. Its owner is a role inside an existing seat.
  GATE 30, decided by code: every ruler returns the registered answer on
  every isomer and impostor in three physics, and the physics-specific
  ruler is flagged. If not, repair the tunnel. Nothing else starts.
DAYS 30-60
  X2. The thin Track-A kernel (the prototype's machine with switches).
  W1 family 1: exact values, break-even surface, predictions registered
  before any search. R0's online-gradient learner. The lattice isomer.
  A second implementation of one world and one ruler by a different
  author and model family, compared by code. The same second author
  writes one isomer per physics without seeing the rulers. A blind
  search for one-bit retention in each panel physics, scored by class
  exclusion; its first certified hit in each physics joins the panel as
  the found organism.
  GATE 60: sampled reach agrees with the census within its stated error;
  W1 predictions are registered.
DAYS 60-90
  X4 in R0 and the Track-A kernel, with lifetime learning on and off,
  ending with the scale check. X3: the kit ported to the runner, and one
  attempt at a positive for the third class (not gated). X5 as a
  background job. R3 thin starts only if GATE 60 passed by day 60.
  Decision record.
  GATE 90: which build goes deep (Track-A kernel, R3 or neither);
  whether the strong sense of RECURSE has earned a place in the
  headline; which unlike physics, if any, earns a search campaign.
What slips. If GATE 30 passes at day 45, R3 and X5 move past day 90. If
capacity is half of this: X1, X2 and X4, in that order; X3's port waits.
Not in these 90 days: the boundary price sweep, causal/nuisance
dissociation, the noise pilot, a RECALL world, the content-addressable
memory machine, R0's external-memory and self-referential learners

I WOULD REVERSE IF
  1. NEUTRALITY. By day 45, after one round of repair, some ruler still
     returns an answer other than the registered one on an isomer or
     impostor in any of the three physics. Then stop cross-physics
     comparison, take one physics deep, and treat neutrality as open.
  2. FREE-FALL. In the census machine, the exact success probability at
     the registered budget falls outside the 95% interval of the
     free-fall estimate in more than 2 of 10 registered cells. Then
     drop scaffold distance as a planning tool.
  3. WHICH KERNEL. Under the SAME black-box search regime and equal
     evaluations, R3 reaches certified retention in at least 20 of 24
     independent lineages where the Track-A kernel does so in 4 or
     fewer. Then R3 is the deep build and Track A the legible control.
     Or the converse.
  4. CONVENTIONAL SUFFICES. I expect R0's in-context learner to match
     the ideal observer inside its training distribution. That is not
     the test. If, on kinds never met in its training and at lifetimes
     twice its training horizon, R0 still matches every certified cell
     of every candidate at lower total cost, training included, then
     unfamiliar substrates are not justified at this scale.
  5. ECONOMICS. Registered break-even predictions are wrong in sign in
     half or more of at least six cells where reach was shown. Then the
     economic framing or the class bounds are wrong; stop mapping.
  6. THE HEADLINE. My recommendation now: carry the strong sense of
     RECURSE as an open problem, and make the charter's headline nested
     improvement by order, with the setting and the bits acquired
     stated. I would reverse that if a designed organism and a
     criterion exist such that the library learner and the search-order
     selector fail and the organism passes, and a second author
     reproduces it. The plan gives X3 its last thirty days and lets it
     slip first, so I do not expect this by day 90.
  7. COST. By day 90 the instrument share (core plus standards) has
     taken more than 80% of tokens and attention, and fewer than six
     registered cells hold a qualified effect or a bounded negative.
     Then measure-first has failed its own cost test (01 section 24,
     last clause): cut the tunnel to class exclusion and receipts and
     spend on cars.
  8. BLIND SEARCH. In X5 some sampled physics has a random-hit rate
     whose lower 95% bound is ten times the upper bound of the designed
     kernels' rate. Then that physics gets the next campaign, ahead of
     any car a model designed.
  9. MY STAKE. The other reviewer, or the author of the DGM design,
     shows that my ranking of R3 over R4 and R5, or the merge of R2,
     follows my stake and not the entry rule. Then take their ranking.
