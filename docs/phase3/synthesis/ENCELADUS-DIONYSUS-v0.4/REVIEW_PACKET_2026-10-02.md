----- 4. Common ground and real policy differences -----

Keep a thin federation: native dynamics, shared evidence contracts. Keep
strong conventional competitors, lawful positives and false-rejection
tests. A reset must erase forbidden influence without erasing authorized
state. Replay fidelity is not reset validity. No automatic deep engine.

The lead proposes INDETERMINATE for qualified statistical gates whose
complete inputs meet neither threshold; execution and observation remain
separate. Existing code is not silently upgraded. Exact bounds remain
mandatory for restricted-class exclusion, not every named empirical or
intervention-relative claim. Do not use a universal L-number ladder.
Record source, scope, reason and every affected dependency, not just a
combined verdict. FAIL names a failed predicate, not absent cognition.

----- 5. Positive and counterfeit ceilings -----

Our finite updater, its flattened form and a fixed sign-gradient R0 each
solve 8/8 tasks. That supplies a narrow attainable control, not economic
advantage or a genuine strong-recursion positive.

Fable's unseen-pair experiment retains value as restricted combination.
For its stated non-linking information class the exact elimination mean
rounds to 3.38 correct of 16. A cache of 512 inherited recombinations,
outside that class, scores 13.46; the label-directed composer scores 16.
Those exploratory scores say nothing about the whole cache class's optimum.

A watcher retaining a prior life's answers scores 15.96 versus 3.46 in
the control when only the intact key repeats. The arms alone falsely say
COMBINED; the delivered repeated-key check refuses this planted case.
Unique hashes are not proof of independent secret keys. Before a new run,
review adaptive map selection, randomized held-out pairs, selective leaks,
and lawful maps the task must retain. NOT_SHOWN is not earned absence.
