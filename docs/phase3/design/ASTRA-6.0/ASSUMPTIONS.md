## Reading and decision rules

- **Design assumption** means a proposed restriction or hypothesis. **Documentary support** means the scoped source supports the motivation, not that this apparatus works.
- **Analytical support** is conditional on a fully specified finite law; an independently executed native implementation still has to match it.
- Every test below is prospective. Operational evidence means actual native traces, independent truth, control outcomes, custody events and resource receipts, not a PASS assertion in a manifest.
- A failed assumption lowers the affected claim or changes allocation. It does not automatically refute every form of development or reasoning.
- D1-D5 are adopted at report-recommendation level. Another operator decision is not needed to state this recommendation; actual host access, spending, staffing, custody and bounded execution remain gated.
- Source documents' earlier proposal-stage or then-pending language remains historical. Stage I is the original snapshot at `eeeda08bb45757298b3cb21ee22d816b44388aef`; later corrections must not masquerade as pre-audit commitments.

## Evidence that would reverse the overall recommendation

1. **Native infeasibility:** a fully specified A0 witness, independent truth path and reset/meter controls still fail after the one bounded S0 repair. Stop A0 expansion and report the precise contract/capacity gap; consider another physics only with a new discriminating contract.
2. **Qualification cost dominates:** measured complete-assay batches plus native intervention checks cannot meet sensitivity, specificity, precision and cap constraints. Deliver an exact finite-world or calibration-only package, rather than several underqualified runtimes.
3. **A simpler learner wins:** qualified independent H/P/I and fresh-family comparisons exclude meaningful advantage and ordinary competitors dominate lifecycle cost. Reframe the tested program around that ordinary mechanism; do not preserve development as a mandatory explanation.
4. **A distinct physical prediction survives:** a qualified A result leaves a locality/addressing/update alternative unresolved, and a separately metered B/C path can discriminate it. Admit that track instead of treating three-runtime symmetry as a goal.
5. **The causal boundary is unidentifiable:** native process interventions cannot distinguish carried solutions, current learning and construction of fresh learners. Stop the recursive interpretation while preserving any supported lower-level effect.
