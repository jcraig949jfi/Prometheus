# Turn historical failures into enforceable boundaries

**All 24 available Tityos classes map to proposed admission invariants, executable test obligations, fault injections, residual blind spots, and claim ceilings.**
The operative word is proposed: these gates are not installed, run, or qualified by writing this document.
The frozen ASTRA-6.0 requirements already demand most of these safeguards; historical evidence supplies concrete ways to attack them, not permission to rewrite the requirements after exposure.
A failed gate blocks the affected scientific claim, while intact raw artifacts remain available as development evidence or a correction record.
Passing an integrity check clears only that boundary; it does not establish an effect, mechanism, transfer, or absence of latent capacity.
Any post-data repair invalidates affected confirmatory standing, triggers requalification, and permits promotion only on new appropriately independent confirmatory data.
The companion [56-slice salvage matrix][MATRIX] separates infrastructure from scientific semantics and supplies costed reuse choices. ([Tityos taxonomy][TAX]; [TI admission contract][TI]; [frozen architecture][ARCH])

## 1. Gates belong on the actual promotion route

Read scope and source identity are those of [SALVAGE_MATRIX.md][MATRIX]: frozen requirements/architecture plus all five ASTRA evidence notes, with selective original statistical-source inspection.
Only ASTRA design content was read; no other architect conclusions or prohibited role trees were used.
The four indexes actually contain **247 perspective records, not the requested 219**; this numerical discrepancy is explained in the matrix and is not converted into independent engine counts.
Tityos's taxonomy has exactly **T01-T24**; the classes overlap and are neither a complete causal model nor measured failure frequencies.
In particular, one leak can trigger answer-bearing measurement, invalid null, construction artifact, and pseudo-independence flags without becoming four independent discoveries. ([TI scope][TI]; [matrix census][MATRIX])

The shared protocol must bind a run to candidate/world/ruler/configuration digests, initial state and ancestry, authorized exposure, random streams, resource envelope, delta*, novelty level, independent unit, and analysis family.
An admission verifier outside candidate write authority checks actual receipts, not producer-supplied PASS fields.
It preserves attempted, eligible, excluded, failed, timed-out and completed denominators separately; absence of a receipt returns INDETERMINATE or the relevant typed failure, never implicit PASS.
Each gate record carries version/regime, required evidence, eligible/fired counts, control lineage, expected/observed response, test receipt, and an invalidation dependency list.
These are enforcement obligations for a future implementation, not claims about current deployed behavior. ([R-PRV-01/02, R-MSR-01/02][REQ]; [TI:C1C2][C1C2])

| Boundary | Enforced responsibility | Mandatory fail-closed behavior | Taxonomy coverage, not independent certifications |
|---|---|---|---|
| Manifest and custody | Exact versions, exposure, budgets, candidate selection, units | Refuse confirmation without a complete pre-outcome freeze | T09-T12, T17-T18, T23 |
| Reality and native adapter | Only allowed state, observations, transitions and interventions | Block hidden-state access, unsupported delivery and unmatched tariffs | T01-T03, T07, T13, T16 |
| World and ruler qualification | Reachable verdicts, capable positives, cheap negatives, shams, exact references | Calibration-only if sensitivity/specificity or demand is unqualified | T04-T08, T10, T12-T15, T22 |
| Independent measurement and interpretation | Read-only evidence, disjoint decisive paths, bounded labels | No model-vote, caller-verdict or proxy-to-mechanism promotion | T02, T14-T17, T24 |
| Execution, publication and correction | Runtime attestation, durable caps, artifact delivery, responder | Pause on missing verifier, stale authority or unknown integrity | T18-T21, T23-T24 |

Every proposed test FG01-FG24 below must run through the production loader, measurement and promotion path, with a clean twin and an adversarial variant.
The identifiers are test specifications, **not existing test filenames or passed checks**.
Finite historical regressions train the instrument; a separate withheld mechanism-family panel estimates its operating-regime reliability.
The frozen targets are a one-sided 95% lower confidence bound on sensitivity at delta* >= .80 and a one-sided 95% upper confidence bound on false-positive rate <= .05, plus sham equivalence, power >= .80 and family-wise alpha=.05 under the registered analysis.
In the ideal independent-binomial all-correct case, at least 14/14 positive recoveries and 0/59 false positives meet the first two bounds; these are planning lower limits, not observed results or sufficient total sample sizes.
Clustering, errors, multiple regimes and simultaneous coverage can require more; no multiplication of episodes turns them into independent lineages. ([Architecture section 6][ARCH]; [TI calibration calculation][TI])

## 2. The first twelve classes attack the estimand

These classes ask whether the system could have measured a meaningful contrast at all.
The historical record repeatedly separates a working executable from a discriminating instrument: WTP-02 omitted a fitted constant, Ananke's mirrored control could alias its signal test, and Harmonia's arithmetic could mislabel an interval's multiplicity.
Their reuse value lies in enforceable counterexamples, not inherited PASS badges. ([TA:F01,F03][TA]; [TI:E1-E3][TI])

### T01 / FG01: measurement carries its own answer
**Invariant and enforcement:** at world/adapter admission, evaluator truth and seeded ancestry must not enter an organism-visible channel except the explicitly priced observation contract. R-WLD-02, R-PRV-01, R-PRV-02, R-REP-02; matrix C14,C26,C41.
**Test and injection:** leak a balanced answer through a payload field, filename, ordering, length, timestamp, allocator ID and undeclared imported founder; run payload-only/reactive readers and differential hidden-truth probes with unchanged legitimate observations.
**Required response:** reject the contaminated adapter/claim and emit PROVENANCE_INVALID; an honest retained-state witness must remain detectable on the clean twin, and permitted sensory information must not be falsely removed.
**Residual / ceiling:** finite probes miss semantic/covert channels; passing permits only the registered observation-boundary claim, never general leakage freedom or endogenous development from a seeded branch.
**Evidence:** taxonomy T01; Ludus's differential probes and Archaeon's founder correction motivate distinct channels, not a universal detection rate. ([T01][TAX]; [SY:F03,F09][SY])

### T02 / FG02: self-verdicting and caller-asserted verdicts
**Invariant and enforcement:** the candidate/generator cannot write authoritative measurements or admission; a read-only independent verifier derives them from reality receipts. R-PRV-02, R-CAU-03; C33,C46,C51.
**Test and injection:** submit fabricated CLEAR/SUPPORTED fields, a caller-provided true mean, a correctly shaped false digest, and a producer assertion of independence; attempt direct writes and alternate promotion endpoints.
**Required response:** reject unauthorized writes with audit events; recompute scientific inputs from authoritative evidence, while accepting a genuine clean receipt through the same path.
**Residual / ceiling:** role separation can preserve a shared wrong definition; passing establishes authority separation only, not truth, independent replication or a causal mechanism.
**Evidence:** Omega is explicitly a caller-mean toy stub; PEW reference shape checking is useful but not byte authentication. ([T02][TAX]; [TI:E5-E6][TI])

### T03 / FG03: controls and guards cannot fail
**Invariant and enforcement:** each claimed safeguard has a live predicate whose input changes actual admission, not just the displayed flag. R-MSR-01, R-REP-01, R-ATT-01; C35,C36,C46.
**Test and injection:** bypass the predicate's real call site, ignore its relevant input, substitute constant PASS, or reuse a mathematically forced control; then restore it and replay planted violations plus clean twins.
**Required response:** isolate the targeted guard in a controlled attribution fixture: disabling it must expose a known violation and restoring it must block that violation; separately test the complete production route with all redundant guards enabled. Missing attribution instrumentation means RULER_UNQUALIFIED, not proof of safety.
**Residual / ceiling:** killing an output flag or equivalent mutant is not causal qualification; passing supports only that tested guard's decision dependence, not independence of all gates.
**Evidence:** AP's forced flag, Koios's constant/mean-residual predicates, and Ananke's mirrored zero_comm illustrate different nondiscriminating checks. ([T03][TAX]; [TI:AP][AP]; [TA:F03,F11][TA])

### T04 / FG04: positive control absent, misdirected or degenerate
**Invariant and enforcement:** a scientific positive of declared sign and meaningful size must traverse the exact production instrument and scope. R-MSR-01, R-SCI-02; C06,C35,C41.
**Test and injection:** independently plant delta*-sized behavior, broken-capability twins, weaker/stronger effects, saturation, noise and low-budget cases; deliberately route a substitute battery around the real kill path.
**Required response:** only controls executed on the real path count toward registered sensitivity; wrong-path, unavailable or underqualified positives produce RULER_UNQUALIFIED and calibration-only output.
**Residual / ceiling:** convenient planted effects need not span natural mechanisms; even a qualified positive does not make every null a bounded negative without adequate power and capacity/world witnesses.
**Evidence:** the Known Truth Battery does not import the F1-F14 instrument it was invoked to qualify; Nyx packet structural acceptance can include positive-control unavailability. ([T04][TAX]; [TI:KTB][KTB]; [TI:PACKET][PACKET])

### T05 / FG05: invalid null, missing chance floor or degenerate null
**Invariant and enforcement:** null construction must destroy the claimed dependency while preserving registered nuisance structure and assignment rules. R-MSR-01, R-MSR-02, R-WLD-01; C16,C19,C24,C41.
**Test and injection:** shuffle inside a signal-preserving group, create zero-variation nulls, imbalance labels, and compare zero with a fitted constant; independently enumerate tiny exchangeable and nonexchangeable cases.
**Required response:** reject invalid null semantics, report analytical/empirical chance floors on the same eligible population, and require the meaningful-effect twin to remain recoverable.
**Residual / ceiling:** synthetic exchangeability need not hold in real lineages; a baseline gap supports only the particular registered contrast, not reasoning or transfer.
**Evidence:** Ensorain's zero-baseline gain and Tityos's null families supply regression cases; shared streams can validly pair arms without adding independent units. ([T05][TAX]; [TA:S02][WTP2]; [SY:F10][SY])

### T06 / FG06: tautology or algebraic identity becomes a finding
**Invariant and enforcement:** a scientific relationship must be capable of failing under admissible conditions, rather than follow from the statistic's definition. R-SCI-01, R-CAU-01, R-MSR-03; C17,C23,C35.
**Test and injection:** encode the certificate directly as a coordinate, construct intercept-residual mean equality, or make both claimed variables share their decisive term; contrast with a genuine contingent relationship and an admissible counterexample.
**Required response:** label identities as implementation/definition recovery, not mechanisms; hold scientific promotion until a contrast distinguishes the phenomenon from the construction.
**Residual / ceiling:** finite symbolic checks miss disguised identities; passing bounds only tested constructions and does not certify a new law or novelty.
**Evidence:** Cosmos's definition rung and Koios's OLS residual means narrow the meaning of apparently successful gates. ([T06][TAX]; [TA:F04,F11][TA])

### T07 / FG07: normalization, construction or catalogue artifacts become structure
**Invariant and enforcement:** preserve the raw estimand, transformation and sampling-frame lineage; assess constructor effects separately. R-MSR-01, R-MSR-02, R-PRV-01; C13,C16,C24.
**Test and injection:** add metadata to numerical vectors, change a variance denominator, pad functionally identical programs, alter lesion dose geometry, or score each policy under its own occupancy rather than a common reference.
**Required response:** report raw and transformed contrasts; block intrinsic-property language if the effect disappears under the registered constructor/dose/occupancy controls, while retaining valid narrower observations.
**Residual / ceiling:** some transformations legitimately remove nuisance while others remove signal; passing supports only a specified invariant or resource-matched effect, not a representation-free property.
**Evidence:** Nestor's fractional-damage correction and Ludus's common-reference demotion are bounded historical corrections, not proof every old effect was false. ([T07][TAX]; [SY:F05,F09][SY])

### T08 / FG08: cheap alternatives were omitted
**Invariant and enforcement:** world and claim admission compare against task-appropriate constant, majority, lookup, reactive, finite-state, closed-form and direct-solution policies at disclosed costs. R-WLD-01, R-SRH-01, R-MSR-01; C12-C17,C41.
**Test and injection:** omit fitted constants, hide an xor solver from a depth-only battery, submit an empty attack builder, or give a baseline fewer eligible cases/resources; include a genuinely demanding matched world.
**Required response:** execute nonempty attacks and reconcile denominators; if a cheap policy explains the target, return WORLD_INSUFFICIENT for that claimed demand or lower the interpretation rather than discard the score.
**Residual / ceiling:** the cheapest demonstrated solver is an upper bound, not an exhaustive minimum; defeating tested policies does not certify unrestricted reasoning.
**Evidence:** WTP-03's post-data N6 is an explanatory future baseline, not a retroactive equal-budget victory; Ludus's Nim case demands broader baseline classes. ([T08][TAX]; [TA:S03][WTP3]; [SY:F09][SY])

### T09 / FG09: selection changes the measured population
**Invariant and enforcement:** eligibility, candidate choice and exclusions freeze before outcome access; all assigned units and consumed cost remain accountable. R-MSR-02, R-REP-02, R-CMP-01, R-OUT-01; C20,C28,C43.
**Test and injection:** choose the maximum heldout score from random-outcome candidates, drop slow/refused jobs, reorder candidates, select only completion-friendly worlds, or omit an admitted coalition from the success clock.
**Required response:** reject outcome-selected confirmation; preserve all attempts/censoring and explicit conditional scope, with train/discovery-only selection followed by untouched evaluation.
**Residual / ceiling:** unknown selection mechanisms limit population extrapolation; passing supports only the declared eligible frame, not all worlds or search trajectories.
**Evidence:** Ares's heldout argmax, Ensorain world admission and Tyche's population-clock mismatch attack different selection boundaries. ([T09][TAX]; [SY:F08][SY]; [TA:F02,F06][TA])

### T10 / FG10: statistical defects inside the gate
**Invariant and enforcement:** uncertainty uses the varying independent unit, correct quantiles/covariance, attainable tests, registered multiplicity and stopping. R-MSR-02, R-SCI-02; C27,C34,C38,C40.
**Test and injection:** df=11, arbitrary alpha, three-plus primaries, zero/one/unequal and power-of-two clusters, duplicated founders, shared baselines and optional stopping; compare independent references and preplanned coverage simulations.
**Required response:** refuse unsupported inputs and unit duplication; wrong arithmetic yields IMPLEMENTATION_DEFECT/RULER_UNQUALIFIED, inadequate precision yields STATISTICALLY_INCONCLUSIVE, never an automatic scientific negative.
**Residual / ceiling:** numerically correct intervals can use a wrong dependence model; passing supports uncertainty for the registered sampling design only, with population claims conditioned on limited families.
**Evidence:** original Harmonia code confirms upward df-table selection and .025 fallback; Diomedes code confirms problematic low-bit sampling but not Nyx's ANY-gate accusation. No probes were rerun here. ([T10][TAX]; [TI:QR][QR]; [TA:S25][DIOM]; [TA:S13][NYXFAIL])

### T11 / FG11: post-exposure changes rewrite the experiment
**Invariant and enforcement:** exact analysis, baseline, adapter, threshold and exclusion bytes must precede any outcome access; correction is a new linked event. R-PRV-01, R-PRV-03, R-REP-02; C16,C28,C36,C51.
**Test and injection:** edit a committed plan after first-add, tune a null on observed results, lower a gate, change exclusions, or rename an already used holdout as sealed.
**Required response:** preserve the original manifest/result, invalidate affected confirmatory claims and descendants, quarantine exposed data to development, and require a new frozen version plus fresh confirmatory data after requalification.
**Residual / ceiling:** hidden out-of-band exposure can escape logs; repaired old-data analysis is a correction/explanation only and cannot promote the repaired scientific claim.
**Evidence:** AP ancestry is not exact-content freezing; WTP-03 N6 and the BEE E-003 history motivate explicit post-data labels rather than retroactive gate repair. ([T11][TAX]; [TI:AP][AP]; [TA:F02][TA]; [SY:F11][SY])

### T12 / FG12: unreachable design and structural zero
**Invariant and enforcement:** preflight must establish a reachable decision/effect under the actual rules, budgets and changing eligibility state. R-DEV-01, R-SCI-02, R-MSR-01; C06,C21,C35,C42.
**Test and injection:** unreachable thresholds, an empty admissible set, a parity-constrained mutation operator, a three-step walk facing a longer path, and initial-only eligibility that later changes; include reachable seeded cases.
**Required response:** stop an impossible finite design before scientific execution; distinguish CAPACITY_UNESTABLISHED, SEARCH_UNDERPOWERED and RULER_UNQUALIFIED rather than force a common null label.
**Residual / ceiling:** nonexhaustive search proves no global impossibility; passing a planted route establishes bounded capacity/detectability, not unseeded discovery or universal reachability.
**Evidence:** Proteus strict ascent does not traverse neutral plateaus; SFE's two-flip parity limits neighborhoods without proving every founder cannot reach a target. ([T12][TAX]; [SY:F06,F10][SY])

## 3. The remaining classes attack causality and authority

A correct score still does not determine its explanation, and a plausible correction can itself be wrong.
The next twelve gates therefore preserve claim scope, compatibility limits, independent scientific pathways and the distinction between observable work and interpreted yield.
They require a real consumer and responder without turning a model or a familiar ontology into the judge of whether an unfamiliar mechanism is allowed to exist. ([R-CAU-01/02/03, R-APR-02/03, R-INF-03, R-ATT-03][REQ])

### T13 / FG13: task, world or organism too weak
**Invariant and enforcement:** qualify both solvability by a within-budget native witness and demand relative to cheap policies before emergence testing. R-ORG-01, R-DEV-01, R-WLD-01, R-SCI-02; C01-C12.
**Test and injection:** world-blind observation, delay beyond causal reach, insufficient storage, one-shot latches on repeated demands, and a lesion that never touches relevant state; pair each with a capable repaired fixture.
**Required response:** report trial-position and depth profiles; use WORLD_INSUFFICIENT or CAPACITY_UNESTABLISHED only with the corresponding evidence, and retain CAUSE_UNRESOLVED when isolation fails.
**Residual / ceiling:** a bad witness does not prove absent capacity; a good witness is a capacity lower bound, and activation propagation alone is neither content transport nor learning.
**Evidence:** Ananke's correction chain separates light-cone limits, latches and search; Aether's positive is explicitly local propagation under specified physics. ([T13][TAX]; [TA:F03,F05][TA])

### T14 / FG14: classifier or proxy mistaken for mechanism
**Invariant and enforcement:** execution state, instrument qualification, effect estimate and scientific interpretation must be separately typed and behavior-grounded. R-MSR-01, R-TRF-02, R-INF-03; C23,C31,C39,C46.
**Test and injection:** COMPLETE/PASS without an effect, a source-read organ without intervention, familiar labels on random behavior, obfuscated known behavior, nuisance recoding and a genuine causal-law change.
**Required response:** completion never auto-promotes to positive; preserve valid nuisance invariance but demand response to causal changes, and publish relevant classifier confusion rather than a single badge.
**Residual / ceiling:** reference labels can share the classifier's ontology; passing establishes a scoped behavioral classification, not mechanism, abstraction or cognition.
**Evidence:** Atlas's regex completion inflation and inherited primitive labels contrast with Nyx's useful but predominantly source-read hypotheses. ([T14][TAX]; [IX:P01,P03][IX]; [PA:C4][PA])

### T15 / FG15: missing prior-art detection becomes novelty
**Invariant and enforcement:** corpus-relative retrieval, functional equivalence, new causal family and novelty to science are different outputs; unfamiliarity is not eligibility. R-WLD-02, R-APR-02, R-APR-03, R-INF-03; C31,C39.
**Test and injection:** renamed known mechanisms, a broken search, out-of-corpus material and valid unclassifiable behavior; measure retrieval recall against independently held known-art examples and retain search logs.
**Required response:** report NOT_FOUND_IN_DECLARED_CORPUS or search unavailable as interpretation metadata; preserve the ontology-blind anomaly queue and block automatic novelty promotion or unfamiliarity veto.
**Residual / ceiling:** no finite literature corpus proves universal absence; passing supports only documented search scope, not historical priority or a novel cognitive category.
**Evidence:** prior-art notes retain learned-library/plasticity/self-modifying-weight competitors; catalogue VERIFIED means URL resolution, not reproduced science. ([T15][TAX]; [PA:P1,P4,P5,C1][PA])

### T16 / FG16: mechanism analysis remains description
**Invariant and enforcement:** nominated causal boundaries must support delivered lesion, equal-damage sham, rescue and qualified transfer before mechanism/reuse claims. R-CAU-01, R-CAU-02, R-CAU-03, R-TRF-01; C08,C24-C31.
**Test and injection:** inert cuts, nonspecific damage, blank/scrambled donors, adapter-only teachers, counter-only transfer and same-organism round trips; ledger donor bytes, compatibility, off-target effects and full cost.
**Required response:** target state must actually change and rescue must restore the qualified contrast; unsupported delivery returns INTERVENTION_UNSUPPORTED/INVALID, not no mechanism or absent cognition.
**Residual / ceiling:** distributed processes may resist valid slicing; passing allows only the tested causal/portable function, not automatic heredity, cross-substrate principle or Q6 nesting.
**Evidence:** Nyx source labels lack survived-transplant standing; Crius clocks, Nestor painters and Ares gain-sensitive ports show why labels and donor success need delivery controls. ([T16][TAX]; [PA:C4][PA]; [SY:F02,F07,F08][SY])

### T17 / FG17: improper independence
**Invariant and enforcement:** each promotion names shared author/model/brief/data/generator/codec/parser/scorer/runtime dependencies; decisive scientific code must be disjoint on the second pathway. R-CAU-03, R-REP-03, R-INF-03; C01,C12,C38,C56.
**Test and injection:** introduce one parser or generator defect shared by nominally distinct seats/wrappers; test an independent implementation authored from the behavioral contract, without copying result-dependent fixes.
**Required response:** relabel same-code agreement as repeatability, not causal independence; retain shared infrastructure in the dependency ledger and require the second pathway before mechanism promotion.
**Residual / ceiling:** independent code can share mathematics or human priors; passing widens confidence in the tested contract, not universal independence or cross-substrate generality by name count.
**Evidence:** Proteus/CA/SFE consumer clusters and Hecate's shared exact-shadow dependencies defeat a seat-count interpretation. ([T17][TAX]; [SY:F01][SY]; [TI:SHADOW][SHADOW])

### T18 / FG18: provenance failures
**Invariant and enforcement:** recompute authoritative content identities and verify parents, exposure, custody and canonicalization at admission and replay. R-PRV-01, R-PRV-03, R-REP-01, R-OUT-01; C22,C26,C27,C29,C32,C51-C53.
**Test and injection:** fake but well-shaped hashes, donor/source swaps, missing parents, imported seeded founders, CRLF/LFS transforms, a cloned database identity and missing negative artifacts.
**Required response:** quarantine unverifiable claims as PROVENANCE_INVALID, distinguish raw bytes from normalized/Git identity, and preserve correction ancestry; a reconstruction must not impersonate an original receipt.
**Residual / ceiling:** correct hashes prove identity, not truthful execution or exclusive write authority; passing supports auditable lineage only within the captured exposure boundary.
**Evidence:** Archaeon founder reclassification, PEW shape semantics, Techne body custody and Ixion's database-clone caveat motivate separate checks. ([T18][TAX]; [SY:F03][SY]; [TI:PEW][PEW]; [PA:C2][PA]; [IX:P14][IX])

### T19 / FG19: wrong status layer or population
**Invariant and enforcement:** installed, imported, executed, controlled, consumed and scientifically supported are distinct; every count declares its unit and coverage. R-PRV-01, R-ATT-03, R-OUT-01; C30,C32,C43-C49.
**Test and injection:** path exists but is uncalled, HTTP success with no artifact, replay counted as replicate, duplicated genealogy events, and absent item in a partial catalogue/search.
**Required response:** reconcile source/derived totals and eligible/fired counts, return unknown for unavailable populations, and prevent completion or catalogue size from advancing scientific standing.
**Residual / ceiling:** receipts and consumer attestations can lie; passing supports the inspected count/status layer only, never a full-program denominator or current deployment inferred from old prose.
**Evidence:** BEE duplicates, Atlas status inflation, catalogue units and the 219-versus-247 index discrepancy are deliberately kept separate. ([T19][TAX]; [SY:F04][SY]; [IX:F1][IX]; [matrix scope][MATRIX])

### T20 / FG20: safeguards not wired, installed, staffed or consumed
**Invariant and enforcement:** every actual promotion/publication route consumes the required verifier receipt and has a bounded responder/fallback. R-PRV-02, R-ATT-01; C33,C35,C43,C47,C54.
**Test and injection:** remove a hook, call an alternate endpoint, ignore a nonzero return code, supply a failed-but-existing gate receipt, detach CI, or make the reviewer unavailable.
**Required response:** promotion pauses or fails closed through the real route; exploratory artifacts remain unqualified, and review exhaustion cannot silently mean approval.
**Residual / ceiling:** privileged bypass needs governance beyond application checks; passing demonstrates tested enforcement connectivity, not global security or scientific truth.
**Evidence:** stale consumers, gate-existence checks and positive counters without enforced execution appear in the Sisyphus operational chain. ([T20][TAX]; [SY:F11][SY]; [IX:D10-D11][IX])

### T21 / FG21: activity or liveness becomes productivity
**Invariant and enforcement:** track liveness, consumed inputs, valid outputs and qualified scientific yield independently; enforce durable budget and no-work bounds. R-ATT-01, R-ATT-03, R-CMP-01, R-ENE-02; C49,C50,C54.
**Test and injection:** self-generated SHA changes, identical receipt rewrites, unavailable upstream, malformed output, restart after parking, and a genuinely slow computation making measurable progress.
**Required response:** no-op activity cannot reset scientific-progress counters; missing inputs and integrity faults get distinct events; park state survives restart and explicit authorization controls resumption.
**Residual / ceiling:** long-horizon useful progress can be hard to observe; passing supports monitoring behavior, not a yield estimate derived from commits, messages, runs or energy proxy alone.
**Evidence:** Achilles's SHA predicate and the null-bound brake's in-memory state are separate defects; neither justifies calling every quiet process useless. ([T21][TAX]; [IX:P05,P07][IX])

### T22 / FG22: auditor error and correction bias
**Invariant and enforcement:** audit claims require primary evidence, clean twins, a reachable valid-surprise path, and independently held adversarial panels. R-MSR-01, R-CAU-03, R-ATT-02; C34,C35,C40.
**Test and injection:** persuasive false accusations, severity inflation, malformed/empty attacks, and unfavorable but valid results; include the claim that zero bootstrap width makes every Diomedes gate pass.
**Required response:** reject the overbroad accusation because the inspected guard requires nonzero error, while retaining the actual sampler defect; record disagreement and correction scope rather than averaging reviewer votes.
**Residual / ceiling:** auditors can overfit known fixtures or share priors; passing estimates only panel-specific detection/false-accusation behavior and does not certify an infallible critic.
**Evidence:** this synthesis inspected both `coordinate_census.py:159-210` and the original Nyx failure report; no numerical reproduction or owner adjudication is claimed. ([T22][TAX]; [TA:S25][DIOM]; [TA:S13][NYXFAIL])

### T23 / FG23: validated and deployed configurations differ
**Invariant and enforcement:** executable, loader, renderer, parser, configuration and environment used in execution must match the qualified freeze or its declared numerical-equivalence envelope. R-REP-01, R-PRV-03; C05,C38,C42,C45,C48.
**Test and injection:** stale executable, body/text swap, extra header, changed threshold/configuration, dropped in-flight state, boundary rounding, partial upload and stale printed verdict despite corrected source.
**Required response:** reject mismatched attestation, retain consumed cost and artifacts, require exact replay or preregistered numerical equivalence, and requalify the changed path before new confirmation.
**Residual / ceiling:** identical source does not guarantee identical numerical behavior across hardware/libraries; passing supports declared replay limits, not independent reproduction or semantic equivalence outside them.
**Evidence:** exact-shadow boundary tests, Ananke complete checkpoints and Ixion's deployed-build caveats cover different sources of drift. ([T23][TAX]; [TI:SHADOW][SHADOW]; [TA:S05][PTE]; [IX:D15][IX])

### T24 / FG24: model-specific measurement hazards
**Invariant and enforcement:** routine execution/search/measurement/admission makes zero external model-service calls; optional recorded interpretation cannot change current admission. R-INF-01, R-INF-02, R-INF-03, R-APR-02; C39,C48,C51.
**Test and injection:** disconnect network, intercept unauthorized service attempts, insert model fallback on unknown cases, and test offline interpretation with refusal, truncated JSON, family-specific context and a scrubber deleting causal content.
**Required response:** disconnected decisions match the deterministic authorized path; parse/refusal failures remain explicit denominators, unknown never triggers a hidden model oracle, and offline suggestions require a new registered experiment.
**Residual / ceiling:** model-authored code/specifications retain priors without runtime inference; passing establishes an inference-free inner loop, not an ontology-free experiment or valid scientific measurement by itself.
**Evidence:** frozen inference requirements and Ixion's deterministic brief boundary permit exceptional interpretation without inheriting model consensus as a judge. ([T24][TAX]; [R-INF-01/02/03][REQ]; [IX inference boundary][IX])

## 4. Corrections lower old claims; fresh data earns promotion

Post-data repair is an evidence-state transition, not a way to turn a formerly losing result into confirmation.
The invalidation graph must follow the changed world, adapter, ruler, statistical function, ancestry or admission rule to affected claims and downstream reused artifacts.
It preserves original outputs and verdicts, appends the defect/correction, records affected and demonstrably unaffected dependencies, and retires exposed holdouts from confirmation.
A new version must pass qualification on separate calibration data before its new candidate/analysis is locked and fresh confirmation opens.
Fresh seeds alone are insufficient when the design was tuned to a shared generator or causal family; novelty and independence must match the repaired claim. ([R-PRV-03, R-REP-02][REQ]; [architecture section 6][ARCH])

| State transition / adversarial example | Required record and enforcement | Claim ceiling after transition |
|---|---|---|
| Defect found before outcome access | Prove lack of exposure; version changed bytes and rerun impacted qualification before execution | Registered plan only; not a result |
| Statistical/adapter defect found after outcomes | Preserve old estimate, mark affected inference invalid, link correction and all dependent claims | Corrected descriptive analysis; no promotion from the same data |
| Better baseline invented after outcomes, as WTP-03 N6 | Record POST-DATA tuning, resource mismatch and mechanism explanation; freeze it for future comparison | Explanation/nomination; no retroactive equal-budget confirmatory win |
| Post-exposure gate or exclusion amendment | Retire that partition to development even if the amended result looks cleaner | Discovery/correction only; confirmation needs new data and frozen rules |
| Ruler or intervention requalified on old fault fixtures | Retain regression receipt separately from withheld-family reliability evidence | Implementation check or calibration-only until operating regime qualifies |
| Fresh preregistered confirmation after repair | New immutable run IDs, qualified versions, independent unit counts, full controls/costs and untouched custody | Only the specific bounded effect supported by those new data |
| Failed cross-track delivery but qualified native mechanism | Keep delivery diagnostics and off-target limits separate from native evidence | Native claim can remain; portability is INTERVENTION_INVALID/UNSUPPORTED |

**Negative claims require positive evidence about the instrument.** A capacity witness alone does not demonstrate reachable search; a search witness does not prove adequate pressure; a qualified ruler does not prove the world demands the target behavior.
Use multi-flags and CAUSE_UNRESOLVED when isolation fails, not a confident single cause manufactured from the taxonomy.
Only qualified prerequisites plus an interval excluding the preregistered meaningful advantage permit BOUNDED_NEGATIVE, conditional on paths/worlds/resources and sampled units.
Budget termination retains consumed resources and censored outcomes; it never counts as a cheap successful learner or a universal failure of emergence. ([R-SCI-02, R-DEV-01, R-MSR-02][REQ]; [typed dispositions][ARCH])

## Conclusion

The implementation priority is a **small fail-closed promotion path whose controls can genuinely fail**, not 24 decorative badges or wholesale restoration of legacy rulers.
Write/update FG01-FG24 tests as demanded by the chosen slice, run the smallest positive/negative/sham and injection checks first, and then qualify the actual operating regime against an independent reference before scientific execution.
These are future scoped actions, not authorization to run campaigns; this pass changed only the two requested documents and performed document/source inspection and validation.
The strongest safeguard is reversible scientific standing: preserve evidence, permit bounded negatives and valid surprises, and make every stronger claim earn new evidence rather than inherit a label.
