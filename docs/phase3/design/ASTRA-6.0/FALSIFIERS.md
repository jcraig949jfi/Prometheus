## 2. A qualified negative needs an adequacy certificate

Every applicable row must be supported; when a prerequisite fails, report that failure instead of pretending the scientific contrast was tested adequately.

| Required eligibility evidence | Direct operational artifact | What its absence permits, and does not permit |
|---|---|---|
| Native capacity within B | Exact witness bytes, independent transition trace, repeated-demand success, broken/sham/rescued witness | CAPACITY_UNESTABLISHED or IMPLEMENTATION_DEFECT; no claim that all latent capacity is absent |
| World demand and feasible effect headroom | Independent oracle, full finite support where claimed, cheap sufficient policies, allowed observation/timing contract | WORLD_INSUFFICIENT for the claimed demand; no cognitive negative from a structural zero |
| Applicable search and pressure | Planted path support, native mutation/acceptance logs, arm-specific recoveries, fixed candidate-evaluation budget | SEARCH_UNDERPOWERED, PRESSURE_UNINFORMATIVE or DEVELOPMENT_UNREACHED; not an impossibility theorem |
| Applicable ruler qualification | Blinded native whole-assay effects/nulls, neutral equivalence, actual promotion-route adversaries, effect/noise coverage | RULER_UNQUALIFIED. Curated-control sensitivity does not automatically cover all natural mechanisms |
| Valid causal intervention, if the claim is causal | Delivery/readback, matched injury and charged work, candidate-specific sham, round trip and rescue | INTERVENTION_INVALID/UNSUPPORTED; qualified native behavior can remain without the causal claim |
| Frozen provenance and custody | Candidate lock, disjoint partitions, all exposure/access events, immutable configurations, attempted/eligible/censored units | PROVENANCE_INVALID; exposed data becomes discovery, never repaired confirmation |
| Qualified uncertainty at the right unit | Independent founder contrasts, registered finite-sample analysis, family-adjusted interval, censoring and stopping receipts | STATISTICALLY_INCONCLUSIVE; significant episodes do not replace independent founders |
| Enough resources actually delivered | Native/sample caps plus process-tree CPU, energy, full search cost, failures/retries and complete job receipts | BUDGET_CENSORED or feasibility stop, not cheap successful acquisition or qualified absence |

The frozen regime targets are one-sided 95% sensitivity lower bound >=0.80, false-positive upper bound <=0.05, and separately preregistered neutral equivalence and power.
The proposed 128 positive/128 null whole-assay panel would require at least 111 recoveries and at most 2 false alarms for those separate binomial confidence gates; these are acceptance calculations, not observations or a simultaneous guarantee across lanes.
With a 96-founder production estimator, each such assay contains 96 planted founder blocks. Passing a deterministic W0 table does not qualify that estimator.
Qualification supports its native mechanism/noise/estimator regime. Extension to an endogenous mechanism class requires an explicit coverage argument and withheld tests; if that bridge fails, natural nulls stay descriptive even when the curated panel passes.

### Typed statistical decisions

| Observed result under the frozen analysis | Disposition and permitted inference |
|---|---|
| Signed family-adjusted interval excludes zero, but spans delta* | Detectable effect; meaningful advantage unresolved. No practical-win claim |
| Relevant lower interval endpoint exceeds delta*, with all applicable gates intact | Meaningful advantage in the registered regime; causal/transfer/nesting labels require their additional evidence |
| Relevant upper interval endpoint is below delta*, with the full applicable adequacy certificate | BOUNDED_NEGATIVE for that registered advantage, initialization/search/history/world/B; this can coexist with a small real positive effect |
| Interval spans delta* | STATISTICALLY_INCONCLUSIVE about practical importance, not confirmation or disproof |
| Any required gate fails | Apparatus/provenance/intervention/budget disposition, with CAUSE_UNRESOLVED or multiple flags if needed; no promoted scientific null |

Detection power against zero at a true delta* is not power to demonstrate an effect greater than delta*.
Four reserved primaries use the frozen family-wise procedure; parked lanes stay p=1 rather than donating alpha after outcomes.
A pilot nominates variance/cost/analysis choices and remains discovery; fresh confirmation never pools its winning pilot founders.

### Zero discoveries are a different endpoint

Zero verified hits among independent founder searches can bound the probability of a **detected hit under that exact search and detector**, provided the sampling and detection event are specified.
A bound on latent successful founders additionally needs perfect native verification or a separately qualified **per-founder** detector with applicable sensitivity and combined confidence accounting.
Never divide a per-founder zero-hit bound by the sensitivity of a whole-assay batch statistic. Those are different events and denominators.
No zero-hit bound establishes a class-wide cognitive limit, and curated planted routes do not establish coverage of all natural paths.

## 4. Six- to twelve-month thesis criteria

These are **program decision rules**, not claims that calendar time or finite tests can refute all reasoning.
Month counts begin with an authorized, staffed program; the present documents do not start a campaign or its spending clock.
Before fresh exposure, nominate the exact comparisons for the thesis review, including world families, thresholds and independent paths; do not choose a favorable subset afterward.

| Review / thesis at risk | Direct evidence required | Revision or rejection rule | Boundary of conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Months 4-6: feasible instrument | One bounded native instrument, exact demand, applicable control qualification, complete costs and a clean-machine rerun by another operator | If one scoped repair still cannot qualify the instrument inside released labor/compute/custody limits, stop expansion and reframe as an instrumentation/feasibility result | Apparatus failure rejects this delivery plan, not development or latent reasoning |
| Month 6: development repays use-case cost | Independent signed Q2 contrast and full lifecycle/frontier analysis at a frozen future-task count, including failed search and retained competence | If ordinary learners dominate the declared frontier and no cost crossover occurs in the registered use case, drop the practical advantage recommendation for that use case | Target-only savings may survive without net practical benefit; this is not a general biological/economic theorem |
| Months 6-9: meaningful transfer survives independent challenge | Predeclared developmental and changed-causal-family transfer comparisons, each with its own applicable certificate and independent scientific path | Require at least two predeclared qualified exclusions of their respective delta*, covering development and new-family transfer, with ordinary competitors explaining gains at lower lifecycle cost, to reject the current advantage thesis in those regimes | Two authored regimes do not sample all possible worlds; fewer or unqualified tests yield a feasibility/inconclusive stop rather than this scientific rejection |
| Months 6-9: multiple physics are worth their cost | A genuinely different second physics tests a signed unresolved prediction; actual adaptation, qualification and independent-checking costs are visible | If the planned comparison yields only redundant discrimination and worse cost, consolidate. If no second path qualified, record a coverage gap, not evidence of substrate equivalence | Rejects compulsory runtime diversity, not potential value of all other physics |
| Months 9-12: recursive claim is operationally distinct | Qualified V/U/S causal protocol, fresh learners, disjoint families, ordinary competitors and independent replication at the registered effect scale | If valid controls and powered results exclude the nested advantage, reject this recursive claim; if only solution/current-learner state transfers, narrow to that mechanism | Broad meta-learning is not ruled out by nesting; a conceptual novelty claim requires separate prior-art evidence |
| Month 12: program makes informative outcomes possible | Evidence ledger shows qualified positives, bounded negatives or a precise feasibility limit, rather than only new names, larger searches and repeated unqualified rulers | Stop/reframe if the central distinction remains unmeasurable after the bounded repair and feasible scoped tests; restart only with a new discriminator and resource plan | A legitimate program-feasibility exit is not a fabricated negative scientific result |
| Any review: rejection is being made unreachable | Successful native witnesses, applicable qualification and narrow intervals satisfy the frozen negative criteria, yet outcomes are repeatedly reclassified as unfair after inspection | Freeze expansion and independently audit the negative-admission route; retain the original registered decision and correction chain | Rejects the program's decision practice; no amount of prose or scale restores falsifiability |

The two-comparison thesis rule is an explicit proposed operationalization of repeated evidence, not an accomplished replication count or a universal statistical theorem.
Positive evidence in one qualified regime and negative evidence in another should produce a boundary map, not a majority vote or forced global verdict.
An ordinary mechanism winning is scientifically useful; neither the absence of novelty nor unclassifiability licenses discarding a valid effect.
