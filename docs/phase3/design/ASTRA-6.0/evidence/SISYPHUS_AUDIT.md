## 3. What can be retained or rebuilt, at what cost, and under which boundaries?

### Takeaway

The following is a **candidate disposition ledger**, not authorization, a reuse percentage, or a selection of the final three tracks. It covers every indexed engine row exactly once. Reuse estimates concern the named bounded capability, not wholesale restoration of the historical service or campaign. Requirement and test IDs identify the conditions under which a candidate could be admitted. [R-OUT-03](../REQUIREMENTS.md#L265-L268)

### Cited Findings

Disposition meanings: **KEEP** preserves a narrow specified contract or immutable evidence, not a certification; **HARDEN** retains an implementation after listed gates; **EXTRACT** ports a pattern/fixture behind a new boundary; **REBUILD** independently reimplements the bounded function; **RETIRE** excludes a mechanism/readout from the specified use, never deletes evidence; **HISTORICAL CONTROL** preserves an explicit seeded, toy or failure comparator; **UNKNOWN** marks missing evidence. All requirement IDs are from [the frozen requirements](../REQUIREMENTS.md).

### Inferences

**Cost and coupling implications, not a final architecture choice:**

- The lowest-scope candidates are historical failure fixtures and small independently checked witnesses, not full legacy services. The table's overlap between reuse and rebuild costs deliberately permits a clean implementation to be cheaper where operational dependencies dominate (SFE/Vivarium, C6 and toolbox). This is an engineering estimate to test, not a measured conclusion. [Architecture protocol and staging](../RSE_ARCHITECTURE.md#L18-L28)
- Early records matter: Daedalus's TODO distinguishes deployment from qualification; Proteus's journal records why the graph was built and exactly what its failure killed; Ares's pressure notes disclaim primary biological sourcing and its annotation changes the next falsifier; Archaeon's prior-art record narrows novelty; Ludus's early cycle-5 demotion prevents rebuilding a representation in response to an exposure artifact. These records change admissible claims, rather than merely adding background. [Daedalus](../../../../../roles/Daedalus/TODO.md), [Proteus](../../../../../roles/Proteus/journal/2026-09-18_m2-7d051790.md), [Ares](../../../../../ares/ARES_CYCLE2_REPORT.md#L259-L276), [Archaeon](../../../../../ops/campaigns/C-001/DEEP_BLOCK_2026-09-27/G_PRIOR_ART.md), [Ludus](../../../../../roles/Ludus/CYCLE_005_verdict_demotion.md#L107-L117)
- Historical mechanisms can become test cases without becoming a universal ontology: block copying, input gating, recurrent latches, stored procedures, exact closure and occupancy-sensitive policy reuse are different phenomena. Nothing requires one internal schema called a skill, belief or organ. Proposed adapters must charge authored information and return unsupported interventions explicitly. [R-ORG-03 and R-CAU-02](../REQUIREMENTS.md), [architecture native interventions](../RSE_ARCHITECTURE.md#L25-L28)
- **Next cheapest discriminators for the parent:** first source-pinned fixtures T02/T04 (copy versus heredity/unit identity), T06/T07 (capacity versus search paths and clocks), T08 (train-only selection), T09 (cheap policy/leak/draw law), and T11 (gate verdict/runtime/exposure). Then compare bounded extraction against independently authored implementations on the same behavioral contracts. These are future test proposals, not executed work or authority to launch campaigns. [Qualification and budgets](../RSE_ARCHITECTURE.md#L167-L185)
