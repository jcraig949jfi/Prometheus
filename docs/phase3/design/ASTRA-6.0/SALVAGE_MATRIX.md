# Preserve useful apparatus, rebuild scientific authority

**Enceladus can preserve many required functions without inheriting an already-qualified RSE.**
This assessment selects 56 bounded component slices from the ASTRA-6.0 evidence notes, not 56 independent engines.
It preserves a conceptual need in 53 slices, proposes retaining runtime cores in 18 and extracting code or patterns in 19, and preserves only narrow interfaces in 19.
Those are overlapping decision axes, not a surviving-program percentage or an estimate of saved engineering work.
The strongest justified reuse is provenance, bounded execution, replay, exact small controls, and failure fixtures; scientific score semantics require separate qualification.
Neither physical propagation, implanted capacity, source-read mechanism labels, nor successful historical execution establishes endogenous transferable or recursive sagacity.
Recommendations remain conditional on the frozen requirements and the admission tests in [FAILURE_TO_GATE_MAP.md](FAILURE_TO_GATE_MAP.md); this document authorizes no campaign or deployment. ([Requirements][REQ]; [architecture][ARCH]; [five-note evidence base][PA])

### Grade correctness separately from claims of reproduction

| Grade | Evidence actually available | Limit on use |
|---|---|---|
| C | Cited ASTRA audit inspected implementation supporting a narrow behavior | Static inspection is not execution, integration correctness, or scientific validity |
| P | Cited audit read a primary report, ruling, or erratum | Historical numbers are reported, not independently reproduced here |
| F | Cited audit executed a specified isolated fixture/probe | Only that fixture is checked; no field error-rate or full-system claim follows |
| D | Dossier/index/catalogue description only | Provisional candidate; inspect exact API and source before adoption |
| U | Required source, runtime guarantee, or compatibility unestablished | Selection blocked pending a bounded inspection |
| V | Original source inspected in this synthesis | Used only for the statistical/correction checks explicitly described below |

Grades can coexist: C/P does not outrank an independent execution, and F does not turn an inadequate ruler into a qualified one.
All candidate recommendations and cost bands below are engineering judgments, not additional correctness evidence.
Source prefixes resolve through the notes: **SY:Fxx** means Sisyphus finding Fxx; **SY:Exx** its indexed row; **TA:Sxx** Tantalus primary-source register; **TI:name** Tityos source key; **IX:Pxx/Dxx** Ixion source/disposition; **PA:Cx** prior-art catalogue section.
Reference links lead to original paths when the audit supplies them, otherwise explicitly to the weaker dossier/index route.

### Count decisions on explicit, noninterchangeable axes

Each row C01-C56 counts once and has exactly one recommendation.
`S` means the slice controls scientific semantics: organism dynamics, world demand, selection, intervention, or measurement.
`I` means infrastructure/evidence plumbing, even when its correctness is essential to science; keeping such a slice cannot count as keeping scientific semantics.
`C/R/W` records conceptual need / runtime route / wrapper retention: C=Y or N; R=core, fragment, none, or ?; W=Y, N, or ?.
Concept Y includes required comparators and negative fixtures, not endorsement of the old scientific interpretation.
Runtime core means KEEP/HARDEN; fragment means EXTRACT and can mean a rewritten pattern rather than copied executable bytes; historical controls have no proposed active scientific runtime reuse.
Wrapper Y means only a named narrow API/schema/receipt boundary, subject to qualification; it never means preserving the surrounding campaign, service topology, or institution wholesale.

Costs are **experienced-engineer days**, approximately eight focused hours each, for the narrow row's inspection, adaptation, and small tests.
`A / B` compares the proposed salvage/disposition route with a minimal clean rebuild of the same useful requirement slice; RETIRE costs include safe disconnection, and historical-control costs cover a small fixture.
UNKNOWN bands price an inspection spike, not successful reuse. No band includes full campaigns, external scientific replication, fleet migration, procurement, or ongoing operation.
Bands overlap because sources, adapters, and tests are coupled; **do not sum them**, infer calendar duration, or convert rows into dollars saved. ([SY cost contract][SY]; [TA cost contract][TA]; [TI cost contract][TI]; [IX cost contract][IX])

### Original statistical sources constrain even the corrections

Reading `roles/Harmonia/qualification/h0h5/qualification_rules.py:242-247,273-284` supports Tityos's narrow arithmetic finding: df=11 selects the df=12 table entry, and any adjusted alpha other than .05 selects .025 in `paired_contrast`.
The displayed alpha can therefore change without the matching interval changing for three-plus primaries; retaining the plan interface does not justify retaining this implementation.
This synthesis did not rerun Tityos's synthetic probes or estimate the historical impact on all consumers. ([TI:QR][QR])

Reading `roles/Diomedes/coordinate_census.py:175-210` supports the low-bit sampler concern, but line 168 requires nonzero `err` to pass `gate_exceeds_error`.
The inspected Nyx report's claim that zero width passes ANY gate is therefore too broad for this source; `includes_zero` also depends on the point/interval, not always False.
Rebuild the bootstrap, preserve the allegation and this bounded correction, and do not replace one unverified universal verdict with another. ([TA:S25][DIOM]; [TA:S13][NYXFAIL])

### Missing functions must be budgeted rather than credited to salvage

The inventory does not establish a qualified minimal rewrite kernel, a complete recurrent mutable-update track, or three independently validated substrate/world/ruler pathways.
It also leaves the nested fresh-V/U/S runner, full lifecycle transfer/censoring accounting, regime-specific sample sizing, sealed custody enforcement, and calibrated physical energy measurement to implementation.
These are **requirement gaps outside the 56-row assessed legacy sample**, not extra REBUILD rows silently added to its denominator.
Ordinary learned libraries, differentiable plasticity, and self-modifying weight matrices remain serious competitor families, not claimed local reproductions or proof of Q6. ([Architecture sections 3,5,6,10][ARCH]; [PA:P1,P4,P5][PA])

Prefer a narrow receipt/executor/control integration over wholesale restoration, and compare its measured adaptation effort against the listed clean-build band before committing.
The independent decisive checker must be separately authored from the behavioral contract; copying an extracted scorer into two wrappers defeats R-CAU-03.
Qualification cost can dominate apparent code savings, particularly where the frozen sensitivity/FPR bounds require independent regimes and families.
If the cap cannot support them, narrow the question before exposure and deliver calibration-only evidence rather than lowering the gate. ([TI qualification budget][TI]; [architecture section 6][ARCH])

## Conclusion

The strongest justified reuse is **a small evidence-and-execution spine plus source-pinned control specimens, subject to independent qualification**, with scientific authority rebuilt around explicit behavioral contracts.
This preserves the requirements that history made more urgent while refusing to preserve interpretations merely because their code still runs.
Next implementation work should write or update the linked conformance, positive/negative/sham, injection, and independent-reference tests, then execute them in an isolated approved slice; this documentation pass ran none of those campaigns or qualification suites.
The useful deliverable is not a large survival percentage but a cheaper route to a result whose scope, alternatives, costs, and failure conditions remain reconstructable.
