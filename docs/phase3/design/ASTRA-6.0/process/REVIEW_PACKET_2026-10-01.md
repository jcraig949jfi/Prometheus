----- 3. Proposed design, not executed science -----
Start A0: a costed native local-rewrite machine, exactly solvable hidden-state
world, breakable witnesses, reset/leakage tests, and independently authored
checker. Qualify planted search paths before interpreting developmental nulls.
Then test one developmental contrast, useful compression or revision, and
independent causal replication. Spatial-local and numerical-recurrent physics
are later alternatives only when they distinguish a specific prediction.
Recursive claims require history-altered construction of fresh learners and
fresh task acquisition, with reset/crossover/lesion/sham/rescue controls. Even
success would not establish a category outside broad meta-learning.
