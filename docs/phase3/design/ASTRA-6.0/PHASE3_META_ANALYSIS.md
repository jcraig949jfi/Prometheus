# Make developmental claims survive independent measurement

**Prometheus should become a staged experimental observatory, beginning with one tiny native organism/world path and an independent checker, not another unqualified engine collection.**
The record supports heterogeneous pre-paradigmatic experimental research: real bounded science and useful corrective practice coexist with prototype apparatus that is not qualified for its strongest claims.
Neither a categorical hobby dismissal nor an assertion that recursive reasoning already exists fits that evidence.
The scientific target is whether experience constructs reusable operations that reduce later acquisition cost, and then whether experience improves the process constructing future learning processes.
This report recommends the post-audit sequential MVP while preserving the earlier first-principles freeze; it does not pretend that historical knowledge preceded that freeze.
The prospective ceiling is **480 core-hours: 372 in days 1-90, 72 conditional, and 36 protected audit**, with zero GPU, 30 kWh, and an optional 20,000-token campaign inference cap.
These are unmeasured planning limits, not achieved runtime, purchased resources, authorized spending, or new RSE evidence. ([MVP resource ledger](MVP_90_DAYS.md#L308-L342), [freeze](process/STAGE_I_FREEZE.md))

## 6. Cognitive development is a chain of separately testable hypotheses

The working chain is **experience -> regularity -> representation -> compression -> reuse -> abstraction -> higher-order processes**.
It is not a law of development, an inevitable ladder, or a claim that all intelligent systems must traverse these names in this order.
Each arrow needs an independently discriminating intervention rather than a narrative inferred from improving accuracy. ([Frozen developmental requirements](REQUIREMENTS.md#L46-L60))

| Proposed transition | Physical hypothesis and discriminating evidence | Simpler rival that must remain live |
|---|---|---|
| Experience to regularity | Correlated histories change future responses more than equal-volume shuffled/irrelevant histories | More examples or a fixed cue trigger |
| Regularity to representation | Persistent organization mediates prediction/action across nuisance recoding; remove/restore it | Observer-readable correlation with no causal use |
| Representation to compression | Selection and consolidation replace redundant structure while preserving prediction/intervention fidelity | Lossy forgetting, serialization trick, or free decoder |
| Compression to reuse | The retained operation lowers fresh acquisition cost; content lesion removes the saving | Stored answers, library size, or extra pretraining |
| Reuse to abstraction | A manipulable construction applies across new compositions and responds appropriately to changed laws | A finite dictionary or supplied semantic operator |
| Abstraction to higher-order processes | Retained operations construct or regulate later operations with causal, costed benefit | Fixed optimizer state or analyst relabeling |

Latent capacity is what a bounded substrate can express; a constructed witness supplies a lower bound, not a search guarantee.
Developmental capacity concerns what allowed histories and native modification can construct from a specified initial distribution, measured by reachability probability and cost.
Realized competence is checkpoint performance with retained learning disabled but legitimate working-state computation intact; use prequential measurement if the separation is invalid.
Transferable sagacity requires reduced new-family acquisition cost, retention, and causal reuse; report target-only savings separately from full-lifecycle amortization.
Recursive sagacity adds improvement in the construction of future learning processes, not merely a better current learner or larger inherited answer store.
None of these finite estimands certifies general reasoning, biological equivalence, consciousness, or a privileged non-ML category.

Creation/deletion/copying, internal work without immediate action, multiple timescales, and rule-bearing state are high-value affordances to test against simpler controls.

Track B offers spatially local interaction and propagation-limited access; it can expose an effect of locality that an A wrapper cannot simulate away by naming.
Track C offers quantized recurrent numerical dynamics with writable couplings/update coefficients; differentiability, precision, and numerical scheduling remain strong priors.
No track is ontology-free; physical diversity means different permitted transitions, not different narratives around the same decisive runtime. ([Track specification](ENGINE_PORTFOLIO.md#L44-L71))

## 8. Developmental physics must identify what selects, consolidates, and composes

There is no free abstraction engine in this proposal. The **selector** is a declared native retention/competition process or outer search pressure whose information and cost are visible.
At the physical level, survival of records, links, local reaction patterns, or numerical couplings changes which future transitions are reachable and reused.
Consolidation is a testable transformation that retains/reorganizes useful material across episodes while transient work is reset, rather than an analyst's label for age.
Compression can result from merging repeated substructures, pruning redundant paths, sharing addressing routes, or numerical reuse; these are candidate mechanisms, not installed cognitive goals.

A reusable object is operationally material that can be readdressed, applied in more than one context, and composed with other material through native transitions.
Object construction can occur through copying/linking/editing records in A, persistent interacting regions in B, or recurrent/update organization in C.
An abstraction claim requires invariance to nuisance variation and appropriate sensitivity to causal variation, not merely a stable symbol or clustered embedding.
Higher-order construction requires an acquired operation to alter the production/use of later operations; internal names alone do not establish that relation.

Distinguish **parameter optimization** from constructive development by what causally changes available operations, routing, reuse, and future learning trajectories.
New nodes or tensor shapes alone can be functionally trivial; fixed-topology parameter changes can implement substantial constructive behavior.
The distinction is therefore intervention-based and comparative, not a metaphysical separation between structural algorithms and numerical learning.
Compare editable structure with fixed topology under matched memory/compute, and compare mutable update physics with fixed-rule learners under full exposure accounting.

Description length must include code, library, decoder, adapter, and residual predictive/causal error; a short genome with a large interpreter is not automatically compressed cognition.
A longer reusable mechanism can reduce total future acquisition cost, so smaller serialization is neither necessary nor sufficient for useful developmental compression.
Evaluate loss, retention, code burden, target cost, and lifecycle crossover jointly rather than rewarding short strings directly. ([Compression requirements](REQUIREMENTS.md#L100-L119))
DreamCoder already learns program libraries and search; differentiable plasticity already learns useful update structure, and SRWM already performs runtime self-modification.
These precedents force a causal evidence claim, not a novelty claim based on the words construction or self-reference. ([Prior-art note](evidence/PRIOR_ART_AND_CATALOGUES.md#L13-L104))

## 9. World depth is a policy bound, not a grid dimension

Define a minimum sufficient policy only relative to a declared controller class, information interface, success criterion, task distribution, and resource ordering.
For a scalar declared cost it is the minimum cost among policies meeting that criterion; for a resource vector it is a set of nondominated sufficient policies, not one universal minimum.
The cheapest demonstrated solver gives an **upper bound** on necessary resources; failure of tried cheap solvers is not a proven lower bound.
A lower bound requires an argument over the specified class, or exhaustive enumeration of a complete finite class with verified coverage.
Equality of a proved lower bound and a successful witness can certify an exact minimum only in that bounded setting. ([World specification](REQUIREMENTS.md#L62-L76), [frozen policy definition](RSE_ARCHITECTURE.md#L44-L55))

W0 reveals a balanced hidden bit, inserts blank delay, then presents identical final observations with no writable external channel.
A stateless controller has expected accuracy at most 1/2; a two-state retained witness can reach 1 when timing/cursor leakage is excluded.
This is one exact memory demand, not a reasoning theorem; repeated independent cue/query pairs expose one-shot latches.
Its 12 finite histories and eight affordance cells yield 96 base rollouts, not 96 independent statistical units. ([W0](MVP_90_DAYS.md#L128-L149))
W1 contrasts structured and shuffled equal-volume histories under hidden XOR laws; delay transfer is within-family, while W1b's event-driven law change is a distinct mechanism test. ([W1/W1b](MVP_90_DAYS.md#L151-L166))
W2 tests primitive-map composition, but an eight-entry transition table is a mandatory cheap solver; W2b introduces an irreversible map rather than merely renamed tools. ([W2/W2b](MVP_90_DAYS.md#L170-L177))
**W3-R/W3b-R replace W3/W3b.** Three accuracy-3/4 cues yield 32 weighted independent-law cases or 16 correlated-law cases with c2=c3. Under independence, hand enumeration predicts majority accuracy 27/32 and low-price gain 0.05 normalized; query at 1/32 only under independence, never at 1/8 or under correlation. Retained c1 resolves the independent purchased cues' 3/8 disagreement probability; native execution remains unverified. ([Corrected revision worlds](MVP_90_DAYS.md#L179-L190))

Each world reports a depth vector: history distinctions, retention horizon, branching, delay, information price, irreversibility, composition range, source ambiguity, and law change.
Increase one demand at a time, demonstrate solvability, and try direct formulas, constants, lookup, short-history, finite-state, and bounded-search policies before calling it cognitively deep.
Training, qualification, discovery, and sealed confirmation require separate exposure identities; new seeds, encodings, compositions, causal laws, and authors are different novelty levels.
Two independently authored generators help identify implementation bias but do not create a statistically representative population of causal families.
If a cheap policy solves the supposed demand, lower the claim or repair the world before new data; increasing size does not repair an invalid construct.

## 10. Search should test when construction beats inheritance

Evolution is optional: enumeration, stochastic search, direct synthesis, gradient methods, and fixed meta-learners are valid competitors.
Inheritance should initially specify low-level material and permissible update behavior; development can construct task-specific or reusable organization; world consequences supply information and selection pressure.
Whether that division is advantageous is the experiment, not an axiom about cognition. ([Search requirements](REQUIREMENTS.md#L78-L92))

The core comparison crosses history H, retained modification P, and initial information I at one resource regime first.
Structured and shuffled histories contain the same episodes; clamping removes cross-episode retained changes without disabling legitimate working-state inference.
Direct-solution founders carry more supplied information and must be charged for it; their success is a valid rival, not a prohibited shortcut by definition.
The primary H-by-P interaction tests whether useful history depends on native modification, not whether abstraction or recursion has appeared.

Lifetime duration, law diversity, maintenance costs, and inheritance bottlenecks can make constructing solutions cheaper than carrying all answers.
They can also eliminate competent pathways or make development too expensive to repay; qualify planted ascent, equal-cost plateaus, and competence-neutral/worse-cost bridges under the actual search kernel. Competence-decreasing valleys remain outside its claimed accessibility boundary.
A strict-ascent algorithm cannot use a neutral bridge, so its failure does not settle the underlying geometry.
Count whole-population evaluations, rejected mutations, invalid draws, and failed searches, not just the champion's efficient final run.

The MVP proposes 32 candidates over at most 32 generations including generation zero: **1,024 development-only evaluations per founder block**. Later generations reserve **24 cost-greedy and 8 competence-neutral bridge slots**; at equal competence the greedy arm rejects higher cost, while the bridge accepts with a fixed independent **1/2 probability even when cost worsens**, under unchanged hard caps; lower competence is rejected.
Slots retain their accepted parents until the next proposal; global cost ranking cannot evict bridge states, and final champion ranking occurs only after the fixed budget. Every fourth generation's uniform proposal consumes a bridge slot; recombination and open-ended ecology remain deferred.
Competence, acquisition cost, retention, diversity, and resource use remain separate pressure axes; any scalarization belongs in the preregistration, not a hidden reward soup.
Quality-diversity is a hypothesis about useful exploration, not evidence that archive size or novelty labels constitute scientific progress.
An ordinary fixed learner winning the total-cost comparison is a useful architecture decision; forced-path reachability and unforced search recovery are separate gates, not repaired by a tie coin after cost rejection. ([Search implementation plan](MVP_90_DAYS.md#L105-L111))

## 11. Qualification must recover effects and reject persuasive false stories

Every ruler version/regime needs blinded known positives, known negatives, neutral/sham handling, effect/noise sweeps, saturation checks, and actual promotion-route fault tests.
An effect-positive establishes detection sensitivity; a planted software defect is instead a positive for fault detection, and the two denominators must never be conflated.
The frozen targets are one-sided 95% sensitivity lower bound >=0.80 and false-positive upper bound <=0.05, plus preregistered equivalence and power.
Controls must traverse the real loader/statistic/admission chain, not a convenient substitute battery. ([Qualification contract](RSE_ARCHITECTURE.md#L90-L114))

The current MVP plans 128 independent positive and 128 independent null **whole-assay trials**, plus 64 paired neutral assays per released primary regime.
At n=128, at least 111 recoveries and at most two false alarms meet the separate exact-binomial bounds; these are arithmetic acceptance values, not observed results.
If each trial contains 96 planted founder blocks, the positive/null panel contains 24,576 planted blocks, not 256 inexpensive single episodes.
Authored controls avoid evolutionary search, not simulation/scoring cost; feasibility is unbenchmarked. Their curated operating regime does not certify sensitivity to every endogenous mechanism. Sham margins are 0.05 for Q2/Q3 and 0.005 for Q4, with Q5 inheriting its nominee's margin. ([MVP calibration](MVP_90_DAYS.md#L217-L240))

The independent unit is a founder block with its own initialization, search stream, and world draw; clones, descendants, episodes, and rescue copies are repeated measures.
Plan four two-sided primaries under family alpha=0.05 with Holm; parked hypotheses remain p=1, not spare alpha recycled after results.
For **Q2/Q3 and their Q5 nominee**, n=96, SD=0.50, **delta*=0.20** gives about 0.92241 normal-approximation detection power, conditional on an unverified model. **Q4 uses delta*=0.02**, and Q5 inherits the nominated threshold, metric, and primary stratum rather than shopping among them.

## 14. Recursive sagacity is a nested causal claim within broad meta-learning

The strongest operational definition is: experience changes a process V such that it constructs a better fresh learning process U, which then improves fresh solution-state S acquisition on a distinct family under matched resources.
S/U/V describe causal roles identified through interventions, not mandatory anatomical modules or instructions installed in the organism.
Changing S is task learning; a better U teaching fresh S is learning-to-learn; improving construction of fresh U is the extra nesting claim.
No finite test proves this lies outside broad meta-learning, and a conventional optimizer state is admissible if it genuinely satisfies the causal protocol. ([Frozen nesting](RSE_ARCHITECTURE.md#L78-L88))

First develop independent organisms on family A versus matched control histories and identify old/developed V or qualified native equivalents.
Cross V provenance with ability to change U; initialize fresh identically distributed U and S, then expose family B with equal information and compute.
Freeze the resulting U-B, initialize fresh S again, and test acquisition on sealed family C without carried A/B solutions or undeclared optimizer state.
Cross U donors independently of V, lesion the specific developed V change, use a damage-matched sham, and rescue the mediated B-to-C advantage.
Repeat on fresh D-to-E families and an independently implemented decisive world/ruler; report tested nesting depth and lifecycle cost.
If distributed state cannot be sliced safely, try qualified whole-organism clamping/counterfactual replay; otherwise the mediation is unidentifiable, not absent.

The actual prior art is substantive, not a list of names: DreamCoder jointly learns reusable program libraries and search, already challenging a simple library-as-sagacity story. ([Ellis et al., PLDI 2021](https://doi.org/10.1145/3453483.3454080))
Differentiable plasticity optimizes recurrent weights and plasticity through outer gradient training, providing an ordinary online-learning competitor. ([Miconi et al., ICML 2018](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/miconi18a.html))
SRWM learns runtime weight self-modification through outer-product/delta updates; self-reference alone cannot establish a novel category. ([Irie et al., ICML 2022](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/irie22b.html))
Enhanced POET couples challenge generation and transfer; it motivates a later curriculum treatment, not contamination of fixed confirmation. ([Wang et al., ICML 2020](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/wang20l.html))
Avida's rewarded evolutionary stepping-stones concern genomic evolution, not automatically lifetime developmental reuse. ([Lenski et al., Nature 2003](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature01568))
Causal representation learning asks how useful causal variables arise; this constrains claims that analyst-defined V/U/S boundaries are prior-free. ([Scholkopf et al., 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.11107))
MDL makes description length model-relative; it does not identify shorter encodings with reasoning. ([Rissanen, 1978](https://research.ibm.com/publications/modeling-by-shortest-data-description))
These **seven primary prior-art sources were reviewed mostly at abstract/landing-page level**, not deeply reproduced; no comprehensive novelty clearance, paper-scale baseline, or published-cost replication is claimed. ([Review limits](evidence/PRIOR_ART_AND_CATALOGUES.md#L115-L122))

## 24. Explicit answers Q1-Q10 preserve the distinctions that matter

### Q1 - Scientific legitimacy
Current Prometheus is heterogeneous pre-paradigmatic experimental research with real bounded science, serious corrections, and prototype apparatus unqualified for many strong claims.
WTP-03, Aether, and Polyhymnia support the positive side; seeded-origin corrections, missing baselines, broken statistics, and self-verdicting gates constrain it. Phase 3 needs executed qualification, independent causal paths, and reachable unfavorable conclusions, not a categorical hobby verdict.

### Q2 - Cognitive sufficiency
A negative becomes scientifically interpretable when a native within-budget witness, genuine world demand, qualified search/pressure, sensitive ruler, valid interventions, custody, and precision jointly make the registered effect observable.
Only then can an interval excluding delta* support BOUNDED_NEGATIVE for the stated paths/worlds/B; there is no universal competence threshold after which NO EMERGENCE means all reasoning is absent.

### Q3 - Developmental adequacy
Costed persistent material, native rewriting/revisitation, multiple timescales, and selection/consolidation pressures can support progressive construction; a supplied planner is not required.
Physical retention and composition of reusable structures are the compression/object-construction hypotheses; causal reuse, full decoder accounting, nuisance/law contrasts, and future acquisition must distinguish them from storage or parameter fitting alone.

### Q4 - Evolution versus development
Inheritance should initially provide low-level material and update possibilities, development should be allowed to construct reusable organization, and worlds should teach through consequences, uncertainty, and information costs.
This division is a tested hypothesis: inherited direct solvers, stochastic search, ordinary optimizers, and fixed meta-learners remain eligible to win on a fully charged resource frontier.

### Q5 - Recursive sagacity
The strongest operational observation is history-altered V improving construction of fresh U on B and fresh S acquisition on C, with reset/crossover/lesion/sham/rescue and D/E independent repetition.
It is stronger than showing a better task learner or library, but cannot prove a category outside broad meta-learning; report tested nesting depth, mechanisms, and cost rather than unlimited self-improvement.

### Q6 - Epistemic escape
Plausible discovery beyond familiar labels requires structural generation, independent physical consequences, calibrated measurement, and admission without LLM recognition.
One-third discovery allocation and bounded unclassified-anomaly review protect that route; opacity never proves novelty, and authored physics/observation priors remain disclosed.

### Q7 - Single engine versus portfolio
Recommend a sequential three-physics observatory with six question lanes, beginning with one A0 path and independently authored checker rather than three runtimes on day one.
Add tracks only for qualified competing predictions; consolidate if diversity adds overhead without discrimination. Shared receipt plumbing must not become shared decisive scientific code.

### Q8 - Existing machinery
In the **56-slice sample**, scientific/infrastructure counts are **35/21**: conceptual need survives **33/35 and 20/21**, conditional runtime cores **8/35 and 10/21**, and extraction **13/35 and 6/21**.
These are not a global program fraction; no whole engine is currently established as ASTRA-qualified. Normatively prefer roughly one-quarter scientific and one-half infrastructure runtime-core reuse in a future demand-matched build, subject to qualification rather than quota; preserve warranted questions and controls more broadly than legacy implementations.

### Q9 - First experiments
First execute W0's exact hidden-bit demand with a breakable native witness, cheap stateless policy, full reset/meter tests, and independent checker; then qualify planted search paths and one H/P/I W1 contrast.
Those experiments separate capacity, apparatus, reachability, and developmental advantage more cheaply than a three-runtime search; one Q3-or-Q4 discriminator and independent Q5 follow only after the earlier gates.

### Q10 - Failure
At 6-12 months, qualified replicated exclusions of meaningful developmental/transfer advantage, ordinary competitors dominating lifecycle costs, or persistent inability to make nesting measurable within feasible budgets require stopping or reframing this program.
Instrumentation defects are not cognitive negatives, but neither are they permission for endless expansion; a fresh discriminator, not more scale alone, is necessary to restart.

## 25. Highest-value unknowns determine the next experiment, not another grand design

The first uncertainty is whether A0's native witness/reset/codec contract and its independently implemented checker can qualify inside S0's engineering cap.
The next is whole-assay throughput: 256 calibration batches of a 96-founder estimator can be the dominant cost despite tiny organisms.
Then test planted-path reachability and ordinary-baseline headroom before asking whether natural developmental contrasts have usable variance or practical effect size.
These are ranked experimental blockers with role owners, bounded effort estimates, and consequences in [OPEN_QUESTIONS.md](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md), not instructions to start new work now.

Load-bearing assumptions include faithful native accounting, genuinely sealed custody, independent implementer availability, biologically unpretentious world depth, and feasible intervention boundaries.
Each assumption has a test and a consequence rather than an optimistic assertion of feasibility. ([Assumption ledger](ASSUMPTIONS.md))
The biggest conceptual unknown is whether the proposed nested distinction identifies a causally separable phenomenon rather than repartitioning ordinary meta-learning.
The biggest practical unknown is whether engineering and review, not CPU supply, can sustain sufficiently independent qualification.
The biggest sampling unknown is generality beyond a few authored finite families; no additional denominator manipulation resolves it.

This package does not settle absent host-local artifacts, historic campaign raw results, unspent sealed materials, live deployments, or deeper prior-art reproduction.
It keeps the WTP-03 and Aether positives, Ananke's planted challenge, seeded/construction corrections, and auditor/statistical defects in the same evidence argument without biasing every correction positive or negative.
The completed report is a decision recommendation with prospective tests; documentation checks do not transform it into a functioning observatory.
The next useful implementation should write/update and run native transition, reset, leakage, cap, oracle, statistical-boundary, intervention, and correction tests before any claim-bearing experiment.

If Prometheus were started today with everything learned but none of its engines written, I would first build one tiny costed local-rewrite organism, one exactly solvable hidden-state world, deliberately breakable positive/negative/sham controls, and an independently authored checker with immutable exposure and resource receipts, because that is the smallest apparatus able to separate real capacity from a supplied answer or a misleading ruler; only after it qualifies would I run one bounded developmental contrast, then admit a second and eventually third genuinely different physical track when a specific unresolved prediction justifies their cost, making the three-track observatory contingent rather than three runtimes promised on day one.
