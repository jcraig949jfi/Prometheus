Architect: FABLE-5.1 (seat Dionysus). Companion to RSE_ARCHITECTURE.md
(sections 5 to 8 define the worlds, substrates, experiments and hypotheses)
and REQUIREMENTS.md (section 1 defines every term). Written 2026-10-01,

## 0. How to read this file

**"Engine" here means an experiment program, not a machine.** The prompt
asks for a portfolio of engines. The old engines each had their own
substrate, worlds, rulers and seat, and so could not be compared (the
Sisyphus crawler found 2 of 146 rulers ever tested on a second substrate).
In this design there is one kernel, one foundry of worlds, a few substrates
and nine experiments. Each experiment has one question. Each is specified
below with the prompt's fields.

**Nothing below is named "the RSE".** Recursive sagacity is a property with
a test (REQUIREMENTS XFER-07), and P6 is where it is tested.

**What is fixed and what is not.** The questions, controls and claim
ceilings are the design. Lineage counts, budgets and thresholds are
defaults; a campaign fixes its own in a preregistration commit before it
runs.

**Planning figures** (all to be replaced by measurements):

| quantity | figure | basis |
|---|---|---|
| WM kernel throughput on M1, all threads | 100 million organism instructions per second | a floor; the P1 prototype measured 1.70 billion on a much smaller machine |
| one M1-day of search at that floor | 8.6 x 10^12 instructions, or 86 million lifetimes of 10^5 instructions | arithmetic |
| power, CPU campaign | 200 W at the wall | assumed; no host has ever been metered (ENRG-02) |
| power, GPU campaign | 350 W at the wall | assumed; the GPU's limit is 180 W |
| energy per M1-day | 4.8 kWh (CPU), 8.4 kWh (GPU) | arithmetic on the assumptions above |
| tokens | order of magnitude only | the only measured figure in this package is 4,174,516 tokens for seven salvage workers |

**Lineages.** "24 lineages" always means 24 independent founders and world
instances (independence level I2), never 24 seeds of one founder.

**Inference.** No experiment calls a model while it runs. Models are used
at typed forks: specification, implementation, independent
re-implementation, attack, naming rivals, interpretation after
certification. P8 is the one exception: a model is one of the generators
under test.

## 2. A correction to hypothesis H1, found while writing this file

RSE_ARCHITECTURE.md section 8 states H1 as: learners with only fast state
certify no BUILD beyond their fast capacity; in RETAIN they sit at the
no-carry bound. Writing P2 out showed what that is. In RETAIN the harness
resets fast state at every episode boundary, and the no-carry bound is
exact for any policy that carries nothing across the boundary. A learner
with only fast state is such a policy. So the prediction is a theorem of the
state partition. A violation could only be a leak. The frozen falsifier
F-H1 already half-says this ("first suspicion: a leak or a failed reset").

A theorem is not a hypothesis. H1 is therefore split, and the split is
recorded as a dated annotation in RSE_ARCHITECTURE.md section 13.5 and in
FALSIFIERS.md.

- **H1a, conformance.** Fast-state-only learners score at the no-carry bound
  on RETAIN probes. True by construction. It is kept as a kernel check: any
  other result is a harness defect.
- **H1b, the empirical claim: amortised against constructed.** A learner
  that was trained offline and can only carry activations or a token window
  through a lifetime (even when that state is not reset) shows two limits
  that a learner writing persistent structure does not:
  1. Retention. The bits it carries across a gap fall with the length of
     the gap and with interference, and never exceed the capacity of the
     carried state (the second half is arithmetic; the first is measured).
  2. Compression. It certifies COMPRESS within a lifetime only for kinds of
     structure that were present in its training distribution. On RECOMBINE
     families with withheld kinds of structure it falls to the lookup bound.
  A learner that updates weights during life is predicted to pass on the
  withheld families, at a sample cost at least ten times that of the
  designed constructive organism.
- Falsified if a fast-state learner certifies COMPRESS on withheld-structure
  families in sealed worlds, or if the weight-updating learner does so at a
  cost within a factor of two of the designed organism. Either result says
  the gap this design aims at is not where I put it (FALSIFIERS F-T5).

One more reference learner follows from this. A sequence model with an
external read-and-write memory is the conventional answer to "it cannot
build", and it is BUILD by design, at scaffold level S3. It is added to the
reference arm as REF-d so that its certified levels and costs are on the
table beside everything else.

## 3. P1 -- calibration slice

**Name / working descriptor.** Calibration slice for BUILD: one world, one
substrate, designed organisms only, every ruler in the chain.

**Scientific question.** Can the apparatus tell a builder from a holder
from a cheat when the answer is known, and with what error rates?

**Organism physics.** WM kernel, first version: compiled, integer, every
word decodes, three declared stores. All organisms are designed:
- builder(m): stores the first m of K mappings in the persistent store. Its
  true probe rate is known: m/K + (1 - m/K)/R.
- holder: the same mechanism in fast memory. It must pass HOLD and fail
  BUILD.
- impostors: a constant; a lookup table in the genome; a reader of a leaked
  label; a smuggler that tries to carry state through an undeclared channel;
  a store with the right identifiers and empty contents.

**Developmental physics.** A lifetime is E episodes of T trials. The harness
zeroes fast state at each episode boundary and never touches the persistent
store. There is no search inside the gate. Forks at episode boundaries
supply the interchange and lesion twins.

**World physics.** RETAIN. A hidden mapping from K stimuli to R responses
is fixed for one life and drawn fresh for the next. After each response the
correct answer is shown. A probe is the first presentation, within an
episode, of a stimulus met in an earlier episode. For any policy carrying
nothing across an episode boundary, each probe is correct with probability
exactly 1/R, so the count of correct probes is binomial under the null. Two
twins ship with it: an absence twin in which the mapping is redrawn every
episode, so building cannot pay; and a scrambled twin with permuted
observation channels. A RECALL micro-world supplies the HOLD ruler's bound.

**Evolutionary/search pressure.** None in the gate. After the gate passes,
one search-power curve: the designed builder with d instructions knocked
out, d from 1 to the whole program, under three acceptance rules, 24
lineages per cell.

**Measurement stack.** Class exclusion in exact binomial arithmetic with a
false-positive bound of 10^-6; the certified level in bits; store
interchange between two lives with a permuted-store sham; lesion of used
against unused cells; reset equivalence (after a reset, behaviour equals a
fresh machine holding only the store); a leak probe on never-seen stimuli;
a power gate that computes the exact power of every preregistered verdict
from the schedules alone and refuses to run below 0.99.

**Known-positive qualification.** builder(K) is certified at log2(R) bits
per stimulus. Each builder(m) is certified with a lower bound that never
exceeds its known true rate. Interchange returns FLIP for the builder.

**Known-negative qualification.** builder(0) and the holder fail BUILD. The
holder passes HOLD. Interchange returns NO-EFFECT for the holder and for
the sham. Every impostor fails. Each control has a fire test: the
instrument is broken on purpose and the control must fail.

**Cheap baseline.** The baseline ladder shipped with RETAIN: constant, most
common answer, echo of the last feedback, memoryless, genome lookup. All
are at 1/R on probes by construction, and the receipt shows it.

**Causal intervention.** Store interchange at an episode boundary, swept
over swap points; lesion of the cells the organism uses, against a matched
number it does not use.

**Transfer test.** None is claimed. Qualification runs on sealed lives the
design never touched.

**Primary hallucination risk.** A harness that does not really reset fast
state. Then the ruler alone passes a holder as a builder. The prototype
showed exactly this (fire test FT1), and only the separate
reset-equivalence check caught it. Second: one author for kernel and oracle.

**Primary false-negative risk.** Verdicts frozen without checking that they
are attainable. The prototype's first gate failed this way. The power gate
is the repair.

**Expected compute.** Minutes for the gate. Under one M1-day for the
search-power curve at the planning floor. No GPU.

**Expected inference use.** The largest of any experiment relative to its
compute, because P1 is where the kernel is built: specification,
implementation, a second implementation of the world and the ruler by a
different author (a different model family if one is reachable), one attack
round. Order of 40 million tokens, unmeasured.

**Expected energy profile.** CPU only; under 5 kWh.

**Reusable Prometheus components.** Charon's three-valued verdict with
counts (M-01); Techne's certify and constancy probe (M-02); Hecate's
corruption operators (M-03) and evaluator contract (W-09); Harmonia's
preregistration checks (M-05); Nemesis's trivial responders (M-04); the
toolbox receipt (I-02); the manifest tool (M-14); the Crius designed
programs as plants to port (O-03); the reference-plus-compiled pattern
(O-04). prototype/p1_slice/ is the first version of the whole slice.

**New components required.** The WM kernel; the RETAIN family with its
certificate and twins; the class-exclusion ruler library; the runner's
refusal gates; custody of sealed lives outside the working tree.

**Kill criteria.** FALSIFIERS F-D1: if by day 45 the calibration set is not
sorted at 95% accuracy with a 95% lower confidence limit, or a fire test
cannot be made to fail, all other work stops and the kernel is simplified.
F-D3: if the smuggler passes, no BUILD claim until the channel is closed.

**What successful evidence would actually mean.** The instrument chain
works for BUILD at this size: a qualification receipt with measured error
rates. It says nothing about any organism that search might find.

**Claim ceiling.** "Ruler R is qualified in cell X with error rates e." No
statement about cognition. In the claim ladder this is an instrument
result, not C1.

**Status.** A miniature exists and has run (prototype/p1_slice/README.md).
Calibration set sorted on 2,400 sealed lives; 8 of 8 fire tests fire;
0 mismatches against a separately written oracle in 3,672 comparisons. One
host, one author: independence I1.

## 4. P2 -- reference arm

**Name / working descriptor.** Conventional learners through the same
worlds, rulers and probes.

**Scientific question.** What do conventional learners certify on each
relocation, at what cost, with what signatures, and where do they stop?
(Hypothesis H1b.)

**Organism physics.** Four learners behind the organism protocol.
- REF-a: a recurrent network trained offline to imitate the exact
  Bayes-optimal policy of each family. Its hidden state is fast state.
- REF-b: a small sequence model with a fixed window. The window is fast
  state.
- REF-c: a small network trained by gradient descent during the lifetime.
  Its weights are persistent structure.
- REF-d: a sequence model with an external read-and-write memory. The
  memory is persistent structure, given by design (scaffold S3).
Training happens in floating point; that is outer search. The deployed
organism is a fixed-point integer network, so the kernel replays it bit for
bit (ORG-08). The loss from quantisation is measured and reported. REF-c's
within-life learning is fixed-point gradient descent inside the organism.

**Developmental physics.** For REF-a, -b and -d the genome is the trained
weights; development is whatever happens in fast state or the external
memory. For REF-c development is weight change. Each learner is run twice
in RETAIN: with its state reset at episode boundaries (H1a, the conformance
check) and with its state carried (H1b, capacity and horizon curves).

**World physics.** RECALL, TRACK, IDENTIFY and RETAIN first; RECOMBINE with
withheld kinds of structure in Phase 3B. Every family ships its certificate
and baseline ladder.

**Evolutionary/search pressure.** Gradient descent, offline for three
learners and online for one. The demand dial of each family is raised by an
adaptive staircase until the learner falls to the bound. The BREAKER role
owns the tuning of this arm, with a budget equal to the search budget of
whatever it is compared with (ASSUMPTIONS B6).

**Measurement stack.** Certified level per family; training evaluations,
instructions and estimated joules per certified level; retention against
gap length and interference; and the standard probe set (interchange over a
sweep of swap ticks, lesion rows, scramble invariance). The probe responses
are stored as signatures. They define the reference class against which
"unfamiliar" is later measured (ANTI-05).

**Known-positive qualification.** REF-a imitates a policy whose value is
known exactly, so its certified level has a known ceiling per family. A
REF-a that misses its ceiling by more than the stated margin is a training
failure, not a finding.

**Known-negative qualification.** A REF-b with a window of one trial must
sit at the memoryless bound in every family. REF-a with its hidden state
zeroed each step must do the same.

**Cheap baseline.** The baseline ladder of each family. A tabular learner.

**Causal intervention.** Interchange of hidden state, window contents,
weights or memory cells between paired lives that differ in one known
latent.

**Transfer test.** Withheld kinds of structure in RECOMBINE (H1b, part 2).
Unseen dial settings elsewhere.

**Primary hallucination risk.** A reference arm tuned too weakly makes every
other substrate look good (the old record shows the tuned same-class
baseline arriving after the data more than once). FALSIFIERS F-D6 is the
check.

**Primary false-negative risk.** Reading a training failure as a limit of
the learner class. REF-a's known ceiling guards one learner; the others
have no such guard, and their limits are reported as "not reached with this
tuning budget".

**Expected compute.** About 60 GPU-hours of training for the first four
families (small networks, hundreds of short runs), plus 2 M1-days of CPU
evaluation. GPU use is justified: gradient training of the arm whose
defining property is gradient training.

**Expected inference use.** Implementation and one attack round on tuning.
Order of 25 million tokens.

**Expected energy profile.** About 20 kWh GPU and 10 kWh CPU.

**Reusable Prometheus components.** Ensorain's exact-Bayes stream worlds
and enumerated window floor (W-02); the Archaeon event-stream grammar
(W-01); Cosmos's cue task and locator (W-12); the planted-system gate as a
check on the probe set (C-03). No learner: the tree has no conventional
learner and no gradient regime.

**New components required.** The four learners; fixed-point deployment;
fixed-point online gradient descent; the adaptive staircase; the signature
store.

**Kill criteria.** If the arm cannot be made to reach REF-a's known ceiling
on RECALL and TRACK by day 60, its training setup is rebuilt before any
comparison is made. F-T1 and F-T5 are the program-level outcomes this arm
can trigger.

**What successful evidence would actually mean.** A measured account of
what familiar machinery does on these certificates: the first rows of the
frontier table, the cost baseline, and the reference class. If H1b holds,
a located limit: amortised learners compress only what their training
covered. If it fails, the program's target moves.

**Claim ceiling.** C2 for effects in sealed worlds after an attack round.
Signatures are measurements, not mechanisms.

## 5. P3 -- affordance knockout

**Name / working descriptor.** Switch each WM affordance off and measure
what is lost, separately for expressibility and for reach.

**Scientific question.** Which organism affordances are necessary for which
relocation under calibrated search? (Hypothesis H4; part of H5.)

**Organism physics.** WM with seven switches: store writes; conditional
instructions; call and return; tag addressing against absolute addressing;
a step cap of one against many; block creation and deletion; zero rent
against positive rent. Register initialisation is swept as a world variable
(zero, random, carried), because both the old byte soups and the P1
prototype found organisms using zeroed registers as free constants.

**Developmental physics.** As P1. The knocked-out machine is a different
physics, so each switch setting gets its own conformance run and its own
random-program census.

**World physics.** RECALL (HOLD), IDENTIFY (ADAPT) and RETAIN (BUILD) first.
CHAIN (COMPOSE) when its certificate exists.

**Evolutionary/search pressure.** From scaffold levels S4 and S3: plants
with parts removed. Three acceptance rules (margin, strict, neutral) and
one population method with lexicase selection. Budgets on a fixed ladder of
evaluations. 24 lineages per cell.

**Measurement stack.** Two separate numbers per cell, never merged:
- expressibility with the switch off: does a designed organism exist that
  still passes the ruler? Shown by construction, or by exhaustive
  enumeration of programs up to a small size;
- reach with the switch on against off: recovery rate of the planted
  target, with interval.
The matrix entry is one of: NEEDED TO EXPRESS (no organism exists without
it, by enumeration up to the stated size), NEEDED TO REACH (expressible
without it, but recovery falls from high to zero at stated power), HELPS,
NO EFFECT, HURTS, or an apparatus label.

**Known-positive qualification.** For each affordance, a designed organism
that uses it and loses its certified level when the switch is off (the
check in ORG-03).

**Known-negative qualification.** A switch the designed organism does not
use (block creation, for HOLD) must show NO EFFECT. If the matrix shows an
effect there, the knockout procedure is confounded.

**Cheap baseline.** Random programs at equal evaluations, per switch
setting. The reference arm's certified level on the same world.

**Causal intervention.** The knockout is the intervention. On found
organisms: lesion of the instructions and store cells they use.

**Transfer test.** Found organisms are certified on sealed worlds and at
dial settings not used in search.

**Primary hallucination risk.** Calling an affordance necessary because a
weak search failed without it. The old record has the pattern in its
purest form: a greedy walk that could not take a neutral step reported its
target unreachable, while 3,132 of 4,881 sampled children were neutral. The
repair is the split above, and power stated on every null.

**Primary false-negative risk.** Two affordances that substitute for each
other each look unnecessary alone. Pairwise knockouts are run for BUILD.

**Expected compute.** About 12 M1-days at the planning floor for three
relocations, two scaffold levels, eight switch settings, three rules.

**Expected inference use.** Small once P1 exists: specification, the
designed organisms for each switch setting, interpretation. Order of 15
million tokens. The campaigns themselves are configurations of existing
code.

**Expected energy profile.** CPU only; about 60 kWh.

**Reusable Prometheus components.** The Crius ladder of designed partial
organisms at graded distance (S-04); reach classes and typed states (S-01);
escrow and keyed pairing (S-08); the budget ladder (S-06); the
random-program census (C-07); lexicase (S-05). The operation mask from the
Nestor byte machine is the precedent for a switch that is a same-length
no-op (O-05).

**New components required.** The switches in the WM kernel; designed
organisms per relocation per switch setting; the enumeration of small
programs.

**Kill criteria.** If matrix entries change with the acceptance rule in
more than a third of cells, the matrix is reported as a property of reach
only, and no entry is called necessary. If no designed organism can be
written for a relocation with all switches on, that relocation is labelled
NOT_SHOWN_EXPRESSIBLE in WM and leaves P3.

**What successful evidence would actually mean.** A table of which generic
affordances each relocation needs, with expressibility separated from
reach. H4 predicts one specific pattern: tag addressing and call matter for
COMPOSE and not for BUILD. If COMPOSE appears with either switch off, built
structure can be reused at depth without designed addressing, which would
be worth more than the hypothesis.

**Claim ceiling.** C1 per cell; C2 after attack. "Necessary" is used only
with an enumeration bound attached.

## 6. P4 -- transition mapper

**Name / working descriptor.** Maps of where each relocation appears as
pressure dials vary, with a prediction registered before each map is run.

**Scientific question.** Where does each relocation appear as pressure
dials vary, and does payoff times reach predict it in every substrate?
(Hypotheses H2 and H3, the null model for emergence.)

**Organism physics.** REF and WM in Phase 3A; PN in Phase 3B.

**Developmental physics.** As the substrate defines. Rent on persistent
structure is one of the dials (DEV-09).

**World physics.** One family per map, two or three dials each.
- ADAPT in IDENTIFY: bits to identify per episode; evidence per
  observation; cost of observing.
- BUILD in RETAIN: lifetime information; episodes per life; storage rent.
- COMPRESS in RECOMBINE: store cap; rent; coverage of combinations.

**Evolutionary/search pressure.** The dials are the pressure. The search
regime is fixed and declared per map. Costs are phased in so that they do
not close the door before a foothold exists (SRCH-10).

**Measurement stack.** Before the run, per cell:
- payoff: the certified value of the capability minus the measured cost of
  the designed organism that has it;
- reach: the recovery rate, from the search-power curve, of a planted
  target of the designed organism's needle size;
- predicted transition probability: one if payoff is positive, else zero,
  times reach. No fitted parameter.
After the run, per cell: the fraction of 24 independent lineages certified.
The map shows observed beside predicted, and the residual.

**Known-positive qualification.** Cells with positive payoff in which the
plant is given one edit away. Reach is near one there, so the transition
must appear.

**Known-negative qualification.** Cells where the capability costs more
than it pays. The designed organism loses to the best restricted policy
there by construction, and search must not converge on the capability.

**Cheap baseline.** The same map for the reference arm. Random search.

**Causal intervention.** Forked clones moved across the predicted boundary:
an organism that has the capability is continued under dials where it does
not pay, and the reverse.

**Transfer test.** The boundary is predicted for dial combinations held out
of the first map, then measured. Under SCI-07 the same relation is then
predicted, in advance, for the next substrate.

**Primary hallucination risk.** Seeing a sharp transition in a coarse grid.
The boundary statistic and its resolution are preregistered, and the
staircase gives the resolution. Second: a prediction "confirmed" because
both sides share an error (payoff computed from a wrong bound). The bound
has a second implementation before a map carries a claim.

**Primary false-negative risk.** Reach near zero everywhere, so the map is
blank and says nothing about payoff. FALSIFIERS F-D5 covers it.

**Expected compute.** About 8 M1-days in Phase 3A (ADAPT, two substrates).
About 36 M1-days in Phase 3B (BUILD and COMPRESS, three substrates).

**Expected inference use.** Specification and interpretation only. Order of
15 million tokens across all maps.

**Expected energy profile.** CPU; about 40 kWh in 3A and 170 kWh in 3B.

**Reusable Prometheus components.** The threshold locator (W-12); the
attainability census (M-09); reach classes (S-01).

**New components required.** IDENTIFY with an exact Bayes-adaptive value
by dynamic programming on small instances; the cost model for designed
organisms; the adaptive staircase.

**Kill criteria.** FALSIFIERS F-H2 and F-H3, per map. If the product misses
in more than a quarter of cells, H3 is dead and the residual cells become
the subject.

**What successful evidence would actually mean.** If payoff times reach
predicts the maps in three substrates and three families, the appearance of
a relocation is designable from two quantities measurable in advance. That
is a candidate principle (C5). If it fails, the cells where it fails are
the first real leads the program has had: something other than payoff and
reach decided.

**Claim ceiling.** C1 per map; C5 only with three substrates, three
families and a prediction made in advance for a new cell.

## 7. P5 -- scaffold descent

**Name / working descriptor.** Start from a designed organism and remove
design, one level at a time, asking search to regenerate what was removed.

**Scientific question.** How far below a designed organism can each
capability be regenerated by search?

**Organism physics.** WM first. PN in Phase 3B.

**Developmental physics.** As the substrate defines. At S3 and above the
lifetime mechanism is given; at S2 it must be found.

**World physics.** RETAIN first (BUILD), then the family of each relocation
as its certificate arrives.

**Evolutionary/search pressure.** Per level, 24 lineages, a budget ladder
from 10^4 to 10^7 evaluations, three acceptance rules and one population
method; for PN, evolution strategies and gradient through the lifetime as
labelled optimiser arms. The levels:

| level | given | search must find |
|---|---|---|
| S5 | the whole designed organism | nothing |
| S4 | the organism with d parts removed | the parts |
| S3 | capability-specific primitives | how to compose them |
| S2 | generic affordances only | the mechanism |
| S1 | generic affordances with one switched off | (this is P3) |
| S0 | no task reward; survival only | everything |

Descent stops for a capability when recovery is 0 of 24 at the top budget
rung on two consecutive levels.

**Measurement stack.** Class exclusion on sealed worlds for every claimed
recovery; the search-power curve; material tracing to show the mechanism
was not in the founders; a natural-history report (how long the carrier of
the capability sat at zero utility and what kept it alive); a comparison of
the found mechanism with the plant by interchange and lesion.

**Known-positive qualification.** S4 at one missing part must be
recovered. In the prototype it was, 24 of 24 under one rule.

**Known-negative qualification.** Random programs at the same number of
evaluations (prototype: 0 of 2,000,000 reach 0.9). A sham descent in the
absence twin, where the capability does not pay, must recover nothing.

**Cheap baseline.** The random-hit rate per instruction set. The reference
arm's cost to the same certified level.

**Causal intervention.** Interchange and lesion on each found organism;
transplant of the found structure into a naive clone, against a sham.

**Transfer test.** Found organisms on unseen dial settings and on the
scrambled twin.

**Primary hallucination risk.** Counting seeded or transplanted structure
as found. The old record: all 26 of 26 "spontaneous" flags in one campaign
were transplants. Tracing decides, not labels. Second: selecting the
reported organism on the evaluation worlds.

**Primary false-negative risk.** The wrong acceptance rule. In the
prototype, recovery of one missing instruction was 24, 9 or 1 of 24
depending only on the rule, and the rule that was worst at distance one was
the only one that found a builder from an empty program. Third: a plant
whose distance does not represent the distance of mechanisms nobody
designed (ASSUMPTIONS A5).

**Expected compute.** About 6 M1-days for BUILD from S5 to S3 in WM (Phase
3A). Up to 10 M1-days per capability per substrate below that. This is the
experiment that can absorb any amount of compute, so it is rationed by the
stop rule and by the frontier table: a level is attempted only where the
level above was reached.

**Expected inference use.** Small: designed organisms and their partials,
then interpretation of what was found. Order of 15 million tokens.

**Expected energy profile.** CPU; about 30 kWh in 3A; several hundred kWh
over the year if every descent runs to its stop rule.

**Reusable Prometheus components.** The partial-organism ladder (S-04); the
tracer's questions (S-05); the heredity record format and fixtures (C-06);
store-content conditions (C-04); the transplant-as-spontaneous fixture.

**New components required.** A shadow tracer for WM; designed organisms and
partials for each relocation.

**Kill criteria.** FALSIFIERS F-T4: if at month 12 nothing at BUILD or
above has been regenerated below S4 in any substrate, in cells with search
power of at least 0.8, the weight of the program moves from search to
design.

**What successful evidence would actually mean.** An emergence map: for
each capability and substrate, the lowest level reached, with the search
power at each level. "Emergence" is claimed only at S2 or below, in
independent lineages, with tracing. The prototype's from-scratch builder is
S3, because its machine has store-read and store-write instructions.

**Claim ceiling.** C1 per level reached; C2 after attack; C3 for a found
mechanism only with a second implementation of the ruler.

## 8. P6 -- fork lab

**Name / working descriptor.** Exact clones with different histories,
measuring what built structure saves on what is learned next.

**Scientific question.** Do built structures reduce the cost of later
acquisition, and does the reduction compound?
(Hypothesis H6. This is where recursive sagacity is tested.)

**Organism physics.** Three designed learners first, then organisms found
by P5.
- Negative: a learner with a fixed procedure and fixed hypothesis space. The
  Aphrodite engine is this as built (O-13).
- Impostor: memorised solutions to the curriculum.
- Positive: a designed learner whose procedure is stored in mutable
  structure and changes with experience. It does not exist yet. It is the
  hardest design problem in the portfolio.

**Developmental physics.** Forks at stage boundaries (DEV-05), with the
developmental control set (DEV-04): continue; lesion the built structure;
transplant it into a naive clone; a wrong-history twin with equal
experience on unrelated families; a same-compute direct arm; a
shuffled-order arm; an identifiers-only sham.

**World physics.** CHAIN: tasks that are compositions of learned
transformations to depth D, with a step budget below flat search. FAMILIES:
a life of many learning episodes over task families that share
meta-structure, where later families need something no earlier one did.

**Evolutionary/search pressure.** None for the designed learners. For found
organisms, whatever P5 used.

**Measurement stack.**
- Savings ratio per stage: cost for a naive clone to reach the certified
  level on the next family, divided by the cost for the experienced
  organism.
- Carried bits: the bits of persistent structure whose removal changes that
  ratio, by lesion.
- Sagacity: savings per carried bit.
- Compounding: the savings ratio rising with stage.

**Known-positive qualification.** The designed adaptive learner shows a
rising ratio in CHAIN and FAMILIES and gains on out-of-span families.

**Known-negative qualification.** The fixed-procedure learner shows a flat
ratio, and no gain on out-of-span families by construction. The memorising
impostor shows zero on any new family.

**Cheap baseline.** The same-compute direct arm: the same budget spent on
the final family alone.

**Causal intervention.** Lesion and transplant of the built structure, with
shams.

**Transfer test.** Out-of-span families (XFER-07): families that need
something no earlier family needed. A gain there, carried by built
structure that transplants, is what would go beyond learning to learn.

**Primary hallucination risk.** Counting a speed-up inside a fixed
hypothesis space as recursion. The old record has it exactly: an improver
that never changed, and a library that reordered a fixed search, described
as self-improvement. Two of that engine's acceptance conditions were
constants. Second: an advantage that exists only relative to a budget.

**Primary false-negative risk.** World families that do not afford
compounding. If the designed adaptive learner shows no rise, the worlds are
wrong and are redesigned (FALSIFIERS F-H6).

**Expected compute.** Small: about 3 M1-days. Designed learners need no
search.

**Expected inference use.** High, and concentrated in design: the adaptive
learner, the FAMILIES generator, the out-of-span construction. Order of 30
million tokens.

**Expected energy profile.** CPU; about 15 kWh.

**Reusable Prometheus components.** The Aphrodite engine as the negative
control, with its escrow and paired selection (O-13, S-08); the Crius
store-content conditions (C-04); exact closure as the flat-search cost for
CHAIN (S-09).

**New components required.** FAMILIES; certified CHAIN bounds; the designed
adaptive learner; the savings and carried-bits rulers.

**Kill criteria.** If no designed adaptive learner exists by month 6, the
RECURSE ruler cannot be qualified and P6 reports COMPOSE only (ASSUMPTIONS
B2). The old record is a warning that this may happen: a fixed-procedure
learner starting from nothing built no reusable structure in 8 of 8 runs.

**What successful evidence would actually mean.** For the designed
learners: that the worlds can show compounding and the rulers can measure
it. For a found organism placed between the fixed and the adaptive learner:
a first measured case of built structure changing how fast the organism
builds. That, on out-of-span families, with transplant, is the operational
content of recursive sagacity.

**Claim ceiling.** COMPOSE and RECURSE are excluded against designed
reference organisms, not against exact bounds. That is weaker, and every
claim at these levels says so. C2 at most until an attack round by a
different author fails to find a cheaper class that does the same.

## 9. P7 -- doubt and reliability

**Name / working descriptor.** Worlds where early evidence misleads and
committing is costly, with one named exclusion test per behaviour.

**Scientific question.** Under misleading evidence and costly commitment,
do organisms come to hold alternatives, seek evidence, revise, and monitor
the reliability of their own state?

**Organism physics.** REF-a (which imitates the Bayes-optimal policy and so
is the designed positive), WM designed organisms, then found organisms.

**Developmental physics.** As the substrate defines.

**World physics.** DOUBT. A hidden rule; observations that are wrong with a
dialled probability, more often early; a cost to observe; a commitment that
cannot be undone and costs when wrong; an opt-out action with a small sure
payoff; a hazard rate at which the rule changes. The exact value of the
Bayes-optimal policy comes from dynamic programming on small instances.

**Evolutionary/search pressure.** The four dials. Search as in P5 for WM.

**Measurement stack.** One exclusion test per named behaviour, each against
the exact value of the restricted policy that lacks it:
- holds alternatives: beats the best policy that tracks a single hypothesis;
- seeks evidence: beats the best policy that never observes, where
  observing is worth its cost, and stops observing where it is not;
- revises: beats the best never-revise policy after a rule change;
- monitors its own state: opts out more when noise is injected into its
  identified memory carrier, with stimulus difficulty held fixed (CAUS-07).

**Known-positive qualification.** A designed organism that keeps a
posterior count and checks its own carrier passes all four.

**Known-negative qualification.** A designed organism that commits on first
evidence; one that never revises; one that opts out on stimulus difficulty
alone. Each must fail exactly its test and may pass the others.

**Cheap baseline.** The restricted policies themselves, whose exact values
ship with the world.

**Causal intervention.** Carrier noise, which needs the carrier identified
first by interchange. Rule changes on forked clones.

**Transfer test.** Unseen settings of the misleading-strength and hazard
dials.

**Primary hallucination risk.** Giving a human name to a behaviour that a
first-order cue explains. The four behaviours are test names, not
faculties, and "monitors its own state" is claimed only through the causal
test, which has no explanation by stimulus difficulty.

**Primary false-negative risk.** An opt-out payoff set so that opting out
never pays, or always does. The demand certificate must show the Bayes
policy opting out on a stated fraction of trials.

**Expected compute.** About 5 M1-days and 20 GPU-hours.

**Expected inference use.** Order of 15 million tokens: the family, its
solver and a second implementation of the solver.

**Expected energy profile.** About 30 kWh.

**Reusable Prometheus components.** The Ludus exact stopping solver as a
pattern (W-04); the misleading-hint dial and the locator (W-12); the
schedule wrapper for rule changes (W-06).

**New components required.** DOUBT with a belief-state dynamic programme in
exact rationals; the carrier-noise test. Neither exists anywhere in the
tree.

**Kill criteria.** If the exact solver cannot reach instances where the
four restricted policies separate from the optimum by four times the
ruler's minimum detectable effect, the family is not admitted and P7 waits.

**What successful evidence would actually mean.** That each of four
behaviours usually described in human terms has a class-exclusion test, and
a table of which substrates pass which test at which dial settings.

**Claim ceiling.** C2 per test. The word "metacognition" is not used; the
claim is the test name and its level.

## 10. P8 -- generator divergence

**Name / working descriptor.** The same cell searched by a blind generator
and by a model, with the found mechanisms compared.

**Scientific question.** At equal certified level, does model-guided
generation find a narrower set of mechanisms than blind variation?
(Hypothesis H7. This is the direct test of the epistemic-escape question.)

**Organism physics.** Whichever substrate has cells certified at S3 or
below. WM is expected first.

**Developmental physics.** As that substrate.

**World physics.** The cells chosen, fixed before either arm runs.

**Evolutionary/search pressure.** Three arms at equal evaluations: blind
structural variation; proposals from a model that is shown the world's
public description and its own past scores; an even mixture. The model's
identity, prompt and settings are recorded per call. Where a second model
family can be reached from code, it is a fourth arm.

**Measurement stack.** Every find is first certified like any other
organism. Then, per certified find, a mechanism signature: its responses to
the qualified probe set (which components flip under interchange, the
lesion row, the pattern of store use, behaviour at off-design dial
settings). Per arm: the dispersion of signatures; the number of signature
cells only that arm occupies, against a permutation null; the distance of
each find from the reference class.

**Known-positive qualification.** Two designed organisms with the same
certified level and known different mechanisms must be far apart in
signature. The prototype already has such a pair: the designed builder
dispatches with a jump, and the found one gates its write with a
comparison.

**Known-negative qualification.** One organism re-encoded without changing
what it computes (registers renamed, blocks reordered) must be at distance
zero from itself. If it is not, the signature measures style.

**Cheap baseline.** Random programs that pass certification, if any exist
at that cell.

**Causal intervention.** The signatures are themselves made of
interventions.

**Transfer test.** The comparison is repeated in a second cell chosen in
advance.

**Primary hallucination risk.** A signature that separates encodings, not
mechanisms. The negative control above is the guard. Second: an unfair
budget, since model proposals cost tokens and blind proposals do not; the
comparison is at equal evaluations, with tokens reported beside it.

**Primary false-negative risk.** Too few certified finds to compare. The
default is at least 20 per arm.

**Expected compute.** About 5 M1-days.

**Expected inference use.** The largest in the portfolio during a run: the
model arm's proposals, perhaps several thousand of them. Order of 20
million tokens for building and 5 to 10 million for proposals, with bulk
proposals from a cheap model (INF-06).

**Expected energy profile.** About 25 kWh locally; the model arm's energy
is off site and is reported as tokens.

**Reusable Prometheus components.** The Theseus divergence statistic and
its one data set, in which model-written genomes sat closest to the known
library (S-07); per-call logging (S-12); the model-call layer (I-08).

**New components required.** The signature space, which does not exist
anywhere and is an open design question (OPEN_QUESTIONS.md).

**Kill criteria.** FALSIFIERS F-H7: with at least 20 certified finds per
arm, equal dispersion within the preregistered margin and no blind find
outside the model-guided set. Then no restriction by the model's prior is
detectable at this scale, and the blind share can be reduced.

**What successful evidence would actually mean.** A measured size for the
effect the whole program worries about: how much a model generator narrows
what is found. It sets the blind share by evidence instead of by doctrine.

**Claim ceiling.** C2, in the cells tested. Nothing about models in
general.

## 11. P9 -- open substrate arm

**Name / working descriptor.** One substrate unlike programs and networks,
admitted through the same gate, on the same worlds.

**Scientific question.** Does a substrate unlike programs and networks
reach the same certified levels by mechanisms outside the reference class?
For the first arm, message passing: is there a memory primitive that is
neither register nor weight?

**Organism physics.** The first arm is the Ananke packet-tensor engine
(O-09): sites running one shared integer program and exchanging summed
payloads through a lossy, delayed medium. State can sit in registers, in
routing weights, or only in packets in flight. Candidates for later arms,
one at a time: a lattice built for organisms, a tensor-network organism
with bond rank as an exact capacity dial, a function chemistry.

**Developmental physics.** To be declared at admission: which arrays are
fast state, which persist, and how the harness resets each. The engine's
existing controls can reset or flush every carrier.

**World physics.** RECALL, TRACK, IDENTIFY and RETAIN, with the same
certificates as every other substrate. The engine's own worlds are not
used: its environment is open loop, and one of them is solved by a clock.

**Evolutionary/search pressure.** As P5, with the search-power curve
measured for this substrate before any null is read.

**Measurement stack.** The shared rulers. The state partition is by array
and by site. Signatures are compared with the reference class from P2.

**Known-positive qualification.** A designed organism for HOLD exists and
holds its bit only in flight; flushing packets in flight must abolish it. A
designed organism for ADAPT must be written; without one, every ADAPT null
in this arm is labelled NOT_SHOWN_EXPRESSIBLE.

**Known-negative qualification.** The same organisms with the carrier
flushed every tick. The zero-communication control.

**Cheap baseline.** The baseline ladder of each family. The engine's known
impostors: a block clock and a copy policy.

**Causal intervention.** Interchange per carrier array between paired
worlds, with the control-identity audit, because this engine's own record
shows two of its swaps were one measurement by design.

**Transfer test.** As P5.

**Primary hallucination risk.** A coarse partition read as a mechanism.
Where interchange cannot localise better than a whole array, mechanism
claims are closed for the arm (FALSIFIERS F-D7).

**Primary false-negative risk.** A substrate that can only just hold the
capability. The capacity margin (ORG-05) is checked at admission.

**Expected compute.** About 10 GPU-days. The engine was built for the GPU;
the CPU cost of the alternative is stated in the preregistration, and a CPU
kernel checked against the engine's independent oracle is part of the
adaptation.

**Expected inference use.** Order of 25 million tokens: the closed-loop
adapter, the store declaration, the new designed organisms.

**Expected energy profile.** About 85 kWh.

**Reusable Prometheus components.** The engine, its independent oracle and
its second-host conformance record (O-09); the Wave-2 intervention library
(C-01); the lens design and its failure modes (C-02).

**New components required.** The closed-loop adapter; genome-bit
accounting; designed organisms beyond HOLD.

**Kill criteria.** If the arm cannot pass a P1-style qualification within
one adaptation effort of two weeks, it is closed. If it reaches nothing
beyond HOLD at S4 with search power of at least 0.8, it is closed with a
certified null, which is a result.

**What successful evidence would actually mean.** Either a third kind of
substrate on the frontier table, which is what a substrate-general
statement needs (SCI-07), or a certified account of why this one stops
where it does.

**Claim ceiling.** C2 for effects. C4 and C5 statements that span
substrates need this arm or another like it.

## 12. Resources across the portfolio

| | Phase 3A (days 0 to 90) | Phases 3B and 3C (months 4 to 12) |
|---|---|---|
| CPU | about 29 M1-days of 90 available | about 150 M1-days, rationed by P5's stop rule |
| GPU | about 60 hours (P2) | about 12 days (P7, P9) |
| energy | about 160 kWh | about 800 kWh |
| tokens | order of 10^8, front-loaded into the kernel | order of 10^8 |
| operator | three gate reviews, a weekly digest, budget and doctrine rulings; no admissions | admissions at C3 and above, as they arise |

Three things follow.

1. **Electricity is not the binding constraint.** A year of this portfolio
   is on the order of 1,000 kWh. Tokens and operator attention bind.
2. **Inference is front-loaded and then optional.** The kernel, the worlds
   and the designed organisms are built by models. After that, P3, P4 and
   P5 campaigns are configurations of existing code. If tokens become
   scarce after the day-30 gate, the maps keep filling with no model in the
   loop, and only new world families, new substrates, P6 and P8 wait.
3. **Utilisation is not a goal.** The plan uses a third of the available
   CPU in the first 90 days on purpose (ENRG-04).

**What is cut first**, if resources shrink: P9, then P8, then PN, then the
COMPRESS map. What is never cut: P1, the reference arm, the second
implementation behind any C3 claim.

## 13. What is deliberately absent

- A soup with survival pressure only. It is the bottom of the descent
  (S0), reached last, where its nulls can be read.
- A large trained model as an organism. It would repeat the conventional
  paradigm at a size a home laboratory cannot win at.
- A language task of any kind. Language carries human priors into the
  world, the organism and the reader at once.
- A model that judges, labels or ranks results.
- A seat per experiment. Experiments are campaigns on a shared kernel.
- An experiment whose stated goal is a novel mechanism. Novelty is not an
  objective anywhere (SCI-14). Unfamiliar mechanisms are measured after
  certification, against the reference class.
