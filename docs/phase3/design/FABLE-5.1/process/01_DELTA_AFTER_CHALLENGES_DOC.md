## What I took from it

1. The developmental control set (its 4g): same-compute direct control,
   shuffled-order curriculum, frozen-development control. I had forked
   clones with a wrong-history twin. Its three named controls are better
   specified, and I adopt them as REQUIRED for any developmental claim.
2. "Convergent capability through divergent mechanisms" as the discovery
   signal (its 4d). It fits my signature-distance idea and sharpens it: the
   unit of comparison is independent developmental runs that reach the same
   certified level.
3. Organisational scale (its 2a): primitive, motif, circuit, architecture,
   ecology, each with its own ablation and transplant operators. My
   state-partition interface was flat. It now carries a granularity level.
4. The evidence-profile letters (Q, S, W, R, B, Rep, M). I keep my
   certificate names and give the mapping, so the operator's vocabulary and
   mine line up.
5. The multi-model disagreement panel (its 3d). I include it, marked
   EXPERIMENTAL and limited to setting investigation priority after
   certification. It is a model-based classifier, and the record shows that
   class failing in both directions (Tityos T15, T17).

## Where I differ or go further

1. REACH is a fourth certificate. The challenges document asks whether
   anything "can get there" through development, but it has no axis for the
   search itself. The record's largest false-negative class is search that
   could not reach targets that provably existed (Ananke XOR and FLIP plants,
   Tyche parity-3, PROTEUS-46). I require a measured search-power curve from
   planted targets before any "not found" is read.
2. Class exclusion as the organism-side ruler. The document lists "minimum
   memory of any policy reaching a target score" as a candidate depth
   measure. I make exact restricted-class bounds the primary ruler, because
   a bound needs no interpretation and no model.
3. Descent from existence proofs. The document requires a hand-built
   capacity proof per affordance. I use those proofs as the starting point
   of the main method: remove scaffolding level by level and measure where
   regeneration stops.
4. I do not re-score the historical record first. The document's first step
   is a legitimacy profile over old results. Most old cells will fail S, W
   or R, so the profile mostly confirms what the crawlers already showed, at
   real inference cost. I compile the failure taxonomy into executable
   fixtures instead, and re-test only the few historical signals that map
   onto a v3 question, inside the new apparatus.
5. A measured reference class. The document measures alienness against the
   literature, after the fact. I add the intervention signatures of
   conventional trained systems on the same worlds, so "unlike known
   mechanisms" is a measured distance and not only a retrieval result.
6. Independence by a second implementation from another model family, with
   differential testing. The document asks for external validation of
   claims. I also apply it to the kernel pieces whose bugs would manufacture
   science.
7. A specific scientific thesis: six relocations of information, each with
   a dial, an affordance and a certificate, and the claim that the gap in
   the transformer paradigm is the within-lifetime relocations. The
   challenges document frames development well but offers no model of it.
