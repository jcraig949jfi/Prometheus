## Position (compressed)

1. Diagnosis. v1 and v2 ran an observe-emergence loop in which the
   apparatus's capacity to reveal was never established first. Four
   deficits recur: worlds did not demand the phenomenon, organisms could
   not express it, search could not reach it, rulers could not detect it.
   Only instruments could succeed in that loop, and instruments are what
   survived.

2. Four certificates before any inference. A cell (world x organism x
   search x ruler) supports a claim, positive or null, only when it has:
   a DEMAND certificate (the world provably requires the capability, shown
   by exact optima of restricted policy classes), an EXPRESSIBILITY
   certificate (a designed organism in that substrate does it), a
   REACHABILITY calibration (the search finds planted targets of comparable
   size at a measured rate), and a DETECTABILITY qualification (the ruler
   sorts a positive, a negative and a cheap impostor with known error
   rates). A null without all four is labelled by the missing one.

3. Class exclusion as the main ruler. Do not ask what faculty an organism
   has. Compute the best any policy in a restricted class (memoryless,
   k-state, non-adaptive, lookup) can score in a world, exactly. An
   organism that beats that bound on held-out worlds is certified outside
   the class, whatever its mechanism. This needs no interpretation and no
   ontology, so it is safe for unfamiliar mechanisms.

4. Descent from existence proofs. v2 tried to ascend from a soup. v3 starts
   each capability from a designed organism that provably has it, then
   removes scaffolding one level at a time and asks whether search under
   pressure regenerates what was removed. The lowest level at which it
   regenerates is the measured emergence depth. Ascent attempts run in
   parallel and are interpretable because the descent calibrated them.

5. The science: six relocations of information. Development is information
   moving from the world into fast state, then into persistent structure,
   then into compressed structure, then into invocable structure, then into
   structure that builds structure. Each move has a driving scarcity that a
   world can dial, an organism affordance that it needs, and a certificate.
   Transformers do the second at scale inside a context window and did the
   later ones once, offline, in training. They do not do the later ones
   within a lifetime. That gap is the target.

6. RSE is a property, not a machine. "Recursive sagacity" is defined by a
   test any substrate may pass. Sagacity is forward transfer per carried
   bit. Recursive means the rate of producing it rises because of what the
   organism built, shown by lesion and transplant on forked clones.

7. Architecture. One shared measurement kernel (protocols, deterministic
   runner, receipts, rulers, calibration triads), a foundry of
   demand-certified worlds, a familiar gradient-trained reference arm, and
   a small set of sharply different substrates with switchable affordances.
   The engines are experiments on that kernel, each with one question.

8. Anti-gravity. Models may generate and, after certification, interpret.
   They never measure and never admit. Admission uses certificate numbers
   only. "Unfamiliar" is defined by distance from a measured reference
   class of familiar mechanisms on qualified probes, not by a model failing
   to name something. A fixed share of search mass comes from blind
   structural variation, and a direct experiment compares what
   model-guided and blind generation find at equal certified capability.

9. Independence by construction. Exact certificates, executed controls,
   and a second implementation of each load-bearing kernel piece written
   from the spec by a different model family, checked by differential
   testing. Model review is confined to building attacks.

10. Operations. Campaigns, not seats, are the unit of work. State is
    derived from receipts by code. Models are called at forks: design,
    implementation, attack, interpretation. No model-written heartbeats or
    status.

11. First build. One end-to-end calibration slice for within-lifetime
    construction: a certified world that demands it, a designed organism
    that does it, one that only holds fast state, a cheater, and the rulers
    that sort the three with known error rates.
