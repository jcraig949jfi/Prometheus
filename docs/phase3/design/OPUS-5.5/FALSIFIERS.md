## 6. What would NOT falsify the design

- A null at a phenomenon the apparatus was not shown able to detect (it is typed as an apparatus null).
- An L1 anomaly that fails to reach L2 (the ladder is working).
- A mechanism that cannot be classified by any model (priority rises if it passed the gates; it is never evidence).
- A slow first quarter that produces qualified instruments and certified worlds but no organism claims (that is the
  plan).
