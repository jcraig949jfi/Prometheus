## 1. What the system is for, in one paragraph

Phase 3 needs a scientific system that can tell, for an artificial organism developing in a world under a pressure,
(a) what the organism can currently do, (b) what it can become through experience, (c) what internal carrier holds
each capability, (d) whether that carrier is reused, transferred and retained, (e) whether prior development makes
new kinds of problems cheaper to learn, and (f) whether development improves the machinery of development itself --
and that can say, for every NO, whether the organism, the world, the pressure, the lifetime, the search, the ruler or
the statistics was incapable of producing a YES. The historical record shows that (f)-style questions were asked of
apparatus that could not answer (a).

## 2. The measured object

The unit of study is the triple

    (developmental program g, environment-family distribution / curriculum C, budget B)

mapped to a DISTRIBUTION of developmental trajectories {(state_t, competence_t)}. Capacity is a property of the
distribution over independent developmental runs (DEV-09). Search acts on g; development acts on state within a
lifetime; the world teaches only through the statistics of C. Each can be switched off independently (DEV-01,
ORG-20).

## 3. Operational definitions

No term below is used in this package about a result unless the matching predicate is TRUE in the verdict job
(SCI-17). Every definition is itself an executable predicate that must pass a qualification battery of planted
organisms, including the cheapest organisms that satisfy each clause without the property (MEA-16).

**Acquisition cost** A(o, T, eps) is a vector: feedback information consumed (bits), environment transitions,
internal steps (including idle-period steps) and metabolic cost, accumulated until frozen-evaluation competence on
sealed instances of T first exceeds eps. It is reported as full curves at >= 3 eps levels with censoring-aware
survival estimators; non-attainment is right-censored, never dropped or set to the budget. Every savings claim has a
yoked-replay arm (the organism fed the experience stream generated by the comparison organism) to separate
exploration efficiency from learning efficiency (MEA-14).

**Realised competence** R(o_t, T): performance on sealed fresh instances, each evaluation episode starting from the
snapshot o_t with every state change discarded at episode end. Within-episode adaptation counts and its curve is
reported. **Development** is any change that survives the retention boundary (DEV-04). This definition does not
depend on any mechanism, so it works where memory and development share one physical process.

**Substrate expressibility** E(S, phenomenon, W): established by a constructive organism obtained by a recorded
procedure (hand construction, compilation, verifier-guided synthesis, privileged optimisation with full world access,
exhaustive search on a small instance) that exhibits the phenomenon and gains from it (ORG-14). Absence is established
only by proof, never by search. (This is the charter's "latent cognitive capacity": what the substrate could
eventually support. The term is not applied to organisms.)

**Developmental capacity** DC(g): a reaction norm -- the distribution of competence on a target family over N runs
under a curriculum panel (standard, shuffled, direct-to-final, impoverished) at experience budgets spanning >= 1.5
decades and ending well beyond the saturation budget of a reference precompiled genome. "Capacity exceeds
realisation" requires crossing evidence: worse at small budgets, better at large (DEV-04). The developable tier of a
target is shown by a genome g* in the search's own genome language that develops the capability from the birth state
within the lifetime (DEV-13).

**Transferable sagacity** TS(o_t; T_new): the censoring-aware acquisition-curve contrast between o_t and an age- and
compute-matched irrelevant-experience twin, decomposed against naive-at-birth (maturation), a temporally shuffled
history (order), a capacity-matched random-content twin (capacity vs content) and yoked replay (exploration vs
learning) (TRF-01, TRF-06), normalised by the available transfer A(naive) - A(B_C) where B_C is the Bayes learner given
the true family prior. T_new is admitted only by a two-sided certificate: surface learners show no savings and a
structure-oracle learner shows savings (TRF-02). In information terms, log2 of the feedback-bit ratio is the number
of bits of task-specific information the organism no longer needs from the world. Storage that does not reduce future
acquisition cost is not sagacity.

**Recursive sagacity** (write-provenance definition, DEV-14 as amended by A14). Every lifetime write is tagged with
its EXECUTOR set: the minimal set of instructions or nodes whose execution performed the write (ablating a member
changes or prevents the write while operands are held fixed). Structures consulted only as operands, templates or
proposal priors do not propagate order. Birth structure has order 0; a developed structure has order k+1 when the
highest-order member of its executor set has order k. Learning is order-1 writing; learning-to-learn is
developed structure executing later writes (a member of their executor set; order >= 2); recursion is order >= 3 --
products of development
modifying the machinery that builds learning components. A recursive-sagacity claim requires: (R1) acquisition cost on
sealed held-out families, as excess over the family-prior Bayes floor, falls beyond the matched irrelevant-experience
and shuffled-history twins; (R2) reverting all order >= 3 structure to birth state, keeping order <= 2 structure,
removes a declared fraction of R1 relative to a size-matched sham reversion; (R3) transplanting the order >= 2 writer
set from same-genome donors at increasing history depth into age-matched hosts gives a monotone fall in acquisition
cost AND a monotone rise in the host's subsequent learning-to-learn slope; (R4) the same transplant from age-matched
irrelevant-experience donors has no effect (excludes maturation clocks); (R5) no outer optimiser acts during the
lifetime; (R6) executor-versus-content transplant: a host given the donor's order >= 2 executors with birth content
carries at least the declared fraction of R1 beyond a host given birth executors with the donor's content. The DEV-14
battery must classify a fixed builder with a deep compositional library and a gradient meta-learned recurrent learner
with persistent activations as order <= 2, and a saturation null must show < 5% of developed structure tagged order >= 3
in random and P1/P2-evolved genomes; otherwise the substrate is RECURSION_UNTESTABLE. In a substrate where invoked
library code itself executes developmental instructions (the DGM), INSEPARABLE MACHINERY-CONTENT is the a-priori
expected outcome unless R6 separates them. External test cases for this definition are self-referential learners
without a separate meta-optimiser (Schmidhuber 1993 self-referential weight matrix; Irie, Schlag, Csordas and
Schmidhuber 2022; Kirsch and Schmidhuber 2022). Expected classifications, to be checked on reimplementations: in the
self-referential weight matrix (1993) and its modern form (2022), every self-modification is executed by the current
matrix, which already holds lifetime-written weights, so executor order grows with every step; the saturation null (the
same architecture from untrained weights) should grow alike, so at weight granularity the expected outcome is
RECURSION_UNTESTABLE, while at the granularity of the fixed update rule that executes every write the writes are
order 1 (learning-to-learn without recursion). DEV-14 must fix the executor granularity before either is run. R5 holds
for the 2022 model at test time (no gradient within an episode). Kirsch and Schmidhuber (2022) remove the gradient
meta-optimiser but keep a fitness-monotonic selection over self-modifying copies: if that selection acts within a
lifetime it is an outer optimiser under R5 and the system is not a strict-sense positive; if the self-modifications
carry the acceleration under R2 and R6 with selection removed, it is the closest external candidate positive.
The acceleration of the learning-to-learn slope is reported but never sufficient: a fixed learning rule with
a compositional library produces it for free. Where writes cannot be order-tagged the result is typed
RECURSION_UNTESTABLE, not negative; where transplant cannot separate machinery from content but a machinery-frozen twin
fails to accelerate, the result is typed INSEPARABLE MACHINERY-CONTENT.

**Compression regimes (MEA-19).** The charter's comparison -- 1,000 stored solutions, 100 recurring patterns, 20
strategies, 5 transferable principles -- becomes four planted organisms the TS ruler must rank before "compression"
is used about any result: stored solutions save only on canonicalised seen instances (TS = 0 on novel families);
patterns save environment transitions spent identifying latent state, not policy feedback; strategies save policy
acquisition within developed families, not on two-sided-certified novel families; principles save on certified novel
families. Candidate measurements map as follows: sample efficiency over developmental age -> TS on a declared age grid;
reduction in future learning cost -> TS (TRF-01); cross-domain reuse -> L4r specificity; minimal description length ->
abstraction criterion 3 in two languages; structural reuse -> shared ablation (MEA-07); retained competence -> retention
(DEV-03); intervention sensitivity -> dose (CAU-03); transfer after representation scrambling -> TRF-03; new-task
adaptation rate -> the within-episode curve of realised competence.

**Abstraction.** A carrier X is an abstraction for family T when: (1) specificity -- perturbing X degrades >= k tasks
of T with distinct minimal policies by >= delta, degrades difficulty- and interface-matched tasks outside T by < delta/3,
and a size- and statistics-matched sham perturbation degrades neither; (2) surface invariance -- under a declared
bijection class on joint observations, re-acquisition is cheaper than naive and interchange shows X still carries the
latent variable; (3) compression -- description length in >= 2 description languages frozen before data (one not the
substrate's native encoding, none equal to the cost function) beats the median per-task developed solution and a
cost-matched control developed on tasks with no shared latent structure; (4) transplant or interchange into a naive
host lowers acquisition cost on held-out members of T.

**Constructing cognition.** Experience-specificity (experience explains more structural variance than developmental
noise), capability carried by developed structure (ablation with sham), and a construction advantage: the value-only
learner class and budget named in the experiment's freeze (DEV-05, A15), on a pre-grown generic structure of the
developed organism's final size, fails at matched
experience and compute, while that same control reaches a planted parametrically reachable target. Each substrate
declares at admission >= 2 form/value partitions; the advantage must hold under each (DEV-05).

**Deliberation signature (an outcome descriptor, not a definition of reasoning).** Competence keeps rising with
internal steps beyond the organism's measured settling time, exceeds a latency-matched reactive baseline and the same
organism with recurrent edges cut, and is concentrated on decisions with high certified d_think or d_hyp. AMORTISED
(certificate demands met, flat curve) is a typed outcome (MEA-07).

**Metacognition.** (i) Internal states carry information about the organism's OWN error beyond a qualified decoder of
the observation history alone (conditional decodability on fresh instances, including at fixed observation history
across internal-noise or internal-budget perturbations; MEA-15); (ii) epistemic actions (verify, abstain, revise)
respond to manipulations of the organism's own processing reliability that leave the observations and the
ideal-observer posterior unchanged (WLD-16); (iii) ablating those states removes (ii) while sparing first-order
competence; (iv) transfer to new epistemic families. A first-order Bayes-optimal policy without own-reliability tracking is a
mandatory baseline and must fail (ii).

**Critical-thought functions (E5).** Each function has a behavioural and causal signature, never a labelled output:
uncertainty -- conditional decodability of posterior entropy beyond the observation-history decoder (MEA-15);
competing hypotheses -- on decisions with d_hyp >= 2, interchange identifies >= 2 carriers each tracking one
hypothesis's likelihood; evidence seeking -- a costly observation is taken when certified value of information exceeds
its cost, and ablation removes this; contradiction detection -- a carrier changes state on surprise beyond a
latency-matched reactive baseline; revision -- carrier update after misleading first evidence; calibration -- the
metacognition clauses above. "Compressed into reusable structure" means the same carrier meets L4r specificity across
>= 3 epistemic families and its minimised description length falls with age; "learns when to invoke" means the
carrier's activation carries increasing information about certified per-decision d_hyp or d_voi over age, with the
gating structure absent at birth (DEV-05).

**Emergence.** X emerges in a run when X's qualified predicate is FALSE for the founding population at birth and
becomes TRUE in a lineage whose material provenance shadow attributes the localised carrier to no fixture, plant or
model-authored material (PRV-10, AGR-14). p_emerge(X) is the fraction of independent runs at the declared unit in
which this happens.

**Reasoning.** Competence in families whose certificate shows that the minimal sufficient policy must carry hidden
state beyond the reactive horizon (d_mem, d_horizon) and compose reused sub-solutions in combinations unseen during
development (d_comp under a declared policy language), with every admission baseline failing (WLD-02). A "reasoning
primitive" is a carrier meeting L4r and a "developmental primitive" one meeting L4d (s7). Benefiting from internal
computation is not part of the definition; it is measured as d_think (a logical-depth-style quantity in the sense of
Bennett 1988) and the deliberation signature.

## 4. Cognitive depth (WLD-01)

World size is not cognitive depth. Every certificate component is reported as (value, bound type, method, policy
language, compute), because reference policies give only upper bounds while admission needs lower bounds:

    gap_react   optimal minus best memoryless policy (exact over STOCHASTIC memoryless policies where |O| permits)
    gap_lookup  optimal minus best seen-episode lookup on held-out instances after canonicalisation by symmetries
    d_horizon   how far back observations must be retained by an optimal policy
    d_mem       bracket: lower bound by fooling-set / automaton minimisation (deterministic hidden-state families)
                or causal-state reconstruction (designed processes); upper bound by an explicit controller; at >= 3
                performance levels
    d_comp      compositional depth relative to a declared policy language
    d_hyp       live hypotheses / belief entropy at decision points
    d_voi       value of costly information
    d_ns        law-change rate relative to lifetime; adaptive-vs-fixed gap
    d_think     log ratio of smallest reactive circuit (or lookup) to smallest iterative controller (WLD-19)
    Delta, rho  adaptive gap V_adapt(L) - V_fix and payback ratio L / tau_id (WLD-18)
    d_unfam     optimum or witness minus best familiar reference policy (WLD-15)
    multiplicity number of functionally distinct near-optimal policies (WLD-20)

CORE requires the first four; the rest are required for families hosting claims about them. A huge reactive world has
gap_react ~ 0 and is shallow; a small world with long d_horizon and large d_hyp is deep. The first empirical test of
the concept is experiment X2: whether certificates predict the held-out performance of learners not used in any
certificate computation (acquisition-matched FSC/PSR learners, the reference learner, DGM populations at fixed budget)
better than the best proxy by a preregistered margin; constant, memoryless and lookup baselines are consistency checks
only, since gap_react and gap_lookup are defined against them. Certificate tools
themselves are cross-checked by two independently authored implementations on sealed known-answer worlds, including a
Nim-like family that has a closed-form optimal policy (WLD-17).

Prior art: d_think is a logical-depth-style quantity (Bennett 1988), and d_mem and the depth proxies are
computational-mechanics measures (causal states; excess entropy and statistical complexity, Crutchfield and Feldman
2003). X2's competitor is the best of these proxies, not raw state count. Each admitted family also states its minimal
sufficient policy and its analytic size (A19; RSE_ARCHITECTURE.md s4 table).

## 5. When does development pay? (PRS-01)

    P0  static environment; development is waste at evolutionary equilibrium on a smooth landscape
    P1  per-lifetime variation with adaptive gap Delta >= delta and payback ratio L / tau_id >= rho_min.
        Prediction: an innate-adaptive program (fixed program with state) when the smallest adaptive controller fits
        the genome budget and identification fits fast state; slow-state plasticity otherwise
    R   ruggedness axis (separate): a needle-shaped innate solution under the declared operators predicts transient
        Baldwinian learning even at Delta = 0, followed by assimilation
    P2  shared latent structure across a sequence of environments in a long lifetime: structure extraction (savings)
    P3  within-lifetime shifts of the useful learning bias, compressible: fixed meta-rules (order-2) suffice
    P3b open-ended bias growth: each family's useful bias composes earlier biases at growing depth, so any fixed
        meta-rule of declared size falls behind after h* families: the only regime predicting recursion (PRS-14)
    G   genome-budget axis: genome capacity swept relative to the description length of the minimal solution;
        a developmental program is predicted when the solution exceeds the budget (A16)
    C   catastrophic shift: rare, large regime shifts, distinct from the change rate (A17)
    P4  ecological interaction, competition and cooperation (deferred to E9: they confound single-organism
        developmental attribution until the instruments are qualified)

A level is in force only when its pressure certificate passes: the capable fixture beats the best incapable baseline
under the exact selection regime with N_e*s >= 10 at every cost-ramp point (PRS-12). The original draft compared the
wrong quantities (entropy of optimal policies against genome size) and cited Hinton-Nowlan as a P1 positive although it
is a static-needle (R-axis) effect; both are corrected. External known positives exist for P1 and R: learning evolves
at moderate rates of environmental change and gives way to genetic control when change is rare or lifetimes short
(external prior art, evidence/ext.md). These are the first qualification targets for the whole stack (X1a, X1b).

**Nulls.** A null is typed at the lowest tier not demonstrated (SCI-03). A phenomenon-level null is a NULL
CERTIFICATE (SCI-08): NOT_OBSERVED(X | substrate, genome language, search, curriculum, lifetime, budget, pressure),
with every element passing a frozen threshold, stated as NOT_DETECTED_ABOVE(MDE) against a constructive SESOI
(SCI-14), and bracketed by a qualified positive of the same type at an easier point of a declared difficulty axis
(SCI-13 as amended: an adjacent-point positive or a rediscovery-from-distance positive; otherwise UNBRACKETED). The
checker is qualified on constructed apparatus nulls, which it must refuse, and on a predicted genuine null, which it
must admit, so the phenomenon branch is reachable. For phenomena with no developable genome, Phase 3 can issue only
UNBRACKETED or developability-not-shown nulls; that is the expected recursive-sagacity outcome in year one.

## 8. How to read the tables

Priorities: REQUIRED (Phase 3 results are not interpretable without it), HIGH VALUE (large expected gain, not a
precondition), EXPERIMENTAL (an arm once preconditions hold), REJECTED/AVOID (judged harmful or premature).
Gates say WHEN a requirement must exist: SLICE (before the vertical slice's known-positive verdict), CORE (before the
first L1 verdict of a discovery experiment), RULE (static check), GATE-NULL (before a null is typed above apparatus
level), GATE-L2, GATE-L3, GATE-L4r, GATE-L4d, GATE-L5, GATE-NOVELTY (before an unfamiliarity claim or externalisation), GATE-EXT, GATE-C:<campaign
type>. Enforcement says HOW: BLOCK (a named component refuses), RULE
(static check in CI), FLAG, AUDIT, PROSE. Each requirement names its historical motive, the alternative considered, the
discriminating test and, where it blocks, a counterfeit artifact the enforcement must reject (each becomes a CI
fixture). After the final review, every SLICE requirement and at least one covering requirement of every failure class
carries a counterfeit (checker-enforced); 101 of 138 BLOCK requirements carry one, the rest a discriminating test.
For each requirement, "success means" is that its discriminating test passes and its counterfeit is refused; the
interpretation it supports is falsified by the FALSIFIERS.md row of the same id or, where none exists, by acceptance of
its counterfeit. Build cost is a band of tokens processed if built in isolation; bands overlap heavily and are for
comparison between gates only.

SLICE-MIN. After amendments A01-A07 the SLICE gate IS the minimal list X1a needs (the SLICE rows of the tables): the
runner, keyed streams and receipts; ledger, signed verdict job and row-class filter; the DGM kernel with its slow
reference interpreter and only the lifetime-modification and internal-tick switches; F1 with exact Delta and rho; the
acquisition-curve dossier on authored plants plus random genomes; the S1 baseline subset; the token and dollar ledgers.
Its standalone band sum (gate table) is not a budget. The S1 work items are a generated table (s11): every SLICE
requirement is built by exactly one of nine items, with token budgets totalling 64M tokens processed against a cap of
80M (40% of the upper 90-day envelope); the checker fails if a SLICE requirement is unassigned or the budgets exceed
the cap, and INF-06's 1.5x stop rule applies per item. Slice scopes (A04-A07, A26, A27) state what each SLICE
requirement demands in the slice and what waits for CORE; in particular F1 is admitted on the S1 baseline subset, and
the slice's I1 plant independence is read from the logged authorship record.
