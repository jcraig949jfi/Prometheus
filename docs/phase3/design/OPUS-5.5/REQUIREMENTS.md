## 1. What the system is for, in one paragraph

Phase 3 needs a scientific system that can tell, for an artificial organism developing in a world under a
pressure, (a) what the organism can currently do, (b) what it can become through experience, (c) what internal
structure carries each capability, (d) whether that structure is reused, transferred and retained, (e) whether
prior development makes new kinds of problems cheaper to learn, and (f) whether development improves the
machinery of development itself -- and that can say, for every NO, whether the organism, the world, the pressure,
the search, the ruler or the statistics was incapable of producing a YES. Everything below serves those two
sentences.

## 2. The measured object

The unit of study is not a circuit and not an organism. It is the triple

    (developmental program g, environment-family distribution / curriculum C, budget B)

mapped to a DISTRIBUTION of developmental trajectories {(structure_t, competence_t)}. Development is stochastic;
capacity is a property of the distribution over independent developmental runs, not of one run (DEV-09).
Evolution (or any other search) acts on g; development acts on structure_t within a lifetime; the world teaches
only through the statistics of C. Each of the three can be switched off independently (DEV-01).

## 3. Operational definitions

No word below is used in this package without the definition and discriminator given here (charter s24).

**Acquisition cost** A(o, T, eps): the task-specific feedback (reward or label events; interaction steps where
feedback is dense) an organism o needs to reach competence eps on task family T, measured from a declared
starting state. Feedback events are preferred to raw steps because steps confound deliberation with experience.

**Realised competence** R(o_t, T): performance on T at developmental age t with structural development frozen.
In-episode adaptation is allowed only if declared, and is then reported separately.

**Latent cognitive capacity** of a substrate: established only by constructive proof -- a hand-built organism in
the substrate that exhibits the capability and gains from it in the target world family (ORG-14). Search
failure never establishes absence of latent capacity.

**Developmental capacity** DC(g, C, B): the distribution of competence on a target family reached from the
genome's standard initial state under standard curriculum C with budget B; summarised by rung reached, trajectory
slope, savings on unseen rungs, and re-development after damage (DEV-04, DEV-10).

**Transferable sagacity** TS(o_t; T_new) = [A(naive, T_new) - A(o_t, T_new)] / A(naive, T_new), measured against
two controls: an equal-compute twin whose experience was scrambled or irrelevant, and a representation-scrambled
version of T_new. T_new must be structurally novel: generated by a different generator sharing latent structure
without surface overlap, verified by a cheap learner showing no transfer (TRF-01..03). In information terms, TS is
the reduction in the conditional description length of future competence given the organism's developed
structure: K(pi_new | organism) < K(pi_new). This is the sense in which the deepest representation "reduces the
amount of new information required to acquire competence on future problems". Compression that does not reduce
future acquisition cost is storage, not sagacity.

**Recursive sagacity.** Let L1(h) be the acquisition cost on a new family after developmental history h, and
L2(h) the rate at which L1 falls per family experienced (the learning-to-learn slope). Recursive sagacity is the
claim that dL2/dh > 0 -- the learning-to-learn slope itself steepens with experience -- AND that this acceleration
is carried by changes to the organism's modification machinery rather than by accumulated content:
(a) transplanting the changed machinery WITHOUT task content into a naive host reproduces faster acquisition on
NEW families; (b) transplanting content WITHOUT the machinery does not reproduce the acceleration; (c) no outer
optimiser acts during the lifetime. "Machinery" and "content" are defined by transplant outcome, not by labels,
which is why the substrate must support transplant of arbitrary substructures (ORG-08). Ordinary
learning-to-learn shows a negative L1 slope (cheaper learning with experience); meta-learning in the usual ML sense
shows an outer loop improving a rule across lifetimes; recursive sagacity requires within-lifetime acceleration of
the slope, carried by transplantable machinery.

**Abstraction.** An internal structure X is an abstraction for family T when: (1) ablating X breaks at least k
structurally distinct tasks of T (shared ablation); (2) after a bijective re-encoding of observations, X is reused
while the input mapping re-develops quickly (surface invariance); (3) the description length of X plus the
task-specific residues is smaller than the sum of task solutions it replaces (compression); and (4) transplanting
X into a naive host lowers acquisition cost on held-out members of T.

**Constructing cognition (versus optimising parameters).** Lifetime structural change that is experience-dependent
(different experience yields different structure), capability-carrying (ablation removes the capability), and
topology-necessary: parameter-only re-optimisation of the birth structure at matched budget cannot reach the
capability (DEV-05).

**Internal computation decoupled from action.** Measured behaviourally by the compute-competence curve:
competence rises with the internal steps allowed at a decision point while observations are held fixed. Reactive
policies are flat.

**Critical thought / metacognition.** Internal states that (i) predict the organism's own error on the current
task (decodable calibration), (ii) are used to choose information gathering, abstention or revision, (iii) when
ablated remove that adaptive behaviour, and (iv) transfer to new epistemic world families. The physics contains no
labelled confidence output; the property must be found by decoding plus intervention.

**Reasoning.** Used only for competence in world families whose depth certificate (s4) shows that the minimal
sufficient policy must carry hidden state beyond the reactive horizon, compose reused sub-solutions in combinations
not seen during development, and benefit from internal computation decoupled from action -- with the corresponding
baselines failing. "Reasoning primitive" is reserved for a structure meeting L4 (s7).

## 4. Cognitive depth (WLD-01)

World size is not cognitive depth. The certificate is a vector, computed exactly for small exactly solvable
families and bounded by reference policies otherwise:

    d_mem      minimal finite-state controller size (or bits) of any policy reaching x% of optimal
    d_horizon  information horizon: how far back observations must be retained by an optimal policy
    gap_react  optimal minus best memoryless policy
    gap_lookup optimal minus best seen-episode lookup, on held-out instances
    d_comp     compositional depth: reused sub-policies chained in the minimal description of the family policy
    d_hyp      live hypotheses (belief-state entropy over latent structure) at decision points
    d_voi      value of costly information: gap between policies with and without paid observation
    d_ns       law-change rate relative to lifetime, and the adaptive-vs-fixed policy gap
    robustness each of the above across generator variants

A huge world solvable reactively has gap_react ~ 0 and is shallow. A small world with long d_horizon and large d_hyp
is deep. Admission (WLD-02) requires the constant, best-memoryless, seen-episode-lookup and sub-d_mem-controller
baselines to fail by stated margins and a reference reasoning-capable policy to succeed. The first empirical test
of this whole concept is whether the certificate predicts which baselines fail on fresh instances (experiment X2 in
RSE_ARCHITECTURE.md).

## 5. When does development pay? (PRS-01)

The pressure question -- when is it cheaper to evolve a process that constructs solutions than a genome containing
them -- has an information-theoretic core that can be tested rather than assumed:

    P0  one static environment                              -> genome encodes the solution; development is waste
    P1  environment drawn per lifetime, I(e; pi*_e) > G      -> the genome cannot hold the answer; a learner wins
        (G = genome capacity available for policy)
    P2  environments share latent structure; long lifetimes  -> a learner that extracts the shared structure
        with sequences of environments                         (abstraction, savings) beats a flat learner
    P3  within a lifetime the bias that makes learning       -> plasticity of plasticity wins, if the shifts are
        work shifts, and the shifts are compressible           themselves compressible
    P4  other adapting organisms are part of the environment -> ecological and social pressures (deferred)

Two further variables modulate all levels: lifetime length relative to learning time (learning that cannot pay
back within a lifetime is selected against), and metabolic cost of structure and computation (which makes
compression and pruning adaptive, but which killed footholds when applied at full strength from generation zero;
ORG-07, PRS-06). P1 has known positives in the literature (Baldwin-effect and evolved-plasticity results); they are
the first qualification targets for the whole stack (experiment X1).

## 6. Organism physics: affordances, not modules

The central design tension -- enough physics for rich cognitive organisation without hard-coding the architecture
we hope to discover -- is resolved by sorting candidate properties into four classes.

    class                    properties                                            rule
    MINIMAL AFFORDANCE       persistent writable state (capacity >= d_mem);         REQUIRED, each with a
    (computational)          an addressing mode (address- or content-based);        capacity proof (ORG-14)
                             conditional control / dynamic routing;
                             structure create / delete / copy / modify in lifetime;
                             internal steps decoupled from action (metered);
                             modifiable modification machinery (>= 1 substrate);
                             metabolic accounting
    INSTRUMENT AFFORDANCE    determinism and seeds; snapshot / restore;            REQUIRED; not cognitive
    (for measurement)        addressable substructures for ablation, transplant,
                             interchange; trace on demand; resource accounting
    PHYSICS OPTION           multiple timescales (evolvable decay/consolidation);   HIGH VALUE; swept as
                             invocable stored structure; neutral g->p redundancy;   variables
                             idle periods in worlds
    DO NOT BUILD IN          working-memory buffers; attention modules;             REJECTED/AVOID
    (familiar ontology)      hypothesis slots; confidence outputs; planners;
                             world-model modules; labelled abstraction operators;
                             gradient descent through a fixed architecture as the
                             primary research substrate
    ALLOWED TO EMERGE        modularity; consolidation policy; hierarchy; binding   measured, never named in
                             scheme; internal simulation; uncertainty               the physics
                             representation; metacognitive control; communication
                             protocol; pruning policy

The difference between providing an affordance and installing a module is the difference between providing the
possibility of a scratchpad emerging and providing a scratchpad API. The first is a property of the physics
(writable addressable state); the second is a named function with a fixed interface. Pressure then decides what
the affordances become; worlds are built so that organisms without something scratchpad-like are measurably
disadvantaged (WLD-02).

Plasticity of plasticity is the one affordance that is close to the target phenomenon. It is REQUIRED in at least
one substrate because recursive sagacity is otherwise unobservable by construction (the record contains an
"improver" that could never change); it is not required in every substrate, so that its effect can be measured by
comparison.

## 7. The claim ladder (SCI-02)

    level  name                         minimum evidence (cumulative)
    L0     observation                  one run, any instrument; internal only
    L1     anomaly                      qualified ruler (MEA-01); >= 3 independent seeds at the declared unit; beats
                                        the cheapest-cheat rungs of the baseline ladder; chance floor reported
    L2     effect                       executable preregistration (SCI-04); power analysis; fresh world instances
                                        from sealed splits; full baseline ladder incl. same-class tuned estimator and
                                        definition-as-rule; reachability shown (SCI-05)
    L3     mechanism                    ablation without forced silence (CAU-01), transplant with sham arms
                                        (CAU-02), dose-response (CAU-03), blind localisation (CAU-06); independent
                                        reimplementation of the ruler and kernel (REP-02); cross-host replication
    L4     developmental or reasoning   structure absent at birth and constructed at an identifiable transition
           primitive                    (DEV-03, DEV-08); topology necessity (DEV-05); reused across >= 3
                                        structurally distinct families (shared ablation); savings; retention; for a
                                        reasoning primitive, the families' certificates require it
    L5     transferable mechanism       L4 plus transfer to independently authored held-out families (TRF-02, TRF-04)
                                        and functional convergence or transplant in a second substrate (TRF-05)
    L6     architectural principle      L5 across >= 2 substrates and >= 3 pressure regimes, with a preregistered
                                        quantitative prediction confirmed on a new substrate or world family, and
                                        replication by a party outside the Prometheus model family (SCI-10)

Promotion above L1 is written only by the Reality-layer verdict service (PRV-02). Interpretation (models, people,
prior-art search) can propose experiments at any level and can never demote or delete (AGR-01).

An interpretable null is a first-class result. A NO EMERGENCE statement is phenomenon-level only when S, W and R
are demonstrated and a needle-size estimate shows the search could have reached a planted instance (SCI-08).
Otherwise it is recorded, typed, as a null about the apparatus.

### SCI -- Scientific requirements

**SCI-01 [REQUIRED | SLICE | BLOCK] Per-claim evidence profile computed by the verdict job.** Every result carries a profile scored separately on Q, S (substrate expressibility), W (world demand), R (ruler validity), B (baseline discrimination), Rep (independent replication), M (mechanism by intervention), D (developmental origin) and T (transfer). The verdict job computes each axis from evidence rows; seats never score axes, and no axis is inferred from another.

- Why: A single PASS/FAIL word hides which part of the apparatus produced the outcome; self-scored axes would be labels.
- Historical motive: T04 T12 T13 ID3; Tantalus 2.5-2.6; Sisyphus E1-E8
- Alternative considered: Verdict word plus free-text caveat.
- Discriminating test / verification: Re-score 20 historical headline results; count category changes.
- Counterfeit the enforcement must reject: A row whose S axis is set to Y by the submitting seat without a capacity-proof row: verdict job ignores it.
- Build inference: M; inference when operated: NONE

**SCI-02 [REQUIRED | SLICE | BLOCK] Mechanical claim ladder; seats write only L0.** Claims occupy L0..L6 (s7). Every promotion, including L0 to L1, is written only by the verdict job, which holds the only signing key (PRV-02); seats and model processes can write only L0 observation rows. Promotion criteria are executable predicates over committed, computed rows.

- Why: Promotion by narrative is how attractive hallucinations became headlines.
- Historical motive: T02 T11 T22; Theseus 2,351 promotions; sigma_kernel PROMOTE without re-execution
- Alternative considered: Prose rubric.
- Discriminating test / verification: Attempt to promote a planted false claim by every route a seat could use; all fail.
- Counterfeit the enforcement must reject: An L1 row inserted directly into the store by a seat process: unsigned, ignored by every reader.
- Build inference: M; inference when operated: NONE
