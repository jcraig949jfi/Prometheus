## C. Questions without a scheduled experiment (yet)

    OQ11  Is the developmental thesis right for reasoning at all? Reasoning-like competence might be best reached as
          amortised structure (flat deliberation curves) built by search, with development adding little. E4 bears on
          it; no experiment isolates it cleanly.
    OQ12  Where should curricula come from without authoring the mechanism? World-organism coevolution (PRS-10) could
          generate them, but is uninterpretable until certificates exist; authored curricula risk smuggling ontology
          (AGR-17 author-dependence meter records it).
    OQ13  Is ecological interaction (P4) necessary for open-ended development, or only for some capacities? E9 is
          deferred; the yoked-replay control is ready.
    OQ14  Which historical anomalies should be re-tested as fixtures under the new instruments? Candidates: the
          delay-invariant reader (latch or memory?), Crius RELAY (a 47-edit reuse mechanism never found by search),
          Ananke FLIP/XOR in-space plants, the C4 "cliff" under a Bernoulli(f) ruler, NPE internalisation (8/144).
          They are cheap known-answer or known-hard probes for X4 and X6.
    OQ15  What should the second substrate be? A continuous or asynchronous substrate is preferred for dissimilarity,
          but its capacity proofs would come from privileged optimisation, which has its own false-capacity rate.
    OQ16  What is the realistic build cost? The 60-200M token estimate is unmeasured; INF-06 replaces it within two
          weeks. If it is several times higher, the slice and E1/E2 are the whole of year one.
    OQ17  How much historical evidence lives off-repo (M2 ledgers, BEE raw results, Icarus cycles, off-repo code), and
          would recovering it change any profile in evidence/historical_profiles.jsonl?
    OQ18  Does a cross-family (I3) re-derivation of X0 and of the salvage matrix agree with this package's same-family
          reading? (FALSIFIERS.md s1 commits to revising if it disagrees on more than a third of items.)
    OQ19  How should the existing fleet be reorganised? Fifty-plus seats designed for parallel engines do not fit a
          single-observatory build; HUM-04 limits concurrent build sessions to three. Retasking is an operator
          decision (Q-OP5).
    OQ20  Is the definition of abstraction too strict? Requiring specificity, surface invariance, compression in two
          languages and transplant/interchange may reject genuine but context-bound abstractions; CONTEXT-BOUND CARRIER
          is typed, but abstraction itself has no typed partial outcome yet.
