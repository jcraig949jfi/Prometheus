## 1. Executive verdict

**Conditional GO for RSO as a thin federation of experiments. NO-GO for a
universal organism runtime, universal decisive scorer, or ten-engine program.**
This verdict covers only supplied sources 01/02/06; missing 00/03/04/05/07
must be reviewed before treating it as a complete-package implementation review.
Common custody, exposure, resource and claim records are valuable. Common
physical dynamics, state decompositions and intervention implementations are
not prerequisites for comparison. The supplied design does not explicitly
demand one simulator; implementation must not accidentally turn its common
protocol into one. [01:sections 3, 6-10]

The strongest change is to build unlike physical specimens before freezing
the interface, while measuring discovery separately from repair. Fund a small
conventional reference R0, a merged R1/R2 kernel with distinct experimental
toggles, an R8 control suite, and a tiny R4 local specimen. Bring R4 forward
from Day 45 to Days 10-15, but do not fund a full chemistry ecosystem. Merge
only the simple fixed-topology plastic R3 variant into the R0 reference effort.

For the first 90 days, propose 30% of authorized engineering/analysis
person-hours for the thin tunnel and qualification fixtures, 40% for these
bounded candidate implementations, 20% for discriminating experiments, and
10% for independent challenge/reserve. These are mutually exclusive labor
buckets, not measured forecasts or a spending authorization. No absolute
staffing or compute budget was supplied. Initial work should be CPU-sized;
paid training or fleet expansion needs a separate decision.

The first deliverable is an instrument that can reject a convincing fake,
admit a familiar genuine mechanism, and distinguish an unreachable target
from an incapable substrate. A natural recursive-sagacity positive is not a
90-day acceptance condition. Agreement among designers adds no evidence.

## 2. Fatal flaws

No supplied statement establishes that the RSO idea is fatally wrong. The
following are **fatal to particular inferences if left unresolved**, not
reasons to discard the speculative portfolio.

1. **Recursive order is not identified by allocation or write ancestry.** A
   fixed compiler can wrap learned task content in newly allocated updater
   objects and produce V -> U -> S traces. Savings, lesions and rescue can
   all follow the cargo. Fresh memory is not fresh information. Without an
   intervention-defined distinction between task content and changes to the
   rule of acquisition, a recursive claim is unlicensed. Section 11 repairs
   the claim's scope; it does not promise a universal depth detector.
2. **Recovery near a chosen solution does not qualify discovery from the
   initial population.** Scaffold descent conditions on a successful witness
   and its residual organization. A beautiful recovery surface can coexist
   with essentially no measured cold-start discovery. It cannot, alone,
   justify a developmental null. [01:sections 13-14]
3. **A public scorer plus a co-adapting generator can select its own evidence.**
   The design already warns about private handshakes and foreign generators.
   That warning needs enforced discovery/confirmation custody, not another
   benchmark seed. Otherwise observed demand and transfer can both be products
   of shared generator grammar or repeated analyst selection. [01:section 12]
4. **A boundary-incomplete reset invalidates the causal contrast.** In-flight
   messages, optimizer state, external marks, time, RNG streams, allocator IDs
   and host caches can carry information after nominal task reset. For an
   inaccessible physical system, use a weaker qualified claim rather than
   pretend a full intervention occurred. [01:sections 6-9, 19]

Separately, treating R8 as a negative by ancestry would make the ruler
inconsistent with the explicit permission for conventional meta-learning to
pass. Some R8 variants are counterfeits; others could genuinely construct
better learning rules. The behavior and intervention result must decide.

## 3. Major revisions

### 3.1 A claim-specific federation, not an organism ontology

Keep a small common envelope: registered cell ID, physical/version identity,
initial and retained-state custody, exposure history, resource vector,
intervention intent and native realization, uncertainty, and artifact hashes.
Allow the substrate to supply native state/observation maps, causal ordering,
reset limits and effect estimators. Fast, persistent and inherited are
operational time/custody categories, not universally separable containers.

Publish capability profiles: a substrate may support behavioral comparisons
without supporting transplantation or full developmental provenance. Missing
capabilities cap claims; they need not exclude the substrate from RSO.
Two unlike realizations are an interface stress test, not proof of universal
neutrality. A shared API does not eliminate shared sensor/readout priors.

### 3.2 Make measurement interference an empirical contract

Reality/Observation separation is necessary but insufficient. An event emitter
can change scheduling, backpressure, numerical precision and energy use before
the out-of-band observer does anything. Distinguish simulated native cost from
host measurement overhead; meter both without treating either as free.

| Execution class | Minimum defensible contract | Failure to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| E0 deterministic | Same native initial state and exogenous schedule; equality of scientifically relevant states/actions under observer off/minimal/full | Checking only score equality while hidden dynamics change |
| E1 stochastic | Explicit random-stream ownership; seeded replay where meaningful plus distributional equivalence | Logging changes random draws; nonsignificant difference called equivalence |
| E2 asynchronous | Native happens-before and message-in-flight closure; schedule policy recorded, no invented global causal clock | Logging serializes contention or reconstructs false parentage |
| E3 continuous/chaotic | Qualified integrator, timestep/precision sensitivity, observation operator, declared statistical/dynamical estimand | Demanding indefinitely identical chaotic trajectories or calling tolerant replay exact |

Register equivalence margins from the smallest scientifically meaningful
effect and the admitted measurement error budget, before confirmation. An
interval must fall within the equivalence margin; failure to reject a
difference is not enough. Missing events needed for a claim mark it unqualified.
Observer-on/off tests are within each physics. Different R1/R4 outcomes are
not interference evidence. No mandatory serialization of every microscopic
event for all future substrates. [01:sections 7-8]

### 3.3 Replace automatic promotion with evidence facets

Keep readable C0-C6 labels if useful, but store independent facets: demand
class, detection operating range, causal identification, developmental origin,
transfer population, independent implementation, and resource scope. A causal
mechanism is not always a prerequisite for a robust behavioral comparison;
a local causal mechanism is not automatically an architectural principle.
Promotion should be a reproducible conjunction of claim-specific predicates,
with demotion on newly discovered defects. [01:section 20]

Likewise, the five qualification questions should have target-indexed answers,
not five timeless booleans. A planted capacity witness is not an endogenous
development witness. Detection on that witness is not detection on every
reachable phenotype. Developability and reach must distinguish an allowed
lifetime path from finding that path under the registered search budget.

### 3.4 Make fossil-to-foundry reuse an executable decision

The logic learns from failure **only if each retained component carries a
specific requalification test and a forbidden inference**. No whole legacy
engine is production-qualified by its historical name. [02:closing examples]

| Fossil | Retain as | Qualification and forbidden inference |
|---|---|---|
| Crius workspace | Candidate bounded storage primitive; semantic opcodes as supplied-faculty controls | Separate retained content from counters/addresses; a built-in matching or simulation opcode is not discovered abstraction |
| Proteus graph/call and search | Candidate execution primitive; known ascent/plateau/valley fixture | Native reset/cost tests; explicit neutral-step acceptance; planted solvability is not search success |
| Ananke packet physics | Candidate transport/queue primitive; planted capacity and light-cone controls | Restore all in-flight state; planted XOR establishes only its demonstrated capacity |
| Aether local propagation | Locality/conservation fixture and possible transport substrate | Verify content-sensitive intervention separately; activation propagation is not learning or reasoning |
| Ensorain factorization | Tuned constant, mean, lookup and capacity-matched predictor baselines | Compare on fresh lawful families with full fit cost; compact prediction alone is not abstraction |
| Aphrodite libraries | Useful acquisition baseline and task-cargo counterfeit fixture | Separate using prior solutions from constructing later update rules; do not reject all libraries |
| Nestor/BEE copying/construction | Copier/painter/transplant and seeded-lineage fixtures | Trace native writes vs host splices, qualify lesion dose and sham; maintenance of seeds is not de novo origin |

Production reuse begins with small primitives: bounded ownership, native
resource metering, explicit randomness/queues and custody receipts, requalified
in the new consumer. Retire unsupported semantic labels, unqualified promotion
rules and inaccessible historical campaign dependencies from production.
Preserve their artifacts as fossils. For every persuasive attack, include a
clean twin the auditor must not falsely accuse. These historical dispositions
are review recommendations, not fresh empirical verdicts on legacy code.

## 4. Strongest elements

- The registered developmental cell prevents a bounded result from silently
  becoming a universal statement. Preserve its full conditioning variables.
- Capacity, demand, reach, detection and developability expose different
  explanations for failure. Preserve the distinction, not merely the labels.
- HOLD/ADAPT/BUILD/COMPRESS/COMPOSE/RECURSE are observational coordinates rather
  than a required module list or a compulsory ladder.
- W0/W1/W2 admits that calibration and developmental ecology have different
  purposes; the correction is to connect them without universalizing W0.
- Local chemistry, message ecology, continuous plasticity and external state
  resist an exclusively software-object interpretation.
- Lifecycle cost, strong ordinary competitors, typed nulls, critic controls
  and explicit falsifiers allow the program to lose usefully.
- Conventional methods are permitted to win; the target is evidence of a
  mechanism, not alien appearance. [01:sections 3-5, 11, 19-24; 02:R0]

These are useful commitments, not demonstrated properties of an implemented
observatory. They deserve preservation because of the alternatives they
separate, not because multiple architects recommended them.

## 6. Missing architecture families

The most underdeveloped family is **embodied dynamical computation**: a
compliant body, hysteretic material or reaction-diffusion reservoir coupled
to sensing and action, where adaptation changes the body/environment dynamics
and useful memory need not be an addressable object. R3 and R4 might eventually
express it, but their present descriptions do not make closed-loop morphology
and control delegation a primary contrast.

A later minimal specimen should compare passive body, fixed controller,
readout-only adaptation, body adaptation, and joint adaptation. Charge physical
preparation, maintenance, sensing and actuation. A clever passive filter or
preconfigured resonator can outperform a controller without having developed
a learner; that is a useful physical result, not recursive sagacity.

Also preserve **self-maintenance/repair under damage** as an axis, and strengthen
finite-state identification, Bayesian/sufficient-statistic inference and simple
change detection as baselines. These are not requests for additional early
runtimes. Collective cognition, message circulation and stigmergy are already
represented; listing them as missing would misread the package.

No architecture can be certified as something LLM designers would not invent.
Anti-gravity means challenging shared organizing assumptions, not proving a
design's originality. Primitive generation inherits a grammar, resource model
and selector even without semantic names. Reserve later exploration mass for
unusual reproducible mechanisms, but demand a discriminating experiment before
funding an architecture foundry. [02:R5-R7, R10 and beyond]

## 7. Hidden common priors

1. **Episodes and lifetimes:** sharp birth, reset and inheritance boundaries
   suit software. Ecologies can retain history with no natural individual reset.
2. **Separable state:** inherited/fast/persistent categories may overlap in
   distributed hysteresis. Define intervention scope, not mandatory buffers.
3. **Addressability and causal legibility:** a convenient lesion API may favor
   small named objects over delocalized effects. Lesion difficulty is not
   evidence that the latter lacks a mechanism.
4. **Shared task semantics:** a sensor codec, global action choice or instantly
   supplied reward can grant cognition or transport that local physics should
   pay for. Record readout training, information bandwidth, latency and control.
5. **Human decomposition:** compression, procedures and hierarchical causal
   levels favor reusable object narratives; equivalent compiled dynamics may
   not preserve the apparent number of levels.
6. **Search favorability:** operators, neutrality tolerances and chosen witnesses
   can privilege the architecture the designers find interpretable.
7. **Task grammar and stationarity:** apparently new causal families may share
   generators, codecs or meta-rules; cheap repeated structure is not independent
   evidence of broad transfer.
8. **Comparable cost:** bytes, lattice sites, parameters and rank are not common
   capacity units. Hardware speed is not native physical efficiency. Report
   both implementation performance and declared native resource constraints.
9. **Clean history:** authored initial states, pretrained weights, curricula and
   optimizer selection all provide information; empty fast state is not a
   blank organism.
10. **Scientific yield scalar:** uncertainty resolved per compute/inference/
    energy/attention is a decision principle, not a dimensionally defined ratio.
    Report resource vectors and named alternatives resolved; disclose weights
    if scalarization is necessary. Do not count billing and its energy component
    twice. [01:sections 6, 18-19, 22-23]

An unlike shadow catches some of these priors. Independent world authors,
readout controls and within-physics interference tests catch different ones.
Neither agreement nor a common protocol substitutes for those checks.

## 8. Search/reachability critique

**Scaffold Free-Fall should change, not be deleted.** It is a useful repair
assay, especially when a specific neutral plateau or missing operator prevents
recovery. Its measured quantity is conditional on selected witness, ablation
path, encoding, operator, starting law and budget. It is not a generic distance
to emergence. [01:sections 13-14]

Before a search-based null receives a meaningful ceiling:

- Use several structurally distinct witnesses implementing the same behavior.
  Count verified functional hits, not rediscovery of particular bytes.
- Include cold starts from the registered initial distribution, separate from
  residual-scaffold starts. Account for useful material left by every ablation.
- Test exact tiny ascent, plateau and valley paths with at least two affordable
  operator/acceptance schemes. Search locality belongs to representation plus
  operator, not substrate alone.
- Estimate viable, neutral, beneficial and catastrophic mutation fractions with
  uncertainty and a registered phenotype tolerance. Sampled neutral connectivity
  generally gives a lower bound, not the whole connected component.
- Report independent-founder hit proportions, budget-conditioned curves,
  failed/sterile trials, and censored hitting times. Do not compute mean time
  to discovery only among successes or extrapolate from all-censored runs.
- Include tuning, restarts and witness construction in the appropriate cost
  account. Rich geometry maps must themselves resolve a decision worth their cost.

Zero verified hits in n independent identically sampled founders yields the
one-sided 95% binomial upper bound 1 - 0.05^(1/n) on the **verified-hit rate**
for that cell. Dependence, adaptive stopping or clustered founders require a
different analysis. A bound on latent qualifying hits additionally needs an
applicable detector-sensitivity bound; curated fixtures do not establish one
for every unknown phenotype. Detection error and search budget remain explicit.

An interpretable negative says which substrate/encoding/operator/history/world/
budget failed to produce which detectable target. Capacity-unestablished,
reach-unestablished and detection-unqualified are informative distinct outcomes.
No finite repair survey justifies retiring all local physics or all program spaces.

## 9. World-program critique

### W0: retain exactness, limit its authority

W0 should qualify leakage, reset, intervention, scoring and known positive/
negative behavior in stated effect/noise regimes. Tiny exact worlds cannot
qualify every future mechanism, nor need they reward deep development. Keep
native positive witnesses and cheap-policy bounds separate: existence of a
good solver does not prove cheaper alternatives fail. [01:section 11]

### W1: map development's economic domain, including its absence

Replace the premise that cheap inherited solutions become less economical with
a question: **where, if anywhere, does development pay?** Sweep initially just
reuse horizon and law turnover, with inherited information and compute fixed
within each contrast. Add ordinary adaptive/meta, finite-state/Bayesian,
lookup, library and fixed-policy competitors; do not force the answer by
comparing only against reactive organisms.

For each architecture j, record componentwise lifecycle cost:

Cost_j(N, theta) = allocated startup/training + development + maintenance
                  + sum of acquisition and use costs over N opportunities.

Disclose the allocation of one-time costs and show both deployment-only and
full-lifecycle views; many deployments can amortize training, one deployment
may not. Compare at matched competence, with failures and capped acquisition
retained rather than dropping unsuccessful organisms. Pick/tune comparators
on discovery data and freeze them before confirmation; do not choose the best
test-set baseline post hoc. Near a crossover, report uncertainty and utility
weights, not a visually selected boundary. A W1 advantage may demonstrate only
ordinary parameter adaptation, not BUILD or RECURSE.

The diagnostic sagacity ratio in [01:section 18] also needs commensurable
numerator/denominator costs, a declared amortization horizon and an explicit
carried-machinery denominator. It is not defined at a zero denominator and
can be misleading near zero. Report absolute savings and the full cost vector;
neither bytes divided into CPU-hours nor hidden cost weights produce a neutral
sagacity ranking.

Two useful world families:

1. **Renewal hidden-law work:** repeated tasks governed by latent causal rules;
   tunable law reuse, turnover and horizon; small exactly solvable cases and
   larger related cases. Compare inference of reusable rules against sufficient
   statistics, caching and full relearning. Fresh generator grammars are needed
   before claiming genuinely new-family transfer.
2. **Persistent worksite:** bounded navigation/construction or repair where
   costly marks can encode past discoveries; same opportunities with internal
   and external retention enabled/disabled. Price writing, reading and decay.
   Separate terrain improvement from retained information via matched controls.

For Causal/Nuisance Dissociation, keep the principle and strengthen the assay:
cross causal-law change yes/no with nuisance change yes/no. Ensure nuisance
transformations preserve task-relevant information or explicitly account for
changed Bayes difficulty, support and sampling opportunity. A causal change
requires appropriate adjustment, not a mandatory competence collapse. Invariance
plus adjustment can still be explained by a conventional estimator; it is not
by itself a reasoning certificate. [01:section 16]

### W2: grow one axis at a time, keep discovery distinct from confirmation

Start with one additional demand dimension, such as persistent external state,
bounded episodes and preregistered causal probes. Do not build an open-ended
universe to rescue an unqualified W1 result. An adaptive generator can discover
hard cases, but confirm on frozen checkpoints and sealed families with separate
custody. Report moving-frontier performance separately from fixed-panel progress.

The (H,L,C,V,I,Q) difficulty vector is a useful registration vocabulary, not a
guarantee that its coordinates are independent or monotonic. More irreversibility
can reduce informative exploration; more hidden state can destroy learnability.
Maintain matched positive feasibility checks at admitted settings and state
which baseline classes are bounded. A few strong baselines do not bound all
cheap policies. W2 retains interpretability by narrowing its claims, not by
pretending exact W0 authority extends to the whole ecology. [01:sections 11-12]

## 10. Cognitive-boundary critique

**Externalized cognition is a major scientific axis, not a distraction. Vary
the boundary early, but hold it fixed within each causal contrast.** Begin with
a 2x2 experiment: internal retention enabled/disabled crossed with writable
external retention enabled/disabled, including the no-retention control.
Do not immediately run all internal/external/social combinations.

Charge external preparation, capacity, read/write work, bandwidth, retention,
decay/repair and history across lives. A free structured worksite is an inherited
scaffold, not spontaneously invented memory. Match allowed information and
opportunities while retaining honest native latency and transport costs.

Use erasure, scrambling, read-blocking and donor swaps, each with matched shams.
Erasing a trail can damage the route rather than memory; blocking a mark can
change sensory difficulty; exchanging a niche can change resource availability.
Separate those physical effects from informational custody where possible.
If they cannot be separated, state the joint mechanism instead of claiming
isolated memory causality.

For shared niches the independent unit is the world/ecology, not each agent
or each episode. Reset or explicitly retain messages, host state, timing and
other agents alongside internal content. Report internal, niche-mediated and
joint-system benefits separately. A niche can legitimately learn, but an
organism-only claim needs an organism-only intervention. Social transfer can
wait until the smaller I/E experiment exposes a reason to pay for it.
[01:section 9; 02:R5]

## 11. Recursive-sagacity counterfeit attack

### Strongest practical counterfeit: a compiler that launders task cargo

During A, a fixed learning algorithm builds V_A: a task-distribution estimator
plus a growing library of macros, cached solutions and optimizer choices.
During B, a fixed compiler combines V_A with B observations to allocate a new
U_B closure. U_B embeds solution fragments, proposal priorities or selected
update rules. All later task-state writes are routed through U_B.

This can produce the proposed evidence without the intended interpretation:

| Apparent evidence | How the counterfeit supplies it |
|---|---|
| Later acquisition becomes cheaper | Reuse cached solutions or shared task grammar |
| V -> U -> S write provenance | Compiler materializes U from V; U emits every task write |
| V lesion/sham/rescue | Lesion removes useful cargo; sham preserves it; rescue restores it |
| Fresh U and S allocations | Compiler inlines the same information into different addresses |
| Donor crosses and irrelevant-history controls | Compatible donors share useful grammar; irrelevant donors do not |
| Further D/E transfer | Nominal families retain an unrecognized meta-grammar or source codec |

Source 01 also proposes cross-substrate reconstruction where possible (step
13). Credit this as a strong challenge to substrate-specific artifacts, but
not an automatic cure: a lookup-and-compiler mechanism can be independently
reimplemented in local or numerical physics while retaining the same task
grammar and answer cargo. Charge its native costs and test the same causal
alternatives in each realization. Independent physics, independent task
generation and absence of cargo are different qualifications; reconstruction
alone proves neither new developmental origin nor higher causal order.

The design's combined test would be stronger than savings alone, but ordinary
dependency and dataflow do not by themselves identify the claimed developmental
order. Its freeze step also needs a precise contract. A saved U snapshot that
continues to meta-adapt during C is not a frozen learning rule. Lock the
parameters/structure defining U's update law and any further writes to that
law; permit its declared runtime dynamics and the S learning it implements.
Reset task state separately as the source already requests. For entangled
systems unable to implement that partition, limit the corresponding claim.
[01:section 19]

### Two cases must not be conflated

- A disguised answer exporter under renamed layers is a counterfeit of
  improved learner construction, even if its reuse is economically valuable.
- A developed optimizer library that actually changes how fresh learning rules
  are constructed, and those rules improve learning on new tasks, may be
  genuine conventional nested improvement. A fixed interpreter, continuous
  parameters or library ancestry does not invalidate it. If this is what the
  strongest R8 does, admit it and abandon the exclusivity claim, not the result.

### Repair the operational claim

1. Define V, U and S by permitted interventions and temporal roles, not source
   labels, pointer boundaries or number of compiler layers. Register exposure
   and all retained channels before judging the candidate.
2. Establish the lifetime origin of the tested V change. Cross developmental
   histories with matched initialization, tuning and training exposure; compare
   inherited/seeded and developed forms. Scaffold recovery alone does not count.
3. After U construction, prevent further V influence during the C assay when
   testing the strictly mediated pathway. Draw C task instances only after U's
   update law is frozen. Fresh seeds are insufficient for new-family claims:
   reserve causal families and challenge the shared generator/codec explanation.
4. Clamp or swap induced U across V histories to test mediation and bypass;
   separately test U's response to identical S states and evidence streams.
   Carry equivalent task-relevant priors in fixed-updater controls where feasible.
   A detector should distinguish changed learning behavior from direct answer
   emission, not assume a universal content-free representation exists.
5. Lesion/sham/rescue and donor crosses must preserve matched damage, capacity,
   information access and resource accounting. Causal slicing that is impossible
   for a substrate limits the nested claim rather than proving its absence.
6. Compare conventional R0, R8-T, legitimate R8-U and compiled/flattened
   equivalents. Equivalent behavior under relabeling must not gain a claim rung.
   Where mechanisms genuinely change, state the contrast rather than forcing
   representation invariance that the experiment cannot establish.

The repaired claim is **operationally demonstrated nested developmental
improvement at the tested depth, boundary and task population**. Report causal
mediation, functional savings, developmental origin, transfer and practical
advantage as separate facets. This does not prove a representation-independent
recursive-depth theorem, a category outside meta-learning, or indefinite
self-improvement. The ruler must reject fake answers without rejecting genuine
learned optimizers merely because they are familiar. [01:section 19; 02:R8]

## 12. Five decisive experiments

The detailed source 04 is missing. These dispositions target the named ideas
and their descriptions in 01/02/06, not an unseen experimental protocol.

| Supplied experiment | Decision | Material change |
|---|---|---|
| Scaffold Free-Fall | CHANGE | Multi-witness, cold-start and exact-path comparison; Experiment 2 |
| Stigmergy Bypass | CHANGE | Rename boundary/custody crossover; external memory is a lawful mechanism, not inherently a bypass; Experiment 4 |
| Causal/Nuisance Dissociation | REMAIN, with protocol changes | Crossed design and information/difficulty qualification; Experiment 3 |
| Hardwire-to-develop transition maps | CHANGE | Search for crossover or its absence against ordinary adaptive competitors and full costs; Experiment 3 |

No idea needs deletion on present evidence. The following five experiments
replace vague promises with decisions. Proposed dimensions are pilot choices,
not measured findings. Before confirmation, an executable registration must
set target effect, equivalence margins, independent units, sample size/power,
caps, multiplicity/sequential policy and stop rules. Pilot observations inform
that registration but cannot also serve as its independent confirmation.

### Experiment 1: adapter, observer and reset torture

**Alternative:** common measurement is benign versus it alters or omits native
causality. Use an R1 deterministic witness, tiny R4 local witness and three-node
message ring. Add a numerical specimen only for the numerical claims actually
made. R4 could begin as an 8x8 lattice with at most four material species,
local hops/binding/unbinding and priced persistence; no global reference API.
First demonstrate native delayed-bit/transport behavior, not all coordinates.

**Arms/unit:** identical native initial states under observer off, minimal,
full, delayed and overloaded collection; randomized replicate/schedule pairs
as appropriate. Perturb reset completeness with a planted in-flight message,
retained host value and allocator/time channel, each with a clean twin.

**Readout/decision:** apply the class-specific contracts in section 3. Reject
the adapter's affected claims if it changes dynamics outside the frozen margin
or misses a required custody channel. Repair the tunnel, not the organism's
score. Two passing unlike specimens license those interfaces, not every physics.

### Experiment 2: repair versus discovery under measured search power

**Alternative:** target physics is incapable versus the chosen operator/start
law makes verified capacity hard to reach. Use distinct authored native
witnesses, scaffold levels and cold founders in R1/R2; compare at least two
bounded operators, including an acceptance scheme able to traverse a known
neutral path. Hold target semantics and verification separate from search.

**Arms/unit:** independent founders crossed with start law, witness family and
operator at fixed resource caps; exact micro-landscapes provide path controls.

**Readout/decision:** phenotype-hit proportions, censored resource-to-hit and
mutation-neighborhood estimates. Successful repair with absent cold hits
changes the search/initialization decision, not the capacity verdict. Spend
more only if a measured reachable interval or stepping-stone contrast justifies
it. Otherwise report a search-bounded negative and stop that scaling path.

### Experiment 3: economic crossover with law/nuisance challenge

**Alternative:** structural development saves lifecycle resources versus an
ordinary learner, lookup/library or inherited policy explains the advantage.
Use the renewal hidden-law W1 family, sweeping initially only reuse horizon
and law turnover. Compare tuned R0, R8-T, fixed-policy/sufficient-statistic
baselines and R1/R2 development toggles; no automatic stronger architecture.

**Arms/unit:** paired independent developmental founders on frozen world panels;
at selected cells cross nuisance/law changes in a 2x2 assay, with feasibility
and information-preservation checks. Freeze tuning on discovery data.

**Readout/decision:** competence-conditioned acquisition curves, failure rates,
native/host resource vectors, deployment and full-lifecycle cost, uncertainty
around any crossover. A confirmable advantage over the frozen comparator set
licenses an economic claim at that regime. No advantage inside informative
margins shrinks the development investment; wide intervals mean inconclusive,
not equivalence. Causal adjustment is graded against appropriate behavior.

### Experiment 4: internal/external cognitive custody crossover

**Alternative:** useful retained information is internal, in the niche, or
inseparable from world modification. Use one bounded persistent-worksite family
and the I-enabled/disabled x E-enabled/disabled factorial; retain lawful priced
external access rather than treating it as cheating.

**Arms/unit:** independently prepared worlds, history-matched donors, erasure,
read-block, scrambling and swaps with physical-damage shams. Charge across-life
preparation; keep shared-world agents in one statistical unit.

**Readout/decision:** future acquisition/use savings and loss/rescue contrasts
under each custody channel. If benefits follow E rather than I, change the
claim to niche-mediated development. If they follow terrain improvement but
not retained information, report that mechanism. If custody cannot be isolated,
stop organism-only causal promotion rather than prohibit external cognition.

### Experiment 5: nested-compiler red team and genuine-positive admission

**Alternative:** the ruler identifies improved learner construction versus
task cargo, labels or authorship. Supply a direct-answer exporter, R8-T,
genuine authored learned-updater constructor, conventional meta reference,
compiled/flattened equivalents and irrelevant-history controls.

**Arms/unit:** independently constructed mechanisms/history donors on held-out
task families; apply the staged resets, mediation clamp, lesion/sham/rescue
and donor crossing from section 11. Hold out attack constructors, not only
random seeds. The intended behavior of each fixture needs independent checking.

**Readout/decision:** false promotion of the exporter, false rejection of the
genuine updater, causal mediation, and lifecycle savings. Reject the ruler
if it rewards relabeled cargo or vetoes a valid conventional mechanism by
pedigree. A clean fixture result qualifies only the tested attack classes.
Run a naturally developed candidate through this assay only after the ruler
and candidate's intervention implementation qualify; no recursive-positive quota.

## 13. Revised 90-day plan

This is a replacement decision envelope because source 05 was not available.
Calendar deadlines do not override qualifications. Every build serves one of
the five experiments; none is an invitation to a platform expansion.

| Interval | Authorized-first proposal | Exit or stop decision |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1-15 | Thin cell/custody/cost schema; exact W0 truth and clean/leaky twins; R0/R8 bounded references; R1 witness; tiny R4 shadow and message fixture | Experiment 1 identifies interface repairs before freeze; no full chemistry or universal snapshot system |
| Days 16-30 | R1/R2 development toggles; multi-witness/cold search pilot; authored recursive counterfeits and genuine positive | Experiment 2 separates repair/discovery feasibility; Experiment 5 exposes ruler defects; no scaling through either failure |

Implementation hours go in candidate/tunnel buckets; running and analyzing
contrasts go in the experiment bucket, never both. Track CPU-hours, GPU-hours,
energy, dollars, tokens and operator/reviewer time separately. Set their
absolute caps before any execution authority; this review does not borrow
authorization from an older design package.

The balance avoids a sterile metrology quarter while resisting ten architecture
teams. If basic qualification consumes the experiment allocation, shrink scope
and return a methods/feasibility result rather than erase the controls. A later
R3 upgrade needs a measured search-plasticity question; a later R4 upgrade needs
a local-physics or ontology-dependence question. Neither wins by calendar alone.

Before implementation, write or update and execute native transition, reset,
observer-equivalence, leakage, resource, oracle, donor/intervention and
statistical-boundary tests. Documentary validation of this review cannot replace
those tests. Independent authorship must be substantive, not a second call to
the same implementation with a different random seed.

## 14. What should explicitly NOT be built

- Ten standalone runtimes, separate R1 and R2 software empires, or an early
  full R5/R6/R7/R9 project. Preserve their hypotheses without prepaying upkeep.
- A universal physics simulator, compulsory global clock, all-event lineage
  database or mandatory exact checkpoint format for all substrates.
- A semantic instruction set with privileged hypothesis, analogy, planner,
  abstraction or skepticism operators and subsequent discovery claims.
- An automated architecture foundry, unbounded W2 ecology or expensive GPU
  training campaign before one bounded discriminatory result justifies it.
- A universal scalar intelligence/sagacity score, dimensionally undefined
  scientific-yield number, or automatic C6 promotion pipeline.
- An observer that silently changes dynamics, an oracle visible to the learner,
  or a reset that ignores host/external/social retained channels.
- A ruler that calls all libraries negative, all familiar optimizers uninteresting,
  or all repeated write layers recursive. No built-in requirement that R0 lose.
- A multi-engine dashboard, fleet expansion or legacy framework transplant whose
  only deliverable is activity rather than a resolved alternative.
- A new claim that past engines proved cognition from labels, propagation,
  seeded construction, library size or source inspection without its own test.

Do not retire the underlying speculative mechanisms solely for being expensive
or unfamiliar. Retire an implementation tranche when it cannot resolve its
registered question within its cap, and record what remained untested.

## 15. Three highest-value unresolved questions

1. **Is improved learner construction causally identifiable beyond a useful
   re-description of ordinary meta-learning?** The distinction must survive
   cargo controls, compiler flattening and legitimate conventional positives.
   If only an operational intervention-relative notion survives, adopt it
   openly and drop stronger ontological language.
2. **Does structural development have any reproducible lifecycle advantage over
   well-tuned fixed/adaptive/library competitors on controlled world families?**
   A qualified negative across the chosen regimes should reduce this investment;
   a positive must survive deployment-horizon and exposure-cost sensitivity.
3. **Is the dominant bottleneck physics, reachable search paths, or the demand
   program itself?** Unlike specimens, cold-start/scaffold contrasts and matched
   feasibility witnesses must separate these explanations before a new engine
   or a larger world is authorized.
