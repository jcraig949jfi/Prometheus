## 2. Fatal flaws
-----

None establishes that RSO itself should be abandoned. Four defects would be
fatal to the corresponding scientific inference:

- **Write ancestry is not recursive order.** A compiler can wrap task cargo
  in fresh updater objects and manufacture V -> U -> S dependency traces.
- **Scaffold recovery is not cold-start discovery.** Residual organization
  conditions the result on a selected successful witness.
- **Adaptive worlds can select their own evidence.** Discovery and sealed
  confirmation need different custody, not merely different seeds.
- **Incomplete resets invalidate causal interpretation.** External marks,
  messages, optimizers, clocks, allocators and host caches can retain content.

Also, R8 cannot be classified as a negative by pedigree while conventional
meta-learning is explicitly allowed to pass.

## 3. Major revisions
-----

Use claim-specific capability profiles. A substrate can support behavioral
comparison without supporting exact transplantation or complete provenance;
limit its claims instead of excluding its physics.

Qualify observer interference within each substrate: exact relevant-state
comparisons for deterministic systems; preregistered equivalence intervals
for stochastic/asynchronous/numerical estimands; native partial orders and
in-flight queues for messages; integrator/precision checks for continuous
dynamics. Out-of-band analysis does not make event emission non-invasive.
Nonsignificance is not equivalence. Different R1/R4 behavior is not evidence
of measurement interference.

Replace automatic claim-ladder promotion with separate evidence facets:
demand, detection, causality, origin, transfer, independence and resources.

Fossil-to-foundry reuse should produce concrete tests:

- Crius: bounded workspace candidate; semantic opcodes as supplied-faculty controls.
- Proteus: graph/call primitives and known ascent/plateau/valley search fixtures.
- Ananke: packet/queue primitives and planted capacity controls, not emergence.
- Aether: propagation/locality controls, not presumed learning or content transport.
- Ensorain: tuned constants, means, lookup and matched-factor predictors first.
- Aphrodite: useful library baseline plus task-cargo counterfeit attacks.
- Nestor/BEE: seeded/copier/painter/transplant provenance fixtures and clean twins.

Requalify every production primitive. Retire unsupported interpretations and
unqualified promotion rules, not the archived failure evidence. Critics must
reject convincing false accusations as well as genuine defects.

## 6. Missing architecture families
-----

The most underdeveloped family is embodied dynamical computation: compliant
bodies, hysteretic material or reaction-diffusion reservoirs in a closed
sensor/action loop. Compare passive body, fixed controller, readout adaptation,
body adaptation and joint adaptation. Charge physical preparation and upkeep.
A useful passive filter is not automatically a developed learner.

Preserve self-maintenance/repair as an axis and strengthen finite-state/Bayesian
system-identification baselines. Collective cognition, messages and external
memory are already present. None of these observations authorizes another
early engine. No architecture can be certified as uninventable by an LLM;
anti-gravity means challenging shared assumptions, not certifying originality.

## 7. Hidden common priors
-----

The remaining priors include sharply bounded lifetimes, separable inherited/
fast/persistent state, addressable lesions, globally convenient sensor/action
interfaces, reusable-object narratives, favorable mutation encodings, shared
task grammars, free authored initial structure and falsely comparable capacity
units. A primitive foundry still inherits its grammar and selector.

Report native and host costs separately. Neither the conceptual scientific-yield
ratio nor the sagacity savings ratio licenses adding unlike units. Disclose
scalarization weights and amortization horizon; report absolute savings and
resource vectors. A zero carried-machinery denominator makes the ratio undefined.

## 8. Search/reachability critique
-----

Keep Scaffold Free-Fall as a repair assay. Add cold founders, multiple unlike
witnesses, exact ascent/plateau/valley controls and at least two bounded search
operators. Score verified functional solutions, not exact witness bytes.

Report independent-founder hit rates, censored time/resource-to-hit, failed
trials and mutation-neighborhood uncertainty. Sampled neutral connectivity is
generally only a lower bound. Include search tuning and witness costs.

For zero verified hits in n independent identically sampled founders, the
one-sided 95% binomial upper bound is 1 - 0.05^(1/n), on the verified-hit rate
in that cell. This is not universal absence or a latent-hit bound without
applicable detector qualification. Adaptive stopping/dependence changes the
analysis. A search null belongs to its physics, encoding, operator, initial
law, world, detector and budget.

## 9. World-program critique
-----

**W0:** retain exact truth for leakage, reset, intervention and ruler checks;
do not mistake calibration success for qualification of every future claim.

**W1:** ask where development pays, including nowhere in the tested range.
Start with renewal hidden-law tasks and sweep reuse horizon/law turnover.
Compare conventional adaptive/meta learners, sufficient statistics, lookup,
libraries and fixed policies at matched competence. Include failures, tuning,
startup, development, retention, maintenance and deployment horizon. Freeze
comparators before confirmation. An advantage may be ordinary adaptation,
not structural construction.

**W2:** add one demand axis at a time with bounded episodes and causal probes.
Adaptive generation is discovery; confirm on frozen panels and separately
held families. The difficulty vector is not guaranteed independent, monotonic
or learnable. A few cheap baselines do not bound all cheap policies.

Keep Causal/Nuisance Dissociation, but cross law change and nuisance change
in a 2x2 design. Qualify information preservation, support and Bayes difficulty.
Measure appropriate adjustment, not compulsory performance collapse. Robustness
plus adjustment alone is not a reasoning certificate.

## 10. Cognitive-boundary critique
-----

Vary the boundary early; hold it fixed within a causal contrast. Start with
internal retention on/off crossed with external retention on/off, not all
seven internal/external/social combinations.

Use a persistent worksite with priced marks, access and decay. Charge external
preparation and history across lives. Erasure, scrambling, read-blocking and
donor swaps need physical-damage shams: damaging terrain is not a selective
memory lesion. If informational and physical effects cannot be separated,
claim the joint mechanism. Shared worlds, not their individual agents, are
the independent units. Benefits that follow the niche are legitimate, but
must not be relabeled organism-only development.

## 11. Recursive-sagacity counterfeit attack
-----

Build V from a learned task-distribution estimate plus macros/cached answers.
A fixed compiler uses V and B data to allocate U_B, embedding useful cargo or
optimizer choices; all future S writes pass through U_B. This can save cost,
produce nested provenance, lose under lesions, rescue successfully, favor
relevant donors and transfer across nominal families sharing a meta-grammar.
Fresh addresses are not fresh information.

Cross-substrate reconstruction is valuable but not automatically decisive:
the same cargo mechanism can be reimplemented in unlike physics. Independent
physics and independent task generation test different confounds.

Repair the claim with intervention-defined roles, lifetime-origin controls,
closed retained channels, later fresh tasks/families, U mediation clamps,
matched lesion/sham/rescue and fixed-updater controls. Lock U's update-law
parameters/structure against further meta-adaptation while permitting declared
runtime dynamics and S learning. Compare compiled/flattened equivalents.

Crucially, a genuine developed optimizer constructor is not a counterfeit
merely because it uses libraries or a fixed interpreter. Admit it if it passes.
The defensible result is operational nested developmental improvement at the
tested depth/boundary/population, not a category necessarily outside meta-learning
or evidence of indefinite recursive improvement.

## 12. Five decisive experiments
-----

1. **Observer/reset torture:** R1 witness, tiny R4 local witness and message
   ring, with observer off/minimal/full/overloaded and planted leaks plus clean
   twins. Reject affected adapter claims if native dynamics/custody fail.
2. **Repair versus discovery:** multiple native witnesses and cold founders,
   exact micro-paths and two operators. Distinguish reachable capacity from
   operator/initialization failure before scaling search.
3. **Economic crossover and law/nuisance challenge:** two-knob W1 map, strong
   frozen comparators, fresh confirmation and full costs. No informative
   structural advantage reduces investment; wide intervals are inconclusive.
4. **Internal/external custody crossover:** independently prepared priced
   worksites with factorial retention and lesion/sham/donor controls. Reassign
   the claim to the niche or joint system when that is where benefit resides.
5. **Nested-compiler red team:** answer exporter, task library, genuine updater
   constructor and ordinary meta controls, including held-out constructors.
   Reject rulers that promote cargo or reject genuine familiar mechanisms.

## 13. Revised 90-day plan
-----

- Days 1-15: thin envelope, exact W0/checker, bounded R0/R8 references, R1
  witness, tiny R4 and message fixture; repair interfaces before freeze.
- Days 16-30: R1/R2 toggles, cold/scaffold feasibility and authored nested
  counterfeits/genuine positive. Do not scale through qualification failures.
- Days 31-55: small W1 discovery map and one I/E cell; select one affordable,
  adequately powered fresh confirmation, or stop with a feasibility result.
- Days 56-75: execute that preregistered confirmation; remaining assays only
  if affordable. Pilot winners are not independent confirmations.
- Days 76-90: independent checker/implementation challenge and stop/redirect/
  continue decision. No promised natural recursive positive.

Labor shares: tunnel/W0/fixtures 30% (including message ring); R0 12%; R1/R2
14%; R8 6%; R4 8%; experiments/analysis 20%; independent challenge/reserve 10%.
Total 100%, with no double-counting implementation and experiment hours.
Absolute staffing and compute caps require later authority. Upgrade at most
one R3 extension or full R4 tranche for a named discriminating question.

## 14. What should explicitly NOT be built
-----

No ten standalone engines, universal simulator, universal scalar scorer,
semantic-faculty instruction set, automatic C6 promoter, all-event global
lineage service, unbounded W2, architecture foundry, expensive training fleet,
or dashboard-first program. No ruler requiring ordinary learning to lose.
Preserve deferred hypotheses rather than deleting inconvenient possibilities.

## 15. Three highest-value unresolved questions
-----

1. Can improved learner construction be causally identified beyond a useful
   description of ordinary meta-learning, without excluding legitimate methods?
2. Does structural development retain any reproducible lifecycle advantage
   over strong fixed/adaptive/library competitors on controlled families?
3. Is the limiting factor physics, search reach, or demand, and which small
   contrast would actually distinguish those explanations?
