- If the permitted carried channel has at most L distinguishable states and there is no additional key-correlated side information, exact recovery cannot exceed min(1, L/M). #### A constructive updater control, and why it is not a strong-recursion positive

Proposed finite rational world for a narrower positive:

- Let beta and x be positive rationals from finite declared sets; let family curvature h = beta*x. During A, expose lawful calibration pairs sufficient for a fixed learner to acquire beta. Record the exposure rather than pretending this information appeared spontaneously.
- During B, expose x. The acquired state V constructs U with update coefficient eta = 1/(beta*x). Freeze that coefficient and sever V's further influence for the strictly mediated assay.
- Only afterward draw fresh task target y from {-1, +1}, independently of A/B given the registration. Task S begins at zero. A legitimate task observation is the gradient g(s) = h*(s-y), and U makes the update s' = s - eta*g(s).
- Algebraically, correct eta gives s' = y after that update. A mismatched h_hat gives s' = (h/h_hat)*y from s=0, hence an incorrect answer when h_hat differs from h. Swapping U should swap this effect; holding U fixed should remove the V-history effect if no bypass exists. Withholding the only task-dependent observation cannot allow one fixed answer to solve both fresh y values.

This is an analytic proposal for checking custody, update-law intervention, and mediation.
