#### Specific strengths, not votes of confidence

- **Fable:** converts criticism into registered scripts, expected verdicts, receipts, negative controls and later probes; retains corrections that narrow its original claims rather than presenting every pass as definitive. The strongest contribution is a reproducible specification-failure corpus, not the author's endorsement of its preferred architecture. [C, L16-70, L494-553][C]
- **Fable:** distinguishes stored capacity from information actually selected by experience, withdraws its ORIGIN classification, and admits its exact certificates presently concern random-content retention rather than structure. These are substantive limits on its own favored instrument. [C, L445-457, L479-492][C]; [F1, L268-287][F1]
- **ASTRA:** treats intervention impossibility as a ceiling on a particular claim, not evidence that the physics lacks learning. It separately addresses donor mediation, V bypass, U-law freezing, compiled equivalents, conventional positives and task-generator dependence. [A, L77-86, L90-103, L442-502][A]
- **ASTRA:** gives operational observer contracts, equivalence margins rather than nonsignificance, external custody/damage controls, and deployment versus full-lifecycle cost views. These are concrete design safeguards absent from a simple "same score" or "same number of entries" comparison, but remain proposals here. [A, L105-125, L336-355, L401-418][A]

### Inferences

- The evidence asymmetry favors Fable for **showing particular loose/partly tightened formulations can pass simple organisms**, not for validating its replacement detector or defeating ASTRA's repaired assay. ASTRA's review is stronger on causal alternatives but cannot claim those alternatives have been experimentally separated. [C, L37-70, L459-474][C]; [A, L3-5, L595-612][A]
- The useful division is not "Fable is rigorous, ASTRA is permissive" or the reverse. Fable narrows certification toward exact memory bounds; ASTRA permits broader but explicitly narrower-in-meaning empirical claims. The design decision is which claim is being licensed and which exclusions have actually been tested. [F2, L452-477][F2]; [A, L127-141, L377-391][A]

## 3. What weaknesses remain, what is compatible, and how should v0.2 harden?

### Takeaway

Import Fable's attacks as precisely labeled fixtures, not as a replacement verdict oracle. Harden ASTRA by converting its causal promises into executable, power-qualified predicates while retaining familiar genuine positives and honest claim ceilings; harden Fable by repairing clause coverage, degenerate controls, curriculum confounds and stale origin labels. The exact-bound ceiling, strong-recursion definition and near-term scope priorities require explicit decisions, not synthetic agreement. [C, L459-553][C]; [F2, L408-477][F2]; [A, L471-502, L516-521, L595-612][A]

### Cited Findings

#### Real weaknesses in Fable

1. **Executed does not mean fully qualified.** Run 1 omits requested resets; run 3 repeats inert-sham/identical-arm weaknesses; run 2 still lacks clause-complete fire tests and broad C transfer. The README's corrections outrank optimistic preregistration titles such as "strict." [C, L90-135, L291-335, L459-469, L494-546][C]
2. **The proposed recursive discriminator still lacks a positive.** "Above every inherited level" needs an inherited-depth definition; "builder acts on itself" can remain representation-sensitive. Fable explicitly concedes that inherited depth cannot be read from behavior and that its proposed origin classifier failed. [F2, L371-377, L440-450][F2]
3. **Exact-bound strength ceiling narrows what can count.** Fable recognizes that its best certificates concern memory and not structure. Making all robustness contingent on an exact restricted-class bound is a design policy, not a demonstrated impossibility of robust causal or comparative evidence without one. Its own plan risks rewarding what is easy to bound. [F1, L268-287, L1325-1330][F1]; [F2, L452-477][F2]
4. **A designed isomer panel is a sampled operating range.** Even second-author and blind-found additions cannot prove neutrality for unrepresented organisms. Memory isomers alone do not qualify deeper construction interventions. Fable states the first limit; the second follows from its chosen panel and the separate missing strong positive. [F2, L194-209][F2]; [F1, L1107-1120, L1056-1061][F1]
5. **Portfolio priorities remain priors.** R3 is preferred for gradient access/tool reuse, not measured developmental superiority; the R1/R2 merge is based on a paragraph rather than the DGM design. The 65% instrument allocation is judgment with an explicit later cost reversal, not evidence of optimal resource use. [F3, L93-101, L181-220][F3]; [F1, L140-151, L1325-1337][F1]

#### Real weaknesses in ASTRA itself

1. **No executed repair or genuine positive.** ASTRA calls for a "genuine authored learned-updater constructor" but supplies no executable specimen or independently established admission threshold in this review. Its sample sizes, margins, power and stop rules are explicitly left for registration. Fable's running kit is a concrete advance ASTRA does not yet match. [A, L3-5, L516-521, L595-612][A]
2. **Mediation is necessary for the intended path, not sufficient to distinguish cargo.** A compiled cargo mechanism can mediate V's effect through U and change U's responses. ASTRA correctly proposes fixed-updater priors and identical-evidence contrasts, but "equivalent task-relevant priors where feasible" is not an operational equivalence test. The boundary between learned optimizer and encoded answer remains a qualification obligation, not an achieved result. [A, L423-449, L473-500][A]
3. **"Fresh causal family" remains underspecified.** ASTRA warns that fresh seeds are insufficient, but does not instantiate the allowed relationships among A, B, C, D and E. Fable's probes show why both curriculum shape and component overlap must be registered separately; simply demanding no overlap can make useful-history and wrong-history conditions collapse. [A, L260-262, L479-482][A]; [C, L54-70, L262-289][C]
4. **Fixture sensitivity cannot justify unknown-phenotype absence.** ASTRA explicitly knows this, but its first deliverable promises to distinguish unreachable targets from incapable substrates. Operational reporting must preserve capacity-unestablished, reach-unestablished and detector-limited outcomes rather than collapse them into that binary. [A, L49-52, L137-141, L305-315][A]
5. **Early breadth can overwhelm a thin effort.** Days 1-30 already include R0/R8, R1/R2, R4, a message fixture, interference/reset controls, search and counterfeit admission; W1 plus I/E follows. The review provides no absolute staffing/compute budget. Its own scope-shrink clause must become a binding stop rule; renaming all of this "thin" is not a feasibility proof. [A, L41-47, L620-657][A]
6. **Faceted evidence still needs a machine-checkable contract.** No promotion predicates, actual U-freeze implementation, adapter equivalence thresholds, or validated cross-physics estimators were implemented here. Preserving the six functional coordinates as noncompulsory names also does not itself repair BUILD/HOLD's state-split ambiguity. [A, L90-141, L167-186, L451-458][A]; [F2, L75-94, L153-159][F2]

- **Known-answer qualification plus capability profiles:** require calibration appropriate to each claimed effect, keep a matched negative and a genuine positive, and admit weaker behavioral observations when internals are inaccessible. Fable's blind-found door helps avoid admitting only legible designer physics. [F3, L83-91][F3]; [A, L90-103, L607-612][A]

### Inferences

#### Ordered hardening suggestions for v0.2 (proposed, not authorized experiments)

1. **Create a claim/fixture matrix before a score.** Rows should distinguish retained content, reuse savings, learned-updater construction, operational nested improvement and strong recursion. Columns should include GEARBOX, BUILDER, STRATEGIST, task exporter, maturation, content leak, world-parked memory, genuine conventional updater, and compiled equivalent. A fixture's expected answer can differ by row. Leave unknown strong-positive cells UNKNOWN rather than inventing a positive or calling all libraries negative. [F3, L293-312][F3]; [A, L461-502, L595-612][A]
2. **Register the full family/curriculum relation.** Specify A as one family or a curriculum, B/D/E's component and grammar overlap, C's required transfer scope, exposure/tuning history, acceptance rule, savings factor, caps/censoring, and what makes a donor wrong. Cross curriculum shape with part sharing in discovery, then freeze a feasible confirmation cell. Every admitted setting needs a positive capable of satisfying its demand. [P2, L31-91][P2]; [P3, L30-46][P3]; [RP, /probes/curriculum, /probes/strict_c][RP]; [A, L516-521][A]
3. **Make each control capable of changing the verdict for the right reason.** Add reset-only false-promotion fixtures, dedicated U_TRANSFER/rescue/random-donor failures, non-inert dose-matched shams and clean twins. Diagnose identical-arm computations before counting them as separate evidence. Preserve damage/capacity/access matching, but report when a sham improves search rather than treating it as neutral. [C, L396-403, L459-469, L540-546][C]; [A, L406-411, L488-490][A]
4. **Implement and adversarially qualify ASTRA's actual repair.** Freeze the U update law while permitting its declared runtime dynamics, block further V influence for the mediated assay, clamp/swap U across histories, and compare responses under identical S/evidence streams with matched fixed-updater priors. Demand both exporter rejection and legitimate-updater admission. If the distinction cannot be independently qualified, report useful reuse or operational transfer, not a resolved builder-origin claim. [A, L451-502, L595-612][A]
5. **Import Fable artifacts with their ceilings and corrections attached.** Keep original receipts immutable; map superseded CONSTRUCTED labels to deprecated source output, never current verdicts. Retain design/confirmation seed and protocol-version separation. Fresh independent implementation/holdout constructors would be a subsequent authorized qualification step, not something this note claims happened. [C, L26-35, L445-457][C]; [A, L602-612, L653-657][A]
6. **Use two complementary qualification suites.** One asks whether equivalent known capabilities in unlike media receive the expected behavioral answers, including a deliberately biased ruler. The other asks whether each native adapter/observer/reset preserves its declared causal/statistical contract. A pass should list organisms, adapters, effects, noise ranges and unsupported claims. [F2, L194-209, L405-407][F2]; [A, L99-125, L523-540][A]
7. **Report costs and negative results without hiding scope.** Pair exact restricted-class certificates with named-comparator results; separate task observations, host work, inherited capacity, development, tuning and amortized deployment costs. Report verified-hit bounds and detector operating range, not universal incapacity. Do not price STRATEGIST's function-class machinery at zero merely because the receipt counts tasks. [G3, L86-104][G3]; [A, L299-315, L336-355][A]; [F2, L452-477][F2]
8. **Replace percentage compromise with one bounded milestone.** Agree the exact W0/native witnesses and counterfeit-admission question, absolute resource caps, owners, and a stop/demotion condition. Retain tiny unlike-physics pressure before interface freeze; expand to R3, richer R4 or the I/E worksite only for a named unresolved contrast. This preserves the best of both plans without authorizing their combined scope. [F1, L1164-1208, L1325-1337][F1]; [A, L620-657, L700-720][A]

### Gaps

- Whether a clean designed positive can separate improved learner construction from useful fixed-builder reuse remains unresolved. Neither author agreement nor the number of review rounds is evidence that it can.
- The feasibility and total cost of the proposed combined qualification work are unknown. These notes recommend a decision sequence, not a spend authorization or new 90-day commitment.
- The appropriate next validation is an independently checked, prospectively registered implementation of the chosen narrow assay. It was explicitly outside this read-only research task; no experiment or scientific success is claimed here.
