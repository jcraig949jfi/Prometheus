# PROMETHEUS STRATEGIC ROADMAP

## From Sterile Mechanism Tests to Transfer Across Worlds

### Operating thesis

Prometheus is not attempting to build a system that merely solves harder and harder tasks.

It is attempting to discover, falsify, characterize, compose, and transfer **reasoning operations**.

The progression from D14 to games, complex simulations, economics, biology, physics, or any other open domain must therefore never be treated as a conventional benchmark ladder.

It is an **earned epistemic ladder**.

Each larger world is admitted only when the previous class of worlds has become too constrained to meaningfully falsify what Prometheus has learned.

The governing rule is:

> **Complexity is not progress. Complexity is earned only when it creates a stronger falsification test.**

Prometheus may spend 50 experiments at the bottom of this ladder.

It may spend 500,000.

The experiment number is irrelevant.

The gates are not.

---

# I. THE NORTH STAR

The eventual target is a system capable of entering unfamiliar environments and doing something qualitatively broader than task optimization.

Given an environment, Prometheus should eventually be able to:

1. infer what constitutes state;
2. determine what actions are possible;
3. identify observable and hidden variables;
4. infer causal transition structure;
5. identify invariants and boundaries;
6. model possible pasts compatible with the current state;
7. model reachable futures;
8. characterize how actions reshape future possibility;
9. discover failure structure;
10. select or construct reasoning operations appropriate to that geometry;
11. recognize when those operations cease to apply;
12. transfer useful operations between structurally related environments;
13. revise its model when prediction fails;
14. distinguish failure of reasoning from failure of its world model;
15. compress recurring structures across many environments into reusable representations.

That is a distant capability.

Nothing in the current program is permitted to claim it.

The purpose of the roadmap is to construct a chain of falsifiable experiments through which such a capability could eventually be earned.

---

# II. THE CORE DOCTRINE

Prometheus operates under six non-negotiable principles.

## 1. Test before interpretation

No mechanism exists because an agent appears to behave intelligently.

No operation exists because an embedding clusters nicely.

No geometry exists because a visualization looks persuasive.

No transfer exists because performance improved.

Every proposed phenomenon must generate a prediction capable of being wrong.

---

## 2. Build the test before building the claimant

Test-driven development applies to scientific claims as strongly as to software.

Before a candidate mechanism is permitted to compete, define:

* expected behavior;
* negative controls;
* counterfactual intervention;
* failure conditions;
* reproducibility requirements;
* promotion threshold;
* evidence format.

Whenever possible:

> **write the executioner before the organism.**

---

## 3. Failure is evidence, not garbage

Every failed mechanism, transfer attempt, world model, prediction, abstraction, or compression should produce structured residue.

Prometheus should progressively learn not merely:

$$
\text{what works}
$$

but:

$$
\text{where it works}
$$

and:

$$
\text{how it fails}.
$$

The goal is not a library of successful tricks.

It is an empirical map of **operation space, world space, and failure space**.

---

## 4. Promotion is evidence-controlled

No result promotes itself.

No model promotes another model.

No LLM textual assessment constitutes promotion evidence.

Every transition between stages requires deterministic or independently reproducible evidence.

Claims move only when the gate moves them.

---

## 5. World complexity must remain downstream of mechanism clarity

Rich worlds are attractive because they look intelligent.

That makes them dangerous.

Prometheus must resist entering complicated environments until it possesses sufficiently sharp hypotheses that those environments can kill.

D14/D15/D16-style experiments therefore remain intentionally small, sterile, controlled, and difficult to misinterpret.

Games, simulations, economics, and science wait downstream.

---

## 6. Every abstraction remains provisional

Human-created concepts such as:

* planning;
* sacrifice;
* exploration;
* hidden information;
* risk;
* option value;
* reversibility;

may be useful descriptions.

They are not privileged coordinates.

Prometheus should eventually discover whether transfer is better predicted by entirely different mathematical properties.

The ontology must be allowed to change under evidence.

---

# III. THE PROMETHEUS EVIDENCE COURT

Every significant claim enters the same structure.

## CLAIM

State precisely what is being asserted.

Example:

> Operation M increases valid search efficiency in environments possessing structural property X.

Not:

> M appears intelligent.

---

## PREDICTION

The claim must predict an observable outcome not used to construct it.

---

## POSITIVE CONTROL

Demonstrate that the measurement system can detect the phenomenon when it should exist.

---

## NEGATIVE CONTROL

Demonstrate that superficially similar conditions lacking the proposed mechanism do not produce the same result.

---

## COUNTERFACTUAL

Intervene on the suspected mechanism.

Remove it.

Reverse it.

Destroy its inputs.

Preserve everything else possible.

If the effect remains unchanged, the mechanism claim weakens or dies.

---

## REPLICATION

Repeat across seeds, instances, implementations, or independent instruments as appropriate.

---

## SCOPE

Specify where the claim is expected to apply.

---

## FAILURE BOUNDARY

Seek environments immediately outside that scope.

A useful mechanism should eventually possess not only a success region but a measured **failure envelope**.

---

## VERDICT

Every claim ends in one of a small number of states:

* UNTESTED
* TESTABLE
* REFUTED
* REPLICATED
* ABLATION_SUPPORTED
* SCOPE_SUPPORTED
* TRANSFER_SUPPORTED
* COMPOSITION_SUPPORTED

Promotion labels should remain deliberately difficult to obtain.

---

# IV. THE WORLD ENVELOPE

A game, synthetic environment, or later scientific domain can be treated as a bounded causal object.

A useful starting abstraction is:

$$
\mathcal W =
(S,A,P,O,R,T,\Gamma,\Theta)
$$

where:

* \(S\) = state space;
* \(A\) = action space;
* \(P\) = transition dynamics;
* \(O\) = observation structure;
* \(R\) = reward/objective structure;
* \(T\) = terminal/boundary conditions;
* \(\Gamma\) = invariants and symmetries;
* \(\Theta\) = parameters.

This is not asserted to be the final representation.

It is scaffolding.

A **world envelope** is the set of causal possibilities permitted by those rules.

Players may act freely inside the envelope.

They cannot penetrate its physics.

This makes bounded worlds unusually valuable research instruments.

---

# V. THREE GEOMETRIES PROMETHEUS MUST EVENTUALLY CONNECT

The program should progressively develop three empirical spaces.

## WORLD SPACE

A representation of environmental structure.

Possible dimensions may eventually include:

* branching;
* reversibility;
* observability;
* stochasticity;
* causal depth;
* information asymmetry;
* action entropy;
* reward latency;
* failure density;
* strategic curvature;
* state compressibility;
* adversarial adaptation;
* counterfactual accessibility.

---

## OPERATION SPACE

Representations of reasoning transformations.

Rather than naming an operation "planning" or "sacrifice," Prometheus should eventually characterize what it mathematically does to a problem or future cone.

---

## FAILURE SPACE

Where operations break.

Failures may expose dimensions missing from both the world representation and the operation representation.

The long-term scientific target becomes:

$$
\text{World Geometry}
\leftrightarrow
\text{Reasoning Operation}
\leftrightarrow
\text{Failure Geometry}.
$$

---

# VI. ROADMAP STAGE 0 — INSTRUMENT BEFORE DISCOVERY

Before attempting to discover transferable reasoning, Prometheus must prove that its measuring apparatus can distinguish signal from artifacts.

### Required capabilities

* deterministic replay;
* frozen evaluation;
* provenance;
* hash-stable evidence;
* counterfactual intervention;
* negative controls;
* reproducible scoring;
* independent validation where practical;
* preserved failed hypotheses;
* exact experiment configuration.

### Gate G0

Advance only when intentionally injected effects can be reliably detected and intentionally absent effects remain absent.

### Kill condition

If the instrument cannot reliably discriminate signal from noise, scientific claims stop.

Repair the instrument.

Do not increase model sophistication.

---

# VII. STAGE 1 — THE D-SERIES: DISCOVER MECHANISMS IN STERILE WORLDS

This is the current frontier.

D14, D15, D16 and whatever follows belong here.

The worlds should remain intentionally constrained.

The central question is:

> Can Prometheus identify reproducible structure in how variation, failure, search, selection, and reasoning interact?

Do not ask whether the agent looks intelligent.

Ask whether a proposed mechanism exists.

### Required evidence

For each candidate mechanism:

1. formal definition;
2. frozen benchmark;
3. positive control;
4. negative control;
5. ablation;
6. matched counterfactual;
7. compute accounting;
8. independent seeds;
9. failure profile;
10. predictive hypothesis for the next experiment.

### Gate G1 — Mechanism existence

A mechanism may promote only when removing or destroying the proposed cause predictably destroys or materially changes the measured effect.

### Gate G2 — Replication

The result survives independent instances/seeds and is not dependent upon a single artifact.

### Gate G3 — Scope prediction

Before entering a different environment, the mechanism must predict where it should work and where it should fail.

Only then has Prometheus earned transfer testing.

There is no target D-number for leaving this stage.

Prometheus leaves when the evidence requires a larger world.

---

# VIII. STAGE 2 — PARAMETERIZED SYNTHETIC WORLDS

Synthetic worlds are the microscope.

Their purpose is not realism.

Their purpose is causal control.

They permit deliberate manipulation of:

* branching;
* horizon;
* randomness;
* reversibility;
* information;
* reward latency;
* failure visibility;
* opponent adaptation;
* resource constraints.

For a proposed mechanism \(M\), vary one dimension while holding others fixed.

Construct a response surface:

$$
Performance(M)
=
f(\theta_1,\theta_2,\ldots,\theta_n).
$$

The result is not merely whether \(M\) works.

It is an empirical approximation of the **operating envelope of M**.

### Gate G4 — Synthetic scope

Promote only when the mechanism's predicted boundary agrees with observed behavior across controlled perturbations.

### Kill condition

If performance cannot be predicted from the hypothesized structural variables, the scope model is wrong.

Return to mechanism discovery.

Do not explain the discrepancy away.

---

# IX. STAGE 3 — GAME WORLDS AS EXTERNAL TRANSFER ARENA

Only after transferable structure begins surviving synthetic tests should Ludus become scientifically active.

Games represent an important transition:

> the world is complicated and human-designed, but its physics remains knowable.

Prometheus did not construct Chess, poker, Diplomacy, Go, or thousands of other games around its theory.

They therefore serve as partially independent stress tests.

Ludus has already assembled a broad candidate atlas, but its own review correctly labels that output an **unvalidated instrument**: broad catalog coverage exists, while rule auditing, classifier accuracy measurement, bench-ready implementations, and actual transfer measurements remain unfinished.

Until those gaps are closed, Ludus remains infrastructure.

---

# X. LUDUS SIDEQUEST — HARDEN THE WORLD LIBRARY

While the D-series continues independently, Ludus should prepare future transfer environments.

This work must not influence D-series promotion.

Its job is infrastructure.

Priorities:

1. authoritative rule acquisition;
2. rule-version tracking;
3. independent source cross-validation;
4. classifier error measurement;
5. executable world specifications;
6. common world interface;
7. common player interface;
8. deterministic replay;
9. baseline players;
10. strategic-source collection;
11. state/action parameterization;
12. world-query API;
13. anomaly detection;
14. gold-set construction;
15. dormant transfer-panel generation.

Prefer depth over breadth.

One audited, executable, replayable game is more useful than another thousand shallow records.

---

# XI. STAGE 4 — WORLD SIGNATURES

Once enough worlds are executable, begin measuring their geometry.

A world need not have one scalar "formula."

Instead derive candidate signatures.

For example:

$$
\sigma(W)
=
[
H,
B,
D,
\rho,
\kappa,
\lambda,
I,
F,\ldots
]
$$

representing measurements such as:

* entropy;
* branching;
* effective depth;
* reversibility;
* curvature;
* spectral properties;
* information structure;
* failure topology.

These coordinates remain hypotheses.

No coordinate earns importance until it predicts something externally measurable.

### Gate G5 — Signature relevance

A proposed geometric variable survives only if it predicts transfer, failure, complexity, or another held-out property better than reasonable baselines.

Pretty geometry is not evidence.

---

# XII. STATES, NOT JUST GAMES

Whole-game signatures may prove too coarse.

The relevant unit may be a **local reasoning environment**.

For a state \(s\), define its reachable future:

$$
\mathcal F(s)
=
\{s' \mid s\leadsto s'\}.
$$

A move changes that future cone:

$$
a :
\mathcal F(s_t)
\rightarrow
\mathcal F(s_{t+1}).
$$

Moves can therefore be studied by how they reshape possibility:

* eliminate branches;
* preserve optionality;
* increase information;
* cross irreversible boundaries;
* enter traps;
* create bottlenecks;
* open new basins;
* change opponent possibilities.

This permits geometric comparison of states from entirely different games.

A Chess state may eventually resemble a poker state more strongly than another Chess state along the dimensions relevant to a particular reasoning operation.

That is an empirical question.

---

# XIII. FUTURE INFERENCE, PAST INFERENCE, COUNTERFACTUAL INFERENCE

For every sufficiently formalized world, three complementary problems emerge.

## Forward inference

Given \(s_t\):

$$
P(s_{t+k}\mid s_t)
$$

What futures are reachable, likely, valuable, catastrophic, or irreversible?

---

## Reverse inference

Given \(s_t\):

$$
P(s_{t-k}\mid s_t)
$$

What histories are compatible with the observed state?

Can previous actions, hidden choices, strategic intent, or latent variables be inferred?

---

## Counterfactual inference

Given action \(a\):

$$
\Delta\mathcal F
=
\mathcal F(s_{t+1})-\mathcal F(s_t).
$$

What did the move change about possibility itself?

The quality of an action may eventually be represented by a vector rather than one value:

$$
M(a)
=
[
\Delta V,
\Delta H,
\Delta R,
\Delta B,
\Delta \kappa,
\Delta I,
\Delta F,\ldots
].
$$

Again, these are candidate measurements.

Transfer data decides whether they matter.

---

# XIV. STAGE 5 — CROSS-WORLD TRANSFER TESTS

When a mechanism has earned transfer testing, Ludus constructs deliberately adversarial panels.

## Near transfer

Preserve relevant structure while changing semantics.

Question:

> Did the mechanism learn the causal property or the wallpaper?

---

## Axis transfer

Change exactly one proposed prerequisite.

Question:

> Does the mechanism fail where its theory predicts?

---

## Genre decoy

Preserve surface similarity while removing the claimed causal structure.

---

## Structural analog

Preserve causal structure while making the world appear unrelated.

---

## Far transfer

Large overall structural distance while preserving the specific hypothesized prerequisite.

---

## Negative transfer

Deliberately select a world in which the mechanism should fail.

Failure is success for the experiment if predicted correctly.

---

## Compositional transfer

Construct environments requiring multiple independently supported operations.

Test whether they:

* compose;
* interfere;
* dominate;
* collapse;
* produce a new effect.

### Gate G6 — Transfer

"Transfer" requires held-out worlds chosen from a prediction made before execution.

Success after seeing the results is adaptation, not transfer evidence.

---

# XV. STAGE 6 — BUILD THE EMPIRICAL WORLD TAPESTRY

Once thousands or millions of states can be measured across many worlds, stop thinking primarily in terms of game titles.

Construct a large empirical corpus of local geometries.

Conceptually:

$$
\mathcal T[
world,\,
state,\,
action,\,
future,\,
history,\,
information,\,
failure,\,
value
].
$$

This tensor cannot realistically be materialized exhaustively.

That is precisely the research opportunity.

Ask:

> What structure permits enormous world spaces to be compressed?

Candidate methods may include:

* tensor trains;
* tensor networks;
* sparse decompositions;
* low-rank factorization;
* spectral representations;
* hierarchical models;
* learned latent operators.

Compression is not automatically intelligence.

The falsifiable question is whether compressed representations retain predictive or transferable structure.

### Gate G7 — Compression utility

A compressed representation earns promotion only if it predicts held-out states, futures, failures, or cross-world transfer better than simpler baselines.

Compression ratio alone is not evidence of reasoning structure.

---

# XVI. STAGE 7 — DISCOVER REASONING OPERATORS

The strongest possible result from cross-world compression would be recurring transformations rather than recurring named strategies.

Actions themselves may become operators:

$$
\Phi_a :
\mathcal F(s)
\rightarrow
\mathcal F(s').
$$

Operations from very different worlds might exhibit similar mathematical effects.

A Chess sacrifice, poker bluff, resource expenditure, or synthetic control action could potentially share latent structure.

Prometheus should not begin by labeling this "sacrifice."

It should discover some recurring operator \(Q_k\).

Only afterward may humans recognize semantic analogies.

### Gate G8 — Operator recurrence

A proposed reasoning operator must:

1. emerge independently in multiple environments;
2. make measurable predictions;
3. survive ablation;
4. transfer to held-out environments;
5. possess a measurable failure boundary.

Only then should it become part of the Prometheus operation library.

---

# XVII. STAGE 8 — UNKNOWN-RULE GAMES

This is the critical bridge between game playing and science.

Up to this point the learner may know the rules.

Now hide them.

Retain the rules only for the Court.

Give Prometheus:

* observations;
* legal interaction channels;
* histories;
* outcomes.

Ask it to infer:

* state;
* action semantics;
* transition rules;
* invariants;
* hidden variables;
* terminal conditions;
* opponent capabilities.

Games become miniature scientific universes.

Prometheus does not know the physics.

The evaluator does.

This allows world-model discovery to be tested exactly.

### Gate G9 — World inference

Promote only when inferred rules make accurate novel predictions under held-out interventions.

Descriptive reconstruction is insufficient.

---

# XVIII. STAGE 9 — DISTINGUISH BAD REASONING FROM BAD WORLD MODELS

In known games, failure can usually be attributed to decision quality.

In unknown worlds, two major possibilities emerge:

$$
\boxed{\text{Reasoning failure}}
$$

versus

$$
\boxed{\text{World-model failure}}.
$$

Prometheus must eventually determine which occurred.

This demands experiments that separately perturb:

* reasoning operation;
* state representation;
* inferred transition model;
* observation model.

### Gate G10 — Error attribution

Promote only when the system can correctly predict whether improving the world model or improving the reasoning operation should repair performance.

---

# XIX. STAGE 10 — SEMI-OPEN SIMULATED SYSTEMS

Only after world inference succeeds in bounded universes should Prometheus enter worlds whose rules are less complete.

Examples may eventually include:

* agent-based economies;
* ecosystems;
* complex logistical systems;
* chaotic dynamical simulations;
* synthetic social systems;
* scientific simulators.

These introduce:

* uncertain parameters;
* latent variables;
* nonstationarity;
* noisy measurement;
* emergent behavior.

But the simulator still retains an answer key.

### Gate G11

Prometheus must make prospective predictions about interventions not used during model construction.

Retrospective explanation does not count.

---

# XX. STAGE 11 — OPEN EMPIRICAL DOMAINS

Economics, biology, politics, physics, and other real-world systems belong here.

They are not "harder games."

They differ epistemically.

The true state may be unknowable.

The transition model may be incomplete.

Important variables may remain unmeasured.

Agents may react to predictions.

Measurements may alter behavior.

Multiple models may explain existing observations.

Causal identification may be impossible under available evidence.

Therefore the Court must become stricter, not looser.

Claims should narrow as environmental uncertainty grows.

Prometheus must quantify what it does not know.

---

# XXI. THE GRADIENT OF EPISTEMIC PRIVILEGE

The roadmap can be understood as progressively removing knowledge from the learner while preserving as much evaluability as possible.

### D-series

**World known.**

Discover reasoning.

---

### Synthetic worlds

**World known and manipulable.**

Discover scope.

---

### Games

**World known but externally designed.**

Test transfer.

---

### Hidden-rule games

**World unknown to learner, known to Court.**

Test world inference.

---

### Complex simulations

**World partially observable, generator known to Court.**

Test uncertain model discovery.

---

### Empirical science

**World incompletely known to everyone.**

Reason under genuine epistemic uncertainty.

The final transition is profound:

> eventually there is no answer key.

Prometheus does not earn that world until it has demonstrated that it can behave responsibly when answer keys still exist.

* intervention definition;
* baseline;

# XXIII. FAIL-CLOSED RULES

The following conditions automatically block promotion.

### Missing baseline

BLOCK.

### Missing negative control

BLOCK.

### Non-reproducible result

BLOCK.

### Evaluation changed after observing results

BLOCK.

### Claim depends on an LLM judge

BLOCK.

### Counterfactual cannot distinguish proposed mechanism

BLOCK or downgrade claim.

### Result depends on one seed or one world

NO GENERALIZATION CLAIM.

### Structural variable selected after seeing transfer results

NO PROSPECTIVE TRANSFER CLAIM.

### World implementation not independently verified

NO WORLD-LEVEL SCIENTIFIC CLAIM.

### Compression lacks held-out predictive value

NO REPRESENTATIONAL CLAIM.

### Mechanism works but predicted failure boundary does not

SCOPE CLAIM REFUTED.

### More complexity is proposed because the existing benchmark seems boring

DENIED.

# XXIV. ROLLBACK IS NORMAL

Prometheus must be designed for epistemic rollback.

A promoted claim may later fail.

That does not require protecting the historical narrative.

Record:

$$
PROMOTED
\rightarrow
CHALLENGED
\rightarrow
REFUTED
$$

without deleting the history.

A healthy scientific engine should accumulate tombstones.

If nothing dies, the Court is probably weak.

# XXV. PARALLELIZATION POLICY

Prometheus should parallelize aggressively where work does not weaken causal discipline.

Good parallel work includes:

* Ludus hardening game worlds;
* rulebook acquisition;
* simulator construction;
* external-source validation;
* world geometry instrumentation;
* baseline-player development;
* replay infrastructure;
* strategy hypothesis ingestion;
* tensor/compression prototypes;
* synthetic-world generators;
* failure-ledger tooling.

These can proceed while D14/D15/Dn remains deliberately narrow.

The rule is:

> **Parallelize infrastructure, not epistemic shortcuts.**

A future capability may be prepared years before it is scientifically authorized for use.

That is desirable.

# XXVII. THE ULTIMATE TEST

The eventual demonstration should not be:

> Prometheus mastered Chess.

Nor:

> Prometheus achieved superhuman game scores.

Specialized systems already do this.

A substantially stronger demonstration would be:

> Prometheus enters multiple previously unseen worlds, reconstructs their relevant causal geometry, identifies which reasoning operations from previous environments apply, rejects operations whose failure boundaries intersect the new environment, constructs or adapts missing operations, and reaches competence using dramatically less environment-specific search than independent learning would require.

Stronger still:

> The same discovered operation transfers between worlds humans consider unrelated, because Prometheus identified a shared mathematical structure before the transfer experiment was run.

That would begin to justify the claim that the system had discovered something about reasoning rather than merely games.

---

# XXVIII. WHY BEGIN WITH D14?

Because the road is long enough that the first steps must be extraordinarily trustworthy.

D14 does not need to resemble general intelligence.

D15 does not need to be impressive.

D50 does not need to play Chess.

D500,000 does not need to satisfy a roadmap written years earlier.

Each experiment has one responsibility:

> make one uncertainty smaller without manufacturing certainty elsewhere.

If a mechanism cannot survive a tiny deterministic world, it has no business entering Chess.

If it cannot survive Chess, it has no business modeling an economy.

If it cannot infer the physics of a world whose physics we secretly know, it has no business claiming discovery about a world whose physics nobody knows.

The path therefore remains:

$$
\boxed{
\text{small}
\rightarrow
\text{causal}
\rightarrow
\text{reproducible}
\rightarrow
\text{scoped}
\rightarrow
\text{transferable}
\rightarrow
\text{composable}
\rightarrow
\text{world-inferential}
\rightarrow
\text{open}
}
$$

with a gate between every arrow.

Prometheus advances only when reality opens the gate.

Never because we want the story to continue.
