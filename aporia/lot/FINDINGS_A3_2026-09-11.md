## R3 -- EXECUTION, reported beside R2 as required

Same 9 episodes: median REIFIED/FLAT late C_execution ratio 0.358, CI
[0.287, 0.999]. PREDICTION P3 FAILED on this number: I predicted 0.8-1.3
(the macro is charged its whole expansion, so I expected execution to
give back most of the search gain). It did not, because C_execution at
discovery is accumulated over every candidate expanded BEFORE the target
appears, and a macro that moves the target a layer earlier removes
thousands of P-cost expansions while adding one 2P expansion per use. The
identity C_execution = 6 C_search held on every FLAT task (X2); the
REIFIED arm breaks it in the same direction on both costs. That is a fact
about this solver's cost accounting worth carrying: "charged at the
expanded rate" does not mean "execution gets more expensive".

## What this attempt establishes, and what it does not

## Self-identified weaknesses

- The MEMO control is mis-built (above); criterion 5 is untested.
- R4's PASS branch was unreachable at the eligible count the reading
  produced; the preregistration fixed a p-threshold without an
  eligibility floor.
- Three seeds with per-seed REUSE mint counts 6/2/1 is a small and uneven
  sample of the mechanism's recall; the 0.25 has a wide interval that I
  have not computed because the rule does not use it.
- The ORACLE ceiling on the reading seeds (0.634) is worse than on the
  calibration seed (0.489); the R2 bar (0.744) was derived from the more
  favourable number. Had R2 been eligible, the bar would have been
  slightly easier than a bar derived on the reading seeds. Direction of
  this confound: toward PASS. It did not matter here.
- Execution-at-discovery is a per-task quantity; the total execution of
  the whole closure (which the macro doubles: 138,131 vs 69,738
  candidates) is a different number and is not what R3 reports. The
  per-arm closure_candidates column in the result file carries it.
