## 0. The reduced hypothesis under test (amendment s2, verbatim)

A reasoning system can discover a useful composition, promote it to a
first-class executable operand, and thereby alter the topology/cost of
subsequent computation in ways not reproduced by merely retaining the same
primitive semantics.

A3 tests the two halves separately and never merges them:

    SELECTION   does the mechanism mint when reuse is real and refrain
                when it is not, using EARLY tasks only?
    BENEFIT     does a minted operand reduce SEARCH cost on genuinely
                UNSEEN later tasks, once execution cost is charged at the
                expanded rate and the gain is compared against random,
                frequency-matched and memoised controls?

## 2. Solver, cost model, promotion mechanism -- frozen here

SOLVER. One procedure for every arm: world3.build_closure over a primitive
LIST L, max_size 6, max_candidates 600_000, with a witness expression
retained per signature (first found) -- the only addition to A2's solver,
and it changes no cost. A task's C_search under L is order[(V, sig)];
C_execution under L is the sum over the expansions made before discovery of
(expansion_size(prim) * P), where expansion_size is 1 for a world primitive
and k for a macro whose body has k primitive applications. For the flat
arm this reproduces C_execution = P * C_search exactly (fixture X2). A
macro therefore costs ONE search decision and its WHOLE expansion in
execution, which is the phenomenon (amendment s5) and is never merged.

MACRO. A macro is a unary V -> V operator whose body is a size-2 expression
over the world primitives with the task input as its free variable (the
same shape world3.sample_motif draws, MOTIF_COST = 2). It is appended LAST
in the primitive list. Names carry no semantics (world3 docstring).

PROMOTION MECHANISM (the thing under test). Input: the 12 EARLY tasks of an
episode and nothing else. Solve them under the flat list; take each task's
first-found minimal witness; enumerate its size-2 V-typed subterms; key
each by its SIGNATURE over the probe set (not by syntax); count the number
of early tasks whose witness contains each signature at least once. Let
c_max be the largest count and s_max its signature. MINT the witness
expression of s_max as a macro iff c_max >= THETA, THETA = 6 (half the
early tasks). Otherwise mint nothing. The mechanism never sees a late task,
a class label, declared_shared, or shared_motif (fixture X4).

Why signatures, not syntax: two syntactically different size-2 chunks with
the same extensional behaviour are the same operand; counting syntax would
under-count reuse and counting signatures cannot over-count it.

Why THETA = 6, fixed now: in NO_REUSE every early motif is an independent
draw among at most 25 size-2 signatures, so c_max is the maximum bin count
of 12 balls in <= 25 bins -- expected about 2-3, rarely 4 (fixture X1b
measures it on the calibration seed and reports the empirical P(c_max >= 6)).
In REUSE every early task contains the shared motif, so c_max = 12 IF the
witness retains it; the whole question of whether first-found minimal
witnesses retain the planted motif is left to the mechanism -- that is the
test, and ORACLE (below) is the ceiling that tells us what was there to
find. In LATE_REUSE the early shared count is 3 by construction, so c_max
is expected below THETA and no mint is expected (F4).

## 3. Arms (every arm solves the same 24 tasks; late = tasks 13-24)

    FLAT     L = PRIMS. The baseline. Early and late under PRIMS.
    REIFIED  early under PRIMS; the mechanism runs; late under PRIMS + [M]
             if minted, else identical to FLAT (recorded as NO_MINT).
    ORACLE   late under PRIMS + [shared_motif of the episode], regardless
             of the mechanism. The CEILING and the CHEAT CONTROL: if this
             arm does not reduce late C_search on REUSE episodes, the
             channel cannot see what A3 claims to measure, and the whole
             rung is INSTRUMENT_INVALID (section 5, X3).
    CONTROL  PRIMS + [shared_motif] from task 1 (the amendment's
             "supplied from the beginning"). On LATE tasks it coincides
             with ORACLE by construction; its distinct reading is the
             EARLY-phase cost, reported and not gated. Nobody may quote
             the CONTROL-vs-ORACLE late coincidence as a finding.
    RANDOM   late under PRIMS + [R], R a size-2 macro drawn uniformly from
             the constructible size-2 expressions with a fresh stream
             (seed * 7919 + episode index), excluding s_max's signature.
             Kill criterion 3, arbitrary chunk.
    FREQ     late under PRIMS + [F], F the size-2 macro whose GLOBAL
             occurrence frequency (as a subterm signature across 2,000
             random size-6 programs drawn once from the calibration seed,
             fixed before any reading) is nearest to that of the minted
             (or, if nothing was minted, the oracle) macro, excluding the
             same signature. Kill criterion 3, frequency-matched chunk.
    MEMO     late under PRIMS with the 12 early tasks' solved signatures
             seeded into layer 0 as zero-cost leaves (a search cache of
             everything early solved, composable but not an operator over
             arguments). Kill criterion 5. If MEMO reproduces REIFIED's
             gain, operandization has earned nothing beyond memoisation.

Every arm reports, per episode, over the 12 late tasks: median C_search,
median C_execution, solve count (must be 12; a miss is a dropped record and
is counted, never silently excluded), and for REIFIED the mint decision,
c_max, s_max, and whether s_max equals the shared_motif's signature.

## 4. Preregistered rules, each with attainable range, eligible count,
##    and an INDETERMINATE branch

Unit of analysis: the EPISODE (12 late tasks share a motif and are not
independent). Reading seeds: 20260912, 20260913, 20260914; 12 episodes per
class per seed; eligible count per class = 36 episodes. Calibration seed:
20260911 (never read for a verdict).

R1  SELECTION (kill criterion 4). mint_rate(class) = minted episodes / 36.
    Range [0, 1]. PASS: mint_rate(REUSE) >= 0.75 AND mint_rate(NO_REUSE)
    <= 0.25. FAIL: mint_rate(REUSE) <= 0.50 OR mint_rate(NO_REUSE) >= 0.50.
    INDETERMINATE otherwise. Reported beside it, ungated: mint_rate for
    DECOY_REUSE (expected HIGH -- early evidence is real by construction;
    a high rate here is CORRECT selection, the cost lands in R4) and
    LATE_REUSE (expected LOW, F4). Also: among REUSE mints, the fraction
    where s_max equals the shared motif's signature (range [0,1]; this is
    the mechanism's precision and is reported, not gated).

R2  BENEFIT, SEARCH (kill criterion 2). On REUSE episodes where REIFIED
    minted: ratio_i = median late C_search(REIFIED) / median late
    C_search(FLAT), i over minted episodes; statistic = median of ratio_i;
    CI by bootstrap over episodes (2,000 resamples). Range (0, inf).
    BAR: derived on the CALIBRATION seed as bar = 1 - 0.5 * (1 -
    median ORACLE/FLAT ratio on calibration REUSE episodes), i.e. the
    mechanism must recover at least half of the oracle's search gain;
    the number is written into RESULT_A3_CALIBRATION.json before any
    reading seed is drawn. PASS: CI upper bound < 1.0 AND point <= bar.
    FAIL: CI lower bound >= 1.0 (no reduction) OR point > 1.0.
    INDETERMINATE: fewer than 18 minted REUSE episodes (eligible count
    below half), or CI straddles the bar.

R3  EXECUTION HONESTY (amendment s5). Same episodes: median of
    median late C_execution(REIFIED) / median late C_execution(FLAT).
    Range (0, inf). Not gated; REPORTED beside R2 always. Expected shape:
    the execution ratio exceeds the search ratio because every macro
    application is charged its expansion. A search gain reported without
    this number beside it is inadmissible.

R4  CONTROLS (kill criteria 3 and 5). On the same minted REUSE episodes,
    paired by episode: REIFIED late C_search vs RANDOM, vs FREQ, vs MEMO.
    Statistic per control: fraction of episodes where REIFIED < control
    (range [0,1]; chance 0.5), with a two-sided exact binomial p.
    PASS (per control): fraction >= 0.75 and p < 0.01. FAIL: fraction
    <= 0.50. INDETERMINATE otherwise. Criterion 3 fires if RANDOM or FREQ
    is not beaten; criterion 5 fires if MEMO is not beaten. All three must
    PASS for "reification earns its keep"; one FAIL is a kill of the
    reduced hypothesis on this world, reported as such.

R5  THE COST OF A WRONG MINT (reported, not gated). On DECOY_REUSE
    episodes where REIFIED minted: the R2 and R3 statistics. Expected:
    search ratio >= 1.0 (the macro widens the search and is not reused),
    execution ratio > 1.0. This is the price of selection under decoy
    evidence and the reason R1 does not gate DECOY.

R6  NOTHING FIRED vs NOTHING COULD HAVE FIRED. Every rule reports both its
    fired count and its eligible count. A rule whose eligible count is 0
    reads INDETERMINATE, never PASS.

## 5. Fixtures -- every FAIL-capable component asserts FAIL on a fixture,
##    every PASS-capable component asserts PASS, and one cheat per channel
##    (amendment s3; base rule 3). Run BEFORE any reading seed.

    X1a  promotion, known-positive: an episode whose 12 early witnesses
         are CONSTRUCTED to contain one planted size-2 signature -> MINT,
         s_max == planted. Asserts the mechanism CAN mint.
    X1b  promotion, known-negative: 200 NO_REUSE episodes on the
         calibration seed -> empirical P(c_max >= THETA) reported; the
         fixture asserts it is < 0.10, or THETA is wrong and the rung
         stops before reading (the bar is checked against its null BEFORE
         the reading, F6).
    X1c  promotion, hindsight guard: replace the 12 LATE tasks of an
         episode with tasks from another class; assert the mint decision
         and s_max are bit-identical. Asserts the mechanism cannot see
         late tasks.
    X2   cost model: under PRIMS, assert C_execution == P * C_search on
         every task of one episode; under PRIMS + [M] with a size-2 M,
         assert every expansion of M is charged 2 * P and one search
         decision. Asserts the two costs are separately instrumented.
    X3   channel cheat (ORACLE): on calibration REUSE episodes assert
         median ORACLE/FLAT late C_search ratio < 1.0 with bootstrap CI
         upper < 1.0. If this fails the rung is INSTRUMENT_INVALID and no
         R2 reading is taken.
    X4   information guard: assert the mechanism's inputs are exactly the
         12 early task signatures and the probe set (no class, no
         declared_shared, no shared_motif) by calling it on an episode
         with those fields deleted.
    X5   CONTROL identity: assert the CONTROL arm's primitive list equals
         PRIMS + [shared_motif] and differs from the REIFIED arm's list in
         no other way (F3).
    X6   gate reachability, R2: a fixture where REIFIED == FLAT (NO_MINT
         everywhere) must read INDETERMINATE (eligible 0), never PASS; a
         fixture where REIFIED == ORACLE must read PASS against the
         calibration bar. Asserts R2 can fire and can refuse.
    X7   gate reachability, R4: a fixture where the control arm is a copy
         of REIFIED must read fraction 0.0 -> FAIL; a fixture where the
         control is FLAT while REIFIED == ORACLE on REUSE must read PASS.
    X8   dropped records: a fixture that makes one late task unsolvable
         under the budget must be COUNTED as dropped in the arm's record
         and must not change the other 11 tasks' medians silently.

## 6. Predictions, written before any code runs (scored in the findings)

P1  X3 holds: ORACLE reduces REUSE late C_search; predicted median ratio
    between 0.2 and 0.6 (a size-6 program becomes size 5 under the macro
    and the closure discovers it a layer earlier; widening by one unary
    primitive costs less than a layer).
P2  R1 PASSES, with mint_rate(REUSE) >= 0.75 -- the risky half of this
    prediction is whether first-found minimal witnesses retain the planted
    motif often enough; if mint_rate(REUSE) lands in [0.5, 0.75) the
    mechanism, not the phenomenon, is the weak link and the findings say
    so.
P3  R2 PASSES on minted episodes, R3 execution ratio between 0.8 and 1.3.
P4  R4: RANDOM and FREQ beaten (>= 0.75); MEMO is the contrast most likely
    to be close -- predicted fraction 0.6-0.8, i.e. the one control with a
    real chance of an INDETERMINATE. If MEMO is NOT beaten, criterion 5
    fires and the mutable-language interpretation has earned nothing on
    this world; that outcome is reported at the same volume as a pass.
P5  R5: DECOY search ratio >= 1.0 in the majority of minted DECOY
    episodes.
P6  mint_rate(LATE_REUSE) <= 0.35.

## 7. What A3 cannot claim, whatever it reads

- Nothing about Level 2. A macro over the existing closure is definable in
  it by construction.
- Nothing about transplantation across domains (a later experiment).
- Nothing about the mechanism's generality: THETA and the size-2 macro
  shape are fixed for this world; a PASS is a PASS on TINYPROG at size 6.
- Nothing from a single seed or from task-level counts.
