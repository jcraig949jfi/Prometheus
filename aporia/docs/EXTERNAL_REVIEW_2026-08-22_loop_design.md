## 3. The diagnosis was wrong — ACCEPTED, with the correction adopted as primary

My claim: *"every instrument built is a verification instrument, and a verification instrument

The real error, stated better than I stated it:

> **Prometheus has built a strong adjudicator but has not yet demonstrated a search policy
> whose proposal distribution improves from adjudication.**

And the experiment I ran (choose something calibratable → measure it → observe mostly known

distribution contained no opportunities*. H2 is the likelier account.

**The architectural finding underneath, which I had not seen at all:** artifacts accumulate,
knowledge accumulates — but the *policy that decides where to look next* does not learn. The

are assigned at thread-mint time and no outcome has ever changed a proposal distribution in 105
passes. That is a genuine defect and it outranks the target question.

**Applied:** the diagnosis of record is replaced. See the loop-doctrine changes in §6.

## 4. Round-robin — REJECTED in favor of checkpoint-bounded microcampaigns

Fixed A→B→C→D→E rotation breaks the conditional state that makes pass *n+1* valuable because
of pass *n*'s specific result, and pays setup cost every switch.

**Adopted rule:** at most **two live threads**; stay on one for up to **three consecutive
passes** or until a predefined decision checkpoint, whichever comes first; then reallocate.
Shape: pass 1 instrument/preregistration, pass 2 primary experiment, pass 3 falsification or
replication or mechanism chase. Each campaign must then emit exactly one of
**ADVANCE / REDESIGN / PARK / KILL** — and there is deliberately **no fifth state** called
"interesting, continue exploring."

## 5. Abandonment criteria — ADOPTED (this omission was a real defect)

Every new thread gets a three-pass allowance. It continues past its checkpoint only if at
least one of these occurred:

1. a preregistered branch was discriminated;
2. the plausible hypothesis space materially narrowed;
3. a reusable capability was produced **and already has a named downstream consumer**;
4. an anomaly survived a stronger null than before;
5. the result changed what experiment should run next.

None → PARK. None again at a second checkpoint → KILL. "Built useful infrastructure" does not
qualify without a waiting consumer — that loophole is exactly how this loop produced 30
paradigm trees with no ingestion path.

**Instant-kill conditions:** positive control fails; a blind test leaks labels; the null cannot
distinguish the hypothesized effect at useful power; a required dictionary does not exist and
building it would itself be a research program; the result is invariant under a control that
should destroy it.

A killed thread is a **successful terminal state**, not a failure.

## 6. Instrumentation of the loop itself — ADOPTED

- **Decision yield (DY₂₀):** fraction of the last 20 passes that caused an ADVANCE, DEMOTE,
  KILL, or material BRANCH. Not commits, not files, not tests — research decisions.
- **Open-thread debt:** threads started but lacking a terminal checkpoint.
- **Restart tax:** fraction of a pass spent reconstructing state rather than advancing it.
- **Failure signature for over-interleaving:** open-thread debt rising while decision yield
  falls. "5 active threads + 12 commits + 0 kills/promotions/branch changes" = manufacturing
  motion.

## 7. The target — ADOPTED, and it is better than anything on my menu

**Blinded relation recovery → Sleeping Beauties.** Before touching the 68,770 unreferenced
sequences, build a benchmark where the machine cannot know which relationships were hidden:
take OEIS relations that are already known and machine-verifiable from terms alone
(differences, partial sums, binomial transform, Möbius transform, subsequence, shift, Dirichlet
convolution where terms permit), find real corpus pairs satisfying them exactly, strip the
relationship information, and ask whether behavioral signatures can retrieve the counterpart
from hundreds of thousands of candidates. Development/frozen split *before* development.

Why this beats the original menu: it tests **search**, which is the untested half, rather than
measurement, which is the competent half. It has ground truth. Failure is informative. And it
does not ask the agent to have a brilliant idea — it asks it to do exhaustive census with
matched controls and nulls, which is empirically what it is good at.

The falsifier is brutal and correct:

> **If the signature system cannot recover deliberately hidden relationships that are already
> known, stop calling it a discovery substrate.**

The target class I had been missing, named: **large finite search spaces containing concealed
structure with partially available ground truth** — between settled mathematics and open
mathematics.

## 9. Reviewer backlog — ACCEPTED with a new rule

Being 42 passes behind is not why there is no new mathematics, but it lets the agent build an
entire local research culture before a human can say the branch is nonsense. Do not slow
execution to match the reviewer. Instead: **unreviewed results may trigger replication,
stronger nulls, and mechanism investigation — but may NOT spawn descendants, new paradigms,
ontology changes, or major backlog reprioritization.** Speculative execution: compute ahead,
do not irrevocably commit architectural state.
