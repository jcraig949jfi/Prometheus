## Why the design packet lives here and not inside H1

`DESIGN_astra_v0.1_H0-H5.md` is Astra's build contract for all six hypotheses,
plus the shared contracts C1 to C6 (artifact loading, typed objects, adaptive
execution, cost accounting, evidence, analysis). Filing it under H1 would
misplace it. H1's own sections are quoted where they matter in
`H1/design/ARMS.md`.

## Standing caution for anyone reading these

Three of the four external documents in H1 disagree with each other on
substantive points, and at least one claim endorsed by a reviewer is judged
incorrect by the designer. Each directory's `README.md` carries the
adjudication. Read that first; do not cite a single document as the position.

Citation status is tracked per hypothesis in `CITATIONS.md`. Most citations in
the H1 material are UNVERIFIED. Two campaign citations were fetch-verified and
one of those corrected a sample size the reviewing report had overstated by
more than an order of magnitude.
