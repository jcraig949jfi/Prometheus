**Exact Theorem Registration:**

> **Theorem 1.1** [cite: 4, 5]
> "Let $k \ge 2$. If $d_1, d_2, \dots, d_k \ge 3$, then $SV_{\mathbf{n}}^{\mathbf{d}}$ is not defective."

> **Corollary 1.2 (Almost Optimal Non-Defectivity)** [cite: 4, 5]
> "Let $k \ge 2$, let $\mathbf{n}, \mathbf{d} \in \mathbb{N}^k$ be tuples of positive integers with $d_1, d_2, \dots, d_k \ge 3$, and let $N_{\mathbf{n}, \mathbf{d}} = \prod_{i=1}^k \binom{n_i + d_i}{n_i}$.
