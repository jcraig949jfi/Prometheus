1. **Positive controls** — claims known to be true and structurally real

## Substrate's reading (Techne)

### Doctrinal pass-through

GPT-5's framing of "positive-control / negative-control / decoy
calibration suite" is **high training-corpus gravity** — it's standard
empirical-science instrument-calibration language. Per
`feedback_llm_convergence_is_gravity_amplifier`, this is a warning
signal that the framing matches the LLM's corpus.

**But the warning fires correctly here AND the critique is load-bearing.**
The substrate IS at the calibration cliff; the framing is conventional
because it's the right framing for "is the instrument calibrated?" —
not because the LLM defaulted to it.

### Substantive (non-corpus-gravity) additions worth keeping

1. **MDL reframe** of kill patterns — operational definition the
   doctrine was missing.
2. **Doctrine refinement**: "navigable residue" → "residue with
   measured counterfactual utility." A label is not residue.
3. **"Learner could become a model of Theseus's biases"** — names the
   failure mode Q10 was groping toward but didn't quite say.
4. **Matched-null permutation assay** is concretely operational and
   the substrate had not designed it.

### Where the substrate sees gaps in the critique

- The critique implicitly trusts the existing promotion filter. The
  substrate knows the filter is biased (training_weight info-content
  fix was the most recent of multiple corrections). If the controls
  reveal that positive-control planted relations DON'T promote, the
  conclusion is NOT "promote more" — that loops back to artifact
  inflation. The control-corpus design must distinguish "filter
  rejects real signal" from "no real signal in claim ecology" — those
  warrant opposite responses.
- Same-domain EC × EC + modular-forms × EC are very LMFDB/Langlands-
  priors-shaped recommendations. They're probably correct but should
  be cross-checked: if Theseus rediscovers modularity-adjacent
  patterns, is that signal-existence or training-corpus-shadow
  echoing through LMFDB's tabular conventions?
- The "rediscovery benchmark" framing assumes the substrate knows
  WHICH known relations to plant. The substrate's hard posture
  per `feedback_anti_gravitational_well` is that traditional
  mathematics is exhausted and Prometheus is the deliberately-
  different bet. Planting known relations risks training the
  substrate to find what it's planted, which is the conventional
  ML-evaluation antipattern.

### Net actionable change

The revised plan adopts GPT-5's structure but with two doctrinal
guardrails:

- **Control corpus must be built BEFORE N1** (agreed; this is the
  highest-priority change).
- **Promotion filter is NOT trusted as ground truth** in calibration;
  if controls fail in the "filter rejects real signal" direction, the
  diagnosis is generator-design failure or claim-language failure,
  NOT filter relaxation.
- **Rediscovery benchmark uses CONSENSUS known-true relations only**
  (BSD, modularity-as-rank-zero-detection, Atkin-Lehner involutions),
  with SECOND-ORDER verification: the substrate's generators must
  rediscover the relation AND emit at least one promoted record that
  encodes the relation's *generalization shape*, not the relation
  itself.
