## The question, honestly re-operationalized
The thread asked "collision yield vs solo yield from git history." A direct yield-rate
comparison between the May ensemble era and the August solo era is CONFOUNDED beyond
repair, and the confound is structural: collisions only become visible when someone
reconciles instruments, and the reconciliation itself happened in the solo era over
ensemble-era artifacts. Producer and revealer are different eras by construction. So the
measurable object is CONFIRMATION WIDTH: for each registry anchor, how many distinct
evidence layers touch it, and which era produced each layer.

## Method
Marker-based touch extraction over three ledgers (anti_anchors verification/basis fields;
BACKCORPUS_MINING routing notes; CONSUMPTION effects), classing markers as ensemble-era
(lethe/acheron/stygian/hecate hunts, couplet registry, May verification passes,
replicates, moros) vs solo-era (live fetches, model-prior concordance, reconciliation,
DR-report consumption). Coarse proxy, deliberately: it need only rank and detect, not
measure precisely.

- 55 anchors touched; 43 with width >= 2 (multi-instrument); 26 with BOTH-era touches —
  the genuine cross-era collisions.
- Widest anchors: AA-031 (Saxl) 6+3=9, AA-033 (rank/decidability) 4+5=9, AA-045 (Mertens)
  9, AA-046 (sensitivity) 9, AA-032 (Schinzel-Zassenhaus) 8, AA-053 (Zauner) 8. These are

  S-Z 5, rank 4, Mertens 4) — the proxy tracks reality where reality is known.
- Era split of touches: ensemble 76 / solo 111 (share 0.41) — REPORTED BUT NOT ADJUDICATED:
  DR reports were COMMISSIONED by the May ensemble and CONSUMED by the August loop;
  reattributing DR-marker touches flips the share above 0.6. The ratio is
  convention-sensitive; per the pre-stated readings, the era-split question is CONFOUNDED.

## Reading: WIDTH-EXISTS (robust) + CONFOUNDED (era ratio, as pre-stated)
What the ensemble demonstrably bought is REDUNDANCY WIDTH: parallel differently-aimed
instruments (hunts by trap-class, couplets, verification passes, built-in replicate runs)
whose outputs later reconciled with zero contradictions across a 3-month gap. What the
solo loop demonstrably bought is DEPTH: live primary fetches, reconciliation, grade
upgrades. The 26 both-era anchors are the program's strongest-verified objects, and
neither era's instruments alone produce that verification shape. This supports the
germline design point (perspective diversity from objective diversity — the hunters were
differently AIMED, not merely parallel) without supporting any yield-rate claim.

## Scope limit (stated before anyone extrapolates)
This measures verification WIDTH, not capability RUNGS. No evidence here bears on whether
ensembles solve problems solo agents cannot — only that ensemble-era breadth plus solo-era
depth produced the registry's best-verified anchors. The ensemble-rung question proper
needs the R12-class instruments, not git archaeology.
