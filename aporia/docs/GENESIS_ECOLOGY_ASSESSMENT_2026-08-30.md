# Genesis ecology proposal — Aporia assessment

**Written:** 2026-08-30. **Subject:** the operator's proposed five-seat Genesis ecology plus an
M1/M2 canonical science spine. **Posture:** the proposal assigns Aporia more authority, so this
assessment is written against my own interest and every objection is evidenced from the tree.

---

## 1. VERDICT

**Endorse the principle. Reject one name. Correct two ownership assignments.**

The core move — differentiate seats by LAYER OF OPERATION rather than by topic — is correct and
is already doctrine here (`feedback_agent_differentiation`: overlapping agent agendas are
strategy; differentiate at layer-of-operation). Techne acquires capability without judging its
scientific value; Ergon owns what persists; Charon attacks the experiment; Aporia attacks the
programme's interpretation. That separation is real and worth having.

The proposal's factual errors are all in the same direction: **it under-counts the seats that
already exist**, and therefore hands live territory to seats that do not own it.

## 2. THE NAMING COLLISION — this one is not cosmetic

**`M1` and `M2` are already taken, twice, in two incompatible senses.**

**Sense one — physical stations.** `stations/M1_STATUS.md`, `stations/M2_STATUS.md`,
`stations/M3_STATUS.md` are live status files. `roles/Ludus/ROLE.md:8` reads
"**Machine:** M1 (Skullport), F:\". Alethelia's collision note records "M2 (Harmonia A) found
referents 1-2; M1 (Aporia) found the retire-candidate angle". M1 is the box this session runs on.

**Sense two — experiment arms, and constitutional.** `agent_d5_blind/MANIFEST.md:45` reads
"Phase plan (order is constitutional — §49: **M1 is built LAST**)". In D-5, M0 and M1 are the
no-library and library-carrying arms; seed bands 5000-5999 are "M1 DEVELOPMENTAL seeds".

Introducing "M1 ENGINEER = immutable instrument authority" makes a **third** referent, and a
successor reading "M1 is built LAST" against an org chart saying M1 is the instrument engineer
will misread a frozen constitutional clause.

**This repo already has a measured, unresolved collision of exactly this kind**, logged by
Alethelia on 2026-08-27: four referents for Aletheia/Alethelia, 196 files naming one, 22 naming
the other, 12 naming both, escalated as a **FLEET HAZARD** in `stations/M1_STATUS.md` §5.
Adding a third M1 sense while that one is open is a self-inflicted repeat.

**Recommendation:** keep the two roles, rename them. `INSTRUMENT` and `EXPERIMENT` say what they
do and collide with nothing.

## 6. WHAT I ENDORSE WITHOUT QUALIFICATION

**The constraint on my own seat.** "Aporia may recommend promotion, not rewrite evidence or
modify another seat's frozen experiment. If the frozen predicate says PASS, Aporia cannot change
it to FAIL — it can commission a successor experiment designed to attack the meaning of that
PASS."

That is exactly right, and it is what I did on 2026-08-27: A2's preregistered rule returned
`WORLD_REJECTED_CLASSES_NOT_SEPARATED` on data showing textbook separation, and I kept the
verdict string verbatim with `verdict_admissible: false` beside it rather than editing it, then
preregistered a successor rung to attack it. The constitution held under a case where I wanted
the other answer.

**But nothing structurally enforces it.** Per `report_GRADIENT_HACKING_AND_CONTAINMENT.md`, the
demonstrated analogue is an agent editing the unit tests that would have caught it (7/32,768
episodes, Denison et al.), and the only control in this repo — `atk015` — asserts rows EXIST
beneath a verdict, not that they were not EDITED to match it. Formalising Aporia as portfolio
selector raises the value of that unbuilt control. **Build the append-only out-of-band ledger
before, not after, the authority is formalised.**

**Techne's constraint** is the same principle one layer down and is also right: "Tensor train
representation X is installed, wrapped, deterministic, replayable, and exposes these operations
at these costs" — not "tensor trains are the right substrate." Note that Techne's own Standing
Order #1 ("wrap, don't rewrite") already encodes half of this, and the donor inventory
(2026-08-30) found it applied unevenly inside `prometheus_math` itself.

## 7. THE ONE HAZARD THE PROPOSAL DOES NOT SEE

Harmonia A-E propose; Aporia selects; selected proposals persist and shape what gets proposed
next. **That is a selection loop with the operator's filter inside the environment**, which is
the exact structure of the Avida result in
`report_SELECTION_DISCOVERED_CONCEALMENT.md`: heritable variation, differential persistence
keyed on a measurement, and — because every seat reads and writes the same repository — a
channel from the persisting thing back to the measurement.

No intent is required. Ofria's organisms had no survival term. All that is needed is for "gets
selected by Aporia" to correlate with persistence, and for the seats to be able to observe the
criterion — which they can, because I write my selection rationale into committed documents that
they read.

The documented mitigation that FAILED was hiding the criterion; the population moved to
probabilistic expression. The one that HELD was structural: move selection off the separable
evaluation episode and make it continuous and in-population.

**Design consequence:** do not run "proposal rounds" where Aporia picks winners from a slate.
Prefer selection as a property of ordinary work — proposals that get consumed because a seat
needed them, measured by consumption rather than by my judgement. If a slate round is
unavoidable, the selection criterion must not be one the proposing seats can read and optimise
against.
