## 3. Failure Mode: Gap in Proof (Lemma Quietly Assumed)

This failure mode is the most pervasive within the derived category and triangulated space literature. Authors establish a sweeping architecture but inadvertently leap over a critical intermediary lemma, either assuming it is trivially true or misapplying a localized truth to a generalized space.

### Case 3.1: Semiorthogonal Decomposition Lemma Overreach

*   **Authors:** Ari Krishna
*   **Title:** All Quiet on the Exceptional Locus
*   **ArXiv ID:** arXiv:2604.16277
*   **DOI / Status:** arXiv:2604.16277v1 (Original, 2026-04-17); arXiv:2604.16277v2 (Withdrawn, 2026-04-20) [cite: 4, 5].
*   **Failure-Mode Classification:** Gap in Proof (Lemma Quietly Assumed).
*   **Etiology:** The paper investigated admissible subcategories of the bounded derived category $D^b(X)$ of a smooth projective surface supported on the exceptional locus of a birational morphism [cite: 4, 5]. The primary claim was that such subcategories are generated by a finite exceptional collection, concluding that no nonzero phantom subcategories can have proper support [cite: 4]. The entire architecture rested on *"a splitting lemma for admissible subcategories inside a semiorthogonal decomposition with a single exceptional block"* [cite: 4]. Within three days of publication, the paper was withdrawn. The retraction notice explicitly states: *"Errors caught by Dimitrii Pirozhkov and Shengxuan Liu in Lemma 2.1"* [cite: 4]. 
*   **`kill_pattern` Signature (v10-Class Battery):** `sig_d3_semiorthogonal_lemma_fracture`. The v10 battery evaluates triangulated category assumptions by checking the orthogonality conditions of the assumed subcategories. The signature triggers when an exceptional block's boundary is assumed to perfectly partition the derived space without verifying the Ext-group vanishing constraints.
*   **Taxonomy Refinement Signal:** This case perfectly highlights the danger of "load-bearing lemmas." The distinguishing signal is that the *logic* connecting the lemma to the theorem is sound, but the lemma itself is flawed. Refining the taxonomy requires the v10 battery to recursively isolate and stress-test foundational lemmas (like Lemma 2.1) independently of the overarching derived category theorem.

*   **Failure-Mode Classification:** Hypothesis Failure (Counterexample Identified).
*   **Etiology:** This monumental paper sought to define enumerative invariants for Spin(7) instantons and coherent sheaves on Calabi-Yau 4-folds [cite: 10]. *   **Taxonomy Refinement Signal:** This is the gold standard for hypothesis failure. The proof logic functioned under nominal conditions, but the *manifold space* was too broadly defined.
