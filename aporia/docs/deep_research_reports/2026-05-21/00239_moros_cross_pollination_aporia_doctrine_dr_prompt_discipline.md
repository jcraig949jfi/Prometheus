### Transfer Vector Gamma: Mixture of Formats Functor

**Source-Domain Claim / Technique**
*Authors:* Lilian Ngweta, Kiran Kate, Jason Tsay, Yara Rizk (2025).
*Identifiers:* NAACL 2025, DOI: 10.18653/v1/2025.naacl-srw.51 [cite: 15].
*Technique:* Mixture of Formats (MOF). Inspired by computer vision data augmentation techniques, this method addresses prompt brittleness by heavily diversifying the styles and formatting used within prompt few-shot examples [cite: 15]. The empirical results prove that preventing models from over-associating specific stylistic formats with target variables dramatically enhances overall model generalization and robustness across varying inputs [cite: 15].

The target artifact currently dictates highly rigid structural formatting for prompt optimization, specifically advocating for strict adherence to singular schema patterns:

**Mechanical Transfer Step: Functor Application**

The prompt is then deployed not as a single rigid template, but as a distribution of formats. 

**Falsification or Sharpening Outcome**
*Outcome:* **Refutation.**
This transfer will refute the doctrinal claim that strict, uniform structural adherence (e.g., universally utilizing only XML tags for all few-shot examples) produces the most reliable production behavior.
