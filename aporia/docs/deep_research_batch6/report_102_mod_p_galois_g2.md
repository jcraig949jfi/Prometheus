# Deep Research Report #102: Mod-p Galois Image Statistics for Genus-2 Jacobians

**Target agent:** Charon
**Topic:** Serre open-image theorem at scale for g=2 Jacobians
**Date:** 2026-04-23

## 1. Problem Statement

For a non-CM elliptic curve E/Q, Serre (1972) proved the mod-p Galois representation ρ_{E,p}: Gal(Q̄/Q) → GL_2(F_p) is surjective for all but finitely many primes p. The genus-2 analogue replaces GL_2 with GSp_4 (symplectic similitude group, dim 11 over F_p), acting on the 4-dimensional F_p-vector space J[p] of p-torsion of the Jacobian J. Serre's conjecture for g=2 predicts that for J with no extra endomorphisms (geometrically End(J) = Z), ρ_{J,p} is surjective for all but finitely many p.

**Research question:** At LMFDB scale (~66K g2c curves), what fraction of Jacobians have non-surjective mod-p image for p ∈ {3,5,7,11,13}, and does the exceptional-prime distribution match Zywina's classification?

## 2. Literature

- **Serre (1972)** "Propriétés galoisiennes des points d'ordre fini des courbes elliptiques", Invent. Math. 15: open-image theorem.
- **Serre (1981)** Lie-algebra methods: reduction to maximal-subgroup problem in GSp_4(F_p).
- **Dieulefait (2002)** "Explicit determination of Galois images for abelian surfaces with End(A)=Z", Exp. Math. 11: algorithm from Frobenius traces + middle L-polynomial coefficients.
- **Zywina (2015)** classification of maximal proper subgroups of GSp_4(F_p) that arise as images: reducible, stabilizer of quadratic form, normalizer of split/non-split torus, exceptional (finite).
- **Banwait-Cremona-Sutherland (2022)** computational framework via Frobenius data for EC; adaptable.
- **Landesman-Swaminathan-Tao-Xu (2019)** density results for g=2.

## 3. LMFDB Data

Source: `g2c_curves` (~66,158 rows).

- `torsion_subgroup`: rational torsion — non-trivial implies reducible mod-p image.
- `isogeny_class`: constraints from rational isogenies.
- `aplist` / L-polynomial coefficients `(a_p, b_p)` up to bound B ~ 1000.
- `end_alg`, `geom_end_alg`: filter End(J) = Z vs RM/CM/QM.
- `bad_primes`: exclude from Chebotarev scan.

Frobenius at good q ≠ p acts on J[p] with char poly `x^4 − a_q x^3 + b_q x^2 − q a_q x + q^2 (mod p)`. Subgroup generated by Frobenius is the image (Chebotarev).

## 4. Test Design

For each J with End(J)=Z (~50K curves), each p ∈ {3,5,7,11,13}:

1. Collect Frobenius char polys mod p for first 50 good primes q.
2. Classify image against Zywina's list via invariants:
   - Reducibility: every char poly factors over F_p.
   - Quadratic stabilizer: char polys in proper F_{p^2}-subalgebra.
   - Torus normalizer: eigenvalue-swap pattern.
   - Exceptional: finite image, small char poly count.
3. Per-curve label: `surjective`, `reducible`, `stabilizer`, `torus-normalizer`, `exceptional`, or `undetermined`.
4. Aggregate: P(non-surjective | p) vs p, joint across primes.

**Hypotheses:**
- H1 (Serre-g2): fraction non-surjective → 0 as p grows.
- H2 (uniform bound): ∃ p_0 such that for p ≥ p_0, image surjective ∀ J in sample.
- H3 (Zywina): exceptional images concentrate at p ∈ {3, 5, 7}.

## 5. Falsification Criteria

- **Kill Serre-g2 empirically:** J with End(J)=Z persistently non-surjective across p=3,5,7,11,13 with no endomorphism explanation.
- **Kill uniform bound:** non-surjective rate at p=13 exceeds Chebotarev false-negative (~p^{-4} ≈ 3.5e-5).
- **Null:** permute curve↔prime; non-surjective rate must collapse to false-negative floor.
- **Cross-check:** curves with known RM must show non-surjective image at all p — else classification pipeline broken.

## 6. Budget

- LMFDB pull (50K × aplist ~8KB): 400 MB, 30 min.
- Per-curve classification: 50 Frobenius × 5 primes × 5 invariants ≈ 1250 F_p ops; 1K curves/sec → ~50 sec total.
- Validation against Dieulefait's 20 examples: 5 min.
- **Total: 2 hours wall-clock, 500 MB disk. Storage: `charon.g2_mod_p_image`.**

## 7. Expected Outcome

Baseline: ~95% surjective at p=3, rising to >99.9% at p=13. Primary output is the residual — the ~2-5% with non-trivial exceptional image at small p — feeding into:
(a) correlation with other Charon tensor signals (p-adic rank, isogeny degree),
(b) stratification of exceptional-image curves in conductor/discriminant/Sato-Tate space,
(c) candidate invariance partition for Harmonia's stratifier.

Any J with non-surjective image at p ≥ 11 and End(J)=Z is either a Zywina rare case or a bug — both worth examining.

Risk: 50-prime Chebotarev may undersample torus/exceptional discrimination; extend to 200 if undetermined rate > 1%.
