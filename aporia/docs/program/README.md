## What belongs here

A document belongs in `aporia/docs/program/` if it would change what the
programme builds across more than one hypothesis or seat. An experiment design
belongs under `aporia/docs/hypotheses/`. A field literature dossier belongs in
`aporia/docs/frontier_campaign_69/`.

## Convention for these directories

Each holds the proposal verbatim, every substantive external assessment
verbatim, and one adjudication that says plainly where they disagree and which
side the evidence supports. Nothing here is a decision until an operator makes
one; the adjudication exists so that a decision can be made from the
disagreements rather than from the most recent or most enthusiastic document.

Verbatim files are marked UNADJUDICATED at the top. If two of them conflict,
that conflict is the point and it is named in the adjudication rather than
smoothed.
