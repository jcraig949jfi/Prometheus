## 0. The unification (why this is one problem)

Three things we have treated separately are one object:

- **The failure landscape** has gravitational wells = states where reasoning
  *succeeds* (low damage), surrounded by a gradient field of failures pointing
  toward them.
- **The reasoning ladder** (R1–R5+) we have read as scalar difficulty. The
  hypothesis here: it is a **basis**, not a scalar — its "rungs" are independent
  *directions*, and each direction is a **desired well** (a correct-reasoning
  attractor).
- **The ejection / convergence pathology:** pretraining installs a suppressor that
  ejects correct-but-uncertain reasoning in favor of **statistically plausible
  answers that sound good** — a *global attractor* (bypass) the model falls into,
  and it *hardens with scale*.

So: the model has a pathological attractor (sounds-good) competing with the desired
attractors (the reasoning wells). The program is to compute **directional weights**
— a steering signal derived from the failure-field's wells — that bend the
architecture off the sounds-good attractor and toward a correct-reasoning well.
Reasoning replaces mortal intuition because the gradient field is portable and does
not expire; the catalog of answers alone is gradient-free and only memorizable.

**Central Law (this protocol).** *A gradient-enriched failure landscape is a
reasoning substrate iff directional interventions derived from its wells bend a
model toward correct reasoning — generalizing BEYOND the generators that built the
landscape — more than (a) random-direction nulls and (b) raw-answer training.*
Until shown, "directional weights bend the net toward reasoning" is a hypothesis.

## 1. Representation — the atomic training/probe datum

Extends the failure-signal record (`failure_signal_protocol_v0.1` §1) into a
reasoning step:

```
{
  "state":            "<problem/proof state>",     // where reasoning is
  "move":             "<operator/verb>",            // candidate next step
  "gradient":         <float>,                       // damage-change of the move
                                                      //  (<0 = toward a well)
  "target_well":      "<basis-direction id>",        // which reasoning attractor
  "outcome":          "CORRECT | SOUNDS_GOOD_WRONG | NEUTRAL",
  "null_p":           <float>,
  "provenance":       {...}, "emitter_version": "<hash>"
}
```

`SOUNDS_GOOD_WRONG` is the load-bearing label: a continuation that is fluent /
high-likelihood under the base model but *wrong*. It is the ejection attractor made
into a training target to push **away** from. It must be generated by a strong
plausible-but-wrong generator, never by random corruption (a weak null poisons
H-R3).

## 2. Hypotheses (each with operationalization, null, falsifier)

### H-R1 — The ladder is a basis, not a scalar
*Operational:* reasoning traces decompose into ≥`d` independent dimensions
(candidate bases to test, not assume: Noesis 11-primitive
[COMPOSE/MAP/EXTEND/REDUCE/LIMIT/DUALIZE/LINEARIZE/STOCHASTICIZE/SYMMETRIZE/
BREAK_SYMMETRY/COMPLETE], the 9 damage operators, Sphinx ontology, or a learned
basis). The multi-D decomposition predicts next-correct-move / solution success
**better than a 1-D difficulty scalar.**
*Null:* a single difficulty scalar matches the multi-D predictive power.
*Falsifier:* basis adds no predictive lift over the scalar → the ladder is scalar,
not a basis, and the rest of this protocol is unmotivated.

### H-R2 — Wells are separable directions in activation space
*Operational:* in a small local model (≤3–4B, `feedback_vram_ceiling`), at the
v_proj lever, `CORRECT` vs `SOUNDS_GOOD_WRONG` continuations are **linearly
separable**, and each basis direction (§H-R1) has a distinct activation signature.
*Null:* a linear probe is at chance once content/length confounds are matched.
*Falsifier:* not separable → there is no "well direction" to steer toward; the
sounds-good and correct attractors are entangled.

### H-R3 — Directional weights bend the model (the keystone)
*Operational:* a steering vector `v_w = mean(CORRECT activations) −
mean(SOUNDS_GOOD_WRONG activations)` per well, added at v_proj at inference, bends
**held-out, out-of-distribution** behavior toward correct reasoning — measured where
pattern-matching fails (problems the base model gets wrong by sounding-good).
*Nulls:* (a) random-direction steering of equal norm; (b) steering at a non-lever
site; (c) steering with shuffled well-labels.
*Falsifier:* well-steering ≤ random-direction steering → the "well" carries no
causal reasoning signal, only correlation.

### H-R4 — Gradient-enriched training transfers (HARD-3-gated)
*Operational:* a tiny model trained on §1 records (state → gradient → outcome)
generalizes to held-out problems better than the same model trained on raw
answers.
*Null:* raw-answer training matches.
*Falsifier:* no transfer lift; OR the lift is explained by oracle-feature leakage
(`feedback_causal_vs_oracle_features` — step_index, steps_to_end, is_last_step are
ORACLE and banned) → the model learned the terminus, not reasoning.

## 3. Staging (smallest-first; HARD-3 compliance explicit)

- **Stage 0 — representation (HARD-3-OK, pure data):** build the §1 records from the
  existing failure catalog; run H-R1. No model. If H-R1 falsifies, stop — the ladder
  is scalar and there are no wells to steer toward.
- **Stage 1 — probe + steer (HARD-3-OK, measurement not training):** H-R2 + H-R3 on
  an existing small local model via activation probing and inference-time steering
  vectors at v_proj. This is representation engineering / measurement, NOT training a
  navigator on an incomplete tensor — but **flag for human ratification before
  running** (it touches the model; HARD-3 caution applies).
- **Stage 2 — train (HARD-3-GATED):** H-R4. Do NOT run until the closed-loop tensor
  condition is met OR a human waiver is recorded. Never silent.

---

## 4. Controls & discipline (every stage)

- **Strong sounds-good baseline:** `SOUNDS_GOOD_WRONG` continuations must be fluent,
  high-likelihood, and wrong — a real competitor, not random noise.
- **Generalization, not memorization:** held-out problems must be from a family /
  structure absent from training, and (per the failure protocol §6.1) sealed against
  operator-closure leakage where applicable.
- **Causal not oracle features** (`feedback_causal_vs_oracle_features`): no feature
  available only at the terminus.
- **Replicate across ≥5 seeds** (`feedback_replicate_seeds`); a single-seed steering
  effect is not a result.
- **Permutation / shuffle nulls** (`feedback_permutation_null`) on well-labels.
- **Surface vs substance:** steering must move *correctness on OOD problems*, not
  just style/fluency. Report task accuracy, not perplexity.

## 5. Honest risks (this is the kind of frame that hypnotizes)

1. **Circularity.** If wells/gradients are computed entirely from operators *we*
   chose, the model learns *our bias*, not reasoning — exactly the artifact H5 just
   exposed (OEIS "voids" that were transform-density). The directional signal must
   be validated as generalizing beyond its generators (the Central Law's "beyond the
   generators" clause).
2. **The attractor is scale-hardened.** The convergence finding says the sounds-good
   suppressor *strengthens with scale*. A steering vector may bend a 3B model and
   fail to bend a 70B one, or revert under distribution shift. Report whether the
   bend *holds OOD*, not just in-sample.
3. **VRAM ceiling.** Only ≤3–4B locally; signal may be weak and noisy. A null at 3B
   does not falsify the idea at scale — state that explicitly.
4. **Steering ≠ reasoning.** Activation steering can change outputs without inducing
   reasoning. The OOD-generalization metric is the guard; perplexity/style is not.

---

## 6. Promotion gate

Promote any claim that "the failure landscape teaches reasoning" to doctrine ONLY
after: H-R1 passes (basis beats scalar); H-R2 passes (separable); H-R3 beats the
random-direction null across ≥5 seeds on OOD problems; and the effect is shown to
generalize beyond the generators. H-R4 (training) only after the HARD-3 gate. Report
nulls. Doctrine means survived the null, not "beautiful unifying story."

---

## 7. What success and failure buy

- **H-R3 beats null:** there exists a computable directional signal that bends a
  transformer off the sounds-good attractor toward correct reasoning — the first
  concrete bridge from the failure landscape to the model, and a direct intervention
  on the ejection finding.
- **Null:** the failure landscape is (so far) a discovery/audit instrument but NOT a
  reasoning substrate at this scale — a clean, important negative that redirects
  effort. Both are wins; only an unreported result is a loss.
