1. CLAIM UNDER REVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A sibling seat mined 69 disciplines of computational discovery into
experiment templates SHAPED FOR an in-house bench with three executors
and a single-scalar outcome rule. Its own critique found the result
thin: the landscape is solved by a one-flip hill climber in exactly L
queries, and the proposed replacement is a puzzle the programme authored.

CLAIM: asking the SAME fields an unshaped practitioner question --
what would I read, install, download, reproduce and build to run
frontier experiments in 2026 -- yields externally-anchored experiments
with published comparison numbers, which bench-shaped templates cannot.

Two preregistered falsifiers were filed BEFORE the deciding data:
  - if the reports never decline an impossible request, every recipe is
    an unverified construction (PREREGISTRATION_recipe_hatch.md)
  - if the substitutions found were merely convenient rather than
    necessary, the corpus is routing around work it could have done

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. METHOD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Google Deep Research, agent deep-research-pro-preview-12-2025, fired
through a repo dispatcher at the documented paid-tier ceiling of three
concurrent. One prompt per field. Eight required parts; three carry the
weight:

  PART 3  software: name, bare URL, language, licence, year of last
          activity, and a verdict of MAINTAINED / DORMANT / ABANDONED
  PART 5  one reproduction recipe: exact versions, every parameter,
          replicate count and seeding, compute cost, the PUBLISHED
          NUMBER to compare against, and the three usual mistakes
  PART 7  negative results, retractions, failed replications, standing
          critiques, and which were never answered

Each prompt is anchored on one concrete method, stated in full, and the
report is invited to correct the anchor. Corrections were treated as the
most valuable return.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. FORMAT CONTROL (a prior failure this design had to survive)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The grounding layer rewrites a finished report and replaces square-
bracketed spans with citation markers. It cannot tell a JSON array from
a citation. A previous run lost 67 of 69 structured blocks this way.

Mitigation: prose output, square brackets forbidden outright, bare
identifiers, IDENTIFIER UNKNOWN in place of invention. The deck builder
asserts no fence and no bracket in any prompt before writing.

Measured across the whole corpus: 10 bracket candidates
flagged, 4 adjudicated as REAL loss by reading. Destroyed
values are logged and NEVER written back from inference, even where the
surrounding sentence makes them obvious.

6. PRIMARY RESULT -- REPRODUCIBILITY AUDITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ten Tier 5 dossiers gave a complete recipe for a DIFFERENT system than
the one asked about. A second deck asked only whether the named
historical system can be run today, with the verdict on line one and
modern substitutes confined to the final section.

  Verdict                    Count   Fields
  ------------------------   -----   -------------------------------
  NOT_REPRODUCIBLE               5   BACON, AM/EURISKO, HR, PCT, creativity
  REPRODUCIBLE_WITH_EFFORT       2   ASD broadly, PROTOS (CBR)
  REPRODUCIBLE                   3   SME, ECHO, Weasel

THE FINDING IS THE REASON, NOT THE COUNT. BACON and AM fail not because
the code is lost. AM's original Lisp was RECOVERED from Stanford
archives in 2023. They fail because the original publications report no
number a reproduction could be checked against -- anecdotal case
studies, hand-built noiseless tables, evaluation by author judgement.

SME, ECHO and Weasel came back REPRODUCIBLE with live code, so three of
the ten substitutions were NOT necessary. Those three dossiers -- 57, 58
and 61 -- each routed away from a system that runs today, and that is a
measured defect rate on the original deck rather than an impression.

7. PREREGISTERED TEST AND ITS RESOLUTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rule filed with 19 of 22 deciding dossiers unfired. k = dossiers whose
PART 5 states no experiment meets the bar.

  k >= 3   HATCH LIVE ....................... not reached
  k = 0    HATCH DECORATIVE ................. not reached
  k = 1-2  INDETERMINATE .................... YES (k = 1)

Result: k=1, substitutions s=10, on-anchor 11.

The prescribed tiebreak was VOIDED, not replaced. It relied on Adam and
Ada being irreproducible in silico by construction; both returned valid
in-silico recipes via flux-balance analysis and an Olympus digital twin.
Inventing a replacement tiebreak after seeing data is the move the
preregistration exists to forbid, so the verdict stands INDETERMINATE.

8. CONTROLS AND WHAT RULES OUT CHEAPER EXPLANATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ELIGIBILITY, checked not assumed. A refusal that cannot fire is not a
refusal. IDENTIFIER UNKNOWN fired 138 times across the corpus,
highest in the thinnest fields, so honest refusal is demonstrably
available in the same prompt slot.

DIRECTION OF CONFOUND, recorded before resolution. Firing order ranked
mature tooling first, and model helpfulness also pushes toward a recipe
existing. Both push the same way, so HATCH LIVE was the harder verdict
and DECORATIVE the cheap one.

INSTRUMENT FAILURES, reported rather than hidden. The bracket screen was
rebuilt twice: it first keyed on the wrong side of the bracket and
scored zero on a case already confirmed by hand, then called six intact
tensor shapes losses. It is now a CANDIDATE generator only; adjudication
is by reading. An identifier check that demanded arXiv ids wrongly failed
a field older than arXiv that had supplied DOIs.

9. KNOWN LIMITATIONS AND CAVEATS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- NOT VERIFIED. No recipe in this corpus has been executed. Every
  parameter, version and expected number is as-reported.
- AGREEMENT IS WEAK EVIDENCE. Each prompt asserts an anchor, so a report
  agreeing with one may be returning the questioner's own framing. One
  dossier confirmed a claim this seat already held, and the prompt had
  told it the gap existed. That is corpus gravity, not confirmation.
  The CORRECTIONS are the load-bearing returns.
- PART 5 MUST BE CHECKED AGAINST ITS ANCHOR. Ten of 22 gave a recipe for
  another system. The check is cheap and it fails often.
- INVENTORY PAIRING IS PROXIMITY-BASED. A URL is paired with a verdict
  only when unambiguous; distance is recorded per entry as gap_chars and
  unpaired items are reported, not resolved. Spot-checked 11 of 11
  correct against two hand-read dossiers; not exhaustively audited.
- THREE DESTROYED VALUES remain destroyed by policy.
- BUDGET. Documented as 20 reports/day; observed well above that with no
  refusal in over 100 dispatches. Three reports were double-fired by an
  operator error and are recorded as spent.
