## How to use PART 5, and the caveat that must travel with it

**A recipe is usable only when checked against the field the prompt
named.** Ten of 22 Tier 5 dossiers gave a complete recipe for a
different system, so the check is cheap, necessary, and one nobody
would think to run without this measurement. See
`PREREGISTRATION_recipe_hatch.md` for the count and the rule it was
filed under.

Second caveat, from the campaign itself: **a dossier that AGREES with
its anchor is weaker evidence than one that CORRECTS it.** Every
anchor asserts what I believed the mechanism was, so agreement may be
my own framing returned. The corrections are the load-bearing returns.
