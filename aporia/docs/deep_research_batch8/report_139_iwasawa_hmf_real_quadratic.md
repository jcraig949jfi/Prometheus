# Deep Research Report #139: Iwasawa Main Conjecture for Hilbert Modular Forms over Real Quadratic Fields

**Target Agent:** Charon
**Date:** 2026-04-25
**Predecessor:** Batch 7 #120 (p-adic L-function structure); Batch 6/7 #118-119 (operator transport); Batch 5 (Katz-Sarnak universality)

## 1. Problem Statement

Let K be a real quadratic field with ring of integers O_K, f a Hilbert cuspidal eigenform of parallel weight (2,2), level N coprime to p, and p an odd prime that is ordinary for f (i.e., a_P(f) is a p-adic unit for every P | p in O_K). Fix an embedding into C_p. Let K_infinity / K be the cyclotomic Z_p^2-extension (rank 2 since [K:Q]=2), Lambda = Z_p[[Gal(K_infinity/K)]] the Iwasawa algebra, and Sel_p(f / K_infinity) the Greenberg-Selmer group cut out by the ordinary filtration.

The **Iwasawa Main Conjecture (IMC)** for f over K asserts:

  char_Lambda( Sel_p(f / K_infinity)^vee ) = ( L_p(f, K) )

as ideals of Lambda, where L_p(f, K) is the Hida-style two-variable p-adic L-function attached to f. Equivalent formulation: the characteristic ideal of the dual Selmer equals the ideal generated by the p-adic L-function. The "weak" IMC is the divisibility "char | L_p" (analytic side bounds algebraic). The conjecture is open beyond GL(2)/Q in essentially all but small special cases.

**Operational target:** for ~500 (f, p) pairs from `hmf_forms`, compute both sides modulo p^k and modulo (gamma_1 - 1, gamma_2 - 1)^k and check ideal equality at finite Lambda-truncations.

## 2. Literature

- **Mazur-Wiles (1984):** IMC for cyclotomic Z_p-extensions of Q.
- **Wiles (1990):** IMC for totally real fields, *one divisibility* (analytic | algebraic) for Dirichlet-twist L-functions.
- **Skinner-Urban (2014):** full IMC for GL(2)/Q at ordinary p, conditional on local conditions; the gold standard we extend.
- **Hida (1989, 1991):** construction of HMF p-adic L-functions and Hida families over totally real K.
- **Dimitrov (2009, 2013):** p-adic L for HMF, control theorems, freeness over Hida-Hecke algebra.
- **Mok (2009):** one-divisibility IMC for HMF over real quadratic, ordinary case, under Taylor-Wiles hypotheses.
- **Bhattacharya-Hsieh (2021):** anticyclotomic IMC for HMF, CM points input.
- **Fouquet-Wan (2015, 2021):** Eisenstein congruence machine extending Skinner-Urban toward HMF; full IMC still conditional on residual irreducibility + non-Eisenstein.

**Status:** one divisibility established under hypotheses; full equality is conjectural. Empirical Lambda-rank measurement at scale has never been performed.

## 3. LMFDB Data

- `hmf_forms`: label, field_label, level_norm, weight, dimension, hecke_ring_index, is_base_change, related_objects (~45K rows; ~12K with K real quadratic).
- `hmf_hecke`: hecke_eigenvalues indexed by `primes_iter`; required for L_p truncation.
- `mf_newforms`: label, weight, level — for base-change cross-check (a_P(f) = a_p(g)·something when f = BC(g)).
- `nf_fields`: label, disc, degree, signature; restrict degree=2 AND r2=0 AND disc>0 (real-quadratic K with disc < 10^6: ~610 fields in LMFDB).
- `ec_nfcurves`: ainvs, conductor_norm, sha_an, rank, torsion_order, isogeny_matrix; Selmer_p surrogate via `analytic_rank` + `sha_primary`.
- `lfunc_lfunctions` / `lfunc_iwasawa` (if present): pre-computed Iwasawa invariants where available.

Estimate after restriction (K real quadratic, p ordinary, level coprime to p): ~3K-6K (f, p) pairs across p in {3,5,7,11}; sample 500.

## 4. Test Design

**Step 1.** Postgres: SELECT label, field_label, level_norm, hecke_eigenvalues FROM hmf_forms JOIN nf_fields ON field_label = nf_fields.label WHERE nf_fields.degree=2 AND nf_fields.disc>0 AND weight = '[2,2]' AND level_norm < 1000.

**Step 2.** For each f and p in {3,5,7,11}: check ordinarity by verifying val_p(a_P(f)) = 0 for all P|p (using `hecke_eigenvalues` indexed against `primes_iter`).

**Step 3 (analytic).** Compute L_p(f, K) modulo (gamma_1 - 1)^k (gamma_2 - 1)^k for k = 4 via Hida-Dembele-Voight algorithm: stabilize f to U_p-eigenform, build Mazur-Mellin transform from modular symbols. Sage `HilbertModularForms` + custom p-adic interpolation. Output: Lambda/I-element mu(f,p).

**Step 4 (algebraic).** Compute Sel_p(f / K_n) for n = 0,1,2 (finite layers of Z_p^2-tower) via Greenberg's algorithm: descent on ec_nfcurves when f corresponds to E/K, otherwise via Galois cohomology of T_p(f) with ordinary local condition. Use `ec_nfcurves.sha_an` and `ec_nfcurves.rank` as ground truth at layer 0; Lambda-corank from layer growth.

**Step 5 (compare).** Form characteristic polynomial of Sel^vee at each truncation; compare ideal generators with L_p truncation. Faltings-Serre-style: equality at finite index k determines equality up to (gamma - 1)^k.

**Step 6 (null).** Shuffle (f, p) pairings: re-attach a random ordinary p to each f. If equality persists, test is vacuous.

**Metrics:** fraction of pairs with ideal match at k=4; failure stratification by (disc K, p split/inert in K, level_norm class).

## 5. Falsification

- **Strong kill:** match rate < 50% across non-pathological cells → IMC formulation or our truncation is wrong. Likely a normalization bug, but publish the cells.
- **Weak kill:** match for split p, systematic failure for inert p → unknown mu-invariant contribution at inert primes (Greenberg's open question for HMF).
- **Structural finding:** failures concentrate at disc(K) divisible by p, or at non-base-change f → identifies the Eisenstein-congruence cells where Skinner-Urban-Fouquet-Wan machinery breaks.
- **Null sanity:** shuffled pairings must show ≫50% failure; if not, our characteristic-ideal computation is degenerate.
