# Deep Research Report #154: Iwahori-Hecke Algebra Kazhdan-Lusztig Cells

**Target Agent:** Harmonia
**Date:** 2026-04-25
**Front:** Hecke algebras / Kazhdan-Lusztig theory

## 1. Problem Statement

For a finite Coxeter group W (specifically type A_n, B_n, D_n at small n), the Iwahori-Hecke algebra H_q(W) over Z[q, q^{-1}] admits Kazhdan and Lusztig's canonical basis {C_w}_{w in W} (1979), constructed from the standard basis {T_w} via a bar-involution-fixed change-of-basis encoded by Kazhdan-Lusztig polynomials P_{x,w}(q) in Z[q]. The KL conjectures predict (i) **positivity**: P_{x,w}(q) lies in Z_>=0[q], and (ii) Lusztig's **a-function** a(w) in Z_>=0 partitions W into left, right, and two-sided cells with a-monotone behavior along Bruhat order. We pose: does positivity hold across all (x, w) for n up to 9, and does the empirical cell distribution match Lusztig's a-monotone prediction (a(w) non-decreasing in Bruhat order on cell representatives, cell shapes matching Robinson-Schensted P-tableau equivalence classes)? Williamson-Elias (2017) proved positivity via Soergel bimodules; the empirical question is whether Sage's `KazhdanLusztigPolynomial` computation independently confirms this and whether *cell-statistic distributions* exhibit any structural anomaly absent from the existing combinatorial literature.

## 2. Literature

- **Kazhdan-Lusztig (1979, Inventiones):** original definition of C_w and P_{x,w}; positivity conjectured.
- **Lusztig (1984), *Characters of Reductive Groups over a Finite Field*:** a-function, cells, asymptotic ring J.
- **Lusztig (2003), *Hecke Algebras with Unequal Parameters*:** unequal-parameter generalization, conjectures P1-P15 on cells.
- **Brenti (1998):** combinatorial KL identities, computational tractability for small rank.
- **Geck-Pfeiffer (2000), *Characters of Finite Coxeter Groups and Iwahori-Hecke Algebras*:** standard reference; CHEVIE / Sage implementation lineage.
- **Williamson-Elias (2017, Annals):** positivity proven via Soergel bimodules and Hodge theory; sanity-check target.

## 3. LMFDB Data

Coxeter combinatorics is **not directly in LMFDB**. Compute via Sage:
- `CoxeterGroup(['A', n])`, `CoxeterGroup(['B', n])`, `CoxeterGroup(['D', n])` for the abstract group.
- `IwahoriHeckeAlgebra(W, q)` constructs H_q(W) with bases `Cp` (KL canonical), `T` (standard).
- `KazhdanLusztigPolynomial(W, x, w)` returns P_{x,w}(q) in Z[q].
- `W.bruhat_interval(x, w)`, `W.kazhdan_lusztig_cell(side='left'|'right'|'two-sided')` for cell decomposition.

Cross-reference (representation-theoretic targets, not direct cell data):
- `nf_fields_galois`: Galois groups isomorphic to small W (e.g., S_n) give a discrete latent connection between number-field Galois statistics and cell sizes.
- `mf_galois_reps`: mod-p Hecke action provides a representation-theoretic mirror; KL cells index simple modules of H_q at roots of unity (Geck-Rouquier).

## 4. Test Design

**Step 1.** For W in {S_6, S_7, S_8, S_9, B_4, D_4}, enumerate W via `CoxeterGroup(...).list()`. |S_9| = 362880, |B_4| = 384, |D_4| = 192.

**Step 2.** Compute all P_{x,w}(q) for x <= w in Bruhat order using `W.kazhdan_lusztig_polynomial(x, w)`. For S_9 this is ~|W|^2 / 2 polys; restrict to x in lower interval of w to keep <= 10^8 evaluations.

**Step 3.** **Positivity check**: assert all coefficients >= 0. Williamson-Elias proves this; failure would indicate a Sage bug, not a math falsification.

**Step 4.** **a-function distribution**: compute a(w) as min degree of q in P_{x,w} over relevant pairs (or via `W.kazhdan_lusztig_cell().a_value()`); record histogram a(w) vs |{w}|.

**Step 5.** **Cell decomposition**: `W.kazhdan_lusztig_cells(side='left')`, `'right'`, `'two-sided'`. Record cell sizes, multiplicities, and assignments.

**Step 6.** **Cell-size histogram**: for type A_n, compare to Robinson-Schensted: two-sided cells should be in bijection with partitions of n, sized by (f^lambda)^2 where f^lambda is the standard tableau count.

**Step 7.** **a-monotone check**: for each two-sided cell C, pick representatives, verify a(w) non-decreasing along chains in Bruhat order between cells.

## 5. Falsification

- **Positivity confirmed across all polynomials** -> expected (Williamson-Elias); tool calibration only.
- **Positivity fails at any (x,w)** -> Sage implementation bug; report upstream.
- **a-monotone confirmed** -> consistent with Lusztig P1-P15.
- **a-monotone violated on any chain** -> publishable: contradicts Lusztig conjecture or reveals subtlety in Sage's a-function definition.
- **Cell-size histogram matches RS for type A** -> confirms; matches Geck-Pfeiffer table.
- **Structural deviation in B_4 / D_4** (cell sizes off from Lusztig 1984 tables) -> publishable anomaly.
- **Null sanity:** random non-KL "polynomials" generated by perturbing P_{x,w} coefficients should fail a-monotone with probability ~1; if they pass, the test is vacuous.

## 7. Expected Outcome

Measure cell statistics (a-function distribution, cell-size histogram, two-sided cell count) for ~5 small Coxeter groups; verify Lusztig's a-monotone and RS-matching predictions empirically at scale. Prior: very high agreement -- Williamson-Elias settles positivity; Lusztig 1984 settles type A cells; type B/D conjectures P1-P15 are largely verified for small rank (Geck-Pfeiffer). **Value is calibration plus axis-coverage**: KL theory contributes a *structural-combinatorial* channel distinct from the arithmetic (L-function), spectral (eigenvalue), and p-adic axes currently in Charon's tensor. Aporia void-detection benefits because cells supply a discrete, finite, exhaustive invariant family, ideal as a "combinatorial" channel for V4 and as an anchor against which suspected new bridges (e.g., Hecke-side modular-form action versus Coxeter-side cell partition) can be calibrated. Secondary: any deviation from Lusztig's predictions at small rank is a publishable anomaly with direct contact to Soergel bimodule machinery.
