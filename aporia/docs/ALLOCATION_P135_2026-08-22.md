## A catch that changes what the 44 PROF rows are

The 44 `PROF-*` rows all read *"Ladder profile: run <Agent> artifacts/config through phase0+R4
probes."* But phase0 grades **reasoners on procedurally-generated probes**. Running an agent's
*artifacts* through a probe battery is not a defined operation for this instrument.

**The 44 PROF rows appear to rest on a category error** — inventory built on a premise the instrument
does not support. That is a finding worth more than the rows are, and it is surfaced here *before*
opening a campaign on them rather than after wiring work that could not have paid off.

## CAMPAIGN R — preregistration (pass 1 of 3)

**The question, which is the ladder's own structure and not an application:** phase0's docstring
states the calibration report is *"a capability ceiling staircase — or not, which is itself evidence
the tiers are orthogonal axes rather than ordered rungs."* R4 was built in August and **has never
been run inside the suite.** So: **does R4 behave as an ordered rung, or as an orthogonal axis?**

**Pass 1 (next pass): wire and verify only.** Wire R4's three families into the phase0 suite; confirm
the battery runs end to end offline; confirm R4's `surface_answer` kill-pattern is emitted; report the
probe count and reasoner count that will constitute n. **Do not interpret the calibration.**

**Preregistration deferred to pass 1 by design, and here is why that is not evasion:** the honest
thresholds depend on what the instrument outputs — how many probes per family, how many reasoners,
what the trace-vector dimensionality is. Fixing branch cuts now, before seeing the instrument's shape,
would be inventing thresholds at design time rather than deriving them, which the P125 doctrine
forbids in the opposite direction. Pass 1 measures the instrument; pass 2 preregisters against it and
runs; pass 3 adjudicates.

**What is fixed now and cannot move:** R4 is wired *without modification to phase0's existing probes*
(the module's own docstring says it "modifies nothing there"); the calibration is read only after the
branches are written; and if the wiring proves that R4 cannot run inside the suite without changing
phase0's semantics, that is a REDESIGN outcome reported as such rather than worked around.

not work.
