## The catch: the instrument did not improve, the benchmark got easier

The tempting read is that 0.964 beats X-2's 0.944 and the instrument is better. **It is not.**

    X-2 development   strict 115/125 = 0.9200    any-valid 119/125 = 0.9520    gap 0.032
    X-3 development   strict 217/250 = 0.8680    any-valid 241/250 = 0.9640    gap 0.096

**Strict went DOWN 5.2 points while any-valid went UP 1.2.** The strict–any-valid gap tripled, and
the cause is visible in the answer sets:

    operator     X-2 dev mean / max        X-3 dev mean / max
    diff           1.080 /  2                2.960 / 52
    partsum        1.280 /  2                1.480 /  6
    binomial       1.000 /  1                1.100 /  2
    moebius        1.200 /  3                1.620 / 10
    shift          4.000 / 27                3.620 / 13

`diff` answer sets go from a maximum of 2 to **52**. The any-valid rule's leniency scales with
answer-set richness, and richness varies by sample — so the improvement is benchmark composition,
not representation. Same lesson as the last three times, now firing on a number that flattered me.

## Consequence for pass 3, pre-committed before frozen is touched

**K1/K2 are protected; K3 is not, and needs a correction stated now.**

K1 and K2 are defined on `D = L2 − raw-term`, a *difference*. Both arms are scored under the same
any-valid rule against the same answer sets, so answer-set richness inflates both and largely cancels
in D. That the preregistration used a difference rather than an absolute rate is protective, and it
was not chosen for that reason — it is luck worth naming.

**K3 contains an absolute condition — "L2 four-real-op top-10 ≤ 3× chance" — and chance was specified
as 10/N.** That is wrong under any-valid scoring: with |V| valid targets, the chance of landing any
of them in a top-10 is ≈ 10·|V|/N. On development's four real operators, mean |V| = 1.79:

    unadjusted chance      10/22,095       = 0.000453
    answer-set adjusted    10 × 1.79/22,095 = 0.000810      (1.79×)
    3× adjusted chance                       = 0.002430

**Pass 3 will use the answer-set-adjusted chance, with mean |V| computed on the FROZEN population
itself** — not carried over from development, which is precisely the error this pass just caught.
Recorded here, before frozen is read, so it cannot be tuned later.

## K0 preview — stated, and nothing changed in response

**Nothing was changed in response to this preview.** The branches are fixed, a development preview
