## What this establishes, and it is the campaign's real output

**1. Structural relations ARE partially recoverable from terms alone.** V2 retrieves at 0.1225
against an adjusted chance of 0.000786 — **156× chance**, with the shuffled control at 1/400
confirming the harness leaks nothing. The operator-agnostic task is not hopeless.

**2. The behavioral signature adds NOTHING over raw terms, and now we can say so with an interval.**
D = −0.0025, paired CI [−0.0354, 0.0304]. **That interval excludes |D| ≥ 0.05** — the effect size the
campaign was powered to detect. This is the first null in this line of work that rules something out
rather than merely failing to find it. A 125-dimensional hand-designed behavioral signature is not
better than a 25-term log-magnitude vector.

**3. The pass-1 structural worry is REFUTED on the real operators and CONFIRMED on the control.**
Order-sensitive V2 was feared to lift L1 while hurting L2. On the four real operators it does not:
V2 0.1225 > V1 0.0975. But on `shift` the reversal is violent — **V1 70/100 versus V2 13/100**.
The mechanism is clean: a shift preserves aggregate statistics almost exactly while displacing every
position, so order-sensitive features are destroyed by precisely the transformation that leaves
aggregates intact. The tension was real, and it lives entirely where the transformation is positional.

## Branch adjudication — and a preregistration defect I have to report

    K1 ADVANCE   D >= 0.05                                        FALSE  (D = −0.0025)
    K2 REDESIGN  0.02 <= D < 0.05                                 FALSE  (D below 0.02)
    K3 KILL      D < 0.02 AND L2 <= 3x adj chance AND K0          FALSE  (0.1225 >> 0.002359)

**None fired. The branches do not partition the outcome space**, and the region we landed in —
`D < 0.02` with L2 far above chance — is uncovered. The script defaulted to REDESIGN.

This is the **fourth** under-specified-branch defect across three campaigns: D1–D5 were qualitative;
D4's numeric cut was invented at adjudication; K3 carried a wrong chance denominator (caught in pass
2); and now K1–K3 fail to partition. The pattern is the finding. **New doctrine:** branch conditions
must be verified to PARTITION the outcome space — every reachable reading mapping to exactly one
terminal state — before the pass that reads them runs.

**REDESIGN is nonetheless the correct state on the merits**, reached by default rather than by a
fired branch, and that distinction is recorded rather than smoothed over. It is not KILL: the
preregistered kill required L2 at chance, and L2 is 156× chance. What is dead is not the
operator-agnostic search but **the hand-designed behavioral signature as the representation for it** —
including for the Sleeping Beauties application, where the signature would have been the instrument.
