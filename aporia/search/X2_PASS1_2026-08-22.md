## 2. Deficit 2 — order-blindness, and the 2×2 that names it

Reading what V1's 33 features *are*: quantiles, entropies, histograms, moments — every one an
aggregate, and aggregates are near-permutation-invariant. They record which behaviours occur, not
in what order. V2 keeps all 33 and adds only order-sensitive, scale-free features: a detrended
log-magnitude profile at 12 fixed relative positions; per-term residues mod 2 and mod 3 in order
rather than collapsed to a density and an entropy; the normalised rank permutation of the first 16
terms; the sign sequence; and a local-extremum profile. **125 dimensions.**

Campaign X could not separate "wrong features" from "wrong metric" and said so. This 2×2 does —
{V1, V2} × {z-scored Euclidean, ZCA-whitened}, L1 top-1 on 125 development pairs, pool 20,358:

    V1 euclid      94/125 = 0.7520     diff 20/25 · partsum 21/25 · binomial 21/25 · moebius 22/25 · shift 10/25
    V1 whitened    92/125 = 0.7360     diff 19/25 · partsum 21/25 · binomial 20/25 · moebius 22/25 · shift 10/25
    V2 euclid     113/125 = 0.9040     diff 23/25 · partsum 23/25 · binomial 25/25 · moebius 23/25 · shift 19/25
    V2 whitened   114/125 = 0.9120     diff 24/25 · partsum 23/25 · binomial 25/25 · moebius 23/25 · shift 19/25

    attribution:  features +0.1520 | metric −0.0160 | combined +0.1600

**The deficit was the features, not the metric.** Whitening is worth −1.6 points on V1 and +0.8 on
V2 — nothing. The order-blindness diagnosis is confirmed by the intervention aimed at it, and the
redundant-block argument for whitening is refuted by its own test.

## 3. The gate, and the carve-out I am not taking

**ENTRY GATE 0.95 — FAILED at 0.9120.** L2 not measured.

The failure is concentrated entirely in the trivial control: the four real operators score
**95/100 = 0.950** and `shift` scores **19/25 = 0.760**. Excluding the control would pass the gate
exactly. **I am not doing that.** The gate was preregistered on overall L1 top-1, and carving out
the worst stratum after seeing it is precisely the move gates exist to prevent. Reported, not acted on.

It is, however, the diagnosis for pass 2, and it points at the benchmark rather than the
representation: for `shift`, `op(A)` is `A` with one term dropped, and OEIS contains many sequences
that are near-shifts or variants of one another. Excluding the source removes one such relative and
leaves the rest. **A `shift` "miss" may be a correct retrieval of a different, equally valid
shift-partner** — which would mean the benchmark's assumption of a unique correct answer is false
for this operator, and that some of the collision rate is not error at all.
