## Question and test spec (from triage, updated to live mirror)
Lehmer's conjecture: M(P) >= M_Lehmer = 1.176280818... for every non-cyclotomic integer
polynomial. Triage expected 9K number fields; the live mirror holds 22,178,569 rows in
nf_fields — the spec's data estimate was stale by 2,400x (more instrument, same question).

## Method
Degree-stratified sample (per feedback_sampling_strategy_is_analysis): 800 fields per degree,
degrees 2-24 (~16,400 fields), Mahler measure via root products (numpy) on the stored
polredabs defining polynomials. Coeffs stored as Postgres array-literal TEXT — parsed
explicitly (the mirror's text-typing trap again, third appearance).

## Pre-stated readings
- SUPPORTIVE-GAP: no non-cyclotomic M in (1, 1.17628).
- CHALLENGE: any such M (instrument-bug-first per doctrine).
- VACUOUS: schema/parse degenerate.

## Result: SUPPORTIVE-GAP — with a structural bonus
- SUSPECTS in (1, M_Lehmer): ZERO across all sampled degrees.
- The observed NON-CYCLOTOMIC FLOOR is 1.324718 (degrees 15 and 21) — this is exactly the
  plastic number, the smallest Pisot number and the constant of SMYTH'S THEOREM (non-reciprocal
  polynomials satisfy M >= 1.3247...). The empirical floor of the sample IS the known
  theorem's constant, surfacing without being asked for: the sample contains no reciprocal
  small-measure specimens (Salem-type), which is where sub-Smyth measures must live.
- Degree-22 slice contains an exact M = 1.000000 (Kronecker: cyclotomic product) — correctly
  excluded from the non-cyclotomic floor.
- Lehmer's own polynomial {1,1,0,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1,0,1,1} is NOT present as a stored polredabs form.

## Instrument caveats (load-bearing)
1. POLREDABS PROJECTION: nf_fields stores ONE canonical reduced polynomial per field.
   Lehmer's conjecture quantifies over ALL polynomials; a small-measure polynomial's field
   may be represented here by a different (larger-measure) reduced form. The reading is
   about the polredabs slice of polynomial space, not polynomial space itself.
2. Sampling window: first-800-per-degree is an insert-order slice, not a random sample
   (doctrine caveat); degrees 13/17/19/23 exhausted at <800 (fewer fields on the mirror).
3. Parse discipline: one parse failure (logged, not swallowed); the first run of this attack
   silently nulled ALL rows via a bad parser inside a bare except — caught because an
   all-empty result is itself a VACUOUS reading demanding diagnosis. Bare excepts around
   parsers are now a known self-trap.
