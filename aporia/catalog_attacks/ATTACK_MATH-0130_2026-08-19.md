## Question and test spec (from triage)
For 2-dim odd Artin representations, reciprocity (Serre's conjecture, PROVEN by
Khare-Wintenberger) guarantees a matching weight-1 newform. Falsification criterion: any odd
2-dim rep with no matching form. Test: existence + count matching across the mirror.

## Pre-stated readings
- SUPPORTIVE-EXISTENCE: every odd 2-dim Artin conductor (in the complete box) has weight-1
  newforms at that level.
- CHALLENGE: any conductor with reps but no forms (instrument-bug-first).
- AMBIGUOUS-UNITS: counts incomparable due to convention mismatch.

## Result: SUPPORTIVE-EXISTENCE + AMBIGUOUS-UNITS on exact counts
Complete box (conductor/level <= 1000), unit-normalized (Artin rows weighted by
GaloisConjugates cardinality; newforms as embedded-form dim-sums):
- EXISTENCE: 470 conductors carry odd 2-dim Artin reps; ALL 470 have weight-1 newforms at
  that level. ZERO existence failures — the falsification criterion has nowhere to land,
  consistent with the proven theorem.
- EXACT COUNTS: only 20% of shared conductors match exactly; discrepancies run in BOTH
  directions (small conductors: artin ~2x mf, e.g. 23: 2v1, 52: 4v2; and 85 levels carry
  1,296 weight-1 forms with NO artin rows at all). Narrative resistance: bidirectional
  disagreement is the signature of CONVENTION + COVERAGE mismatch (dual/conjugate counting
  conventions; artin_reps coverage holes; embedded-vs-orbit units), not of reciprocity
  failure — a genuine violation would be an existence gap, and there are none.
- Full-table context: 244,811 odd 2-dim rep rows across 163,954 conductors; weight-1
  newforms exist only at 2,437 levels — the raw tables live in different coverage boxes,
  and a naive full-table join "finds" 233,931 phantom counterexamples that are purely
  box-boundary artifacts (the selection-artifact lesson from MATH-0063, at higher stakes).
