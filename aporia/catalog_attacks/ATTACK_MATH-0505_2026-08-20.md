## Question and test spec (from triage)
Morton-Silverman uniform boundedness for preperiodic points of z^2+c. At degree-2 over Q
(D=1), Poonen's refined conjecture: at most 9 rational preperiodic points including the
fixed point at infinity — i.e. at most 8 AFFINE. Known extremal: c=-29/16.
UNIT CONVENTION (fixed per ELEN-P17 axis f): all counts in this artifact are AFFINE;
total = affine + 1 (the fixed point at infinity). Ceiling: 8 affine / 9 total.

## Method (and the bug that taught a theorem)
Exact Fraction arithmetic; candidates x=a/m for c=p/m^2 (denominator-structure theorem:
preperiodic rationals for c with denominator m^2 have denominator exactly m); escape radius
(1+sqrt(1+4|c|))/2; 64-iteration cycle detection. TWO failed runs preceded success:
run 1 (over-generous bounds, killed), run 2 hung 4.5 HOURS at zero output — diagnosis:
exact squaring doubles digit counts each iteration, so non-preperiodic orbits that neither
escape fast nor repeat produce numbers with ~2^40 digits. The fix is mathematics, not
engineering: preperiodic points have BOUNDED HEIGHT, so a 64-bit cap on numerator/denominator
is a sound rejection rule. The theorem repaired the performance bug. Post-guard runtime:
seconds.

## Result: SUPPORTIVE-CEILING, calibration exact
- 86 c values tested (c = p/m^2, m in 1..4, lowest terms, -4 <= c <= 1 range).
- CALIBRATION: c=-29/16 -> exactly 8 affine preperiodic points
  {+-1/4, +-3/4, +-5/4, +-7/4} — the known extremal orbit, integer-exact.
- Max affine count anywhere in sample: 8, attained at c=-29/16 AND c=-21/16 (both known
  8-point parameters). Six-point parameters: -13/9, -10/9, -45/16, -37/16, -13/16, -5/16.
- Nothing at 9+ affine: the sample SATURATES the conjectured ceiling exactly without
  breaching it — the strongest supportive shape (a ceiling that is touched is evidence the
  instrument can see the ceiling; a ceiling never approached would leave coverage doubt).

## Caveats
1. Sample: m <= 4, small |c| — the published Poonen verification vastly exceeds this;
   the reading is a calibration-grade consistency check, not new territory.
2. The falsification criterion's real frontier (growth with discriminant at fixed (d,D),
   number fields D > 1) needs a number-field harness — not attempted; noted for the queue.
3. 64-bit height cap: sound for rejection (preperiodic => small height at these c), but
   the exact height bound was not derived — 64 bits is generous by orders of magnitude.
