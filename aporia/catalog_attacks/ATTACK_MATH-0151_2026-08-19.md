## Question and test spec (from triage)
Chowla: C(h) = sum_{n<=N} mu(n)mu(n+h) = o(N) for every fixed h != 0. Effective exponent
alpha(h) = log|C(h)|/log N must stay below 1. Known: Tao 2016 log-averaged two-point case;
Helfgott computations to ~1e10. Novel axis per spec: 100 shifts measured simultaneously.
No mirror table needed — Mobius sieved from scratch (numpy, N+100 to 1e7).

## Pre-stated readings
- SUPPORTIVE-SQRT-CANCELLATION: all alpha(h) well below 1, clustering near the random-model
  reference ~0.5, stable or falling as N grows.
- CHALLENGE: any alpha(h) drifting toward 1 with N (instrument-bug-first).
- VACUOUS: sieve degenerate.

## Result: SUPPORTIVE-SQRT-CANCELLATION (all 100 shifts, both scales)
    N     | mean alpha | max alpha (shift)   | min    | random-model ref | shifts > 0.6
    1e6   | 0.4160     | 0.5450 (h=60)       | 0.2092 | ~0.464           | 0 of 100
    1e7   | 0.4346     | 0.5402 (h=20)       | 0.2304 | ~0.469           | 0 of 100
Every shift exhibits square-root-type cancellation; the WORST exponent is stable across a
tenfold N increase (0.545 -> 0.540 — no drift toward 1); the ensemble hugs the random-model
reference from below. Chowla-consistent across all 100 shifts simultaneously, extending the
single-shift computational literature in breadth (not height — Helfgott's 1e10 exceeds our N).

## Narrative resistance / caveats
1. Mean alpha slightly BELOW the 0.5 reference is expected small-N behavior (log corrections);
   no sub-random-cancellation claim is made.
2. N=1e7 is modest vs the 1e10 literature; the novel contribution is the simultaneous
   100-shift ENSEMBLE reading (no shift-dependent anomaly exists in this range — worst-shift
   identity even changes with N, h=60 -> h=20, i.e. noise, not structure).
3. Which-Chowla discipline (AA-037) applied: this attacks the correlation conjecture; central
   non-vanishing is a different theorem-space entirely.
