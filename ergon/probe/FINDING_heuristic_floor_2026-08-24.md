# A one-line heuristic beats the solver — the band's floor is not the chance floor

## 1. The measurement

A non-LLM control over the pinned `nearmiss_mix-M30` manifest, scored by lookup table with
cross-validation and re-scored on **400 fresh tasks from the same generator at a different
seed**:

```
predictor                                    CV acc    fresh-seed acc
count of the five integers coprime to 30      0.5700        0.5225
count ending in 1/3/7/9                       0.4100        0.4450
count odd                                     0.3900        0.4350
max digit length                              0.3800        0.4025

chance (majority class, 4 equiprobable answers)              0.2500
observed free-host solver accuracy, same manifest            0.4794
```

**"Count how many of the five numbers are not divisible by 2, 3, or 5" scores 0.5225 on unseen
tasks. The solver scores 0.4794.** A one-line arithmetic filter, with no primality reasoning of
any kind, beats the model by ~4pp — and it generalizes, so it is not a lookup table memorizing
this manifest.

Supporting controls, same run: a deterministic solver recovers gold from the prompt text at
**1.0000** (the family is well-posed and the gold is right), and uid-index/answer correlation is
**r = −0.0072** (the v1 index defect has not returned).

## 2. Why this matters more than it first looks

**The band was reasoned against the wrong floor.** `[0.35, 0.60]` exists to guarantee headroom —
that a solver is neither flooring out nor ceiling out, so residue has room to move the number.
The floor it was reasoned against is chance, 0.25. **The attainable-without-reasoning floor is
~0.52.** A solver at 0.48 is not "half-way up the difficulty curve"; it is *below the trivial
baseline*, and the movable mass we are measuring is not obviously mass that reasoning moves.

**The confound is now sized, not just named.** §6.3 already carries a `TOPIC-CONDITIONING` row
for the outcome `F-prom ≈ F-null ≈ F-generic ≫ F0`. This measurement puts a number on its
magnitude: residue that does nothing but remind the solver to filter small factors first could
move accuracy from 0.48 toward 0.52 — **≈+4pp, half the +8pp the probe is powered for**, with
zero metabolization. Note the D0 residue is rendered as a census of `METHOD_VOCAB`, and that
vocabulary *contains* `parity-or-last-digit` and `digit-sum-rule` — i.e. the packet names the
trivial filter by construction.

**Cleared, so it is not confused with the above:** the method census does **not** predict the
answer. Cross-validated 0.2900 against a permutation null whose 95th percentile is 0.2950 —
within the null. (The naive fit-and-score-on-the-same-rows number was 0.4000; I would have
reported a leak that is not there.)

## 3. The arm that should control this is itself confounded

`F-generic` exists precisely to separate "residue carries something specific" from "any on-topic
text primes the solver." But `ergon/probe/packet_invariants.py` — decidable checks, no
classifier — reports:

```
base_identical      TRUE    every arm's prompt ends with the F0 prompt, byte for byte
no_forbidden_tokens TRUE
no_verbatim_gold    TRUE
envelope_identical  FALSE   F-null and F-prom share ONE envelope; F-GENERIC HAS ANOTHER
```

F-generic is a prose paragraph; F-null and F-prom are bracketed records. **They are separable by
shape alone, no classifier required.** So the one comparison that would bound the
method-priming confound is confounded on a different axis.

## 4. What I propose, and what is yours

**Mine to note; yours to rule**, because both touch arms you sized:

- **(a) Give `F-generic` the residue envelope.** Same bracketed frame, generic content inside.
  Cheap, removes a decidable leak, and makes the specificity margin mean what it says.
- **(b) Add a deterministic `F-method-hint` arm** carrying *only* the trivial-filter instruction
  ("check divisibility by 2, 3 and 5 before testing primality") — no residue, no task-specific
  content, generated by a fixed string rather than by a model. **Its lift measures the
  method-priming confound directly**, which turns §2's ~+4pp estimate into a measured
  subtraction rather than a caveat. It is non-LLM by construction and costs one arm's calls.

I have not built either. (b) is a new arm, which is a design change, and (a) changes the sizing
of an arm whose sizing ruling I have already re-derived once today.

**If neither is adopted**, then any `Δ_carry` from this family must be reported with the
heuristic floor stamped beside it, and a positive result cannot distinguish metabolization from
method-priming on the evidence available.
