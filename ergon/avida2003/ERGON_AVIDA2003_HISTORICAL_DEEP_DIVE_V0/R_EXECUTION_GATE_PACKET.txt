1. THE PHYSICS, AND ONE FINDING WORTH THE PASS ON ITS OWN
======================================================================

environment.cfg assigns each of the nine tasks a reward exponent under
type=pow, so merit multiplies by 2^v:

    NOT 1   NAND 1   AND 2   OR_N 2   OR 3   AND_N 3   NOR 4   XOR 4   EQU 5

Supplementary section II independently publishes, for the same nine functions,
the MINIMUM NUMBER OF NAND OPERATIONS required to compute each, stated to have
been proven by exhaustive search:

    NOT 1   NAND 1   AND 2   OR_N 2   OR 3   AND_N 3   NOR 4   XOR 4   EQU 5

They are identical. The reward exponent IS the minimal NAND depth. The reward
landscape is a computational-depth ladder rather than an arbitrary bonus
schedule, and two independent artifacts agree on it. This is why the task and
reward rows in E are VERIFIED_EXACT for 2003 rather than merely for 2.2.

Also verified: ECHO is describable in the substrate but is NOT rewarded --
absent from the nine-function legend and absent from environment.cfg. A
reconstruction that rewards ECHO is not this specimen.

======================================================================
2. THE FINDING THAT INVERTS THE ASSIGNMENT
======================================================================

The directive asks whether modern instrumentation can recover a precursor
signal the original experiment was not designed to measure, and treats
one-step mutational-neighbourhood analysis as the modern addition.

IT IS NOT. Avida already had one.

source/main/landscape.{hh,cc} defines cLandscape, which enumerates a genome's
mutational neighbourhood and reports GetProbDead, GetProbNeg, GetProbNeut,
GetProbPos, mean and mean-square mutant fitness, and two-step epistasis
counts. Process(int in_distance = 1) makes the radius a PARAMETER, so k=2 is
supported. ProcessInsert and ProcessDelete give separate indel landscapes.

The original investigators could enumerate the adjacent possible in 2005, and
in more ways than Prometheus currently does.

THE ACTUAL BLIND SPOT, located precisely rather than assumed:

    landscape.hh contains ZERO references to tasks.
    landscape.cc touches cPhenotype at four places and every one reads a
    scalar -- GetFitness, GetMerit, GetGestationTime.

The historical analyzer is FITNESS-VALUED, not PHENOTYPE-PARTITIONED. It can
say what fraction of your neighbours are dead, worse, same or better. It
cannot say WHICH logic functions they gained or lost, how many distinct
phenotypes are reachable, or how that reachable set is distributed.

So the honest statement of what P-MED / R1 / H1 add is not "we can enumerate
the neighbourhood". It is:

    the historical instrument asked  "how good are my neighbours?"
    the modern one asks             "what can my neighbours DO that I cannot?"

Those differ only where fitness is not a monotone function of the phenotype
vector -- which, under a 2^v ladder over nine tasks, it is not.

Six historical detector parts are recorded in O for adoption, including the
distance parameterisation, the separate indel landscapes, the explicit
neutrality band, and the epistasis counters. Prometheus has no epistasis
instrument at all, and its own D-5 substrate has INSERT-complete physics yet
never separated indel from substitution neighbourhoods. Avida did, in 2005.

3. WHY H1A CANNOT RUN -- THE BINDING GAP
======================================================================

The first scientific target is a MATCHED comparison: g_succ(t) against 3-5
contemporary NON_EQU_DESCENDANT_CONTROL genotypes at the same generation,
matched on fitness, genome length and task count.

NO CONTEMPORARY GENOTYPE EXISTS IN ANY RECOVERED ARTIFACT. The supplementary
publishes the winning line of descent and nothing else. There is no organism
that was alive at the same update and did not lead to EQU.

Consequences, stated as they are:

  K6 (dominated by one run / survivorship) is ACTIVE NOW, not a risk. One
  population, successful lineage only, no base rate. Any metric computed today
  is a property of one survivor.

  K9 (signal is merely proximity to a near-EQU task combination) is
  UNTESTABLE. Excluding it requires post-EQU genotypes, which live in the
  345-genotype file we do not hold. A detector that cannot be checked against
  its most obvious confound should not be run.

  Of seven survivorship controls, two are available -- and both are
  label-permutation nulls that measure the detector's noise floor, not
  survivorship. Every control that actually addresses survivorship needs
  artifacts we lack.

Running H1A now would produce a metric table on three genotypes with no
controls and no base rate. That is not a weak experiment. It is an
uninterpretable one.

5. WHAT IS FROZEN, SO IT CANNOT BECOME A DEGREE OF FREEDOM LATER
======================================================================

Frozen BEFORE any accessibility metric was computed. None has been.

CHECKPOINTS ARE IN PHYLOGENETIC DEPTH, NOT UPDATES.
    A = pd 101, B = pd 61, C = pd 11.
    Reason: the two clocks are wildly non-linear. Depth steps 0-17 span updates
    0-1118 while single later steps span thousands. In updates, T_EQU - 100 is
    update 27350, which lies INSIDE the final depth step, collapsing all three
    checkpoints onto one or two genotypes.

MATCHED-CONTROL ESTIMATOR IS THE MEDIAN, not the mean, because control sets of
3-5 are small enough for one lethal-heavy outlier to dominate a mean.

RPD DENOMINATOR IS "VIABLE MUTATIONS". All three denominators are reported;
only this one enters any preregistered comparison. Rationale fixed in advance:
"all mutations" is dominated by the lethal fraction that P-MED already reports
separately; "novel viable" discards the silent class, which is the robustness
signal a precursor hypothesis might turn on.

N2 IS FROZEN FOR ALL GENOTYPES SIMULTANEOUSLY and may not be raised for one
that "looks interesting".

6. COMPUTE
======================================================================

Static analysis is not compute-bound. |M1(g)| = L * 25 = 1250 to 1525 mutants
per genotype; H1A over 12-18 genotypes is order 1e4 genome evaluations.

The dominant cost is NOT arithmetic, and it is unmeasured: seconds per Avida
genome evaluation, because Avida 2.2 HAS NOT BEEN COMPILED. It is 2005-era C++
with a CVS-era build. This is a BINARY risk -- either the historical evaluator
runs, or P-MED must be implemented against a reimplementation, which
immediately raises K8. Attempt the build BEFORE writing metric code, because
the outcome decides whether the analysis uses the historical evaluator or a
surrogate.

No H1B estimate is quoted, because with population size, mutation rates and
run length all UNSPECIFIED any number would be invented.

7. THE ORIGINAL QUESTION, AND OURS -- KEPT SEPARATE
======================================================================

Lenski et al. asked whether historically accumulated mutations, some
individually deleterious, were CAUSALLY INVOLVED in the later appearance of a
complex feature. They answered it with lineage reconstruction and reversion.
The recovered supplementary shows the evidence directly: many of the 111
transitions on the line of descent carry relative fitness below 1.00.

We ask a DIFFERENT question: whether ancestral genotypes had already acquired
a different distribution of REACHABLE FUTURE PHENOTYPES than fitness-matched
contemporaries that went nowhere.

That is a population-level accessibility question. It is not equivalent to
theirs and it does not retroactively bear on their causal analysis. Nothing in
this pass is a criticism of the 2003 experiment, and the strongest finding of
the pass is that their instrument was better than the directive assumed.
