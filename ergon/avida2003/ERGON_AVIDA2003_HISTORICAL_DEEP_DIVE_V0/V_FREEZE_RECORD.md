## The distinction frozen into the record

> **Reward-map degeneracy proves the detector channel CAN contain extra
> information. It does NOT prove Avida 2003 lacked an instrument capable of
> observing it.**

The first half is established from contemporaneous evidence and does not
depend on any version question: `task_set.extreme` (gzip mtime 2001-06-03)
gives multipliers 2,2,4,4,8,8,16,16,32 applied multiplicatively, so scalar
merit is many-to-one over the 9-bit phenotype. A phenotype-partitioned
neighbourhood can therefore carry information a scalar-fitness neighbourhood
cannot.

The second half is **SUSPENDED, not refuted**. The V0 claim that Avida already
had cLandscape came from version 2.2; the experiment used 1.6, which was not
recovered. If 1.6 had phenotype-aware neighbourhood machinery the historical
novelty claim shrinks or vanishes. If it had only scalar machinery, or none,
the historical contrast survives. We do not know, and no claim may rest on
either branch.

> neighbourhood reorganise in a way cheap scalar observables do not reveal?

returning to Avida becomes materially more interesting -- and at that point the
