machine-level reuse in the one consumer with a metered appetite. The
answer is that the consumer's appetite is for CONTENT, not for
ABSTRACTIONS, and this is a property of its interface rather than a
preference or a ranking. The D-5 library slot eats whole executable
genotypes used as mutation seeds. It has no application site, no splice
site, and no primitive-extension path. So the characteristic product of
Family A -- a reusable parameterised abstraction that becomes a new
primitive -- has nowhere to go AT ANY ACQUISITION PRICE. No primary arm
ran. Two independent gates stopped it, and the brief names both as
valid results.

Two findings sit in that table. Coverage is higher than expected at

with CTRL at 0/10 and F3 at 3/10 against F1 and F4 at 12/12. A
rewriter restricted to the jump-free fragment would be systematically
blind to specific families rather than uniformly weaker.

THE DEEPER BLOCKER, WHICH COVERAGE DOES NOT FIX. Equality saturation
returns terms EQUIVALENT to its input. Equivalent genotypes compute
the same function. D-5's admission is already behaviour-distinct
de-duplicated on output fingerprints. So egglog alone can supply, at
best, behaviourally redundant seeds to a slot that already dedupes on
behaviour -- measurable in principle, and predictably null. That is
not the Family-B hypothesis.

THE SMALLEST NEXT FAMILY-B CAPABILITY, stated so a successor builds
exactly this and nothing larger:

  BUILD an equational theory of RM-D5 over the JUMP-FREE fragment (11
  opcodes) that can (a) ingest a straight-line genotype, (b) saturate
  under a rule set derived from prior solved genotypes, and (c) emit
  the SHORTEST equivalent straight-line genotype.

  WHY SHORTEST: length is the only rewrite product that can move the
  D-5 readout. The slot eats whole genotypes as mutation seeds, so a
  shorter seed of identical behaviour sits in a different, smaller
  neighbourhood under the frozen mutation physics. That makes it a
  REACHABILITY claim, and reachability is what D-5 measures.

  ATTAINABLE RANGE FIRST (G3): compute the length reduction available
  on the jump-free fragment BEFORE building. If mean
  shortest-equivalent length equals mean witness length, the readout
  is bounded at zero and the build is disqualified exactly as Apollo's
  battery was.

  PREREGISTERED KILL: if shortest-equivalent seeds do not change
  per-task CFR against a LENGTH-MATCHED random-genotype control at
  identical metered budget, Family B is dead for this consumer however
  elegant its equivalence classes are.

  BLOCKED ON: nothing external. A build, not an acquisition.

15. THE NEXT EXPERIMENT, AND WHY IT IS NOT A DONOR ARM
====================================================================

PERSIST THE LIBRARIES, THEN VARY THE ADMISSION RULE.

Three independent lines meet here:

  - D-5 varied ORDER (shuffled history retained 100%) and CONTENT
    SOURCE (random library retained 39%) but NEVER varied the
    admission rule. "Solver + 4 behaviour-distinct" was frozen in
    every arm including both ablations.
  - Lexis G1 FIRED on exactly this: 5.94% load-bearing, "the admission
    criterion is the problem, not the primitives."
  - Gen-0's structural finding: the slot's appetite is for content,
    and the only lever its interface exposes is WHICH content enters.

So the question is whether choosing what to retain pays above the 39%
floor of retaining anything executable -- and whether an active
RETIREMENT rule beats keeping everything to the cap of 64. Every
Prometheus memory result to date is an accumulation result. No
experiment in this repository has ever measured a deletion. A memory
system that cannot forget is a log.

PRECONDITION: a D-5-class run that WRITES its libraries to disk. One
field in a row writer. Cheapest unblocking act available.

of the CFR readout under admission-rule variation first. If the range
