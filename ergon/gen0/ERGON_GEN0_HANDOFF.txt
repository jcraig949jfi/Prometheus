0. ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The assignment was to test APPETITE: does donor-derived accumulated
structure earn machine-level reuse in the one consumer that has a
metered proof of eating? The answer is that the consumer's appetite is
for CONTENT, not for ABSTRACTIONS, and this is a property of its
interface rather than a preference. The D-5 library slot eats whole
executable genotypes used as mutation seeds. It has no application
site, no splice site, and no primitive-extension path -- its opcode set
is 14, frozen, and dispatched from a hardcoded chain. So the
characteristic product of Family A (a reusable parameterised
abstraction that becomes a new primitive) has nowhere to go, AT ANY
ACQUISITION PRICE. The Family A / Family B cost asymmetry Techne
surfaced is real but it is not the operative question, because the
cheap family's product does not fit the only metered slot. No primary
arm ran. Two independent gates stopped it, and the brief names both as
valid results.

Two findings sit in that table. 68% of the corpus is reachable by an
equational rewriter -- more than expected. But CTRL is 0/10 and F3 is
3/10, so control flow is not uniformly distributed; it concentrates in
specific families, and a rewriter restricted to the jump-free fragment
would be systematically blind to those.

THE DEEPER BLOCKER, which coverage does not fix: equality saturation
returns terms EQUIVALENT to its input. Equivalent genotypes compute the
same function. D-5's admission is already behaviour-distinct-
deduplicated on output fingerprints. So egglog alone can supply, at
best, behaviourally redundant seeds to a slot that already dedupes on
behaviour -- measurable in principle, and predictably null. That is not
the Family-B hypothesis.


6. THE SMALLEST NEXT FAMILY-B CAPABILITY (precise requirement)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Written so a successor builds exactly this and nothing larger:

  BUILD: an equational theory of RM-D5 over the JUMP-FREE fragment (11
  opcodes: ADD AND MOD MOV MUL OR SET SHL SHR SUB XOR) that can
    (a) ingest a straight-line genotype,
    (b) saturate under a rule set derived from prior solved genotypes,
    (c) emit the SHORTEST equivalent straight-line genotype.

  WHY SHORTEST: length is the only rewrite product that can move the
  D-5 readout. The slot eats whole genotypes as mutation seeds, so a
  SHORTER seed of identical behaviour sits in a different, smaller
  neighbourhood under the frozen mutation physics. That makes it a
  REACHABILITY claim, and reachability is what D-5 measures.

  ATTAINABLE RANGE FIRST (G3): compute the length reduction available
  on the jump-free fragment BEFORE building. If mean shortest-
  equivalent length equals mean witness length, the readout is bounded
  at zero and the build is disqualified exactly as Apollo's battery
  was.

  PREREGISTERED KILL: if shortest-equivalent seeds do not change
  per-task CFR against a LENGTH-MATCHED random-genotype control at
  identical metered budget, Family B is dead for this consumer however
  elegant its equivalence classes are.

  BLOCKED ON: nothing external. This is a build, not an acquisition,
  and it needs no licence question resolved.

11. NEXT EXPERIMENT -- one, named, and cheap
---------------------------------------------------------------------
PERSIST THE LIBRARIES, THEN VARY THE ADMISSION RULE.

Rationale, and it is where three independent lines meet:
  - D-5 varied ORDER (shuffled: 100% retained) and CONTENT SOURCE
    (random library: 39% retained) but NEVER varied the admission rule.
    "Solver + 4 behaviour-distinct" was frozen in every arm including
    both ablations.
  - Lexis G1 FIRED on exactly this: 5.94% load-bearing, "the admission
    criterion is the problem, not the primitives."
  - Gen-0's structural finding: the slot's appetite is for content, and
    the only lever the interface exposes is WHICH content enters.

So the first real memory-metabolism experiment is not a donor arm. It
is: does the choice of what to retain pay, above the 39% floor of
retaining anything executable -- and does an active RETIREMENT rule
beat keeping everything to the cap of 64? Every Prometheus memory
result to date is an accumulation result; no experiment here has ever
measured a deletion.

PRECONDITION: a D-5-class run that WRITES its libraries to disk. That
is the cheapest unblocking act available and it costs one field in a
row writer.
