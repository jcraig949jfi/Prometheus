1. THE QUESTION PUT TO THIS PASS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Did Kouvaris 2017 already measure longitudinal change in a population's
reachable phenotype distribution under an intervention, and causally
relate it to subsequent acquisition, strongly enough to make HC-T01
redundant?

Answer: NO. But the reasons are not the ones the programme expected, and
FOUR of the findings below count AGAINST HC-T01, one of them a correction
to this pass forced by another seat.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT THE SPECIMEN ACTUALLY DID
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Physics. A single genotype [G,B] under strong-selection-weak-mutation.
G is 16 embryonic gene-expression values; B is a 16x16 regulatory matrix
that develops G into an adult phenotype over T steps of a recurrent
non-linear map. Targets are 8 patterns built from 4 independent binary
modules; 3 are selected for (training), 5 are never selected (test).
Selection is benefit minus lambda times a cost on B.

Detector. Draw 5000 points uniformly in the cube [-1,1]^16 using a
scrambled Sobol sequence. Develop each through the CURRENT B. Bin by
sign. Count against target patterns. Compare by chi-squared against the
training set and against the whole class. Repeat for every stored B
along the trajectory.

Interventions, all four of them:
  environmental switching interval K
  Gaussian noise on the target, scale kappa
  L2 cost lambda * sum b_ij^2
  L1 cost lambda * sum |b_ij|

Acquisition. Freeze the evolved B, re-randomise G, evolve G alone for
2500 generations, count generations to target, 1000 runs per target per
arm, all 8 targets, failures censored at the ceiling.

4. D-LADDER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D0  no accessibility measure ................. exceeded
D1  selected individuals only ................ exceeded
D2  snapshot accessibility .................... ACHIEVED

    subsequent acquisition ................... NOT ACHIEVED

D5 fails for three independent reasons, each sufficient:
  (a) No mediation. The knobs change fitness, fitness changes B, and
      BOTH readouts are then read off B. Common cause is not excluded.
  (b) The two readouts are deterministic functionals of the SAME frozen
      B over the SAME genotype space through the SAME map. If B cannot
      express a phenotype from anywhere, hill-climbing under B will not
      find it either. Their agreement is close to definitional. The
      paper notices the agreement and reports it correctly as a
      consistency check, which is what it is.
  (c) No run-level uncertainty exists to support a causal claim.

6. WHERE THE SPECIMEN IS STRONGER THAN HC-T01
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Recorded because the directive forbids protecting HC-T01.

  1. NON-TAUTOLOGICAL INTERVENTION. No Kouvaris knob touches the

  4. SAME-PROBE COUNTERFACTUAL by construction: one fixed 5000-point
     Sobol set reused at every epoch and in every arm.

7. FOUR FINDINGS THAT DAMAGE HC-T01
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7a. AN A-LOCAL DETECTOR ALREADY EXISTED IN THIS LINEAGE AND WAS NEVER
    REPORTED. The recovered repository
    KKouvaris/Phenotypic-Plasticity-Logical-Inference contains
    computeM.m and evalMutCorr.m: 300 SINGLE mutations applied to EVERY
    individual in a population, mutant phenotypes developed, mutational
    effect vectors differenced, mutational variance and pairwise
    mutational correlations computed. The mutation draws are generated
    once, saved, and REUSED ACROSS REPLICATES. That is a working,
    population-wide, same-probe, LOCAL detector. It appears in no
    publication and ZERO times in 159 pages of the thesis. It is
    endpoint-only; making it longitudinal is a loop, not a research
    problem.
    Consequence: the one-step offspring detector is not an instrument
    the field lacked the capability to build. HC-T01's novelty is a
    COMPOSITION AND REPORTING novelty, not an instrument novelty.
    Note this is the SECOND time this programme has found unreported
    detector code beside a published experiment that omits it; the
    first was Toussaint's own avgfit. Two is a pattern, and it weakens
    every argument of the form "nobody measured X".

7b. THE FORWARD SEARCH FOUND THE COMPOSITION, QUALIFIED.
    102 distinct citing works enumerated (OpenAlex 106 records, Semantic
    Scholar cross-check), 18 screened in full. Two carry the conclusion
    and both were re-verified from primary source by this seat, not
    taken from a summary.

    TISO 2024, thesis ch.3, Groningen, "The evolution of mutational
    transformers". A-local, verified quote: "produced by 1000 different
    mutations at each locus of the best-performing GRN at a certain time
    point". B-within-run, verified: "Distributions of the phenotypic
    effects of mutations at four different time points during the
    simulation". C-mechanism: arms are GRN architecture x transfer
    function with environment and selection held fixed. D-within-run:
    descriptive link between detector state and recovery speed early
    versus late in the same run.
    HAS ALL FOUR ELEMENTS. Its three defects are what leave HC-T01
    anything: the detector is NOT reported for the trivial network,
    which is the negative-control arm; the within-run link is
    descriptive, n=1, over "a representative simulation", with no
    statistic relating detector at t to adaptation at t+n; and it was
    never published.

    PETAK ET AL. 2025, PNAS. A-local with the REAL operator, verified
    quote: "Offspring were generated with the same mutation operation
    used in the evolutionary runs". B-within-run, verified: the axes are
    "the slopes of the lines of best fit of the percentage of offspring
    with higher fitness than the parent after each environmental change
    over time". 15 replicates with 95 percent confidence intervals.
    Intervention is selection-side; acquisition link is arm-level.
    Strictly stronger than the specimen on A and B, and published.

    KOUNIOS ET AL. 2016, arXiv 1612.05955, same lineage, four months
    EARLIER than the specimen. A genuine representational on/off
    ablation: one-to-one G-P map versus evolving GRN, with the mutation
    operator on G identical in both. Longitudinal outcome in both arms
    over 30 replicates with min/max bands and Mann-Whitney U tests. It
    also tracks a real accessibility quantity over evolutionary time,
    the developmental basin of attraction, "the number of G vectors that
    map into the concentric squares phenotype". Still not the
    composition: the basin detector runs in ONE unablated condition, and
    in the ablated arms the longitudinal quantity IS the outcome.

10. THE DIRECTIVE'S FINAL QUESTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If Prometheus had existed in April 2017 and been handed the complete
Kouvaris experiment, what question about changes in future computational
accessibility would still have remained unanswered?

NOT "none". Three, in the order that matters:

  1. Does what is reachable FROM WHERE THE LINEAGE STANDS track what the
     map can express FROM ANYWHERE? Only the second is measured. In this
     model the one-step neighbourhood is a perturbation of at most 0.1
     on one of sixteen coordinates, a vanishing fraction of the cube the
     detector samples. The two can move independently and nothing in the
     paper would notice if they did.
  2. Is the accessibility change a CAUSE of the acquisition change or a
     co-symptom of the same change in B? The design cannot tell.
  3. Does any of it exceed the noise of a single run? Unknown; no
     replication was reported.

Question 1 is what HC-T01 proposes to answer. Question 2 is what
Prometheus most wants and is hardest. Question 3 is cheap and a
reconstruction settles it first.

11. WHAT WAS FOUND WORTH STEALING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Seven detector parts and three mechanism parts are recorded in
L_HISTORICAL_DETECTOR_PARTS.jsonl and
M_CANDIDATE_COMPUTATIONAL_PARTS.jsonl. The four that matter:

  FIXED SHARED PROBE. Draw the sampling randomness once, reuse it at
  every checkpoint and in every arm. Removes probe noise from every
  contrast for free. HC-T01 specifies a sample count but not shared
  draws; it should.

  ENTROPY WITH A STRUCTURALLY DERIVED TARGET. For k independent modules
  the generalising optimum is EXACTLY k bits. A gate with an attainable
  range computed before the run, which this literature almost never
  supplies and which Prometheus doctrine demands.

  CENSORED ACQUISITION. Report runs that never reach the target
  separately from the median time of those that do. Distinguishes three
  outcome types instead of one.

  KEEP THE REJECTED MUTANTS. The specimen's own SSWM loop generated one
  real one-step offspring sample per generation for 9e6 generations and
  discarded every one. Logging them costs one write per generation and
  converts the paper's own design into an A-local longitudinal detector
  with NO change to the physics. This is the single highest-value change
  available and it was available in 2017.

12. RECONSTRUCTION PROPOSAL, NOT AUTHORISED, NOT RUN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ranked by expected information gain:
  1 DETECTOR CALIBRATION. Reconstruct the four arms, hand a Prometheus
    detector the trajectories BLIND to arm, ask it to rank arms by
    generalisation and locate the over-fitting change point. Ground
    truth is published and was set by someone else. A detector that
    cannot recover a known strong effect is not ready for an unknown
    one.
  2 COMPOSITION TEST, nearly free on top of 1: log rejected mutants,
    obtain the A-local longitudinal detector, ask whether local
    accessibility moves before global expressibility.
  3 CONVERT TREATMENT-LEVEL TO WITHIN-RUN: freeze B at several points
    ALONG one run and run the paper's own Fig 5 assay at each. No new
    instrument. The authors were one sweep away from this.
  4 positive control (by-product of 1).
  5 transfer test under held-out worlds. NOT recommended now; it would
    test a Prometheus construct rather than recover a historical one.

BLOCKER: ambiguity 1 must be resolved first. If G is clamped to the
target in the main runs, as the committed code has it, then there is no
stream of rejected G mutants and rank 2 does not exist.

Validation targets fixed in advance: entropy converges to 4 bits under
L1; untrained map reads 16 bits; L1 generalisation error reaches zero
over ln(lambda) in [0.15,0.35] and fails at 0.4. Primary targets scored
against the reconstruction's own run-to-run spread, with the spread
computed BEFORE any tolerance is chosen.

Cost: CPU-hours to CPU-days. No GPU, no model in the loop, no external
service.

13. THE COMPOSITION CANDIDATE, RECORDED AND NOT RUN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Toussaint supplies a variation operator that can be switched off and a
detector of what that operator actually produces. Kouvaris supplies a
selection regime that provably shapes the machinery producing future
variation, plus a constructed class with held-out environments.

The composition: variation machinery that changes itself under selection
pressures favouring generalisable future variation.

Two warnings recorded with it. The tautology hazard COMPOUNDS if the
operator's own parameters are heritable and the detector is the
distribution that operator induces. And the coupling hazard persists
unless acquisition is scored on genuinely held-out environments, which
is the Kouvaris half. The two halves need each other.
