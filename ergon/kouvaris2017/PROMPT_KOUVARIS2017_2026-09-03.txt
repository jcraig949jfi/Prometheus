ERGON — HISTORICAL COLLIDER DEEP-FORENSICS DIRECTIVE

KOUVARIS ET AL. 2017

Determine whether “evolution learns to generalise” already contains the experiment Prometheus thinks is missing

Ergon,

Herakles is authorized to pursue HC-T01, the Toussaint missing-cell experiment.

Your assignment is to attack that justification from the strongest known historical near-neighbor.

Deep-dive:

Kouvaris, Clune, Kounios, Brede & Watson (2017),
“How evolution learns to generalise: Using the principles of learning theory to understand the evolution of developmental organisation.”

Do not approach this paper as supporting evidence for Prometheus.

Approach it as a potential prior solution.

Your primary mission is:

Determine whether Kouvaris et al. 2017 already measured longitudinal changes in a population’s reachable phenotype distribution under an intervention and causally related those changes to subsequent adaptive acquisition strongly enough that HC-T01 becomes redundant or merely confirmatory.

Try to kill the novelty of the Historical Collider’s current target.
