1. ESTIMAND
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The causal effect of a RETENTION (eviction) POLICY on future capability,
INCLUDING the downstream search trajectory that the policy induces.

NOT the effect of library composition holding candidate generation fixed.
Exact stream freezing is architecturally impossible under the frozen D-5
consumer; this is an accepted limitation, established in Gen-1 and ruled on
in Gen-1A.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
2. INDEPENDENT UNIT AND DESIGN
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Unit of inference        LINEAGE
n                        30 paired lineages per arm
Pairing                  lineage L uses nav_seed base 200000 + 1000*L in
                         EVERY arm, so arms are paired on starting stream
Task corpus              the 42 non-control D-5 tasks (families F1-F4),
                         identical and in identical order in every arm
Budget                   30,000 evaluations per task, every arm
Library cap              64, every arm
Admission                frozen m1.admissions (solver + up to 4
                         behaviour-distinct), every arm
Search core              frozen m1.m1_rx, imported unmodified
Mutation operators       frozen physics.mutate

----------------------------------------------------------------------
3. ARMS -- EVICTION IS THE ONLY TREATMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------------
I0  D5_BASELINE_MRU
    Evict the oldest resident (list position 0). Inherited semantics.

I1  MUT_REDUNDANT
    Evict a resident that is redundant BOTH behaviourally AND mutationally:
    it shares an exact behaviour fingerprint with another resident AND their
    offspring-behaviour sketches have Jaccard >= 0.50. Among qualifying
    residents evict the older. If none qualify, fall back to MRU.
    Sketch: 32 matched mutations (seed 4242) evaluated on the shared 64-point
    domain, computed ONCE at admission and cached.
    REPAIRED after Phase 1: behavioural duplicates are usually NOT
    evolutionarily redundant (median MR 0.333; only 23% of duplicate pairs
    reach tau=0.50; median genotype edit distance 20 of 24).

I2  EFFECTIVE_USAGE
    Evict the resident with fewest EFFECTIVE USES among residents with >= 100
    draw opportunities; if none qualify, minimise over all residents.
    Ties broken by insertion order.
    GRACE = 100 draws, taken from the Phase 2 exposure curve, where
    P(>=1 effective use) is 0.02-0.25 below 150 draws and 0.67+ above.

I3  RANDOM
    Evict a uniformly random resident. The arbitrary-memory comparator.

DIAGNOSTIC ONLY, NOT SCORED: raw draw-count / LRU_DRAW, and pure behavioural
redundancy. Both are reported in simulation only.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
4. EFFECTIVE USE -- FROZEN DEFINITION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
A library draw is CREDITED to the drawn artifact when the child produced by
mutating it scores strictly better (lower distance) than the MEDIAN of the
last 32 scored candidates.

Online, learner-visible, no oracle field, no future information. The window
matches the frozen psize. Credit is linked by observing three points --
library draw, mutate(parent -> child), FastTask.dist(child) -- with no change
to control flow, randomness or the evaluation counter.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
5. PRIMARY ENDPOINT AND ANALYSIS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Primary endpoint     per-lineage CFR = fraction of the 42 tasks solved
                     within the 30,000-evaluation budget
Primary contrasts    I1 - I0, I2 - I0, I3 - I0   (vs inherited policy)
                     I1 - I3, I2 - I3            (vs arbitrary memory)
Test                 two-sided paired sign-flip permutation on the 30
                     lineage-level deltas, 20000 permutations, seed 424242
Uncertainty          lineage-level SD and SE; 90% percentile bootstrap CI
                     resampling LINEAGES
Preregistered MDE    1.9 pp at n=30 (Gen-1A power analysis, sigma 0.0412)
Multiplicity         5 contrasts; Holm-Bonferroni across the five, reported
                     alongside uncrorrected p-values. A contrast is called
                     ONLY if it survives Holm.

Analysis is fixed here and will not be altered after outcomes are seen.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
6. SECONDARY / MECHANISM MEASURES (exploratory, never promoted)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
terminal behavioural diversity; terminal distinct offspring behaviours;
eviction count; MRU-fallback rate; total library draws; effective-use total;
artifacts with >=1 effective use; final library median age; effective-use
concentration; per-artifact draw/credit/admission records; eviction log with
age, draws and credit at eviction.

Compression, diversity and divergence measures are DESCRIPTIVE. None may be
promoted to the primary endpoint if the primary is null.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
7. COMPUTE ACCOUNTING
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Scientific budget is identical across arms: 42 tasks x 30,000 evaluations x
30 lineages = 37,800,000 organism-evaluations per arm, 151,200,000 total.

POLICY COMPUTE, charged separately and NOT free:
  I0, I3   negligible (index selection)
  I2       two integer counters per artifact
  I1       32 mutations + 32 fingerprint evaluations per admitted artifact,
           measured at ~5,395 extra mutations per lineage in simulation.
           This is reported, not hidden, per the no-free-intelligence rule.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
8. STOPPING RULES
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The run is fixed-length: 30 lineages x 4 arms. There is no interim look, no
optional stopping, and no extension of n after seeing results. If the run
cannot complete, whatever lineages have completed are reported with their
count, and the primary analysis is declared underpowered rather than
reinterpreted.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
9. DECLARED DEVIATIONS AND SHAKEDOWN
----------------------------------------------------------------------
A 2-lineage, 3000-evaluation engineering shakedown of the runner was executed
before this freeze, to a SEPARATE output directory (_smoke), to confirm the
four arms execute and produce differing CFR. It is not part of the analysis
set and its outputs are discarded. Disclosed rather than omitted.

The corpus used for Phases 1, 1b and 2 is the Gen-1A baseline corpus over the
same 42 tasks, 5 lineages, generated under I0 only.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
10. WHAT WOULD MAKE THIS RUN UNINTERPRETABLE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  - if any arm's admission stream mechanics differ from I0 (checked in code:
    admission is the same frozen call in every arm)
  - if an arm retains more than 64 artifacts (checked: single shared cap)
  - if effective-use credit consults any future or oracle information
    (checked: credit uses only the trailing 32-candidate window)
  - if the pairing is broken (checked: nav_seed base is a function of lineage
    index only)
