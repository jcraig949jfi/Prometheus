2026-09-01. Self-contained external-review packet. Written for a reviewer who

HEADLINE

    Choosing what experience survives DID change future capability.
    A preregistered retention policy beat the inherited one by +2.78 pp
    (p = 0.0008, Holm 0.0040, n = 30 paired lineages), above the
    preregistered 1.9 pp MDE.

    It did NOT beat ARBITRARY memory after correction (+1.51 pp,
    Holm 0.0912). The design set that second bar precisely to separate
    "better than history" from "better than arbitrary", and it was not
    cleared.

    The strongest defensible statement is that THE INHERITED MRU POLICY IS
    THE WORST OF THE FOUR TESTED, not that principled selection beats
    arbitrary eviction.

1. MUTATIONAL REDUNDANCY AUDIT (Phase 1)
======================================================================

Gen-1A reported 487 of 834 artifacts (58%) duplicating another artifact's
behaviour fingerprint. The brief asked whether that is EVOLUTIONARY
redundancy. It is not.

PRECONDITION FIRST, AND IT COULD HAVE VOIDED THE NUMBER. A fingerprint is the
program's output over the task's input domain, so cross-task comparison is
meaningful only if the domain is shared. Verified: every D-5 task in F1-F4 uses
the identical 64-point domain (0..63). Had it not, the 58% figure would have
been an artifact of comparing different domains and would have been withdrawn.

MUTATIONAL REDUNDANCY, defined and preregistered before use:

    MR(a,b) = Jaccard( N_K(a), N_K(b) )

N_K(x) is the set of distinct offspring behaviour fingerprints from K = 120
MATCHED mutations of x -- every artifact receives the same seed and therefore
the same random stream, so differences are attributable to the genotype.

RESULT over 6,505 same-fingerprint pairs (585 artifacts in duplicate groups):

    MR   min 0.029   p25 0.200   median 0.333   p75 0.429   max 1.000
    median genotype edit distance between duplicates ...... 20 of max 24
    operator-matched neighbourhood Jaccard (median) ....... 0.500
    distinct offspring behaviours per artifact (median) ... 6
    offspring behaviourally NOVEL vs parent (median) ...... 83.3%
    P(strict improvement) on an 8-task panel (median) ..... 0.0344

    threshold sweep -- duplicates that are ALSO evolutionarily redundant
        tau 0.20 -> 81.5%      tau 0.50 -> 23.0%  (preregistered)
        tau 0.30 -> 52.2%      tau 0.70 ->  4.1%
        tau 0.40 -> 34.5%      tau 0.90 ->  2.7%

SO: 77% of the apparent redundancy does NOT survive the stronger test.
Behavioural duplicates are typically near-maximally different PROGRAMS that
happen to compute the same function, and they open onto substantially
different futures. Evicting one destroys roughly two thirds of a distinct
mutation neighbourhood.

    SAME PHENOTYPE != SAME MUTATIONAL AFFORDANCES.  Confirmed.

I1 therefore took the brief's option A and was REPAIRED to require redundancy
BOTH behaviourally AND mutationally, using a 32-mutation offspring sketch
cached at admission. Had Phase 1 been skipped, the arm would have run under a
name it did not deserve.

I1 SIMULATED BEFORE FREEZE (Phase 1b), so it was not a ceremonial arm:
    terminal Jaccard vs baseline 0.664 (pure-behavioural variant 0.546)
    MRU-fallback rate 0.597
    terminal behavioural diversity 0.878 (behav-only 1.000, MRU 0.706)
    distinct reachable offspring behaviours 176 (behav-only 187, MRU 144)

======================================================================

Is the credit counter secretly an exposure counter?

    Spearman  effective uses vs draw opportunities ....... +0.350
              effective uses vs residence .............. +0.373
              effective uses vs admission position ..... -0.339
              draws          vs residence .............. +0.792
              RATE           vs draw opportunities ..... +0.230

Moderate, not dominant. And the zero population is mostly real:

    zero-effective-use artifacts ......................... 352 of 843 (42%)
    of those, WITH >= 100 draw opportunities ............. 227 (64%)
    median draws   zero-credit 206   vs   nonzero-credit 302
    median residence  zero 12.0 tasks  vs  nonzero 15.0 tasks

So 64% of the zero population is well exposed and still produced nothing --
genuinely inert, not merely young.

EXPOSURE STILL MATTERS, and the gradient is steep:

    draw bin      n     P(>=1 effective use)   median rate
    0             31         0.000               0.0000
    1-49          51         0.020               0.0000
    50-149        91         0.253               0.0000
    150-299      313         0.671               0.0056
    300-599      320         0.706               0.0033
    600+          37         0.838               0.0039

DECISIVE FOR THE EVICTION MARGIN. Eviction only ever touches the bottom of the
ranking, and there:

    bottom-64 by COUNT vs by RATE ............ overlap 64 / 64
    bottom-64 by COUNT vs by empirical-Bayes . overlap 50 / 64

Count and rate select the IDENTICAL eviction set, because zero is zero under
either definition. Opportunity normalisation would not change a single
eviction, so none was adopted -- the minimal repair is no repair.

ONE GUARD WAS ADDED. Because P(>=1 effective use) is 0.02-0.25 below 150 draws,
I2 evicts only among residents with >= 100 draw opportunities, falling back to
the whole library if none qualify. GRACE = 100 was read off the exposure curve
above, not off CFR, and was frozen before the run.

3. FINAL POLICY DEFINITIONS
======================================================================

Eviction is the ONLY treatment. Admission (m1.admissions), search core
(m1.m1_rx), mutation operators, cap 64, task corpus, budget and scoring are
frozen and identical in every arm.

I0  D5_BASELINE_MRU
    Evict the oldest resident. Inherited semantics.

I1  MUT_REDUNDANT
    Evict a resident redundant BOTH behaviourally (identical fingerprint to
    another resident) AND mutationally (offspring-sketch Jaccard >= 0.50),
    older first. Fall back to MRU when no pair qualifies.
    Sketch: 32 matched mutations, seed 4242, computed once at admission.

I2  EFFECTIVE_USAGE
    Evict the resident with fewest effective uses among those with >= 100 draw
    opportunities; if none qualify, minimise over all. Ties by insertion order.

I3  RANDOM
    Evict a uniformly random resident. The arbitrary-memory comparator.

EFFECTIVE USE, frozen: a library draw is credited when the child it produced
scores strictly better than the median of the last 32 scored candidates.
Online, learner-visible, no oracle field, no future information. Credit is
linked by observing three points the frozen core keeps apart -- library draw,
mutate(parent -> child), FastTask.dist(child) -- with no change to control
flow, randomness or the evaluation counter.

7. STREAM AND LIBRARY DIVERGENCE -- THE MOST IMPORTANT NEGATIVE
======================================================================

MEASURED TERMINAL LIBRARY OVERLAP between arms, 30 lineages, Jaccard on
artifact identity:

    I0-I1  0.003     I1-I2  0.005
    I0-I2  0.006     I1-I3  0.003
    I0-I3  0.003     I2-I3  0.045

Essentially disjoint. But the control that matters:

    SAME ARM, DIFFERENT LINEAGE
    I0 0.002    I1 0.003    I2 0.005    I3 0.002

BETWEEN-POLICY divergence is INDISTINGUISHABLE FROM WITHIN-POLICY divergence.
Two libraries built by the same policy on different seeds share as little as
two libraries built by different policies. At the level of artifact IDENTITY,
the retained library is essentially unique to a run and carries no recoverable
policy signature.

This is why the estimand was narrowed in Gen-1A to "policy + induced
trajectory". The measurement now shows that narrowing was not a formality: the
trajectory component dominates identity-level composition completely.

WHAT DOES CARRY A POLICY SIGNATURE is the DISTRIBUTION, not the identities --
see section 8. The libraries share no artifacts but differ systematically in
their statistics.

DECLARED GAP. The brief's Phase 4 asked for first candidate-stream divergence
index and candidate overlap. The frozen runner records per-artifact and
per-library state, NOT per-candidate genotypes, and it was hashed before the
run, so adding that logging mid-experiment was not available. Library-level
divergence is reported; candidate-level divergence is NOT measured. Listed
again in section 14.

8. MECHANISM AUTOPSY
======================================================================

Means over 30 lineages.

    measure                                  I0       I1       I2       I3
    terminal distinct behaviours            44.1     54.8     45.3     46.3
    terminal reachable offspring behaviours   --    165.4      --       --
    evictions                              102.2    102.8    102.1    101.4
    MRU fallbacks                            0.0     62.0      0.0      0.0
    library draws                          47868    47052    47955    47628
    total effective uses                   385.3    417.9    421.7    403.9
    artifacts with >= 1 effective use       92.8     91.7     72.9     84.1
    distinct artifacts admitted            165.9    166.6    166.0    165.1
    terminal median artifact age (tasks)     8.0      8.9     15.3     11.3
    top-artifact share of terminal credit   0.158    0.149    0.087    0.161

EVERY POLICY DID WHAT IT WAS DESIGNED TO DO.
  - I1 raised terminal behavioural diversity from 44.1 to 54.8 distinct
    behaviours, using its redundancy rule on about 40% of evictions (62 of 103
    fell back to MRU because no pair met both criteria).
  - I2 more than doubled terminal artifact age, 15.3 tasks against 8.0, and cut
    credit concentration nearly in half (0.087 vs 0.158).
  - Admission was untouched: all four arms admit ~166 distinct artifacts and
    perform ~102 evictions. The treatment really was eviction only.

BUT MEDIATION IS NOT ESTABLISHED. Across lineages, the association between a
mechanism delta and the CFR delta:

    I1 - I0                                  I2 - I0
    terminal distinct behaviours  r +0.159   terminal behaviours   r +0.212
    terminal median age           r +0.134   terminal median age   r +0.123
    artifacts with >=1 eff use    r +0.026   artifacts w/ eff use  r +0.010
    total effective uses          r -0.168   total effective uses  r -0.491
    library draws                 r -0.697   library draws         r -0.837

The property I1 was built to change -- behavioural diversity -- moved
decisively in the mean but correlates only r = +0.16 with the CFR gain across
lineages. Diversity preservation is the confirmed PROXIMATE change; it is not
shown to be the MECHANISM.

THE STRONGEST CORRELATE IS AN ARTEFACT, AND IS REPORTED AS ONE. Library draws
correlate at r = -0.70 and -0.84 with the CFR delta. That is reverse causation:
solving a task early terminates the run and therefore consumes fewer draws.
Fewer draws is an OUTCOME of solving, not a cause of it. It is the largest
number in the autopsy and it means nothing.

10. EXPERIENCE HALF-LIFE
======================================================================

Separate observational pass, baseline policy, 5 lineages x 42 tasks, 827
artifacts, 2,045 timestamped effective-use events. Not part of the Gen-1
analysis set.

THERE IS NO HALF-LIFE. Usefulness does not decay with use; the discrete
hazard RISES and then plateaus.

    h(j) = P(reach use j+1 | reached use j)

    j     risk set   continued   h(j)
    0        827        460      0.556
    1        460        324      0.704
    2        324        244      0.753
    3        244        177      0.725
    4        177        146      0.825
    5        146        117      0.801
    6        117         94      0.803
    7         94         73      0.777
    8         73         58      0.795
    9         58         49      0.845

The only steep transition is the FIRST one: 44% of artifacts never earn a
single credit, but an artifact that has earned two has a ~75-85% chance of
earning another, indefinitely. This is a threshold-then-plateau structure, not
exponential decay.

    CORRECTION, disclosed. A first version of this estimator binned artifacts
    by their FINAL use count and asked whether they had "more", which is
    definitionally zero in every bin and manufactured a spurious step at j = 2.
    It was rewritten as the risk-set hazard above before anything was reported.

TIMING AND RESURRECTION

    ever effectively used ............................. 460 of 827 (56%)
    lag admission -> first effective use ........ median 5 tasks, p90 12, max 18
    gap between consecutive uses ................ median 0, p90 3, max 15
    gaps of 0 tasks (same-task bursts) ............... 76.5%
    gaps >= 5 tasks (dormant, then revived) ........... 7.2%
    artifacts showing a revival after >= 5 dormant tasks  111 (24% of used)

SUBPOPULATIONS -- three, not a continuum
    never used ....... 367 (44%)  median 210 draws  -- well exposed, inert
    used once ........ 136 (16%)  median 307 draws
    used 5+ times .... 146 (18%)  median 329 draws, median span 6 tasks
    top 10% of artifacts hold 53% of all effective uses

WHAT FORGETTING SHOULD MEAN HERE, empirically. Evict on ZERO credit after
adequate exposure: h(0) = 0.556 means 44% never start, and they are exposed
enough to be judged. Do NOT evict on dormancy: once an artifact has been useful
twice its hazard is ~0.8 and stays there, and 24% of used artifacts revive
after five or more silent tasks. A hand-picked "retire after N idle" rule would
delete exactly the wrong population.

11. MEMORY TRANSPLANT -- FOLLOW-ON DESIGN (not executed)
======================================================================

WHY. Gen-1's estimand includes the induced trajectory, so a winning policy may
mean a better persistent substrate OR a luckier path. Section 7 makes this
urgent rather than theoretical: retained libraries carry no identity-level
policy signature at all.

DESIGN. Freeze each arm's terminal library. Disable admission and eviction
entirely. Hand it to a fresh searcher on 20 held-out ALIEN tasks no lineage
ever developed against. Same searcher, same budget, same tasks, same fresh
stream; the only difference is which 64 artifacts it starts with. No trajectory
remains to take credit.

    unit          library (one per lineage per arm), paired by lineage index
    n             30 per arm
    battery       20 held-out ALIEN tasks
    endpoint      held-out CFR
    contrasts     I1 - I0, I1 - I3, each arm - EMPTY, each arm - RANDOM_LIBRARY
    test          paired sign-flip permutation, Holm
    controls      EMPTY library; RANDOM-GENOTYPE library size-matched to 64;
                  SHUFFLED-CONTENT library sampled across arms to break the
                  policy-content relation while holding size fixed
    cost          ~20 minutes wall, ~72M organism-evaluations, $0

PRIOR, STATED BEFORE RUNNING: D-5's G7 measured frozen alien transfer at +5 pp,
p = 0.26, NOT ESTABLISHED. The transplant runs on an axis where the
accumulation effect itself did not transfer. A null is the most likely outcome
and should be powered for.

STOP CONDITIONS: if no arm separates from I0 there is no superior policy to
transplant and the design collapses to a re-test of G7; if held-out CFR is at
floor or ceiling for every arm including the empty control, report
ALIEN_BATTERY_UNINFORMATIVE.

A transplant null would NOT show the Gen-1 effect was unreal. It would show the
effect did not survive separation from its trajectory. Narrower, different.

13. CLAIM CEILING
======================================================================

  - +2.78 pp is one extra task in forty-two. It is a real, preregistered,
    Holm-surviving effect and it is small in absolute terms. Both halves of
    that sentence travel together.
  - "Selective retention beats arbitrary retention" IS NOT SHOWN. I1 - I3 does
    not survive correction. Only "beats the inherited MRU rule" is shown.
  - Mechanism is unresolved. Diversity preservation is the confirmed proximate
    change, not a demonstrated mediator (r = +0.16).
  - Identity-level library composition carries no policy signature: within-arm
    divergence equals between-arm divergence. Any future claim that a policy
    produces "a better library" must be made about DISTRIBUTIONS, never about
    which artifacts are in it.
  - The treatment is policy PLUS its induced trajectory. Nothing here isolates
    library content. That is what section 11 exists to test.
  - Everything is measured on one consumer, one substrate, one 42-task battery
    with a shared 64-point domain, at one budget. No claim of generality.
  - The hazard analysis is descriptive, from a single-policy observational
    pass, and is not a causal claim about what retirement rules would do.
  - No capability, cognition or intelligence claim is made anywhere. What was
    measured is the probability that a fixed search finds exact solutions
    within a metered budget.

15. RECOMMENDATION FOR THE NEXT GENERATION
======================================================================

RUN THE TRANSPLANT, BUT NOT FIRST. It is cheap (~20 min) and it targets the
right confound, but section 7 has already shown that identity-level library
content carries no policy signature, so a transplant null is likely and would
be hard to interpret against an inherited alien-transfer null.

FIRST, CLOSE THE ARBITRARY-MEMORY GAP. The single most valuable cheap
experiment is a powered rerun of I1 vs I3 alone. That contrast sits at +1.51 pp
with Holm 0.0912 -- suggestive, underpowered, and it is the contrast that
decides whether ANY of this is about selection rather than about churn. Two
arms at n = 60 lineages costs about 55 minutes and roughly halves the MDE.
Until it is settled, the honest summary of Gen-1 is "MRU is the worst rule we
have tried."

SECOND, INSTRUMENT THE TRAJECTORY. The one thing Gen-1 could not see is the
mechanism it most needs: what the induced candidate stream actually did
differently. The next runner should log first-divergence index and candidate
overlap, which this one could not.

THIRD, BUILD THE HAZARD-BASED RETIREMENT RULE, because Phase 7 now supplies the
empirical shape rather than a guess: evict on zero credit after adequate
exposure, never on dormancy. That is a policy derived from measurement, and it
is the first one in this programme that would be.

DO NOT: acquire another donor (Lexis G1 is fired), widen the consumer, build a
memory database, or promote +2.78 pp into a capability narrative.
