====================================================================
1. PERSISTENCE INSTRUMENT -- PERSISTENCE_INSTRUMENT_CLEAN
====================================================================

The libraries behind D-5's +10.95 pp were built in memory and discarded. That
hole is now closed.

DESIGN CHOICE THAT MATTERS. The instrument is observational BY CONSTRUCTION,
not by care. The frozen search core touches the library through exactly two
surfaces:

    m1_rx.fresh()      extra_pool[rng.randrange(len(extra_pool))]
    m1.update_library  admission and most-recent-first eviction

So a list SUBCLASS whose __getitem__ is overridden observes every draw, and a
thin wrapper observes admissions while the FROZEN update_library performs the
actual mutation. m1.py is imported unmodified. No randomness is consumed, no
branch is taken differently, no evaluation counter moves. Forking m1_rx to add
hooks was rejected: a forked core cannot be shown identical to the one that
produced the inherited result.

GATE RESULTS, all PASS:

    A  serialise a search-discovered library
    B  destroy from memory and restore
    C  reload exactly
    D  same-seed parity: ROWS identical, persistence on vs off
    D  same-seed parity: LIBRARY CONTENTS identical
    E  behaviour fingerprints survive round-trip
    G  no oracle or future field persisted anywhere
    H  schema replays the event log to the identical final library

2. THE THREE-POINT WRAP -- WHY EFFECTIVE USAGE IS IMPLEMENTABLE
====================================================================

James's Q2 ruling requires credit only when a draw produces a measurable
improvement. That fact is split across three places in the frozen core that do
not talk to each other:

    which artifact was drawn   RecordingLibrary.__getitem__
    what it became             physics.mutate(parent) -> child
    how good the child was     FastTask.dist(child)

Linking all three by observation is what makes an EFFECTIVE-USAGE eviction
policy implementable at all without forking the search core. This is a
feasibility finding, and it was not obvious in advance.

CREDIT RULE, preregistered in code before any outcome was inspected: a draw is
credited if the child it produced scores strictly better than the median of the
last 32 scored candidates. The window matches the frozen psize, uses no future
information, and needs no oracle field.

====================================================================
4. JAMES'S Q2 RULING, NOW VINDICATED BY MEASUREMENT
====================================================================

The Gen-1 packet argued from the uniform-draw rule that raw retrieval count
would be residence-time noise. The corpus settles it with data:

    property           min   median   max    mean     zero
    raw draws            0      286   1208   276.75   31  ( 4%)
    effective uses       0        1     30     2.37   351 (42%)
    genotype length      1       21     24    17.88     0  ( 0%)

Raw draws are non-discriminating: only 4% of artifacts are never drawn, and the
median artifact is drawn 286 times. Effective uses discriminate hard: 42% earn
ZERO credit. A retention policy built on raw draws would be sorting noise; one
built on effective usage has a real signal to sort.

A SECOND PROPERTY WORTH A POLICY. 834 artifacts carry only 347 distinct
behaviour fingerprints, so 487 (58%) are behaviourally redundant with another
admitted artifact. D-5's admission rule dedupes on behaviour WITHIN a task;
across tasks the redundancy accumulates unchecked. That is a large, cheap,
learner-visible target that no current policy exploits.

5. ATTAINABLE POLICY RANGE -- RETENTION_POLICY_RANGE_NONTRIVIAL
====================================================================

Method: replay the SAME recorded admission stream through different eviction
rules and measure library divergence.

    admission stream   170 artifacts/lineage
    evictions          105/lineage
    turnover           1.63x the 64-slot cap on this battery

    terminal Jaccard overlap with the inherited MRU baseline
    (1.00 = identical library, 0.00 = disjoint)

        policy       terminal    mean trajectory
        RANDOM         0.461         0.702
        RETIRE_N       0.113         0.464
        LRU_DRAW       0.000         0.504
        EFFUSE         0.000         0.575

Two policies end with libraries sharing NOT ONE artifact with the baseline.
The gate does not merely clear, it clears by a wide margin.

HONEST LIMIT, stated rather than buried. All policies share the baseline's
admission stream. In a live run each policy induces its own trajectory (see
section 6), so these divergences are the SHARED-STREAM component only and are a
LOWER BOUND on how different the libraries would actually become.

SECOND LIMIT. The EFFUSE simulation applies effective-use credit pro rata from
each artifact's observed baseline effective/draw ratio. The live policy would
accumulate credit directly. The simulated arm is therefore a proxy for arm
DIVERGENCE, not an estimate of arm PERFORMANCE.

====================================================================
6. THE ESTIMAND, AND WHY IT IS NARROWER THAN IT LOOKS
====================================================================

Exact candidate-stream replay is architecturally impossible under the frozen
consumer, established in Gen-1 and accepted in the Gen-1A ruling. Two policies
holding different libraries return different genotypes from the same draw, and
mutate() consumes a variable number of rng values, so the shared stream
desynchronises at the first divergent draw and never recovers.

    THE ESTIMAND IS: the causal effect of a RETENTION POLICY, including the
    downstream search trajectory that policy induces.

    IT IS NOT: the causal effect of library composition holding subsequent
    candidate generation fixed.

Gen-1 must report first-divergence index, shared-candidate fraction, and
library-composition divergence through time, so the size of the trajectory
component is measured rather than assumed.

CALIBRATION AVAILABLE, and its limit. M1 vs M1-shuffled-history already shows
that stream divergence alone need not destroy the advantage: those arms
genuinely diverged (94 of 290 rows differ) and re-converged to 100% retention.
That is one calibration point, not a proof that arbitrary divergence is
harmless.

7. LINEAGE DEPENDENCE AND POWER -- THE DECIDING SECTION
====================================================================

REPRODUCTION CHECK FIRST. Re-deriving the frozen statistic from committed rows
gives delta +0.1095, n = 42, SE 0.0342, matching D-5's published values
exactly. The re-derivation is therefore trustworthy before it is used to say
anything new.

THE SAME EVIDENCE AT THE LINEAGE UNIT

    contrast                              mean      SD      SE(n=5)
    library vs none (D-5 headline)      +0.1095   0.0598   0.0267
    library vs library, TRUE NULL       +0.0000   0.0412   0.0184
    adapted vs random content           +0.0667   0.0391   0.0175
    library vs half-frozen              +0.0286   0.0391   0.0175

VARIANCE DECOMPOSITION of the per-cell delta

    between-LINEAGE variance   0.00286    1.8% of cell variance
    between-TASK    variance   0.04800   31.0% of cell variance
    per-cell        variance   0.15467

So lineages are nearly exchangeable: the lineage component is SMALLER than the
task component, and the lineage-level SE (0.0267) is smaller than the
task-level SE (0.0342). The pseudo-replication worry that motivated Q3 does not
inflate the inherited D-5 uncertainty.

BUT THE RULING STILL BINDS, AND IT STILL COSTS. In Gen-1 the treatment is
ASSIGNED at the lineage, so the lineage is the unit regardless of how small its
variance component is. What changes is n: 5, not 290.

THE SIGMA THAT MATTERS is a library-vs-library contrast, not library-vs-none.
M1 vs M1-shuffled-history is the pure case: identical content, different
accumulation order, true effect zero. SD 0.0412 is the noise floor a retention
experiment must beat.

MDE AT 80% POWER, simulating the FROZEN one-sided sign-flip permutation test

    n_lineages    MDE (sigma 0.0412)    MDE (sigma 0.0598, conservative)
        5             10.1 pp                   14.2 pp
        8              4.1 pp                    6.2 pp
       10              3.5 pp                    5.3 pp
       15              3.0 pp                    4.1 pp
       20              2.3 pp                    3.5 pp
       25              2.1 pp                    3.0 pp
       30              1.9 pp                    2.9 pp
       40              1.6 pp                    2.4 pp
       50              1.5 pp                    2.2 pp

READ AGAINST THE EFFECTS A RETENTION POLICY WOULD BE MODULATING

    full accumulation effect (M1 - M0)                    +10.9 pp
    content-adaptedness component (M1 - random library)    +6.7 pp
    order component (M1 - shuffled), measured              +0.0 pp

At n = 5 the MDE of 10.1 pp EXCEEDS the entire content-adaptedness component
and nearly equals the whole accumulation effect. A retention experiment at the
inherited five lineages could only detect a policy that recovers essentially
all of memory's value. It cannot answer the question.

The n = 5 penalty is mostly DISCRETENESS, not heterogeneity: a sign-flip test
on five pairs has only 2^5 = 32 sign patterns, so the attainable p-value grid
is coarse. This is why the MDE falls so sharply from n=5 to n=8.

THE FINDING IS NOT FATAL, BECAUSE LINEAGES ARE CHEAP. Measured throughput is
about 38,000 evaluations/second, and one instrumented 42-task lineage at the
30k rung costs 27.2 seconds. Thirty lineages is minutes, not months. The
correct unit costs power; buying the power back costs an hour.

8. PROPOSED POLICY ARMS
====================================================================

Eviction only. D-5 admission, cap 64, search core, budget, task corpus,
scoring and mutation operators all frozen. Deterministic tie-break by
insertion order in every arm. Identical cap in every arm.

    I0  D5_BASELINE_MRU     inherited: evict the oldest resident.
                            SIMULATED, the reference.

    I1  REDUNDANCY          evict a resident whose behaviour fingerprint
                            duplicates another resident, oldest first;
                            fall back to MRU when none.
                            Justified by the 58% redundancy finding in
                            section 4. NOT YET SIMULATED -- owed before
                            freeze.

    I2  EFFECTIVE_USAGE     evict the resident with fewest effective uses.
                            SIMULATED: terminal Jaccard 0.000 vs baseline.

    I3  RANDOM              evict a uniformly random resident. The
                            "better than arbitrary memory" comparator.
                            SIMULATED: terminal Jaccard 0.461.

    diagnostic  LRU_DRAW    evict fewest raw draws. Retained ONLY as the
                            age/residence control James allowed, never
                            described as utility. SIMULATED: 0.000.

RETIREMENT, as a first-class treatment. RETIRE_N: retire a resident with zero
effective uses after N = 200 DRAW OPPORTUNITIES (unit stated in draws, not
tasks, because draws are the causal exposure). Under the frozen search
parameters it fired 53 retirements per lineage in simulation, so the rule is
ACTIVE rather than ceremonial. N was fixed before any CFR was inspected.

10. CLAIM CEILING
====================================================================

  - Nothing here is a retention result. No policy was compared on CFR. No
    Gen-1 arm has run.
  - Policy divergence is measured on a SHARED admission stream and is a lower
    bound; live divergence would be larger and is not estimated.
  - The EFFUSE simulation uses pro-rata baseline credit ratios, so it estimates
    DIVERGENCE, not performance.
  - Sigma is estimated from D-5 ablation arms at n = 5 lineages, so sigma
    itself carries meaningful uncertainty, and every MDE inherits it. The
    conservative column exists for that reason.
  - The corpus covers 42 non-control tasks with fresh nav seeds; it is not a
    replication of D-5 and its solve rates should not be read as one.
  - The parity fixture ran 20 tasks at budget 3000. Parity is a determinism
    claim and that is adequate for it, but it is not a claim about behaviour
    at the 30k rung.
  - "Lineages are nearly exchangeable" is measured on the headline contrast in
    the inherited data. It is not guaranteed to hold for policy contrasts.

13. VERDICT AND WHAT I RECOMMEND
====================================================================

    GEN1_READY_TO_RUN, at n = 30 paired lineages.

Three conditions I would attach, and the first is not negotiable:

  1. n >= 20 lineages, and 30 preferred. At the inherited n = 5 the experiment
     is not capable of answering its own question, and running it there would
     produce a null that means nothing.
  2. Simulate I1 REDUNDANCY before the freeze. It is the arm most likely to
     matter, justified by the largest structural finding in the corpus, and it
     is the one arm whose divergence is not yet measured.
  3. Preregister and hash the design before the first experimental
     organism-evaluation, with the estimand written as "policy + induced
     trajectory", the unit as the lineage, and MDE 1.9 pp at n = 30.

DECLARED CONFLICTS. This seat proposed the retention question in its own
charter before any evidence existed, and a GEN1_READY verdict authorises its
own expensive run. The countervailing pressure is also declared: a STOP verdict
would have been cheap and defensible. The mitigations are structural. The
power analysis first reproduced the frozen statistic exactly, so the machinery
was validated against a known answer before being pointed at a new one. The
attainable-range gate could have returned near-identical libraries and did not.
The n = 5 finding is the one this seat least wanted, and it is reported as the
headline of section 7 rather than a footnote.
