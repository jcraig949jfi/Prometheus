THE QUESTION

    DOES RETENTION POLICY MEASURABLY MATTER IN THIS CONSUMER AT ALL?

    I3 RANDOM versus I0 D5_BASELINE_MRU, and nothing else.

Project 1 (2026-09-02) bounded the SELECTION question: principled selection
does not beat arbitrary churn (-0.31 pp, 95% CI [-1.12, +0.55] pp, n 100).
Gen-1B's headline I1 - I0 = +2.78 pp (Holm 0.0040, n 30) then has exactly
two readings left, and this experiment separates them:

    (a) MRU is actively harmful: evicting the oldest resident throws away
        artifacts the search would have used, and ANY churn beats it.
        Prediction: I3 - I0 >= T.
    (b) Gen-1B's I1 - I0 was inflated the same way its I1 - I3 was (a
        marginal contrast selected for attention). Prediction: I3 - I0 is
        a bounded null, and the honest position becomes "retention policy
        does not measurably matter in this consumer"; Gen-1B's headline
        falls with it.

                    ergon/gen3/p3_common.py

seed space          L in 300..399. Disjoint from Gen-1B (0..29), Project 1

                    run_jobs); each lineage is an independent unit with its
                    own seeds, so the pool changes no number. Every lineage
                    writes its rows and summary to ergon/gen3/p3_results/
                    before it is reported.

WHY FRESH LINEAGES. Gen-1B's 30 I0/I3 lineages have been seen (I3 - I0 was

4. ANALYSIS -- FIXED HERE (p3_analyze.py)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
endpoint            per-lineage CFR (fraction of 42 tasks solved in budget)
unit of inference   LINEAGE

                    (positive = MRU is worse than churn)

No subgroup, task-level, family-level or stratum-level analysis enters the
decision. No rescue of a failing contrast.

5. DECISION RULE -- COMPLETE AND EXHAUSTIVE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Let d = observed mean delta (I3 - I0), p = permutation p-value, [lo, hi]
the 95% bootstrap CI, SE the standard error of the deltas.

    INDETERMINATE                       checked FIRST, before p is read:
        eligible pairs < 100, OR any interpretability assertion of section
        7 fails, OR all 100 pairs are tied (nothing could have fired), OR
        SE > T/2 = 1.00 pp (the gate would be inside two SE of zero).
        The statistic is still reported beside the label.

    MRU_HARMFUL                         p < 0.05  AND  d >= T
    MRU_HARMFUL_BELOW_USEFUL_SCALE      p < 0.05  AND  0 < d < T
    MRU_BETTER_THAN_RANDOM              p < 0.05  AND  d < 0
    RETENTION_POLICY_DOES_NOT_MEASURABLY_MATTER
                                        p >= 0.05  AND  -T < lo  AND  hi < T
        (a bounded null on BOTH sides: the data exclude a strategically
        meaningful effect in either direction)
    UNDERPOWERED                        p >= 0.05  AND  NOT (-T < lo AND hi < T)

CONSEQUENCES, stated now so they cannot be improvised later:

    MRU_HARMFUL                 the Gen-1B headline stands as "MRU is
                                harmful", not as "selection works"; ERGON-06
                                (measure a deletion) proceeds against the
                                I3 comparator, since any rule must beat

    MRU_HARMFUL_BELOW_SCALE     reported as real and below scale; ERGON-06
