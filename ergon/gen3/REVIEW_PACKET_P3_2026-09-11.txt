--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CLAIM UNDER REVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Tier A gate packet (c3797f865) left one discriminating experiment after
Project 1 bounded "selection beats churn" to a null: is the inherited MRU
eviction rule ACTIVELY HARMFUL (any churn beats it), or was Gen-1B's I1 - I0
= +2.78 pp (n 30) a marginal contrast that would not replicate on either
leg? The preregistered falsifier of "MRU is harmful": I3 - I0 fails to
reach T = 2.00 pp with p < 0.05. The preregistered falsifier of "retention
policy does not matter": the 95% CI leaves (-2.00, +2.00) pp.

Result: the CI [-0.24, +1.33] pp lies inside (-2, +2). MRU is not harmful
at a strategically meaningful scale, and neither leg of the Gen-1B
headline survives at n 100.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. METHOD / SUBSTRATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Consumer: the D-5 organism (agent_d5_blind/): boolean genotypes over a
64-element domain, frozen search core m1.m1_rx, frozen admission
m1.admissions, library cap 64, budget 30,000 evaluations per task, the
frozen 42 non-control tasks (families F1-F4). No model judgement anywhere
in the inference path.

Arms (the only treatment is eviction):
    I0  D5_BASELINE_MRU   evict the oldest resident (inherited D-5 rule)
    I3  RANDOM            evict a uniformly random resident

Runner: ergon/gen1b/gen1_run.py, UNCHANGED since Gen-1B (blob 5771258...),
called verbatim through ergon/gen3/p3_common.py; lineages distributed over
an 8-process pool (each lineage is an independent unit with seeds that are
pure functions of its index, so the pool changes no number). Pairing:
lineage L uses nav_base 200000 + 1000 L in both arms. Seed space L in
300..399, disjoint from Gen-1B (0..29), Project 1 (100..199) and the cheat
controls (200..299).

10. KNOWN LIMITATIONS & CAVEATS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- One consumer, one cap (64), one budget (30,000), one battery (42 tasks,
  4 families). "Does not matter" is scoped to that; a cap of 8 or a budget
  of 3,000 is a different experiment.
- The library is small relative to the search: the D-5 whole-library
  effect was +10.95 pp, and the between-arm SD (about 4.5 pp per lineage)
  is dominated by search noise, so any order effect under 1.2 pp is below
  MDE80 by design and would be reported UNDERPOWERED only if the CI
  crossed T.
- 26 of 100 pairs tied (P1 had 20). Ties are eligible and contribute zero
  to the sign-flip null; they are reported, not dropped.
- The planted-witness footprint (section 4) is a weak signal, not a
  result: a good resident's descendants helped other tasks by about
  +0.95 pp net. No claim; logged as residue.
- The 8-process pool: each lineage is seed-determined and independent;
  verified by the determinism control, not by a serial re-run of P3.
