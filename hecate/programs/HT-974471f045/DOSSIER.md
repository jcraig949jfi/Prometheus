## RAW INTERPRETATIONS
- [spec] I1: The reservoir's fading memory is a specious present (retention + protention fringes around the input now). Evolution does not select what is known but how wide the living present is: heritable leak rate and spectral radius are selected to match the temporal structure of the environment, and the retention/protention balance is itself an evolved trait.
- [spec] I2: The reservoir/readout split is an epoche: the fixed dynamics are the bracketed background that is not modified in life, while the readout is the active attitude learned in life. Evolution then acts on the bracketed layer only, producing a Baldwin-style division of labour between heritable fixed dynamics and lifetime-trained readouts.
- [spec] I3: The reservoir is not random but a sediment of ancestral readouts: across generations, readouts that were learned actively are compiled back into fixed recurrent dynamics (sedimentation / genetic assimilation), so the 'random' reservoir of a lineage carries a stratigraphy of past tasks that makes their relearning cheap.
- [spec] I4: Intentionality as readout multiplicity: one state stream supports many intentional objects via many readouts. Evolution can select reservoirs for noematic richness (how many distinct target functionals are linearly decodable) rather than for any single task, yielding reservoirs that transfer to unseen tasks.
- [spec] I5: Lifeworld incommensurability as speciation: a readout is meaningful only relative to its reservoir, so readouts cannot be exchanged between divergent reservoirs. Readout-transplant failure is a reproductive-isolation criterion, and species are sets of individuals sharing a horizon of decodability.
- [spec] I6: Epoche as an evolved input gate: when readout capacity is limited, lineages that suspend (mask) distracting input channels without losing them from the world gain fitness; bracketing is a heritable, selected information bottleneck rather than a method.
- [spec] I7: Noema as invariant across reservoirs: if the readout is heritable but each individual's reservoir is freshly resampled in development, only readouts addressing properties invariant across reservoir realizations survive; evolution performs an eidetic variation over reservoirs and finds their invariant.

## CANDIDATE PHYSICS
- [spec] M1 (selection mechanism): Horizon tuning: leak rate and spectral radius are heritable; fitness is lifetime readout error on a task with characteristic delay D; the population's memory-capacity profile migrates to centre on D.
- [spec] M2 (learning pressure): Bracketed inheritance: reservoir weights are heritable and never changed in life; readout is re-learned from scratch each life with a fixed small sample budget. Low lifetime budget pushes task information into the heritable reservoir; high budget leaves reservoir near-random.
- [spec] M3 (mutation operator): Sedimentation operator: with probability p per birth, the parent's learned readout w is compiled into the offspring reservoir as a fixed feedback loop W <- W + eps * v w^T (v a random input-like vector). Actively learned functionals become passive background dynamics of descendants.
- [spec] M4 (selection mechanism): Noematic richness selection: fitness counts how many of K random target functionals of input history are linearly decodable above threshold, not performance on one task. Predicts better transfer to held-out functionals than task-selected reservoirs.
- [spec] M5 (selection mechanism): Readout-transplant speciation: mating succeeds only if parent A's readout, applied to parent B's reservoir, achieves error below a threshold. Compatibility classes (shared horizons) become species; isolation emerges where reservoirs diverge in decodable geometry.
- [spec] M6 (representation): Retention/protention split: state decomposes into subspaces predictive of past inputs (retention) and of future inputs (protention); in environments with learnable regularity, selection shifts capacity from retention toward protention, with total bounded by N.
- [impl] M7 (information bottleneck): Epoche gate: heritable binary mask over input channels; readout limited to k taps. When k is small, evolution brackets high-variance uninformative channels (keeps them in the world, suspends them from the reservoir); when readout is unconstrained, masking is not selected.
- [spec] M8 (computational primitive): Passive synthesis: during life, before readout training, the reservoir undergoes slow unsupervised intrinsic plasticity (gain/bias homeostasis) driven only by input exposure; the plasticity rate is heritable. Pre-reflective shaping precedes active readout.
- [spec] M9 (causal constraint): Echo-state heredity boundary: because the echo state property erases initial conditions, environmental regularities with period longer than the reservoir horizon cannot be carried in state and must be carried genetically. Predicts a gap: structure at timescales below horizon is learned, above horizon is inherited or absent.
- [spec] M10 (interaction law): Lifeworld commons: one reservoir is shared by the whole population; individuals are only readouts. Individuals' outputs are fed back as reservoir input and the shared reservoir drifts under cumulative feedback, so readouts evolve against a world they collectively shape.
- [spec] M11 (dynamical law): Edge drift: under mutation plus selection on robustness of offspring fitness, spectral radius drifts toward the stability boundary because mutational robustness of readout performance peaks there; phenomenologically, horizon widens until it becomes unstable.
- [spec] M12 (learning pressure): Protention-fulfilment fitness: fitness is the rate of fulfilled vs disappointed anticipations (sign agreement of predicted and actual next input change), not squared error; predicts selection for reservoirs that anticipate qualitative events rather than magnitudes.
- [spec] M13 (organism architecture): Embodied reservoir: the reservoir is a mass-spring body whose morphology is heritable; the body computes by being perturbed by the world, and only a linear readout of spring lengths is learned. The lived body is the zero-point of computation.
- [impl] M14 (selection mechanism): Eidetic variation over reservoirs: the readout is heritable but each individual's reservoir is resampled at birth from a heritable distribution. Selection keeps only readouts addressing reservoir-invariant properties (e.g., permutation-invariant aggregates), and the distribution evolves to make more properties invariant.
- [spec] M15 (error-correction mechanism): Adumbration voting: several small reservoirs each receive a partial profile (subset of channels); a population-level readout reconciles them. Evolution selects channel-to-reservoir assignments; the object is only decodable jointly.
