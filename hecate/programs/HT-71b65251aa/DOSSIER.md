## CANDIDATE PHYSICS
- [spec] M1 (interaction law): Hedge-token equilibrium: speakers attach one of K meaningless tokens to each answer; listeners choose accept or verify-at-cost. Under best-response dynamics tokens become calibrated confidence labels, and the mutual information between token and correctness is capped by the speaker's metacognitive resolution and collapses when speaker-listener interests diverge.
- [impl] M2 (computational primitive): Metacognitively gated recursion depth: a listener interprets utterances by level-k reasoning over alternatives and stops recursing when its self-estimated interpretive uncertainty falls below a threshold; the threshold is in equilibrium with how deeply speakers design their utterances.
- [spec] M3 (selection mechanism): Clarification equilibrium: the listener asks for clarification when its posterior over referents has max below tau; the speaker chooses redundancy r. The pair (tau*, r*) is an interior Nash equilibrium whose location moves predictably with the cost of asking and the cost of words.
- [spec] M4 (error-correction mechanism): Utterance-surprise gate: the listener keeps a model of how informative a cooperative speaker should be in this context; an utterance that under-informs relative to that model triggers non-literal reinterpretation. Speakers flout only when the listener's gate is precise enough for flouting to pay.
- [spec] M5 (organism architecture): Internal parliament: a generator and a monitor inside one agent have separate payoffs (generator rewarded for answering, monitor for avoiding errors); calibrated abstention is a Nash equilibrium of this internal game and the monitor's messages to the generator take meaning from task context.
- [spec] M6 (learning pressure): Confidence inflation with pragmatic discounting: when listeners weight speakers by stated confidence, speakers inflate; listeners learn to discount; the equilibrium has public confidence words whose meaning is shifted from private calibrated confidence, producing a measurable private-public calibration gap that vanishes under proper-scoring listeners.
- [spec] M7 (information bottleneck): Content-confidence channel split: a channel of fixed capacity must carry both the answer and its confidence; at equilibrium confidence moves out of the explicit channel into implicature (e.g. choice of phrasing) when context makes it recoverable.
- [spec] M8 (causal constraint): Self-as-model substitution: agents approximate the partner's strategy by their own self-model (metacognitive access used as a proxy for common knowledge); equilibria reached this way are correct in homogeneous populations and systematically miscoordinate in heterogeneous ones, with miscoordination concentrated on context-dependent (pragmatic) signals.
- [spec] M9 (memory structure): Common-ground ledger with uncertainty: each agent keeps a ledger of established facts plus a metacognitive estimate of whether each entry is shared; acknowledgement acts are spent on entries with low shared-estimate; equilibrium acknowledgement rate depends on channel loss.
- [spec] M10 (dynamical law): Mixed-strategy ambiguity cycles: speakers randomize between explicit and ambiguous forms; listeners' interpretive vigilance tracks the recent ambiguity rate; the population cycles around a mixed equilibrium, and listeners with better metacognitive tracking damp the cycles.
- [spec] M11 (mutation operator): Reliability-tagged lexicon mutation: each word carries a metacognitive reliability tag; mutations that reassign a word's meaning are accepted only if the resulting lexicon remains a Nash equilibrium of the referential game; low-reliability words mutate more.
- [spec] M12 (developmental process): Hedges acquire meaning only after self-calibration: in a developing agent, confidence tokens stay babbling until its internal confidence map reaches a resolution threshold, then snap to an informative equilibrium; the order (calibrate first, then hedge) is required, and reversing it locks in a babbling convention.
- [spec] M13 (representation): Meaning as equilibrium-membership: an utterance's meaning is represented as the set of equilibria of the ongoing game it is consistent with; metacognitive confidence is the entropy over that set, so confidence is about which game is being played rather than about the world.
- [spec] M14 (world rule): Silence as an equilibrium speech act: agents may abstain from answering; whether silence means 'I do not know' or 'I will not say' is fixed by equilibrium and depends on whether abstention is driven by metacognitive uncertainty or by strategic withholding.
- [spec] M15 (interaction law): Self-deceptive monitor: when outward confidence pays, a monitor whose internal calibration is shifted toward overconfidence is favoured over one that reports honestly-but-strategically, because the second pays a monitoring cost to maintain two confidence maps; inner and outer games are coupled through the cost of keeping private and public confidence distinct.
