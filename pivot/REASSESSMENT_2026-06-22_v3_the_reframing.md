## 1. The decision

The honest conclusion is **not** that Prometheus is already a reasoning engine or a
discovery engine. It is not. Not yet. The stronger, defensible claim:

> **Prometheus is the test-driven-development layer for building one.**
> A falsification substrate, progress meter, and directional compass for synthetic
> reasoning research. Not the organism — the instrument that tells us whether an
> organism is emerging.

This is now the project's live thesis. It does not need Prometheus to *be* the
discovery engine. It needs Prometheus to reliably answer, every cycle, about any
candidate system built against it:

1. **Are we there yet?** Has the candidate produced a result that survives independent
   falsification and qualifies as genuine novelty?
2. **Are we closer than yesterday?** Are its failures becoming more structured, its
   search more efficient, its claims more robust, its generalization less confounded?
3. **What should we try next?** Does the accumulated kill geometry point toward a
   specific mutation, domain shift, operator change, curriculum adjustment, or missing
   capability?

If Prometheus answers those better than human intuition or benchmark scores alone, the
20-year bet is alive — as the **instrument panel, crash-test facility, and evolutionary
pressure system** for building a reasoner. If it cannot, the fallback is still valuable:
a world-class mathematical and reasoning audit substrate.

**The decisive experiment is no longer "does Prometheus discover?" It is: "can
Prometheus tell whether a would-be discovery engine is actually getting closer?"**

## 2. Why this resolves the contradiction

v1/v2 found three unreconciled success-states: **A** audit substrate, **B** discovery
engine, **C** recognition instrument. The reframing introduces **D — the TDD layer —
which subsumes them:**

- It *keeps* C (the recognition instrument) as the engine room — already the program's
  defensible, mostly-GREEN achievement.
- It *reframes* B: Prometheus no longer has to *be* the discovery engine or *prove*
  discovery. It has to **measure movement toward** discovery in a separate candidate.
- It *preserves* A as the honest fallback if even measurement fails.

The central diagnosis — "an immune system with no organism" — stops being a terminal
failure the moment the immune system becomes **the way organisms are tested, trained,
and evolved.** You build candidate organisms *against* the substrate; the substrate's
job is to grade and direct them. The body no longer has to be Prometheus's own.

## 3. The three questions, compiled into standing gates (v2 ethos)

Each question becomes an executable gate run on every candidate cycle — not prose, a
measurement with a pass condition:

| # | Question | Executable gate | Pass signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Are we there yet? | candidate output → full falsification battery + **novelty check vs calibration manifold** | survives independent falsification AND lands outside the known-truth manifold (depends on M0 — see §5) |
| Q2 | Closer than yesterday? | cross-cycle deltas on 4 axes: failure-structure, search-efficiency, claim-robustness, generalization-confound | ≥1 axis improves AND none regress, each vs a null |
| Q3 | What next? | kill-geometry → typed direction (mutation / domain shift / operator change / curriculum / missing capability) | the suggested direction, when taken, beats a random/no-op control next cycle |

**Q2's four axes, made concrete (the "closer than yesterday" instrument):**
- *Failure structure:* is `kill_pattern` mutual-information rising? are failures
  clustering into fewer, sharper modes (vs scattering)?
- *Search efficiency:* improving moves found per unit compute (the discriminator from
  the Apollo crossover analysis — 0/8000 single-step vs 6.1%/pair).
- *Claim robustness:* survival rate under *harder* nulls cycle-over-cycle.
- *Generalization confound:* out-of-distribution lift that survives the synthetic-null
  gate (not modal-class recovery — the trap that retracted the +1.37×–18× "lifts").

## 4. The candidate-organism harness (M1, reframed)

M1 ("build one organism") is no longer "build the discovery engine." It is: **build a
candidate that runs against the substrate and answers the three questions each cycle**,
with a replayable ablation card (organism is real only if removing the substrate input
degrades it — the 5-condition definition in v2 §2 still holds). Hephaestus→Learner
remains the seed loop. The substrate grades; the candidate climbs.

## 5. M0 is still the keystone — the reframing raises its stakes, doesn't escape it

Be hostile to the conclusion: **the reframing does not dodge M0.** Question 1 ("genuine
novelty") is only answerable if the selector can *recognize novelty outside its
calibration manifold* — exactly the M0 type-II experiment (v2 §2). If the battery can
only recognize known-truth-shapes, then Q1's "qualifies as genuine novelty" is
unmeasurable, and the progress meter is blind precisely where it matters most. So:

> **M0 is the keystone of the reframing, not a casualty of it.** Under the old thesis,
> M0 asked "can Prometheus discover?" Under the new thesis it asks "can Prometheus
> *recognize* a discovery a candidate produced?" — which is the same selector property,
> and still the one experiment that decides whether D is alive or collapses to A.

## 6. The failure mode this reframing introduces (ships with its own guard)

Every reframing has a new way to fail. This one's is sharp and must be gated from day
one:

> **A progress meter can be Goodhart'd.** "Measure movement toward discovery" can
> degrade into rewarding *looking* closer without *being* closer — the same
> reward-capture the program keeps re-paying, moved up one level onto the instrument
> itself. A candidate could learn to produce failures that *look* more structured, lifts
> that *look* less confounded, directions that *look* productive — gaming the meter
> instead of approaching discovery.

**Guard (meta-falsification — the instrument must falsify its own metrics):**
- Every Q2/Q3 metric carries a null and an ablation; "improvement" that doesn't beat
  its null is not logged as progress (the v2 doctrine, now applied to the *meter*).
- Q3 directions are *predictions*: a suggested direction must beat a random/no-op
  control on the *next* cycle, or the compass is decorative. The compass is scored by
  its hit-rate, not its plausibility.
- The meter is validated against the only Goodhart-proof anchor available: **does
  following Prometheus's direction beat human intuition and raw benchmark scores at
  actually advancing a candidate?** If not, the meter has no edge and D fails to A.
- Apply the cross-family rule (v2 §0): the meter's own "this candidate improved"
  verdicts need a second-family or runnable-artifact check before they count.

## 7. The bet, restated

- **Alive (success-state D):** Prometheus answers the three questions better than human
  intuition or benchmark scores, becoming the instrument panel + crash-test facility +
  evolutionary pressure system for building a reasoner. The 20-year bet holds — as the
  *measurement layer*, not the engine.
- **Fallback (success-state A):** if it cannot measure movement, it is still a
  world-class mathematical and reasoning audit substrate.
- **Keystone experiment:** M0 — can the selector recognize novelty outside its
  calibration set? Everything in D rests on it.
