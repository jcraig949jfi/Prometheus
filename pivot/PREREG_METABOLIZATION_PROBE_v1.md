## 3. Difficulty leveling + contamination screening (R4, R13) — one procedure

Both are the same measurement — a cold, no-residue, minimal-budget pass — so they run once.

**Difficulty dial** (per domain, monotone; gold stays computed and exact at every level):

- L0 = the June ranges (primality `10^2–5·10^4`; coprime 4-digit; summation `n ≤ 400`; modular
  `m < 50`; perfect-square roots `12–700`; divisibility divisor ≤ 97; inequality `k ≤ 22`).
- L1 = ×10^2 on operand magnitude. L2 = ×10^4 and adversarial structure mandatory (semiprimes
  with both factors > 10^3; near-squares at off-by-1 from `k²` with `k > 10^4`; `n` within
  `±n` of `n(n+1)/2`; `2^k` vs `n` within 1% of `2^k`). L3 = ×10^6.
- The near-miss structure is the point: without tools, a raw solver must actually execute the
  arithmetic; recognition of surface form is insufficient by construction.

**Leveling rule (executed before arms, on cold probes only).** For the strongest available
solver, pick the smallest L such that cold F0 accuracy ∈ **[0.35, 0.60]**. If no L satisfies it,
the run is `HEADROOM-FAILURE` and no residue verdict is issued (spec §4.5 row 1). ≥25pp headroom
to the instrument ceiling (R4) is then satisfied by construction at the top of that band.

**Contamination probe (R13).** Every task is posed cold to every solver, no residue, minimal
budget, before arms. An item answered correctly by **all** solvers on **both** of two cold
repetitions is `CONTAMINATED-OR-TRIVIAL` and is stratified out of the primary analysis and
reported separately. Regenerated large-operand instances at a fresh seed cannot be memorized from
a pretraining corpus, so this probe is expected to be mostly a *triviality* filter — which is the
same threat to headroom and gets the same treatment.

**Power floor (R13).** Minimum post-stratification **N = 300** (§6). Below it, replenish from the
pool and re-screen **before any arm runs**. An underpowered `Δ_carry ≈ 0` is never a verdict.

## 4. Residue, provenance, and the transfer-distance ladder

### 4.1 Two residue populations, labeled — the honesty note this probe must carry (R6)

- **Native residue** — what Prometheus actually accumulated (Theseus REJECTED records, forge
  ledger scraps, `signature_index` shape-classes, `kill_pattern` strings with their 33.6% nulls,
  `kill_vector` at 0% populated). Supplies **D2 and D3**.
- **Self-generated first-cycle residue** — produced by this probe's pre-pass (§4.2), written
  through the substrate's own emission schema, provenance-stamped. Supplies **D0 and D1**.

These answer different questions and are reported separately, always. D0/D1 measure whether a
recorded failure helps the *next attempt at the same or adjacent problem*; D2/D3 measure whether
the corpus we spent a year accumulating carries anything. **A D0 win with a D3 null is not a
success for the accumulated corpus** and must never be reported as one.

### 4.2 The pre-pass (creates D0/D1 residue; runs after leveling, before arms)

Each task is attempted once, cold (this is the same pass as §3's contamination probe — one
execution, two uses). Every attempt emits a typed failure record — attempt text, extracted
verdict, gold-agnostic diagnosis fields the substrate already defines, `uid`, ledger id,
monotone `seq`. **No gold label, no correctness flag, and no grader output enters the record**
(`derives_from_gold=true` records are excluded by *type*, not by time — §4.4). The pre-pass
ledger is `probe_prepass` and is closed and hashed before arms begin.

### 4.3 D-tagging is by construction, not by post-hoc judgement (spec §9)

Each task is assigned exactly one stratum; the assembler is permitted to draw residue **only** at
that stratum's relation level:

- **D0** — residue from the *same* `uid`'s pre-pass attempt. *Note, preregistered:* D0 is
  conditioned on a prior attempt existing, so its F0 base rate is depressed by construction; D0
  levels are never compared across strata, only its Δ.
- **D1** — residue from sibling `uid`s of the same generator + domain, different parameters.
- **D2** — residue from a *different* domain sharing a **mechanism tag**. Tags, fixed here:
  `exact-large-integer-arithmetic` · `divisor-or-factor-search` · `gcd-computation` ·
  `modular-reduction` · `square-detection` · `magnitude-comparison` ·
  `closed-form-formula-application`. Two tasks are D2-related iff they share ≥1 tag and differ in
  domain.
- **D3** — native substrate residue from a different mathematical domain sharing a **latent
  obstruction**. Obstruction classes for v1: `asserted-equality-without-executing-computation` ·
  `surface-plausible-arithmetically-false` · `near-miss-margin-below-threshold`. Sources:
  Theseus `invariant_equality` REJECTED records, forge-ledger scraps with failure reasons,
  `signature_index` classes.

**Preregistered non-result [AMENDMENT].** If fewer than **40 eligible records** exist for a
stratum after the R14 firewall, that stratum is reported as
**`NOT-RUN-FOR-LACK-OF-RESIDUE`** — never as Δ = 0. *"No residue exists at this distance"* and
*"residue at this distance does not help"* are different findings, and the corpus audit
(2026-06-03) makes the former a live possibility for D3.

### 4.4 R14 firewall — mechanical, from provenance IDs and ledger order

Wall-clock is untrustworthy (M3 CMOS reset), so the firewall uses a **cutoff vector**, not a
timestamp. For task T, τ(T) := `{ledger_id → max_seq present at cutoff}` over every ledger the
assembler may draw from, snapshotted and frozen into the packet header.

Assertions in the assembler, all fail-loud, none skippable:
1. `∀ r ∈ packet: ledger(r) ∈ dom(τ(T))` — a record from an unregistered ledger aborts assembly.
2. `∀ r ∈ packet: seq(r) ≤ τ(T)[ledger(r)]` — the spec's
   `packet_max_provenance < target_attempt_provenance`, made comparable across ledgers.
3. `∀ r ∈ packet: derives_from_gold(r) = false` — type-based exclusion of grader-derived records.
4. A **planted-violation test** must fail loud in Tier A exit (spec §4.2), executed as a unit
   test committed with the assembler.

If a task class cannot be firewalled mechanically because its provenance is too coarse, that
class is **EXCLUDED, listed, and reported** (spec §7) — never waved through.

### 4.5 Packet construction and the token ceiling (spec §9)

- **Ceiling: 8,000 tokens** for `F-prom-retrieved`, and every other packeted arm is matched
  **per task** to that task's retrieved-packet token count **within ±5%**. F0 carries no packet.
- `F-prom-whole` is exempt from the ceiling (it is the existence arm) and is capped at
  *solver context − 20%*; the `signature_index` prefix is placed in a cacheable prefix position
  so the cost is paid once per solver rather than once per task.
- Assembly is deterministic, committed, and stamped: assembly version, source record IDs, τ(T),
  token count. No hand-enrichment; where the substrate recorded nothing useful the packet says so
  and the sparsity is the measurement (R6).

### 5.2 `F-shuffle` — **OUT of v1** (spec §9 item 3), one line of reasoning

F-shuffle scrambles *within-record correspondence* across (diagnosis ↔ approach ↔ falsifier ↔
margin ↔ break location). On M1's measured corpus those fields are largely absent: `kill_vector`
is 0% populated across 5.4M records and `kill_pattern` is 33.6% null (Charon, `E1`). Scrambling a
record that carries one populated relational field is a no-op, and a no-op arm reported as a
structure control would be decorative. **Reinstatement condition, preregistered:** F-shuffle
returns when ≥3 populated relational fields per record are available (e.g. after a `kill_vector`
backfill), as an amendment-commit to this document, not a redesign.

### 5.3 `F-prom-whole` sampling rule

Cost-bounded: `F-prom-whole` runs on a **seeded random subsample of 60 tasks** (12/12/18/18 across
D0/D1/D2/D3 — weighted toward the native-residue strata, where the existence question actually
bites), one long-context solver. Its CI will be wide; it is an existence arm and is reported with
its own interval, never pooled into the primary endpoint.

## 6. Statistical plan (R15) — executed numbers, not asserted ones

### 6.1 Primary endpoint — exactly one

**Paired task-level `F-prom-retrieved` − `F-null` on the pooled `PROBE-NT-v1` manifest.**
Unit of analysis = **task** (never task × solver: solvers on the same task are correlated and
counting them separately is pseudo-replication). Per-task statistic = mean success across
admissible solvers. Test = **two-sided paired bootstrap over tasks, 10,000 resamples, seed 0**,
reusing the verified machinery in `ergon/learner/eval/routing_eval.py:179` (`paired_bootstrap_p`),
which passed positive/negative/floor instrument validation in the 2026-06-09 routing eval (`E1`).
Reported as `Δ_carry` with a percentile CI. Per-solver McNemar exact tests are **secondary**.

### 6.2 Power (`E3`, computed on M1 2026-08-13)

Paired-bootstrap power by simulation (400 reps × 2,000 resamples, per-task difficulty
`N(0.45, 0.18)`, 2 solvers):

```
N=400  true Δ=+5pp  ->  power 0.61
N=400  true Δ=+8pp  ->  power 0.93
N=300  true Δ=+8pp  ->  power 0.81
N=494  true Δ=+5pp  ->  power 0.68
N=100  true Δ=+8pp  ->  power 0.40      <- this is a single D-stratum
```

McNemar MDE (80% power, α=.05 two-sided), by discordance rate ψ:

```
N=300: 7.21pp (ψ=.20) / 7.89pp (ψ=.24) / 8.82pp (ψ=.30)
N=400: 6.25pp          / 6.84pp          / 7.65pp
N=494: 5.62pp          / 6.16pp          / 6.89pp
```

**Consequences, preregistered.** (a) The design is powered for **Δ\* = +8pp**; N = 400 with a
floor of 300. (b) Detecting the practical floor of +5pp would need ≈750 paired tasks (ψ=.24,
`E3`) — out of budget this cycle, and therefore **a +5pp true effect is expected to be missed
about 40% of the time.** (c) **Every D-stratum is underpowered by construction** (0.40 at +8pp)
and is labeled *exploratory* in every report; a D3 null at N=100 is not evidence of absence.

### 6.3 Thresholds and verdict classes [AMENDMENT to spec §4.5]

The spec invites the co-signers to own this row; here it is, quantified:

- **`✓ strong`** — `Δ_carry ≥ +8pp` AND bootstrap CI lower bound > 0 AND
  `harm_rate ≤ 0.5 × gain_rate`.
- **`✓ weak`** — CI lower bound > 0 but `Δ_carry < +8pp`.
- **`✗ (bounded null)`** — CI lower bound ≤ 0 **AND** CI **upper** bound < +5pp; i.e. we can
  positively exclude a practically meaningful effect. Wording is fixed as *"no detectable carry
  under the tested models, tasks, packetization, and context budgets"* — never "at any capacity".
- **`INCONCLUSIVE-UNDERPOWERED`** — CI lower bound ≤ 0 AND CI upper bound ≥ +5pp. **This is not a
  row of the diagnostic matrix and never routes to Path γ.** It routes to replenish-and-rerun.
  This class is the quantitative form of Aporia's §7.5b residual.
- **Minimum practical effect (R15): +5pp.** Below it, a result is reported as *statistically
  detectable, practically inert* — a +0.7pp lift on 400 tasks is not a program direction.

**New matrix row [AMENDMENT] — topic conditioning.** The spec's matrix has no cell for the most
likely benign-looking outcome: `F-prom ≈ F-null ≈ F-generic`, all **≫ F0**. That is not carry; it
is *any on-topic text primes the solver*. Diagnosis: **`TOPIC-CONDITIONING`**; next move: the
lift belongs to prompting, not to residue, and no retrieval work is justified. F-null and
F-generic are exactly the two controls that separate this from carry, which is why the center of
gravity is F-prom vs F-null and never F-prom vs F0.

### 6.4 Multiplicity, attempts, missing data, exclusions

- **Attempts:** k = 1 per task × arm at temp 0, plus a 50-task determinism re-run per solver;
  if the re-run disagrees on >5% of items the solver is re-pinned or dropped.
- **Multiplicity:** primary endpoint is single and uncorrected. All secondary quantities (D-strata,
  the §2 decomposition, per-domain, behavioral metrics) carry Benjamini–Hochberg FDR at q=0.05
  within their family and are labeled exploratory.
- **Missing API responses:** retried 3×; unrecovered ⇒ the whole batch is discarded, never
  averaged (R11). A task missing any arm is dropped from the paired analysis and counted in a
  reported attrition line.

  decisive run: **N = 120 tasks × 5 arms** (`F0`, `F-null`, `F-prom-retrieved`, `F-oracle`,
