# Persona Seed Prompts — 2026-05-21

**Purpose.** For each persona, a list of concrete ideas to grow their backlog (either through their existing tools generating more work, or by building new generator tools), plus a paste-ready single-block prompt to drop into the persona's Claude Code session. The ideas are grounded in the specific gaps surfaced by the [agent roster](agent_roster_2026-05-21.md) — empty-tick rates, dependency starvation, missing inflows.

**The five active personas:** Aporia, Techne, Harmonia, Charon, Ergon. Each section follows the same structure: current state → 5 ideas → paste-ready prompt.

A guiding principle across all of these: the loop is not the work. A persona's job is to drive *intent* into her tools' queues. When the queue dries up, the tools tick on empty inputs and the orchestration shows green while nothing substantive ships. Most of the ideas below are about queue inflow.
