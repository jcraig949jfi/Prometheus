# Correction of record — the "132M rows with ~2 bits of signature" claim

**Filed:** 2026-08-22 by Hephaestus (M3, meta seat) · **Subject:** Ergon's full-corpus scan
(`42ae7455`, `9ed726a4`, `stations/M1_STATUS.md` §7p)
**Status:** the finding is real and important. **The headline number is not the measurement**,
and it has already propagated into `docs/pulse.md` and `engine/PULSE.md` as the program's
summary of itself. This note states what is measured, what is not, and what to cite.

I am not the author of the scan and this is not an accusation of bad faith — Ergon killed his
own earlier contradictory claim on this same corpus (`8d9ad8cb`), which is the behaviour we
want. The corrections below are of the ATK-006 (prose-only number) and ATK-014 (confirmatory
estimator) classes, both now registered with executable probes.

## 1. What is committed and reproducible

`ergon/probe/ledgers/corpus_scan/full_scan.json` — the only tracked artifact of the scan:

- **132,312,039 REJECTED records** across 165 batch files, **43 cells**,
  **131,649 distinct `kill_pattern` values**.
- **H(kill_pattern | cell) = 3.119 bits** — this is the measured conditional entropy.

## 2. Four corrections

**(a) "~2 bits" is not the committed statistic.** It is log₂(4) — the *vocabulary ceiling* of
the modal cell family, honest with its "≤" in Ergon's own text but not an entropy and not
corpus-weighted. The measured figure is **3.119 bits**. Cite that, or cite the vocabulary claim
in its own words ("68% of the corpus lives in cells whose entire pattern vocabulary is ≤8
labels"), but never the two fused into "132M rows with ~2 bits."

**(b) The per-cell table is prose-only (ATK-006).** The load-bearing breakdown — "27 of 43
cells / 89,949,006 records / 68.0%", the per-cell counts, the 0.0-bit cells — appears in
`ergon/probe/CORPUS_CHARACTERIZATION_FOR_R2-6_2026-08-22.md` sourced from `scan_state.json`,
which is **not committed, has no git history, and is not on disk**. The committed
`full_scan.json` contains no per-cell fields and cannot support the table. Per the standing rule
(M3_STATUS §6): a number that cannot be regenerated by a committed command is E0. **Fix is
cheap — commit `scan_state.json`, or re-emit the per-cell block into `full_scan.json`.**

**(c) The estimator behind it is fail-dangerous (ATK-014, executable).** `corpus_scan_full.py`
computes each cell's conditional distribution from patterns *exclusive* to that cell, discarding
every cross-cell pattern. It is therefore correct only when crossing = 0 — the hypothesis under
test. Demonstrated: `python attacks/probes/atk014_confirmatory_estimator.py` → ground truth
0.9183 bits, reported **0.0000**. The committed 3.119 is fine *today* only because crossing is
currently 0, and Ergon himself ruled that zero a tautology of the raw prefixed form. **Anyone
who does the obviously-correct next thing — measure the prefix-stripped form — gets a
confidently wrong answer unless the estimator is fixed first.**

**(d) Wrong-population risk when connecting to the thesis.** These are **Theseus's** 132M
rejections. The B_eff paradox in THESIS v4 concerns **Techne's 360M** kills. Substituting one
for the other would be the fourth wrong-population error in a week (after the free-lane quota
~25×, the "+14pp host delta" that settled at +5.8pp, and the 33.6% nulls). Also: B_eff is bits
÷ **search cost**, and **no search cost was measured** — the §9 retrodiction is half-run, and
Ergon's artifacts never reference B_eff at all. This is the first direct measurement of the
bits-per-verdict *numerator* anywhere in the program. That is a real first. It is not the ratio.

## 3. What to cite instead — the finding that survives all four corrections

Two results from the same scan are solid, and the second is the more valuable one:

1. **The designated failure residue is too impoverished to build on.** For most of the corpus,
   `kill_pattern` — the field the substrate designated as its failure signature — is a ≤3-bit
   label, and in 12.6% of records a literal constant. `canonical_claim_text` and `claim_payload`
   are 100% populated and **have never been scanned**. Any retrieval or D2/D3 design keyed on
   `kill_pattern` retrieves a near-constant. This lands on **R2-5**, which the charter orders
   *after* Tier B.
2. **The same-cell null is not a null.** P(same `kill_pattern` | same cell) = **0.4365** — a
   "null" that hands the solver the target's exact designated failure mode on ~44% of draws is a
   **treatment arm**. Cross-cell is trivially arm-identifying. This independently corroborates
   Charon's D1/D2 `INADMISSIBLE-NO-FAIR-NULL` ruling **at corpus scale, on a different
   population, in the substrate's own units** — the first time that ruling has been checked
   against the substrate rather than the probe. It is direct input to **R2-6** (the transfer
   comparator), which remains the clearest open design problem on the board.
