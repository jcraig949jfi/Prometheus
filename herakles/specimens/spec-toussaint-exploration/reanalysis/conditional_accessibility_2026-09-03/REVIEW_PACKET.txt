3. PREREGISTERED RA-1 ESTIMATOR, AND WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Partial Spearman of accessibility against gain, conditioning on current fitness,
computed on the runs that still have headroom at t.

    rho_partial = (r_AG - r_AF * r_GF) / sqrt((1 - r_AF^2)(1 - r_GF^2))

  A = md_on(t)        B = gain(t, h) = best(t+h) - best(t)      F = best(t)

Rank-based rather than regression residualisation because gain is heavily tied,
bounded above by -best(t), and best is discretised to multiples of 1/25. An OLS
residual assumes a linear conditional mean on variables that are neither
continuous nor linearly related. A rank partial is also the conditional analogue
of the marginal Spearman K7 used, so the comparison is like for like.

Restriction to runs with headroom IS the treatment of H-2, not a workaround. A
run at the optimum contributes gain = 0 by construction and can only add a tie
whose value is fixed by F.

Stability guard: a point with |r_GF| > 0.95 or |r_AF| > 0.95 has a
near-zero denominator and is recorded UNSTABLE, never reported as a large
effect.

Ordering disclosure, made in the preregistration itself rather than here: the
data audit including the horizon-feasibility table was inspected BEFORE the
eligibility thresholds were set. No association between accessibility and
acquisition existed in any form before the plan was frozen and committed.

6. WHY THAT COMPARISON COULD NOT HAVE COME OUT DIFFERENTLY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
At the 100->500 window, beta=0.1:

  alpha    n with headroom at t=100   r(F, G)     1 - r(F,G)^2   partial
  -----    ------------------------   --------    ------------   ---------
  0.03                          21    -1.0000          0.00000   UNDEFINED
  0.06                           8    (E1 fails)             -           -

The Spearman between current fitness and subsequent gain is exactly minus one.
Once a run reaches the optimum, gain = 0 - best(t) = -best(t) identically, so
gain is a deterministic decreasing function of the conditioner. The partial
correlation has a zero denominator. It is not small; it does not exist.

This is Lexis's H-2, and it is stronger than argued: at the very window where K7
was computed, no conditional statistic is definable at all.

Read carefully, because part of that is structural: md_on(t+h) is contemporaneous

currently is, and gives no support to reading it as a leading indicator.

NC2 is the permutation null above and it did not need to be beaten, because
nothing reached significance to begin with.

12. IS MACHINERY COUNT SUFFICIENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Count is not quite the right word. PRESENCE is.

nops alone accounts for a rank R-squared of 0.74 and 0.54, and the bin table
shows almost all of it is the zero-to-one step. Beyond one operator the detector
is flat. So the detector is largely reading:

    does this genome contain at least one production rule?

A residual generation trend does survive inside strata, mean absolute 0.29 and
0.26, and it is largest inside the floor-zero bin at +0.65 and +0.76. But that
bin is the least trustworthy: nops in these rows is a POPULATION MEAN, so the
floor-zero bin contains populations averaging anywhere in [0, 1) operators and
is internally heterogeneous. The residual is therefore not clean evidence of
anything beyond presence.

Consequence, applying the prompt's own instruction. Language of the form
"evolved reorganisation of the generator" or "history changed machinery
organisation" is NOT supported by these rows. The defensible statement concerns
accumulated machinery, and specifically its presence. CP-REPRESENTATION-REWRITE
is downgraded accordingly in HC_T01_REANALYSIS_CORRECTIONS.md.

Not proven, and not claimed: that structural organisation plays no role. RA-2
shows count-or-presence is largely sufficient here. It does not show
organisation is absent, and the prompt's warning against jumping from "not
count" to "organisation" applies symmetrically in the other direction.

13. RECIPROCAL-TRANSPLANT FEASIBILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    TRANSPLANT_REQUIRES_NEW_COMPUTE

The frozen rows preserve ONE genotype per checkpoint, in parseable form, being
the shortest genotype that still encodes the correct phenotype. The POPULATION is
not preserved, and HC-T01's detector is a population mean over 100 individuals.
Matching it would require regenerating populations by deterministic replay, which
is cheap at about 8 seconds per run but is new compute and outside this pass.

Qualification: a weaker single-genotype transplant IS feasible with zero
evolution, by parsing the preserved string and calling the existing detector
under both settings. It would answer a narrower question than HC-T01 asked and
could not be compared with any HC-T01 number. Recorded as an option, not a
recommendation. Nothing was run.

16. LIMITATIONS AND AMBIGUITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The eligibility thresholds, 15 runs with headroom and 8 distinct gain values,
were set after seeing the feasibility table and before computing any association.
The prompt permits inspecting distributions for estimator validity; the ordering
is disclosed rather than hidden, and a reviewer who prefers different thresholds
can recompute from RA1_TIME_SERIES.csv, which reports every candidate point
including those excluded.

The stability guard at 0.95 is a judgement. It is vindicated at the extreme,
where r(F,G) is exactly -1, but points at 0.94 pass and points at 0.96 do not.
The sensitivity table reports every unguarded value so the guard's effect is
fully visible.

nops is a population mean, not a per-genome integer, so RA-2's integer bins
conflate populations of differing composition. The floor-zero bin is the worst
affected and it carries the largest residual trend.

The ablated arm has no operators, so its RA-1 result speaks about the detector
and not about the mechanism. It should not be read as a test of the HC-T01 claim.

NC1's strength is partly structural, since md_on(t+h) is contemporaneous with
best(t+h). It is diagnostic of direction, not a clean effect size.

Only four points in the entire dataset yielded a stable partial, and each of the
four has a bootstrap interval spanning zero. Even the two cells that produced a
verdict rest on two points each.

The Lexis corrections in section 15 are taken on trust from a sibling seat.

re-analysis of these rows will repair it.

statistic and the machinery it measures are this close to the same variable?
