4. T0 MECHANICAL EFFECT, AND WHY THE DIRECTIVE WAS RIGHT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  M(P_0) modular degree ......... 0.00000  EXACTLY
  M(P_0) neutral degree ......... -0.00004

The generation-zero null is VACUOUS in this experiment. Experiment 2 starts
from the one-symbol egg cell <a>. A second-type mutation needs a subsequence of
length at least two to extract, so at generation zero beta has literally
nothing to act on and the mechanical contrast is identically zero.

Had HC-T01 relied on the generation-zero null alone, as the original
preregistration proposed, it would have had NO baseline at all. The
contemporaneous frozen-population probe was not a refinement of the design. It
was the experiment.

The contemporaneous mechanical contrast inside evolved populations is also
small:

  M_on(t), modular degree, all checkpoints ...... +0.003 to +0.027
  noise floor ................................... 0.021

So turning the knob NOW, on a population that has already evolved, moves the
detector by about the width of the measurement error.

The preregistration confines any precedence claim to a window in which the
run-level outcome slope is indistinguishable from zero, precisely so that a

peaked and begun to fall.

There is no plateau while the signal emerges, so T2 was not attempted. The
one-checkpoint lead is NOT reported as precedence. This is the preregistration
doing its job against the seat's own interest.

16. WHAT THIS RESULT DOES NOT ESTABLISH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
It does NOT establish that the accessibility change predicts, causes or
explains acquisition. K7 fired. Current fitness is a better predictor.

It does NOT establish temporal precedence. T2 was not attempted because the
historical design provides no fitness plateau while the signal emerges.

It does NOT establish that the effect is adaptive, or that broader or narrower
exploration is better. No direction was predicted and none is claimed.

It does NOT establish generality. One substrate, one target, one period, one
task. The primary statistic is tailored to a period-5 target and does not
transfer as written.

It does NOT fully escape the tautology concern. Populations evolved at beta = 0
cannot contain operators, so part of the contrast is structural. What the
experiment adds is that the difference is MEASURED rather than asserted, that
its magnitude is quantified against the instrument's own noise, that the
contemporaneous mechanical component is roughly 100 to 600 times smaller than
the historical component, and that the difference persists at identical
phenotype and identical fitness.

It does NOT reproduce the thesis's post-generation-200 stability. Our genomes
bloat where the record says they fluctuate.

17. RECOMMENDATION FOR EVCA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EvCA remains FROZEN and untouched, as directed. HC-T01 consumed no part of the
EvCA gate.

HC-T01 sharpens what EvCA should look for. The transferable move is not the
modular degree, which is substrate-specific, but the METHOD: freeze the
population, apply the identical variation operator across arms that differ only
in history, and take the difference-in-differences against a measured
generation-zero baseline. In EvCA the analogue is to freeze an evolved rule
population, apply the identical mutation operator, and measure the distribution
of reachable rule behaviours, contrasting lineages that did and did not pass
through a particular structural transition.

One warning carries over directly. Build the acquisition-prediction comparison
against cheap state variables INTO the EvCA design from the start. In HC-T01
that comparison is what capped the result, and it would have been cheaper to
know first.
