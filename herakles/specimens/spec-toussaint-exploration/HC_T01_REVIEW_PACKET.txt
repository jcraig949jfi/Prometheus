1. CLAIM UNDER REVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Asserted: by 2003 Marc Toussaint's programme already contained every component
of a longitudinal accessibility detector -- a formal exploration-distribution
concept, a working Monte Carlo estimator, per-generation population-wide
application, and a mechanism ablation with an adaptive outcome -- but the
estimator was never run inside the ablation.

This packet answers only whether that is historically true, and whether the
experiment that fills the cell is reconstructable. It reports NO experimental
result, because no compute has been run.

What would have killed the claim, pre-stated:
  - the combined experiment existing anywhere in Toussaint's corpus
  - the combined experiment existing in any bibliographic descendant
  - the ablation turning out not to be a real 2x2, or to lack acquisition
  - parameters too under-specified to reconstruct

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Object        phenotypic exploration distribution Xi_sigma: the distribution
                over the phenotype of ONE offspring of a fixed parent, under
                the model's full mutation operator
  Estimator     Monte Carlo, 2000 samples PER INDIVIDUAL PER GENERATION
  Coverage      whole offspring population, lambda = 100
  Longitudinal  yes, 1000 generations
  Statistics    neutral degree n = Xi_sigma(parent phenotype)
                normalised mutual information matrix over 25 phenotypic
                  variables, I' = 2 I_ij / (H_i + H_j)
                modular degree: summed probability that a variation at

4. THE ABLATION, AS RECOVERED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: same thesis, section 1.5.4, Figure 1.7.

  Design        a genuine 2x2, confirmed against the seat's own doubt
  Axis 1        second-type mutations, beta = 0.1 versus beta = 0
  Axis 2        first-type mutation rate, alpha = 0.03 versus alpha = 0.06
  Replicates    10 independent trials per cell, 40 runs
  Mechanism     second-type mutations are the structural rewrites that create,
                apply, invert and delete production rules. beta = 0 freezes
                the representation as a direct encoding.
  Outcome       fitness only: negative percentage of symbols not matching the
                target 5 x abcde
  Acquisition   REAL. Starts from a one-symbol egg cell with no operators and
                must acquire a modular encoding. This is the decisive
                difference from the CEC 2002 sphere experiment, where there is
                no what, only a how fast. Claim level T3 is therefore
                available.

7. RECOVERED CODE, AND WHY IT SHARPENS THE CLAIM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The GECCO 2003 plants paper says source code was on the author's home page.
That page is gone; the Internet Archive holds the 2003 site. Recovered: the
FULL SOURCE of 02-stringRuleJTB/main.cpp, the driver for the string-rule
experiments, plus MT/evolution.h and the plants driver.

Three facts follow, all machine-checked:

  1. The string-rule driver's monitor() computes the detector for the best
     individual AND loops it over the entire population, and the loop is
     enabled, not compiled out.
  2. evolution.h calls monitor() once per generation. The instrument was wired
     into the simulation loop, not bolted onto one experiment.
  3. The plants driver's monitor() computes NO detector, exactly matching the
     published plants figures.

So the same instrumented binary plausibly produced detector output for BOTH
string-rule experiments, and only Experiment 1's output was ever plotted. This
is tagged INFERRED, not verified: the headers containing spectrum() were never
crawled and no run logs survive.

The code also computes a FIFTH statistic that appears in no figure anywhere:
avgfit, the mean fitness of the sampled offspring, per individual, per
generation. It is the most direct evolvability measure in the whole programme
and it was never reported.

10. CONTROLS AND PRE-STATED WAYS THIS COULD BE VACUOUS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TRAP 1 IS LIVE. beta parameterises the very operator that induces the
detector, so "the arms differ on the detector" is TRUE BY CONSTRUCTION and is
not a finding. A generation-zero MECHANICAL-EFFECT NULL is therefore mandatory
-- same initial population, each arm's operator settings, no selection, no
reproduction. The preregistered claim is the EXCESS over that null, and
whether the excess GROWS as populations restructure.

TRAP 2 IS NOT FATAL. The detector and the outcome are not two readings of one
quantity, and a genuine fitness plateau exists in the historical record, so a
T2 precedence window is available conditional on an explicit coupling check.
avgfit is excluded from the precedence analysis as coupled by construction.

Pre-stated conditions that make the experiment worthless, each a first-class
negative to be reported and not rescued:
  - the mechanical null accounts for the whole between-arm difference at all
    times, so the mechanism moves the detector definitionally
  - estimator noise at 2000 samples exceeds the between-arm difference, so the
    historical instrument cannot see its own experiment
  - the reconstruction misses validation target V1, V2 or V6

Unit of analysis is frozen as THE RUN. Not the generation, individual,
offspring sample or lineage point.

11. KNOWN LIMITATIONS AND CAVEATS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE CLAIM IS NARROWER THAN THE SEAT SET OUT WITH, AND THIS IS THE MOST
IMPORTANT CAVEAT. Kouvaris, Clune, Kounios, Brede and Watson 2017 measures a
Monte Carlo sampled phenotype distribution longitudinally across all arms of a
manipulation and relates arm to adaptation rate. It differs in three specific
ways: it samples uniformly over the whole genotype space as a drift
approximation rather than the one-step offspring neighbourhood; its
manipulated variable is selective pressure rather than an operator switch; and
its acquisition link is arm-level on frozen architectures rather than
within-run. So the defensible claim is that nobody has composed a
ONE-VARIATION-STEP offspring distribution inside an ON/OFF ABLATION of a
variation operator related to acquisition WITHIN RUNS.

Five documented access gaps, recorded as gaps and not counted as negatives.
Three are not load-bearing because a superseding full text was read. The two
that remain:
  - Webb 2016 thesis, Manchester: Cloudflare defeated four retrieval routes.
    Fails criterion C regardless, since it manipulates selection.
  - Bornhofen and Lattaud 2008, evolving L-systems with neutral networks:
    closed access, no repository, no author copy. This is the closest SYSTEM
    to Toussaint's in the whole descendant set and it is the most material
    gap. This seat also failed to close it.

The inference that the same instrumented binary produced Experiment 2 is
INFERRED and cannot be verified. It is not required for the verdict.

No compute has been run. Nothing here is an experimental result.

The seat proposed this claim and it is convenient for the programme. The
in-corpus negative is machine-checkable; the descendants negative rests on 24
screenings with a full search log, and a reviewer who wants to attack this
should attack the screening criterion or the five access gaps.
