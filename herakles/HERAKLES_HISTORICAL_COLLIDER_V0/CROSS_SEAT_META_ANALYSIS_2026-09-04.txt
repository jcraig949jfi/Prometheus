2. NEW CONVERGENCE A: TWO SEATS RAN A SAME-PROBE CONDITIONAL TEST AND
   BOTH GOT AN EMPTY CONDITIONING SET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the sharpest finding in this report and neither existing comparison
contains it, because it needs two EXECUTED results and Elenchus and Lexis do not
execute.

  HERAKLES, RA-1, 2026-09-03
      Preregistered partial Spearman of accessibility against subsequent gain,
      conditioning on current fitness, on runs that still had headroom.
      RESULT: zero eligible points in BOTH treated cells at both horizons.
      CAUSE: the outcome is a deterministic function of the conditioner. At the
      original K7 window the fitness-to-gain Spearman is EXACTLY -1.0000 and
      1 - r^2 is 0.00000. There is no residual to associate anything with.

  TECHNE, E1, 2026-09-04
      Preregistered same-probe counterfactual on evolved representations.
      RESULT: every arm returned INSUFFICIENT_MATCHES with ZERO matched pairs,
      including both internal controls.
      CAUSE: treatments A and B were given DISJOINT trait supports, so they are
      exactly orthogonal. Cross-history minimum distance 27.1 against a
      within-history median nearest neighbour of 0.38, a factor of 72.

Two seats, two substrates, two preregistrations, and the same structural
outcome: THE SET THE TEST CONDITIONS ON IS EMPTY. They got there from opposite
directions. Herakles had overlap and no residual variation. Techne had residual
variation and no overlap.

Techne states the missing precondition in its own words, and it generalises to
both:

    a same-probe counterfactual needs treatments that differ in the generator
    AND overlap in the observable being conditioned on. I maximised the first
    and drove the second to zero. The preregistration never stated a
    positivity/overlap requirement, so nothing checked it.

  CONSEQUENCE, AND IT IS A DESIGN RULE THIS PROGRAMME DOES NOT YET HAVE.
  Every same-probe counterfactual must, BEFORE freezing, compute and report the
  size of its own conditioning set on the actual data it will use. Not the
  effect. Not the power. The count of units that will survive conditioning.

  Both seats got the interpretation right despite the failure, and that is the
  part worth protecting: Techne wrote "this is not a null and K2 remains
  untouched"; Herakles returned INDETERMINATE and refused to collapse it into
  NO_SIGNAL. An empty conditioning set is an instrument failure. Reporting it as
  a negative result would have been the real error.

3. NEW CONVERGENCE B: THE PRINCIPLED CONSTRUCT LOSES TO THE ARBITRARY BASELINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Three seats, three substrates, and in each the carefully motivated quantity
fails against a comparator that embodies no theory at all.

  HERAKLES, K7        Accessibility statistics were beaten by CURRENT FITNESS,
                      the cheapest state variable available, at Spearman -1.000
                      against -0.950 and -0.765. The structured detector added
                      nothing over a free measurement.

  ERGON, Gen-1B       Selective retention beat the inherited MRU policy by
                      +2.78 percentage points, Holm 0.0040, and did NOT beat
                      ARBITRARY MEMORY after correction. And the sharper one:
                      terminal library overlap BETWEEN policy arms, Jaccard
                      0.003 to 0.045, is indistinguishable from overlap between
                      lineages WITHIN the same arm, 0.002 to 0.005. The policy
                      leaves no recoverable signature in the artifact identity.

  TECHNE, Crucible 3  Every structured coordinate system scored BELOW RANDOM at
                      predicting a held-out methodological property it was not
                      built from.

The three are independent. None cites the others. The common shape is that the
principled construct is being asked to beat a baseline that is either free
(fitness), unstructured (arbitrary memory) or noise (random), and it does not.

  WHY THIS IS NOT JUST THREE NULLS. In all three the construct DOES behave
  lawfully on its own terms. Accessibility separates the arms at 24 to 108 times
  noise. Selective retention beats the policy it was designed to beat. The
  coordinate systems classify well. What fails is the step from internal
  lawfulness to external predictive value.

  Elenchus and Techne independently named a neighbouring pattern, that a result
  is rigorous exactly where the phenomenon is not. This is its measurement-side
  twin: a construct is well-behaved exactly where it is being scored against
  itself.

  A partial explanation for the Herakles instance arrived from Elenchus and is
  worth generalising as a hypothesis, not a conclusion: the Kashtan and Alon
  detector is CONTENT-ADDRESSED, keyed to a named goal set, whereas HC-T01's
  three statistics are all BREADTH measures. Text S1 reports that organisms
  under unstructured variation vary MORE broadly and score LOWER. If that
  generalises, a breadth statistic has no reason to beat fitness anywhere, and
  two of the three instances above may be the same mistake.

8. WHAT THE OVERLAP MEANS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Five seats read five different literatures over four days and converged on six
patterns. Not one of the six is about evolution. Every one is about measurement.

  instruments exist and are not reported
  results are rigorous where the phenomenon is not
  decision rules are fixed without checking their own attainable range
  conditioning sets are empty and the test never runs
  principled constructs lose to free, unstructured or random baselines
  degenerate measurements are caught late, by internal checks, not reviewers

The programme's working hypothesis has been that the historical literature is
missing compositions. What five seats actually found is that the literature is
missing REPORTING, and that this programme reproduces the same defects at speed.
Every convergence above has at least one instance authored by a seat here, in
the last four days, not by a 2003 thesis.

  THE OPERATIONAL READING. The Collider's scarce resource is not detectors and
  not compute. It is a correctly drawn population, a conditioning set that is
  non-empty, and a baseline that is hard to beat. All three are cheap to check
  in advance and none of the five seats checked all three.

9. FOUR RULES THIS ANALYSIS PRODUCES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  R1  Before freezing any conditional or same-probe design, compute and report
      the SIZE OF THE CONDITIONING SET on the data that will be used. An empty
      set is an instrument failure, never a null.

  R2  Name the hardest cheap baseline in the preregistration, not the most
      interesting comparator. Free, unstructured and random baselines have
      beaten three principled constructs in this corpus in four days.

  R3  Before publishing any novelty claim, grep the repository's recent commits
      for the same specimen. Elenchus's rule, adopted here. The cheapest
      disconfirmation of "nobody has measured this" is a sibling's commit
      message, and it has now been the actual disconfirmation twice.

  R4  Draw the population from the phenomenon and from at least two seed
      lineages, and write the seed choice into the claim. Both of this seat's
      frame errors were single-lineage populations screened impeccably.
