## Honest reading of this version

- **Zero artifacts recovered.** Manifest J is still empty. This pass read papers; it did not obtain code or data.
- **Zero reconstructions built. Zero SFE worlds created.**
- **The blind-spot hypothesis was over-reach.** Altenberg 1994 formalised evolvability as a property of the offspring distribution and gave a rate law for its change; Mengistu, Lehman and Clune 2016 measure reachable-region size directly, with the measure inherited from work published in 2011 or earlier. Both were on the seat's own list of places to look, and only two of eight candidates were checked. The residual claim is narrow and may not be quoted until the other six are cleared.
- **The V0 registries contained one error that would have burned the first experiment.** The particle-strategy transition rate was recalled as a single "rare" figure. It is two different experiments: 0 of 50 runs in the Physica D configuration, and 7 of 300 (2.3%) in the PPSN III configuration. A reconstruction of the former would have targeted a setup in which the phenomenon never occurred.
- **What survived verification exactly as recalled:** the full EvCA parameter set, and Lindgren's gene-duplication operator including its neutrality, which the paper states in its own words.
- Field map A still carries roughly 120 families from recall. Only three are sourced. Treat every other date, count and "source survives" belief as a lead.
