3. THE FINDING THAT MATTERS MOST: HCA-2 IS ALREADY DONE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Petak, Frati, Vroomans, Pespeni and Cheney 2025, "The variability of
evolvability", PNAS. Read in full as the bioRxiv preprint.

  substrate ............ recurrent gene-regulatory network developing a
                         spatiotemporal expression pattern, selected against
                         patterns from elementary cellular automata rules
  machinery matched .... YES. Identical architecture, identical mutation rule,
                         identical selection. ONLY the environment-switching
                         schedule differs.
  operator ............. identical, and the SAME operator is reused for the
                         accessibility sampling
  detector ............. LOCAL. 2500 clones for robustness, 10000 mutated
                         clones for phenotypic variability, applied to the
                         population's OWN current best genotype
  longitudinal ......... YES, slopes taken after each environmental change
  replication .......... 15 replicates with 95 percent confidence intervals

And the row that decides everything:

  predictor                                         r-squared    p
  ----------------------------------------------    ---------    ------
  local mutational-variance accessibility measure       0.502    0.0067
  cross-environment fitness correlation (cheap)         0.089    0.279 (n.s.)

That is the literal criterion HC-T01 failed. An accessibility-shaped variable
outpredicting a matched current-state fitness variable, in a machinery-matched
substrate, published, with code at
github.com/Cpetak/Variability_of_Evolvability and data at Zenodo
10.5281/zenodo.16094874.

  HCA-1 supported in a second substrate ............ YES
  HCA-2 supported at all ........................... YES, here
  Stop condition S3 ................................ FIRES

Caveats, and they are not small. The version read is the preprint, not the
copy-edited PNAS text. The regression rests on 15 data points, one per
landscape pair. Two outlier rules were explicitly excluded from it. So this is
a real effect and a fragile one, and the honest description is that the field
has cleared the bar once, thinly.

4. A LANE CONFLICT, RESOLVED FROM PRIMARY SOURCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Two lanes disagreed about Kounios et al. 2016, arXiv 1612.05955. One reported
an internal frozen-depth sub-analysis reaching H4. The Ergon lane reported the
basin detector measured in a single unablated condition. This seat read the
paper. THE ERGON LANE IS CORRECT.

  Figures 4 and 5, the freeze experiment: G-P maps frozen at episodes 800 and
  1600, 1000 genotypes microevolved for 40000 generations under each, alongside
  a one-to-one map. The reported outcome is MEDIAN FITNESS and endpoint
  fitness histograms. No accessibility distribution is measured anywhere.

  Figure 8, the basin detector: the caption states the experiment evolves "only
  the regulatory interactions under natural selection for 1600 evolutionary
  episodes", sampling 10000 genotypes against maps from the end of every
  episode. ONE condition. No arms. No ablation.

So Kounios 2016 does NOT reach H4. What it is instead is more interesting:

THE SAME MISSING CELL AS TOUSSAINT, IN A SECOND PAPER, FOUR MONTHS EARLIER. A
longitudinal accessibility detector in one experiment. Machinery-MATCHED
history arms with an acquisition outcome in another. Never composed.

Finding that structural failure twice, independently, in unrelated substrates,
makes it a property of the field rather than of one author. That is a stronger
and more useful claim than HC-T01's original one.

It also hands us the cheapest experiment in the programme. The 800-versus-1600
frozen maps are machinery-matched, static, and already held locally. Running a
detector across them is a machinery-matched H2 test that attacks precisely the
confound that downgraded HC-T01, at negligible compute.

5. PART C DOES NOT EXIST, AND HERE IS HOW IT GETS FAKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ten search lines were worked. Nothing assembles shaper plus detector plus
controller. The closest single candidate:

  Edmonds 1998, Meta-Genetic Programming, CPM Report 98-32.
    online estimate ....... YES. Operator fitness is the measured average
                            proportionate fitness change in the operand genes,
                            from 50 sampled operand pairs per operator per
                            generation, three-generation running average.
    fed back .............. YES. That score IS the operator's fitness, and
                            ordinary selection on the OPERATOR population
                            constructs the next generation's variation
                            machinery.
    generative ............ YES. Operators are tree structures over primitives,
                            so novel operator forms can be assembled. Not a
                            menu.
    shortfall ............. the estimate is BACKWARD-LOOKING, a realised effect
                            of operators already applied, not a forward estimate
                            of a distribution over future consequences
    author's own caveat ... the loop is fragile: it destroyed population
                            diversity in initial trials, needed manual fixes, is
                            sensitive to operator syntax, and degrades under an
                            unrelated depth ceiling which the author says
                            "disrupts the effective estimation of the operator's
                            effectiveness"

CMA-ES is the strongest engineered analogue and is precisely specified and
robust, which is exactly what Edmonds is not. But its update law is fixed by
the authors, and what it estimates is the covariance of PAST selected steps.

  THE WARNING, WHICH IS THE MOST USEFUL NEGATIVE IN THIS PASS

Four independent lines show the way future variation is generated CHANGING over
a run, with NO estimate computed or consulted anywhere:

  Baeck self-adaptive GA ......... mutation rate in the genome, pure hitchhiking
  Altenberg 1994 ................. constructional selection in GP
  Spector 2001 Pushpop ........... reproduction strategy drifts sexual to
  asexual
  mutator alleles ................ in Avida and in biology

Altenberg states it himself: "the constructional fitness is never evaluated by
the GP algorithm, nor is it obtained by averaging current program fitnesses."

Observing that variational behaviour changed over a run is not evidence of a
controller. Four substrates prove it. Any future Part-C claim in this programme
must exhibit the estimate and the feedback pathway, never the outcome. Recorded
as a standing rule in registry R.

7. THE DETECTOR STACK, AND TWO THINGS WORTH STEALING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Fourteen rows in registry S. Two matter beyond their row.

Smith, Husbands, Layzell and O'Shea 2002, "Fitness Landscapes and
Evolvability". Read in full. It independently invented the offspring-
distribution detector A YEAR BEFORE Toussaint's thesis, in the Cavalli-Sforza,
Feldman and Altenberg transmission-function formalism. It enumerates the full
one-mutant neighbourhood, it defines four statistics on the offspring-fitness
distribution, and critically it VALIDATED THE MEASURE ONLINE, computing it from
genotypes collected during a real hill-climbing run and showing the biased
online sample gives the same qualitative predictions as unbiased sampling.

Then it stopped one question short. The portrait is built BY conditioning on
fitness, plotting evolvability as a function of current fitness, and the paper
never asks the marginal question. Our K7 is the question this line set up and
did not ask.

van Nimwegen, Crutchfield and Huynen 1999, "Neutral evolution of mutational
robustness". Average neutral degree equals the principal eigenvalue of the
neutral network's adjacency matrix, and a population at quasi-equilibrium
samples that eigenvector by construction. So the statistic is inferable from
population data already being collected. Effectively zero marginal cost,
against our 2000x. Worth stealing on cost grounds alone if the
quasi-equilibrium precondition can be met.

Also on record, and it is the mirror of our own failure: Weinberger's
correlation length is DOCUMENTED BLIND to neutrality even when neutrality
demonstrably changes search speed. Our detector saw a difference that did not
matter; that one misses a difference that does. Both directions are now in
registry R.

How many detectors in the stack were ever tested for predictive power after
conditioning on current performance? One. Petak 2025. Everything else is a
static landscape characterisation or an aggregate difficulty predictor. The
strongest named predictive result in the stack, a local-optima-network model
explaining 99 percent of variance, predicts ALGORITHM success across problem
INSTANCES, which is the wrong unit entirely.

8. HISTORY VERSUS HIDDEN STATE, DIRECTIVE SECTION 8
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The directive warns against overclaiming path dependence when ordinary hidden
state suffices. Applying that discipline honestly:

  STATE-LOCATION ACCESSIBILITY ........ SUPPORTED. Everything found is
                                        consistent with current hidden state
                                        determining future reachability.
  HISTORY-CONDITIONED ACCESSIBILITY ... NOT SEPARATED from the above by any work
                                        found, including our own.
  PATH-DEPENDENT ACCESSIBILITY ........ NO EVIDENCE. Nothing found requires
                                        information persisting outside the
                                        measured state.

In HC-T01 the two arms differ in rule-table content, which is present state. In
Petak the arms differ in evolved network weights, which is present state. In
Kounios the frozen maps differ in accumulated regulatory strength, which is
present state.

So the honest answer to directive question 8 is that hidden current
representation is sufficient, and history has not been shown to be necessary
anywhere. Which makes the directive's section 9 the live question rather than a
fallback: the finding is that phenotype and fitness are insufficient state
descriptions, and the useful research programme is asking WHICH representation
coordinates carry the information. That is a smaller and more tractable claim
than historical memory, and it is the one the evidence supports.

AND ONE SUBSTRATE INVERTS THE SIGN. Ancel and Fontana 2000 compared three
sequence classes sharing an identical minimum-free-energy structure and
differing only in history: random, neutrally evolved, and canalised. Their
neutralities are 0.184, 0.412 and 0.456. The class with the RICHEST history and
the HIGHEST robustness became an evolutionary dead end. More history bought
LESS subsequent acquisition.

So history is not merely unnecessary as an explanation. Where it has been
varied and followed through to acquisition, it has once pointed the wrong way.
Any claim in this programme that accumulated history improves future
accessibility must now carry that counterexample.

9. NEUTRAL NETWORKS: THE ANSWER IS YES, AND IT WAS SETTLED IN 2008
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The lane returned after first drafting and it overturns the cautious answer
written here. Neutral-network position is the cleanest natural control, AND the
field has already used it, with exact enumeration rather than sampling.

Wagner 2008, 'Robustness and evolvability: a paradox resolved', Proc R Soc B.
Sequences are drawn from the SAME neutral network by inverse folding, so
phenotype is identical by construction and fitness identical by assumption. All
3n one-mutant neighbours are ENUMERATED for 75,000 sequences of length 30. The
result, in the paper's own words: "most structures found near two or more
sequences sampled from the same neutral network are different from each other",
median overlap Q = 0.6.

That is HCA-1, at matched phenotype, matched fitness, matched machinery, exact
rather than estimated, published in 2008. HC-T01 was not the first observation
of this phenomenon and is not the cleanest.

Draghi et al. 2010, Supplementary Information sections 6.1 and 6.2, read
verbatim. This answers the directive's specific question. SI 6.1 is a clean H2
at matched phenotype: neutral neighbours with an identical fold have
significantly different accessible-phenotype sets, with roughly 30 per cent of
the neighbourhood redrawn by ONE neutral mutation, from 100,000 sampled
genotypes per length. And SI 6.2 DOES contain a longitudinal
accessibility-and-acquisition measurement on evolving populations.

But it fails the conditioning test BY CONSTRUCTION, and this is the sharpest
methodological finding of the lane. The accessibility parameters were not
tested against robustness as competing predictors; they were MODELLED AS
FUNCTIONS OF IT. Verbatim: "the mutation rate to high-fitness types declined
monotonically with q in the RNA data, and so we used a linear regression of
this relationship in our analytic predictions." Accessibility and the cheap
state are collinear on purpose. It is a near-miss of exactly our kind, and it
is re-runnable.

Greenbury, Schaper, Ahnert and Louis 2016, and Aguirre et al. 2011. The
best-quantified accessibility measurement in the lane: 10,000 genotype triples
per genotype-phenotype map, full one-mutation neighbourhood enumerated,
Bhattacharyya coefficient comparing neighbourhood phenotype distributions with
mutual neighbours excluded. Genotypes sharing a phenotype have systematically
more similar neighbourhoods than genotypes that do not. Acquisition arm:
COMPLETELY ABSENT in both.

And a number this programme should size its bets against: outside RNA the
effect is modest, 1.025 to 1.063. A programme betting on this phenomenon in a
non-RNA-like substrate should expect a small effect and compute the standard
error before choosing any gate.

So the answer to question 7 is yes, and the gap is not where the directive
supposed. Accessibility at matched state in this lineage is measured, exactly,
repeatedly, and has been for seventeen years. What nobody in the lineage does
is connect it to subsequent acquisition in a way that survives conditioning on
robustness. The missing cell is the acquisition link, not the detector.

  TWO RESULTS THAT CUT AGAINST THE PROGRAMME, AND THEY MATTER

Cowperthwaite et al. 2008. Exhaustive folding of ALL RNA sequences of length 12
to 18 gives the exact one-mutant neighbour-phenotype distribution per
phenotype. Across 2400 forward simulations, TARGET phenotype abundance
predicted whether a population reached it (r = 0.76, P = 2.2e-4). FOUNDING
phenotype abundance did not predict the outcome. Where you start, measured
exactly, did not matter. That is the closest thing to a direct refutation of
the programme's premise that anyone has produced.

Ancel and Fontana 2000, which the detector lane could not reach and this lane
did. Three sequence classes sharing an IDENTICAL minimum-free-energy structure,
differing in history: random, neutrally evolved, and canalised. Their
neutrality differs sharply, 0.184, 0.412 and 0.456. And the class with the
richest history and the highest robustness became an evolutionary DEAD END.
More history and more robustness bought LESS subsequent acquisition, not more.

The directive said do not assume the sign. One substrate has already inverted
it. A tautology warning travels with that specimen too: its accessibility
measure IS neutrality, so accessibility cannot be shown to beat robustness
there because the two are collinear by construction.

  ONE DISCREPANCY LEFT OPEN RATHER THAN RESOLVED

Draghi et al. 2010 characterise Wagner 2008 as having found "no relationship
between the robustness of a sequence, and a measure of its evolvability". The
primary text of Wagner 2008 reports a strong NEGATIVE relationship, Spearman s
= -0.64, p < 1e-17, and states plainly "the greater a sequence's robustness,
the lesser its evolvability". Either they refer to different measures or one
characterisation is wrong. Recorded in registry R and NOT silently resolved.

10. PARTS ARCHAEOLOGY VERDICT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    CP-REPRESENTATION-REWRITE promoted to a cross-substrate class ... NO

Registry T records five candidate implementations of the motif "phenotype-
preserving internal reorganisation changes the distribution of future
variation". Exactly one has an accessibility measurement, and its arms are not
machinery-matched. Every other implementation fails on one of three counts. It
is not phenotype-preserving (gene regulatory evolution, constructional
selection), it has no accessibility measurement (gene duplication, MVG), or its
intervention is selection-side rather than a reorganisation mechanism (Petak).

Petak strengthens the PHENOMENON without strengthening the PART. That
distinction is the whole of the answer to directive question 12.

What would promote it: an H2-or-better measurement of a phenotype-preserving
reorganisation under machinery-matched arms in a second substrate. The cheapest
route is the Kounios 800-versus-1600 frozen maps.

11. WHAT THIS DOES TO EVCA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EvCA remains FROZEN and unauthorised. Full design in
EVCA_HCA1_HCA2_DESIGN_NOTE.md. The four things that changed:

The question is now HCA-2, not HCA-1. Petak has established HCA-1 in a
machinery-matched substrate, so establishing it a third time is not worth a
run. The primary EvCA question becomes whether a same-state same-probe
accessibility signal predicts later transition probability beyond current
classification performance and cheap rule-state metrics.

EvCA's structural advantage is now the point of choosing it. Every rule is a
128-entry binary table under the same bit-flip operator, so the machinery-
presence confound cannot arise. And the one-step neighbourhood is EXACTLY 128
rules, fully enumerable, so the sampling error that dominated HC-T01's
estimator protocol is identically zero.

The cheap-state baseline is specified first, not last, and includes mean
neighbour performance, which is the EvCA analogue of the avgfit statistic that
beat our structured detector. The detector must beat that or it is another K7.

The detector must not be performance-based. Choosing a performance-derived
detector and then testing it against a performance baseline is how K7 fires.
The recommendation is a behavioural-distance cloud over the 128 neighbours as
primary, with the historical particle and domain descriptors as the
interpretable secondary. Recovering the historical particle-filtering method as
a detector part is the main prerequisite and it is not done.

One question for James, flagged rather than decided: Stage 0 is pure
archaeology on six recovered genomes and consumes nothing. Stage 1 onward
GENERATES new evolutionary histories, which is a different kind of object from
HC-T01 and may need separate authorisation.

unaddressed: the history arm simply carries more standing genetic variation,
with more mutations per molecule and greater diversity, all reported. So faster
adaptation may be a mutation-supply effect rather than a positional
accessibility effect. The paper measures the confound and never separates it.

That is the trap worth naming: a causal design can be strong, engineered rather
than inferred, and still leave the conditioning question entirely untouched. It
is our own K7 failure wearing better clothes.

14. RECOMMENDATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Four actions, in cost order. None is a new experiment. Two are re-analyses of
data that already exists, and one is reading a file we already hold.

FIRST, AND NEARLY FREE: steal the conditioning method from Wagner 2023. This is
the single most useful thing in the pass and it is a method, not a result.
Wagner defines an evolvability-enhancing mutation so that the neighbourhood
improvement must EXCEED the mutation's own fitness benefit. The additive
current-state contribution is subtracted before anything is claimed, so the
accessibility term is a residual rather than a fitness proxy. That is precisely
the discipline HC-T01 lacked when K7 fired, it is published, and the code is at
github.com/andreas-wagner-uzh/EE_mutations.

And Wagner names our target level as unoccupied, in his own words: "I define
but do not explicitly study neutral EE mutations further here, because such
mutations cannot be reliably identified in large populations with today's
technology to measure fitness." A published author defining the matched-fitness
case and declining it for a stated technical reason is far better evidence of a
genuine gap than any absence-from-the-literature argument this seat has
produced.

SECOND: re-analyse Petak 2025. Code and data survive. The published result is
r-squared 0.502 on 15 points with two outlier rules dropped. Re-run with the
outliers in, with a proper conditional model rather than two marginal
regressions, and on the full 105 landscape pairs. If it holds, the programme
has its foundation from data someone else already collected. If it does not,
that is the most important negative available and it costs a day.

THIRD: re-run the Draghi 2010 supplementary analysis without the collinearity.
SI 6.2 already contains a longitudinal accessibility-and-acquisition
measurement on evolving populations, and it fails the conditioning test only
because the accessibility parameters were derived from robustness by regression
rather than entered as competing predictors. The design is sound and the defect
is in the analysis, which makes it re-runnable.

FOURTH: elevate Zheng 2019 for independent reconstruction, per S4, but scope it
to the question the original left open. The accessibility side is already
established causally by engineering. What is missing is whether path
accessibility outpredicts standing genetic variance, which the paper measures
and never uses as a rival. That is a conditioning analysis on an existing
design, not a new experiment.

Then, and only then, EvCA. It stays frozen until at least the first two of
these report, because if the conditioning method is already published and the
phenomenon already established twice, EvCA's value is the rigour cell and
nothing larger, and that should be decided before compute is spent.

REVIEWER'S BOTTOM LINE: given that HCA-2 is already established by someone else
in a machinery-matched substrate, is this programme's remaining contribution
the rigour cell (quantified, all arms, run-level uncertainty, operator-side
intervention), or should it stop building microscopes and start asking which
representation coordinates carry the information phenotype and fitness do not?
