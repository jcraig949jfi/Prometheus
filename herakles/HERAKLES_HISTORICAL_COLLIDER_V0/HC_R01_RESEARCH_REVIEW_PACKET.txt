3. THE FINDING THAT MATTERS MOST: HCA-2 IS ALREADY DONE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Petak, Frati, Vroomans, Pespeni and Cheney 2025, "The variability of
evolvability", PNAS. Read in full as the bioRxiv preprint.

  substrate ............ recurrent gene-regulatory network developing a
                         spatiotemporal expression pattern, selected against
                         patterns from elementary cellular automata rules
  machinery matched .... YES. Identical architecture, identical mutation rule,
                         identical selection. ONLY the environment-switching
                         schedule differs.
  operator ............. identical, and the SAME operator is reused for the
                         accessibility sampling
  detector ............. LOCAL. 2500 clones for robustness, 10000 mutated
                         clones for phenotypic variability, applied to the
                         population's OWN current best genotype
  longitudinal ......... YES, slopes taken after each environmental change
  replication .......... 15 replicates with 95 percent confidence intervals

And the row that decides everything:

  predictor                                         r-squared    p
  ----------------------------------------------    ---------    ------
  local mutational-variance accessibility measure       0.502    0.0067
  cross-environment fitness correlation (cheap)         0.089    0.279 (n.s.)

That is the literal criterion HC-T01 failed. An accessibility-shaped variable
outpredicting a matched current-state fitness variable, in a machinery-matched
substrate, published, with code at github.com/Cpetak/Variability_of_Evolvability
and data at Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.16094874.

  HCA-1 supported in a second substrate ............ YES
  HCA-2 supported at all ........................... YES, here
  Stop condition S3 ................................ FIRES

Caveats, and they are not small. The version read is the preprint, not the
copy-edited PNAS text. The regression rests on 15 data points, one per landscape
pair. Two outlier rules were explicitly excluded from it. So this is a real
effect and a fragile one, and the honest description is that the field has
cleared the bar once, thinly.

4. A LANE CONFLICT, RESOLVED FROM PRIMARY SOURCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Two lanes disagreed about Kounios et al. 2016, arXiv 1612.05955. One reported an
internal frozen-depth sub-analysis reaching H4. The Ergon lane reported the
basin detector measured in a single unablated condition. This seat read the
paper. THE ERGON LANE IS CORRECT.

  Figures 4 and 5, the freeze experiment: G-P maps frozen at episodes 800 and
  1600, 1000 genotypes microevolved for 40000 generations under each, alongside
  a one-to-one map. The reported outcome is MEDIAN FITNESS and endpoint
  fitness histograms. No accessibility distribution is measured anywhere.

  Figure 8, the basin detector: the caption states the experiment evolves "only
  the regulatory interactions under natural selection for 1600 evolutionary
  episodes", sampling 10000 genotypes against maps from the end of every
  episode. ONE condition. No arms. No ablation.

So Kounios 2016 does NOT reach H4. What it is instead is more interesting:

**THE SAME MISSING CELL AS TOUSSAINT, IN A SECOND PAPER, FOUR MONTHS EARLIER.**
A longitudinal accessibility detector in one experiment. Machinery-MATCHED
history arms with an acquisition outcome in another. Never composed.

Finding that structural failure twice, independently, in unrelated substrates,
makes it a property of the field rather than of one author. That is a stronger
and more useful claim than HC-T01's original one.

It also hands us the cheapest experiment in the programme. The 800-versus-1600
frozen maps are machinery-matched, static, and already held locally. Running a
detector across them is a machinery-matched H2 test that attacks precisely the
confound that downgraded HC-T01, at negligible compute.

5. PART C DOES NOT EXIST, AND HERE IS HOW IT GETS FAKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ten search lines were worked. Nothing assembles shaper plus detector plus
controller. The closest single candidate:

  Edmonds 1998, Meta-Genetic Programming, CPM Report 98-32.
    online estimate ....... YES. Operator fitness is the measured average
                            proportionate fitness change in the operand genes,
                            from 50 sampled operand pairs per operator per
                            generation, three-generation running average.
    fed back .............. YES. That score IS the operator's fitness, and
                            ordinary selection on the OPERATOR population
                            constructs the next generation's variation
                            machinery.
    generative ............ YES. Operators are tree structures over primitives,
                            so novel operator forms can be assembled. Not a
                            menu.
    shortfall ............. the estimate is BACKWARD-LOOKING, a realised effect
                            of operators already applied, not a forward estimate
                            of a distribution over future consequences
    author's own caveat ... the loop is fragile: it destroyed population
                            diversity in initial trials, needed manual fixes, is
                            sensitive to operator syntax, and degrades under an
                            unrelated depth ceiling which the author says
                            "disrupts the effective estimation of the operator's
                            effectiveness"

CMA-ES is the strongest engineered analogue and is precisely specified and
robust, which is exactly what Edmonds is not. But its update law is fixed by the
authors, and what it estimates is the covariance of PAST selected steps.

### THE WARNING, WHICH IS THE MOST USEFUL NEGATIVE IN THIS PASS

Four independent lines show the way future variation is generated CHANGING over
a run, with NO estimate computed or consulted anywhere:

  Baeck self-adaptive GA ......... mutation rate in the genome, pure hitchhiking
  Altenberg 1994 ................. constructional selection in GP
  Spector 2001 Pushpop ........... reproduction strategy drifts sexual to
  asexual
  mutator alleles ................ in Avida and in biology

Altenberg states it himself: "the constructional fitness is never evaluated by
the GP algorithm, nor is it obtained by averaging current program fitnesses."

**Observing that variational behaviour changed over a run is not evidence of a
controller.** Four substrates prove it. Any future Part-C claim in this
programme must exhibit the estimate and the feedback pathway, never the outcome.
Recorded as a standing rule in registry R.

7. THE DETECTOR STACK, AND TWO THINGS WORTH STEALING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Fourteen rows in registry S. Two matter beyond their row.

**Smith, Husbands, Layzell and O'Shea 2002, "Fitness Landscapes and
Evolvability".** Read in full. It independently invented the offspring-
distribution detector A YEAR BEFORE Toussaint's thesis, in the Cavalli-Sforza,
Feldman and Altenberg transmission-function formalism. It enumerates the full
one-mutant neighbourhood, it defines four statistics on the offspring-fitness
distribution, and critically it VALIDATED THE MEASURE ONLINE, computing it from
genotypes collected during a real hill-climbing run and showing the biased
online sample gives the same qualitative predictions as unbiased sampling.

Then it stopped one question short. The portrait is built BY conditioning on
fitness, plotting evolvability as a function of current fitness, and the paper
never asks the marginal question. Our K7 is the question this line set up and
did not ask.

**van Nimwegen, Crutchfield and Huynen 1999, "Neutral evolution of mutational
robustness".** Average neutral degree equals the principal eigenvalue of the
neutral network's adjacency matrix, and a population at quasi-equilibrium
samples
that eigenvector by construction. So the statistic is inferable from population
data already being collected. **Effectively zero marginal cost, against our
2000x.** Worth stealing on cost grounds alone if the quasi-equilibrium
precondition can be met.

Also on record, and it is the mirror of our own failure: Weinberger's
correlation
length is DOCUMENTED BLIND to neutrality even when neutrality demonstrably
changes search speed. Our detector saw a difference that did not matter; that
one misses a difference that does. Both directions are now in registry R.

**How many detectors in the stack were ever tested for predictive power after
conditioning on current performance? One. Petak 2025.** Everything else is a
static landscape characterisation or an aggregate difficulty predictor. The
strongest named predictive result in the stack, a local-optima-network model
explaining 99 percent of variance, predicts ALGORITHM success across problem
INSTANCES, which is the wrong unit entirely.

8. HISTORY VERSUS HIDDEN STATE, DIRECTIVE SECTION 8
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The directive warns against overclaiming path dependence when ordinary hidden
state suffices. Applying that discipline honestly:

  STATE-LOCATION ACCESSIBILITY ........ SUPPORTED. Everything found is
                                        consistent with current hidden state
                                        determining future reachability.
  HISTORY-CONDITIONED ACCESSIBILITY ... NOT SEPARATED from the above by any work
                                        found, including our own.
  PATH-DEPENDENT ACCESSIBILITY ........ NO EVIDENCE. Nothing found requires
                                        information persisting outside the
                                        measured state.

In HC-T01 the two arms differ in rule-table content, which is present state. In
Petak the arms differ in evolved network weights, which is present state. In
Kounios the frozen maps differ in accumulated regulatory strength, which is
present state.

**So the honest answer to directive question 8 is that hidden current
representation is sufficient, and history has not been shown to be necessary
anywhere.** Which makes the directive's section 9 the live question rather than
a fallback: the finding is that phenotype and fitness are insufficient state
descriptions, and the useful research programme is asking WHICH representation
coordinates carry the information. That is a smaller and more tractable claim
than historical memory, and it is the one the evidence supports.

9. NEUTRAL NETWORKS, DIRECTIVE QUESTION 7
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COVERAGE DISCLOSURE: the dedicated neutral-network lane had not reported when
this packet was written. What follows rests on the detector lane's coverage of
the same literature, which reached Schuster, Fontana, Stadler and Hofacker 1994,
Wagner's genotype-network evolvability, Ancel and Fontana 2000, van Nimwegen
1999, and Greenbury, Johnston, Louis and Ahnert. Draghi et al. 2010 and the
specific H-level screen of Wagner 2008 are NOT yet done. This is the top
outstanding item.

On the evidence in hand:

**In principle, yes, it is the cleanest natural control available anywhere.**
Many genotypes map to one phenotype, so phenotype, fitness and machinery class
can all be held identical while only position differs. That removes our confound
by construction rather than by argument.

**In the literature, it has never been used that way.** Every instrument found
in that lineage is a STATIC characterisation of map structure: neutral degree,
network size, phenotype accessibility counts, constrained-versus-unconstrained
partitions. They are computed over genotype space, not over an evolving
population, and not one of them was tested for predictive power after
conditioning on current performance.

So the cleanest available control is also the one the field has left unused for
the question we care about. That is a genuine missing cell, and unlike HC-T01's
it is in a substrate where the confound cannot arise. It should be the next
archaeology target after the Kounios frozen maps.

One access gap is material and remains open: Ancel and Fontana 2000,
"Plasticity, evolvability, and modularity in RNA", is the closest published
analogue to our own instrument in that lineage, and could not be read. The
author-hosted PDF has an expired TLS certificate and every mirror returned 403.

11. WHAT THIS DOES TO EVCA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EvCA remains FROZEN and unauthorised. Full design in
EVCA_HCA1_HCA2_DESIGN_NOTE.md. The four things that changed:

**The question is now HCA-2, not HCA-1.** Petak has established HCA-1 in a
machinery-matched substrate, so establishing it a third time is not worth a run.
The primary EvCA question becomes whether a same-state same-probe accessibility
signal predicts later transition probability beyond current classification
performance and cheap rule-state metrics.

**EvCA's structural advantage is now the point of choosing it.** Every rule is a
128-entry binary table under the same bit-flip operator, so the machinery-
presence confound cannot arise. And the one-step neighbourhood is EXACTLY 128
rules, fully enumerable, so the sampling error that dominated HC-T01's estimator
protocol is identically zero.

**The cheap-state baseline is specified first, not last**, and includes mean
neighbour performance, which is the EvCA analogue of the avgfit statistic that
beat our structured detector. The detector must beat that or it is another K7.

**The detector must not be performance-based.** Choosing a performance-derived
detector and then testing it against a performance baseline is how K7 fires. The
recommendation is a behavioural-distance cloud over the 128 neighbours as
primary, with the historical particle and domain descriptors as the
interpretable
secondary. Recovering the historical particle-filtering method as a detector
part is the main prerequisite and it is not done.

One question for James, flagged rather than decided: Stage 0 is pure archaeology
on six recovered genomes and consumes nothing. Stage 1 onward GENERATES new
evolutionary histories, which is a different kind of object from HC-T01 and may
need separate authorisation.

14. RECOMMENDATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Three actions, in cost order, none of which is a new experiment.

**FIRST, and it is nearly free: re-analyse Petak 2025.** Code and data both
survive. The published result is r-squared 0.502 on 15 points with two outliers
dropped. Re-run it with the outliers in, with a proper conditional model rather
than a comparison of two marginal regressions, and on the full 105 landscape
pairs rather than the 15. If it holds, HCA-2 is real and the programme has its
foundation from someone else's data. If it does not hold, that is the most
important negative available and it costs a day.

**SECOND, and it is cheap: compose the Kounios cell.** The 800-versus-1600
frozen maps are machinery-matched, static, and held locally. Run a local
one-step
detector across them. That is a machinery-matched H2 test attacking exactly the
confound that downgraded HC-T01, and the missing cell it fills is one we have
now found twice independently.

**THIRD: read the two files we already hold.** Tiso chapter 3, to settle whether
its arms are assigned or evolved. And finish the neutral-network lane, because
the cleanest natural control in existence is sitting unused.

EvCA stays frozen until at least the first of these reports.

REVIEWER'S BOTTOM LINE: given that HCA-2 is already established by someone else
in a machinery-matched substrate, is this programme's remaining contribution the
rigour cell (quantified, all arms, run-level uncertainty, operator-side
intervention), or should it stop building microscopes and start asking which
representation coordinates carry the information phenotype and fitness do not?
