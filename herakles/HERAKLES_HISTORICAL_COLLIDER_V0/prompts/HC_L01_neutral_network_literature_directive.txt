You are searching the historical literature for the Prometheus/Herakles seat. This is the highest-priority lane of a directive. Report only what you actually read; documented negatives are valuable, undocumented ones are worthless.

THE PHENOMENON, named neutrally as HISTORY-CONDITIONED ACCESSIBILITY:
A difference in the distribution of future states reachable under an IDENTICAL variation process, between two systems whose PRESENT measured performance and phenotype match.

WHY NEUTRAL GENOTYPE NETWORKS ARE THE CLEANEST POSSIBLE NATURAL CONTROL: many genotypes map to the SAME phenotype, so two systems can be phenotypically identical, equal in fitness, and built from identical machinery, while occupying different positions in genotype space. Their mutational neighbourhoods may still differ enormously. That removes the confound that spoils our own experiment, in which only one treatment arm was permitted to build a class of representational machinery at all.

CLASSIFY EACH WORK ON THIS FIVE-LEVEL SCALE. Do not collapse levels.
  H0 STATE DIFFERENCE ONLY - history changes current phenotype or fitness. Not relevant.
  H1 HIDDEN ORGANIZATION DIFFERENCE - present phenotype and fitness can be matched but internal representation differs; no accessibility measurement.
  H2 SAME-PROBE ACCESSIBILITY DIFFERENCE - matched present state plus an identical probe yields a different distribution of reachable future states, MEASURED.
  H3 ACQUISITION ASSOCIATION - the accessibility difference predicts subsequent acquisition BETTER THAN matched current-state variables such as fitness, robustness, genotype length or phenotype complexity. This is the level we most need and the one our own experiment failed.
  H4 CAUSAL ACCESSIBILITY EFFECT - perturbing or removing the historical organization changes BOTH the future distribution AND subsequent acquisition.
