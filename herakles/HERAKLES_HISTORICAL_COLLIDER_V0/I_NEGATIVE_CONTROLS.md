# I. NEGATIVE CONTROL SET

Systems that produce complicated, dynamic, often beautiful trajectories that a naive detector will mistake for accumulating organization. A detector that flags these is producing false positives. All rows `MODEL_RECALL_UNVERIFIED`.

Directive §21 classes: endless novelty without accumulation · high fitness without evolvability · cycling without expansion · complexity without functional gain · massive exploration without reusable machinery.

## How these are used

1. **False-positive rate is a reported number.** Any Prometheus detector (failure-topology included) is run blind on H and I mixed together. The detector's score is (particles found) and (controls not flagged), reported with n and SE. Neither number alone is a result.
2. **neg-15 is a constructed control, not a historical one.** It exists because the historical corpus does not conveniently supply a disconnected-neutral-network system; we build it. Constructed controls are labelled as such and never counted as historical evidence.
3. **A control that the detector correctly rejects for the wrong reason is a failure.** Ask the detector for its reason and check it against the established cause.
