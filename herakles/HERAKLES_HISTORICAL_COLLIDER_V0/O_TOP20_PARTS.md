## The three observations worth carrying forward

1. **Every ablation in the top tier used a contemporaneous-fitness endpoint.** Not one, as recalled, measured whether the part changed *descendant acquisition*. So even the best-evidenced parts are evidenced for the wrong claim. That is the seat's opening thesis and its first testable prediction.

2. **The parts split into two incompatible substrate classes.** `part-particle-computation` and `part-genotype-network-connectivity` are total-substrate-dependent; `part-innovation-protection` and `part-structural-duplication` are nearly substrate-free. A composition claim that mixes classes needs the substrate controlled, or it is comparing a property of the map with a property of the algorithm.

3. **One part is deliberately from outside evolution.** `part-failure-driven-hidden-variable` (Drescher's schema mechanism) is a learning-system mechanism. It sits in the pool to test whether cross-paradigm composition is even meaningful, or whether the causal-function translation quietly smuggles in an equivalence that does not hold. If it turns out that non-evolutionary parts never compose with evolutionary ones, that is a finding about the translation method itself.
