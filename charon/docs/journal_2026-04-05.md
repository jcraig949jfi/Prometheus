## What The Spectral Tail Actually Sees

The picture from five days of work:

### Layer 1: Rank Channel (Between-Class)
- Counts forced zeros at s = 1/2
- Compresses gaps in zeros 5-19 relative to RMT prediction
- Dose-response across ranks 0, 1, 2, 3 (genus-2)
- Decays logarithmically with conductor
- 16 arithmetic mechanisms orthogonal to this channel

### Layer 2: Regulator Channel (Within-Class)
- Measures geometric height of Mordell-Weil generators
- Compresses gaps within fixed rank
- Dose-response across regulator quartiles (rho = -1.0)
- Rank-independent (holds in both rank-1 and rank-2)
- 3x larger effect than rank channel (d ~ -0.15 vs -0.05)

### Layer 3: The Local Arithmetic
- Non-archimedean component dominates the regulator channel (17x)
- Local heights at bad primes drive the compression
- Tamagawa product shows two-hump spectral fingerprint (same domain)
- Faltings height correlates (same domain)
- Archimedean height barely registers

### What It Doesn't See
- Character order (Dirichlet)
- Representation dimension (Artin)
- Spectral parameter R (Maass)
- Level/conductor (Hilbert MF, Maass)
- Field discriminant (Number Fields)

The tail encodes the Mordell-Weil group through two separable channels,
both rooted in the non-archimedean local arithmetic at bad primes.
