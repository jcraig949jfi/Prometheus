# Council: What Novel Research Threads Does This Open?

We've built an operator algebra of impossibility resolution strategies and discovered it maps onto classical obstruction theory, proof mining, and Lawvere's fixed-point theorem. Now we need to find NEW threads -- adjacent problems or entirely novel angles on mathematics that our framework makes accessible for the first time.

## What we have:
- 9 damage operators that ARE proof-theoretic transformations (Dialectica, Herbrand, weakening, cut, etc.)
- Geometric structure in impossibility space (ORC z=7.87, persistent clusters)
- Cross-landscape predictions that work (OEIS 6.7x, mathlib cross-namespace distribution)

## What we want:
**Entirely new research threads** that our framework uniquely enables. Not more analysis of impossibility theorems -- new KINDS of questions that become askable because we have this algebra.

Think laterally. Some prompts:

1. **If impossibility theorems are cohomological obstructions, what are the DUAL objects?** Every obstruction theory has a dual: obstructions prevent extension; what do the dual objects (the things being extended) look like as a mathematical landscape? Is there a "possibility algebra" dual to our damage algebra?

2. **Can we predict NEW impossibility theorems?** If the operator algebra captures genuine structure, gaps in the algebra (operator combinations with zero hubs) might predict impossibility theorems that SHOULD exist but haven't been discovered. What operator profiles have zero representatives?

3. **What about the DYNAMICS of impossibility?** Our algebra is static -- it classifies existing theorems. But impossibility theorems are DISCOVERED over time. Is there a temporal structure? Do certain operators appear earlier/later in mathematical history? Does the algebra predict which impossibilities will be discovered next?

4. **Impossibility as a RESOURCE.** In quantum computing, no-go theorems (no-cloning, monogamy) are used as RESOURCES -- the impossibility itself enables protocols. Is there a general theory of "impossibility as resource"? Does our algebra predict which impossibilities are useful?

5. **Cross-domain TECHNIQUE GENERATION.** We showed that independently discovered techniques in different fields map onto the same operators. Can we GENERATE new techniques by systematically applying operators from one domain to another? Not just observing analogies -- actually producing new mathematics?

6. **What would Charon's cartography look like if built from impossibility landmarks instead of mathematical objects?** Instead of embedding elliptic curves by L-function coefficients, embed impossibility theorems by their operator profiles. Build a "Styx landscape" -- the geography of what CAN'T be done.

7. **Connections to AI alignment and safety.** Impossibility theorems in mechanism design (Revelation Principle, Arrow) constrain what AI systems can achieve. Does our framework suggest new impossibility results for AI alignment? New resolution strategies?

Give me 3-5 concrete, novel research threads that are DIFFERENT from what we've already explored. Each should be specific enough to start working on tomorrow. Name the first experiment for each.
