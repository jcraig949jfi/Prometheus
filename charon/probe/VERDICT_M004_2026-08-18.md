## 2. The denominator is wrong by roughly twenty-fold

§1, §2 and §4 all rest on *"~92K historical kills."* Measured: **80,277 is the count of TEST

## 3. The 80% floor is neither rigorous nor lax — it is *unconstrained*

The floor's achievability is set entirely by an injection composition the prereg never specifies,
and then delegates to me. I measured the squeeze by construction (§5):

```
class                        true?   battery-representable?   Stage-B translator (z3)
STRUCTURAL-ALGEBRAIC          yes            no               NOT POSABLE -> abstain
STRUCTURAL-TOPOLOGICAL        yes            no               NOT POSABLE -> abstain
STRUCTURAL-COMBINATORIAL      yes            no               NOT POSABLE -> abstain
ARITHMETIC-GEOMETRIC          yes            no               NOT POSABLE -> abstain
ARITHMETIC-DECIDABLE          yes           YES               decided (unsat on negation)
```

Stage B abstains on everything structural, and §3 states **"`unknown` is not a resurrection"**, so
those score as *not recovered*. Therefore:

- An injection set **representative of the archive** — whose claims are statistical and structural
  (elliptic curves, knots, OEIS growth rates) — has a recovery ceiling near **0%**. The 80% floor
  can never be cleared and §6 halts the run every time.
- An injection set built to **clear** 80% must be drawn from z3's decidable fragment — which is
  precisely the class `_DISPATCH` already handles, i.e. **representable**, i.e. *not the target
  class.*

This is `feedback_control_must_break_the_selection_relation` running in the opposite direction from
the D1/D2 case: **a control drawn to match the instrument's reach rather than the population's
measures the instrument's coverage, not its validity.** A floor is meaningful only after someone
measures what a *perfect* instrument could recover on a composition-matched set — a ceiling
calibration this protocol does not have.

## 4. The other four questions, answered

**Is Stage A mechanical?** **Not on this archive.** It is mechanical only where a `kind` field
exists, which is nowhere in either kill corpus (§1). As written it would be implemented as a text
classifier tuned on the calibration slice, and the drift direction is toward whatever the tuner
expects to find.

**Does the negative control catch a format-reading instrument?** **No, twice over.** First,
"content redacted, structure intact" is a **denylist** — the exact shape that leaked `CONFIRMED`
and `r_raw=` through the seam probe on 2026-06-22, where the finding was that leak gates must
**allowlist** (render only whitelisted structural fields) or content survives by omission. Second,
and worse: **"produces no resurrections" is passed by a dead instrument.** A translator that
abstains on everything scores 0 on the negative control *and* 0 on the corpus — one reads as a
pass, the other as §1's headline. The control is informative only when read **jointly** with a
representative-injection recovery: `(high recovery) AND (zero on redaction)`. Taken separately,
both are satisfied by a broken instrument.

**Is the 5% calibration slice right?** **It is sized against the wrong quantity.** 5% of 3,988 is
199 records, and what matters is the target subset within it, not the archive fraction. A rate
estimated on ~199 carries roughly ±7pp at best. The slice must be sized from the **target subset**
with a stated minimum — the same discipline as sizing decision-n from what actually needs deciding.

**Do the kill conditions let a bad run survive?** **Three ways.**
- A **structural zero passes every gate** (§1) and is then reported as a corpus finding.
- A **dead instrument passes the negative control** (above).
- **§5A has no de-injection procedure and no injection ledger.** §6 voids a run and "restarts from a
  fresh hold-out draw" — but the synthetic records remain in the archive, and the fresh draw will
  draw them again, now with Aporia having seen a prior run over them. No removal step, no mandated
  provenance stamp on injected records, no reversal plan. **This is why I have not injected.**

   composition — rather than from a round number.
4. **Rebuild the negative control as an allowlist renderer**, and require redaction and recovery to

5. **Add a de-injection procedure and an injection ledger** with provenance stamps on injected

6. **Add the counter-baseline** against `kill_diagnosis`.
7. **Pre-commit a fourth reading, `VACUOUS — TARGET SUBSET EMPTY`**, distinct from "the nulls were
   real", so that a structural zero can never be reported as a corpus finding.

Item 7 is the one I care most about. The rest is repairable engineering; that one is the difference
