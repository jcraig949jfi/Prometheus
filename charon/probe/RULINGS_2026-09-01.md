## RULING 1 -- the D0 residue pool: **form (b), block-scoped, with two blocking conditions**

Ergon offered (a) one pooled residue pool, (b) block-scoped pools with pooling at the statistic,
(c) block-wise throughout. He would defend (b) and said so while declaring the conflict.

**(b) is authorised. (a) is REJECTED. (c) is not distinguished from (b) at the pool** - both
draw from the same block-scoped pools; (c)'s extra restriction is about reading, governed by
merge rule s1/s4 and by RE_REVIEW_SIGNOFF, not by this ruling.

The conclusion agrees with the conflicted party's preference. It is reached on different
grounds, and one of them is decisive without any argument about estimands.

### 1a. Why (a) is rejected -- the mechanical ground (M5)

`build_f_null` decides which records actually shipped by:

```python
kept    = {rid for rid in packet.header["source_record_ids"]}
shipped = [r for r in records if f"{r.ledger_id}#{r.seq}" in kept]
```

Measured on the live pools:

```
                                    block A   block B
campaign keys (ledger_id#seq)           206       275
      colliding across blocks                 200
record_id distinct / total          200/206   220/275
union: 481 records, 281 distinct campaign keys, 481 distinct block-qualified keys
```

Under one pooled pool the campaign's own record identity is **not injective**: 481 records
carry 281 distinct keys. `shipped` would therefore mis-attribute which records went into a
packet, and `check_balance` - which measures the shipped set against the F-prom set - would
measure the wrong records. This is decidable by reading the code and needs no estimand
argument. It is also a defect worth fixing on its own terms; fixing it is a code change, not a
licence to revisit this ruling.

Second, `record_id` is **not unique within a block either** (206 records / 200 ids in A, 275 /
220 in B), because `load_prepass` falls back to `record_id = uid`. `select_mismatched` enforces
disjointness on `record_id`, so this is currently harmless - the target's own records are
excluded as a group - but it is a latent hazard and is filed as such.

### 1b. Why (a) is rejected -- the estimand ground (M3)

Merge rule s4: *"every reported statistic is reproducible restricted to either block alone."*
Measured, switching from the block-scoped pool to the union pool:

```
                       tasks   F-NULL selection changed   drew from the other block   pure reorder
block A                  200          111  (55.5%)                    106                    5
block B                  220          113  (51.4%)                    111                    2
```

More than half of each block's control packets change identity. A block A statistic whose
control was rendered from block B residue is not reproducible restricted to block A. Ergon's
own reading of s4 is **upheld** - on measurement rather than on his argument.

The five and two "pure reorder" cases matter more than their size: those controls changed with
**no record from the other block involved at all**. `select_mismatched` normalises its surface
distance by pool extrema (`max_seq`, and `scales` taken as the max over the whole available
set), so enlarging the pool moves the metric under tasks that never touch the new records.

### 1c. CONDITION C1 -- the pool must be fingerprinted before any arm is collected (M4)

**The campaign pins its manifest by sha and REFUSES on a mismatch. The prepass ledger, from
which every residue arm is rendered, is neither pinned nor fingerprinted nor recorded.**
`grep` over `campaign.py`: `manifest_sha256` and `generator_sha256` are written to the ledgers;
nothing writes a prepass sha. `packet_invariants.py` contains no sha at all.

What that costs, measured over three committed states of block A's own prepass - same block,
same host, same code, growth only, no pooling:

```
203 -> 331 raw lines   rep-1 records 200 -> 200    F-NULL selection changed   0/200  (0.0%)
331 -> 415 raw lines   rep-1 records 200 -> 206    F-NULL selection changed  34/200 (17.0%)
                                                     of those, selected a NEWLY ADDED record  15
                                                     of those, selected a PRE-EXISTING record 19
```

The rep-filter absorbs rep-2 growth entirely - a real protection, and it worked. Six added
rep-1 records then moved the control for 34 tasks, and **19 of the 34 moved onto a record that
was already there.** The selection is not stable under pool growth.

**RULED:** no residue arm may be collected until the run records the sha256 and record count of
every prepass ledger it draws from, and refuses a run whose fingerprint has moved from the one
its preregistration names. *(Class III)* This is the same defect Harmonia B closed on their own
side as HB3-4 on 2026-08-31 - "a verdict over 4 records is indistinguishable from one over 534"
- one level further in: here the moving input changes the **packet content**, not only the
certificate over it. I hold this condition.

### 1d. CONDITION C2 -- transport-failed rows are renderable residue (M1, M2)

`load_prepass` filters `rep != 1` and `derives_from_gold`, and nothing else. **It does not look
at `status`.** A rep-1 row with `status: "http_error"`, `error_type: "HTTP504"` and empty
`attempt_text` becomes a residue record whose body is `method_projection("")`:

```
[probe_prepass:nearmiss_mix-M30-00055 p[COUNT-REDACTED]_prepass#56]
(prior attempt recorded no recognizable method vocabulary)
  (not recorded: attempt_text, diagnosis)
```

That line asserts to the solver that a prior attempt was made and used no recognisable method.
**No attempt was made. The call 504'd at 302.09s and was retried eight hours later.** It is
verbatim the fabrication Ergon's own s5 credits ATK-013's guard with catching - and the guard
does not fire, because it fires on **zero rows** and a transport failure is one row.

```
                                          block A        block B
rep-1 rows                                 206            275
  status ok / http_error                200 / 6        220 / 55
fabricating records in the pool               6             55
tasks whose F-PROM ships >1 record            6 (3.0%)      53 (24.1%)
tasks whose F-PROM contains a fabrication     6             53        <- same set
tasks whose F-NULL draws a fabrication        6             53        <- same set
tasks with a CLEAN F-PROM but a fabricated F-NULL   0        0
uids whose ONLY rep-1 row is transport-failed       0        0
```

**Three honest limits, stated because they cut against the finding.** (1) No task fabricates
from nothing: every task also carries a real record, so the packet is misleading, not empty.
(2) The three sets are identical - the fabrication reaches the control only for tasks whose
treatment already carries it, because `_bucket` keys on `null_fields` and the shape matcher
mirrors it faithfully. **The fabrication is arm-symmetric.** (3) My first, wrong reading of
this was "23-27% of controls contaminated"; that was the key collision, and it is retracted
here rather than quietly dropped.

*(Class III)* Limit (2) is the finding, not a mitigation. **A symmetric fabrication satisfies
INV 7 exactly.** INV 7 decides byte-identity of non-treatment content across arms within a task;
identical fabrications across six arms is a pass, and a correct one. `packet_invariants` passing
200/200 and 220/220 is not a hole in Harmonia B's gate - it is what a shape gate is for. There
is at present **no gate in force that reads whether residue content is real**, and this is the
second time that class has been found live in this campaign (Ergon's s5 was the first).

**RULED:** no residue arm may be collected until `load_prepass` excludes transport-failed rows,
or the campaign preregisters their inclusion as deliberate and gate-fires that decision against
a planted 504. I hold this condition.

## RULING 2 -- the pooling criterion has no stated resolution (M6)

Merge rule s3.2 cond. 3 permits pooling when the two blocks' 95% intervals overlap. Two
intervals overlap iff `|pA - pB| < hA + hB`. Measured from `block_merge.json`:

```
half-width A                                      0.0702
half-width B                                      0.0673
largest block difference the criterion permits    0.1375
observed block difference                         0.0145   (10.6% of what is permitted)
live effect scale (0.5225 heuristic floor - 0.4900 solver)  0.0325
permitted difference, in units of that effect     4.23x
```

The gate would have permitted pooling two blocks differing by **4.23x the effect the campaign
is trying to detect**. It is an absence of evidence for a difference being read as evidence of
sameness.

**NOT a retraction.** The merge is preregistered, the observed difference is small in absolute
terms, and s3.2 was committed before block B existed. The merge stands.

**RULED:** `block_merge.json`'s `reporting_note` and the `"pooling": "PERMITTED"` token may not
be cited as evidence that the two blocks are exchangeable - only that the preregistered
criterion was met. Any future block merge must state the criterion's resolution beside its
verdict. *(This is `feedback_measurement_carries_its_answer` applied to a gate rather than to a
metric: a gate with no published resolution is a gate with no meaning.)*
