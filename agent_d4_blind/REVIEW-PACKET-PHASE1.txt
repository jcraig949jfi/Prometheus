1. THE QUESTION AND THE CLAIM LADDER
====================================================================

Question: once a machine-native computational physics is fixed, does it
provide broad, navigable access to executable behavioral change -- or
has the designer already built privileged corridors that predetermine
what kinds of change are reachable?

This generation deliberately contains NO learner. It tests only whether
a fair substrate for a later learning experiment can exist. The claim
ladder is:

  P0  executable self-transformations exist (witness programs)
  P1  generic mutation reaches viable behavioral diversity
  P2  the viable phenotype space is intrinsically navigable
  P3  navigation does not depend on designer-privileged corridors
  P4  strong history-free search (M0) can fairly navigate it
  P5  developmental history changes future acquisition   -- NOT tested
  P6  learned transformations alter later transformation -- NOT tested

Only P1-P4 are claimed, and only as properties of specific frozen
substrates under specific frozen budgets. Forbidden conclusions
(preregistered): AGI, intelligence, consciousness, understanding,
autonomous diagnosis, human-independent cognition, elimination of human
priors, open-ended evolution.

History: two predecessor generations died. D-2: SUBSTRATE_INVALID (all
four bases failed census). D-3: NO_BASIS_PASSED, and its own
"taxonomy-neutrality gate" was found to be a relabelling of the
designer's operator menu -- a classifier scored the generator's own
output categories back onto themselves. D-4's design goal was to make
that specific failure impossible, so circularity is the first thing to
hunt for here.

2. DESIGN: SUBSTRATES (all frozen before any measurement)
====================================================================

Four machine-native computational bases, all with TOTAL DECODE: every
byte string is an executable program. Syntactic validity is therefore
trivially ~1.0 by construction; this is disclosed and never rewarded.

  S1_REG      48 x 16-bit words; 4 x 16-bit registers; dense 16-opcode
              decode (ALU, shifts, load/move/swap, skip-if-zero,
              skip-if-greater, relative jump, IN, OUT, HLT); program
              counter wraps mod 48; 128-step budget per probe.
  S2_STACK    96 one-byte instructions; circular 16-deep 16-bit stack
              (over/underflow wraps -- total semantics); 8 dense opcode
              families (push, ALU, stack ops, compare, branch-if-zero,
              branch, IN, OUT); pc wraps mod 96; 128-step budget.
  S3_REWRITE  16 rules x 3 bytes over an 8-symbol alphabet;
              leftmost-first pair rewriting (rule = lhs symbol pair,
              rhs of length 0-3, active bit, 6 reserved bits); tape cap
              16; halts when no rule matches; 64-rewrite budget.
  S4_MEM      72 operations (low 3 bits of each byte; 5 reserved bits);
              16-cell wrapping byte tape; inc/dec/left/right/IN/OUT/
              skip-if-zero/back-jump-6; pc wraps mod 72; 128 steps.

Expressivity witnesses (P0): each substrate has a hand-written echo (or
repeat-collapse, for S3) program, verified viable before census; a
substrate whose witness fails dies immediately. All four passed.

Behavioral probes and fingerprint (frozen):
  - 8 probe inputs, 8 nibbles each, drawn once from seed 20260827;
  - per-probe row: (output tuple <= 8 nibbles, log2-bucketed step
    count, log2-bucketed touched-locations count, halted flag, trace
    set of visited program locations); for S3_REWRITE the third field
    is the bucketed final tape length and the trace is the set of
    fired-rule indices;
  - phenotype key = exact identity of (outputs, buckets, halted) across
    the 8 probes; trace excluded from identity;
  - d1 (primary distance) = mean over probes of positional nibble
    mismatch on outputs padded to 8 with a sentinel; range [0,1];
  - auxiliary distances (reported, never gated alone): step-profile L1,
    touched-profile L1, trace Jaccard;
  - VIABLE := nonempty output on >= 4 of 8 probes AND >= 2 distinct
    output tuples across probes (input sensitivity) AND >= 1 executed
    step.
  - Disclosure: phenotype equality is equality on 8 frozen probes; no
    global program-equivalence claim is made anywhere.

Mutation physics (frozen; content-blind; identical across substrates;
defined on raw encodings, cell = the substrate's alignment unit: 2
bytes for S1, 3 for S3, 1 for S2/S4):

  OP0  bitflip burst: k flips, k = 1 + min(Geom(0.5), 7)
  OP1  cell substitution: one uniform cell replaced with uniform bytes
  OP2  block copy: 2-8 cells duplicated within the genome
  OP3  block swap: two disjoint equal-length blocks exchanged
  OP4  rotation: whole genome rotated by a uniform cell offset
  XOVER one-point crossover at a cell boundary -- registered mechanism,
       reachable ONLY through the recombining navigator, censused and
       ablated like any other mechanism.

The menu distribution is physics (uniform over OP0-OP4 via a substrate
method); navigators never choose named operators and never see operator
semantics, substrate identity, graph structure, or oracle output.

====================================================================
3. DESIGN: TARGETS, NAVIGATORS, ORACLE, COUNTERFACTUALS, GATES
====================================================================

Target selection (deterministic, independent seed stream, before any
navigation): 64 random-viable starts; uniform-menu random walks of 150
accepted viable steps; pool = deduped phenotypes at every accepted
step; walk edges are kept OUT of the oracle graph. Remoteness of a pool
member = mean d1 to 48 fresh random-viable reference genomes. Pool
filter: members with remoteness < 0.15 (= hit radius 0.10 + 0.05) are
removed, because a target inside the ab-initio hit ball can be "hit" at
the start sample with zero mutation steps. Strata = remoteness extremes
of the filtered pool: near = bottom decile, mid = middle decile
(45-55%), far = top decile. Within each stratum: 6 lowest-density + 6
highest-density members (density = pool-mates within d1 <= 0.15),
SHA-256 tie-break. 36 targets per substrate.

Disclosure: this measures REPRODUCIBLE ACCESSIBILITY of phenotypes the
physics itself exhibited under an independent seed -- not reachability
of arbitrary mathematically-existing phenotypes.

History-free navigator suite (M0; budget 1,200 metered evaluations per
run; hit = d1 <= 0.10, reachable by construction since the target
phenotype itself scores 0):

  N1 RESTART-WALK  random-accept viable walk, restart after 25
                   consecutive non-viable children (floor baseline)
  N2 HILLCLIMB     greedy d1 descent, plateau-tolerant, restarts after
                   60 non-improving or 25 non-viable evaluations
  N3 NOVELTY       archive novelty walk (5-nearest, add threshold 0.05,
                   cap 400); targetless mapper, incidental hits counted
  N4 RECOMBINER    population 16, tournament size 3 on d1-to-target,
                   one-point crossover + 50% menu mutation, 5% fresh
                   ab-initio injection

Per target: N1 x3, N2 x5, N3 x2, N4 x5 seeds = 540 runs per substrate.
Competitive pair for gates: (N2, N4) -- single-trajectory descent vs
population recombination. Every run records ALL its viable starts and
whether each hit was scored on an ab-initio draw ("start") or on a
mutated/recombined child ("search"); both rates are reported.

Oracle (analysis only; output never reaches any navigator): per-episode
attribution by reverse BFS from each target's hit ball over the
observed single-parent transition graph, restricted to viable
endpoints, with crossover and target-generation edges excluded. An
episode counts as oracle-reachable iff ANY of its own starts lies in
the ball's basin. Used solely to attribute navigation failure to
demonstrated topology vs search weakness; both attributions are fatal.

Counterfactual suite (frozen): ablation of each single mechanism
(OP0..OP4, 36 targets x 4 seeds each) and of crossover (N4 without
crossover); menu reweighting by three frozen Dirichlet(2) draws (seeds
101/102/103); doubled bitflip radius (k = 1 + min(Geom(0.75), 15));
representation re-coding = a frozen random permutation of all 256 byte
values (seed 3301) applied at decode time. Re-coding preserves the
reachable program set exactly (bijection) while genuinely changing
bit-level mutation adjacency; cell-level operators commute with it.
Reweight/radius/re-coding run 36 targets x 2 seeds and are compared
against the baseline RESTRICTED to the same seeds per target
(seed-symmetric populations). The re-coded substrate gets fresh targets
under the same frozen procedure (its physics differs; conclusions, not
matched items, are compared). A counterfactual that cannot be measured
FAILS its gate; there is no silent skip.

Frozen gates, evaluated in causal order; primary flag = first failure;
all margins, CIs, and diagnostics reported:

  G1 PHENOTYPE_POVERTY       viable fraction < 0.005 (n=10,000) OR
                             viable phenotype classes < 250 counted on
                             census UNION target-generation pool
  G2 DISPLACEMENT_COLLAPSE   pooled identity rate > 0.85 OR pooled
                             effective rate (non-identity AND viable
                             child) < 0.05 OR alive operators < 3 of 5
                             (alive = effective rate >= 0.02)
  G3 ACCESSIBILITY_FRAGMENTED  navigation fails AND oracle far-stratum
                             episode reach < 0.20
  G4 NAVIGATION_FAILURE      fewer than 2 competitive-pair navigators
                             with pooled hit >= 0.25, OR best pair
                             navigator far-stratum hit < 0.10
  G5 PRIVILEGED_CORRIDOR     (only around a passing navigation
                             baseline) ANY single-mechanism ablation
                             cell with per-stratum baseline >= 0.15,
                             relative drop > 0.60, AND two-proportion
                             z >= 1.96; identifiability accuracy and
                             displacement anisotropy are DIAGNOSTICS
                             ONLY (see Section 5, finding 1)
  G6 REFINDABILITY_FAILURE   best pair navigator re-find < 0.40, where
                             re-find for a target hit by k of S seeds
                             is (k-1)/(S-1) -- the qualifying discovery
                             does not count itself
  G7 REPRESENTATION_SENSITIVE  |pooled hit (re-coded) - baseline| >
                             0.15 AND z >= 1.96
  G8 COUNTERFACTUAL_UNSTABLE worst |pooled hit (reweight/radius) -
                             baseline| > 0.15 AND z >= 1.96
  PASS = ACCESSIBILITY_GEOMETRY_ESTABLISHED for that substrate.
  Overall NO_BASIS_PASSED if no substrate passes.

Statistical discipline: Wilson 95% CIs on every gated proportion plus a
cluster bootstrap CI over targets (the unit where independence actually
holds; seeds within a target are correlated). Attainability was checked
for every threshold before freeze; e.g. the ablation z-gate is
attainable across its whole eligible region (total collapse at the 0.15
floor reaches z = 2.8 with 60 baseline / 48 ablated runs).

4. INSTRUMENT-FIRST METHOD AND VALIDATION HISTORY
====================================================================

No real substrate was measured until the metric suite proved it could
classify eight SYNTHETIC geometries with known pathologies, using the
same code path end to end (same census, navigators, oracle, gates):

  C1 FRAGMENTED   400 isolated islands            -> must flag fragmentation
  C2 IDENTITY     ring, 98% identity mutations    -> must flag displacement
  C3 CORRIDOR     two clusters, ONE operator      -> must flag privilege
                  crosses via a gateway edge
  C4 TRAPPED      500 one-way chains in R^8 into  -> must flag one of
                  an absorbing sink                  fragmented/navigation/
                                                     refindability
  C5 NAVIGABLE    torus, five overlapping         -> must PASS
                  isotropic operators
  C6 CHAOS        100k nodes, hash fingerprints,  -> must flag navigation/
                  teleport moves                     refindability/fragmented
  C7 TINY         40-node complete graph          -> must flag poverty
  C8 SPARSE       C5 with 40% of nodes non-viable -> must PASS

The instrument FAILED validation twice and was repaired each time; all
iterations are committed with their failure evidence:

  Run 1 (v1): INVALID. C1 falsely flagged PRIVILEGED (crossover edges
  let the oracle BFS hop between islands; ablation fired on n=12
  noise); C3's corridor was MISSED (tertile strata diluted the far
  stratum with barely-above-median members, halving the measured
  ablation drop to exactly the gate value); C4 passed (control
  mis-specified: with uniform ab-initio support, restart sampling
  legitimately reaches everything).

  Run 2 (v2): INVALID. C1 misattributed (union-of-starts oracle:
  in a fragmented space with broad ab-initio support, SOME run always
  starts near any target, so BFS from the union of all starts says
  "reachable" even though every individual episode is trapped); C4
  still passed (2-D radial geometry structurally couples the hit-ball
  radius to branch spacing; also distance clipping degraded remoteness
  stratification to noise).

  Run 3 (v3): VALID on 7 controls (per-episode reverse-BFS oracle;
  decile-extreme strata; z-guarded ablation; C4 rebuilt with 500
  branches along random unit vectors in R^8, where cross-branch
  distance ~ r*sqrt(2) regardless of branch count).

  Run 4 (v4, after the adversarial review of Section 5, with C8 added
  and everything at the exact binding scale and configuration): VALID
  on all 8 controls. This is the instrument that was frozen,
  byte-identical except a status stamp.

5. PRE-FREEZE ADVERSARIAL REVIEW (31 agents)
====================================================================

Before freezing, the draft constitution and code were attacked by four
independent review agents (lenses: circularity, statistics, leakage,
contradictory incentives), producing 47 raw findings; the 27 non-minor
findings each went to an independent verification agent instructed to
REFUTE them against the actual files. Result: 23 CONFIRMED, 4 REFUTED,
plus 20 minor findings. All 23 confirmed defects were repaired before
freeze. The most consequential:

  1. FATAL: the planned privilege gate on mutation-source
     identifiability x displacement anisotropy had ZERO positive
     calibration (no control ever entered its firing region), gated
     absolute accuracy against a data-dependent chance level, and its
     anisotropy threshold was calibrated on signed-coordinate synthetic
     features while real substrates emit non-negative mismatch features
     with a built-in linear dependence. On a real substrate it would
     have relabelled MENU DISTINCTNESS as privilege -- the predecessor
     D-3 tautology, inverted. Repair: demoted to diagnostics-only;
     privilege gating rests on ablation collapse and re-coding.
  2. FATAL: nothing separated target strata from the hit criterion --
     on the corridor control, "near" targets had remoteness 0.079-0.097
     vs hit radius 0.10, so an average random START was already inside
     the hit ball and navigation gates could be carried by ab-initio
     adjacency with zero mutation steps. Repair: pool remoteness floor
     (>= 0.15) plus start-vs-search hit provenance reporting.
  3. FATAL: the ablation evaluator z-tested only the MAX-drop cell, so
     a large-but-insignificant drop could mask a smaller qualifying
     one. Repair: every eligible cell is tested. Also: at 2 ablation
     seeds even a TOTAL collapse could not reach z = 1.96 for baselines
     in [0.15, ~0.21) -- an unattainable gate; repaired with 4 seeds.
  4. Oracle attribution biases (confirmed): hit balls included
     non-viable phenotypes navigators can never score, and
     target-generation edges made oracle reachability partially true
     by construction. Both repaired.
  5. Freeze integrity: the freeze script originally rewrote two
     thresholds AFTER validation, so the frozen instrument would never
     have classified the controls. Repaired: freeze stamps a status and
     records hashes only; validation re-run on the final bytes.
  6. Assorted confirmed findings: diversity floor mathematically
     unattainable for viable fractions in [0.005, 0.025) (repaired:
     census UNION pool); counterfactual comparisons were
     2-seed-vs-5-seed asymmetric (repaired); a never-measured
     counterfactual silently skipped its gate (now fails closed);
     validation ran at a smaller scale than binding (now identical);
     the validation set never exercised viability (C8 added).

Two further repairs were adopted from MINOR (unverified) findings, not
from the 23 confirmed: oracle episodes are now credited for ALL their
starts (initial, restarts, fresh injections), not just the first; and
the re-find ratio was redefined to (k-1)/(S-1) so the qualifying
discovery does not count itself (the packet reports provenance
honestly: the related non-minor refind finding was REFUTED).

The four REFUTED claims (kept in the findings ledger) included a
composite-gaming construction and a claimed IN/OUT bit-adjacency
corridor that the re-coding counterfactual in fact perturbs.

from the identical frozen seed (12000). Post-repair footprint observed
at relaunch: ~0.5 GB across all four processes (session observation,

8. HUMAN-TAXONOMY RED TEAM (weaken-only, post-run)
====================================================================

Per the charter, human edit-family classification returns only as an
attack: it can weaken a PASS narrative, never strengthen one, and
unclassifiable residue is never counted as novelty. Method: independent
diagnostic sample (separate seed stream, ~1,500 viable parents x 5 ops
x 3 reps per substrate), each non-identity transition classified by
which decoded instruction-class counts changed (I/O count, control
count, other opcode change, operand-only).

  - Large displacements (d1 > 0.3) on S1/S2/S4 are dominated by
    REARRANGEMENT of existing code (OPCODE_OTHER family: 70% / 78% /
    79%), not by edits changing the count of designed I/O primitives
    (9% / 7% / 8%). No evidence of an "I/O vending machine" behind the
    passes.
  - Block swap and rotation change no instruction-class counts by
    construction (pure permutation edits); bitflip, substitution, and
    copy can all change them. For S2 this aligns with Disclosure 2
    (OP1's modal edit family is I/O-count change, 30% of its effective
    transitions) and WEAKENS the S2 narrative exactly as the mechanism
    is designed to.
  - S3's edits split between only two families: per-mechanism
    OTHER_UNKNOWN shares are 84.5% (bitflip), 40.4% (substitution),
    39.0% (copy), 100% (swap), 100% (rotation); pooled
    large-displacement OTHER_UNKNOWN share 56%. Substitution and copy
    are majority-classifiable (mostly CONTROL_COUNT: active-rule-count
    edits). Unclassifiable residue is recorded as unclassifiable, not
    as novelty.

Frozen overall verdict:
  ACCESSIBILITY_GEOMETRY_ESTABLISHED on S2_STACK and S4_MEM (2 of 4);
  S1_REG NAVIGATION_FAILURE; S3_REWRITE ACCESSIBILITY_FRAGMENTED.

  "Under the frozen computational physics, executable behavioral change
  forms a substantially diverse, reproducibly navigable accessibility
  geometry under multiple generic history-free search processes,
  without evidence that navigation is dominated by any single
  designer-privileged mutation mechanism or by the particular binary
  encoding."

Nothing stronger. This is a property of two frozen substrates under a
1,200-evaluation budget, 8 frozen probes, and a d1 <= 0.10 hit ball.
Per the frozen protocol, the passing substrates are frozen; a Phase-2
preregistration (whether accumulated executable history changes what
can be acquired under fixed resources) MAY be designed but is not
executed, and no learner exists in this generation.

10. KNOWN LIMITS (carried explicitly; attack them)
====================================================================

  1. S2's pass sits inside cluster-level noise on the pooled gate and
     one z = 1.85 privilege near-miss. S4 is the load-bearing basis.
  2. Privilege was tested for SINGLE-mechanism ablations and byte-level
     re-coding only. Mechanism-PAIR privilege is combinatorially
     untested; only the reweight counterfactuals and the red team touch
     it.
  3. "Accessible mass" is relative to phenotypes the physics itself
     exhibited under an independent seed -- reproducible accessibility,
     not reachability of arbitrary existing phenotypes.
  4. Navigation regret is substantial (oracle far-reach minus achieved:
     0.35 on S2, 0.20 on S4, 0.41 on S1): the M0 suite under-exploits
     demonstrated topology. A stronger M0 would RAISE the bar for any
     future learner comparison, so this conservatism cuts against
     Phase 2, not for it.
  5. Phenotype identity is exactness on 8 frozen probes; behavioral
     distance is output disagreement on those probes. Trace and
     resource profiles are auxiliary only.
  6. The far-hit gate (0.10) at 12 target clusters has cluster-level SE
     ~0.09 at the gate; S2's far 0.15 is a marginal read (S4's 0.53 is
     not).
  7. The "best pair navigator" for far/refind gates is selected on the
     same rows those gates consume (winner's curse); both navigators'
     full statistics are reported, and on S4 both clear the gates
     regardless.
  8. All navigation used one budget (1,200), one hit radius (0.10),
     8 probes, and one seed family; robustness to these frozen
     constants beyond the preregistered counterfactuals is unknown.
  9. The oracle sees only observed transitions; FRAGMENTED vs
     NAVIGATION attribution is about demonstrated topology. Both flags
     are equally fatal, so no verdict hinges on the distinction.

12. QUESTIONS FOR THE REVIEWER
====================================================================

  1. Circularity: targets are generated by random walks under the same
     mutation physics navigation uses (disclosed as "reproducible
     accessibility"). Does any gate thereby become true by
     construction rather than merely easier? The pool remoteness floor
     and start-vs-search provenance were the mitigations -- are they
     sufficient?
  2. Would you overturn the S2_STACK PASS? The frozen rule binds on
     point estimates; the cluster CIs contain the pooled gate and the
     OP1 ablation sits at z = 1.85 with a 72% far drop. Is reporting
     these as disclosures (rather than flags) defensible, given they
     were preregistered before any data existed?
  3. Is per-episode reverse-BFS over observed viable transitions a
     sound attribution instrument, given the observed graph
     undersamples the true graph? Does excluding target-generation
     edges overcorrect toward FRAGMENTED?
  4. The viability predicate (output on >= 4 probes, input
     sensitivity, >= 1 step) is a designer choice. Does it privilege
     I/O-adjacent programs in a way the ablation and re-coding
     counterfactuals cannot detect?
  5. Is the re-coding counterfactual (byte-value permutation at decode)
     a sufficient representation-invariance test, given cell-level
     operators commute with it and only the bitflip mechanism's
     adjacency actually changes?
  6. Demoting identifiability/anisotropy to diagnostics removed a
     tautology but also removed a detection channel: a corridor
     implemented by a COMMON, directional mechanism with no single-op
     ablation signature would now be caught only by reweighting. Is
     that an acceptable hole, and is it disclosed adequately (limit 2)?
  7. S3's fragmentation verdict rests on far oracle reach 0.00 plus far
     hits 0.00. Could a longer budget or a different navigator suite
     plausibly overturn it, and does the packet claim more than
     "inaccessible at this budget"?
  8. E1 (post-freeze memory repair): does the equivalence argument
     (trace never enters identity/distance-gated quantities; bitmask
     Jaccard == set Jaccard, verified < 1e-12; nothing observed before
     the change) fully preserve freeze integrity?
  9. Are there claim inflations anywhere in this packet -- any sentence
     stronger than the rows and margins reported here support?
 10. The stated Phase-2 gate is "design a preregistration only". Given
     the disclosures, would you authorize Phase 2 on S4_MEM alone, on
     S4+S2, or on neither?
