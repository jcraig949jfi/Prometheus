   unresolved_anomalies                 |   1   | E3 (ANOM-ASAL-MANY-TO-ONE)
   MECHANISMS_THAT_SURVIVED_TRANSPLANT  |   0   | E3 (the headline target)

   predictions_falsified is reported, never hidden.
