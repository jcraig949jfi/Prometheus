4. RESULTS
   4.1 The mechanism read out of Avida (claims, NOT yet adjudicated)
       * Descent is recorded between GENOTYPES (classes of identical
         genomes), not organisms. A parent edge is written only when a
         newborn's genome matches neither its parent's class nor any LIVING
         class. Births that breed true, or land on a living class, write
         nothing.
       * Each genotype has two reference counts: living units, and child
         genotypes. When the last unit dies and no child refers to it, it is
         unlinked at once and its parents are released in turn.
       * So a save holds the living genotypes plus the ancestors of the
         living, and nothing else.
       * The founding time stamp runs on a clock that is -1 through the end
         of update 0 and never 0.
       * One field is written unquoted; a value with spaces shifts every
         later column of that row.
       * An event that re-injects every organism without a parent erases the
         whole prior record, although each new organism is a copy of an old
         one.
