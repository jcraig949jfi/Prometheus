1. CONTEXT
   POET co-evolves environments and agents. The original 2019 version
   described an environment by 5 hand-chosen numbers. Enhanced POET instead
   uses PATA-EC: it scores every current and archived agent on the
   environment and keeps only the RANKS. An environment's novelty is the mean
   distance to the k=5 nearest rank vectors.
   The operator asked (09-18) for this descriptor to be cut and tested by a
   "basis-population ablation": keep the environments fixed, change the agent
   set, and see whether novelty changes.

2. QUESTION
   What does the descriptor keep and lose, what does the pinned code actually
   do, and is novelty a property of the environment or of the population it
   is measured against?
