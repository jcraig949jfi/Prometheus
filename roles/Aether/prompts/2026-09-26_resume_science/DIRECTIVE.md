AETH-03 DESIGN QUESTION

The central failure of v1 appears to be that most continuing change is either:

* static/uncontested copying;
* external perturbation;
* short-lived resource movement;

while the functional interaction graph has little mechanism for history-dependent persistence.

Explore minimal physics changes that could introduce endogenous causal depth.

Possible design dimensions to investigate — not prescriptions:

Arbitration memory

Current contested winners are effectively rerolled over time.

Could local state/history influence future arbitration without inserting a hand-designed circuit?

State-dependent interaction

Can what a site previously experienced affect how it acts later?

Endogenous topology

Can interaction choice become meaningfully conditioned on local state/history rather than essentially fixed argument bytes plus perturbation?

Resource feedback

Can successful state transformations affect later resource availability or routing without hard-coding a fitness function?

Multi-step computation

Can a local mechanism preserve intermediate state long enough for sequences of interactions to compose?

Conditional copying

Can copying itself depend on state rather than remaining largely redundant transmission?

Local memory

What is the minimum added state or persistence rule needed for history to become physically consequential?

Do not assume all or any of these are required.

Search the design space.

The objective is not to find “life.”

WHAT COUNTS AS PROGRESS

At this stage, weak signals are acceptable.

Mechanically trivial signals are not.

A tiny effect deserves follow-up if:

* it survives an appropriate null;
* it depends on internal state/history;
* an intervention removes it;
* it cannot be reduced to direct transition-law copying;
* it persists without requiring injected perturbation;
* it composes across multiple interactions.

A dramatic-looking pattern with a trivial mechanical explanation is less interesting than a 0.1% effect with real causal depth.
