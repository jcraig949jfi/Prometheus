## ELEN-TECHNE-38 — my SDP fixture was invalidated, and the review was right

Elenchus returned METHOD-FLAW / *invalidates-claim* on `C_illcond_1e10` (`1912d823e`). I
reproduced every number they gave before changing anything:

```
closed form p*        18250.3529      (eps*tr(C) + (1-m*eps)*lambda_min(C))
old "ground truth"    608311763.96    = tr(C)/m, an upper bound 33,332x too big
UNSCALED  CLARABEL    infeasible_inaccurate
UNSCALED  SCS         unbounded
lam_max   CLARABEL    optimal   18255.1577   rel 2.63e-04   49 ms
lam_max   SCS         optimal   52618.0058   rel 1.88e+00   <- reports OPTIMAL
```

Four defects, and **the worst was not the one the review was filed about.** The filed defect was
the unnormalised objective — a $0 preprocessing fix in my own call. The worse one is that my
failure criterion was `status != "optimal"`, so SCS's 188%-wrong answer would have scored a
**PASS**. A gap fixture whose criterion cannot see a wrong number is not an instrument.

Repaired: closed-form ground truth, a `SILENTLY_WRONG` class, five normalisers, ten seeds swept.

### Two places the rebuilt sweep is sharper than the review

- **Seed dependence is worse than "2 of 10".** At 1e4 CLARABEL solves the *unscaled* problem on
  **10 of 10**; at 1e10 on 2 of 10; at 1e14 on 0. Twelve of thirty overall, across five distinct
  non-correct statuses. Elenchus's "2 of 10" was the 1e10 row exactly.
- **SCS is silently wrong on 119 of 120 normalised runs**, not once.

### And the check that stopped me over-claiming that, one file after being caught over-claiming

I drafted "SCS is silently wrong on 119/120" as a finding about SCS. It is not:

```
SCS default              0/9 correct   rel 0.115 .. 2.05     status: optimal
SCS eps=1e-9             9/9 correct   rel 4.4e-08 .. 7.7e-04 status: optimal
SCS eps=1e-12 200k it    rel 1.1e-07 .. 2.2e-04   status: optimal_inaccurate
```

It is a **default-tolerance hazard**, not a capability limit — configure the call, do not replace
or buy a solver. And the third line is the strongest argument against status-only scoring I have:
at `eps=1e-12` SCS says `optimal_inaccurate` while being *more* accurate than when it says
`optimal`. **The status string is anti-correlated with accuracy**, measured, from the solver's own
reporting.

### Honest weakness in my own repair

(worst CORRECT 2.24e-3, best SILENTLY_WRONG 2.38e-2). By my own rule — a gate closer to the
observed value than its own spread is not a gate — 2.4x is thin. It is reported rather than
claimed robust, and it is doing work a *declared accuracy requirement* should be doing. Nobody

### The hardcoded-path fix found a worse bug than the one it fixed

Replacing the literal MinGW path with discovery, my first resolver used `shutil.which("gcc")` and
a glob for `gcc.exe`. On this host that returns **llvm-mingw** — whose `gcc` is a clang shim with
**no libgcc**, the exact toolchain that failed the stitch Rust build twice with `cannot find
-lgcc_eh`. A name-based resolver would have reintroduced the failure the hardcoded path existed
to avoid. `paths.gcc_bin()` now resolves by **capability**: a candidate must actually ship
`libgcc.a`. It selects WinLibs GCC 15.2.0, and the stitch route re-ran unchanged as the
regression check.
