One day: an operator-ordered experiment on a learned interactive world model
(Open-Oasis 500M), run on rented GPUs, from preregistration to result.
Four pods, $0.48. The result is a qualified negative: replayable and
action-sensitive for about two seconds of generated video, not persistent.

CONFLICT OF INTEREST: the author wrote the preregistration, the module, the

Grades: E3 = executed this session, numbers from committed files; E1 =

1. CONTEXT

Prometheus wants an external "world" that an agent can act in and that
keeps state, as one candidate leg of a "fifth engine". Open-Oasis is an
open 500M-parameter diffusion transformer that generates Minecraft-like
video frames conditioned on keyboard and mouse actions, with a context
window of 32 frames. The operator asked three questions: is a trajectory
reproducible (Q1); do actions cause different futures beyond sampling
noise (Q2); how long does an intervention persist (Q3). Claim ceiling: the
strongest allowed positive is "stable, action-conditioned, persistent
enough to justify further experiments as a substrate". No claims about
intelligence, physics or world-model correctness.

2. THE QUESTIONS, AS PREREGISTERED

Q1  Same prompt, seed, actions: how reproducible? Rulers: exact replicate
    pairs; different-seed same-action pairs give the stochastic spread.
Q2  E(t) = D_cf(t) - D_same(t): latent-space distance between a forward
    walk and a counterfactual action family at the same seed, minus the
    distance between two seeds under the same forward walk. CAUSAL if the
    bootstrap 95% interval excludes 0 for >= 75% of horizons 4..63.
Q3  P(t) for t >= 32 after an 8-frame turn embedded in a forward walk at
    frames 24-31: PERSISTENT / DECAYING / NO_EFFECT by whether the
    interval excludes 0.

9. QUESTIONS FOR THE EXTERNAL REVIEWER

 1. The preregistered primary Q2 gate (75% of horizons) failed at 67% with
    a clear early effect. Was the gate wrong, or is "causal to frame 46,
    noise after" the right reading and the gate did its job?
 2. Is adding a non-colliding baseline after a pilot, dated and before the
    production launch, a legitimate amendment or a second shot at the
    same question?
 3. WHAT WOULD FALSIFY "rapidly forgetting": name the intervention design
    whose null result you would accept. Is a 16-32 frame turn against the
    BACK baseline that design?
 4. Latent MSE between two seeds under identical actions grows to 0.71.
    Is seed-to-seed divergence the right null for action causality, or
    does it penalise the model for being generative?
 5. WHAT SHOULD WE STOP: should Prometheus stop evaluating learned video
    world models as persistent substrates until one is designed with an
    explicit state, or is a two-second action-conditioned generator useful
    enough for an action-effect assay to be worth $1 per prompt?
 6. 10 of 33 trajectories freeze into a static image. Instability or
    stability? Which reading serves the substrate question?
