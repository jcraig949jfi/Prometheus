3. GROUND TRUTH / CERTIFICATION / KEY DEFINITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Closed forms (solver never sees them):
  odd cycle C_n     theta = n cos(pi/n) / (1 + cos(pi/n))    n = 5, 21, 61, 121
  Paley P_p         theta = sqrt(p)                            p = 29, 61, 113
  Kneser K(m,k)     theta = C(m-1, k-1)                       (7,2) (9,3) (10,3)
Checked at n=5 (sqrt 5) and Petersen K(5,2) = 4 before use.

Certificate for graphs with no closed form, computed by numpy from what the
solver RETURNED, never from what it REPORTED:
  lb   returned X, edges zeroed, |lambda_min| * I added if lambda_min < 0,
       trace rescaled to 1 -> feasible by construction, so sum(X') <= theta
  ub   Lovasz theta_3: for ANY symmetric Y on the edge set,
       theta <= lambda_max(J + Y); Y direction = solver's edge duals, scalar
       chosen by 1-D minimisation (valid at every scalar, tight at the best)

Classes, one bar for every arm (SILENTLY_WRONG_REL = 1e-2; a reporting
threshold, NOT an accuracy requirement -- none exists, TECHNE-46):
  closed form   CORRECT rel <= bar | SILENTLY_WRONG rel > bar (status optimal)
                | DECLARED_FAILURE | RAISED
  certificate   CERTIFIED  max(|v-lb|,|v-ub|)/ub <= bar
                SILENTLY_WRONG  value leaves [lb,ub] by more than bar
                INDETERMINATE / UNCERTIFIED  instrument branches, never a pass
