Alife and POET.  I'd like to gather what we can on these:

POET (Paired Open-Ended Trailblazer) is showing significantly more practical promise for pushing models past their current capability ceilings, particularly when engineering autonomous multi-agent research swarms.
While Artificial Life (ALife) is experiencing a renaissance--fueled by using language model collectives as substrates for open-ended ecologies--it optimizes for pure emergence. ALife is brilliant for studying self-organization and survival dynamics, but it is notoriously difficult to steer toward concrete computational outputs like tensor decomposition, falsification testing, or analyzing OEIS integer sequences.
Here is how the two paradigms compare for computational research:
| Feature | POET (Co-Evolutionary Open-Endedness) | ALife (Pure Emergent Open-Endedness) |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Goal | Continuous discovery of capabilities and solutions. | Open-ended complexity and self-organization. |
| Environment | Generated dynamically to match agent abilities. | Often unconstrained, focusing on survival mechanics. |
| Optimization | Directed but diverse (Quality-Diversity). | Indirect or non-existent explicit objectives. |
| Output Utility | High for algorithmic problem-solving and reasoning. | High for complex systems research and simulation. |
Why POET is the Stronger Focus
When building automated empirical science frameworks--especially those utilizing evolutionary engines like MAP-Elites or TransformerLens--pure emergence isn't enough. The system needs to systematically solve increasingly complex problems without hitting brittle fitness gradients.
POET solves this by co-evolving the problem landscape alongside the solver:
 * The Curriculum Terrarium: Instead of relying on a static fitness function or a purely adversarial world generator (which often creates impossible "damage boundaries"), POET actively generates environments that remain precisely at the frontier of the swarm's current abilities.
 * Lateral Transfer: The algorithm's defining advantage is its ability to take stepping-stone solutions developed in one environment and laterally transfer them to solve entirely different problems. This maintains a diverse population and forces reasoning architectures past static capability plateaus without the system collapsing into chaotic noise.
