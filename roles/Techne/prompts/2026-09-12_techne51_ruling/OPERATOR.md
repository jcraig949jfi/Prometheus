TECHNE — OPERATOR RULING

TECHNE-51: APPROVED.

Retire the DisCoPy and cvc5 adapters.

Preserve provenance, receipts, tests, and the reason for retirement.
Delete nothing necessary to reconstruct the history.

The broader ruling is:

THE DONOR FOUNDRY SURVIVES.
THE UNIVERSAL ADAPTER CONTRACT DOES NOT.

Eleven days with zero external importers is sufficient evidence against
techne.lib.donors as the required provenance/consumption route.

Do not manufacture consumers to save the abstraction.

Direct donor consumption is acceptable where it is cleaner. Techne’s
durable product is the evidence-backed acquisition receipt:

identity
version
provenance/license
reproducible acquisition
execution test
capability control
known limitations
named consumers

A wrapper exists only when a consumer actually needs one.

Do not retire DisCoPy or cvc5 themselves from consideration merely because
their Techne adapters were unused. Retire the unused adapters and record
the distinction.

After TECHNE-51, proceed with TECHNE-21, then TECHNE-23, unless new
consumer-backed Quartermaster work arrives.

For future donor acquisition:

DEMAND -> FIND -> PIN -> EXECUTE -> CONTROL -> RECEIPT -> CONSUME

and only:

-> WRAP

when the consumer requires an interface Prometheus does not already have.

No speculative downloading.
No software museum.
No wrapper written merely to make an acquisition look integrated.

The Donor Foundry is now a capability quartermaster, not an abstraction
factory.

Land TECHNE-51, verify the merged tree, and continue.
