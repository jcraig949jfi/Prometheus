1. HARM-55 IS NOW THE PRIORITY DEPENDENCY

Run ASAL's own Flax/JAX CLIP observer against the preserved 395-rollout corpus.

Use the already-delivered 128x128 frames and the committed hash manifest. Do not regenerate trajectories on the scoring host and do not allow the observer implementation to alter the worlds, organisms, seeds, sampling, frame timing, or preprocessing contract.

The HARM-55 output must contain, keyed by stable stage/index identity:

* original observer score;
* Flax/ASAL score;
* absolute and signed difference;
* alive/dead status;
* existing mechanism class where available;
* threshold relationships for the catalogue, mean-garbage, and 2-sd boundaries;
* pairwise discordance classification using the preregistered +/-0.01 band;
* path/version/hash identity for both scorers and input frames.

The purpose is not to see whether the numbers are "close."

The purpose is to ask:

WHICH SCIENTIFIC CONCLUSIONS SURVIVE A CHANGE TO THE FOSSIL'S
NATIVE OBSERVER?

In particular test separately:

A. Does any ordinary catalogue life still cross the garbage reference?

B. Does S2_135 or another coherent/genuine organism still cross it?

C. Do the deepest search witnesses still cross?

D. Are the low-score METRIC_EXPLOIT cases preserved as low-score cases?

E. Does the ordering of genuine / unclassified / exploit regions change?

F. Are any current mechanism classifications observer-dependent?

A different absolute scale is acceptable. A changed scientific ordering is the important event.

Do not average discordance away.

If a small subset flips while aggregate correlation remains high, preserve those flips as objects for Nyx. Those may be more informative than global agreement.

Use any available AVX-capable host. This is an execution placement issue, not a scientific blocker. Do not let TECHNE-100, the off-host mirror, or artifact-store work hold HARM-55.

2. HARMONIA -- HARM-56 FOLLOWS HARM-55

Once the native-observer scores exist, adjudicate observer dependence before authorizing the full-domain replication.

Produce a compact cross-observer map of the 395 executed rollouts:

stable crossing
lost crossing
gained crossing
stable non-crossing

and separately:

classification stable
classification discordant
classification unresolved

Do not reduce this to correlation, RMSE, or one global agreement statistic.

Look specifically for structured discordance:

* coherent morphing life that only one observer rewards;
* turbulence that only one observer rewards;
* catalogue organisms near the boundary;
* search descendants whose ranking moves sharply;
* families or morphological regimes concentrated among discordant pairs.

If discordance has structure, that structure becomes a mechanism-archeology input rather than an instrumentation nuisance.

3. NYX -- HOLD THE CURRENT CLAIM AT THE RIGHT RESOLUTION

Until HARM-55/HARM-56 return, the reusable claim is:

On the executed 395-rollout domain under the current observer,
the ASAL scalar assigns scores below the scene-garbage reference
to both exploitative dynamics and coherent living Lenia forms.
Search is not required for such crossings, although search reaches
substantially deeper examples. The scalar therefore does not, by
itself, identify the mechanism generating the low score.

Do NOT presently promote this to:

"the ASAL metric is broken,"
"CLIP rewards garbage,"
"search alone explains the phenomenon,"
or
"the complete ASAL domain behaves this way."

Those are not established.

Equally, do not weaken the result merely because the original I0 prediction failed. That failure taught us something important: the phenomenon exists in the pre-existing catalogue and is therefore not merely a pathology manufactured by the search.

The failed prediction is evidence.

After HARM-56, run a fresh replication over the extended port.

Do not retroactively "complete" the original 1,045-draw experiment.

The original experiment remains a 395/1,045 executed-domain result.

Create a new preregistered replication whose domain is demonstrably executable before the first scientific score is observed.

Required pre-run controls:

* refuse the experiment if unexplained coverage holes remain;
* freeze exact seeds;
* freeze observer path(s);

Where feasible, score the new replication through both observers rather than making the native-observer run an afterthought.

Do not optimize the search in response to HARM-55 findings before this replication. Preserve the replication as a genuine test.

5. THE SCIENTIFIC TARGET IS NO LONGER "CAN WE CROSS?"

We already know crossings exist.

The next scientific question is:

WHAT DYNAMICAL OR REPRESENTATIONAL PROPERTY MAKES DISTINCT
LENIA PHENOTYPES OCCUPY THE SAME LOW-SCORE REGION?

Move from threshold hunting to mechanism archaeology.

After replication, Nyx should cut the population by behavior rather than by score alone.

Candidate contrasts include, without presupposing which matters:

* coherent persistent morphology vs turbulent texture;
* locomotion vs deformation;
* stable identity vs rapid morphological turnover;
* spatial frequency content;
* occupancy and edge density;
* temporal novelty;
* frame-to-frame displacement;
* persistence;
* periodicity;
* spectral change;
* object/background separability;
* morphology-preserving motion;
* catastrophic expansion/collapse;
* scene-scale texture generation.

These are probes, not the mechanism taxonomy.

Nyx's job is to discover what separates equivalently low-scoring organisms, not to force them into familiar categories.

6. TECHNE -- PRESERVE DISCORDANT SPECIMENS

When HARM-55/HARM-56 identify unusual observer disagreements, preserve those rollouts as first-class fossils.

In particular preserve:

* strongest cross-observer disagreements;
* genuine organisms with very low scores;
* exploit cases with unexpectedly ordinary native-ASAL scores;
* catalogue members that cross one threshold but not another;
* nearest behavioral pairs with sharply different metric values;
* nearest metric-score pairs with sharply different behaviors.

These are higher-value fossils than another large random sample.

Continue the atlas skeleton work for the six ALife bodies. ASAL organs should become addressable atlas objects rather than remaining hash-bound cuts.

TECHNE-100 remains worthwhile preservation infrastructure, but it is not on the critical scientific path. Do it opportunistically; do not serialize HARM-55 behind it.

7. THEOPHRASTUS -- PREPARE THE BENCH, DO NOT VALIDATE THE SCORE

The milestone after mechanism extraction is not "ASAL reproduced."

It is:

A CANDIDATE MECHANISM EXTRACTED FROM ASAL PRODUCES A PREDICTED,
MEASURABLE EFFECT WHEN TRANSPLANTED INTO AN INDEPENDENT ECOLOGY.

Theophrastus should therefore prepare to receive a mechanism, not the ASAL metric itself.

Do not make CLIP score the fitness function of the receiving world.

The transplant should ask whether an extracted dynamic changes something native to the receiving ecology -- persistence, adaptation, exploration, recoverability, ecological occupation, lineage survival, state reuse, or another independently meaningful property.

A successful transplant is much stronger evidence of reusable machinery than another ASAL-score reproduction.

10. STOP CONDITIONS

Stop extending this ASAL branch if, after native-observer validation and full-domain replication:

* the apparent phenomenon disappears under the fossil's own observer;
* the result reduces to a trivial implementation/preprocessing mismatch;
* no behavioral distinction can be recovered among the low-score classes;
* or repeated mechanism cuts fail to produce any independently testable consequence.

If instead the low-score region continues to contain several genuinely different dynamical regimes, that is the reason to go deeper.

That would mean the interesting object is not simply "metric failure."

It is the many-to-one compression performed by the observer: different dynamics becoming equivalent under its representation.

That is potentially reusable machinery.
