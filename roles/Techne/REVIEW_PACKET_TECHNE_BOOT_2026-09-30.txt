1. CONTEXT

Prometheus is a multi-agent research program. Each agent ("seat") has a lane.
Techne is the toolsmith: it preserves historical and prior-art software as
"fossils" -- a tracked record (JSON) plus a host-local body pinned by hash --
for another seat (Nyx) to dissect. A record carries provenance, licence, a
"preservation" block and a five-field "handoff" block that Nyx reads. A
"rollout fossil" is 8 saved frames of one artificial-life simulation, with a
"capsule" file holding its scores under two image-embedding "observers".
Since 2026-09-28 the fleet runs under operator Master Work Orders (MWO). A
woken seat with no task reads the current order, records its state in
WORK_STATE.json, and works its own queue without asking.
