1. CLAIM / SUBJECT UNDER REVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The charter asks whether colliding three distant concepts (historical
Hephaestus/Nous triplicates) and converting them into executable,
falsifiable worlds yields candidate principles of intelligence that
survive hostile tests, and whether triplicates add value over single
concepts, pairs and ordinary prompts. This cycle built the machinery and
ran it end to end once on 16 triplicates. Claims made: (a) the funnel
works mechanically with provenance and preregistration at every step;
(b) no candidate survived Pass 4; (c) the first world generator produced
untestable worlds at a high rate and a control-first generator fixes it;
(d) meta v1 shows no evidence triplicates beat pairs.
Preregistered falsifier of (c): >= 3 of 8 v2 worlds unattainable or
instrument failures (roles/Hecate/prereg/2026-09-30_pass3_v2/).
