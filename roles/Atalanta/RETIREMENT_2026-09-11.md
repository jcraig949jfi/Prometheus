## The laws lifted, and where they now live

The findings outlive the agent. None of them is Atalanta's to enforce.

1. THE ROOT DEFECT (operator's formulation, 2026-09-11): "A consumer was
   allowed to invent the producer's interface, and then mistake failure of
   that invented interface for failure of the producer." This is the law.
   Everything else here is a consequence of it.
   Routed to Archaeon as its own proposed ruling, deliberately NOT bundled
   into rule 10, because prevention and containment are different claims
   and should be argued separately.

2. CONTAINMENT IS NOT PREVENTION. Proposed base rule 10 (bound consecutive
   non-productive ticks, park at N, post once to a named accountable seat)
   turns 354 punctual useless ticks into at most N. It does not stop the
   consumer from launching against an invented interface, nor from
   accusing the wrong party when it fails. Filed with that limit stated in
   its own section 5.
   roles/Atalanta/PROPOSED_INVARIANT_2026-09-11.md, with a 120-line
   reference implementation and 9 controls, 9 passed.

3. EMISSION IS NOT PRODUCTIVITY. A heartbeat, an artifact, a database row
   or a clean exit is evidence that machinery operated, never that useful
   work occurred. Atalanta wrote a well-formed artifact on every one of
   its 354 dead ticks and would score 354/354 productive under any
   emission-keyed measure. This is why rule 10 must key on rule 8's
   productivity predicate and on nothing else; the dependency is the
   anti-cheat, not an implementation detail.

4. ALERT EMITTED IS NOT AN ESCALATION PRIMITIVE. Escalation requires an
   accountable recipient PLUS an obligation-or-action transition.
   Otherwise it is another artifact generator. Atalanta's alarm was
   correct, precise and continuous for six days and eight hours into a
   table, and 641 such rows across four agents have never been read by
   anyone. The census (18 of 30 loops unrouted, 9 more routed to a status
   file or "the operator", 3 where something acts) says this is
   architectural, not an Atalanta quirk.
   roles/Atalanta/CENSUS_LOOP_RISK_2026-09-11.md.

5. A CORRECTION TO THE P47 AUTOPSY, filed as an annotation and never as an
   edit to another seat's record: "consumer of a dead producer"
   back-projects the 2026-09-01 Apollo suspension onto May. Apollo
   committed 12 times during Atalanta's exact window. The corrected
   boundary is finding 1 above.

6. THE PARSER DISTINCTION, preserved on the operator's instruction:
   Atalanta was NOT invalidated by primitive_sequence, which is still live
   throughout apollo/src. The organism model was sound. The defect was
   producer discovery and container assumptions. The distinction says
   where to look next time.
