-----
0. SUMMARY
-----
Mandate (operator charter, 2026-09-30): test whether synthetic novelty can
emerge when human concepts (G0) are compiled into an EXECUTABLE substrate
and collided recursively by machinery, with no LLM in any collision,
selection or verdict path, so that later generations build on the ecology's
own history. The charter's central object is the CONCEPT TENSOR: every
collision inserts a generated k-ary interaction law whose gains come from
the parents' latent factors.

Starting point (v0/v0_1, 2026-09-30): the preregistered niche test H1
FAILED. THESEUS-24 confirmed the FAIL at n = 175 per arm.

What the push established, all preregistered and gated:

 1. No behavioural novelty, no composition of planted parts.
    - Only one of five structure rulers (R5) tells designed from random
      programs.
    - Deep descendants carry less graded causal structure than one-shot
      collisions.
    - Writer + reader parts from different lineages almost never compose,
      whatever was tried without selection (0/1011 in context; with parts
      available and aligned, D 2/120 vs random 5/120).

 2. A real, replicated CAPABILITY effect. Programs got an input channel and
    a cue-recall task that EVERY 1-part program fails (1-part controls .195-
    .203 at chance .25; store+release pairs 1.0). Pooled over 3 seeds:

      task selection vs reproducibility selection   RD .197 [.119, .274]
      collision-generated laws vs none              RD .213 [.134, .293]

    Both transfer to a held-out harder variant (V 8, k 16):
      selection .147 [.065, .229]
      laws      .263 [.178, .349]

    Law-built solvers generalise far better (fresh seed: 85% vs 27% at V 8).

 3. The mechanism is mundane, and the concept tensor is not part of it.
    a) The no-law deficit is at RELEASE: putting the stored cue back into
       the sensor channel. Among store-intact solvers, RD .216 [.132, .301].
    b) Appending ANY storage->sensor react coupling to failing solvers
       rescues release in about half of them. A single-source coupling
       rescues best (37/79). Law-bearing ecologies have such a coupling in
       93% of solvers, no-law ecologies in 19%.
    c) Replacing the tensor's learned law gains with RANDOM gains, all else
       identical:

         supply (random-gain laws vs none), 4 seeds
           capability     RD .143 [.075, .210]
           generalisation RD .216 [.128, .303]
         tensor content (tensor vs random gains), 6 seeds
           capability     RD -.007 [-.054, .041]
           generalisation RD .021 [-.034, .076]

       Preregistered bound: the tensor's content contributes less than 10
       points of deep-solver share; the CI caps it near 4.

 4. Compressed SYN concepts are a known function in a new implementation.
    - 13/20 are indistinguishable on the task from a hand-written 2-rule
      store+release program; the rest are worse.
    - As re-injected collision matter they beat matched non-solving matter
      only narrowly (2 seeds, RD .09 [.007, .173]), and not at all without
      task selection.
    - What helps is law-bearing matter, solving or not. Lawless random matter
      with the same head start does worse than no injection (45 vs 63).

 5. Calibration: 32 wrong calls are in the ledger.
    - Every first-seed effect size and every post-hoc pattern tested on fresh
      data shrank or vanished.
    - Every pooled, preregistered effect held.
    - Three successive mechanism readings of the generalisation effect failed
      (essential law, redundancy, multi-source law form) before the plainest
      account (supply of a read-back coupling) survived.

Precise language (charter):
  - Strongest instance: solvers whose essential step is a k-ary interaction
    law with no direct raw-human provenance, generated at collision time.
  - What carries the effect is that such a coupling EXISTS, not the
    tensor-derived values inside it.
  - Interpretation is limited to the store/release locus.
  - "Human priors eliminated" is not claimed: the op set, task, compiler and
    G0 corpus are human priors.

2. WHY THE CAPABILITY LINE IS THE RIGHT ENDPOINT
-----
H1 asked whether recursion reaches new behaviour. It does not, at this
scale and with these rulers. The capability line asks something sharper:
does recursive collision build a multi-part mechanism that a task needs
and that no single part supplies? The composition-necessary task makes
"one part suffices" false by construction.

The follow-up asks whether the charter's distinctive ingredient, the
concept tensor, is what makes the difference. That is the question the
charter most needs answered, and the push now answers it for this task.

5. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH
-----
DOES, conditional on this substrate, op set, compiler and task family:
  - a recursive ecology that inserts a generated k-ary coupling into every
    collision child builds store/release solutions that are more frequent
    and generalise better than recombination of human-derived rules alone
  - the benefit is located at RELEASE and is causally reproducible by
    appending a read-back coupling
  - the concept tensor's learned gains contribute less than 10 points
    (6 seeds; point estimate zero)

DOES NOT:
  - behavioural niche novelty (H1 FAIL)
  - a novel FUNCTION (SYN concepts re-derive store+release)
  - composition of planted parts
  - any value of the concept tensor's CONTENT on this task
  - anything beyond this task family: other tasks, rulers or substrates
    might reveal tensor content that matters; none was found here

Claim ceiling: "recursive collision that always inserts a new k-ary
coupling supplies the read-back step of a two-step memory function; the
concept tensor's content is not what makes it work."

7. DECISION / RECOMMENDATION (HITL's call; Theseus's lean)
-----
Options:
  (A) Retire the concept-tensor line. Its central object does no detectable
      work on the only task where the ecology shows a capability. Keep the
      machinery and the capability result as a documented negative.
  (B) Give the tensor one more chance on a task where coupling CONTENT, not
      just existence, should matter: one whose solution needs a specific
      sign or ratio among several sources. If random gains match tensor
      gains there too, retire.
  (C) Drop the tensor and pursue the capability line directly (task
      families, harder compositions), treating "always insert a coupling"
      as the operator that matters.

Theseus leans to (B) as a bounded final test of about 1 day, with
retirement of the tensor line as the preregistered default if it fails.
(A) is a fully defensible answer now.

8. QUESTIONS FOR THE REVIEWER (written to resist agreement)
-----
  1. Is the composition-necessary task too easy to discriminate (60-90% of
     solvers everywhere)? Does the supply result just reflect that any
     react-into-sensor helps on THIS readout?
  2. RANDLAW keeps the tensor-derived law placement, sources and
     destination, and only randomises gains. Is that a fair test of
     "tensor content", or does content live in which parents and channels
     get coupled? If the latter, the negative is narrower than stated.
  3. Three mechanism readings failed before "supply" survived. Is supply
     the fourth story that will fail, or is it simply what the 50 swap and
     the 51-53 ablation directly show?
  4. Should "the ecology inserts a coupling at every collision" count as
     synthetic ancestry at all, or is it a fixed human-designed operator,
     making the capability result a property of the operator?
  5. The niche test (H1) failed and was not revisited. Is the push's turn
     to capability a legitimate reframing or a goal shift?
