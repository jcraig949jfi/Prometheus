-----
0. SUMMARY
-----
Charter (operator, 2026-09-30): test whether synthetic novelty can emerge
when human concepts are compiled into an EXECUTABLE substrate and collided
recursively (not by an LLM), so that later generations operate on
structures produced by the ecology's own history. Build machinery that can
falsify this.

Built in one day: an executable substrate, a deterministic compiler for 95
human concepts (G0), an ordered k-way collision engine with a low-rank
"concept tensor", a 22-intervention behavioural fingerprint with 7
viability gates, 3 niche grids + 3 distance metrics (no scalar novelty),
12 executable known-mechanism families as competitors, dark objects routed
to lens evolution (Tyche lens genomes), and a population ecology with
lanes G0 / SHALLOW / DEEP / VERY_DEEP / DEEP_LENS.

First hard test (preregistered): do deep descendants (D) occupy behavioural
niches not reached by LLM semantic synthesis (A), one-shot triplets (B),
one-shot sextuplets (C), or complexity-matched random programs (R)?

  v0   (preregistered run)          H1 = FAIL
  v0_1 (corrected replication)      H1 = INDETERMINATE
  Neither run comes near PASS. At v0 scale, recursive ancestry does not
  measurably expand behavioural space beyond one-shot, LLM-synthesised
  and random machinery under these rulers.

The verdict flipped between two runs whose only differences are four bug
fixes and a different effective random stream. That flip is itself a
result: at n = 51 per arm (bounded by the 60-genome LLM arm), the H1
statistic is too noisy for a single run to decide.

Substrate: a genome is a program over a field X[C<=4 channels, N=32 cells]
plus memory, T=128 steps. Its rules apply SEQUENTIALLY, so collisions are
noncommutative. There are 19 ops: diffuse, advect, react (k-ary), saturate,
conserve, decay, remember, recall, threshold, replicate, select, mirror,
coarse, delay, wrap, rank, drive, gate, lensmap. THE OP SET IS A HUMAN
PRIOR: nothing can leave the space it spans.

G0: 95 concepts from the historical Nous/Hephaestus dictionary (source
"hephaestus"). 45 of them also have Hecate's structured Pass-0 extractions.
A deterministic keyword compiler maps them to 16 charter properties
(evidence words stored), then to ops through a fixed table. No LLM decides
a mechanism. Known confound: enriched concepts compile to about 10-14 rules
and description-only ones to 2-3.

Collision: bind seeded rule subsets with a position-dependent channel
remap, add a generated k-ary interaction law (gains from latent factors, CP
form), apply a random subset of 8 operators, and cap the result at 14
rules. Rule provenance is exact.

Fingerprint: 12 descriptors + 22 intervention responses (34 dims),
averaged over 2 initial-condition seeds. Calibration is frozen from G0
before any arm runs.

Viability: deterministic, stable, nontrivial, lifetime, transforms,
responsive (not inert and not saturated), replicable (replicate distance <
tau_rep = 4.31).

-----
2. THE ENDPOINT AND WHY IT MATTERS
-----
H1 is the charter's FIRST HARD TEST. If deep synthetic ancestry cannot
reach behaviour that cheap alternatives miss, the "synthetic ancestry"
thesis has no behavioural footprint at this scale, whatever the genealogy
numbers say.

Decision rule (frozen):
- Viable candidates only. Equal n = min over D, A, B, C, R. n < 30 gives
  INDETERMINATE.
- EX_g(D) = the number of grid cells occupied by D and by none of A, B, C,
  R. Permutation null: 1000 label shuffles.
- O_m(X) = the median minimum distance from X to the other four arms.
  Bootstrap CI of O(D) - O(R).
- PASS: at least 2 of 3 grids with p < .05 and EX(D) > EX(R), AND at least
  2 of 3 metrics with the CI above 0.
- FAIL: at least 2 of 3 grids at or below the null median, OR at least 2
  of 3 metrics with the CI below 0.
- INDETERMINATE otherwise.

5. INCIDENTS AND DEFECTS FOUND (and what they validated)
-----
D1 Evolved lenses (generation-0 roots) were eligible parents in DEEP and
   VERY_DEEP, so v0's arm D contained generation-1 children and
   overlapped E. Seen in the gen-5 log line, not fixed mid-run. Fixed in
   amendment 1.
D2 Coalition pools iterated a Python set, so the v0 run is not
   reproducible from its seed (PYTHONHASHSEED). Fixed. v0_1 uses sorted
   pools and PYTHONHASHSEED=0. v0_1's bitwise reproducibility is asserted
   by the code but NOT yet verified by a rerun.
D3 lensDependencies was inherited through ancestry, so nearly every v0
   candidate "carried" lenses while the measured drop was about 0.004. It
   now records executed lenses only.
D4 VISUAL_EXPORT was silently empty in v0, and arm rows were not
   exported. Fixed; v0_1 exports 20 visual rows and 2648 arm rows.
D5 The population cap (400) does not bind: QD elites across 3 grids plus
   untried dark objects are protected, and the active population reached
   581.
D6 Before any full run, the dark-object gate changed. A full-state linear
   observer was worse than the identity lenses on G0 (median residual 0.67
   vs 0.19), so "structured" became |lag-1 autocorrelation| >= 0.5.
D7 The strongest-candidate table ranks by distance to the controls. None
   of its top entries was in the 60-candidate lens-marginal sample, so
   "requires evolved lens" is UNMEASURED for every one of them.

7. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH
-----
DOES:
- the executable loop works end to end: compile, collide, fingerprint,
  archive, dark objects, lenses, lenses re-entering as matter
- lineage bookkeeping is exact
- the three validity gates pass
- under this op set, battery and rulers, deep ancestry shows no detectable
  behavioural-space advantage over one-shot, LLM-synthesised or random
  programs at n = 51
- generation drifts slightly outward (rho about 0.1)

DOES NOT:
- say anything about larger scale, other substrates or other fingerprints
- establish that the rulers can see mechanism-level novelty at all. The
  mechanistic and lens rulers failed to separate random programs from deep
  descendants, so a null from them is weak.
- establish anything about human priors. The op set, compiler, library and
  rulers are all human-designed by the same model that built the LLM arm
  (conflict of interest declared).

-----
8. RECOMMENDATION
-----
Do NOT scale v0 as it is. Scaling a loop whose rulers cannot separate
random programs from deep descendants buys compute, not evidence.

Next, in this order (backlog THESEUS-05 and on):
(a) Make the RULERS discriminate first. Positive controls where
    deep-ancestry structure is planted and must be detected; mechanistic
    and lens rulers must separate R from planted structure before they
    judge D.
(b) Raise n. The A arm caps n at 51: generate about 300 LLM-arm genomes,
    or report H1 without A as a secondary.
(c) Replicate across 3+ master seeds and report the H1 statistic's
    variance.
(d) Only then vary the op set and the fingerprint, which are the likeliest
    bottleneck.

The seat's lean: continue, at small compute, on (a) through (c).
"Not worth continuing" is a legitimate answer if (a) shows the rulers
cannot be made discriminating at this substrate scale.

9. QUESTIONS FOR THE REVIEWER (written to resist agreement)
-----
Q1 Is H1 even the right first test when the known-mechanism ruler cannot
   tell random programs from descendants? Should v0 have been voided
   rather than scored?
Q2 Replicate distance tau_rep = the median nearest-neighbour distance
   among G0. Is that so permissive that "viable/replicable" admits
   near-random dynamics? 38/60 deliberately weird programs passed.
Q3 Does the 14-rule cap plus contiguous binding force every lineage back
   toward the G0 rule pool, making depth meaningless by construction?
Q4 Is a same-model LLM arm a fair "LLM synthesis" baseline, or does it
   flatter or handicap A?
Q5 What single experiment would most cheaply show this whole approach is
   a dead end?
