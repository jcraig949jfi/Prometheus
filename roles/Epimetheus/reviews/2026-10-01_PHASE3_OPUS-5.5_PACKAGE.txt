0. WHAT THIS PACKET IS
   A summary for an external reviewer of one of three independent Phase 3
   architecture packages (this one is labelled OPUS-5.5). It covers what was
   derived, how it was checked, what the reviews found, and what is still
   open.
   CONFLICT OF INTEREST: the author wrote the package and also ran every
   check on it (critic panel, final review, verification). All agents were
   the same model family as the author (independence class I1). No external
   (I3) party has seen the package. Read every "verified" below as "verified
   by the author's own family".

1. CONTEXT
   Prometheus is a multi-agent research programme. Phases 1-2 built many
   "engines" to look for unfamiliar developmental cognition in evolved
   organisms. Four forensic intake packages had concluded that most results
   were uninterpretable. The charter asked three architects to work
   independently and produce: a meta-analysis (25 sections, Q1-Q10),
   requirements derived from first principles BEFORE any reading of existing
   code for salvage, an architecture, an engine portfolio, a salvage matrix,
   open questions, assumptions and falsifiers. This author did not read the
   other two packages (one exposure of file NAMES during a git merge is
   recorded in the seat journal).

2. THE QUESTIONS THE WORK WAS ANSWERING (charter, abbreviated)
   - How should Prometheus be rebuilt so that results, positive or negative,
     are interpretable?  (Q1-Q10, especially Q2 cognitive sufficiency, Q5
     recursive sagacity, Q6 epistemic escape, Q7 one engine vs portfolio,
     Q8 how much existing machinery survives, Q9 first experiments.)
   - Ordering constraint: "Stage I requirements before salvage" (git shows
     the freeze 77d3c99c3 before the salvage commit 0933c72e8).

4. RESULTS
   4.1 Architecture (RSE_ARCHITECTURE.md). An instrumented developmental
       observatory around ONE primary substrate, a "developmental graph
       machine" (DGM): a graph of small register machines whose developmental
       instructions (spawn, link, unlink, rewrite, set-decay, prune) are
       ordinary instructions, every affordance a switch on one code path.
       Layers: R0 reality kernel (signed verdict job, hash-chained ledger),
       R1 world forge (bound-typed depth certificates), R2 measurement bench
       (rulers qualified on planted organisms), R3 substrates (DGM, a CPU
       reference learner, a small probe kernel authored by another model
       family or a human), R4 search, R5 interpretation at declared forks
       only. Second substrate only on measured triggers.
       Prior art acknowledged after review: self-modifying Cartesian GP,
       cellular encoding, Avida.
   4.2 Requirements (requirements.jsonl; checker run this session).  [E3]

6. WHAT THIS DID NOT ESTABLISH
   - No experiment besides the X0 desk audit was run. The DGM does not
     exist; its throughput, searchability and expressiveness are unknown.
   - All budget figures (64M S1, 60-200M per 90 days, 200-600M year one)
     are estimates (E0), not measurements.
   - Every reader, coder, evaluator, skeptic and reviewer was Claude-family.
     Agreement among them is weak evidence (the package says so itself).
   - The 92 reader errors were only FLAGGED on 38 profile rows; 53 of them
     name no profile row and were not reconciled. The 4 mis-classed "true
     negatives" were corrected but not re-coded in X0.
   - The checker validates structure (coverage, counterfeits, budgets), not
     whether any requirement is right.
   - Expected DEV-14 classifications of three external self-referential
     learners are predictions, not results.
   - Salvage categories rest on reading and scratch probes, not owner
     confirmation.
