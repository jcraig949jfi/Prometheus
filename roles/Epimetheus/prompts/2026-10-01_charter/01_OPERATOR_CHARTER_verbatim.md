5. DEVELOPMENTAL SAGACITY

Give special attention to the developmental problem.

A human infant and a mature expert share broadly similar biological substrate but exhibit radically different realized cognitive competence.

The working hypothesis is that cognitive development may progressively transform experience into reusable internal structure.

Consider the possible chain:

experience

→ regularity

→ representation

→ compressed structure

→ reusable cognitive operation

→ abstraction

→ reasoning over abstractions

→ reasoning about reasoning processes

Do not accept this chain merely because it is plausible.

Analyze:

* what must be physically true for this to happen,
* what kinds of compression are involved,
* how abstractions might become manipulable objects,
* how developmental stages create new reachable stages,
* what role environmental curriculum plays,
* and what empirical signatures would distinguish this from ordinary training.

Develop a rigorous distinction among:

Latent cognitive capacity

What the substrate could eventually support.

Developmental capacity

What structures the organism can construct through experience.

Realized competence

What it can currently do.

Transferable sagacity

How much prior development reduces the cost of solving genuinely new classes of problems.

Recursive sagacity

Whether developmental experience improves the machinery by which future developmental improvement occurs.

Attempt to operationalize these concepts.

Do not rely solely on task accuracy.

8. THE LLM EPISTEMIC ESCAPE PROBLEM

Prometheus is largely designed and interpreted by LLMs trained on human artifacts.

Can a search system mediated by historical human priors discover genuinely unfamiliar cognitive machinery?

Analyze the distinction between using an LLM as:

* architect,
* mutation generator,
* coder,
* hypothesis generator,
* interpreter,
* classifier,
* admission gate.

The dangerous loop is:

LLM generates → LLM recognizes → LLM admits

because unfamiliar mechanisms may be rejected as noise.

Design principles should consider separating:

Generation

LLMs, mutation, recombination, program synthesis, stochastic search.

Reality

Deterministic worlds, developmental trajectories, causal interventions, survival.

Measurement

Calibrated rulers and controls.

Interpretation

LLMs, humans, prior-art search, theoretical analysis.

Consider whether:

failure of all available models to classify a reproducible causal phenomenon should increase rather than decrease investigative priority.

Develop explicit anti-gravity mechanisms that prevent the search from collapsing onto familiar ML architectures.
