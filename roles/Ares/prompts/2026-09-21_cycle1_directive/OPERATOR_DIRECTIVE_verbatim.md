Give Ares one more round, but make it a closure/validation round—not an open-ended Cycle 1. Then probably park him.

Cycle 0 earned that much. Ares did three important things at once: produced a genuinely interesting W4 fossil, found evidence that different tasks exploit different memory carriers, and caught weaknesses in his own instruments rather than silently converting them into discoveries. That is exactly the behavior you want from a new lens. But the strongest claim is still only 3 seeds, and the known ablation/threshold defects mean parking him now would freeze an unnecessarily ambiguous fossil.

I would give him this directive:

ARES CYCLE 1 — VALIDATE, COMPRESS, EXIT

Do not broaden the world catalog and do not begin another exploratory sweep.

1. Repair the three known instrument defects:
    * no_state must eliminate every cross-step memory channel, including plastic weights.
    * repair W5 scoring/control semantics.
    * recalibrate W12 thresholds from its own mode rather than borrowing them.
2. Rerun W4, W5, and W12 at 10 independent seeds with the repaired controls.
3. For W4, establish whether the GATE+MAX structure is:
    * repeatedly rediscovered,
    * merely one implementation among several,
    * or an idiosyncrasy of the original lineage.
4. Run the combined W4+W5 pressure only as a carrier stress test: does evolution preserve one memory substrate, recruit the other, or construct something different?
5. Perform ancestry reconstruction and causal transplant on every qualifying mechanism. Preserve failures too.
6. Give Nyx/Harmonia the artifacts, but do not wait for their vocabulary before recording the behavior.
7. End with a hard disposition:
    * VALIDATED_FOSSIL
    * MECHANISM_DIVERSE
    * CONTROL_ARTIFACT
    * NO_REPLICATION

Stop after this round. Do not self-authorize Cycle 2.

The key question isn’t whether Ares can find more. He almost certainly can. The question is whether his pressure-engineering lens has demonstrated a distinct yield curve. Cycle 1 can answer that cheaply.

If W4 survives 10 seeds and especially if multiple lineages independently invent functionally equivalent memory machinery, Ares has proven the lens and can be parked successfully with a reusable pressure catalog. If W4 evaporates, park him because you learned the original signal was brittle. If the W4+W5 combination unexpectedly causes a qualitatively new carrier or architecture to appear, that is the one result I would allow to earn Ares another round.

Given your “many lenses, not billions of years” strategy, I would resist turning every productive engine into a permanent research program. Ares should prove the lens, fossilize what it exposes, then surrender compute to the next weird engine.
