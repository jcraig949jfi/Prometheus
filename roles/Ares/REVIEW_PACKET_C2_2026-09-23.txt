WHAT WAS ASKED
  One focused round, most adversarial yet: attack the carrier cycle 1
  found. (1) why does recurrence win, (2) will evolution route around
  it when blocked/taxed, (3) is the carrier modular or entangled,
  (4) does carrier choice change under hostile pressure. Fix five
  instrument defects first. End on a three-gate stopping rule; if the
  answer is only "recurrence wins again", close and export.

THE ANSWER (and it is not the one preregistered)
  All three carriers the substrate offers are INDIVIDUALLY SUFFICIENT:
      only_recurrence  10/10 at the cap, median 10 generations
      only_plasticity  10/10 at the cap, median 17.5
      only_keep         9/10 at the cap, median 30 (tail to 90)
      none              0/10 (floor arm: the exclusion machinery works)
      all available    10/10 at the cap, median 10 == recurrence-only
  So the preference is a SPEED phenomenon, not a capability one. Four
  candidate causes were tested and three died:
   - intrinsic superiority: dead BEFORE the cycle ran. Hand-built, the
     DESIGNATED carrier scores 33.75 and recurrence 24.56 on W4. The
     carrier evolution never picks has the better optimum.
   - gradient at first appearance: dead. One mutation on a stripped
     champion, 300 times, 5 lineages: mean effect ~0 and probability
     of immediate improvement 0.00 for BOTH carriers.
   - raw opportunity: dead. Recurrence is only 1.32x more reachable;
     an 8x mutational subsidy on keep raised its creation rate 4.8x
     and did NOT flip the carrier (RECUR 5, REDUNDANT 4, PLAST 1).
   - USEFUL reachability: looked decisive -- one mutation makes a
     usable recurrent edge with p=0.0173 and a usable keep with
     p=0.0000 (0 of 8000), because keep steps by N(0,0.3) and needs
     ~0.9. So it was TESTED, not asserted: an arm with a wider keep
     step (usable-keep creation 0.0231, ABOVE recurrence's 0.0173),
     prediction recorded first at ">= 3/10 keep load-bearing".
     RESULT 2/10. MY PREDICTION LOST. 7/10 champions now carry a keep
     node at 0.98 and selection still routed through recurrence.
  WHAT SURVIVES -- basin width (EXPLORATORY, post-hoc, hand-wired):
      KEEP   best 33.75, viable in 3 of 25 swept values, only
             [0.94, 0.98] -- a 4% sliver against its own ceiling
      RECUR  best 40.00, viable in 14 of 23, [2.75, 6.0], SATURATING
             (4.0 -> 40, 5.0 -> 40, 6.0 -> 40; larger never worse)
  Keep must be loaded and held by one scalar, and as it approaches 1
  the input term (1-keep) vanishes, so the working region is a knife
  edge. A self-loop has no such trade-off. Two unplanned corroborations:
  jittering every recurrent edge at every reproduction did not dislodge
  recurrence (7/10, and the FASTEST arm at 5 generations -- jitter
  inside a flat basin is harmless), and keep's time-to-threshold has
  the long tail (10-90) expected of a target that must be found AND held.

  THE TRANSFERABLE STATEMENT: a supplied primitive is not "available"
  to evolutionary search because it exists, works and is reachable in
  one mutation. It is available when its VIABLE REGION IS WIDE AND ITS
  GRADIENT FLAT. The substrate routed around design intent because the
  designated carrier's working region was a sliver and the
  undesignated one's was a plateau.

WHAT THIS CYCLE FOUND WRONG -- INCLUDING IN ITSELF
  1. CYCLE 1 DOES NOT FULLY REPLICATE. It reported W4 activation
     memory 10/10 and plasticity 0/10. On ten fresh lineages with
     edge/SCC-aware attribution: recurrence load-bearing 7/10,
     plasticity 4/10, keep 1/10. The 0/10 has probability ~0.6% under
     these proportions. Recurrence is the most common carrier, not the
     only one. Cycle 1's coarser ablation could not separate keep from
     recurrence and labelled both "ACT".
  2. HELD-OUT SELECTION BIAS (D1). The reported champion was chosen by
     argmax over 128 organisms EVALUATED ON THE HELD-OUT SET. Shuffled
     controls read 4.22 / 13.47 / 1.41 / 1.50; the clean number
     (training-selected champion, already logged) is 0.00 / -2.38 /
     -0.14 / 0.00. Every headline sits at the structural cap and is
     unaffected, and everything was re-derived from the clean number.
     The reassuring consequence: with the bias removed the controls
     sit exactly at the floor, so the WORLDS do not leak -- the
     apparatus did.
  3. BIASED GATE STATISTIC (D2). Gate C first printed "median recovery
     1.0" using the best donor of nine per host. Per PAIR: median 0.02,
     >= 0.5 in 16/59; edge-aware transplant portable 4/9. Gate C is
     SHUT. A transfer claim is scored per attempt, never by the best
     attempt.
  4. Prediction scorecard: P1 P2 P5 P7 P10 held; P3 P4 lost; P6 and P8
     split; P9 held on transplant and lost on the honest swap
     statistic; the post-hoc c3 prediction lost. Four ledger rows.

WHAT WAS NOT ESTABLISHED

  Redundancy under attack (W15: recurrence 8/10 AND plasticity 7/10,
  recurrent edges up 4.5x) is unexplained and was predicted by nobody.
