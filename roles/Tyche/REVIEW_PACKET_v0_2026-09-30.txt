--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0. SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Charter: evolve executable perceptual transformations ("lenses") that make
part of the DARK RESIDUAL -- what the current ecology of lenses and weak
learners has not made exploitable -- reproducibly exploitable, kept only for
measured consequence, never for intelligibility. v0 had to show the loop
(mutation -> differential capability -> reproduction -> residual shift ->
new generation) works end to end without leakage or semantic judgment.

Verdicts (preregistered, computed in code):
  H5 instrument PASS | H2 false gradients PASS | H1 planted controls
  INDETERMINATE | H3 residual shift FAIL | H4 redundancy FAIL |
  H6 end to end INDETERMINATE.

Headline, as failure shapes:
  (a) No false gradients: 0 of 8 structure-destroyed / keyed-PRF worlds
      produced a replicated, null-beating gain; 0 of 210 admission tests
      admitted a lens on them. The leakage guard caught a planted cheat.
  (b) The search reaches structure that leaks partial information and
      misses zero-marginal structure: running count mod 3 and sign of a
      delayed product were found from chance; a delayed XOR (each half
      carries zero information) stayed at the noise floor for 40
      generations.
  (c) Two of my instruments manufactured or mis-measured residuals: an
      organism's attention budget created "dark residual" that evolution
      then "solved" (H4), and the residual definition tracked organism
      disagreement rather than unexploited information (H3).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Evolution: N = 96, 4 epochs x 10 generations. Case vector per lens =
  val marginal gain over the current ecology for 24 worlds x 2 rulers x
  3 organisms x 3 residual scopes (all / err = every organism wrong /
  dis = organisms disagree) = 432 cases; epsilon-lexicase parents; elites
  best per (world, ruler); dark reserve 14 slots (age protection, novelty
  of a behaviour signature, random quota); 25% cross-lineage graft.
Admission (epoch end, CONF split only): paired z >= 4 and gain >= 0.01,
  1 per world per epoch; admitted lenses join a GLOBAL ecology whose
  outputs become features for every organism on every world.
Pass D per admitted lens: test split; replication on 2 fresh seeds;
  64 matched random lenses (same size) as null; causality audit (future
  replaced at 5 cuts, output must be bit-identical); channel ablation;
  transfer to all 31 other worlds (counted only with fresh-seed
  replication AND beating 32 matched random lenses there).

4. FAILURE SHAPES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F1 residual instrument (H3 FAIL). On negative worlds accuracy did not move
   (PRF1 lin 0.495 -> 0.499, tree 0.506 -> 0.491) but the err residual
   fell 0.261 -> 0.140 and dis rose 0.492 -> 0.735 (TSD1, TSD3 alike).
   More ecology features decorrelate the organisms, so "all wrong"
   shrinks mechanically. The residual had no negative control of its own.
F2 manufactured residual (H4 FAIL). tab's 10-feature budget (candidate,
   then admitted lenses newest first, then raw) pushes raw channels out
   as the ecology grows: tab baseline on K1_ident 1.000, 0.995, 0.547,
   0.998, 0.518 by epoch; a lens restoring the channel earned +0.49. tab
   took 28/44 admissions (4, 6, 11, 7 by epoch). On P2 a tree lens lifted
   tree to 1.000 in epoch 0 while tab stayed at 0.338-0.362 all run, so
   three later P2 tab admissions re-bought information the ecology had.
F3 needle blindness (H1 INDETERMINATE). Everything reached had a partial
   gradient or a one-op path. Zero-marginal structure (P1 xor, P7 parity,
   the xor inside ADV_decoy) was not reached. Three of my "inaccessible"
   controls were accessible to random lenses (design error, VOID).
F4 seed fragility (open). Three P5/R2/tree lenses: +0.236 / +0.159 /
   +0.134 on the selection seed's test split vs 0.033 / -0.006 / 0.000 on
   fresh seeds (L-3c1c67ab5c exactly 0.000 on both). Caught by
   replication; mechanism unknown.
F5 revived lineages: 37/44 admitted lenses have a reserve ancestor, but
   no base-rate null was computed; with 14 slots x 40 generations this
   may be what any lineage looks like. Not evidence for the reserve.

6. PROCESS DEFECTS (mine)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Compute estimate "< 1 core-hour" was wrong by > 10x (pilot stopped at
  2 epochs; never reached the largest ecology).
- Positive-control design assumed delayed laws hide from 1-4 op lenses;
  3 of 6 did not. Initial access should have been measured before freeze.
- Residual and organism-budget instruments shipped without negative or
  cheat controls of their own -- the exact doctrine the base role states.

7. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DOES: the leakage guard works (cheat caught, 0 false gradients over 8
negative worlds and 210 admission tests); local mutation + lexicase +
graft reaches depth-3-to-5 compositions when partial gradients exist
(P2 from chance to +0.675, replicated); held-out transfer exists within a
law family (P3 -> P3 sibling).
DOES NOT: show any lens exploiting structure that was dark to the whole
lens grammar; show residual shift (instrument invalid); show the dark
reserve mattered; say anything about the Hecate alien worlds beyond "15
admissions there, mostly tab" (confounded by F2). The 28 "opaque
successful" lenses are opaque only because nobody looked; several are
likely capacity patches or F2 artifacts.

8. RECOMMENDATION (operator's call; seat's lean)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Lean: CONTINUE to v1, instrument first, small. Order: (1) remove tab's
ecology-order budget and re-run the H4 check; (2) a residual with its own

conclusion is that this search family is gradient-following and cannot
see zero-marginal dark residual -- which is itself the most useful v1
answer. STOP: the global unbounded ecology (cost 2 s -> 18 s per
generation and it created F2).
