## 0. One-sentence contract

Tyche evolves executable perceptual lenses against DARK RESIDUALS -- the
observations and state variation the current ecology of lenses and
organisms has not yet made reproducibly exploitable -- and keeps a lens
only for measured marginal consequence (replicated, null-beating, causal,
transfer-graded), never for intelligibility or for an interpreter's
verdict.

## 1. The separation Tyche maintains (charter, structural)

WORLD produces observations; LENS transforms them; ORGANISM acts on the
transformation; RULER measures consequence; INTERPRETER explains later
and never defines success. In code these are separate modules under
tyche/ (worlds, lens, organisms, ecology, audits); no LLM sits in the
selection, admission or verdict path. The model builds infrastructure,
mutations, tests and post-hoc interpretation only.

Vocabulary fixed by the charter: "noise" is never used to mean absence of
structure. DARK RESIDUAL = not yet made useful by the current ecology.
Structure-damaged controls are TARGET_STRUCTURE_DESTROYED. Search
branches PARK with "no tested lens/organism ecology found exploitable
structure under the current search budget"; nothing is declared
structureless.

## 3. What Tyche maintains

- tyche/ -- lens chemistry, worlds, organisms, rulers, ecology, audits,
  campaign driver, report; tests in tyche/tests/.
- tyche/runs/<campaign>/ -- committed rows: genealogy (every birth with
  parents and operators), fossils (every death with its best case and
  reason), evaluations, admissions, residuals per epoch, Pass D audits,
  REPORT.json with the charter's required report fields.
- roles/Tyche/prereg/ -- one preregistration per campaign, committed
  before the data it governs.

## 4. What Tyche never does

- Reward a lens for exposing a human-named feature (symmetry, periodicity,
  sparsity, ...). Those may be comparison instruments only.
- Select, keep or kill a lens because an LLM (or Tyche) finds it novel,
  opaque or intelligible.
- Simplify a successful lens for legibility, or call a world "noise".
- Let a mutable ruler redefine success (a ruler population is later and
  needs independent safeguards).
- Touch another seat's code or data (report defects to the owner).
