--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0. SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Question (operator): can a lens lineage acquire a useful sense whose
necessary precursors have no individually measurable utility? Gate 6:
show one case where every precursor would have died under v0 selection
yet the dark ecology assembles a useful sensor.

Verdict (preregistered, computed in code): GATE 6 = FAIL.

The failure shape is more informative than a pass would have been:
  - the counterfactual was false: v0-style selection did NOT kill useless
    precursors. Epsilon-lexicase over 39 noisy cases kept a lineage alive
    for ~30 generations on noise-level "value" (0.02-0.03, mostly on the
    structure-destroyed twin worlds), then a graft from a sibling world
    (exaptation) and one insertion completed a delayed xor (+0.30).
  - explicit preservation + pair evaluation did not beat plain selection
    at equal budget (Z-world cells solved: V0 3, DENR 2, DE 1 of 10).
  - the directive's mechanism was seen once, below the bar: two lenses
    with individual home gains 0.004 and 0.006 fused into a replicated
    weak sensor (+0.075 test; +0.101 / +0.109 fresh seeds; twin clean).
  - true needles (3-way parity; xor of 3-op windowed precursors) were
    reached by no arm.

4. FAILURE SHAPES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F1 Noisy lexicase is an implicit dark ecology. Exploratory genealogy trace
   of V0 s1's Z1 solver: home gain -0.002..+0.002 from gen 0 to 31; the
   lineage's best cases were 0.019-0.030 on N3/N4/N1 twin worlds and Z5
   (noise). Gen 32: graft from a lineage valued +0.489 on C1 (xor with a
   raw channel). Gen 39: insert delay(ch3, 4) -> +0.302 on Z1.
F2 Soft precursors: a 6-window sum over the missing channel plus the exact
   other delay gave +0.076 (gen 15). 2-way xor has graded footholds in a
   chemistry with windows and smoothers.
F3 Preservation + pairing did not pay at equal budget (pairs cost units;
   DE ran 36 generations vs V0's 80).
F4 Useless-plus-useful coalitions are common: a zero-value partner lifted
   a ~0.1 lens to ~0.2 (DE s2 Z1) and completed +0.513 (DENR s1 Z1).
F5 Z3 (parity of 3) and Z5 (deep precursors): unreached by every arm.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. RECOMMENDATION (operator's call; seat's lean)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Lean: one more calibration round before the natural habitat, redesigned
around what v1 showed. (1) A strictly utility-gated arm (survival needs a
significant gain on some case) so "would have died" is true by
construction, beside noisy lexicase as its own arm. (2) Gate on the real
needles: parity of order >= 3 and deep-precursor xor, with triple
coalitions. (3) Cheaper pair screening so budget parity stops penalising
combination. (4) Preregister a neutral-persistence rate. If DE still does
not beat both V0 arms on order->=3 parity, retire "explicit preservation"
as a mechanism in this chemistry and keep noise persistence as the finding.
STOP: using 2-way xor as the canonical dark-ecology control in a chemistry
with window and smoothing operators.
