Tyche just found something more important than a positive v0 result.

The delayed-XOR failure is almost a perfect demonstration of why the dark ecology exists.

Each precursor can have approximately zero marginal value:

I(A;Y)\approx 0,\qquad I(B;Y)\approx 0

while jointly:

I(A,B;Y) > 0

If selection says “a lens survives only when it individually improves prediction,” then both proto-senses die before evolution ever gets the chance to combine them.

That means Tyche v0 is not merely “the search wasn’t powerful enough.” Its selection ontology excludes an important class of emergent senses.

The running-count-mod-3 result is exactly what that ecology can discover: a gradient exists all the way up the hill. Delayed XOR requires crossing a region where the intermediate mutations appear useless.

That is enormously informative.

I would change the priority order somewhat.

The residual catalogue is valuable, but I would not yet start perturbing dozens of historical residuals. The experimental organism isn’t mature enough. Otherwise we’ll feed precious Prometheus anomalies into an apparatus we already know is blind to exactly the kind of synergistic structure we’re interested in.

First, make delayed XOR the canonical dark-ecology positive control.

Tyche v1 should ask:

Can a lens lineage acquire a useful sense whose necessary precursor components have no individually measurable utility?

That is a much sharper test than “can evolution find a lens?”

The dark ecology should preserve candidates for reasons other than current ruler performance: behavioral diversity, lineage novelty, unusual output distributions, age protection, random persistence, perhaps pure neutral drift. Then periodically evaluate combinations of those apparently useless lenses.

Something like:

L_a \rightarrow \Delta F \approx 0

L_b \rightarrow \Delta F \approx 0

but

L_b\circ L_a \rightarrow \Delta F \gg 0

or even:

O(L_a(X),L_b(X)) \rightarrow \Delta F \gg 0

where the organism itself learns how to combine them.

That would demonstrate something profound:

A sensory organ can evolve from components for which no contemporaneous ruler could individually detect value.

That’s biological evolution territory.

And it points directly at your earlier observation: if our ruler can already measure the path to the new sense at every step, perhaps we’re mostly climbing through perceptual structure we already know how to value.

Tyche’s residual ruler also needs a conceptual repair

Tyche already caught this: organism disagreement isn’t dark residual.

I would define the operational residual by capability deficit, not disagreement.

For a given world, give ourselves an oracle or privileged evaluator only for calibration worlds:

D(W)=P_{\text{oracle}}(W)-P_{\text{ecology}}(W)

That tells us there is exploitable information the ecology cannot currently reach, without telling evolution what representation exposes it.

For genuinely unknown Prometheus residuals, of course there is no oracle. Those stay unresolved, not “structure-free.”

So there are two lanes:

Calibration dark residuals: we secretly know exploitable structure exists. Delayed XOR belongs here.

Natural dark residuals: the 122 Prometheus anomalies. We genuinely don’t know whether another lens can extract anything.

We should not mix them until the first lane proves the machinery.

Then make the grammar itself evolvable

At some point the “lens genome” should probably be closer to a tiny bytecode VM than a menu of named transforms.

Then Hecate/Claude doesn’t say:

That starts looking much more like Sagacity.

* unexpected discontinuity,
* unexplained transfer,
* substrate disagreement,
* threshold behavior,
* survivor outlier,

Those cross-engine behavioral clusters can become ecological niches for lens evolution.

Then perhaps a lens evolved on some meaningless synthetic world suddenly lights up on three unrelated Prometheus residuals.

That would be far more interesting than asking an LLM whether those three cases are analogous.

So I would give Tyche this immediate order

Do 1 and 2 on the catalogue, but make the primary scientific effort v1 of the dark ecology.

If it achieves #6, I would consider that the first real evidence for the mechanism behind Sagacity, even though it would still only be a planted control.

Because then we will have shown experimentally:

a cognitive ecology can preserve apparently useless perceptual mutations until their interaction creates a dimension of usable information that none of the precursors exposed individually.

How many things Prometheus currently calls NULL are merely XORs for senses it hasn’t evolved yet?
