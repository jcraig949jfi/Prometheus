PROJECT: DARK RESIDUAL / DARK ECOLOGY

The working thesis is:

What appears to be noise may instead be structure that no currently available observer, representation, or lens knows how to exploit.

Therefore:

Do not define “noise” as absence of structure.

Use the term:

DARK RESIDUAL

to mean:

observations or state variation for which the current ecology of lenses and organisms has not yet found reproducible exploitable structure.

This distinction is foundational.

The objective of this program is to evolve lenses, representations, probes, and eventually reasoning organs capable of extracting new usable structure from dark residuals.

The lenses may become opaque to humans and language models.

That is allowed.

The primary requirement is not intelligibility.

The primary requirement is reproducible consequence.

⸻

CORE SCIENTIFIC QUESTION

Can a population of mutable perceptual transformations evolve new ways of exploiting structure that:

1. is not accessible to the current lens ecology;
2. is not defined in advance using familiar human conceptual categories;
3. produces measurable downstream advantage;
4. survives replication, nulls, perturbation and transfer;
5. may remain difficult or impossible for humans or LLMs to interpret?

A successful result is NOT:

“the model discovered an interesting pattern.”

A successful result is closer to:

“an evolved transformation allowed an organism to reliably predict, control, transfer, navigate, discriminate, compress, or exploit something that the previous ecology could not.”
