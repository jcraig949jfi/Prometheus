WEAK SIGNAL

Do not discard a weak anomaly because it misses an arbitrary headline threshold.

* Is it seed-local or population-wide?
* Does it strengthen under a meaningful intervention?
* Does it reproduce in a neighboring world?
* Does it disappear under a simpler baseline?
* Is there a lineage, transition, state, interaction or failure mode concentrated around it?
* Is the ruler compressing away the interesting distinction?
* Is there a mechanistic variable we were not measuring?
