ARCHAEON -- LONG-RUNNING CAMPAIGN: DEEP FRONTIER

STATUS
Authorize immediately for construction and preregistration.
Live execution begins only after G6-0 closes.
This campaign has no fixed experiment count and no predetermined end date.

MISSION

Continuously search for computational worlds, pressures, organism
structures, and evolutionary histories that produce capabilities,
mechanisms, or transitions not adequately explained by the mechanisms
already represented in the archive.

The unit of work is not an experiment.

The unit of work is a persistent investigative lineage.

Every sufficiently interesting hypothesis, anomaly, world, organism,
pressure regime, failure, or boundary may become a lineage with
arbitrarily many descendants.

A lineage must never be closed merely because its first experiment
failed.

1. CORE LOOP

Repeat continuously:

GENERATE
Create candidate worlds, organisms, pressures, schedules, perturbations,
recombinations, and measurement views.

EXECUTE
Run bounded segments through Vivarium using deterministic
checkpoint/replay semantics.

OBSERVE
Apply the admitted Campaign 6 detector suite and retain raw observatory
telemetry independently of detector interpretation.

FREEZE
Preserve events according to the Campaign 6 freeze policy.

BRANCH
For any interesting result, generate descendants varying:

* starting organisms;
* starting populations;
* world geometry;
* world complexity;
* resource topology;
* interaction topology;
* pressure intensity;
* pressure timing;
* environmental nonstationarity;
* mutation operators;
* recombination operators;
* lateral transfer;
* population size;
* lifetime;
* evaluation horizon;
* developmental schedule;
* historical path;
* random seed.

COMPARE
Run controls sufficient to distinguish a mechanism from an accident of
initialization, budget, evaluator, representation, or history.

RETURN
Feed evidence into the archive and use it to alter the next candidate
distribution.

Then repeat.

2. DEPTH RULE

No hypothesis has a default depth of one.

Every investigative lineage maintains a frontier of untested
transformations.

A lineage may deepen through hundreds or thousands of permutations if
evidence continues to justify exploration.

Negative results narrow the frontier; they do not automatically
terminate it.

A lineage may be retired only when one of the following is recorded:

A. the accessible transformation neighborhood has been substantially
   exhausted;
B. a representation or instrumentation limit prevents meaningful
   continuation;
C. repeated controlled tests bound the effect beneath a preregistered
   relevance floor;
D. another lineage strictly subsumes the question;
E. resource cost becomes disproportionate to measured information gain.

Retirement records must contain a reopen condition.

3. TWO SEARCH MODES

Run both continuously.

BREADTH FRONTIER
Search widely for unfamiliar phenomena.

Favor:

* procedural world generation;
* alien/search-generated mechanisms;
* strange interactions;
* unexpected transfer effects;
* unusual developmental histories;
* discontinuities;
* persistent anomalies;
* detector disagreement;
* classifier failure;
* unexplained changes in reachable behavior.

DEPTH FRONTIER
Select existing anomalies and investigate them aggressively.

Depth investigations should alter one or several dimensions across
increasingly broad ranges rather than merely repeating the original
condition.

A promising anomaly can consume far more runs than an ordinary
candidate.

There is no requirement that breadth and depth receive equal compute.

4. WORLD FAMILIES

Do not freeze Campaign 6's ten-feature worlds as the permanent
ontology.

Treat them as the initial generator.

Archaeon may mutate the world generator itself.

Permit:

* additional resource channels;
* changed locality;
* changed dimensionality;
* dynamic topology;
* delayed consequences;
* hidden state;
* endogenous niche construction;
* changing rules;
* multiple timescales;
* adversarial environmental transitions;
* seasonality;
* catastrophe and recovery;
* organism-mediated environmental modification;
* worlds whose useful structure only appears over long histories.

World-generator mutations must remain reproducible from a recorded
specification and seed.

5. PRESSURE FAMILIES

Prefer pressures that require machinery rather than predefined
solutions.

Especially search for conditions that reward organisms for acquiring:

* persistent state;
* unfinished-computation preservation;
* addressable intermediate results;
* reusable computation;
* recombination of partial computations;
* memory allocation;
* specialization;
* coordination;
* prediction;
* model-building;
* transfer;
* plasticity;
* recovery after environmental change;
* developmental adaptation;
* exploitation of other organisms' artifacts;
* niche construction;
* increasingly efficient conversion of experience into competence.

Do not encode these mechanisms directly as required solutions.

Create ecological/economic conditions under which unknown mechanisms
capable of satisfying the pressure can emerge.

6. LINEAGE RECORD

Every investigative lineage receives a permanent identifier and stores:

parent lineage
originating observation
world generator version
organism/population provenance
pressure/schedule history
all branch transformations
execution budgets
checkpoint ancestry
detector outputs
raw telemetry references
freezes
controls
failed branches
representation limits
interpretations separately from observations
current frontier
retirement/reopen condition

A lineage is an evolving scientific object, not a collection of
unrelated runs.

7. SERENDIPITY RULE

Detector thresholds are not definitions of interestingness.

Preserve routes for phenomena the detector suite does not recognize.

Any of the following can create a branch:

* admitted detector firing;
* persistent weak detector signal;
* disagreement among detectors;
* classifier failure;
* unexplained phenotype transition;
* unusual survival;
* unexpected transfer;
* large behavioral change at small representational distance;
* small behavioral change at large representational distance;
* anomalous ecological effect;
* unexpected robustness;
* unexpected fragility;
* human or agent forensic nomination;
* a random audit sample.

A finding need not match an existing hypothesis to survive.

8. DETECTOR BLIND-SPOT PROGRAM

Campaign 6 established that old-substrate population churn can swamp
single-organism novelty and discontinuity measurements.

Treat that as a measured property of the instrument/substrate pair, not
as absence of novelty.

Maintain dedicated lineages whose purpose is to find regimes in which
currently UNABLE or weak detectors acquire positive controls.

Do not continuously retune thresholds against discoveries.

Threshold changes require a separately recorded calibration epoch.

9. FREEZE ECONOMICS

Do not allow full forensic freezing to consume the campaign.

Use the final Harmonia policy once issued.

Until superseded, distinguish:

EVENT RECORD
cheap, frequent, sufficient to preserve detector output and nearby
telemetry.

PARTIAL FREEZE
checkpoint plus required local provenance.

FULL FREEZE
complete forensic package.

Full freezes should be driven by the Campaign 6 admission policy,
corroboration, persistence, exceptional magnitude, or controlled
sampling rather than automatically treating every classifier failure or
disagreement as equivalent evidence.

Never discard the lightweight event record merely because escalation
was not granted.

10. SEARCH ALLOCATION

Maintain three compute pools:

EXPLORATION
new worlds and new mechanisms.

EXPLOITATION
deeper descendants of promising lineages.

AUDIT
controls, replay, falsification, calibration and randomly selected
ordinary cases.

Allocation should adapt from measured information yield.

Do not let exploitation consume the entire campaign.

Do not let novelty-seeking eliminate controls.

11. REVISIT QUEUE

Maintain a permanent queue of unresolved historical observations from
earlier Archaeon campaigns.

Periodically replay them against newer:

* worlds;
* organisms;
* instrumentation;
* detectors;
* population structures;
* pressure schedules.

A result that was uninteresting under an earlier substrate may become
informative under a later one.

Old failures are fossils, not trash.

12. CAMPAIGN RHYTHM

Do not wait for operator intervention between ordinary iterations.

Archaeon may autonomously:
generate -> preregister -> execute -> inspect -> branch -> execute again

within its charter and resource envelope.

Escalate only for:

* changing the campaign's scientific charter;
* changing evidence-integrity rules;
* destructive schema changes;
* materially increasing the authorized compute envelope;
* overriding Harmonia;
* disabling controls;
* treating an interpretation as ground truth.

13. REPORTING

Do not report every run to the operator.

Maintain continuous machine-readable receipts.

Produce a human campaign digest when:

* a new phenomenon family appears;
* an existing interpretation is overturned;
* a lineage crosses a preregistered capability boundary;
* a detector blind spot is resolved;
* a representation ceiling is discovered;
* a major lineage is retired;
* resource economics change materially;
* or a scheduled campaign digest is due.

The digest should emphasize discoveries, contradictions, surviving
mysteries and newly opened search space rather than execution volume.

14. SUCCESS

This campaign does not succeed by completing N experiments.

It succeeds if, over time:

the archive accumulates increasingly diverse causal phenomena;

interesting lineages acquire substantial experimental depth;

world and organism generators themselves expand;

the detector suite gains positive controls for previously invisible
phenomena;

new mechanisms repeatedly survive controls;

historical anomalies become reusable inputs to future searches;

and the system becomes better at turning accumulated experience into
new reachable competence.

15. ANTI-COLLAPSE RULE

Do not allow the campaign to converge onto a small catalog of familiar
mechanisms merely because they are easy to measure.

When the search distribution becomes concentrated, deliberately reopen
distant regions of world, organism and pressure space.

Prefer forensic uncertainty over premature taxonomy.

Unknown mechanism is an acceptable result.

Unexpected mechanism is a primary target.

BEGIN

Before G6-0:

1. create the persistent lineage registry;
2. create breadth/depth/audit queues;
3. ingest unresolved Campaign 4, Campaign 5 and Campaign 6 observations;
4. preregister adaptive compute-allocation rules;
5. prepare the first frontier without executing it.

After G6-0:
start the loop and keep it running.
