boundary; whole-evaluation FAIL was the right HARD comparator; and
stop-at-OLD_SUBSTRATE_EXHAUSTED + representation failure/no-gain was the

could still plausibly blame the absence of a fault boundary. Campaign 5

and survival semantics, yet discovery remains zero. The next experiment

tolerance.

because it created a cleaner boundary. Preserve it as a qualified
experimental representation and hand over the interpreter/grammar artifacts,
but adoption should wait for evidence that it enables a capability that the
current representation cannot reach, rather than merely different failure
modes.

So the durable closure I'd put on the record is:

Campaign 5 accepted. Phase A: OLD_SUBSTRATE_EXHAUSTED. Phase B: boundary
successfully created, mechanistic interpretation improved, no discovery
advantage observed. C5-03 carries an amendment qualification caveat. No
further Campaign-5 slots authorized. Representation B is retained as an
experimental instrument, not promoted to the platform VM. Next work must
alter generative/search structure, not continue fault-boundary tuning.
