1. BUILD ATTRIBUTION v0

Define a minimal event-level attribution record that can describe reproduction-like events in NPE, BEE and Archaeon without translating everything into one engine’s vocabulary.

At minimum, it should be capable of recording separately:

CARRIER

What entity performed the relevant operation?

Examples:

* code currently executing;
* host organism;
* neighbouring organism;
* harness;
* migration mechanism;
* recombination operator;
* world physics.

MATERIAL

Where did resulting material come from?

Prefer per-locus/per-region ancestry when possible.

Do not force a singular parent where ancestry is mixed.

PRODUCTION

What process physically produced the child/state?

This is distinct from whose material appears in it.

DEPENDENCE

Under what named counterfactual intervention does the event cease to occur?

Dependence must always name the intervention.

Do not write “X caused Y” without recording which alternative was tested.

CAPABILITY

What can the resulting entity demonstrably do now?

Examples:

* exact self-copy;
* conditional copy;
* host-assisted copy;
* reproduce under some inputs;
* transmit a reproducible capability;
* no demonstrated reproductive capability.

CONTRAST

Against what baseline is a difference or attribution being measured?

AGGREGATION

How were local facts converted into an organism-level or lineage-level label?

Make aggregation explicit rather than implicit.

If the four-axis synthesis cannot represent one of these cleanly, change the synthesis.
