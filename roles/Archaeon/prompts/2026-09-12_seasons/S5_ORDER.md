comparatively shallow future partition, while a weaker immediate
probe can expose a substantially better subsequent partition.

The construction must be independent of observed W/M victories.

hidden target -> harness only

PHASE 2 — FIND AN OBSERVABLE MYOPIA COORDINATE

This is the central requirement.

“L is large” is not sufficient.

Seek a quantity computable from information legitimately available BEFORE the hidden target is known that measures something like:

* disagreement between immediate and deeper partition quality;
