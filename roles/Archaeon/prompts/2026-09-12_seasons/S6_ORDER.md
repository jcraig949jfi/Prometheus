ARCHAEON — SEASON 6 ORDER

S5 is closed. Treat its committed receipt and artifacts as evidence, not as conclusions you are required to preserve.

The frontier has narrowed.

S5 established three facts worth carrying forward:

1. The exact full-horizon optimum O beats incumbent G by approximately 2.7–2.8% of probes at both L=8 and held-out L=9, is never worse at the state level, and clears the preregistered bar in a substantial minority of states.
2. W and M did not recover that gap despite costing materially more. GH recovered some of it but regressed in multiple states. RH recovered more while invoking O sparsely, but κ_H was not sufficiently routable.
3. G’s exact myopia is concentrated in the endgame. More importantly, S5 exposed a concrete representational defect in G’s objective: expected remaining count is a collision statistic. Distinct probe partitions can receive the same one-step ER score while having materially different future identification costs. The demonstrated N=6 example, {3,3} versus {1,4,1}, is one witness, not a theory to assume universally.

S6 IS NOT A PRODUCER SEARCH.

Do not add another general-purpose producer zoo.
Do not rescue W or M.
Do not promote GH.
Do not begin by inventing another routing coordinate.
Do not assume entropy is the answer merely because the S5 witness involved entropy.
Do not use O as a production mechanism.

The question is:

WHAT INFORMATION ABOUT FUTURE IDENTIFIABILITY DOES G DISCARD WHEN IT
COMPRESSES A PROBE PARTITION TO ITS ONE-STEP EXPECTED-REMAINING SCORE,
AND IS THERE A CHEAP DETERMINISTIC REFINEMENT THAT RECOVERS A
MEANINGFUL FRACTION OF O'S ADVANTAGE WITHOUT CAUSING MATERIAL
REGRESSIONS?

The desired outcome is not necessarily a new policy.

A clean negative result that eliminates plausible cheap sufficient statistics is useful.
A finding that exact/deeper search is actually required is useful.
A finding that the defect is only a tie-break problem is useful.
A finding that near-ties, not exact ties, carry the residue is useful.

Do not force a repair.

1. FREEZE THE ELIGIBLE ENDGAME

⸻

Use S5 evidence to define the endgame before testing repairs.

Start from the observed concentration:

feasible targets 5–8    : approximately 47–51% of G-myopic decisions
feasible targets 9–16   : approximately 32–36%
feasible targets >16    : approximately 0–3%
feasible targets <=4    : 0%

Do not blindly copy N<=16 merely because this order mentions it.

Using S5 artifacts only, choose and justify a deterministic eligibility rule before S6 comparisons. N<=16 is the obvious candidate and should be preferred unless the evidence gives a principled reason otherwise.

Treat N<=4 explicitly: solved/vacuous, eligible, or excluded — choose before the experiment and say why.

The primary S6 comparison should occur inside the frozen eligible endgame.

Keep the full-state universe available as a safety/regression audit.

⸻

2. CHARACTERIZE THE INFORMATION LOSS BEFORE REPAIRING IT

⸻

For each eligible decision state and candidate probe, represent the complete outcome partition available from fossils alone.

Study collisions under G’s current one-step objective.

At minimum determine:

- how often exact ER ties occur;
- how much of G->O regret occurs at exact ER ties;
- how much occurs at near-ties rather than exact ties;
- whether the O-preferred probe differs systematically in partition shape;
- whether the useful distinction persists after controlling for ER;
- whether one statistic suffices or several apparently independent
  distinctions are present.

Candidate partition-derived quantities may include, but are not limited to:

outcome entropy
singleton mass
number of outcome cells
maximum cell size
second moment / concentration of cell sizes
sorted partition shape
cheap estimates of next-step separability

These are hypotheses to falsify, not features to accumulate.

Do not fit an unrestricted predictor to O labels and call the result understanding.

Prefer exact enumeration, equivalence classes, counterexamples, dominance relations, and small interpretable statistics.

Search for minimal witnesses.

If two partitions are identical under some proposed summary statistic but have different optimal futures, preserve that as a counterexample against the statistic.

⸻

3. BUILD THE SMALLEST REPAIR LADDER YOU CAN JUSTIFY

⸻

Only after the characterization is frozen, preregister a SMALL deterministic ladder of candidate refinements to G.

The preferred form is:

G remains primary.
A refinement acts only when G has discarded information that the
characterization phase identified in advance.

Separate at least:

A. exact-tie refinements;
B. near-tie refinements, if characterization licenses a principled
   definition of "near";
C. a deliberately stronger positive-control refinement if needed to
   determine whether the ladder has enough expressive power.

Do not make a kitchen-sink weighted objective.

Do not tune arbitrary coefficients against the evaluation worlds.

If coefficients or thresholds are unavoidable, derive them without access to the held-out comparison and freeze them.

Every repair must have a deterministic WORK accounting path.

⸻

4. O IS THE SEALED ORACLE, NOT THE PRODUCER

⸻

O may be used to establish the true full-horizon value of decisions and policies.

It must not leak into candidate construction, eligibility, feature selection after freeze, seeds, or production decisions.

Pay for O honestly.

Fix the S5 accounting gap: O’s work must not appear as zero merely because memoized DP was paid by the audit layer.

Distinguish at least:

one-time oracle/audit computation
candidate-policy work
amortized reusable work, if any

Wall-clock time is descriptive only.
Deterministic work units remain normative.

⸻

5. PRIMARY MEASURE: RECOVERED G->O GAP

⸻

Do not make “beats G somewhere” the principal result.

For each state, measure G, candidate C, and O under the same exact expectation.

Define and preregister a sensible measure of recovery of the available G->O gap.

The intended quantity is conceptually:

recovery(C) = (cost(G) - cost(C)) / (cost(G) - cost(O))

where defined.

Handle states with zero G->O gap explicitly rather than hiding them in an aggregate.

Report at least:

fraction of eligible states with G->O gap
fraction of gap states improved
fraction worsened
mean/median recovered gap
aggregate probe reduction versus G
residual distance to O
worst regression
work cost
full-universe regression audit

A cheap repair that recovers only part of O’s advantage may be more interesting than a complicated repair that approximates O.

⸻

6. ROUTING IS SECONDARY

⸻

S5 showed that sparse intervention can matter: RH invoked O on only roughly 7–10% of calls and recovered measurable value.

It also showed that κ_H was not a sufficient router.

Do NOT therefore begin S6 by designing κ_2.

First ask whether routing can disappear.

If a deterministic tie/near-tie refinement is:

cheap,
harmless outside the relevant endgame,
and naturally inactive when its condition is absent,

then the policy itself supplies its own eligibility and no learned coordinate is needed.

Only if a successful repair remains expensive enough to require selective invocation may you open a routing subproblem.

That requires a separate preregistration.

9. VERDICT SPACE

⸻

Write and commit the S6 preregistration before comparative execution.

Do not use a verdict rule so brittle that one mathematically irrelevant tiny-state accident destroys an otherwise valid instrument.

The verdict space should distinguish at least:

INSTRUMENT_FAILURE
NO_OBJECTIVE_RESIDUE
    The apparent G->O opportunity does not survive the S6 eligible
    comparison/audit.
RESIDUE_NOT_COMPRESSIBLE
    G loses to O, but the tested cheap partition statistics do not
    recover a useful, safe fraction of the gap.
CHEAP_REPAIR_WITH_REGRESSION
    A cheap refinement recovers real gap but causes enough regressions
    that it is not production-safe.
CHEAP_ENDGAME_REPAIR
    A preregistered cheap deterministic refinement recovers a meaningful
    fraction of the G->O gap, survives confirmation, stays within the
    regression bound, and meets the work criterion.
DEEP_SEARCH_REQUIRED
    Evidence shows that materially recovering the residue requires
    information not available to the tested cheap summaries and that
    deeper search has earned its cost.

Choose quantitative bars BEFORE the comparative run.

Do not add a verdict after seeing an inconvenient result.
