2. THE CLAIM UNDER CONSTRUCTION AND WHY IT MATTERS
---------------------------------------------------
Methods question (plan v0.4 s1): can one small evidence path admit lawful
retained information, reject a forbidden delayed leak without destroying
allowed state, and refuse a claim whose receipt provenance was rewritten?
Finite world W-S1: 6 episodes, 4096 histories, reset evaluated at 3
boundaries, delay horizon 3, channel delay <= 3. Every receipt carries three
separate fields: execution, instrument authority, outcome. A correct
negative (T02: exact 1/2) is a ruler outcome, never a FAIL.

7. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH
----------------------------------------
Does: a frozen, versioned finite contract; an independent answer key made
before the code; modules whose tests fail on naive stubs and on at least one
targeted mutation each.
Does NOT: any retention, reset or custody claim; any gate qualified beyond
author-tested; any statement about native runtimes, recursion or a class of
organisms. All mutations above were chosen by the authors or integrator --
a same-family check, not independent attack.
