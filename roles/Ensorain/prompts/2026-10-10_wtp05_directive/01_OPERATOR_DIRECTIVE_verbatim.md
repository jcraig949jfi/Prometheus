ENSORAIN — WTP-05

DESERT CROSSING: STATEFUL DEVELOPMENTAL TENSOR WORLDS

This is Ensorain’s Phase 2-B restart campaign.

Record this directive verbatim.

Preserve WTP-04 exactly as recorded.

Do not reinterpret its result.

WTP-04 successfully mapped one-axis habitability around the WTP-03 founders. Its mechanical verdict remains:

ISLANDS_MAPPED

But WTP-04 also establishes the limit of the current line.

Single-axis perturbation mostly shrinks or preserves inherited islands. It rarely changes carrier class, does not create convincing new habitability, and still operates entirely inside tensor-native completion worlds.

Hestia’s audit identifies the more important limitation:

Ensorain has an enormous world search space but an impoverished organism-mechanism space.

Its current organisms are fixed regressors coupled to fixed policies. There is no genuine working state, temporal credit assignment, mechanism construction, compositional action sequence, reusable learned program, or developmental path by which a longer run could produce qualitatively new machinery.

Therefore:

Do not merely run WTP-04 longer.

Time can cross a rarity barrier.

Time does not by itself cross a composition barrier.

WTP-05 will first make composition and hidden state expressible and selectable.

Then it will give the search enough time to attempt the crossing.

⸻

1. CENTRAL QUESTION

Ask:

Can a bounded organism with working state, lifetime plasticity, reusable modules and a search archive cross a reachability desert that the existing Ensorain regressors cannot cross?

And separately:

If it succeeds, which mechanism made the crossing possible?

Do not collapse these into one fitness number.

The desired result is not simply better prediction.

The desired event is:

Experience or evolution constructs a reusable computational mechanism that enables behavior that was previously unreachable under the same world physics.

⸻

2. WHY THIS EXPERIMENT EXISTS

Hestia found a fleet-wide composition wall.

Systems repeatedly learn or evolve individual useful pieces but fail when success requires combining pieces whose intermediate states are weakly rewarded, neutral or individually useless.

Ensorain has not genuinely tested this wall because its organism cannot construct such machinery.

Its current ceiling is essentially:

observation
  → fixed representation
  → scalar prediction
  → fixed action heuristic

Replace that ceiling with a small but genuinely compositional developmental organism.

Do not make it enormous.

Make it capable.

⸻

3. WTP-05 MUST CONTAIN REACHABILITY DESERTS

A reachability desert is a region where:

start mechanism

and

successful mechanism

are connected by legal modifications,

but the useful complete mechanism is separated from the start by intermediate mechanisms that provide little or no immediate fitness advantage.

Create several such worlds intentionally.

Every world must also have a known constructed solution used only for:

expressibility validation
solvability
minimum-mechanism estimation
instrument calibration

The constructed solution must never be supplied to the experimental search.

⸻

4. USE A COMPOSITION LADDER

Adopt a ladder analogous to Hestia’s fleet ladder.

Use something approximately like:

R0 — persistent one-bit / one-value latch
R1 — retain two independent pieces of information
R2 — conditional transformation:
     FLIP / XOR / context-dependent operation
R3 — three-part composition:
     parity-3 / conditional A→B→C / equivalent
R4 — reuse:
     acquire a mechanism once and invoke it in a novel composition
R5 — representation or mechanism promotion:
     compress/reify a learned multi-step procedure into a reusable unit

The exact tensor implementation may differ.

The conceptual hierarchy must remain.

Score the highest independently certified rung.

Do not use ladder rung as direct fitness.

⸻

5. THREE WORLD FAMILIES MINIMUM

Build at least three world families requiring hidden state and composition.

FAMILY A — DELAYED CONTEXT TENSOR MAZE

An organism receives a contextual clue.

The clue disappears.

Much later, two locally identical graph junctions require different actions depending on the earlier context.

The correct decision may additionally depend on a tensor transformation learned elsewhere.

A stateless regressor must fail.

A reactive greedy policy must fail.

A short-history baseline should clear only the easy rungs.

⸻

FAMILY B — TWO-FACTOR / THREE-FACTOR TENSOR LOCK

Information about latent factors is distributed spatially.

Neither factor alone earns the final reward.

A later lock requires a composition such as:

A then transform(B)

or

contract(A, B)

or

context(A) selects operation(B)

or an equivalent relationship.

Create R2 and R3 variants.

Literal lookup should be useless on held-out compositions.

The task should reward possession of a reusable procedure rather than merely reconstruction of one tensor value.

⸻

FAMILY C — LAW-SWITCH WORLD

The cue-to-action or observation-to-payoff law changes during the organism’s lifetime.

A static stored mapping becomes wrong.

The organism must:

detect the regime change
retain useful prior structure
update the relevant rule
avoid destroying unrelated competence

This is the lifetime-plasticity world.

The current Ensorain menu should lose.

A genuinely stateful/plastic mechanism has a reason to exist.

⸻

6. OPTIONAL FAMILY D — NESTED REACHABILITY DESERT

Only if A-C work.

Create a graph in which solving one subproblem exposes another subproblem whose solution requires reuse of the first learned mechanism in a new context.

Do not make D necessary to declare WTP-05 informative.

⸻

7. ADD ONE GENUINELY STATEFUL ORGANISM FAMILY

Do not add twenty architectures.

Add one minimal developmental organism family capable of representing compositions.

Call it provisionally:

TENSOR-TAPE

or another clear name.

It requires three distinct stores.

WORKING STATE

A very small bounded recurrent state:

z_t

which may depend on:

current observation
previous state
previous action
reward / consequence
selected memory output

This state must be physically bounded.

Examples:

8
16
32
64 floats

Do not let it hide unlimited history.

⸻

LONG-TERM MEMORY

Permit selectable substrates such as:

dense
sparse
lowrank
CP
TT
graph/external memory

But long-term memory is not itself the new mechanism.

The important new capability is interaction between working state and reusable computation.

⸻

PROGRAM / MODULE GRAPH

Give the organism a small typed graph of computational operations.

Candidate primitives can include:

read observation
read working-state slot
write working-state slot
compare
threshold
add
multiply
select
gate
tensor contraction
small mode permutation
factor lookup
memory read
memory write
branch
emit action

Keep the primitive set small enough that exact small solutions can be constructed.

But make composition expressible.

⸻

8. MODULE DUPLICATION AND PROMOTION

Introduce an operation that can:

duplicate a useful subgraph
reconnect typed ports
freeze or stabilize a module
call a module from another module

This is essential.

A search restricted to scalar parameter perturbations is not sufficient.

Test whether modular structural operations make higher rungs more reachable.

Do not automatically promote modules.

Promotion must cost something.

Examples:

parameters
memory
compute
developmental time

⸻

9. THREE TIMESCALES

WTP-05 should deliberately separate:

FAST — cognition

State changes during an episode.

MEDIUM — lifetime learning

Weights, factors, routing or module parameters may change during the organism’s life.

SLOW — evolutionary/developmental search

Architecture, module graph, plasticity rule and representation choices may change between organisms or generations.

This is critical.

Do not allow the evolutionary layer to simply memorize every world.

Do not allow the lifetime layer unlimited architecture search.

⸻

10. LIFETIME PLASTICITY

Add a bounded plasticity mechanism.

Keep it minimal.

Candidate forms include:

local gradient update
three-factor update
eligibility-modulated local update
small finite-difference perturbation
substrate-native factor update

The exact rule is less important than giving experience a causal route to alter later behavior.

Track separately:

inherited structure
lifetime-acquired structure
working state
promoted modules

⸻

11. THE NULL LADDER REMAINS MANDATORY

Cheap competitors first, always.

Every world should run against:

N0 constant / zero
reactive memoryless policy
short-history policy
fixed policy with bounded memory
existing Ensorain best regressor
short-horizon planner
N6 tuned batch fit where meaningful
exact / constructed solver reference

Add the new stateful organism only after these.

The world is interesting only if simpler mechanisms leave a meaningful oracle gap.

⸻

12. GOLDILOCKS WORLD ADMISSION

Do not admit worlds that are either trivial or impossible.

Use the best cheap null and oracle.

Prefer worlds where the strongest cheap null captures roughly:

0.2–0.8

of the oracle gap.

A world where the null gets ~100% is trivial.

A world where every feasible organism gets ~0 and the only successful mechanism is an enormous oracle may be inaccessible rather than informative.

Preserve some deliberately harsh desert controls, but distinguish them.

⸻

13. SEARCH ARMS

The central comparison should manipulate mechanisms for crossing the desert.

Run a small factorial around three levers:

ARCHIVE
PROMOTION / MODULE DUPLICATION
LIFETIME PLASTICITY

Use:

OFF / ON

for each where feasible.

This gives up to eight arms.

If measured throughput makes all eight too expensive, use a fractional design but preserve the interactions most important to distinguish:

archive alone
promotion alone
plasticity alone
archive + promotion
promotion + plasticity
archive + promotion + plasticity

Keep the baseline mutation/search arm.

⸻

14. ARCHIVE / GO-EXPLORE STYLE FRONTIER

Do not use a generic “save best fitness” archive.

Archive states by certified stepping-stone descriptors.

Possible descriptors:

highest ladder rung demonstrated
distinct module count
working-state usage
successful delayed-context episodes
number of reusable subskills
behavioral novelty
world region reached
representation structure

Preserve:

organism state
developmental state
relevant world checkpoint where appropriate

Allow branching from rare frontier states.

At final evaluation:

ARCHIVE OFF.

A final organism must perform without the search archive doing its thinking.

Include a random-checkpoint archive control.

⸻

15. STEPPING-STONE VS DESERT CONDITIONS

For each core world, create paired conditions.

DESERT

Only the final composed behavior receives meaningful reward.

STEPPING-STONE

Subskills receive legitimate environmental value before the full composition is required.

YOKED CONTROL

Deliver the same amount/timing of intermediate reward but attach it to unrelated behavior.

This distinguishes:

useful gradient

from

merely more reward.

⸻

16. DO NOT HAND THE SEARCH THE ANSWER

A stepping stone may reward:

correctly remembering context
correctly performing primitive A
correctly performing primitive B
reaching a meaningful intermediate world state

It must not encode:

"assemble exactly this final program."

Likewise, archive descriptors may certify capabilities but must not directly specify the final construction.

⸻

17. REACHABILITY ASSAYS

When a final mechanism is known to exist but search fails, diagnose the failure.

Every failed rung must be assigned one or more:

EXPRESSIBILITY
REACHABILITY
REWARD
CREDIT
DETECTION
OPTIMIZATION
RESOURCE

Do not write “didn’t learn.”

Name the bottleneck.

For small worlds, where possible:

estimate shortest structural edit path
enumerate local neighborhood
plant the complete mechanism
break it by one edit
measure repair probability
seed partial modules
test whether search can recover the whole

These are diagnostic arms, not the main result.

⸻

18. LONGER RUNS — NOW THEY ARE JUSTIFIED

Do not spend long runtime until:

planted solver passes
null ladder behaves
at least one stateful arm clears R0/R1
throughput is measured

Then run longer.

Use finish-in-place campaigns with durable checkpoints.

Suggested structure:

PROBE

Approximately 1–2 hours.

Validate instrumentation, planted solutions, archive, module mutation and plasticity.

SCREEN

Approximately 6–12 hours.

Use ~8 seeds per experimental cell.

Kill cells whose upper confidence bound does not separate from baseline.

DEEP RUN

For surviving cells only.

Run 24–48 hours of wall-clock search where measured throughput supports it.

EXTENDED DESERT RUN

One best-justified arm may continue up to the operator’s 72-hour ceiling.

This is not permission to leave a broken experiment running.

A longer run must produce:

frontier growth
new rung acquisition
expanding reachability
or informative saturation.

If the archive/frontier is flat for a preregistered duration, stop.

⸻

19. RUN-LENGTH TELEMETRY

A key Hestia question is whether more time crosses only rarity or actually changes attainable complexity.

A curve that occasionally crosses R1→R2→R3 is different.

20. EXPANDED REACHABILITY

Add an explicit metric:

RG = Reachability Gain

Ask whether experience/search makes useful states attainable that were previously unattainable under the same subsequent resource budget.

Examples:

final lock becomes solvable
new graph region can be reached
novel held-out composition succeeds
reusable module becomes callable
a previously unaffordable search becomes cheap

Measure RG causally by resetting or ablating the learned artifact.

⸻

21. COMPOSITION, NOT JUST PREDICTION

Prediction accuracy remains diagnostic.

It is not the WTP-05 headline.

The headline requires behavior involving at least two temporally or structurally separated components.

A static batch regressor that predicts the world perfectly but cannot execute the composition does not solve WTP-05.

This directly attacks the WTP-03/WTP-04 ceiling.

⸻

22. TRANSFER AND REUSE

Any R2+ mechanism that appears should be frozen and tested on:

new seeds
reskinned graph
reordered tensor modes
equivalent latent law
novel combination of familiar subproblems

For R4, require the same learned/promoted module to improve at least one novel composition.

If it only reproduces the training behavior, it is not reuse.

⸻

23. COGNITIVE ACCOUNTING

Every apparent success must report where the competence resides.

Attribute contribution to:

environment
organism inherited structure
lifetime learning
working state
search/archive
promoted module/library
certifier/instrument

A search archive finding the answer is not the same as the final organism possessing the answer.

A planted world feature performing the difficult computation is not organism intelligence.

Keep these accounts separate.

28. RESULT CLASSES

At campaign end, issue one primary result:

DESERT_CROSSED

At least one unseeded experimental arm reproducibly reaches R2+ or R3+ where baseline search does not, and causal controls attribute the gain to archive/promotion/plasticity machinery rather than leakage or planted computation.

STEPPING_STONES_REQUIRED

Composition becomes reachable with genuine stepping stones but remains absent in the desert condition, while yoked reward fails.

This is an important result.

RARITY_ONLY

Longer compute improves success frequency at an already-achieved rung but does not increase maximum compositional rung.

This supports Hestia’s “time crosses rarity, not composition” claim.

COMPOSITION_WALL_CONFIRMED

Expressibility and instrumentation pass, but even archive/promotion/plasticity fail to cross the certified desert within measured budgets.

INSTRUMENT_FAILURE

The experiment cannot distinguish the mechanisms because controls or planted gates fail.

Do not convert this into a scientific negative.

29. WHAT WOULD BE EXTRAORDINARY

Any of the following should trigger a fossil, replay and causal autopsy:

A mechanism constructed from pieces whose pieces were not individually sufficient.

A module learned in one task that is reused in a novel composition.

A long run crossing to a higher compositional rung after a prolonged plateau.

A representation changing because of experience and unlocking a previously inaccessible task.

Archive + promotion outperforming either independently with matched compute.

Lifetime plasticity converting a law-switch world from impossible to survivable.

Two independently evolved lineages discovering functionally equivalent modules.

A non-tensor-native world selecting a tensor substrate for reasons not reducible to ordinary tensor completion.

Do not celebrate before replication.

But preserve these immediately.

Do not spend 72 hours watching yesterday’s organism fail more precisely.

Build the smallest organism that can genuinely remember, compose, adapt and reuse.

Verify that the desired mechanism is expressible.

Construct worlds where those capabilities are necessary.

Put deserts between the organism and the solution.
