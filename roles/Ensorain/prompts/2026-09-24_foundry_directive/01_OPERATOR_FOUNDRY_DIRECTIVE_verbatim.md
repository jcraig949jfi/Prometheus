ENSORAIN — TENSOR PHYSICS OF INTELLIGENCE FOUNDRY

Operator directive

Ensorain has earned the right to continue, but its purpose now changes.

E0–E1.5 established something useful:

* tensor representations can confer real representational advantages when environmental structure matches them;
* apparent capacity transitions can be artifacts of optimization/search;
* discovering the representation or topology is more interesting than merely exploiting a representation we supplied.

Do not spend the next phase polishing the previous tensor-completion experiment.

Instead, turn Ensorain into an experimental foundry for the physics of intelligence.

The objective is deliberately broad:

Construct strange computational worlds by wildly varying tensor mathematics, graph topology, memory, learning, search, resource constraints, communication, irreversibility, representation and environmental dynamics. Populate them with bounded organisms. Observe what computational structures appear. Preserve anomalies. Mutate promising regimes. Kill boring ones.

We do not know the correct variables.

Therefore, search the variables.

We do not know the correct representation.

Therefore, mutate representations.

We do not know the correct mathematical substrate.

Therefore, compose mathematical substrates.

Do not force the search toward neural networks, reinforcement learning, familiar agents, transformers, ordinary evolutionary algorithms or any other fashionable modality merely because an LLM recognizes them.

Create necessity. Do not prescribe mechanism.

This campaign is exploratory rather than a single-hypothesis confirmation experiment.

Its product is not one benchmark score.

Its product is a growing empirical map of:

WORLD LAW × REPRESENTATION × MEMORY × SEARCH × CREDIT × RESOURCE PRESSURE × TOPOLOGY × TIME

and the phase regimes that result.

A previously unknown combination of pressures repeatedly causes systems to develop reusable internal structure.
