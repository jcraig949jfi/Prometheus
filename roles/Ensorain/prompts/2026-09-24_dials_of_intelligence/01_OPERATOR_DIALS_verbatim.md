Search for the dials of intelligence; 

What if we find creative ways to adjust the dials the organisms intelligence physics has:

plasticity ↔ stability
exploration ↔ exploitation
compression ↔ fidelity
specialization ↔ transfer
modularity ↔ integration
prediction ↔ surprise
internal generation ↔ external correction

the deepest “physics” is not a collection of components at all, but a collection of opposing pressures whose sustained disequilibrium produces intelligence.


Create worlds in which individual mechanisms are completely different while these abstract relationships are measurable, then ask which quantities consistently increase before our intelligence rulers fire.

    timescale separation — how differently fast and slow processes evolve;
* causal reach — how far consequences can modify upstream structure;
* information permeability — how easily signals cross subsystem boundaries;
* representation fluidity — how readily the system can change the space in which it thinks;
* self-disturbance — how aggressively it perturbs its own internal state to discover alternatives;
* forgetting geometry — not how much is forgotten, but what kinds of structure disappear first;
* credit diffusion — whether success reinforces a precise mechanism or an entire neighborhood of mechanisms;
* counterfactual depth — how many unreal states can influence present behavior;
* boundary softness — how fixed the distinction is between organism, tool, memory, environment, and other agents;
* internal/external learning ratio — how much adaptation is driven by reality versus self-generated experience;
* constraint pressure — how hard scarcity forces compression and prioritization;
* modularity pressure — how strongly useful substructures are encouraged to become reusable semi-independent units;
* rewiring freedom — whether learning changes parameters, connectivity, operators, representations, or the learning rule itself;
* error persistence — how long an unresolved contradiction remains active rather than simply being overwritten;
* search granularity — whether variation happens at the level of parameters, modules, representations, algorithms, or entire developmental programs.

And even that framing may still be too simple, because the important variable may not be the setting of each dial.

It may be the couplings between dials.

For example, high plasticity alone may be disastrous. High plasticity plus replay plus selective consolidation might be powerful. High imagination may produce hallucination. High imagination plus strong grounding may produce planning. Strong compression may destroy information. Strong compression plus representation search may produce abstraction.

So instead of:

Intelligence = f(memory, learning, recurrence, …)

it may look more like:

Intelligence = phase behavior of a coupled adaptive system.

That would make intelligence more analogous to magnetism or fluid dynamics. You don’t explain turbulence by listing the components of water. You identify quantities like viscosity, velocity, scale, and their ratios—and discover regimes where qualitatively new behavior appears.

That suggests something very Promethean for Cosmos: do not predefine the important dials. Search for them.
