ENSORAIN — WTP-02 AUTHORIZATION

WTP-01 is frozen exactly as recorded.

Its verdict remains:

SEARCH SPACE MOSTLY DEGENERATE — REDESIGN

That is not a failure of the Foundry concept. WTP-01 successfully discovered weaknesses in the experimental apparatus:

* variance-normalized competence could explode when worlds collapsed;
* memory could exceed environmental information content;
* generous economies allowed behavior without learning;
* detector controls were incomplete;
* transplant RNG streams were confounded;
* dial sweeps were chosen generically rather than causally;
* short lives sometimes tested optimization speed more than representational capability.

Repair those.

Then run WTP-02.

The objective remains unchanged:

Search experimentally for computational regimes in which experience causes bounded systems to acquire reusable future capability.

Do not optimize TT.

Do not optimize benchmark performance.

Do not make the worlds easier.

Make the measurement harder to fool.

1. WTP-02 PRINCIPLE: NECESSITY BEFORE NOVELTY

Before admitting a world to the expensive search, establish that the world actually requires learning.

A world that can be survived by:

* random walking;
* fixed behavior;
* memorizing the entire state table;
* exploiting energy economics;
* waiting;
* local greedy behavior;

is not an intelligence-physics world.

Reject it before campaign execution.

⸻

2. NECESSITY PREFLIGHT

Every candidate world must pass a cheap preflight.

At minimum:

N1 — Memory pressure

The organism’s persistent memory must be materially smaller than explicit storage of the relevant world information.

Do not measure only number of cells.

Estimate the bytes/floats required by a straightforward explicit representation.

Require something like:

organism_memory <= 0.25 × explicit_world_representation

and include more severe regimes.

Prefer:

0.01×
0.03×
0.10×
0.25×

as distinct pressure bands.

The exact ratios may vary with substrate, but explicit memorization must not be a viable explanation.

⸻

N2 — No-learning control loses

A frozen organism or memoryless/random baseline must fail to maintain positive survival economics or useful competence.

If it can prosper without learning, reject the world.

⸻

N3 — Competence is physically possible

A matched planted learner/oracle must demonstrate that the world contains learnable/useful structure.

If the positive control cannot improve, the world is an instrument failure rather than an interesting negative result.

⸻

N4 — Shuffled structure matters

Destroy the intended latent relationships while approximately preserving cheap local statistics.

The planted learner’s advantage should substantially weaken.

Otherwise the supposedly meaningful structure may not actually matter.

⸻

N5 — Noncollapsed world

Detect degenerate states:

* zero variance;
* almost-zero variance;
* dead topology;
* no usable actions;
* total resource extinction;
* disconnected objectives.

Label these explicitly.

Do not score them as competence.

⸻

3. REPAIR THE COMPETENCE METRIC

Never normalize against a quantity that the organism/environment can drive toward zero.

Use a fixed reference established at world birth or from an independent calibration distribution.

Track multiple observables independently:

prediction improvement
held-out task improvement
future search reduction
future compute reduction
survival improvement
transfer
representation efficiency

Do not collapse everything into one scalar early.

Keep Competence Gain (CG), but define it relative to an immutable baseline.

Also retain absolute performance.

A giant CG from terrible → mediocre must not outrank excellent competence automatically.

⸻

4. STRUCTURE-DEPENDENCE SCORE

For every apparent positive phenomenon calculate a structure-dependence quantity.

Conceptually:

effect(real structured world)
  -
effect(shuffled matched control)

Call it something like:

SD

A competence anomaly with:

CG > threshold
but SD ≈ 0

is not a world-structure discovery.

It may still be interesting for some other reason, but classify it separately.

⸻

5. EVERY DETECTOR GETS ITS OWN NULL

WTP-01 exposed an important flaw: some detector classes could become REPLICATED even when their matched controls behaved identically.

Fix this.

Every anomaly detector must have a corresponding null/control test.

Including:

* positive competence;
* sudden competence jump;
* compression;
* anti-learning;
* metric disagreement;
* survival anomaly;
* representation transition;
* communication anomaly;
* rank collapse/expansion;
* whatever new detectors are added.

Use statuses such as:

STRUCTURE-DEPENDENT
STRUCTURE-INDEPENDENT
MECHANICAL
METRIC ARTEFACT
UNRESOLVED

before promotion.

A replicated failure mode is valuable, but it must not count as evidence for rich intelligence physics merely because it replicates.

10. DO NOT SWEEP MEMORY BY DEFAULT

This was an important WTP-01 lesson.

When an anomaly appears, first identify which variables appear locally causal.

Use:

* nearest genome comparisons;
* family mutations;
* feature attribution only as a heuristic;
* explicit single-dial interventions;
* shuffled controls.

Then sweep the variables implicated by those tests.

If the anomaly disappears when credit delay changes, sweep credit delay.

If freezing topology kills it, sweep topology-change rate.

If irreversibility matters, sweep irreversibility.

Search the relevant local physics.

11. PHASE-BOUNDARY SEARCH

Around each replicated structure-dependent anomaly, search outward until you find both:

phenomenon present

and

phenomenon absent

Then refine between them.

The prize is not the best organism.

The prize is a boundary such as:

no reusable competence
    ↓
weak competence
    ↓
abrupt stable competence

as one or more physical/control variables change.

Allow multidimensional boundaries where needed.

⸻

12. PAY ATTENTION TO RATIOS

Raw dial settings may not generalize.

Track candidate dimensionless quantities such as:

Look for phenomena that align better with ratios than raw constants.

13. KEEP THE STRANGE WTP-01 FAILURE FOSSIL

Do not discard the specimen:

TT + Hebbian update + delayed credit + irreversibility + changing structured world → sudden jump followed by severe anti-learning.

It is not evidence of intelligence.

But it is unusual enough to retain as a failure-physics fossil.

Give it a small separate forensic lane.

Ask:

* Which of the four conditions are actually necessary?
* Is there a sharp instability boundary?
* What variable explodes?
* Does tensor rank change before failure?
* Does credit accumulate with the wrong sign?
* Is irreversibility trapping corrupted representations?
* Is the changing world turning Hebbian persistence into catastrophic interference?

Do not let this lane consume WTP-02.

A small factorial attack is enough.

Failure physics may eventually tell us what successful adaptive systems must avoid.

14. NEW ANOMALY: REPRESENTATION REORGANIZATION

Add a detector for cases where a system’s internal representation changes substantially while competence improves.

Examples:

* TT ranks reorganize;
* mode order changes;
* sparsity pattern changes;
* tensor factors merge/split;
* memory converts representation family;
* graph memory reorganizes;
* reusable module appears.

Require causal follow-up.

Representation change itself is not intelligence.

Representation change that creates future reusable competence may be.

⸻

15. NEW ANOMALY: EXPERIENCE CHANGES REACHABILITY

Track whether experience allows an organism to achieve states/actions/tasks it could not reach previously under the same resource budget.

Examples:

previously unsolved puzzle becomes solvable
previously unreachable region becomes reachable
previously unaffordable contraction becomes cheap
new shortcut appears because of learned representation
new environmental manipulation becomes possible

This may be a stronger observable than raw reward.

Call it something like:

Reachability Gain (RG)

A system that turns expensive experience into cheap repeated competence deserves attention.

WTP-01 taught us not to worship a percentage.

If we find exactly one such world among thousands, freeze it and investigate aggressively.
