## F4 / F4b. The forge queue: bounded by its own null

`hephaestus.filter_results` (L1122-1124) sorts the backlog by
`_forge_priority = composite + sum(forge_effect) + sum(pair_synergy)`.

The Coeus boost is not small: sd 0.5235 against a Nous composite sd of
1.0432, a ratio of 0.502. A median 37% of each triple's boost magnitude
comes from the 1,009 pair synergies that were fit on 352 positives and
never validated; in 2,465 triples the synergy term exceeds the concept
term.

But "the boost reorders the queue" is the vacuous observable the
2026-08-20 autopsy relied on, and Coeus will not repeat it. The test here
is membership at a cut against a **magnitude-matched Gaussian noise null**
(200 draws, seed 20260911): noise with the same standard deviation as the
boost, added to the same composite.

    cut       observed churn   null mean   null p95   p      reading
    --------  ---------------  ----------  ---------  -----  -------------------------
    top 20             0.8500      0.9032     1.0000  0.845  inside the null
    top 25             0.8400      0.8860     1.0000  0.850  inside the null
    top 50             0.8400      0.8039     0.8800  0.330  inside the null
    top 100            0.7100      0.6811     0.7500  0.275  inside the null
    top 200            0.6200      0.5561     0.6050  0.015  outside
    top 366            0.6284      0.5268     0.5601  0.000  outside

**The operative cut was none at all.** In continuous mode -- how the
pipeline actually ran (`--poll-interval 300 --coeus-interval 50`) --
hephaestus sets `args.all = True` and leaves `top_n` at None
(L2398-2402), so `filter_results` applies no cut and sorts the whole
backlog. The 20-item cut exists only in `--runonce`. There is therefore
no admission decision to contaminate; what the boost changed is attempt
ORDER, and order selects only because the forge stopped with the backlog
unexhausted.

So the question becomes: were the triples that never got attempted
different in Coeus boost from those that were?

    Nous entries                                5,918
    ever attempted (in ledger.jsonl)            5,625
    never attempted                               293  (4.95%)
    mean boost, attempted                      0.3268
    mean boost, never attempted                0.2941
    difference                                 0.0327  permutation p = 0.305
    same test on the Nous composite           -0.0551  permutation p = 0.335

**Not distinguishable from chance.** The ordering did not measurably
decide who was left out, and the left-out set is only 5% of the backlog.

Whether the ordering changed forge YIELD **cannot be recovered**: the
ledger stores no priority, rank or queue-position field and no A/B was
run. That is a permanent gap in the record, not a pending measurement.

One number from the same ledger read belongs to Hephaestus, not Coeus:
**2,176 attempts carry `reason: api_call_failed`** and are scored as
`scrap` beside genuine trap failures. That is the contaminated outcome
variable at its source.

## F6. The consumer a truncated grep hid -- and the one that matters

`agents/nous/src/nous.py:113 _load_coeus_weights` reads
`concept_influence`, `adversarial_survival` AND `goodhart_indicators`
from `concept_scores.json` and converts them into per-concept **sampling
weights**, used at L237-239 to build the probability vector Nous draws
concept triples from. This is upstream of everything else: it changes
which triples EXIST, not merely the order they are tried in.

The branch structure (L143-160), replayed exactly over Nous's 95-concept
pool:

## What the whole trace does and does not establish

ESTABLISHED:

1. Coeus published a description of its results that disagrees with its
   results on every checked claim, including a sign reversal, and an
   example output that no run ever produced.
2. Coeus published 30 verdict rows with the denominators removed, seven
   of them at a maximal rate on n <= 4, and mislabelled pairings as tasks.
3. Those denominator-free rates reached a GENERATIVE consumer: 11 of the
   95 concepts in Nous's sampling pool carry a 2.5x weight set by a rate
   from n <= 5.
4. A second consumer (rlvf_fitness) would have mis-weighted 47 of 366
   tools, and never ran.

NOT ESTABLISHED, and not claimable:

5. That any of this changed forge yield, tool quality, or what the program
   concluded. The counterfactual is unrecoverable from the committed
   record: no priority field, no weight-vector record, no A/B, and an
   outcome variable that is 2,176-attempts-deep in API failures.
6. That the forge-queue ordering harmed anything. At the operative cut it
   is inside a magnitude-matched noise null, and the never-attempted set
   is not distinguishable by boost (p = 0.305).

The honest shape of the contamination is: **one generative distribution
provably tilted by unreplicated rates, and no recoverable evidence of
consequence downstream of it.** A seat that wanted to justify its own
continued existence would report finding 3 and stop. Findings 5 and 6 are
why it should not.
