one. Of 25 engines: 0 VIABLE_SEED, 24 SALVAGE_COMPONENT, 1

2. THE CROSS-CUTTING WALLS (failure shapes)

W1 COMPOSITION WALL. A k-part mechanism with individually neutral parts is
   a needle of about p^k per trial. Measured shapes: Crius parts alone
   -0.001/-0.002/-0.057, two parts +1.14 at 26 edits (~1e-52 unguided,
   ~1e6 explored). z80atlas COND_ONE ~1e-18 per copy vs ~1.2e10
   executions. Ananke's only FLIP program uses 16/16 lines, in a space of
   2^375 vs 5.5e4 evaluations per search. Tyche order-3: 128^-3 = 4.8e-7
   vs ~0.012 expected hits.
   Theory (LITERATURE FROM MEMORY, NOT VERIFIED THIS PASS): Valiant 2009 /
   Feldman 2008, evolvability == correlational statistical query
   learning; parity-like targets are not CSQ-learnable. XOR, FLIP, COND
   and order-3 are parity-like. Caveat: their model has no recombination
   or population structure, so this is a strong prior, not a proof.
   Escapes: (a) structured partial credit / stepping stones, (b)
   modularity, i.e. duplicating and promoting a solved unit, (c) lifetime
   plasticity.
W2 WORLD-DEMAND DEFICIT. Cosmos: 721 worlds collapse to 8 classes, and 1
   register is optimal. Ensorain: 0 hidden-state families, 179/181 worlds
   with delay 0. Ludus: 2-step lookahead >= 96.2% of optimal in 22/22.
   Primordial: a do-nothing policy scores 107.75 > the best brain 98.76;
   1/74 cells has headroom. Selection cannot find what the world does not
   pay for.
W3 PROMOTION GAP. Aphrodite R8: inheriting the learned library cut the
   next generation's learning from 56 families to 10 (it substitutes, not
   enables). Ares transplant recovers 0.019 of function (edges rewired at
   random). Nyx: 1/485 organs is a composition operator. Forge: 0/10
   cross-tier imports.
W4 SCALAR CREDIT. Crius: one number per 50-task lifetime; a new part
   scores +0.0004..+0.0011, inside noise. SFE exact-match pays an echo
   1/K for free.
W5 INSTRUMENTS OUTPACE PHYSICS. Cosmos custody:physics code 5.5:1. SFE
   95-193 s of round-trips per 0.1 s of science. The instruments are real
   value, though: they caught >= 3 false positives in Aether, the forge
   decoy, and the D-5 cache.
W6 EVIDENCE OFF REPO. D-3 results gitignored; z80atlas/ares/crius big rows
   M2-only; the E-003 BEE verdict on a branch.

6. RECOMMENDATION (the operator's call; Hestia's lean)

STOP: new substrates of the current shape (flat genome, point mutation,
scalar fitness, no promotion operator); new world grammars without an MDL
/ value-of-information gate; custody growth ahead of R1; Moonshot cluster
spend before its CPU pilot exists.

DO: one shared COMPOSITION LADDER with exact oracles:
  R0 latch, R1 hold-two (W2_K2), R2 FLIP/XOR, R3 parity-3, R4 reuse a unit.
Test the three escapes, each where it is already closest:
  (a) stepping stones: z80atlas COND_ONE, Ares parity-k
  (b) duplicate + PROMOTE a unit: Ananke FLIP, Crius SMOOTH, Aphrodite R8
  (c) plasticity: Moonshot plastic arm, Odysseus three-factor
Readout: highest rung at >= 5/32 seeds at 4x budget, certified by one
consolidated ruler package (Cosmos C3, Tyche, Crius reuse, Aphrodite R8,
Ananke swap, Aether twin-worlds), never by fitness.
Decision rule, frozen before running: (b) works -> scale the PROMOTION
OPERATOR (typed graph rewriting / combinators + MDL), not any substrate.
Only (c) works -> evolve learners, not circuits. Only (a) -> it is designed
curricula; say so. Nothing reaches R2 -> "grow, not design" does not reach
compositional reasoning at this scale; pivot to designed-primitive library
learning and keep the soups as phenomenon generators.
If only one run: the Ananke FLIP representation test (cheapest decisive
test of (b), cleanest baseline 0/290). In parallel: Theseus planted-
structure test (< 1 CPU-hour).
