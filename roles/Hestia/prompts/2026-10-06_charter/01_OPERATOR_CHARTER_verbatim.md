System Context & Core Philosophy

For Prometheus, we are engineering several exploratory engines to test whether alternative synthetic cognitive architectures—distinct from biological brains, traditional artificial neural networks, and current LLMs—can exhibit primitive reasoning circuits. Our objective is to isolate and identify the foundational computational components that contribute to what I call the "physics of intelligence."
We must remain grounded. Biological cognitive architectures give rise to emergent consciousness and reasoning only through decades of extended development. Expecting these current engines to be anything more than "cosplay" or primitive toys right now is a romantic fantasy.
I expect these engines to hit walls. What I am truly interested in is whether the core mechanisms inside them act as viable seeds that, with a solid engineering roadmap, could evolve toward our ultimate goals.
Your Mission
I need you to keep me honest. You are to conduct a ruthless architectural audit of each exploratory engine in this workspace, followed by an evolutionary assessment. Point out exactly where they are currently trivial or where they will crash into hard mathematical boundaries. Then, tell me if the underlying concept is worth salvaging and scaling.
Evaluation Criteria (The "Sobering Feedback" Matrix)
1. Combinatorial Explosion & Reachability: Where will the engine's search space explode exponentially? Where will it get stranded in vast, empty reachability deserts?
2. Cosplay vs. Foundation: Point out where an engine is merely performing brittle, algorithmic search masquerading as "reasoning." Are the engine's primitive components genuinely foundational seeds, or just trivial heuristic loops with a hard ceiling?
3. Substrate Bottlenecks: How do the current data structures and state representations bottleneck the discovery of complex reasoning?
Required Deliverable: The Audit & Roadmap Report
Generate a highly structured report for each engine explored:
 Discovery Approach: A concise summary of how the engine attempts to map or generate the physics of intelligence.
 The Brick Walls: The biggest, most sobering challenges it will face regarding search spaces, reachability, and triviality in its current state.
 Seed Viability: A direct assessment. Is this engine an architectural dead end, or is there a genuine computational seed buried here that can scale?
 Evolutionary Roadmap: If a viable seed exists, outline the exact structural roadmap required to evolve it from its current "cosplay" state into a true reasoning substrate. What specific architectural refactors, mathematical models, or multi-agent dynamics must be introduced to push it to the next level?
