1. **EXTREMELY HIGH — A3: Does reification/operandization actually earn its keep?**
   This is the clean continuation of Aporia's live LoT arc. TINYPROG is now an admissible world, and Aporia explicitly ended A2 saying A3 is the **first experiment that actually tests the hypothesis**. The key is not whether a repeated composition can be given a name; that is definitionally trivial. Test whether promoting a recurring motif makes *future unseen tasks cheaper to find*. Charge execution at the fully expanded primitive cost and search decisions separately, so a macro cannot win merely because one macro call is falsely priced like one primitive call. Promotion happens on earlier episodes; benefit is measured only later. Include random/frequency-matched macros, NO_REUSE, DECOY and LATE controls. **Kill it** if the advantage disappears under expanded execution cost, is matched by arbitrary macros, or does not appear prospectively. This is probably the best immediate use of her first tokens. 

2. **EXTREMELY HIGH — Turn Q45 into an exact “search failure vs representation failure” oracle.**
   This may be the most important new instrument discovered in the whole session. Aporia initially labeled 2,136 targets unreachable when a primitive was removed at depth 5. Going deeper recovered 10.4% of them—meaning those were **search insufficiency masquerading as representation insufficiency**. But the marginal recovery rate then collapsed roughly 291× while the search exploded, suggesting a genuine residual floor. 
   The next job is to stop using finite depth as ground truth. Because TINYPROG has finite extensional signatures, investigate whether she can compute closure to a fixed point, prove subalgebra membership/non-membership, or otherwise obtain a true depth-independent certificate. The desired output is a mechanically grounded discriminator:
   `SEARCH_FAILURE / REPRESENTATION_FAILURE / UNRESOLVED`.
   **Kill the instrument** if labels remain materially depth-dependent or the supposed representation-failure set keeps dissolving under a stronger exact procedure.

3. **EXTREMELY HIGH — Define and then manufacture a genuine Level-2 representational extension.**

4. **EXTREMELY HIGH — Build a generic “scientific gate compiler/linter.”**
   Aporia was bitten twice in one pass by verdict rules that mathematically could not behave as intended. One statistic had its own hard ceiling; another used a same-data null whose bar inflated toward the bounded statistic's maximum.    Generalize that into an instrument every Prometheus assay must pass before scientific use: attainable-range analysis, known-positive, known-negative, monotone intervention sweep, metamorphic invariance, branch-table reachability, boundary saturation, and “perfect signal must cross the gate.” In other words, automate **“can this test ever say what we claim it can say?”** This could prevent an enormous amount of future false science across every seat.
