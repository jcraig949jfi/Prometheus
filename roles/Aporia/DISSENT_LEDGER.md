D-A01  2026-09-11  Preregistration and the north star pull apart exactly
                   where the north star cares most
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FRAMING DISPUTED   Base role s2: preregister before touching data, never
                   add a feature mid-measurement, never redefine a
                   population. North star: allow mechanisms to be
                   discovered "including mechanisms no human explicitly
                   conceived".
DISSENT            A preregistered gate is a gate over an observable the
                   experimenter could name in advance. A mechanism no human
                   conceived has, by construction, no such observable. Run
                   as written, the instrument doctrine can only ever
                   confirm or kill things we already knew how to describe,
                   which is the opposite of what the north star says the
                   programme is for. This is not a reason to drop
                   preregistration; it is a reason to preregister the
                   DETECTOR OF SURPRISE rather than the surprise. The
                   Q045 certificate is the existence proof: non-membership
                   in a frozen closure is a pre-registrable observable that
                   does not presuppose what the new thing is. APO-06 and
                   APO-11 (fixed-point closure; a Level-2 target certified
                   outside the old closure) are the constructive form of
                   this dissent. Until something like them exists, every
                   H0-H5 gate is a gate on a human-named observable, and
                   the programme should say so instead of calling that
                   "open-ended".
WHAT WOULD SHOW THE DISSENT IS WRONG
                   An H0-H5 preregistered gate fires on a mechanism that
                   was not describable at preregistration time, with the
                   rows to show the observable was fixed in advance. One
                   such case ends this row as WRONG.

                   ever worked had written for it by hand
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FRAMING DISPUTED   North star: "whether sufficiently rich computational
                   primordial soup can pressure-cook its own functional
                   equivalents". H0-H5 as the programme's hypothesis set.
DISSENT            The campaign literature, none of it written with this
                   programme in mind, says: Avida and Tierra both begin
                   with a hand-written replicator (dossier 74); novelty
                   search does not diverge in an unbounded space (dossier
                   03, critique unanswered); classical MAP-Elites dies past
                   three or four behavioural dimensions (dossier 02);
                   Loreley found no QD advantage at matched budget (Chen

                   substrate proposal ("the proposal never says what gets

                   question is not whether the soup is rich enough; it is

                   programme is willing to write that by hand (as every
                   working precedent did) and say so, rather than expect
                   it to condense. Until a copying hypothesis exists, the
                   soup framing is a wish with a chemistry vocabulary.
