# Convergent blind spots

Aporia inference harvest, 2026-09-30. No fleet authority.

This file is not an engine-local TODO list. It names properties shared by NPE (Nestor),
PTE (Ananke), WTP/ARC3 (Ensorain), BEE (Bellerophon), Cosmos, Archaeon/Proteus, AGE
(Aether), Hecate, Tyche and Theseus. Together those properties make certain phenomena
invisible or impossible before any search begins.

---

## B1. An external judge or no judge, never an internal one

Every engine with an objective scores it outside the world:
- NPE and Z80: in a scratch arena with world operations disabled;
- PTE: an external GA over five fixed task families;
- Ensorain: held-out MSE;
- Archaeon: 4-bit recall;
- Graph-world: a progress ratio;
- Hecate: preregistered predicates.

The one engine with no objective, Aether, says of itself: "Nothing in Aether's
observatory can say a structure does something useful."

**Excluded before search:** function defined by consequences inside the world, such as
a structure that changes what the world does next beyond its own footprint.

**Classified as failure:** anything that persists or spreads while scoring zero. That is
most of what these soups actually produce.

---

## B2. A fixed variation operator nobody studies

Across engines the search operator is point mutation, sometimes a splice, on
fixed-length strings or parameter vectors. It is held constant and treated as plumbing.
The PTE engine card lists "questions whose answer depends on the GA" as a bad fit.

The evidence says the operator is decisive:
- Ananke H6: reachability bounds PTE.
- CW01: a 4-edit valley with 0 one-edit hits in 12,880.
- e09: no compose/reuse.
- BEE LADDER2: 0/960.
- PTE: XOR 0/83.
- Archaeon: trap-NOP length grows by acceptance.
- The splice manufactured 88% of an earlier campaign's replicators.

**Excluded before search:** duplication and divergence, reuse of executed segments,
recombination of behaviour rather than bytes, and variable arity. Any capability more
than a few point edits from the plateau is out of reach.

---

## B3. The accumulation null is missing

Almost no engine compares "history" against "fresh draws with the same operator and
budget":
- NPE ran a neutral-walk pilot (1.75, p = .20) but never the full WP-9.
- Theseus ran a random arm and lost to it.
- D8 was parked as a major campaign.

Every lineage contrast is internal: what changed inside the lineage. None asks whether
the lineage beat a fresh draw.

**What the machinery cannot notice:** that nothing accumulates. It is structurally unable
to report "the soup is a lottery", because no standing baseline would say so.

---

## B4. The end-of-run snapshot

Readouts are taken at the end: dominance at tick 2000, runaway depth at the end,
survivors, a censored option-value clock.

**Invisible:**
- Re-origination rates.
- Brief, repeated appearances. The REPL-01 K1 events had median tick 1400, and 7/57 were
  still present at 2000.
- Cross-trial carryover, which PTE files as a failure mode.
- Any phenomenon whose signature is recurrence rather than persistence.

---

## B5. The individual in a slot

Every organism engine defines the individual by the world's slot or oid and credits
"own code". The following are classified as artefact or excluded:
- pair-level reproduction (all 1,031 Z80 flags were PAIR_EXECUTION);
- environment-assisted births (2.32M);
- context-dependent copiers;
- self-location supplied by resets;
- replacement lineages.

**What the machinery cannot notice:** reproduction or function that belongs to an
interaction, an assembly, or an organism-environment coupling.

---

## B6. Localized mechanism as the default causal unit

Knockout, swap, cut and carrier vocabularies assume a part is responsible. The same
evidence recurs:
- TH-015: 66% of the tape needed;
- carriers are trajectories;
- a readout spread over 5 sensors;
- the locality term measured at +.024 against a dose term of +.145.

Distributed, dose-like causation reads as "ROBUST_WITHOUT_MECHANISM", "NOT A CARRIER" or
"UNRESOLVED".

---

## B7. Human priors in task, op set and possibility reference

- Task families are XOR, ADD, RELAY, MAJ, recall and tensor completion.
- Theseus: "the op set is a human prior; nothing can leave the space it spans."
- Hand plants define where an absence counts (PTE A3), though evolution beat the hand
  design (0/253).
- Hecate's positive control must contain "the effect present by construction", which
  favours what the generator already knows how to build.
- Ensorain admission requires learnability: 0 of 2,000 random candidates were
  inhabitable.

**Excluded before search:** worlds and effects no human can plant.

---

## B8. Novelty judged by description or by distance from a library

- Hecate's classifier cannot return UNFAMILIAR once universal formalisms are allowed as
  priors.
- Theseus's NOT_REPRODUCED_YET measures distance from the library.
- Neither looks at consequences.

---

## B9. One regime, one cell, one world per round

- Aether ran a single energy regime.
- Hecate reads one cheapest world per program per round, and 76% of mechanisms were never
  tested.
- C-ATOMIC holds in one cell, and its generality arm was null.
- Archaeon found the same Z80 world "built three times".

Phenomena that exist only as a regime dependence, a phase diagram, are sampled at a
single point and read as present or absent. The CW01 e06 TAPE/TREE asymmetry flips
across mixing parameters. It was parked as "no referent".

---

## B10. One model family designs, runs, attacks and scores

- Hecate: "same model family throughout".
- Theseus's LLM arm was generated by the model that built its op set, compiler, library
  and rulers.
- Every calibration ledger shows the seats mispredicting their own results. REPL-01 lost
  3/3, REPL-02 5/5, and Tyche RH1-RH4 were all false.

A designer whose predictions are this poorly calibrated is also setting the rulers,
which is consistent with the recurring unreachable-ruler findings. Correlated blind spots
between generator, attacker and judge cannot be seen from inside.

---

## B11. Observers that cannot be readers

Measurement happens from outside the world:
- ASAL's observer scores turbulence and coherent life the same;
- the XOR content signature cannot see transport in a rich soup;
- Hecate's AUC measures confidence.

There is no instrument that is itself a process inside the world, whose reading of a
structure would be the world's own reading.

---

## What the current machinery literally cannot notice

1. That nothing accumulates (B3, with no standing null).
2. Function without an external task (B1).
3. Reproduction or function owned by an interaction rather than an individual (B5, O5).
4. Capability more than a few point-edits from the plateau (B2).
5. Phenomena whose signature is recurrence, not persistence (B4).
6. Regime-dependent phenomena sampled at one regime (B9).
7. Novelty that is only visible in consequences (B8).
8. Whether a trivial or definitional baseline already explains a claim, when the rung is
   added after the search. C3's zero-parameter rung came five days late; WTP-03's N6 and
   the graph-world sham came after the claim.

Items 1 and 8 are the most consequential. Item 8 is partly fixed already: C4 now
preregisters the definition rung.

---

## Which baselines embody assumptions every seat shares

- **Random-bytes implants and constant/zero/running-mean floors** test the organism
  against dumb organisms. They never test the search process against a dumb search
  process: neutral drift at the same operator and budget.
- **Hand plants** set the possibility reference at human skill.
- **"Scratch" comparisons** without a sham credit the treatment's content for what
  disturbance alone does. The graph-world record: "against scratch alone this pair would
  have read PASS."
- **Lineage-internal contrasts** presuppose that the lineage is the right unit.
