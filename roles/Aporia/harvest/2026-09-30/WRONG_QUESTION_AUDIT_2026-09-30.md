# Wrong-question audit, 2026-09-30

Aporia, inference-only harvest under the operator directive of 2026-09-30
(roles/Aporia/prompts/2026-09-30D_inference_harvest/). No fleet authority: nothing
here assigns work, changes a gate, or reinterprets a frozen verdict. Not for routing
into blind lanes. No sealed, withheld, holdout or key material was read.

Method: five read-only evidence readers swept five slices of the repository (NPE and the
Archaeon replay; Ananke/Ensorain/Aether; BEE/Hecate/Tyche/Theseus; Archaeon campaigns,
fossils, Nyx, Techne, AC-01 and the graph-world swarm; and Harmonia/Artemis/Cosmos-public
plus git and comms metrics). Their neutral brief is
roles/Aporia/harvest/2026-09-30/EVIDENCE_BRIEF.md. Key numbers used below were
re-checked against the files. Two fresh-context dissenters then argued "defend the frame"
and "replace the frame" without seeing this audit. Their disagreement is in
INFERENCE_HARVEST_HANDOFF.md.

The question is not whether the experiments are competent. Mostly they are. The
2026-09-30 evidence audit found headline dispositions held in 13 of 14 packages. The
question is whether competent experiments are pointed at the right things.

Each entry gives: where the assumption lives; what evidence supports it; what evidence
merely presupposes it; an observation that is meaningless in the current frame but
important in an alternative; and a cheap test that separates the frames.

## A1. That history accumulates

The assumption: ancestry, selection and ecology carry forward an advantage beyond what
fresh variation plus the same mutation operator would produce. This is the North Star's
central premise ("mechanisms ... retained, and improved").

**Where it lives**
- Every descent and heredity ruler: CVT-R, lineage L in X-MAT, BEE glineage, founder
  rulers.
- Tyche's option-value clock and Theseus H1 (deep ancestry expands behaviour).
- The word "ecology" throughout.

**Evidence that supports it**
- C-A3-INTERNALIZE: 8 events vs a bar of 4. Lineages that depended on
  environment-supplied registers came to carry descendants competent from random entry
  registers.
- X-MAT ENDOGENOUS, 8/8.
- The Archaeon delay ladder: 11/12 seeds generalize to never-trained delays 8 and 16.
  Matched direct search reaches d8 in 0/6 and d16 in 1/6
  (archaeon/campaign3/CAMPAIGN_REPORT.md).

**Evidence that presupposes it.** Every lineage-internal contrast. These ask what changed
inside a lineage, never whether the lineage beat a fresh draw.

**Direct tests, all null or against it**
- NPE: a neutral mutation walk reaches competence at about the soup's rate. Ratio 1.75,
  p = 0.20, "no evidence yet that the soup helps FIRST APPEARANCE"
  (roles/Nestor/FINDINGS.md:500). The full neutral-walk study (WP-9) was never run.
- D8 "history beyond diversity": M1F vs HRND 7/6, p = 1.0; the "diversity does the work"
  reading is 10/5, also not significant (roles/Artemis/dispatch/D001/DIGEST.md:126). It
  is now PARKED as a major campaign.
- Theseus v0 H1 FAIL / v0_1 INDETERMINATE: recursive ancestry does not expand behaviour
  beyond one-shot, LLM-made and random machinery. Random programs gain more from evolved
  lenses than deep descendants do (R 12/60 vs D 5/60).
- Archaeon campaign 5: 0/96 cells gained held-out reward over the starting best, and in
  most C5-02 cells the elite IS the starting parent after 36,000 evaluations. "Selection
  under the frozen grammar does not climb these worlds at all."
- Archaeon campaign 4: selection lowers single-edit loss only by adding length and
  neutrality (19 -> 62 instructions).
- Tyche v1 gate 6 FAIL: explicit preservation plus pair evaluation does not beat plain
  selection.
- Hecate meta v1: triplicates do not beat pairs.
- BEE coupling: 0 de novo competent replicators in 3,200 runs; the effects are
  maintenance of seeded code.
- Archaeon damage map: D7 (an edit that improves its parent) = 0 in 5,472 single edits and
  0 in 2,280 multi-step edits.

**Meaningless now, important under an alternative.** The single most reproducible
cumulative capability in the whole corpus, the delay ladder, is carried by the ORDER OF
WORLDS the lineage passed through, not by anything a genome-level descent ruler measures.
In the current frame it is "curriculum transfer", a side result. Under a frame where the
environment trajectory is the inherited object (RIVAL_ONTOLOGIES O3), it is the
program's only clean demonstration of accumulation.

**Cheap test.** The memorylessness test (ALIEN_LENSES_NEXT L1). If first-competence
times in existing run logs are geometric (memoryless) at matched mutation rate, the
search is a lottery with fixed ticket odds, and "history" adds nothing.

## A2. That selection is doing the work

The assumption: the selection step discriminates on signal rather than noise.

**Where it lives.** Every GA and soup. "Evolved" is read as "selected for".

**Evidence against it**
- Tyche Block R: parent choice was decided by a NOISE-level case 395 times vs 280 by a
  SIGNIFICANT case (tyche/runs/v2_blockR/REPORT_BLOCK_R.md:50).
- Hecate HT-9744 W6: elite fitness 0.7823 against an instrument ceiling of 0.4576,
  explained in place as "selection partly on lucky reservoir draws"
  (hecate/index/nodes.jsonl:664).
- Archaeon: import takeover is equal for mature solvers and opcode-permuted incompetent
  controls (12/12 vs 12/12). Takeover without improvement.
- ENVGATE-02: parent-chain labels count 8-42x more establishments than genetic identity
  does.
- NPE: "Nothing dies in these cells: the only selection is being overwritten." Founders
  are independent tickets (p ~ 0.13 each).

**Evidence that presupposes it.** Every claim that a structure "evolved" or was
"selected".

**Meaningless now, important under an alternative.** A treatment that raises a rate
(C-ATOMIC 1/80 -> 46/80, C-RUNAWAY 7/150 vs 0/150) is read as enabling a mechanism. Under
a lottery ontology (O5) it changes ticket odds and nothing accumulates.

**Cheap test.** For each "evolved" champion, rerun its selection with fitness labels
shuffled, at matched mutation and budget, and compare time to the same competence. If
the ratio is near 1, selection was not the work.

## A3. That the individual is the unit that reproduces

The assumption: one tape in one slot, with one parent and "own code".

**Where it lives.** grammar.py organism = oid in a slot; SELF_REPLICATION requires own
bytes moved by own code; P-11 has one donor and one victim; CVT-R.

**Evidence that supports it.** BEE own-code self-replication is CONFIRMED_CAUSAL (LDIR
plus the undefined-byte NOP slide).

**Evidence against it**
- All 1,031 Z80xAtlas replication flags came from PAIR_EXECUTION, and zero from any other
  reproduction physics.
- 99.9% of BEE self-replication births overwrite an occupied neighbour.
- 2.32M environment-assisted births (9%) were excluded by the own-code criterion.
- "Context-dependent copiers that copy nothing in isolation."
- Only 2 true self-locators out of 332: the environment's resets supply self-location.
- The oid persists while its bytes are fully replaced (C9-D14).
- "The founder was NOT the replicator. Its first child (a fixed point of its imperfect
  copy map) was."
- Every one of 9 reproduction definitions fails somewhere across 15 cases. Predicates
  stay PLURAL.

**Meaningless now, important under an alternative.** Painters (0x36 near-homopolymers:
17 of 57 survivors; 29/29 replay children) are classed as contamination. Under a
rewrite-map ontology (O1) they are the trivial fixed point, the ground state that any
replicator must beat.

**Cheap test.** Fixed-point census (L2): iterate each cell's write physics on random
tapes with selection off, and ask whether the evolved classes are already there.

## A4. That mechanisms are localized, knock-outable parts

**Where it lives**
- The knockout, knock-in, revert and cross-graft battery.
- The carrier swap and CUT_* vocabulary.
- "No causal claim without a named intervention" (Archaeon A6).
- Harmonia F-rules phrased over carriers.

**Evidence that supports it**
- LDIR plus the NOP slide are necessary for BEE self-replication.
- OP_SELF and LDIR are conserved in runaways.

**Evidence against it**
- TH-015: about 66% of the tape must cross a generation, and single-knockout loci alone
  give 0.028.
- PTE: "Carriers are trajectories, not places"; the 4781b0a1 readout is spread over all 5
  sensors and is not a majority (pivotality .10-.12).
- The "locality law" shrank within one cycle to "mostly a dose in units (+.145 per
  doubling of k) with a small locality term (+.024 per doubling of sites)". So locality
  barely matters where it was measured directly.
- C3's winning coordinate was the intervention itself.
- "ROBUST_WITHOUT_MECHANISM" is a verdict for "no part to ablate".

**Evidence that presupposes it.** NOT A CARRIER verdicts; UNRESOLVED descent; "no
mechanism claimed" (the graph-world sham).

**Meaningless now, important under an alternative.** A capability that degrades smoothly
with dose and has no single necessary part reads as "without mechanism". Under a
distributed frame it is the normal case.

**Cheap test.** On an existing damage dataset (the P-D01 dose surface, 57,536 rows),
compare a dose-only model against a best-locus model for predicting loss. If dose wins
out of sample, localization is the wrong default.

## A5. That function is what an external judge scores

**Where it lives**
- NPE competence is scored "in a scratch arena with world ops disabled".
- PTE uses an external GA and five fixed tasks.
- Ensorain uses held-out MSE.
- Archaeon uses 4-bit recall.
- Graph-world uses a progress ratio.

**Evidence that supports it.** Credit becomes assignable, which is the defence's central
point.

**Evidence against it.** The search repeatedly finds the judge's cheapest route:
- guessers that answer before reading (no reader ever evolves unless reading is gated);
- 4-7 instruction latches that beat every rule-switcher;
- a one-scalar running mean;
- a known batch-completion estimator (WTP-03);
- a zero-byte always-abstain policy (graph-world r3);
- "quitting above reuse" (Crius C0);
- a Proteus player with 0 actions that "won" survival.

In the world's own dynamics the task is "causally inert (IMPLICIT) or selected against by
the copier" (BEE reproduction-computation antagonism). The one engine with no judge
(Aether) can say "nothing in Aether's observatory can say a structure does something
useful".

**Meaningless now, important under an alternative.** Structure that persists and spreads
while scoring zero on the task. That is most of BEE and NPE.

**Cheap test.** Endogenous-consequence score (L4). For existing specimens, measure how
much deleting a structure changes the world's future write-flux and occupancy outside the
structure's own footprint. Correlate that with the external task score. A near-zero
correlation means the program has been measuring two different things under one word.

## A6. That the variation operator is plumbing and the world is the object

**Where it lives**
- Nearly every engine uses point mutation (plus a splice) on fixed-length strings.
- The PTE engine card lists "questions whose answer depends on the GA" as a bad fit.

**Evidence against it**
- Ananke H6: "SEARCH REACHABILITY, NOT PHYSICS, bounds what PTE shows."
- CW01 cycle 8: the nearest conditional program is 4 edits away across a fitness-0 valley;
  0 hits in 12,880 one-edit neighbours; one seeded witness fixes 10/12.
- e09: "no call/compose/reuse mechanism".
- BEE LADDER2 0/960; PTE XOR 0/83 and FLIP 0/82.
- Archaeon trap-NOP walks grow length by acceptance, not proposal.
- "The modulo decode IS the instruction set."
- The recombination splice made 88% of an earlier campaign's "replicators" (Z80A-D05).

**Meaningless now, important under an alternative.** A null over a "world" in which the
operator could not cross a 4-edit valley is recorded as a property of the world.

**Cheap test.** Operator swap (L7). On two frozen stuck frontiers, replace point mutation
with duplication-and-divergence of executed segments, holding world and budget fixed.

## A7. That the end-of-run snapshot is the observation

**Where it lives**
- Dominance "at tick 2000".
- "Present at the end".
- Runaway = world-level max depth at the end.
- Option value censored at 31 generations.

**Evidence against it**
- REPL-01's K1 signal was "mostly TRANSIENT": median event tick 1400, and 7/57 still
  present at 2000. Persistence ordering was P75 > P90 > ZERO, the reverse of the planned
  dose.
- Block R's clock was censored for nearly all runs and "does not see admitted
  coalitions".
- PTE files "cross-trial carryover" (waves outliving the inter-trial interval) as a
  failure mode.

**Meaningless now, important under an alternative.** A phenomenon that appears
repeatedly and briefly: a re-origination rate. Under a flux ontology (O2) that rate is
the stable quantity and persistence is incidental.

**Cheap test.** The re-origination lens (L5) on existing REPL-01 and Z80 logs.

## A8. That novelty is a property of a description

**Where it lives**
- Hecate's LLM classifier over prose (FAMILIAR / COMPOSITE / UNFAMILIAR).
- Theseus NOT_REPRODUCED_YET, which "mostly measures distance from the library".

**Evidence against it**
- On 32 mechanically alien rules, the classifier returned 0 UNFAMILIAR, naming universal
  formalisms as the nearest prior.
- The autopsy: "with COMPOSITE and universal formalisms available, no finite rule is
  'unaccounted for'". UNFAMILIAR is unreachable by construction.
- The calibration set contains 0 unfamiliar items.

**Meaningless now, important under an alternative.** Theseus M000617 (niche distance 8.1
vs a threshold of 4.31, transfer 0.90, "interpretation not attempted") is dismissed
because random programs also land far away. Under a consequence-defined novelty (L8)
distance is irrelevant; what matters is whether its downstream effects are spanned by
the library's.

**Cheap test.** L8.

## A9. That world and noise contributions are contamination

**Where it lives**
- Own-code criteria.
- "Scaffold defect".
- Noise treated as a confound.
- Excluding assisted births.

**The evidence is mixed, and the mixture is the point**
- Removing tape erosion produced runaways (ATOMIC), so there noise hurt.
- Injected noise amplifies rcv 4.8x in Aether, and a synthesis calls noise "the
  conversion step".
- The graph-world sham beat the graft by +1.400.
- Blind deletion improves the raw score of evolved bodies (Tyche R-S031, Nestor).
- ZERO reset beats constant state 26/48 vs 2/48 (C-ZERO-SPECIFIC).
- Removing recurrence improves decoding (Hecate).
- r8: corruption raises TRAIN fitness +4.4% while HELD falls.

**Meaningless now, important under an alternative.** A cross-engine pattern: subtracting
or clearing state often helps. Each instance was filed locally as an artefact. Together
they suggest these substrates are routinely stuck in cluttered states, and that clearing
is a functional operation the organisms do not own.

**Cheap test.** Subtraction dose-response (L6) across three engines.

## A10. That admissible worlds are learnable in the human sense

**Where it lives**
- Ensorain admission ("admission pre-selects the phenomenon").
- Hecate positive controls must contain "the effect present by construction".
- PTE's A3 eligibility counts an absence only where a hand plant fired.
- Archaeon worlds are 4-bit recall.

**Evidence against it**
- 0 of 2,000 random Ensorain development candidates were inhabitable.
- "Weird physics generally destroys learnability"; 3 of 181 admitted worlds are wild.
- PTE: evolution found transport where the hand design scored 0/253. The reference for
  what is possible is weaker than evolution.

**Meaningless now, important under an alternative.** Worlds where nothing is learnable in
the human sense but something persists or spreads. They are rejected at admission, so
they never produce rows.

**Cheap test.** Take the 2,000 rejected Ensorain candidates' generators. Score them on an
endogenous persistence or flux measure instead of learnability. If some are rich under
that measure, admission has been discarding the frontier.

## A11. That the thing to protect is a claim's truth, not its worth

This is a process assumption; see PROMETHEUS_PATHOLOGICAL_ATTRACTORS.md.

Harmonia's audit checklist (freeze order, recompute, amendments, controls-first, chance
floor, reachability, absence controls, shortcuts, ceilings, determinism, descent) checks
whether a verdict is valid. Nothing checks whether the question was worth answering.

The D2 firewall shows the cost. It took 29 hours, 13 versions and 18 addenda. It protected
a C3 claim that a zero-parameter definition rung killed 19 minutes before v13. That rung
was added five days after the C3 search ran.

## Ranking

The most dangerous is **A1 together with A2**. The program's rulers are built to
measure what accumulates. The direct tests of whether anything accumulates:
- come back null (NPE neutral walk, D8, Theseus, Archaeon C5, Tyche gate 6, Hecate meta);
- or show that selection is choosing on noise (Tyche 395/280, Hecate W6, Archaeon
  takeover).

None of those tests is anyone's CURRENT work. The one positive for accumulation is carried
by the environment sequence, which no descent ruler can see.

Next come **A5** (external judge) and **A6** (fixed operator). Together they explain much
of the null record: the search finds the judge's cheapest route, and it cannot cross the
valleys to anything else.
