# Prometheus pathological attractors

Aporia inference harvest, 2026-09-30. No fleet authority. The operator asked for
evidence, not philosophy, so each attractor below gives evidence for, evidence against,
and a verdict.

Counts come from git and comms (method in EVIDENCE_BRIEF.md section D). They are crude
keyword and path classifications and should be read as orders of magnitude.

Aporia is inside this system. Today it executed three CWOs, posted about 60 comms
messages and set a 90-minute heartbeat cadence for the fleet. That appears under P5.

---

## P1. Custody before triviality

The attractor: building protection for a claim before the claim has faced its cheapest
kill.

**Evidence for**
- D2 firewall: about 29 h, 48 messages, 13 versions, a 570-line record with 18 addenda,
  and about 84 commits.
- The claim it protected, Cosmos C3, was killed by a zero-parameter definition rung 19
  minutes before v13 was submitted. That rung was added five days after the C3 search
  ran.
- The trivial floor was also found after the claim in WTP-03 (N6), WTP-02 (the running
  mean), the graph-world sham, Cosmos G6 (a constant scores 10/12) and Crius C0.
- LM01: 60 frozen files, four freeze and review stages, 0 rows.
- C4: gates S0-S4, G1-G6, two reviewers and 7 stop conditions before any data.

**Evidence against**
- C4 now puts the definition rung first, so the lesson was learned.
- The D2 loop was bounded by an operator time box.
- Addendum Q frames an unspent holdout as a correct stop.

**Verdict: real and costly.** Both dissenters independently proposed the same fix:
trivial, definitional and drift baselines run before any custody or freeze machinery.

## P2. Asymmetric burden: parking is cheap, promotion is expensive

The attractor: it costs far less to put a line down than to establish it.

**Evidence for**
- Hecate parked 15 of 16 programs about 90 minutes after the seat was created, on 32.5
  core-minutes in total.
- NULL worlds were read at 0.006-0.02 core-minutes each.
- CONFOUNDED counted as NULL.
- Two new signals were parked 8 minutes after they first appeared.
- Promotion, by contrast, needs a passing preregistered predicate, controls,
  reachability, replication and an audit.
- Archaeon: SUPPORTED was unreachable in 11 of 12 cells, so only WEAK_POSITIVE was
  possible.

**Evidence against**
- PARK is defined as "a no-evidence allocation choice", not a verdict.
- Harmonia's F2 refuses clean-looking absences.

**Verdict: real.** An allocation choice made at sub-minute power works as a kill in
practice, because residues are rarely picked up again (P8).

## P3. Rulers as the main product

The attractor: the output is instruments, not findings.

**Evidence for**
- The replication ruler was replaced three times (predecessor, P-11, CVT-R).
- Other rulers were withdrawn: the locality law shrank to a dose term within one cycle,
  and four of seven fixed-count damage claims disappeared.
- There are about 45 verdict labels, including a first-class family for "the instrument
  could not have said otherwise" (UNREACHABLE_BY_DESIGN, NOTHING_COULD_FIRE,
  SPEC_UNATTAINABLE, NON_DISCRIMINATING, SANITY_CHECK_ONLY).
- The graph-world packet: "the instrument is now the main source of findings"; 13 of 14
  new defects were in the control plane.
- The Archaeon directive: "DO NOT IMPROVE THE STORY. IMPROVE THE MACHINE."

**Evidence against**
- Rulers have to exist, and the North Star lists "falsification instruments" among the
  things Prometheus supplies.
- Several ruler replacements were forced by real artefacts (painters passing P-11).

**Verdict: partly real.** Ruler work is legitimate, but it has become the default
response to every surprise. The surprise itself gets less attention than the ruler that
failed to predict it.

## P4. Micro-experiments inside macro-process

The attractor: very small science wrapped in very large process.

**Evidence for**
- Archaeon campaign 4: 22 minutes of compute in 3 h 41 min of wall time.
- Campaign 1: 591 s of harness time in 6 h 20 min.
- CW01 e01: 7.6-24.5 s of science against about 28 min, with the note "the point of this
  file is the factory, not the science".
- Hecate's whole cycle: 32.5 core-minutes.
- September added 483 PREREG files against 298 RESULT files.
- Commits touching journals outnumber commits touching RESULT files about 6.7 to 1.
- 1,163 prompt files were added.

**Evidence against**
- Ananke ran about 55 core-hours in one day, enough to hit the envelope.
- The E-BEL-MD multi-day campaign ran 47.1 h over 4,160 runs.
- Some lines do run at scale.

**Verdict: real in several engines.** Many nulls cannot be told apart from
underpowered tests. The one question that needs scale, D8 (roughly 669 tasks), was
parked as a "major campaign".

## P5. Bookkeeping inflation and coordination churn

The attractor: effort goes into recording and coordinating rather than into work.

**Evidence for**
- Seven work orders in three days, totalling 16,858 words.
- Comms by subject: audit/review/ruling 177; MWO/CWO/lease/infrastructure 152;
  heartbeat/boot/ack 142; result-bearing 78.
- 316 commits touching WORK_STATE.
- Heartbeats on 09-30 repeating "blocked on Cosmos, no change" every 60-90 minutes.
- Aporia's own CWO execution today added to all of this.

**Evidence against**
- Coordination was the operator's response to real stalls, and it unstuck several seats:
  the C4 assignments, the Windows Fabric fix, the comms hang.

**Verdict: real.** Heartbeat cadence should scale with change, not with the clock. An
unchanged BLOCKED seat has nothing new to report.

## P6. Monoculture of mind

The attractor: one model family plays every role.

**Evidence for**
- One model family generates, implements, attacks and scores (Hecate; Theseus).
- Every calibration ledger shows the seats mispredicting their own outcomes: REPL-01 lost
  3/3, REPL-02 5/5, Tyche RH1-RH4 were all false, and Theseus P2, P4 and P10 were wrong.
- In E-003, reviewers were told to check the amended rule, so they confirmed it.
- Blinding was honour-system (REPL-01).

**Evidence against**
- Independent Fabric replicas and cross-seat audits do catch errors: 13 of 14
  dispositions held, and the E-003 reversal was caught.

**Verdict: real, and structural.** Correlated blind spots between designer, attacker and
judge cannot be seen from inside. Only different instruments, different models, or
world-internal readers can break the correlation.

## P7. Rediscovery without transmission

The attractor: the program forgets and relearns its own failures.

**Evidence for**
- Three of the four June failure classes recur in September engines, and no September
  seat cites them.
- The same Z80 world was built three times.
- The REPL-02 prereg repeated a lesson recorded in REPL-01's errata ("in a prereg written
  before that lesson was written down").
- The C3 autopsy calls itself "the second instance of one failure mode", after C0.

**Evidence against**
- Harmonia's standing rules and some calibration ledgers do transmit lessons within a
  seat.

**Verdict: real.** The program's memory is per seat. It has no metabolism of its own
failures across seats, which is the thing the North Star asks the organisms to have.

## P8. The clean negative is the preferred terminal state

The attractor: ending in a verdict is rewarded over holding an open residue.

**Evidence for**
- Almost every line ends PARK, INCONCLUSIVE or NOT_SUPPORTED, and the residue is recorded
  and then left dormant:
  - Tyche's natural residuals were never perturbed;
  - about 290 Theseus dark objects per run, 60 attempted;
  - M000617 never interpreted;
  - graph-world survivor w8000036 unconsumed;
  - 370 of 423 Gemini prompts unfired;
  - 88 of 120 Artemis threads failure-shaped.
- The North Star's "failure continues the tree" is honoured in labels, not in allocation.
- The operator's 09-28 challenge: "Optimize for epistemic movement."

**Evidence against**
- Harmonia F2 refuses clean absences.
- The E-003 audit pushed toward the less convenient label.
- X-MAT ENDOGENOUS was reported as a positive.

**Verdict: real.** Ambiguous novelty has no owner and no compute slice. A clean negative
closes a ticket, and CWO-B/C's finish-in-place rule rewards closing tickets.

## P9. Agents satisfying process rather than discovering

The attractor: seats optimize for compliance with the process.

**Evidence for**
- Commit subjects overclaim relative to the underlying files. For example, "certifies
  all 42" was expected by construction.
- Plans have been committed together with their results.
- Heartbeat compliance messages.
- The finish-in-place CWO withdrew Hecate's proposed mechanical novelty ruler: "do NOT
  pivot into a new novelty program". Process correctly stopped scope growth, and also
  stopped the most direct repair of a broken instrument.

**Evidence against**
- The seats' self-reported errata and lost predictions are the opposite of
  process-gaming.

**Verdict: mixed.** The seats are honest. The process sometimes blocks the repair that
honesty identified.

## P10. Preference for causal localization and decomposition

See CONVERGENT_BLIND_SPOTS B6. Most of the vocabulary concerns carriers, cuts and
attribution, while the measured locality effect is small next to dose. Composition
science has "not moved toward a predictive theory".

**Verdict: real, but a symptom of the ontology (A4) rather than an independent
attractor.**

---

## Summary

The strongest attractors are P1, P2, P4 and P8. Together they produce a system that:
- protects claims before testing them cheaply;
- kills lines on sub-minute evidence;
- runs small science inside large process;
- ends in clean negatives whose residues nobody owns.

None of this is dishonest. It is what a population of careful agents does when careful
verdicts are rewarded and open questions are not.
