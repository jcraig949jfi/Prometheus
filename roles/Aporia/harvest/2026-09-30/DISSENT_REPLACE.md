# Replacing the Prometheus framing: an economy of load-bearing regularities

## 0. The claim

The current frame asks one question in many forms: is there an organism here, does it
copy, and do its parts cause its success? Nearly every ruler in the brief answers that
question and then fails:
- P-11, CVT-R and the K3 founder snapshot;
- glineage and the oid;
- the nine reproduction definitions;
- the LLM novelty classifier.

They fail in one consistent direction. The phenomena keep appearing between the things
the frame counts: across a parent and its executing context, across a lineage and the
reset schedule, across a site and the reader that never reads it.

Drop the organism as a primitive. Replace it with an **economy of load-bearing
regularities**. The world is one trajectory. What persists in it are regularities; each
is held up by something and may hold up something else. The science becomes: where
support sits, how it moves, and how deep the chains of support grow.

The North Star is preserved. Its own anchor for sagacity, "a compact handle through
which a receiver reconstructs a much richer lesson", is a statement about handles,
receivers and reconstruction. It says nothing about organisms. "Grow, not hand-design"
becomes: deepen the support graph without installing its nodes. "Kill claims, never
lineages" becomes easier: there are no lineages to kill, and every regularity stays in
the supply catalogue.

## 1. Primitives and their measurement

**P1. Regularity R.** A pattern in the whole world's state-time record, found by a
general-purpose compressor over windows of the trajectory. Its strength is Delta-L, the
description-length saving over two surrogates:
- a dead-world surrogate, frozen at the window's first state;
- a scrambled surrogate, with the same marginals but time-shuffled.

A regularity counts only if it beats both, which blocks the trivial "the medium froze"
regularity. Delta-L is never assigned to an entity.

**P2. Support vector S(R).** For each candidate source X, the fractional loss of R's
Delta-L when X is clamped, randomized or withdrawn. Sources include:
- environment channels: resets, supplied register values, the scoring distribution,
  noise, erosion;
- spatial regions;
- the execution budget;
- other regularities.

Attribution goes to sources of maintenance anywhere in the world, never to loci inside
an organism. S is expected to be plural.

**P3. Load(R).** The number and summed Delta-L of other regularities that weaken when R
is disrupted. This is PTE's "channel content is a READER-side verdict", formalized.
- A regularity with Delta-L > 0 and Load = 0 is **supply**.
- A regularity with Load > 0 is **load-bearing**.
- Chains of load give **support depth D**.

**P4. Handle ratio H.** The smallest seed patch k which, written into a fresh world with
none of R's history, re-establishes R. H = (Delta-L restored) / (bits in k), reported
together with the receiver-only baseline: what a random patch of the same size restores.

**Invariants to track over time:**
- support migration between environment, context and internal dynamics;
- depth growth dD/dt;
- the capture rate of supply into load.

Sagacity is a regularity with high H, high Load and internal support, which survives
withdrawal of the external support it once leaned on.

## 2. The same evidence, re-read

**The two most reproducible positives are the same event.**
- C-A3-INTERNALIZE: lineages that leaned on environment-supplied register values came to
  carry competence from random registers.
- The Archaeon corridor ladder: delay 0 -> 1 -> 2 -> 4 reached W1_d4 in 4/6 seeds, vs 1/14
  for direct search. It yields a delay-invariant reader in 11/12 seeds, "the campaign's
  only reproducible positive capability [and it] is unexplained".

Both are graded withdrawal of an external support source, followed by migration of that
support inward. It is "unexplained" only because the old frame has no noun for support
location.

**Other re-readings**
- **Replacement by a different lineage (18 runs) is not a confound.** Whether the migrated
  support landed in the donor's bytes or a stranger's is a descent question and noise
  here. 8 + 18 runs form one migration dataset.
- **Environment doing self-location is the baseline, not a defect.** About 99% of
  self-location support sits in the reset channel (2 of 332 true locators). The question
  becomes the schedule on which reset support is withdrawn.
- **Guessers are measurements of the scorer.** Output regularities are supported by the
  task's marginal statistics. Gating removed that support abruptly, and competence went to
  0.000. The ladder predicts that graded removal behaves differently. The 3.8% weak
  readers are the migration's first foothold.
- **Painters are receivers.** Painters pass P-11 at 0.90-1.00 and win under budget (20/20
  vs 0/20): the executing context does most of the reconstruction. Tighter budgets should
  push support toward the receiver. The program has been discarding the receiver half of
  its own anchor.
- **Context-dependent copiers are the native unit.** Support is jointly located, and the
  2.32M environment-assisted births are the main dataset.
- **Noise, erosion and transients are support sources.** Removing erosion (C-ATOMIC)
  produced runaways conserving only OP_SELF and LDIR. Aether templates are about 93%
  static without noise, and noise amplifies rcv 4.8x. K1 persistence runs P75 > P90 >
  ZERO. Disturbance supplies turnover, and turnover keeps support distributed.
- **Present-but-unused carriers and integrators on HOLD are supply.** So are the unused
  rule register and Tyche's fragments that never co-store (max 0.007). Supply is
  abundant; capture is the bottleneck. Tyche shows capture is possible: exaptation 63/92,
  coalition 62/92, and two useless lenses fused into a replicated sensor.
- **Outward drift (Theseus, rho ~0.1, p = 0.001) is the frame's only arrow of time.** Ask
  whether drifting regularities carry more internal support.
- **Negative load and added-without-support.** Blind deletion that improves score marks
  negative-load regularities. The sham beating the graft (+1.400) is a graft that added a
  regularity without its support. The same reading applies to decoding that improves once
  recurrence is removed.
- **Regularities are attractors of a reconstruction map, not lineages of material.** The
  oid persists while bytes are replaced. "The founder was NOT the replicator; its first
  child (a fixed point of its imperfect copy map) was." TH-015's 66% says the support is
  diffuse.

**Celebrated positives that become uninteresting**
- C-ATOMIC: self-support collapses onto two instructions. A monoculture.
- BEE CONFIRMED_CAUSAL self-replication, CVT-R pass counts and heritability 0.9987: one
  corner of support space, heavily instrumented.
- EXPLICIT_FITNESS +0.30: the scorer is a support source by construction.
- C-CORE: purifying selection.
- RELAY 0.875: support sits in the graph topology.

What survives is PTE's 8/352 communication-dependent cells, because that is load.

## 3. Predictions the old frame does not make

None of these probes needs a new engine.
1. **Graded withdrawal beats gating for guessers.**
   - Setup: on C9-H1R, three arms at matched budget: (a) the abrupt read-gate, (b) no
     gate, (c) a linear ramp that flattens the answer marginal's skew over the same
     epochs.
   - Prediction: the ramp arm's reader share exceeds both, and the 3.8% base.
   - Falsifier: ramp reader share <= max(a, b) within a 95% CI.
2. **Self-location migrates under a reset ramp.**
   - Setup: constant resets vs abrupt removal vs geometric decay of reset frequency.
   - Measure: true locators.
   - Falsifier: ramp <= abrupt, or ramp stays at <= 2/332.
3. **Disturbance maximizes depth at an interior point.**
   - Setup: rerun the C-ATOMIC cell at erosion 0, 1, 2.5, 5 and 10%/byte/epoch. Compute
     P1 regularities and the P3 load graph over the top 20.
   - Prediction: support depth D peaks at an interior erosion rate, while old-frame
     runaway heredity peaks at 0.
   - Falsifier: D is monotone non-increasing, or peaks at 0. This is the probe most able
     to embarrass the frame.
4. **Supply is captured above chance.**
   - Setup: in archived PTE or C-A3 lineage corpora, compare carriers flagged
     PRESENT-BUT-UNUSED at generation g with a matched random set.
   - Measure: whether each is load-bearing at g + k.
   - Falsifier: capture rate <= matched random.
5. **Budget moves support to the receiver.**
   - Setup: on the 57 P-11 survivors, sweep execution budget over 4 levels.
   - Measure: the receiver share of H.
   - Prediction: it rises as budget tightens, with painters as the endpoint.
   - Falsifier: no monotone trend.

## 4. What the frame stops measuring, and why that is safe

**Stop:**
- oid and lineage identity, descent certification and replication rulers (P-11, CVT-R,
  K3, glineage);
- reproduction-boundary adjudication;
- per-organism carrier localization;
- LLM prose novelty;
- "two NULLs -> PARK";
- count thresholds on organism events.

**Why this is safe:**
- These rulers measured a noun the evidence repeatedly refused to supply.
- Heredity reappears as a special case (internal support plus a small handle), measured
  by H and S.
- Novelty becomes a support configuration not seen in any prior world.
- PARK disappears, because every regularity stays in the supply catalogue.

**Also stop auditing claim by claim before data exists.** D2 ran 13 versions to protect
a claim already dead; LM01 has 60 frozen files and 0 rows; journals are touched 6.7:1
over RESULT files.

**Keep:**
- the two surrogates;
- a planted-regularity positive control per world (the June lesson: 2/2 of what it could
  express, 0/12 of what it could not);
- disjoint windows for support and load.

Preregistration shrinks to one falsifier per probe, frozen before the run.

## 5. Where this frame is most likely wrong, and its guards

**Tautology (the C3 failure mode).** Support and load are both computed by intervention.
- Guard: disjoint time windows and disjoint intervention families, withdrawal for S and
  pairwise clamps for Load.
- Guard: run the claim first on a neutral walk (no selection); the 1.75 / p = .20 walk is
  the right null.

**The compressor is a human prior.**
- Guard: two unrelated compressors (dictionary-based and context-mixing), and count a
  regularity only if both see it.
- Guard: report planted-control recall.

**Combinatorial false loads.**
- Guard: a fixed top-k frozen before intervention, a Bonferroni-held edge threshold, and
  depth only over edges that replicate on a second seed.

**The ramp is a curriculum, which is hand design.**
- Guard: one generic ramp for every source.
- Guard: the regularity must persist at zero support and be restorable in a fresh world
  with no ramp history (the handle test).

**Unfalsifiable breadth.** The five falsifiers are directional. If probes 1-3 all fail,
the frame has no distinctive prediction left and should be retired, not amended (avoid
the E-003 amendment pattern).

**Expected false-discovery rate.** Higher per claim than the current frame, because it is
deliberately permissive about what counts as a phenomenon. It should be lower per
compute-hour of science, because it stops spending those hours adjudicating identity
questions the substrate does not answer.
