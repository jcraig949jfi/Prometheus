## The disagreement, and what it settled

Two fresh-context thinkers were given the same neutral evidence brief. Neither saw this
audit.

**The defence** (DISSENT_DEFEND.md) argues that the current frame is a machine for
assigning credit correctly, and that the kill list is the frame working:
- 94% artefact replication;
- the running mean;
- disguised batch completion;
- C3 restating its own definition;
- LLM confidence mistaken for novelty.

It also argues that every alternative readout would have let those errors survive. For
example, a population heritability of 0.9987 "passes for any faithful copier". Its
strongest point: the frame revised its own ontology through measurement (plural
reproduction predicates, carriers as trajectories, the child rather than the founder as
the replicator).

**The replacement** (DISSENT_REPLACE.md) drops the organism for an economy of
load-bearing regularities with support vectors, load and a handle ratio. Its most
important observation is one this audit had missed: the two most reproducible positives
in the corpus are the same event.
- C-A3-INTERNALIZE: lineages that leaned on environment-supplied registers became
  competent from random registers.
- The Archaeon delay ladder: 11 of 12 seeds generalize, while direct search mostly
  fails.

In both cases an external support is withdrawn gradually and the capability migrates
inward.

**Where they agree** (and this audit with them). These are the most reliable conclusions
of the harvest:
1. Run trivial, definitional and drift baselines before building custody or freeze
   machinery.
2. PARK is too cheap. A PARK should state its power; CONFOUNDED is not NULL; and residues
   need an owner and a compute slice.
3. Positive controls and rulers lean toward what humans can already build. Add planted
   "alien" positive controls.
4. The world's contributions (resets, erosion, noise, assistance) should become declared,
   swept variables, not excluded contamination.

**Where they disagree.** Whether to retire the organism/descent rulers (replacement) or
keep them and widen them (defence).

**This audit's position.** Keep credit discipline and do not retire the rulers yet. Let
the evidence decide with three cheap probes, each with a directional falsifier (below).
The defence is right that looser readouts produce more false positives. The replacement
is right that the current unit of description has failed to find accumulation anywhere,
and that its one clean case of accumulation is invisible to its own rulers.

## The most dangerous hidden assumption

**History accumulates, and selection is doing the work.**

Every descent, heredity and lineage ruler, and the North Star's "retained and improved",
presupposes that ancestry carries an advantage beyond fresh variation from the same
operator. The direct tests are null or point the other way:
- NPE: the neutral walk matches the soup (1.75, p = .20), and the full neutral-walk study
  was never run.
- D8, history vs diversity: 7/6, p = 1.0, now parked.
- Theseus: ancestry does not beat random, and random programs gain more from evolved
  lenses (12/60 vs 5/60).
- Archaeon C5: the elite is the starting parent after 36,000 evaluations, and 0/96 cells
  gained.
- Tyche: gate 6 failed.
- Hecate meta: triplicates do not beat pairs.

Selection frequently acts on noise:
- Tyche: parent choice was decided by a noise-level case 395 times vs 280 by a
  significant one.
- Hecate W6: elites above the instrument ceiling, from lucky draws.
- Archaeon: competent and incompetent material take over equally (12/12 vs 12/12).
- NPE: founders are independent tickets.

No seat's CURRENT work tests whether anything accumulates. The machinery cannot report
"this soup is a lottery", because no standing baseline would say so.

## The strongest alternative framing

**Support migration.** O3 in RIVAL_ONTOLOGIES.md, from DISSENT_REPLACE.md.

Capability is a regularity held up by sources: environment channels, context, budget,
and other regularities. Accumulation, where it exists, is the migration of that support
from external sources to internal ones. It is driven by gradual withdrawal of the
external support, and a descent ruler would not see it.

This framing:
- unifies the corpus's two most reproducible positives;
- reads guessers as measurements of the scorer, and painters as receivers (the missing
  half of the North Star's handle/receiver definition of sagacity);
- reads present-but-unused carriers as unconverted supply;
- makes directional, falsifiable predictions: graded withdrawal beats abrupt gating, and
  support depth peaks at an interior disturbance level.

The **lottery ontology** (O1) is its essential null. Run both.

## Three especially valuable alien probes

These are bounded and use existing engines or logs. Details in ALIEN_LENSES_NEXT.md.
1. **L1, memorylessness: the accumulation null.**
   - Method: fit time-to-first-competence hazards across five engines' existing logs,
     conditional on proximity to competence.
   - Falsifier: an increasing hazard in 2 or more engines refutes the lottery.
   - Cost: no new runs.
2. **L3, graded withdrawal vs abrupt gating.**
   - Sites: the C9-H1R guessers, and reset-supplied self-location.
   - Rules: one generic ramp shape; the capability must persist at zero support and be
     restorable in a fresh world.
   - Falsifier: the ramp is no better than abrupt removal, which kills the support
     framing's distinctive prediction.
3. **L2, fixed-point census of the rewrite map.**
   - Method: run the physics with selection off on random tapes and compare class
     inventories with evolved runs.
   - Falsifier: evolved classes more than 10x enriched over the census, which means
     selection builds what the physics alone does not.

## One thing Prometheus should stop doing

**Stop building custody, holdout, freeze and multi-gate adjudication machinery for a
claim before that claim has faced its trivial baseline, its definitional baseline and a
same-operator drift null.**

The D2 firewall spent 29 hours and 13 versions protecting a claim a zero-parameter rule
had already killed. Every trivial-floor kill in the corpus arrived after the claim:
WTP-03's N6, WTP-02's running mean, the graph-world sham, Cosmos G6, and Crius C0. Both
dissenters independently proposed this ordering rule. It costs nothing in rigour and
reorders where rigour is spent.

## One capability Prometheus currently lacks entirely

**A way to recognize function without an external judge.**

Every engine with an objective scores it outside the world, and the search reliably
finds the judge's cheapest route: guessers, latches, running means, a zero-byte abstain
policy, a zero-action "winner". The one engine without a judge says "nothing in Aether's
observatory can say a structure does something useful."

Prometheus has no instrument that can say a structure matters to the world itself. Such
an instrument would measure an endogenous consequence, a consultation edge, or load, in
the sense of O3 and O4.

Until it exists, the North Star's "sagacity for which we have no human name" can only be
recognized when it happens to also score well on a human-named task.

## Closing note on the harvest

The fleet's experiments are mostly competent, and the evidence audit confirms it. The
danger identified here is not sloppiness. The program's units and rulers are well
calibrated to catch false organisms, false replication and false novelty. They are not
built to detect whether anything accumulates, or to recognize function the world itself
uses. Those are the two things the North Star most needs to see.
