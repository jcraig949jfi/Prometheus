# Rival ontologies for Prometheus phenomena

Aporia inference harvest, 2026-09-30. No fleet authority. This file offers five
descriptions of the same evidence. They are mutually incompatible: each predicts at
least one outcome another forbids, and each names the probe that would embarrass it.
Evidence citations are in EVIDENCE_BRIEF.md and WRONG_QUESTION_AUDIT_2026-09-30.md.
O3 is adapted, with credit, from the fresh-context "replace the frame" dissent
(DISSENT_REPLACE.md).

The current ontology, for reference: organisms are byte tapes in slots, with parents,
own-code reproduction, localized mechanisms and carriers, competence scored by an
external task, and novelty judged from descriptions.

---

## O1. The lottery (the null ontology)

**Primitives.** Physics x variation operator = a fixed sampling distribution over
states. A run is a sequence of independent draws. Every observed "event" is a hitting
time.

**What the evidence looks like under O1**
- Founders are "independent tickets, p ~ 0.13 each". C-CRITICAL-MASS was withdrawn for
  exactly this reason.
- Lineages under held-out selection are "either 8/8 or 0/8 competent": a coin, not a
  gradient.
- The neutral walk matches the soup (1.75, p = .20). D8 history vs diversity is 7/6.
- Parent choice is mostly noise-decided (395 vs 280).
- Mature solvers and incompetent controls take over equally (12/12 vs 12/12).
- Every treatment effect (C-ATOMIC 1/80 -> 46/80, C-RUNAWAY, C-DENSE-COPY 1/64 -> 39/64)
  is a change in ticket odds.

**Minimizes.** Organism, memory, representation, copying and intelligence all become
labels on draws.

**Unique predictions**
- Time to first competence is geometric (memoryless) at matched mutation rate.
- Conditioning on a run's history (its current best, its ancestry depth) does not
  shorten the remaining time.
- Every "accumulation" contrast is null once the budget and the operator are matched.

**Embarrassing probe.** L1 (memorylessness). If the hazard of first competence rises
with time spent near competence, O1 is false and something accumulates.

## O2. Fixed points of the rewrite map (no organisms)

**Primitives.** The world's write operator W. It includes copy primitives, resets,
erosion and the overwrite policy, and it acts on the whole memory. The objects of study
are its fixed points, cycles and basins under W composed with mutation. Identity is not
a primitive.

**Measurements**
- Basin volume for each attractor class.
- Escape rate.
- Class inventory for each physics.
- Hitting time from random tapes.

**What the evidence looks like under O2**
- "The founder was NOT the replicator. Its first child (a fixed point of its imperfect
  copy map) was."
- The oid persists while all its bytes are replaced.
- Founder material goes to 0.0, and founder-snapshot rulers cannot pass: descended
  genomes differ at 58-62 of 64 bytes.
- Painters (0x36 near-homopolymers) are the trivial fixed point, and the cheapest one:
  painter 20/20 vs bytewise copier 0/20 under budget.
- TH-015's 66% is the size of the replicator attractor's support.
- "Replacement by a different lineage" (18 runs) is basin entry from another direction.

**Minimizes.** Organism, parent, copying and descent. Heredity becomes return to the
same basin.

**Unique predictions**
- The replicator classes seen in evolved runs are already present, at their basin
  frequencies, when W runs on random tapes with selection off.
- Founder identity predicts the end class only through hitting time.

**Embarrassing probe.** L2 (fixed-point census). Evolved classes that are absent or
vanishingly rare in the selection-off census would mean selection builds structure that
W alone does not. That would refute O2 in that cell.

## O3. An economy of load-bearing regularities (support migration)

Adapted from DISSENT_REPLACE.md.

**Primitives**
- **Regularities:** compressible patterns in the world's state-time record, which must
  beat both a frozen-world surrogate and a time-scrambled one.
- **Support vector S(R):** how much a regularity's strength falls when each source is
  withdrawn. Sources include environment channels, regions, budget and other
  regularities.
- **Load:** which other regularities depend on it.
- **Handle ratio H:** the regularity restored per bit of seed patch in a fresh world, net
  of what the receiving context restores alone. This is the North Star's own
  handle/receiver definition of sagacity, made mechanical.

**What the evidence looks like under O3**
- The two most reproducible positives are one event: graded withdrawal of an external
  support, followed by migration of that support inward.
  - C-A3-INTERNALIZE: lineages that leaned on environment-supplied registers came to be
    competent from random registers.
  - The Archaeon delay ladder: 11/12 seeds generalize, while direct search mostly fails.
- Self-location: about 99% of its support sits in the reset channel (2 of 332 true
  locators).
- Guessers measure the scorer's marginal statistics.
- Painters are receivers: the executing context does the reconstruction.
- Present-but-unused carriers and stored fragments that never co-store are unconverted
  supply. Their capture rate is measured: exaptation 63/92, coalition 62/92, full
  precursor sets 0.007.

**Minimizes.** Organism, lineage and carrier location. Heredity reappears as a special
case: internal support plus a small handle.

**Unique predictions**
- Graded withdrawal of a support produces internal capability more often than either
  abrupt removal or none.
- Support depth peaks at an interior disturbance level, not at zero erosion.
- Supply is captured into load above a matched-random rate.

**Embarrassing probes**
- L3: graded vs abrupt removal on the C9-H1R guessers, and a reset-frequency ramp for
  self-location.
- The erosion sweep in ALIEN_LENSES_NEXT.
- If the ramp arms are no better than abrupt removal and depth is monotone in erosion,
  O3 loses its distinctive bets. The dissent itself says it should then be retired, not
  amended.

## O4. Reader-relative function (deliberately awkward for ML language)

**Primitives**
- **Writers:** any process that leaves a difference in the world.
- **Readers:** any process whose future depends counterfactually on that difference.
- **A function:** a writer-reader pair, and nothing else.

There is no loss, no representation, no agent and no task. The world holds a set of
differences, and "meaning" is the relation "this difference is consulted downstream". A
difference that nothing consults is not memory and not information; it is simply
unconsulted.

**Measurements.** The consultation graph, built from counterfactual dependence between
parts over time windows inside the world.
- Its density.
- Its depth: chains of consultation.
- Its growth.

**What the evidence looks like under O4**
- PTE's own corrections become the core theory: "channel content is a READER-side
  verdict", "PERSISTENT TRACES ARE NOT MEMORY".
- "Cue-dependent traffic and routing that nothing reads are common." These are writers
  waiting for readers.
- "The search builds integrators even on HOLD." Writers are cheap.
- The C9-H1R plateau: no reader ever evolves unless reading is gated, and 3.8% weak
  readers exist.
- CW01 cycle 8: the organism answers before reading, and the nearest reading program is 4
  edits away.
- The E-P1 content signature cannot detect transport in a rich soup, because the
  observer is not a reader in the world.
- ASAL's observer maps turbulence and coherent life to the same score. The observer is
  the only reader, and it is the wrong one.

**Unique prediction.** The frontier is reader-limited. Evolving readers against frozen,
already-present writers produces new consultation edges faster than evolving writers
against frozen readers.

**Embarrassing probe.** L4 (reader-first evolution in PTE). If writer-first unlocks
consultation as fast, O4's asymmetry is false.

## O5. Interaction-flux ecology

**Primitives**
- Flows of write operations and energy between regions.
- Occupancy turnover.
- The who-writes-whom interaction graph and its motifs: pairs, cycles, hubs.

The unit of persistence is an interaction motif, not a string.

**What the evidence looks like under O5**
- All 1,031 Z80xAtlas replication flags were PAIR_EXECUTION, the only physics in which
  reproduction happened at all.
- "Context-dependent copiers that copy nothing in isolation."
- 2.32M environment-assisted births, 9% of all births.
- 99.9% of self-replication births overwrite a neighbour.
- Reproduction-computation antagonism is competition for write bandwidth.
- Aether's 3.04% irreducible energy leak is a flux fact.
- ENVGATE-02 shows 7x the predicted establishment with the window closed: "something
  outside the frozen window model carries establishment".

**Unique predictions**
- Motif statistics of the interaction graph predict which regions persist better than
  genome content does.
- Removing an interaction partner kills persistence even when the "organism's" bytes are
  intact.

**Embarrassing probe.** L3b (write-graph motifs vs genome features on held-out runs from
existing BEE and NPE logs). If genome features win, O5 is wrong about the unit.

## How they conflict

| Question | O1 lottery | O2 fixed points | O3 support | O4 readers | O5 flux |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Does anything accumulate? | no | no (basins are static) | yes: support migrates | yes: consultation depth grows | yes: motifs entrench |
| Does selection matter? | changes odds only | incidental | shapes migration schedule | selects readers | selects motifs |
| Unit | draw | attractor class | regularity + support | writer-reader pair | interaction motif |
| Painters are | lucky draws | the ground state | receivers | writers with no readers | cheap flux sinks |
| Decisive cheap probe | L1 memorylessness | L2 selection-off census | L3 graded withdrawal | L4 reader-first | L3b motifs |

The current ontology is a sixth column: organisms, descent, carriers, an external
judge. The defence dissent (DISSENT_DEFEND.md) argues it is the right discipline for
assigning credit. The audit agrees that credit discipline must be kept. What it disputes
is that credit discipline requires this particular unit of description.

Every probe above can be run with the existing credit discipline: a frozen falsifier,
controls first, and a planted positive control.
