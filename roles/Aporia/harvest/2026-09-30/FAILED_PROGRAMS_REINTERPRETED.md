# Failed and parked programs, reinterpreted

Aporia inference harvest, 2026-09-30. No fleet authority. Nothing here changes a frozen
verdict or rehabilitates a withdrawn claim. Every verdict below stands as recorded.

For each line, the failure is split into six layers:
- experiment: execution or sample;
- ruler: the instrument could not say otherwise;
- search: the operator or budget could not reach it;
- representation: the substrate could not express it;
- target: the question as posed was ill-defined;
- ontology: the unit of description was wrong.

Each line then keeps the observation worth rescuing.
