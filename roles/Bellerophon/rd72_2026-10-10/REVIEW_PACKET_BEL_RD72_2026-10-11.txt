----- 0. SUMMARY AND VERDICT -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

Mandate (operator directive BEL-RD-72): 72 h, no HITL. Test whether evolution in the z80atlas substrate can cross
reachability deserts, compose independently useful mechanisms, and accumulate them. Highest-value workstream: C,
cumulative organization. Rules: frozen preregistration, independent origins, replay receipts, triviality controls; do
not optimise for positive results.

Verdict:
(a) Minimal computations (ECHO, INC: 2-6 critical bytes) are found, paid for and kept. A computing capability persists
    after every carrier is removed, regenerated from near-functional material left in the world (environmental
    regeneration, confirmed).
(b) NO composite computation was built by evolution anywhere:
    - 0 / 1,320 two-block worlds (K2);
    - 0 / 150 long-horizon worlds (E1b);
    - 0 / 160 worlds founded >= 2 steps from the composite.
    The only crossings were 2 one-point repairs in 100 worlds founded one step away.
(c) The decisive, causal, replicated, transplanted finding: a planted composite cannot be MAINTAINED at the mutation
    rate at which the minimal capabilities are found. COND_ONE composite kept to tick 2,000 in 1/20 worlds at MED vs
    13/20 LOW vs 17/20 VLOW (p 2.0e-7). Reproduced on fresh seeds (12/20 vs 1/20). Transferred to a 14-byte COND_MULTI
    composite (19/20 vs 0/20) and to a WELL_MIXED world (20/20 vs 2/20). Payment structure is NOT the cause (removing
    half-credit: 2/20 vs 1/20). Lowering the rate keeps but does not find; a search-then-keep schedule did not help.
    => Composition in this substrate is bounded by an ERROR THRESHOLD before reachability can be tested.
(d) Horizontal uptake has two opposite, separable components:
    - imports into precursors (LDIR carriers) suppress origination: blocking them raises origins 72 -> 150 and
      33 -> 111, p < 1e-14 in both substrates;
    - imports into non-LDIR organisms promote it: blocking them lowers origins 72 -> 50 and 33 -> 26.
    The BEL-48H "uptake suppresses origination" headline was their net balance; it was weak and non-significant this
    time (82 vs 68 discordant, p 0.14).
(e) Operator-aware geometry did NOT predict completion better than substitution or byte content (AUC 0.592 vs 0.586 vs
    0.606). Geometry predicts sharply only at distance 1.

----- 2. THE CLAIMS AND WHY THEY MATTER -------------------------------------------------------------------

The operator review framed three questions:
- origin (can a mechanism appear?);
- composition (can useful mechanisms combine without destroying each other?);
- accumulation (can organization retain earlier capabilities while acquiring more?).
BEL-48H answered origin. This campaign attacked composition and accumulation, and found that the binding constraint is
neither reachability nor selection but MAINTENANCE under the variation needed for search.

Budget coupling (Q4, static, deterministic): copy-length coupling has two separable components.
- EXHAUSTION (input read before the copy): vanishes at budget >= 384.
- I/O DAMAGE (input read after the copy): 95/95 failures at every budget up to 4,096.
- Compute-first machines are immune to both.

----- 7. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH ------------------------------------------------------------

Established (causal, frozen, replicated where stated):
- RM2: an error threshold for composite maintenance in z80atlas v3. Causal (F3), replicated (F4-R), transplanted to a
  second composite and world model (F6).
- RM4: two opposing components of uptake, in 2 substrates.
- RM1: environmental regeneration of a minimal capability.
- RM3: two-component copy-length coupling, on 4 specimens.

NOT established:
- That composites are unreachable: distance-1 crossings happen (2/100), and >= 2 steps was never reached in 2,000-3,000
  ticks. "Inaccessible" is not claimed.
- That RM2 holds for EVOLVED composites with redundancy: only hand-built witnesses were tested.
- The quantitative location of the threshold: critical-byte count does not predict it (F6 side prediction wrong).
- Any long-term exploration benefit of uptake.
- Any C3 dependency.
- Prevalence of the two coupling types in evolved populations.

Conditionality: v3 K40, lifespan 40, 256 cells, 64-byte tapes, single substrate family.

----- 9. QUESTIONS FOR THE REVIEWER (written to resist agreement) ----------------------------------------

Q1. Is "error threshold" more than a restatement that mutation breaks programs with more critical sites? F6's wrong
    side prediction says byte count is not the whole story. What decides the threshold, and does RM2 earn the word
    "mechanism"?
Q2. All composite maintenance tests used HAND-BUILT witnesses with no redundancy. Is it fair to generalise to "composition
    is bounded by maintenance" when evolution never got to build a composite of its own?
Q3. C2R's regeneration is of ECHO, which a material-free copier population re-invents in 9/16 worlds. Is
    "environmental regeneration from stored material" distinguishable from "ECHO is cheap" at this effect size (13/15)?
Q4. D2's arms reverted wildly different doses (S_REF: 122M bytes in block_ldir vs 0.37M in block_noldir). How much of
    "two opposing components" survives a dose-matched block?
Q5. K-P1 was NOT_TESTABLE by an extinction rule I added in amendment A1, while the raw count was 0/60 in both cells in
    the predicted direction. Was that rule too strict, or correctly strict?
Q6. F2-F5 reused F1's 20 seeds. The paired design gains power, but does it hide seed-set idiosyncrasy? Only F4-R and F6
    were fresh.
Q7. Should the campaign have stopped running composition experiments after F1 and spent the remaining compute on the
    heritable-fidelity test, rather than F4/F5?
