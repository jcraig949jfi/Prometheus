## A1 -- where are the deserts? (static; B2 audit + review R1 + DP + F1 baseline)
- Desert under ALL single operators:
  - every composite task (COND_ONE, COND_MULTI), and SUM2, from every simpler rung and from the bare copier;
  - COND_MULTI from its two half-block fixtures: >= 4 / >= 5 substitutions, 0 competent in ~3e9 two-step neighbours.
- One-operator needles: CONST -> ECHO and ECHO -> INC exist ONLY under insertion.
- Downward moves are dense: hundreds of routes from a composite to its halves.
- Evolved material: E1b's ECHO / INC replicators are 0 / 3.9M one-step and 0 / 52,000 three-step from the COND_ONE
  composite, including splices from the coexisting partner.

## A3 -- does operator-aware geometry PREDICT completion in unseen worlds? (prereg s5)
- FALSIFIED: AUC operator-aware 0.592 vs substitution 0.586 vs non-zero bytes 0.606; CIs include 0.
- The reason is distributional: 97% of harvested non-FUNC tapes have no one-step route, and no tape had a MOVE-only
  route. The operator-aware score therefore carries the same information as substitution on this harvest.
- Descriptive: one-step SUB-route tapes complete 8/9 vs 37/581 for no-route tapes (background level; Fisher p 5e-9).

## Disposition
- Q1 (operator-aware prediction): NO, not better than the simpler ruler. Both predict well only at distance 1.
- Q2 (deserts as artifacts of an incomplete operator model): NO, in this campaign. The deserts survive SUB, MOVE, INS,
  DEL and donor splices, and a two-source operator that opens one geometric route did not yield a heritable crossing.
  BEL-48H's move-rich precursors (N1) remain real but came from complementation worlds; they are not typical of
  random non-FUNC material.
- Q3 (a genuinely difficult desert crossed): NO, for K2 and E1b. For F1, see COMPOSITION s4.
