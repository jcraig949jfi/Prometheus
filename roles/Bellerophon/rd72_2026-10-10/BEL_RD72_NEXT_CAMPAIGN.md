# BEL-RD-72 NEXT CAMPAIGN (ranked; each item names the result it follows from and a frozen-able first test)

The organizing result of BEL-RD-72: in z80atlas v3, the mutation rate that lets evolution FIND minimal capabilities
(ECHO, INC: 2-6 critical bytes) DESTROYS a ~10-critical-byte composite even when it is planted. This is an error
threshold (F3, reproduced F4-R); payment structure is not the cause (F2). No fixed rate both finds and keeps the composite
in 2,000 ticks (F4). Composition and cumulative organization are bounded by MAINTENANCE before reachability is tested.
The next campaign should attack that bound directly, and keep reachability work tied to it.

1. VARIATION-MAINTENANCE CONFLICT: schedules and heritable fidelity. (F3, F4, F5)
   - F5 (two-phase MED -> VLOW) is this campaign's first probe; its result is in RESULTS_LOG.
   - Next: copy fidelity as a HERITABLE trait (an opt-in per-organism mutation-rate gene within bounds). Test whether
     composite-carrying lineages evolve lower rates (second-order selection), against a fixed-rate yoked control.
   - First frozen test: in KO1-founded worlds with heritable fidelity, composite kept at 3,000 ticks > fixed MED.

2. REDUNDANCY AS A ROUTE ABOVE THE THRESHOLD. (F3 limit: one hand-built composite with no redundancy)
   - Plant composites with duplicated critical segments (2 copies of the compare-branch), measure the error threshold
     shift (ruler: redundant_pairs), and test whether evolved composites acquire redundancy at LOW mutation.
   - Directive anti-triviality dimension "robustness under perturbation" made causal.

3. C3 MECHANISM DEPENDENCY, NOW TESTABLE. (C3 NOT_TESTABLE for want of composites)
   - At a maintaining rate (LOW / VLOW) or under the best F5-type schedule, re-run A-present vs A-ablated vs sham with
     A = ECHO and B = COND_ONE, from KO1-type precursors (the only distance at which crossings occurred: 2 in 60 KO1 worlds).
   - Equal-search-budget control: same exposure without A.

4. MULTI-STEP REACHABILITY BEYOND DISTANCE 1. (A3: geometry is predictive only at distance 1; DP/F1: 0 crossings from
   >= 2 steps in 120 worlds)
   - A two-step neighbourhood scorer (exhaustive for SUB x SUB on critical windows, sampled for mixed operators), frozen
     as a predictor, tested on KO2 founders under a maintaining schedule.

5. UPTAKE ECONOMICS: dose-matched two-component test. (D2: blocking imports into LDIR carriers raises origins 2-3x;
   blocking imports into non-LDIR organisms lowers them; block-all is their sum)
   - The arms blocked very different doses (disclosed). Next, a dose-matched block: revert the same NUMBER of imports
     per class, chosen at random. Also measure precursor state directly (one-step distance to FUNC) instead of the
     LDIR marker.
   - Then: does the two-component balance predict where the net block-all effect appears (SOUP, budget 384)? That is a
     frozen out-of-sample prediction the UF2 ratio rule failed.

6. COMPOSITE-TASK PAYMENT ON EVERY INPUT CLASS. (F2 rejected half-credit as the cause; it did lengthen persistence:
   last composite census >= 1,700 in 6/20 vs 2/20)
   - Lower priority. Payment structure is a modifier, not the bottleneck. Keep paired-halves payment available
     (tools/paired_pay.py) for item 3.

7. ABANDON / DEPRIORITIZE

   - Long-horizon accumulation runs at MED mutation in this substrate (E1b): they can only produce minimal capabilities.
