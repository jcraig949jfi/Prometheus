0. SUMMARY
-----
Mandate: review three instrument repairs adversarially, then 48 h of autonomous
experiments on how hereditary information, reproduction and computation arise,
persist and interact in an artificial-life substrate of 64-byte Z80-like tapes.

Verdict:
- All three repairs survived after one was fixed: an independent reviewer found a
  real defect in the lineage repair.
- Six mechanism-level results are CAUSALLY_CONFIRMED or REPRODUCED on unseen
  populations.
- 16 frozen predictions were falsified and reported as such.
- Two of my own mechanism claims were retracted.
- One design flaw was found and amended: seeds shared across cells.

Strongest single result: horizontal uptake is BOTH a route to the first
replicator AND a net suppressor of replicator origin.
- Blocking it raised origination three times: 84 vs 61, 83 vs 60, 63 vs 46
  (paired seeds).
- The effect holds only in one substrate, and its boundary is now measured.

1. WHAT WAS BUILT (all committed before the data it measures)
-----
Kernel repairs (prometheus/z80atlas; opt-in switches, defaults byte-identical):
- DEF-008 PROVENANCE lineage. The VM now tracks the pre-execution origin of every
  written byte, multi-hop, through copy ops and register-A moves.
- DEF-009 PAIRED init: every init slot consumes the same RNG draws.
- DEF-010 'written' replication. A position counts only if its material origin
  is the same tape position.

Byte-identity of defaults was verified on two independent checks:
- 478 full-output cases, from the reviewer's own harness;
- the golden v1 replay.

The repairs were merged to main 2c6072c9d.

Measurement instruments (campaign tools; each tested for hook invariance by
comparing end-state hashes with and without the hook):
- DualWorld: both lineage rulers on one trajectory.
- FUNC: a tape alone lays down >= 0.9 L window bytes whose origin is its own
  byte at the same position.
- HeredityWorld: founder tags per byte; ASSEMBLY birth classes; knockout-critical
  sets; single-source reconstructions.
- OriginWorld: eager tags for mutation / self-construction / uptake / self-move;
  dissection of the first-FUNC event with per-byte reversion.
- CompWorld: competence x FUNC.
- UptakeBlockWorld: a campaign-level physics ablation that reverts partner bytes
  imported into an organism's own tape.

Procedure for every block:
- prereg section written;
- plan generated deterministically and sha256-hashed;
- analysis script committed BEFORE the runs;
- runs executed from a pinned code copy.

-----
2. WHY IT MATTERS
-----
The repaired rulers let us separate:
- material inheritance from resemblance;
- paired from unpaired comparisons;
- real copying from zero-filler artefacts.

The questions are where replicators come from, whether heredity can be assembled
from several sources, and how computation and reproduction interact. These map
onto Prometheus's interest in compositional mechanisms.

-----
3. REVIEWS OF THE REPAIRS (two independent adversarial reviewers)
-----
Review A (implementation): FAIL as shipped.
- The provenance rule was one-hop. Bytes copied from the window were always
  credited to the target, even when the writer had written them earlier in the
  same execution.
- Effect in short runs: 335/540 COPY, 337/938 PARTIAL and 385/1,085 PAIR
  'capture' labels were wrong.
- The 'written' rule passed a 6-byte tape that sweeps scratch zeros over the
  window.

Disposition of Review A:
- Repaired with multi-hop origin tracking, plus 8 regression tests.
- Validation added.
- The 478/478 identity check was re-run and passed.

Review B (scientific validity): SOUND_WITH_LIMITS. Limits recorded and disclosed:
- PROVENANCE measures material ancestry, NOT parentage. A 2-way label cannot
  represent multi-parent children; 13-16% of random-world births execute bytes of
  two origins.
- PAIRED init gives no variance reduction: the paired/independent ratio was
  0.977-1.318, and RNG streams split at tick 0-40.
- 'written' passes sterile near-copies and rejects prefix and shifted replicators.

Review B's k-generation functional test was adopted as an extra measurement.

5. RESULTS (exact numbers; class in brackets)
-----
5.1 Baseline (W1 v2, 3,240 runs)

Physics vs measurement:
- PROVENANCE does not change dynamics under IMPLICIT pressure (100/100 identical
  end hashes). It does under MINIMAL_CRITERION (20/20 differ).
- PAIRED is a no-op without transplants (100/100).

What survives the corrected rulers:
- SR-level statistics: SR_res vs SR_prov differ on 0.022% of 1,581,755 births.
- Spontaneity: 6/7 cells. BYTECODE32 gave 0/150 (historical 2/200).
- Sustained fraction: 34.8% vs historical 39.4%.

What does not survive:
- Lineage roots: 22-26% of living organisms change genetic root between rulers in
  mixing worlds.
- Historical G6a ('160/160 built by copy'): already withdrawn by my seat on
  2026-09-29. Its corrected form (64% copy-born) reproduced on fresh seeds: 38/45
  populations.

5.2 Origin of replicators [REPRODUCED]
Discovery set: 21 populations. Confirmation set: 85 unseen populations, each a
distinct seed.
- The completing event is a single necessary change in 54/75 in-place origins.
- The precursor copies 0 own bytes in 60/85.
- The carrier was built and assisted by others' copying in 56/85.
- Causes: mutation 53, UPTAKE 13, born-assembly 10, self-move 7,
  self-construct 2.
- LDIR writes the window in 85/85.
- Replay of the discovery origins: 27/27 byte-identical.

5.3 Two-fragment complementation [CAUSALLY_CONFIRMED, general]
Two non-replicating fragments are combined: a writer of LD T,n and a carrier of
LDIR.
- They yield a FUNC child in which each byte set is necessary and single-source
  reconstructions fail.
- Origin worlds: A 95/100, A+B 99/100, B 6/100, none 8/100.
- When B is present it supplies the decisive LDIR byte: 64/99.

Ablations and generality:
- Complete-overwrite physics abolishes it (97/100 runs with none).
- Ablating target material abolishes it (39/40 -> 1/40).
- 3 confound-free variants (writer holds no LDIR and builds its bytes by
  computation): first event two-source in 120/120.
- 3 world models: GRID 32-38 vs 0, GRAPH 39 vs 0, SOUP 38 vs 0 (PARTIAL vs COPY
  discordant pairs).

5.4 Capability persists without lineage [REPRODUCED]
In 100 fresh worlds:
- function is alive at the end in 98/100;
- the first assembled lineage is alive in 36/100;
- no assembly lineage is alive at all in 42/100.

5.5 Operator-matched completion [post-hoc -> CAUSALLY_CONFIRMED by N1]
Precursor neighbourhoods:
- needle precursors: median 4 substitution routes, 0 segment-move routes;
- move-rich precursors: 870-2,067 move routes.

Intervention: the same 55 precursors re-placed in new worlds, mutation VLOW vs
HIGH.
- Needle: 0.14 -> 0.78.
- Move-rich: 0.98 -> 1.00, with 63/63 completions at VLOW non-mutational.
- Interaction: 0.62 [0.50, 0.73].

Fault line: a fragment can be 0/16,320 substitutions from a replicator yet
8/50,512 segment moves away.

5.6 Uptake: creator and net suppressor [CAUSALLY_CONFIRMED x3, bounded]
Blocking imports into the own tape changes origins (paired seeds):
- U: 84 vs 61, p 0.018;
- U2 on fresh seeds: 83 vs 60, p 0.011;
- U3: 63 vs 46, p 0.027.
It is not a survival artefact: extinction was higher in U and lower in U2.

Mechanism (UF, 99,595 uptake events):
- imports break precursors more than they make them: 4,124 vs 3,194;
- they destroy working replicators far more than they create them: 2,363 vs 418.

U3 showed the origin gain comes only from imports by NON-replicating organisms.

Boundary:
- No effect in SOUP (37 vs 35) or at budget 384 (124 vs 119).
- There, imports' FUNC loss/gain ratio is 0.24 and 0.77, against 1.63 where the
  effect exists (post-hoc).

5.7 Computation x reproduction [CAUSALLY_CONFIRMED]
Contingent payment raises competent self-replicators:
- conflict repair: 10/300 vs 0/300 (this was historical P6, previously
  underpowered);
- ECHO: 20/150 vs 0 OFF vs 3 SHUFFLED;
- reproduced at 15/300 and 41/300.

Repair is in place, inside the damaging lineage: 16/25. Cross-lineage repair:
0/25.

BUDGET COUPLING:
- In 17/41 fresh competent machines, the copy-length operand is competence-
  critical because an unbounded copy starves the task of the shared 256-step
  budget.
- Causal on a specimen: doubling the budget restores competence.
- It vanishes as the budget grows: 0.52 / 0.50 / 0.00 at budget 192 / 256 / 384.

Architecture selection:
- Payment favours compute-first over copy-then-compute at MED mutation: pairs
  23/24 and 24/24; 4 x 4 panel 14 of 15 pairings, p 0.0005.
- At HIGH mutation it is indeterminate: one pair each way; panel 3 vs 2, 5 ties.

7. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH
-----
Establishes, in this substrate and these physics:
- the origin pathway;
- fragment complementation;
- capability-without-lineage;
- operator-matched completion;
- the payment effect on architecture at MED;
- budget coupling as a budget phenomenon;
- uptake's dual role in GRID WELL_MIXED at budget 256.

Does NOT establish:
- parentage (the rulers measure material ancestry);
- that uptake suppression generalises (it does not: SOUP, budget 384);
- any HIGH-mutation architecture rule;
- behaviour beyond 500 ticks;
- anything about other engines.

Rulers are single-execution. Knockout misses redundancy: 2/85 machines carry two
LDIRs. The FUNC criterion rejects prefix replicators that do work under partial
physics.

9. QUESTIONS FOR THE REVIEWER (please try to break these)
-----
Q1. Is the eager origin classifier sound? It attributes the first FUNC to the
    single change that made it FUNC. Could FUNC's single-execution criterion
    misplace the true origin in time?
Q2. The uptake ablation is itself a physics change. Is "blocking imports" too
    blunt to attribute the effect to scrambling, given that U3 shows only
    non-replicators' imports matter?
Q3. Complementation needs target bytes to survive into the child. Is
    ENDOGENOUS_PARTIAL physics so permissive that 'assembly' is trivial, and the
    result uninformative for any engine without partial writes?
Q4. Budget coupling vanishes at budget 384, but shared critical bytes persist
    (31/41) through a "damage" component. Is the budget/damage split real, or a
    classifier artefact of comparing b vs 2b?
Q5. 16 falsified predictions and 2 retractions in 48 h. Is the preregistration
    discipline catching errors, or is the prediction quality too low to trust the
    confirmed items?
