# BEL-48H -- Next experiments (durable queue; ranked)

Each item names the claim it would move, the design, the artifact that proves it done, and its blocker.

1. **Operator-matched completion, predictive test (CL-17).** Claim: move-rich precursors are completed by moves
   (uptake / self-move), needle precursors by point mutation. Design: transplant 30 W6 precursors of each kind into
   random backgrounds under three physics: mutation only (EXTERNAL reproduction, no endogenous copying), endogenous
   copying with mutation OFF, both; endpoint time-to-first-FUNC and completing cause. Prediction: needle precursors
   complete only where mutation is on; move-rich ones complete with mutation OFF. Done = frozen prereg + result table.
   Blocker: none.
2. **Architecture selection, general form (CL-16).** Two specimen pairs so far (W5 block 3; W6 block 4 X). Next: a
   panel of 10 budget-coupled and 10 separated machines from W6 block 3 K1, round-robin competitions, ON/OFF x
   mutation 3 levels; does the regime that payment favours flip at a predictable mutation rate? Blocker: none.
3. **Uptake as a route under its own pressure.** If W6 block 4 U shows routes substitutable, ask whether worlds with
   uptake BLOCKED reach the same replicator ARCHITECTURES (critical-set shapes) or different ones (route-dependent
   outcomes). Design: compare the dominant machines' critical-set signatures between arms. Blocker: U results.
4. **Redundancy-aware criticality ruler.** Single-byte knockout misses redundant elements (2/85 origins carry two
   LDIRs). Build pairwise knockout for flagged machines and re-count 'necessary steps'. Blocker: none (instrument work).
5. **Budget coupling as an evolutionary pressure.** Vary the execution budget (192 / 256 / 384) in the W4 M2 setting:
   prediction -- the BUDGET_COUPLED share of competent machines falls as the budget grows (the coupling is a budget
   artefact) or stays (it is favoured for another reason). Blocker: none.
6. **Rearrangement-aware reachability ruler for the kernel.** Add segment-move neighbourhoods beside geometry.scan
   (fault-line CL-08). Kernel change with tests; historical outputs unchanged (new function only). Blocker: owner = me.
7. **Complementation in other engines.** Packet to Nestor (NPE) / Daedalus (SFE): the single-source-reconstruction test
   (BEL_48H_MECHANISM_DISCOVERY.md M1 field 10). Blocker: their bandwidth.
8. **Seed independence audit of historical campaigns.** Every historical pooled count built on SEED_BASE + lane*1e9 + k
   (grounding, coupling, multi-day) should be re-stated per distinct seed. Blocker: none (analysis only).
9. **Split CL-20 into its two components.** Uptake's net suppression has (a) imports that break other organisms'
   precursors (UF) and (b) self-inflicted wrap-around imports of LD T,0xA0 staging copiers (post-hoc, +17 of +46 origins).
   Design: three arms on paired seeds -- normal; block all imports; block only imports executed by a FUNC organism's own
   copy loop (staging self-damage). Prediction under (b): the third arm recovers most of the 0xA0 gain. Blocker: none.
