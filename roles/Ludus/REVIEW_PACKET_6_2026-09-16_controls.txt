    "admitted by depth", never "qualified".
  - The default n=250 uniform sample put NIM345 at gap(4)=0.200, exactly on

    uniformly. The exhaustive 517-state reading (0.240) is what this packet
    judges on, and LUDUS-34 makes exhaustive-when-small and stratification
    the instrument's default.
