3. WHAT WAS CHANGED, AND WHAT WAS PRESERVED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- ludus/bench/worlds.py MartianDice: state is now (tanks, rays, humans,
  cows, chickens, dead); rays are claimable on every roll; a roll with
  nothing claimable moves its unclaimable dice to `dead` so the state is a
  forced end that scores. rules_state = "AUDITED_BY_SEAT". Docstring
  carries the source and both corrections.
- MartianDiceRecon: the 2026-08-27 behaviour, kept verbatim as a class
  (name MARTIAN_DICE_RECON_2026-08-27), NOT in ALL_WORLDS. It reproduces
  the committed column exactly (8,555 states, EV 2.093806), which is how
  the diff below is known to be the rule change and nothing else.
- ludus/bench/verify.py check_martian: rewritten from the sheet for the
  6-tuple (pot formula; counts never decrease; at most one earthling type
  per transition and never one already claimed; dead dice only in a forced
  end). New ledger hook: rules_audited comes ONLY from rules_audit.json.
- ludus/atlas/transfer_matrix.json: MARTIAN_DICE recomputed (all 10
  circuits, both axes); the old column preserved under
  MARTIAN_DICE_RECON_2026-08-27 with a superseded note.
- ludus/atlas/rules_fidelity.json: committed from this run (4 verified
  internally, MARTIAN_DICE rules_audited=true with the source hash; 17

- Annotations (not edits): RULES_AUDIT.md marks with citations;
  roles/Ludus/CYCLE_002_stochastic_stopping.md head; ludus/stopworlds.py
  docstring (its MartianDice is the reconstruction and stays that way).

Reading, in failure-shape form: once rays can be re-claimed, the claim
decision stops being where the difficulty lives (you can always fix the
ray deficit later), and the difficulty moves to WHEN to stop. r0004 and
r0007 (two STOP circuits) go from "kills" (0.23, 0.87) to near-ceiling
(0.97) on the same world with the rules fixed. Kills are cells; two cells
of this column were facts about a wrong rule.

5. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Establishes: every Martian Dice rule line has a quoted sentence from a
published sheet with a hash; two were wrong; the corrected world passes
its rewritten invariants and its column is recomputed with the old one
kept. The LUDUS-03 controls re-run on the corrected world: 25/25 as
predicted.

Does NOT establish: that the sheet is the current edition (metadata says
2011; the reconstruction may have been of a later printing -- no evidence
either way); that the audit is independent (one seat read one sheet and
wrote the correction; LUDUS-16's second implementation is the independent
instrument); that any circuit's maturity rung moves (LUDUS-36); anything
about FLIP7, INCAN_GOLD, CANT_STOP (still HYPOTHESIZED, LUDUS-15). W3 = 1
of 30 only if the operator accepts P3; otherwise it is 0 of 30 with one
seat-performed audit awaiting a tick.

Conflict of interest: the seat that reconstructed the rules wrongly is the
seat that audited them. The mitigation is that every mark quotes the
sheet, so a spot check is a one-minute read.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. WHAT WOULD FALSIFY THIS / WHAT TO STOP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- A different edition of the sheet (or the publisher's current FAQ) that
  says rays are NOT repeatable would reverse R1; the recon column is kept
  for exactly that case.
- If the operator rules P3 insufficient, the W3 count returns to 0 and
  the next action is a delegation to Herakles (LUDUS-16) rather than more
  seat audits.
- Stop signal: if the other three audits (LUDUS-15) also move rules, the
  entire pre-2026-09-16 matrix is a reconstruction artifact and cycle 002
  should be re-run in full rather than annotated.
