**Sources.** Wikidata (CC0) + Wikipedia (CC BY-SA). BoardGameGeek was rejected:
401 on every endpoint, now needs credentials. Multi-language Wikipedia was
measured and rejected: de covers 32 of 180 uncovered worlds, everything else
single digits, and each language would need its own non-English rule set.

**Source ceiling.** ~41% of catalogued worlds have no English Wikipedia article
and can never be classified beyond a Wikidata description. Reported as a
ceiling, not a backlog — every enrichable world has been enriched.

**Thirteen defects, eleven fixed.** Full table in `ATLAS_OF_WORLDS.md` §6 and
`REVIEW_PACKET_3_2026-09-01.md` §9. The ones worth remembering:

- `ELIMINATE` matched "removed from the game" with no subject, so *captured
  seeds* and *discarded cards* read as player elimination. Caught by sampling
  four rows before applying a repair that would have written
  `loss_shape=ELIMINATION` across 47 worlds with no player elimination at all.
- The `method` ladder was unenforced on the two paths that write most. A
  read-then-write race clobbered reviews while leaving `method='reviewed'` — rows
  *claimed* human provenance while holding machine values.
- Enrichment selected on `LENGTH(wp_extract) < 3000`; real articles are often
  shorter, so 216 worlds were re-fetched forever while 384 never-fetched ones
  starved. The backlog never moved: 621 → 621.
- `luck_factor` returned 0.35 when no randomness was found — a confident number
  for worlds where nothing had been observed.

**D13 remains OPEN:** `year_created` conflates a game's *setting* with its
creation date. Civilization is dated −4000, Commands & Colors: Ancients −3000.
This corrupts exactly the oldest-N ordering any historical claim would rest on.

## Phase 2 — Arena (executable worlds)

**Mandate:** stop optimising for breadth; convert catalogue rows into auditable
executable environments. §22 asked for a vertical slice to find where a
universal interface breaks.

Five worlds chosen to break it, each with externally known ground truth:
tic-tac-toe, Nim, Pig, rock-paper-scissors, Kuhn poker. 20/20 checks pass over
~19,830 seeded episodes.

**The interface broke on three of five.** `ApplyAction(player_id, action)`
failed immediately: Pig's die is nobody's action, RPS has no turn order, Kuhn's
observation is not the public state. Resolved with `current_player()` returning
a player id, `CHANCE`, or `SIMULTANEOUS` — the same shape OpenSpiel reached.

**Ground truth reproduced:** Nim's XOR theorem exactly (+1.000/−1.000), Kuhn at
−0.0535 against theoretical −1/18, the Pig die at 0.1679 against 1/6.

**Defect:** determinism failed on all five worlds because wall-clock timing sat
inside the compared payload. Instrumentation is not identity; `Replay` now
separates `to_json()` from `digest()`.

## Phase 3 — Epistemic layer (ontic vs observation)

**Mandate:** model existence, visibility, knowledge, uncertainty, information
flow, information loss, and what cannot be known.

Delivered `epistemic.py` (13-class information taxonomy, `InformationState`,
observational equivalence, `LawfulModel`, epistemic readiness ladder E0–E7),
`worlds_epistemic.py` (BALL_UNDER_COUCH with E0/E1/E2/E4/E5/E6 rungs and an
exact information set), `audit.py`, `test_epistemic.py`. 25/25 checks pass.

**`NOT_VISIBLE` is a sentinel distinct from `None` and from `DESTROYED`**, so "I
cannot see it", "it does not exist", and "the field is absent" are three facts.
Declaring UNKNOWN scores **+1 when genuinely unresolvable, 0 when resolvable** —
§16's requirement that correct refusal to collapse uncertainty counts as
success, made to pay.

**Defect: contradiction was scored as uncertainty.** An impossible observation
history yields an *empty* information set; the scorer read empty as "more than
one answer remains" and paid +1 for declaring UNKNOWN — rewarding humility about
a world that cannot exist. Resolution is now three-valued:
`KNOWN / UNCERTAIN / MODEL_CONTRADICTION`.

**Defect: a test passed for the wrong reason.** Test [6] asserted two
information sets *differ*; it passed because the second was empty. That is how
the contradiction defect was found. Caught by reading the detail column, not the
PASS.

**The audit was wrong twice before it was right.** v1 substring-matched `"cards"`
and fired on the public key `cards_dealt`; v2 matched the private *value* and
fired because the value was `0`, which appears in `"pot": [1, 1]`. Substring
matching cannot separate a leak from a coincidence. Replaced with a purely
differential test across 11 player-reachable channels.

**One leak stands:** the full `Replay` is an omniscient log naming chance
outcomes in plaintext. Added `Replay.redacted_for(player)`.

**Packet-4 F2 resolved.** `LawfulModel` searches the states an information set
*admits*; under perfect information it collapses to one root and reduces to
ordinary minimax. The oracle no longer needs privileged access.

## The standing methodological result

**Three predicted fixes in a row were wrong**, and a fourth was wrong within one
turn:

| iteration | prediction | reality |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | multi-language fallback is the highest-value change | a third of the value at twice the cost |
| 6 | enrichment "no cleverness compresses" | concurrency gave 5.3× within one turn |
| 8 | the determinism rule over-fires | it was *right*; deferring would have deleted correct answers |
| 9 | fix the determinism rule | the defect was in the randomness *vocabulary* |

Every correct diagnosis came from printing sampled rows and reading them, never
from reasoning about the system. Charter §35's cheating assumption, turned
inward: the seat's flattering account of its own state was wrong more often than
it was right.

**Prefer NULL to a fabricated cell.** Repeatedly the right fix was to *withdraw*
a value rather than replace it. A coverage grid is only worth reading if its
cells mean something.
