0. FAILURE MODES, FIRST
====================================================================

F1. MY PRECISION GATE FAILED ON THE FIRST PRODUCTION ATTEMPT, BY 0.0003.
    The frozen G3 gate is median row TV <= 0.010 and p95 <= 0.020 between two
    independent full-space kernel estimates. At the frozen n = 12,000:

        median row TV   0.010333   limit 0.010   FAIL
        p95    row TV   0.016333   limit 0.020   PASS
        p99    row TV   0.018833
        max    row TV   0.023250   (reported, never a criterion)
        edge-presence disagreements   0
        escaped valid structural states  0

    The run stopped there, by design, before computing any current, stationary
    distribution, entropy production, cycle affinity or attribution. Preserved
    as RESULT_FULL_n12000_GATE_FAIL.json. Cost: about 100 minutes of
    measurement.

F2. THE CAUSE WAS THE MARGIN I CHOSE, NOT THE FIT.
    The pilot fitted median TV = 1.0775/sqrt(n) on a 103-state positional subset
    (every 20th state). The full space gives 1.1320/sqrt(n) -- the pilot
    constant was 5.1% LOW, because a stride-20 positional subset
    under-represents the rows with larger support. I then sized n = 12,000
    against a predicted median of 0.00984, leaving 1.7% margin. A 5% error in
    the constant was therefore fatal. A positional subset is unbiased in
    position and NOT unbiased in support size; that is the lesson, and it is
    mine.

F3. THE COUNTS WERE WRITTEN AFTER THE GATES, SO THE FAILURE DESTROYED THE
COMPUTE.
    The first attempt's transition counts were only persisted after the gate
    block, so the gate failure discarded roughly 100 minutes of measurement that
    could have been pooled with a second batch. Counts are now written
    immediately after each kernel is measured. This is an engineering defect
    that cost time rather than correctness, and it is reported because it
    changed what the second attempt had to do.

F4. THE METROPOLIS CONTROL DID NOT HIT ITS OWN TARGET (found before production).
    I used plain Metropolis acceptance, min(1, tgt_j/tgt_i), with a
    uniform-neighbour proposal. On an irregular graph that proposal is
    ASYMMETRIC, so detailed balance fails and the chain converges somewhere
    other than its declared measure -- caught converging to 0.0625 where uniform
    is 0.0833. Corrected to the Hastings ratio min(1, tgt_j*k_i / (tgt_i*k_j)).
    A control whose only job is to read zero, silently missing its own target,
    would have produced a confident wrong answer about the instrument.

F5. THE ANALYSIS TOOLS ASSUMED A CLOSED STATE SET (found before production).
    On a truncated subset a leaking row is not a stochastic matrix, which made
    the two stationary solvers disagree by L1 0.026 and made the Metropolis
    control raise. Escape folding is now explicit and is a NO-OP on the
    production space, which is proved closed. The defect was in how I exercised
    the tools and would have stayed invisible had I only ever run them on the
    full space.

8. THE COST OF THE WRONG MICROSCOPE
====================================================================

Required by the brief to stay visible, and to say more than "the tests
improved". What each inadequate test would have made us BELIEVE:

  V0 through V0.2 measured a marginal length slope with no geometry control. It
  failed three times and we believed the grammar had a length ratchet. Acting on
  that belief I revised the grammar twice. THE BELIEF WAS FALSE: a
  symmetric-by-construction joint walk reproduces the same slope. The second
  revision introduced a REAL tape ratchet that cost another whole pass to find.
  FALSE POSITIVE -- geometry mistaken for authored direction, and the repair did
  the damage.

  V0.3 and V0.4 then tested marginal drift against proper geometry nulls and
  found nothing surviving correction. Had the sequence stopped there we would
  have believed the Foundry was dynamically neutral. THE BELIEF WOULD HAVE BEEN
  FALSE: V0.5 found authored circulation with almost no marginal displacement,
  because detailed balance is far stronger than zero drift. FALSE NEGATIVE --
  authored direction hidden behind flat marginals.

  A third failure was live in this pass and the gate caught it: an underpowered
  full-space kernel would have let us believe the truncated finding did not
  generalise. At n = 12,000 the measurement was not good enough to tell, and the
  design refused to let it answer. That is the first time in this sequence an
  instrument stopped itself before producing a believable wrong number.
