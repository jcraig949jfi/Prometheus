3. PAIRED TAPE-TRANSITION SYMMETRY: THE PROOF
====================================================================

The structural state space is small enough to enumerate exactly: tape_words in
{16,32,64,128,256,512,1024,2048,4096} and genome_length in [1, tape/4] gives
2,044 valid states. Nothing here is sampled.

PRIMITIVE PROPOSAL PROBABILITY. config_perturbation is chosen with weight
0.0833333333, then one of six fields uniformly, then up or down with probability
one half:

    q(t -> 2t) = q(2t -> t) = 0.0833333333 / 6 / 2 = 0.0069444444

Equal by construction, and equal in both V0.4 and NC5.

REALISED EDGES. For every state, the only permitted causes of rejection are a
published bound or manifest validity. Counting the cases where a shrink was
blocked even though the genome FITS in the destination:

    grammar v0.3 :  510 of 1020 state pairs   <- the defect V0.3 measured
    grammar v0.4 :    0 of 1020 state pairs
    NC5          :    0 of 1020 state pairs

LIVE-OPERATOR CROSS-CHECK. The model is checked against the code, not merely
described: for a grid of states the real op_config_perturbation is sampled 400
times and every valid destination must be observed and every invalid one must
never be observed. Passes at every grid point.

Every tape edge in the v0.4 graph satisfies p(A->B) == p(B->A) to 1e-15
(test_tape_edges_are_symmetric_under_v0_4).

====================================================================
4. NC5 -- DEFINITION
====================================================================

A joint reversible manifest walk over (genome_length, tape_words). No genome
content, no VM, no probes, no fitness, no world.

Coordinate selection is computed, not asserted: a configuration field is
included only if its value changes whether a structural proposal is valid, and
only tape_words does. n_regs, tick_budget, out_cap, code_writable and persist
are excluded on that test.

NC5 uses the published bounds, the grammar's own step sizes, the same validity
constraints and the same no-op rejection semantics. Its primitive paired moves
are symmetric by construction: the length kernel is replaced by
p_sym(d) = (p(d) + p(-d))/2, and tape doubling and halving are equiprobable.
NC5 inherits NO asymmetric V0.4 rule. Every unit of drift NC5 shows is therefore
produced by the reflecting bounds and by the coupled validity constraint that a
genome must fit on its tape, and by nothing else.

  (b) v0.4 still has 16,172 asymmetric directed-edge pairs, and every one of

authorizes exactly one grammar change and it was spent on the tape rule. Note
that unequal edge probabilities do not by themselves imply drift -- the expected
length displacement per mutation is near zero by weight construction -- and G3
below tests the drift directly against NC5, which is symmetric. The honest
summary is: the length kernel is NOT reversible, and its irreversibility did not
produce a detectable directional effect at these horizons.
