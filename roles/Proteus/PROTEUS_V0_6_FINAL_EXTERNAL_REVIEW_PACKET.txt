WHAT PROTEUS IS. A deterministic factory for small, semantically sterile
computational organisms. A "player" is a data object -- a bounded tape machine
manifest whose genome is a list of 32-bit words, interpreted 4 words to an
instruction with opcode = word mod 25. Every word sequence is a valid program,
so there is no parse failure and no authored notion of a well-formed organism.
Players contain no natural language, no LLM, and no knowledge of any world.
Proteus does not run worlds, does not select, and is not its own neutral
operator.

WHAT V0.6 ASKED. Whether the MUTATION MACHINERY is dynamically neutral over the
complete space of valid structural states -- not whether it drifts on average,
but whether it satisfies detailed balance. V0.5 had found circulation on a
truncated 124-state kernel; V0.6 asks whether that survives on the full space
with a properly powered live measurement.

K. ATTRIBUTION -- THE MECHANISM, NOT ONLY THE OPERATOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------

The single most important thing in this section is a distinction that the
counterfactual table alone would have hidden, and that I got wrong in my own
first reading of it.

THERE ARE TWO DIFFERENT QUESTIONS, AND THEY HAVE DIFFERENT ANSWERS.

  (a) HOW MUCH CURRENT FLOWS?  Measured by entropy production and total |J|,
      both of which are FLUX-WEIGHTED -- they weight each edge's rate asymmetry
      by how much probability actually crosses it.

  (b) IS THE CHAIN REVERSIBLE AT ALL?  Measured by cycle affinity, which is
      pi-free and unweighted. Kolmogorov's criterion: a chain is reversible if
      and only if every cycle affinity vanishes.

Reweighting the authored operators moves (a) enormously and (b) barely at all:

    zeroing unreachable_removal     entropy production      -89.7%
                                    cycle-affinity spread    -2.5%

    THEREFORE: authored operator weighting controls the MAGNITUDE of the
    current. It does NOT control its EXISTENCE. Under every weight vector
    tested the chain remains non-reversible.

WHICH OPERATORS CONTRIBUTE THE MAGNITUDE. Exact offline reweighting from
recorded per-operator destination counts, with state space, published bounds
and validity rules IDENTICAL in every variant (full table in section L).
unreachable_removal dominates the flux-weighted current: zeroing it removes
75.5% of total |J| and 89.7% of entropy production while that operator carries
only 8.8% of outflux -- grossly disproportionate to its traffic.

WHY NO OPERATOR CAN BE BLAMED FOR THE EXISTENCE OF IRREVERSIBILITY. A
per-operator test was run and came back VACUOUS, which is itself the finding
and is reported rather than deleted. Restricting the kernel to any single
operator yields ZERO evaluable cycles out of 7,063, because a cycle affinity
requires both directions of every edge and THE OPERATORS ARE STRICTLY
DIRECTIONAL: insertion only lengthens, deletion only shortens. No single
operator can close a cycle. Irreversibility is therefore a property of the
MIXTURE of complementary directional operators, not a defect localisable to one
of them -- which is exactly why reweighting moves magnitude without removing
the phenomenon.

WHICH REGIONS CARRY IT. Interior 70% of sigma and 1,037 of 1,494 material
edges; tape_max 29% and 418 edges; the three remaining boundary regions
together under 1.5% and 39 edges.

WHICH TRANSITIONS DOMINATE ENTROPY PRODUCTION. Genome-length transitions at
fixed tape size. The largest material edges are all single- or
few-instruction length changes.

WHETHER ZERO-REVERSE TRANSITIONS DOMINATE. No. THERE ARE NONE, on either
kernel. The circulation is rate asymmetry on bidirectional edges.

IS IT VALIDITY/REJECTION MECHANICS OR STORAGE BOUNDARIES? LARGELY NOT.
Rejections under this grammar return the state unchanged with a recorded reason
(published_boundary, manifest_validity), which is a SELF-LOOP. Self-loops alter
the stationary distribution but cannot carry current between distinct states,
and a cycle affinity never traverses one. Consistently: zero one-way edges, and
70% of sigma in the interior. The tape_max region carrying 29% is the one place
a storage boundary plausibly participates.

IS IT AUTHORED, OR IS IT PUBLISHED GEOMETRY? THE MAGNITUDE IS DEMONSTRABLY
AUTHORED. Holding geometry exactly fixed and varying only the authored weight
vector moves sigma by an order of magnitude (1.4010e-02 down to 1.4418e-03, or
up to 1.5853e-02). Geometry is constant across those variants, so geometry
cannot explain a swing of that size. This is what rules out
FULL_SPACE_CURRENT_ATTRIBUTED_TO_GEOMETRY and licenses the headline.

WHERE THE EVIDENCE RUNS OUT, AND I AM NOT CLOSING IT. The underlying rate-ratio
asymmetry -- the part that survives every reweighting -- is NOT attributed. With
the dominant operator zeroed, sigma is still 1.4418e-03: roughly 8e14 times the
reversible reference, and 19 times the K_A/K_B measurement disagreement in sigma
(7.65e-05), so it is not a noise floor. Whether that residue is authored
proposal asymmetry common to the directional operators, or a consequence of the
published bounds interacting with those operators, CANNOT be separated by the
four preregistered variants.

    THEREFORE, EXPLICITLY:
    The MAGNITUDE of the current is ATTRIBUTED to authored operator mechanics,
    by direct causal demonstration with geometry held fixed. This licenses the
    headline on its own.
    The EXISTENCE of the irreversibility -- the residue no reweighting removes
    -- is recorded as
        FULL_SPACE_CURRENT_SOURCE_UNRESOLVED
    as a scoped sub-finding, and is not resolved by a plausible story.

No mechanism was repaired. The specimen is intact.

   insertion_deletion_equal (-4.8% alone), the honest summary is that
   insertion/deletion balance is a second-order lever whose sign depends on
   what else is present.

3. NO REWEIGHTING ON THE SAME SUPPORT ELIMINATES THE CURRENT. This is the
   preregistered question of brief section 6, and the answer is NO for all four
   variants, on both the flux-weighted and the pi-free statistic.

for it, and forbids manufacturing a post-hoc test. Here is the evidence and
the honest limit.

WHAT IS ESTABLISHED.
  * The violation is large in its own units: 11.3% of two-way flux is net
    imbalance, and sigma is 1.40e-02 nats per mutation step.
  * It is reproducible: 0.5% agreement in sigma across independent kernels, and
    PERFECT sign agreement on the 1,341 shared material edges.
  * It is systematic, not diffuse: it runs along the genome-length axis.
  * It survives every authored reweighting tested.
  * It coexists with NO replicated marginal directional drift (V0.3-V0.5, 350
    coordinate-cohort hypotheses under global step-down Holm, zero global
    survivors).

WHY THE ONE ARM THAT COULD SPEAK TO CONSEQUENCE CANNOT.
The matched long-run trajectory comparison ran 2,000,000 steps of the active
kernel against the additive reversible reference:

    active                mean genome length 137.662, mean log2 tape 10.324
    reversible reference  mean genome length 125.783, mean log2 tape 10.199

An 11.9-instruction gap looks like exactly the structural preference the
question is about. IT CANNOT BE READ THAT WAY, FOR TWO INDEPENDENT REASONS.

  (i) BY CONSTRUCTION THE TWO CHAINS SHARE A STATIONARY DISTRIBUTION. The
      additive reference is built to satisfy detailed balance with respect to
      the ACTIVE kernel's own pi. Its stationary mean length is therefore
      identical to the active kernel's, exactly. Any observed difference is a
      finite-time effect, not a preference. A reference that shares pi can
      never exhibit a stationary structural difference no matter how large the
      circulation -- so this arm was incapable of answering the question as
      built, and that is a design limitation I am reporting, not excusing.

  (ii) NEITHER TRAJECTORY IS CONVERGED. Both sit 0.085-0.092 total variation
      from pi after 2 million steps, against a relaxation time near 1,432 steps
      implied by the spectral gap of 6.9836e-04. With one trajectory per arm
      and no replicate seeds, the 11.9-instruction gap cannot be separated from
      trajectory-to-trajectory variability.

WHAT WOULD BE REQUIRED, AND WHY IT WAS NOT DONE. Answering the question needs a
reference that is neutral in the RIGHT sense -- one whose stationary
distribution is NOT inherited from the active kernel -- run over horizons
matched to an actual campaign, with replicate seeds. That is a new
preregistered experiment. Building it now would mean choosing a reference and a
horizon AFTER seeing the current map, which is the precise failure mode this
programme exists to avoid. Replicate trajectory seeds are also outside the
frozen surface the completion directive imposes.

    THEREFORE: OPERATIONAL_SIGNIFICANCE_NOT_YET_ADJUDICATED

STATED PLAINLY FOR THE ENGINEERING DECISION. V0.6 establishes that the mutation
kernel carries a real, reproducible, authored circulation that no reweighting
removes. It does NOT establish whether that circulation would bias the
structural composition of a population over a realistic campaign. Both of the
following remain live and this packet deliberately does not choose between them:

    microscopic circulation with negligible observable consequence
    circulation capable of producing meaningful structural preference

Detailed-balance violation does not automatically make a player generator
unusable. Zero marginal drift does not make it neutral. The decision is
external, and V0.6's job was to make it an informed one.

====================================================================
N. COMPLETE FAILURE CHRONOLOGY
====================================================================

Required to stay visible. V0.6 did not work on the first attempt and this
section is part of the result, not an apology attached to it.

N1. THE n=12,000 PRECISION GATE FAILED, BY 0.0003.
    Frozen criterion: median row TV <= 0.010 and p95 <= 0.020 between two
    independent full-space kernel estimates. At the frozen n = 12,000:

        median row TV   0.010333   limit 0.010   FAIL
        p95    row TV   0.016333   limit 0.020   PASS
        max    row TV   0.023250   (reported, never a criterion)
        edge-presence disagreements   0
        escaped valid structural states   0

    The run stopped there by design, BEFORE computing any current, stationary
    distribution, entropy production, cycle affinity or attribution. Preserved
    under its own identity as RESULT_FULL_n12000_GATE_FAIL.json with
    classification_note FULL_SPACE_KERNEL_UNDERPOWERED. Cost: about 100 minutes.

N2. THE CAUSE WAS THE MARGIN I CHOSE, NOT THE FIT.
    The pilot fitted median TV = 1.0775/sqrt(n) on a 103-state positional subset
    (every 20th state). The full space gives 1.1320/sqrt(n): the pilot constant
    was 5.1% LOW, because a stride-20 positional subset under-represents rows
    with larger support. I then sized n = 12,000 against a predicted median of
    0.00984, leaving 1.7% margin, so a 5% error in the constant was fatal. A
    positional subset is unbiased in position and NOT unbiased in support size.
    That is the lesson and it is mine.

N3. THE THRESHOLD WAS NOT LOWERED. EFFORT WAS RAISED.
    Median <= 0.010 and p95 <= 0.020 stand exactly as committed. What changed
    was n, 12,000 -> 20,000. This cannot be outcome-motivated by construction,
    not by promise: the precision gate is evaluated BEFORE any substantive
    quantity is computed, and the process exited at exactly that point, so the
    only thing ever observed from the full space at that moment was a precision
    statistic. Recorded in PREREG_ADDENDUM_2026-09-03.md and committed BEFORE
    the second attempt started, in aafce2bd2. (Interim Packet No. 2 cited
    d5185d092 for this; that SHA was rebased away and is NOT reachable from
    origin/main. See F3 in the verdict ledger.)

N4. COUNTS WERE PERSISTED AFTER THE GATE, SO THE FAILURE DESTROYED THE COMPUTE.
    The first attempt's transition counts were written only after the gate
    block, so the gate failure discarded ~100 minutes that could have been
    pooled with a second batch. Counts are now written immediately after each
    kernel is measured. An engineering defect that cost time, not correctness,
    and reported because it changed what the second attempt had to do.

N5. THE METROPOLIS CONTROL MISSED ITS OWN TARGET (found before production).
    I used plain acceptance min(1, tgt_j/tgt_i) with a uniform-neighbour
    proposal. On an irregular graph that proposal is ASYMMETRIC, so detailed
    balance fails and the chain converges somewhere other than its declared
    measure: caught converging to 0.0625 where uniform is 0.0833. Corrected to
    the Hastings ratio min(1, tgt_j*k_i/(tgt_i*k_j)). A control whose only job
    is to read zero, silently missing its own target, would have produced a
    confident wrong answer about the instrument itself. In production it now
    hits its declared uniform target to 1.08e-19.

N6. THE ANALYSIS TOOLS ASSUMED A CLOSED STATE SET (found before production).
    On a truncated subset a leaking row is not a stochastic matrix, which made
    the two stationary solvers disagree by L1 0.026 and made the Metropolis
    control raise. Escape folding is now explicit, and is a NO-OP on the
    production space, which is proved closed. The defect was in how I exercised
    the tools and would have stayed invisible had I only ever run them on the
    full space.

N7. A KEY-PARSING DEFECT THAT WOULD HAVE CRASHED THE REPLAY AFTER 90 MINUTES.
    Both count loaders parsed persisted keys with eval(), which returns a list;
    a list is unhashable and cannot be a dict key. The operator-count replay
    would have measured for ~90 minutes and then died at its verification step.
    Caught by testing the parse standalone BEFORE launching, and replaced with
    json.loads() plus tuple().

N8. I RE-COMMITTED MY OWN DOCUMENTED WINDOWS SPAWN MISTAKE.
    A sensitivity script was written without an `if __name__ == "__main__":`
    guard. Under Windows spawn the child re-imports __main__, so the script
    re-executed itself. This exact failure mode is documented in the V0.6
    record from an earlier pass; I repeated it anyway. Fixed by writing a real
    runner file with the guard.

N9. A DUPLICATE PRODUCTION JOB RAN FOR 45 MINUTES BECAUSE I MISREAD AN ERROR.
    My first launch of the operator-count replay DID start. Only my subsequent
    `cat` of its log ran in the wrong working directory, because a `cd X && cmd
    &` construction put the cd inside a background subshell. I read "no such
    file" as "nothing started" and relaunched. Two identical 12-worker replays
    then competed for 16 cores and were on course to race on the same output
    files. Found by grouping processes by parent process id and checking parent
    liveness; the duplicate and its twelve workers were killed. NO OUTPUT WAS
    CORRUPTED: neither instance had reached its write step. Killing a parent
    also orphans its spawned workers, which keep running, so those were killed
    explicitly by pid on both occasions rather than left to compete.

N10. EVIDENCE THAT SUBSTANTIVE QUANTITIES WERE UNAVAILABLE BEFORE G3 PASSED.
    The runner's control flow makes this checkable rather than assertable. In
    run_full.py the precision gate block ends with an early return that writes
    the failed artifact and exits before the stationary solve; the failed
    artifact RESULT_FULL_n12000_GATE_FAIL.json contains identities, the escape
    count and the precision report, and NO stationary distribution, NO current,
    NO entropy production, NO cycle affinity and NO attribution. The file is in
    the repository and can be inspected.

O. WHAT V0 THROUGH V0.6 TAUGHT US ABOUT THE WRONG MICROSCOPES
====================================================================

Not "the tests improved". What each inadequate instrument would have made us
BELIEVE, and which direction the error ran.

O1. FALSE POSITIVE, and the repair did the damage.
    V0 through V0.2 measured a marginal length slope with no geometry control.
    It failed three times and we believed the grammar had a length ratchet.
    Acting on that belief I revised the grammar twice. THE BELIEF WAS FALSE: a
    symmetric-by-construction joint walk reproduces the same slope, so the
    signal was joint geometry, not authored direction. Worse, the second
    revision INTRODUCED a real tape ratchet that cost another whole pass to
    find and remove. Cost of a missing null: two grammar versions and two
    passes.

O2. FALSE NEGATIVE, hidden behind flat marginals.
    V0.3 and V0.4 then tested marginal drift against proper geometry nulls and
    found nothing surviving multiplicity correction. Had the sequence stopped
    there we would have believed the Foundry was dynamically neutral and shipped
    it. THE BELIEF WOULD HAVE BEEN FALSE: V0.5 found authored circulation with
    almost no marginal displacement. Detailed balance is a far stronger
    condition than zero drift, and V0.6 confirms the gap is not subtle --
    11.3% of two-way flux is net imbalance while the marginal battery reads
    nothing.

O3. A CONFIRMATORY TEST KILLING A DISCOVERY.
    The V0.4 halt/yield finding did not replicate under V0.5's confirmatory
    design: v0_4 delta -0.0191 against v0_5 delta +0.0118, opposite sign,
    one-sided p 0.9716. Recorded as V0_4_CONTENT_DISCOVERY_NOT_REPLICATED. The
    lesson is that a discovery found in the same pass that generated it is a
    hypothesis, and the confirmatory arm is what tells you which.

O4. AN INSTRUMENT THAT STOPPED ITSELF, for the first time in this sequence.
    An underpowered full-space kernel would have let us believe the truncated
    V0.5 finding did not generalise. At n = 12,000 the measurement was not good
    enough to tell, and the design refused to let it answer. This is the first
    time in the programme that a gate prevented a believable wrong number from
    existing rather than catching one after it had been written down.

O5. THE ANALYTIC SHORTCUT THAT WAS SIMPLY WRONG.
    The V0.4/V0.5 analytic kernel differs from the live operator by median TV
    0.0162 and maximum 0.1661. It is retired from adjudication in V0.6 and
    appears only as historical evidence. A closed-form model of your own
    sampler is a hypothesis about your code, not a description of it.

O6. THE COMPARISON THAT ONLY LOOKS LIKE ONE.
    Cycle affinity magnitudes are NOT comparable across bases. V0.5 reported a
    top three-cycle affinity of -1.21 nats; V0.6 reports a maximum of 3.0007
    nats over a spanning-tree fundamental cycle of roughly 230 edges. These are
    different objects and the larger number is not a larger effect. The
    basis-independent comparison is entropy production per step. This packet
    reports both and leans on the second.
