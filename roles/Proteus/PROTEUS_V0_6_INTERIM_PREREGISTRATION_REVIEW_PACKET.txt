1. WHY V0.6 EXISTS
====================================================================

V0.5 measured a 124-state TRUNCATED structural kernel at 50,000 samples per
state and found authored probability current: 166 of 506 pairs above the noise
floor, stationary entropy production 9.97e-03 nats per step, three-cycle
affinities to 1.21 nats, with 165 of the 166 material pairs attributed to
operator weighting and none to boundary or validity geometry. It also found that
the current does NOT move coarse structural occupancy at two million neutral
steps.

Its preregistered full-space arm, at 300 samples per state over 2,044 states,
FAILED its own frozen tolerance (median row TV 0.0667 against a limit of 0.02)
and its stationary solve hit the iteration cap without converging. So the
question "is the current an artifact of the truncation" was left INCONCLUSIVE,
not answered. V0.6 closes exactly that gap and nothing else.

External review has additionally supplied a PROSPECTIVE rule, written before any
V0.6 measurement: a reproducible stationary current caused by authored mutation
mechanics rather than published geometry constitutes a directional mutation
prior even with no marginal drift, because neutrality concerns the dynamics the
Foundry supplies and not merely the first moments of selected observables.

====================================================================
5. THE KERNEL IS THE LIVE OPERATOR, AND THE PARALLELISM IS EXACT
====================================================================

V0.5 showed the analytic kernel differs from the live one by median TV 0.0162
and maximum 0.1661, so the analytic form is inadmissible here as the primary
kernel and appears only as historical evidence. The V0.6 kernel is estimated by
EXECUTING grammar.mutate, marginalising over uniformly random genome content,
with self-loops from rejected proposals included and each operator's own
recorded rejection reason retained.

Parallelism is exact rather than approximate. Each state's stream is
root.derive(L, T), and derive does not advance the parent, so a state's samples
depend only on (seed, tag, L, T) and are independent of how the work is split. A
unit test asserts that serial and 4-worker measurement produce identical counts.

Per state the run records: transition counts, self-loops, per-operator
destination counts, and rejection reasons -- the operator-conditioned counts are
what make section 7's counterfactual exact rather than heuristic.

7. ATTRIBUTION WILL BE COUNTERFACTUAL, NOT A LABEL
====================================================================

Because per-operator destination counts are recorded, the kernel can be exactly
recomposed offline as

    P'(i -> j) = sum_op w'_op * ( count_op(i -> j) / count_op(i -> .) )

With the frozen weights this reproduces the measured kernel, which is checked
and reported as a reconstruction test. With any other weight vector it is an
exact offline counterfactual that changes no grammar. Variants to be evaluated,
all fixed in advance: length-balanced weights, unreachable_removal removed,
config_perturbation removed, insertion and deletion equalised.

The question this answers is the brief's, not a relabelling: if the authored
operator weights were replaced by a reversible weighting on the same support,
would the current disappear?

8. NUMERICAL REPLAY CONTRACT
====================================================================

EXACT layer, byte-identical across every tested runtime: state enumeration and
its hash, state ids, mutation seeds, per-state transition COUNTS, manifest and
source identity hashes, integer ledgers.

NUMERICAL layer, tolerances set by conditioning rather than by observed
differences, each far below the smallest quantity that could change a
classification:

    stationary probabilities   1e-9 absolute or 1e-6 relative
    probability currents       1e-12 absolute
    entropy production         1e-9 absolute
    residuals                  1e-10 absolute

Every adjudicated sum uses math.fsum, which is exactly rounded. The CPython 3.12
sum() change that broke V0.5's byte-identity therefore cannot enter an
adjudicated quantity by construction, rather than being tolerated after the
fact.

9. TWO INSTRUMENT DEFECTS FOUND BEFORE PRODUCTION, BOTH FIXED
====================================================================

D1  THE METROPOLIS CONTROL DID NOT HIT ITS OWN TARGET. I used plain Metropolis
    acceptance, min(1, tgt_j/tgt_i), with a uniform-neighbour proposal. On an
    irregular graph that proposal is ASYMMETRIC, so detailed balance fails and
    the chain converges somewhere other than its declared measure -- the test
    caught it converging to 0.0625 where uniform is 0.0833. Corrected to the
    Hastings ratio min(1, tgt_j*k_i / (tgt_i*k_j)). A control whose whole job is
    to read zero, silently missing its own target, would have invalidated the
    instrument and produced a confident wrong answer.

D2  THE ANALYSIS TOOLS ASSUMED A CLOSED STATE SET. On a truncated subset a
    leaking row is not a stochastic matrix, which made two stationary solvers
    disagree by L1 0.026 and made the Metropolis control raise. Escape folding
    is now explicit and is a NO-OP on the production space, which is proved
    closed. The production path is unaffected; the defect was in how I exercised
    the tools, and it would have been invisible had I only ever run them on the
    full space.

Ten V0.6 gate tests plus the existing suite pass: 67 tests.

10. ARMS DECLARED NOT RUN, IN ADVANCE

BEHAVIORAL COUPLING (brief section 16) -- OMITTED, with the reason. Under the
active grammar a single operator draw produces the structural change and the
content change as ONE event: an insertion both lengthens the genome and writes
random instructions. There is therefore no way to substitute the reversible
structural kernel while holding content treatment fixed without altering more
than the structural kernel, and a rejection filter that forced structural
reversibility would also change which content mutations survive. The brief
instructs that this be said and the arm omitted rather than forced. It is
omitted, and this was recorded before the run rather than after it failed.

11. THE COST OF THE WRONG MICROSCOPE
====================================================================

The brief requires this stay visible, and requires more than "the tests
improved". What each inadequate test would have made us believe:

  V0 through V0.2 measured a marginal length slope against a one-dimensional
  criterion with no geometry control. It failed three times, and we believed the
  grammar had a length ratchet. Acting on that belief, I revised the grammar
  twice. THE BELIEF WAS FALSE: V0.4 showed a symmetric-by-construction joint
  walk reproduces the same slope. The second revision introduced a REAL tape
  ratchet that took another whole pass to find. FALSE POSITIVE: geometry
  mistaken for authored direction, and the repair did the damage.

  V0.3 and V0.4 then tested marginal drift against proper geometry nulls and
  found nothing surviving correction. Had the sequence stopped there we would
  have believed the Foundry was dynamically neutral. THE BELIEF WOULD HAVE BEEN
  FALSE: V0.5 found authored circulation with almost no marginal displacement,
  because detailed balance is a far stronger condition than zero drift. FALSE
  NEGATIVE: authored direction hidden behind flat marginals.

  A third failure is possible and V0.6 is designed against it: an underpowered
  full-space measurement would let us believe the truncated finding did not
  generalise. That is why the sample count was calibrated first and why the
  precision gate is checked before any substantive quantity is computed.
