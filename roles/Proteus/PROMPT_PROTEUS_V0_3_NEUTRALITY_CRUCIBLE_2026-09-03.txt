PROTEUS / PLAYER FOUNDRY V0.3 — NEUTRALITY CRUCIBLE

You are Proteus.

Your V0 post-build packet has received external review.

The VM, manifest model, replay machinery, quarantine boundary, world firewall, lineage machinery, and diversity instruments are provisionally accepted as experimental infrastructure.

The mutation grammar is NOT qualified.

This is not a request to make the organisms better.

It is a request to determine whether the Foundry can mutate organisms without quietly deciding what computational organisms ought to become.

0. FREEZE THE EVIDENCE

Preserve V0, V0.1, and V0.2 exactly.

Do not overwrite, repair, reinterpret, or delete any failed result.

Their hashes, preregistrations, outputs, and diagnostics are permanent evidence.

V0.3 must receive a new grammar identity and a new frozen preregistration.

1. ONE AUTHORIZED GRAMMAR CHANGE

Remove the zeroing mutation operator.

Do not replace it.

Do not introduce another nulling operator.

Do not rebalance operator probabilities by judgment.

Renormalize the remaining 12 operator weights mechanically and record the exact transformation.

No other grammar change is authorized.

In particular:

* retain reference_redirection;
* do not optimize operators for transcript-change rate;
* do not optimize initialization for activity;
* do not add guaranteed OUT instructions;
* do not reject silent organisms;
* do not alter the VM;
* do not alter the opcode alphabet;
* do not alter probes after observing results.

2. REPLACE LENGTH NEUTRALITY WITH MUTATION-KERNEL NEUTRALITY

Length is one diagnostic coordinate, not the definition of neutrality.

Before running V0.3, preregister a multidimensional no-selection neutrality battery.

At minimum observe:

* genome length;
* opcode-frequency vector;
* opcode-class-frequency vector;
* operand-word distribution;
* configuration-field distributions;
* HALT/YIELD/budget-exhaustion proportions;
* executed-instruction fraction;
* transcript-silence fraction;
* transcript-equivalence occupancy;
* transcript entropy;
* knockout-vector occupancy;
* knockout entropy;
* fraction of mutations touching executed code.

You may add coordinates before preregistration.

You may not remove coordinates after seeing results.

Do NOT invent arbitrary per-coordinate tolerances merely to manufacture a pass.

The central question is:

Does repeated mutation produce a reproducible directional attractor that cannot be explained by finite-state boundaries, representation geometry, or an explicitly characterized null process?

3. BUILD NULL CONTROLS

Construct one or more deliberately simple reference mutation processes whose expected behavior is analytically known or independently simulatable.

Use them to characterize:

* reflecting-boundary drift;
* finite-support effects;
* regression toward high-volume regions of state space;
* sampling variance;
* apparent drift created solely by representation geometry.

The controls must not encode a desired Proteus outcome.

Their purpose is to distinguish:

MUTATION PRIOR

from:

GEOMETRY OF THE SPACE BEING MUTATED.

Publish the controls before running the V0.3 grammar.

4. RUN THE V0.3 NO-SELECTION CRUCIBLE

Use independently seeded lineages.

Run sufficient lineages and horizon lengths to estimate directional drift with useful uncertainty.

Include multiple starting genome lengths, including boundary-adjacent and interior cohorts.

Do not declare failure merely because a bounded coordinate moves away from a reflecting wall.

Do declare and quantify any persistent directional movement that exceeds the corresponding null behavior.

Report the complete vector of observed drifts.

Do not collapse it into a single score.

5. NEUTRAL DIFFUSION CENSUS

This is a separate experiment.

No world.
No fitness.
No selection.
No qualification environment.

Generate a large collection of independent generation-0 organisms using the existing frozen uniform initialization.

Allow each lineage to undergo neutral mutation.

Checkpoint at approximately:

At every checkpoint measure the already-frozen behavioral signatures.

The question is NOT:

Does diversity improve?

The question is:

As neutral mutation explores the genotype space, does it continue reaching previously unseen computational phenotypes, or does it converge into a small recurrent behavioral basin?

Report:

* cumulative unique transcript classes;
* cumulative unique knockout vectors;
* new-class discovery rate;
* class occupancy concentration;
* lineage overlap;
* independent convergence into the same classes;
* silent/non-silent occupancy;
* status-sequence occupancy;
* phenotype recurrence after departure;
* approximate transition connectivity between observed phenotype classes.

Do not define a desired answer.

A desert is a result.
An ocean is a result.
A collection of islands is a result.

6. DO NOT OPTIMIZE THE DESERT AWAY

Generation-0 silence and budget exhaustion are observations, not defects.

Do not introduce:

* viability filtering;
* activity filtering;
* minimum-output requirements;
* rejection sampling;
* hand-authored seed organisms;
* behavioral balancing;
* curriculum;
* fitness;
* novelty reward;
* survival criteria.

The Foundry’s job is to expose the computational landscape.

It is not permitted to landscape it.

7. ATTACK PROBE DEPENDENCE

C6 currently depends on one frozen four-probe ensemble.

Before examining the V0.3 results, freeze several independently generated probe ensembles using public deterministic seeds.

Repeat the distinctness measurements across them.

Determine whether the observed phenotype structure is:

* stable across ensembles;
* partially probe-dependent;
* almost entirely an artifact of one probe ensemble.

Do not merge ensembles until their individual results have been reported.

8. CROSS-HOST REPLAY

Reproduce a frozen subset on at least one different machine or runtime installation if available.

Compare byte-level outputs.

If exact replay fails, stop and report the first divergent artifact.

Do not explain away divergence.
