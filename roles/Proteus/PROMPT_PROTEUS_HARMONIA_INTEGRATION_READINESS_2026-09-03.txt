1. OPERATING DECISION
   ===============================================================================

For the coming integration test, distinguish two completely different uses of
Proteus.

USE A - ALLOWED NOW

Proteus as a deterministic source of frozen, semantics-free player
specimens.

USE B - STILL NOT QUALIFIED

Proteus mutation machinery as a neutral evolutionary operator from which
population-level evolutionary conclusions may be drawn.

The first integration uses USE A.

Nothing in this directive adjudicates USE B.

The V0.6 circulation must remain visible anywhere a consumer could otherwise
infer that the Foundry is neutral.

Specifically:

* generated organisms may be exported and run;
* immutable manifests may be selected by ID;
* fixed specimen populations may be supplied to Harmonia;
* players may be executed in worlds through the established player ABI;
* lineage/replay machinery may be exercised;

but:

* no claim may say the Foundry samples structural states neutrally;
* no population comparison may silently rely upon that assumption;
* no mutation campaign may be interpreted as unbiased evolution;
* no V0.6 repair is authorized.

2. PRIMARY DELIVERABLE - THE PLAYER INVENTORY

Harmonia previously had no canonical registry/dictionary/catalog.

Create the smallest consumer-facing surface necessary to fix that.

Call it a registry, inventory, dictionary, index, or another
repository-consistent name, but preserve this semantic rule:

THE REGISTRY DESCRIBES SPECIMENS.
IT DOES NOT DEFINE PLAYER "TYPES."

Do NOT introduce authored semantic families such as:

explorer
predator
learner
cooperator
strategist
memory-player
random-player

unless such a label is purely an externally supplied experimental annotation
and clearly outside Proteus identity.

Proteus players are semantically sterile organisms.

The registry should make them ADDRESSABLE, not explain what they "are."

At minimum, Harmonia must be able to discover for each registered specimen:

organism_id
lineage_id
generation
schema_version
runtime_hash
grammar_hash

    n_regs
    tape_words
    genome instruction count
    code_writable
    persist
    tick_budget
    out_cap

If useful, include clearly non-semantic operational descriptors such as:

estimated execution cost
maximum state footprint
manifest validation status
audit identity
checkpoint compatibility

Do not turn those into a quality score.
