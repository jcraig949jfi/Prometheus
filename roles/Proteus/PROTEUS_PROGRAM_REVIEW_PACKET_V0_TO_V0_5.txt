====================================================================
1. WHAT PROTEUS IS, AND WHAT IT IS NOT
====================================================================

Proteus supplies the PLAYER side of the Prometheus Incubator: a deterministic,
seeded generator that manufactures large numbers of small, semantically sterile
computational organisms so that a neutral operator can drop them into worlds the
Foundry has never seen. Daedalus's Serendipity Foundry Engine and the World-0
line supply the worlds. Proteus does not design worlds, does not read their
internals, does not tune to them, and does not judge survivors.

An organism is a content-addressed manifest: a genome of 32-bit words plus six
bounded limits. The genome is copied to the front of a bounded tape and executed
by a 25-opcode interpreter in which op = word mod 25, so EVERY word sequence is
a valid program and no mutation can produce an illegal one. Genomes are data,
never host code. There is no fitness anywhere in the Foundry.

NOT CLAIMED, anywhere in six passes: that any organism reasons, is intelligent,
is capable, is interesting, or that useful evolution will occur. No campaign has
been launched. No world has been touched. No adapter exists.

3. THE TWO FINDINGS THAT MATTER
====================================================================

3.1  THE LENGTH FAILURE THAT DROVE THREE REVISIONS WAS GEOMETRY, NOT A PRIOR.

V0, V0.1 and V0.2 each measured genome-length drift against a one-dimensional
criterion with no geometry control, and each failed. V0.4 built NC5, a joint
walk over the coupled (genome_length, tape_words) space that is symmetric BY
CONSTRUCTION and therefore has no mutation prior at all. NC5 reproduces the old
effect almost exactly:

    instructions per generation      cohort 8    cohort 128
    grammar v0                        +0.0707      -0.0234
    grammar v0.1                      +0.0412      -0.0280
    grammar v0.2                      +0.0328      -0.0136
    grammar v0.4                      +0.0270      -0.0168
    NC5 (symmetric, no prior)         +0.0408      -0.0159

Three grammar revisions chased the shape of the state space. The second of them
introduced a REAL ratchet in the tape coordinate that took a whole further pass
to find and remove. The old verdicts were correct under the rules they declared;
those rules simply could not distinguish geometry from prior.

Cost of the missing control: two grammar revisions and one genuine defect.

3.2  THE MUTATION KERNEL IS A NONEQUILIBRIUM MARKOV PROCESS.

V0.4 catalogued 16,172 asymmetric directed-edge pairs in the length kernel and
could not show they were dynamically active. V0.5 measured the structural kernel
from the LIVE operator over 124 enumerated states, 50,000 mutations per state,
and solved it:

    connected pairs                        506
    total |J|                         2.84e-02      J = pi_i P_ij - pi_j P_ji
    max |J|                           2.45e-04
    Monte-Carlo noise floor           4.16e-05      (max |J| between two
                                                     independent samples of the
                                                     SAME kernel)
    pairs above that floor                 166
    stationary entropy production     9.97e-03 nats/step, 1,012 terms
    one-way edges                            0      (so sigma is finite)
    top 3-cycle affinity                 -1.21 nats (Kolmogorov violated)
    reversible reference max |J|      2.17e-19      (the control behaves)

Attribution of the 166 material pairs: OPERATOR_WEIGHTING 165,
OPERATOR_IMPLEMENTATION 1, PUBLISHED_BOUNDARY 0, MANIFEST_VALIDITY_GEOMETRY 0.
The geometry that V0.4 spent an entire pass isolating contributes nothing above
the noise floor. The current is authored: it comes from operator weights that
Proteus chose.

AND IT DOES NOT MOVE THE MARGINALS. Two million matched steps under the active
kernel and under a detailed-balance reference on the same support:

    mean genome length          21.927   vs   21.863
    occupancy TV between them            0.0198
    sampling floor at that horizon      ~0.019
    length moves up/down ratio   0.932   vs    1.003   (replicated, two seeds)

So four passes of marginal drift testing could not see this, and one pass of
equilibrium analysis found it immediately. Zero marginal drift is a much weaker
condition than detailed balance, and the Foundry satisfies the first and fails
the second.

5. THE DISCOVERY THAT DIED, AND THE ONE THAT MIGHT BE NEXT
====================================================================

V0.4 declared exactly one effect: the halt/yield opcode-class share at cohort
128, delta -0.0191 against its length-matched null, z 3.53. V0.4's own packet
said it did not replicate across cohorts, was absent against the second null,
and had no mechanism -- and declared it anyway, because the preregistered rule
fired and waiving it would have been a post-hoc rescue.

V0.5 gave it one independent confirmatory test with every parameter frozen
before the seed existed. Result:

    V0.4   delta -0.0191   z 3.53
    V0.5   delta +0.0118   95% CI [-0.0008, +0.0236]   z 1.90
           one-sided p in the claimed direction = 0.9716

    V0_4_CONTENT_DISCOVERY_NOT_REPLICATED

The sign reversed. The effect did not weaken; it inverted. The discovery is
retained in the record, not erased.

STANDING CANDIDATE, DELIBERATELY NOT DECLARED AND NOT CHASED: genome_length at
cohort 256 against NC5 is negative on both independent seeds -- V0.4 -6.53 (not
significant), V0.5 -12.80 with interval [-19.99, -5.86] and z 3.55. It survives
within-cohort correction and does NOT survive the preregistered global family.
Same sign twice, growing magnitude, one cohort. That is exactly the shape
halt/yield had before it failed. No extra seed was run to chase it, because
choosing a test after seeing the result it would rescue is the failure mode this
whole sequence exists to prevent.

6. INSTRUMENT FAILURE LEDGER -- EVERY DEFECT PROTEUS FOUND IN ITSELF
====================================================================

This is the most useful section for a reviewer. Across six passes the
instruments have been wrong more often than the Foundry has.

6.1  V0: "subtraction mass exceeds addition mass" was true of operator WEIGHTS
     (0.13 vs 0.12) and false of expected INSTRUCTIONS (0.25 removed vs 0.30
     added), because one operator deletes a single instruction while the others
     move about 2.5. The neutrality gate caught it. It was the only confident
     design claim made before measuring, and it was wrong on the quantity that
     mattered.

6.2  V0: the quarantine audit reported PASS on a tree that did not yet contain
     export.py. The V0 packet's "string layer PASS" was true of the code when it
     ran and false of the code it shipped. Closed in V0.4 by an atomic
     audit-identity stamp binding a per-file digest of every covered source;
     staleness was demonstrated, not asserted.

6.3  V0.3: the Holm correction was inert. It compared a z-equivalent against a
     RATIO of normal quantiles instead of the quantile, and had no step-down
     stop, making the "corrected" test WEAKER than simply excluding zero. It
     would have published twenty surviving coordinates; the corrected version
     published one. Fixed, then given frozen fixtures and a second independent
     implementation that aborts on disagreement.

6.4  V0.3: content coordinates were preregistered against a null that FREEZES
     genome length, which is a wrong-population comparison wherever length
     changes. Six coordinate declarations at one cohort evaporated under the
     length-matched null.

6.5  V0.4: the analytic structural kernel used by NC5 differs from the kernel
     measured from the live operator by median total variation 0.0162 and
     maximum 0.1661. Causes: one operator was modelled as always firing when it
     fires conditionally, and the k-clamping inside deletion and duplication was
     not modelled at all. This qualifies V0.4's NC5-based claims. It does not
     overturn V0.4's tape-symmetry proof, which was exhaustive over the real
     rule rather than the model.

6.6  V0.5: cross-runtime replay is no longer byte-identical. CPython 3.12 on
     Windows and on Linux agree exactly; CPython 3.11 differs at 1e-14 relative
     in the stationary distribution. Cause demonstrated rather than guessed:
     CPython 3.12 changed the builtin sum() to compensated summation, and the
     stationary solve normalises with sum(). Twelve orders of magnitude below
     the measurement noise, changes no conclusion -- but no numerical replay
     tolerance was preregistered, so it is reported, not adjudicated.

6.7  V0.5: the preregistration made two of the three kernel classifications
     non-exclusive. Both fit the data. Resolved toward the stronger statement so
     the attribution finding would not be suppressed, and the defect disclosed.

6.8  V0.5: THE PREREGISTERED FULL-SPACE SENSITIVITY ARM FAILED ITS OWN FROZEN
     TOLERANCE. At 300 samples per state over 2,044 states, two independent
     samples of the same kernel differ by median TV 0.0667 against a frozen
     limit of 0.02, and the stationary solve hit its iteration cap without
     converging. So the question "is the primary current a truncation artifact"
     is INCONCLUSIVE, not answered. Partial support against the artifact
     reading: the full space is closed with zero escaped mass, the primary
     space's escaped mass is at most 0.0075 per state, the largest currents are
     interior length edges far from any boundary, and the full-space attribution
     agrees qualitatively (8,085 of 9,087 pairs OPERATOR_WEIGHTING, cycle
     affinities to -2.54 nats). A properly powered full-space run needs roughly
     two orders of magnitude more sampling than this pass could afford.

7. METHOD CHANGES THAT PAID FOR THEMSELVES
====================================================================

  * A GEOMETRY NULL THAT IS SYMMETRIC BY CONSTRUCTION. A symmetrized reflected
    walk drifts +18 instructions off the minimum bound and -19.6 off the cap
    with a flat interior. Before that control existed, three passes read those
    walls as mutation priors.

  * A JOINT NULL OVER COUPLED COORDINATES. A genome at a tape boundary does not
    live in the same mutation geometry as one with spare capacity. The
    single-coordinate null could never have shown that the tape coordinate
    drifts in OPPOSITE directions at the two ends purely from geometry.

  * GLOBAL MULTIPLICITY OVER (coordinate x cohort). V0.4 corrected within
    cohorts while the decision searched across both. On the V0.5 data, two cells
    would have been declared under the old architecture and are rejected by the
    correct family -- caught prospectively, on live data, in the first pass
    after the change.

  * MEASURING THE KERNEL FROM THE LIVE OPERATOR INSTEAD OF MODELLING IT. That
    is what exposed 6.5, and it is why the V0.5 current numbers do not inherit a
    model/code gap.

  * TESTING DETAILED BALANCE RATHER THAN DRIFT. The single highest-yield change
    in the sequence. It found in one pass what four passes of marginal testing
    could not see.

    agreement between primary and sensitivity nulls, or replication across
    cohorts. Proteus deliberately did not create either rule retrospectively.

D3  A NUMERICAL REPLAY TOLERANCE. None was preregistered; byte-identity is no
