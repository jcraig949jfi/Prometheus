F2. THE GRAMMAR HAS A CONTENT BIAS THE LENGTH GATE CANNOT SEE.
    The prereg promised an ungated opcode-frequency diagnostic. Under
    no selection, 300 generations, the share of opcode 0 (NOP) rose by
    +0.133 (from ~0.04 to ~0.17 of instructions) while every other
    opcode fell by <= 0.011. Cause: the zeroing operator writes
    all-zero words (NOP 0 0 0) at 0.04 x ~2.5 instructions per
    generation, and only uniform replacement/randomization dilute
    them. This is a ratchet in CONTENT toward the null instruction. It
    is exactly the kind of "authored direction" A3 warns about, it was
    not in the gate, and it is reported here rather than fixed because
    fixing it is a fourth revision. Recommendation in section 8.

F3. 79% OF GENERATION-0 ORGANISMS EMIT NOTHING ON THE PROBES.

    3,175 / 4,000 are silent; 2,513 exhaust a tick budget. This is a

    a first world will see a population that is mostly inert at
    generation 0. That is the brief's expectation ("most of your

2. THE ORGANISM, IN ONE PARAGRAPH
====================================================================

A player is a content-addressed manifest: a genome of 32-bit words
(4..4096, multiple of 4) plus six bounded limits (registers 2..16,
tape 16..4096 words, tick budget 8..65536, output cap 1..256, a
code-writable flag, a persist policy in {none, regs, tape, all}). The
genome is copied to the front of a bounded tape and executed in place
by a 25-opcode interpreter; op = word mod 25, so EVERY word sequence
is a valid program and no mutation can produce an illegal one. The 25
opcodes fall into nine published classes: halt/yield (incl. NOP),
read/write, indirection, arithmetic, logical, comparison, control,
opaque I/O, externally supplied randomness. There is no cost opcode
(A2). Self-modification exists only as ST into the genome region when
code_writable is true; there is no other plasticity mechanism. The
(M,T,C,Pi) lens of the brief does not appear in the code: in a single
address space they are all patterns of words, and whether an organism
has any of them is a fact about the organism.

Affordance table hash f1607ee8be680acc... (published BEFORE any
measurement, proteus/contracts/affordance_table.v0.json). Runtime hash
folds it in and is computed over LF-normalised source, so a CRLF
checkout does not fork identity.

4. THE FIREWALL (A1, brief section 10)
====================================================================

roles/Proteus/READ_LEDGER.md lists every world-side file opened, with
line ranges and reasons. No entry was added during the build. The ABI
defines NO channel layout, NO recommended shape, NO fixed channel
count, and NO World-0 binding; channels are addressed by register
value mod channel count so a genome runs unchanged under any count.
What Proteus fixes is the machine (32-bit words, output cap, tick-
scoped cursors, three status values), not the world. Multiplayer is
operator wiring of outputs into inputs; the player cannot tell a
world from another player from a recording.

One honest tension: the diversity probes are inputs on channels, so
the Foundry does execute organisms against SOMETHING. Those inputs are
uniform noise derived from the addendum hash (a public value Proteus
did not choose), never used for selection, and the relation induced
is named probe_transcript_equivalence and nothing else (A4).

====================================================================

C1  Same Foundry manifest + same runtime hash => byte-identical
    population, transcripts, children and checkpoint continuations.
    Ceiling: tested on 300-organism populations and 40-organism
    probe sets on one machine and one Python version; cross-machine
    replay is untested. Breaks if: sha256 of any result differs on
    another host (run tests/test_replay.py there).

C2  Every word sequence is a valid program; no manifest within bounds
    can make the runtime raise. Ceiling: 12,000+ organisms across all
    runs without a runtime exception; not a proof. Breaks if:
    qualify.py ever ledgers RUNTIME_RAISED.

C3  The player sees no string, no world identity, no score, no other
    player; the runtime imports nothing that could carry one.
    Ceiling: string layer only. Breaks if: the ontology reviewer names
    an opcode as a cognitive primitive, or a binding layer authors a
    channel layout and calls it Proteus's.

C4  Grammar v0.2 is length-stationary at the middle cohort
    (slope +0.009 [-0.012, +0.029]) and NOT at either bound-adjacent
    cohort. Ceiling: 300 generations, 60 lineages, three start sizes,
    one tape size. Breaks if: the reviewer rules bound drift a
    ratchet (then the grammar is unqualified); or a longer horizon
    shows drift at 32.

C5  Grammar v0.2 biases content toward NOP under no selection
    (+0.133 share over 300 generations). This is a NEGATIVE claim
    about my own grammar. Ceiling: one run; one start-size mix.
    Breaks if: a rerun with zeroing weight 0 shows the same drift
    (then the cause is elsewhere).

C6  The two signatures separate organisms (303-313 classes, 941-961
    vectors of 4,000). Ceiling: 4 probes, one ensemble, uniform
    initialisation; says nothing about any world. Breaks if: a
    different frozen ensemble collapses classes to ~1 (instrument was
    probe-dependent), which would be worth knowing.

No claim is made about intelligence, reasoning, capability, emergence,
