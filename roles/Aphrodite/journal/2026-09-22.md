THE FINDING WORTH KEEPING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The donor could not abstract, and the reason is structural rather than a
bug.

Its two observed semantic classes were `(acc - (v * v))` and `(v + acc)`,
which anti-unify to nothing. For the sum-of-squares family it did not
find body `acc + v*v`; it found body `acc - v*v` with final
`first - acc` -- accumulating the NEGATIVE sum and negating at the end.
Identical program, different factorisation.

  THE BODY IS NOT AN INVARIANT UNIT. A program factors into
  body x final in many equivalent ways, and the body can absorb a sign,
  scale or offset that its final compensates. Abstraction over one
  component of a factorisation is unreliable when that component's form
  is contingent on its partner.

No improvement to the anti-unification procedure repairs this. The fix is
to abstract over whole-program semantic classes, or to canonicalise the
factorisation before abstracting.

AND THE CONTROLS RAN THE EXPERIMENT. Each sham carries six random
semantic classes plus one random schema. SHAM_0 and SHAM_5 both drew
`(acc + {H})` -- precisely the schema the donor should have formed -- and
both solved BOTH unseen-body families 16/16. SHAM_1 drew `(acc - {H})`
and SHAM_2 `(acc * {H})`; neither covers, both 0/16. EVOLVED and PRISTINE
carry no schema: 1/16 and 0/16.

That is a natural experiment I did not design and could not have faked.
The hole-bearing schema is the causal ingredient; the abstraction
hypothesis is strongly supported by the control distribution, because the
treatment arm never built one.

THE APPARATUS FINALLY WORKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

ZERO false positives across three families and ten arms in Tier 3C,
against Tier 3A's 14-of-16 recipients and Tier 3B's 4.688 per recipient.
The cause is GENERATOR qualification: 200 independent draws per candidate
development size, battery grown until the 95% upper bound on surviving
wrong semantic classes fell below 0.05. It REJECTED THREE OF NINE
FAMILIES, including both gcd-final families -- exactly the ones that
poisoned Tier 3B.

THE CONFORMANCE GATE was green before Tier 3C began: 900 program shapes
x 24 input configurations = 21,600 differential comparisons between the
search evaluator and the emitted artifact evaluator, zero mismatches,
covering overflow, division by zero, modulo zero, out-of-range exponents
and sequence lengths 1 to 200. First slice in this program to start from
a verified agreement between its two evaluators. Nothing hung; memory
held at 45 MB against 1.65 GB the slice before.
