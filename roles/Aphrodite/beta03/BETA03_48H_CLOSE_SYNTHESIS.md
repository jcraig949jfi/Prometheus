## 3. The eight questions
**Q1. Was R8's failure caused partly by unequal residual learning headroom?**
**YES, mostly.**
- On fresh supply, 80-93% of the inherited library's own-start deficit disappears when all arms are scored on a
  common, pre-recipient opportunity set.
- The inherited library ENDS at the highest competence.
- The residual contrast on identical opportunities (-7, or -13 on the pairwise residual) is not significant.
- Separately, a NON-solving inherited library (the sham) interferes strongly with observation. Plausibly its prefix
  exceeds the 30k observation escrow (untested).

**Q2. Does g12 learn to prefer transferable abstraction without a hand-coded memorisation ban?**
**Partly.**
- An endpoint-aligned acceptance rule (designed, not learned) rejects memorisation without naming it. It did so in
  18/18 tempting seeds, and the reach requirement does it, not the size penalty.
- It is **inferior to g11** at choosing transferable abstractions (142 vs 179), and it misses significance vs I_0
  (p 0.053).
- **g11 remains the positive control.**

**Q3. Can an endogenous abstraction become a composable primitive with exact semantics?**
**YES (engineering).**
- W5P primitives have exact semantics, are hashed and transplantable, and record their lineage.
- In 19/22 natural pairs the next generation derives schemas that use the inherited primitive as a constituent.

**Q4. Does promotion expose genuinely new attainable second-order structure?**
**NOT ESTABLISHED.**
- **Natural world:**
  - Every eligible promoted candidate re-expresses a schema the plain path also derives. Promotion adds deeper hole
    fills, not new candidates.
  - One acquisition (1/22 pairs) needed a body outside G5.
- **Structured world:** the instrument is unvalidated. Depth-two families exist and are attainable with a PLANTED
  primitive at the 1M cap, but the learner cannot reach them.

**Q5. Does inherited knowledge make new learning better under those conditions?**
**NOT ESTABLISHED in this engine.**
- E5-N P1 -6, n.s. The test could have reached significance.
- Even oracle selection among derived promoted-dependent schemas does worse than pristine (W12).

**Q6. Maximum abstraction-dependency depth supported by clean causal evidence?**
**NOT ESTABLISHED (depth 1 established; depth 2 not).**
- Depth 2 was selected in 4 pairs and acquired in 1.
- It was never put through a dependency ablation, and it falls below the multiple-lineage bar (>= 3).

**Q7. Which specific instrument or representation limits continued progress?**
**CANDIDACY / REPRESENTATION, upstream of selection:**
1. **One-hole first-order LGG over observed W5 bodies** proposes only re-fillings of the same additive templates.
   Promoted-dependent candidates duplicate plain ones (W12, review finding 3).
2. **Observation searches only the base W5 space under a 30k escrow.** Level-two bodies are never observed, even with
   oracle level-1 inheritance (W9-H pilot, O1).
3. **Acceptance by charge savings** admits memorisation (I_0: 11/24 seeds) and prefers re-expressions. It is fixable
   (g11 / g12), but it is not the binding limit.
4. **On natural W8 supply, structure beyond the base additive class is sparse** (Hestia; Beta-01/02).

**Q8. Another 48 hours on the current engine, or transplant the machinery?**
**Transplant (MIGRATE_SUBSTRATE, basis Outcome C).**
- The cheap repair failed. Further W5 repairs would mean widening the one-hole grammar, which the directive advises
  against ("don't spend days trying to coerce a one-hole grammar").
- **What transplants:**
  - the causal membrane;
  - artifact hashing;
  - escrow;
  - same-loader controls;
  - common-random-number paired fair search;
  - behavioural class certification;
  - the hostile tribunal;
  - the exact sign-flip statistics;
  - **the common-residual R8 endpoint (an E1 lesson, now a hard requirement);**
  - the prereg / red-team / amendment discipline;
  - the failure fossils (DEFECT_REPAIR_LEDGER).
- **The migration's own first step must be a known-positive depth-two control (M1), with a frozen W5 comparator arm**
  (W5P + O1 + g12 + alias fix). A later claim that migration was necessary can then be falsified.

## 4. Statement (the operator's target form)
"First-order improvement in this engine is real and replicated: excluding memorisation from acceptance doubles held-out
reuse, and an endpoint-aligned rule can reject memorisation without naming it. Its apparent failure to compound (R8)
was mostly saturation: inherited competence consumes the next generation's opportunities. When learned abstractions
are made composable primitives with exact semantics, the next generation does build on them, but only as deeper
re-fillings of the same one-hole templates. These are worse than pristine search even under oracle selection, and they
enable no new learning. In a world built to contain second-level mechanisms, the learner cannot observe them even when
handed the right primitive. **The chain stops at candidacy and representation in a one-hole, first-order, base-space
observation substrate. That is an apparatus limit, not a demonstrated impossibility of recursion.** The scientific
machinery should move to a typed, compositional substrate whose first experiment is a known-positive depth-two
control."
