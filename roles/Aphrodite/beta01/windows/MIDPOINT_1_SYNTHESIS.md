## 1. The operator's midpoint questions

**Q1. Which rung is currently limiting progress?**
**R7, improver change.** Nothing in four tests changed the improver. Below R7, the limiting rung depends on
geometry:
- in constructed recurrence it is R3 (selection only recovers SHOWN structure, T52);
- in natural recurrence, reuse beyond the base class (compounding via composition) is weak (T51: +7..+20 families).

**Q2. Are apparatus repairs changing prior interpretations?**

1. A23's G1-specific YES does not survive distinct-class accounting (5/10 < 6). GENERIC 3/3 survives.
2. Two of A23's three H1 controls could never score.

5. Three constant R5 gates sat inside BOUNDED_RSI conjunctions (no YES rested on them).
The repaired tribunal changes A23 family admission for 0/330 families. Not every repair moves a result.

**Q3. Are experiments converging on motif recovery rather than abstraction?**
In CONSTRUCTED recurrence, yes, decisively:
- T52: dose-dependent recovery of shown structure, with below-chance recovery when unshown;
- T53-C: capability only among recovered selections (33/35 vs 0/5).
In NATURAL recurrence the picture differs. The pristine donor derives a genuinely reusable base abstraction (G1
class) from 4 observed families, without being shown it in VALIDATE by construction. That is ENDOGENOUS ABSTRACTION
(S3-like) on natural supply, and the strongest non-recovery signal so far. It is still first-order.

**Q4. Is recurrence, visibility or representation the limit?**
- **Recurrence:** present (W8; T51 shows the base class recurs widely).
- **Visibility:** solved for the base class at escrow 30k (T51: derived and reused). The historical 250k escrow WAS
  the visibility limit (W8 F6, W2).
- **Representation:** untested for compositions beyond depth (G3; W5P not run).
The binding limit for COMPOUNDING is now neither recurrence nor visibility. **The improver can only re-find or select
structure it is shown or can derive by LGG from observations. It has no mechanism to change how it proposes.** That
is R7.

**Q5. Has anything yet changed the improver itself?**
No. Every test held the improver fixed: LGG + composition move + paired selection + fixed escrow. What changed was
inherited LIBRARY content. **No R7 evidence exists.**

**Q6. Are shams keeping pace with the treatment?**
Yes, everywhere:
- T53-C: shams 8/9/10 vs G1 5;
- T52: the same dose curve for G1 and the shams;
- T51: PA, not G1, is the high-dose schema, and G1 is never above the pooled line.
G1 has no privileged status in any v2b result.

**Q7. Is another first-order experiment more valuable than preparing second order?**
- **Not another constructed-recurrence experiment.** That geometry is now understood (selection = shown-structure
  recovery; capability generic and budget-relative).
- **One first-order confirmation is worth its cost:** a FROZEN test of the T51 exploratory finding (endogenous
  derivation recovers most of the inherited benefit) on fresh LIN seeds. It is the only positive in Beta-01 that is
  not recovery, and it is currently exploratory.
- **The higher-value next step is the R7 probe (GTC).** A negative at R8 is uninterpretable while R7 is impossible,
  and T51 points exactly at the improver's proposal mechanism as the limit.
- **Second order (W5P):** remains design-only (authorisation boundary). Its justification now depends on R7: if no
  improver genome has transferable value, promoting primitives would add representation without a mechanism to use
  it.
