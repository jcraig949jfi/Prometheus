## 1. The midpoint questions

**Q1. Which rung currently limits progress?**
**R3, the SELECTION CRITERION, on natural supply.** The chain of evidence:
- T52: selection recovers structure that is shown consistently.
- T07: a CORRECT base-class candidate is rejected in a third of natural seeds.
- T08: more validation evidence makes this worse, because heterogeneous natural families dilute the paired saving of
  an abstraction that helps only a subset.

R7 remains untested in a world where it could show up, but the R3 criterion is what currently blocks the endogenous
route from which any R7/R8 effect would have to grow.

**Q2. Are repairs changing prior interpretations?**
Since midpoint 1: T51's exploratory finding does not survive its frozen confirmation (T06). The pattern is stable
across both seed sets (all-or-nothing derivation; 100% recovery when derived), but the rate is lower: 8/15 pooled,
which is descriptive.

**Q3. Converging on motif recovery rather than abstraction?**
- In constructed recurrence: yes, decisively (midpoint 1).
- In natural supply: the endogenous route derives a genuine base abstraction from 4 observations about half the time.
  It is not motif recovery, but it is first-order and unreliable.

**Q4. Recurrence, visibility or representation?**
- **Recurrence:** present.
- **Visibility:** adequate at escrow 30k.
- **Representation:** not binding at first order.
- **The binding limit is a property of the IMPROVER's selection criterion,** which assumes consistent benefit across
  validation. Natural structure gives sparse, subset benefit.

**Q5. Has anything changed the improver?**
No positive R7. Three attempts:
- T05: instrument-limited.
- T07: control-limited. Its g8/g9 readouts are unreadable; g8's printed total was +3.
- T08: a breadth parameter, measured negative.

**Q6. Are shams keeping pace?**
Not re-tested since midpoint 1 (T06-T08 had no G1-vs-sham contrast). G1 has no privileged status in any v2b result.

**Q7. Another first-order experiment vs second order?**
- **One first-order R7 test is now well targeted:** a subset-benefit selection criterion. Accept a candidate if it
  yields a qualified saving on >= k validation families with bounded total loss. It is content-free, it aims at the
  diagnosed mismatch, and it is cheap on the existing 15-seed apparatus.
- If it rescues the validation-limited seeds without NULL false acceptances, it is the first R7 positive: an
  improver-rule change that makes the improver better at acquiring improvements on fresh natural supply, with no
  library carried.
- **W5P / second order stays design-only.** Justifying it still requires a working endogenous first order, which the
  selection criterion currently prevents.
