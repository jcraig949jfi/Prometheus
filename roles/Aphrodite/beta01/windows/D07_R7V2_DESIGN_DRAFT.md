# D07 DRAFT -- R7 v2: A TRANSPLANTABLE PROPOSAL-LEVEL IMPROVER CHANGE, IN A TWO-CLASS NATURAL WORLD

Status: DRAFT, written during TEST-6. It becomes the DEV-7 design once T06 is diagnosed. Not frozen.

## 1. Why v1 (T05 GTC) could not see anything
1. UNSEEN was a transfer floor: no derivable abstraction reached the multiplicative/modulo/gcd families.
2. The rule genomes changed ordering, insertion or observation count. Selection was invariant (20/20), and
   solved-counts are blind to efficiency.
3. The planted overfitter's premise (dev win) failed on the supply.

## 2. Requirements for an R7 probe that can return a positive (instrument doctrine)
- R0/R1 certificate: on the UNSEEN side, SOME abstraction derivable by the improver must reach families beyond
  PRISTINE. That rules out the v1 floor.
- The lever must change CANDIDACY (T52: candidacy, not preference, is the binding limit). It must not be
  ordering/insertion only.
- Transplant with RESET: the recipient gets the genome (rule) and a PRISTINE library. No inherited content crosses
  (W4 P-C: library carry-over swamps improver effects).
- Endpoint: the charge-based D delta AND the solved count.
- The overfitter control must be pre-verified on the dev supply (known answer of the control itself).

## 3. World: two-class lineage supply (W8 generator variant; world design, NOT a representation change)
- Class A (dev): the LIN supply as is. It is dominated by additive accumulation (G1 class).
- Class B (unseen): a LIN-style lineage whose founders are drawn from a different root-operator family:
  multiplicative accumulation, (acc * {H}) / gcd(acc, {H}). Same grammar (W5), same depth, same T4 screen.
  - Pre-freeze screen: a pristine donor must DERIVE a class-B base schema on class-B supply in >= 5/8 seeds, and that
    schema must reach >= 10% of held-out class-B families beyond PRISTINE.
  - This is a world change only; the DSL is untouched. It is inside the V2-B authorisation, and NOT W5P.

## 4. Genomes (content-free; proposal-level)
| Genome | Rule |
|---|---|
| g0 | I_0 |
| g6 | compose-derived: the composition move is applied to the donor's OWN LGG-derived schemas, not only to inherited schemas. Candidates may then include wrap(derived, op, atom), which is within-donor compounding (G1 -> G2 inside one donor) |
| g7 | adaptive observation: OBSERVE cells where START fails get a 2x escrow share, budget-neutral (W4 P18-like allocation; changes WHICH programs are observed, hence candidacy) |
| g5' | an overfitter, re-specified and pre-verified to win on dev: observed-finals restricted to finals seen in BOTH OBSERVE and VALIDATE |

## 5. Design
- **Stage A (development):** chains on class-A supply, g0 vs g6 vs g7 vs g5'. Endpoint: held-out class-A families.
- **Stage B (transplant, the R7 test):** the GENOME only is carried to a fresh recipient on class-B supply, with
  PRISTINE start, fresh seeds and no library from stage A. Endpoint: held-out class-B families beyond PRISTINE,
  charge delta and solved count. Compare the genome vs g0 within the same class-B seeds (common random numbers).
- **R7 readout:** a genome that beats g0 on class B, without carrying any library, has changed the IMPROVER (the
  machinery that generates improvements), not its stored data. That is the first experimentally observable R7.
- **R8 readout (within-donor):** g6's selected compositions of derived schemas solving held-out families that the
  derived base schema alone does not.

Total about 8-10 core-h.
