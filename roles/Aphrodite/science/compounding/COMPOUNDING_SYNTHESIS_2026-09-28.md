0. THE ANSWER TO THE CENTRAL QUESTION, AS FAR AS IT GOES
==============================================================================

"When abstraction composition becomes accessible, can an inherited abstraction become a
genuine stepping stone to capability that neither the inherited abstraction nor a
pristine search can reach efficiently?"

  PREREGISTERED ANSWER (AMENDMENT 19 / C2, constructed supply, n = 8):
    G1_STEPPING_STONE = NO     GENERIC_STEPPING_STONE = 0/2     S-NAT = UNTESTABLE
  Ladder for DONOR_G1 (8 replicates):
      REPRESENTABLE    yes (analytic)
      REACHABLE        8/8
      SELECTED         4/8
      SOLVED           0/8
      REUSABLE         0/8
      CAPABILITY       0/8
  Also: NOVELTY 2/8 and COMPOUNDING 4/8. Controls selected 0/8 (G1 without composition,
  PRISTINE, OFF). The G1 sign tests are 4 vs 0, p = 0.0625 each, missing 0.05.
  AMENDMENT 18 / C1 (the first design) = UNTESTABLE (supply), because of the seat's
  supply-screen flaw.

  FORENSIC EXISTENCE PROOF (one replicate; not a disposition): in C2 replicate CON1 the
  G1 donor selected the composition (v - (acc + {H})). On TWO distinct transfer families,
  (v - (acc - (last - acc))) and (v - (acc - (v - last))), that library solved 8/8
  T4-qualified cells within 4.5k-52k charges. The inherited L1 library and PRISTINE BOTH
  failed at 10M (40 x escrow; >= 190 x the G2 charge).
  That is representable -> reachable -> solved -> selected -> reusable (2 families) ->
  capability-expanding at practical budgets. The frozen ladder gave it no credit, because
  those families were drawn from SHAM_0's supply group, and SHAM_0 = (v - (acc - {H}))
  is itself a re-expression-composition of G1. The seat flagged that confound at panel
  selection: the structural check tests only literal G1.

  WHAT THIS MEANS:
    - The phenomenon EXISTS.
    - Under a treatment-blind, symmetric, preregistered test it is RARE and NARROW.
    - The binding reason is measured: selected compositions are family-specific. Each
      composed family is an instance of one of ~60 different compositions, so a
      composition selected on one validation family seldom recurs in transfer families.
      In 4/4 G1 selections the selected schema's instance set contained no transfer
      family's literal generating body. CON1's two solved families were matched
      EXTENSIONALLY (equivalent programs on the task domain).
    - REUSE, not reachability, is now the bottleneck. A stepping stone pays only when the
      world repeats the structure it enables.

2. WHAT CHANGED IN UNDERSTANDING
==============================================================================

  1. "Is recursion/compounding possible here?" has been answered, stage by stage. The
     blockers were, in order:
       (a) the old tribunal (it admits only commutative bounded folds = G1's span);
       (b) depth-2 instantiation (compositions have no extent);
       (c) no composition move (compositions are inaccessible);
       (d) junk supply (compositions are partial functions);
       (e) REUSE (compositions are too diverse to recur).
     Each has been removed or measured except (e).
  2. The E1 negative (A17) was partly forced by its task world. T4 classifies both of
     E1's non-G1 OBSERVE families as junk. The label stands; the interpretation is now
     narrower (RB-9 will document it).
  3. Novelty is cheap: 16% of random schemas qualify. Compounding claims must rest on
     selection plus reuse plus capability against matched shams, never on novelty alone.
  4. The improver's tested levers (hole count, lookahead and median selection) are nearly
     inert (RB-6: <= 4% spread). Composition is the lever that matters, and it belongs to
     the compounding program, not to P2.
  5. The learnability window of the natural T4 world is nearly empty (bimodal). This is
     probably a library/fallback budget cliff (T31). Endogenous curricula (RB-8) are the
     principled next lever.

3. IS ABSTRACTION COMPOUNDING A GOOD RESEARCH DIRECTION? (the operator asked for candour)
==============================================================================

  FOR:
    - The phenomenon exists (the CON1 existence proof).
    - The apparatus can now isolate every rung of the ladder exactly.
    - Nothing in the published record runs this controlled test.
  AGAINST:
    - In a treatment-blind world it is rare. Making it frequent requires supplies where
      the enabled structure recurs, which moves toward constructing the curriculum around
      the answer.
    - In a finite grammar "capability" is always budget-relative. The CON1 case is
      >= 190 x, not infinite.
  JUDGEMENT: worth ONE more focused step, and then a stop/continue decision on evidence.
    The step is REUSE-CONTROLLED supply: a world where each composed structure appears in
    >= 3 families, built by the same rule for G1 and for matched shams (clean shams: the
    structural check must also exclude re-expression-compositions of G1).
    - If G1 then passes H1 and shams do not: an abstraction-specific stepping stone.
    - If both pass: the stepping stone is a GENERIC property of inheritance +
      composition. That is the more interesting claim.
    - If neither passes with reuse guaranteed: stop the compounding line in this DSL.
    Expected cost: one preregistered assay (~2-3 h on M4).
    I have NOT launched it, because it changes the supply's scientific character toward
    constructed recurrence. See section 6.

6. WHAT NEEDS THE OPERATOR (high-level only)
==============================================================================

  ONE value-level choice:
    Should the next compounding assay use a REUSE-CONTROLLED supply (composed structures
    guaranteed to recur across families, built symmetrically for G1 and shams)? That is
    the only way to give reuse a fair test. It moves the supply further from "natural"
    toward "curriculum", so a positive would show that inheritance + composition CAN
    compound when the world repeats structure. It would not show that natural worlds
    present such structure.
    The alternative is to pursue the natural-world route first (RB-8 endogenous
    curricula / ladders). That is slower and more surprising if positive.
    Both are defensible. My recommendation: reuse-controlled first (fast and decisive for
    the mechanism), with RB-8 in parallel by another worker.
