rho_P is a conservative, effect-size-dependent LOWER BOUND on carrier
prevalence (sensitivity ~0.1 at +11 points, ~0.65 at +24, ~0.97 at +42).
Translating a Campaign 1 rho_P into prevalence requires stating the
assumed effect size; the confirmed-effect estimate supplies it.

## Winner's curse

Screen-stage inflation for nominated true carriers: +0.2 to +8 points
(largest for small effects, e.g. +5.5 at J 0.5). Estimate-stage bias:
within +-0.5 points in every gated world; coverage 0.92-0.97 per cell.
The three-stage split does what it was built for: selection bias stays
in the screen and never reaches the reported effect.

## Blind spot found (reported, not gated): family-correlated discoveries

FC (pi 0.10, J 2.0 on one family type in three; true effect ~+14 points):
P(DISCOVERY) 0.005 at L 32 and 64; q_confirm 0.002-0.004 against a true
carrier rate 0.10. A discovery that works on one TYPE of family is
diluted across 8 random fresh families and fails the per-lineage test
against delta. The secondary procedure detects discoveries that
transfer across the sealed family distribution, NOT family-specialised
ones. That is consistent with its purpose (transferable discovery), but
it means a Campaign 1 null on the secondary endpoint says nothing about
family-specialised discoveries.
