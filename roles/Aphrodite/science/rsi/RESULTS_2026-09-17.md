## Failure shapes (what each result is, and is not)

E1. Attributable feedback beats an end-to-end scalar by a factor that
grows with system size, and misattributed "dense" feedback is worse than
the scalar: it solved 1 of 600 runs at K >= 4 against 600 of 600. The
volume of feedback is not what helps; its attribution is. EXPLORATORY
(prereg said no prediction): DENSE_NOISY with 10% flag noise needed a
median 307.5 evals at K8 and 2379.5 at K16, WORSE than SCALAR (132.5,
351) from K8 up, and failed 5% of K16 runs. Per-signal noise multiplies
with the number of signals, so dense feedback's advantage is fragile
exactly where it is largest. Near-analytic toy; the seat's instrument
check, not a discovery.

E2. The seat's stand (saturation, no recursion dividend) was not
falsified, but the preregistered design could not test it well: the
deliberately poor theta_0 barely moved itself (lambda stayed 1, log10
sigma0 moved by at most 0.09, patience at most -6 in every chain), so
"recursive" and "fixed-meta" were nearly the same optimiser. The
gains were real on held-out tasks but ordinary.
  EXPLORATORY X1 (post-hoc noise floor, 40 random perturbations of
  theta_0 of the same size): 42.5% of single random perturbations score
  at least as well as the recursive chains' median at generation 1, and
  25% match generation 4. corr(U_train, U_test) over perturbations 0.97,
  so the landscape is real but rugged; the weak self-improver did about
  what random local search does.
  EXPLORATORY X2 (post-hoc, competent start theta = (-1, 1.5, 1, 400)):
  HONEST arm: held-out U improved in 7 of 10 chains over two
  generations, median 0.123 log10 units, mean CI [0.002, 0.151] --
  MARGINAL, unreplicated, no reading stated. Delta1 median 0.020, Delta2
  0.000. LEAKY arm: the accounting exploit was found in ONE generation
  (lambda 16 in 5 of 10 chains, >= 12 in 8 of 10 by g2; real/declared
  evaluations up to 16x), and under HONEST measurement the hacked
  optimisers were worse by a median 2.35 log10 units (~200x worse final
  objective), CI [2.15, 2.76]. The independent counter caught every
  case.
  Reading, stated as a stand to be attacked: the competence that lets an
  improver change itself is the same competence that finds the hole in
  its evaluator, and it arrives first for the hole (one generation)
  rather than for honest improvement (marginal after two). This is the
  toy analogue of the Darwin Godel Machine's verified objective hack
  (SOURCES.md). It rests on one toy and one planted bug.

E3. Verified distillation generalises to unseen shapes (0.99 vs chance)
and verification removes planted poison (0.99 vs 0.49). But the verifier
failed its own negative control: probing only training shapes, it
admitted false abstractions in worlds where the abstraction is wrong,
because probes drawn from the evidence's distribution share its
confound. That is RSIAgent's self-reported "incomplete verification"
failure, reproduced by construction. It caused no accuracy harm here
only because the fallback was chance-level; with a better fallback the
false rules would cost accuracy. Seat error, recorded.

E4. Evolving against a leaky set buys the leak (0.53 on held-out),
pooling does not guard when the leaky set dominates the pool (0.55),
and the disjoint min-fitness criterion recovers ~0.805 against a noise
ceiling of ~0.85 -- but its CI straddles the 0.80 gate, so H4b is
INDETERMINATE as preregistered. The disjoint regime still put a nonzero
weight on the leaked hint in 45% of seeds (outvoted, not removed).

## What would falsify the seat's overall reading

The reading "RSI's leverage and its hazard both sit in the feedback and
verification harness, not in self-reference per se" survives E1 (H1c),
E2 (H2c; X2 hazard) and E3/E4 (H3b, H4a/H4c), and is weakened by H3c
(the verifier itself is a harness that can be confounded) and by H4b
being indeterminate. It would be falsified by a recursion dividend in a
design where the improver can actually move itself (not yet run
preregistered), or by verification that fails to protect under an
out-of-support probe distribution.
