# ARC3 PORTFOLIO REVIEW 1 (2026-09-28 ~08:00Z; no launches while writing)

Q: Is reuse still the strongest bottleneck?
A: NOT as stated. Independent evidence has split it into three narrower claims:
   - SELECTION / VALIDATION VALLEY (W6 R2, partly supported). Reusable G1 compositions
     that solve >= 2 transfer families exist in 6/8 C2 replicates; selection kept one in
     1/6. Four of the five misses had no validation signal or were ineligible.
   - DEPTH WALL (W6 R1, Block G). 22/80 C2 families are unreachable from G1 at any number
     of compositions in W5. G3 = wrap(G2) has no extent without primitive promotion
     (PKG-5: with promotion, 30 G3 candidates are NEW with extent).
   - MEASUREMENT (W2, W3). Reuse must be counted extensionally. The frozen REUSABLE rung
     is near-unreachable by design (own group, >= 2 families).
   The composition horizon (R1) and non-specificity (R3) rivals were NOT supported.

Q: Has another bottleneck replaced it?
A: Yes, two, and both are about the INSTRUMENT, not the improver:
   (a) the escrow/walk cliff (W2): the bimodality and the CON1-style ">= 190x" ratios are
       generic cliff properties;
   (b) my own assay-construction defects: C1 (supply screen), C3 (observe floor vs the
       cliff), C3R (inert motifs).
   Three consecutive assays failed on design rather than biology. That is a warning that
   the apparatus is now complex enough to hide defects. The corrected assay must be
   pre-checked on supply before freezing, which is now being done.

Q: Is task-world engineering becoming the dominant source of apparent success?
A: Not yet: there is no success to attribute. The risk is live. C3R2 is explicitly
   mechanistic, and a positive will be labelled "mechanism under recurrence" only.

Q: Are we testing compounding or constructing curricula that guarantee it?
A: C3R2 constructs RECURRENCE, not selection success. The donor must still:
     - pick the composition from ~60 candidates by validation;
     - transfer to held-out instances;
     - beat G1_NC, P and OFF.
   The anti-guarantee checks are the sham symmetry (the shams get the same construction)
   and the OTHER-motif / off-path negatives.

Q: Is the current DSL still scientifically informative?
A: For FIRST-order compounding, yes. For SECOND-order compounding, no: depth is exhausted
   at G2. PKG-5 shows that promotion (a representation change) is the minimal fix. That
   is exactly the operator's trigger condition for a DSL-level change, but only after a
   first-order positive.

Q: Is the compounding line earning another round?
A: One: the corrected C3R2, checked on supply first. If a FAIR, pre-checked C3R2 returns
   NO with G1 AND shams unable to reuse genuine recurring compositions, that is the
   operator's stop condition ("reuse-controlled supply is fair and still produces no
   reliable compounding") and the line stops in its current form.
