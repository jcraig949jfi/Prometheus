## Sensitivity (reported, not gated)

- Holm is load-bearing: without it null false positives rise to 10.5-17.5%
  (W6) and 10.5-14% (W9).
- Pseudoreplication (every task treated as independent) manufactures
  power and false confidence: W1 recovery at L 16 rises 0.795 -> 0.94,
  and in the heterogeneous null it declares D_VAULT EQUIVALENT far more
  often (L 32: 0.375 vs 0.085). It did NOT raise false-positive flags
  much here (W9 2.5% / 2.0% / 0.5%); its harm in this generator is
  overconfidence, not false alarms.
- delta: SPECIALIZATION depends on it strongly (W4 at L 64: 0.385 at
  delta 0.02, 0.905 at 0.03, 1.000 at 0.05). The specialisation verdict
  is only as good as the delta Campaign 1 adopts. Other worlds move <=
  0.04.
- Lineage bootstrap (CORRECTED; see defects): agrees with the t analysis
  at L 32/64 (every world within 0.065); at L 16 it is more liberal (W6
  false positives ~7.5%), the known undercoverage of percentile
  bootstraps at small n. FRAGILE at L 16 only (W3 +0.155, W5 +0.155,
  W8 +0.175).
- Misspecified generator (lineage heterogeneity and improver x family
  interaction doubled): every world except W4 is still reliable at
  L <= 64; W4 falls to 0.815 at L 64. Specialisation is the fragile
  distinction.

## Defects found in the seat's own work (calibration ledger)

1. The first cheat control could not observe pseudoreplication on an
   exact null (eligibility failure); fixed by AMENDMENT 1 (W9) before any
   run.
2. The bootstrap sensitivity arm used 300 resamples: its p-value floor
   (1/300) times Holm's 16 is 0.053 > 0.05, so it could never find
   significance. First-run bootstrap recoveries (~0 for all non-null
   worlds) measured that floor, not the assay. Corrected with 4000
   resamples on identical observations; the primary analysis was never
   affected.
3. The prereg expected pseudoreplication to inflate null false positives;
   in this generator it mainly inflates power and equivalence claims.
   Reported as found.

## Unresolved ambiguities for the operator's review

- Specialisation vs transfer is delta-dependent and the least robust
  distinction; it needs L = 64 AND a well-justified delta.
- Multi-module attribution is weak; Campaign 1 can name a responsible
  module only when one module dominates.
- All of the above is conditional on this generative model (binomial
  tasks, additive logit effects, the variance components in the prereg).
  Real lineages may be heavier-tailed or interact non-additively.
- Nothing here estimates how many GPU hours 64 lineages x 8 generations
  cost; that conversion needs the substrate and host decision.
