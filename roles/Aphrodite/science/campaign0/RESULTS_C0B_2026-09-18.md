keep the false-flag upper bound far below 0.10 (false flags 0.0-1.5%),

## What the failure means

- Heavy tails and sign-changing family effects cost little: only the
  already-fragile specialisation distinction slips, just under the bar.
- Jackpot structure breaks TRANSFER detection. When ~10% of lineages
  carry the whole gain, the across-lineage mean is unchanged but its
  variance is large, and a Holm-corrected mean test at L = 64 finds it
  a quarter of the time. The assay answers "does the AVERAGE evolved
  improver transfer?" -- under jackpot structure the answer is
  small-and-noisy even though SOME lineages carry large, real,
  transferable improvements.
- This is the operator's "no effect vs below our resolution" in its
  sharpest form: a real, rare, large effect can sit below the mean
  test's resolution.

## Repair: NOT done inside 0B (prereg s1); proposed for a separate, preregistered step

The failure is an ESTIMAND question before it is an analysis question:
(E-mean) "the typical evolved improver transfers" vs (E-exist) "evolution
produces transferable improvers in at least some lineages". Candidate
repair for E-exist, to be qualified on the same nine worlds + P1-P3
before any GPU: a two-stage SCREEN-AND-CONFIRM assay -- stage 1 ranks
lineages on the existing contrasts; stage 2 re-tests the top k lineages
on fresh VAULT tasks with a per-lineage test (family-wise controlled);
a real jackpot replicates, a noise winner regresses. Its cost is a
second evaluation round for k lineages. The choice of estimand is the
operator's; see the Campaign 1 design packet.
