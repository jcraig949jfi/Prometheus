Starting scientific position

The historical record is useful but not clean.

The local engine demonstrated transferable search leverage and a successful abstraction transplant.

A23 demonstrated a generic inheritance + composition + recurrence mechanism under constructed recurrence.

That mechanism is structure-specific, budget-relative and not specific to G1; the clean shams also succeeded.

The A23 donor frequently recovered the planted motif itself. Therefore the positive should be read primarily as a demonstrated mechanism of selection and transfer under visible recurrence, not as spontaneous invention of a qualitatively new abstraction.

Natural lineage task generation produced substantial recurrence, but much of that recurrence occurred outside the assay’s learnability window.

The historical 250k escrow setting sat near an especially poor visibility region and therefore cannot be treated as a neutral observation window.

The improver itself remained immutable throughout the principal engine programme. What evolved was primarily an inherited proposal/search library.

Therefore historical BOUNDED_RSI = NO does not constitute strong evidence against recursive improvement in general. The apparatus largely could not express improver-level change.

Second-order chaining hits an explicit representation wall: G2 consumes W5’s available grammar depth, leaving G3 without extent.

The V2-B causal ladder

Every serious experiment identifies which rung it addresses:

R0 — REPRESENTABLE
Can the representation express the candidate mechanism?

R1 — REACHABLE
Does a path to it exist under the declared operators?

R2 — FINDABLE
Can the declared search reach it under the allowed resources?

R3 — SELECTABLE
Does the validation/fitness process prefer it for the intended reason?

R4 — SOLVED
Does it solve held-out members of the target family?

R5 — REUSED
Does the inherited object help on genuinely new instances/families?

R6 — CAUSAL
Does removing or ablating the inherited structure remove the effect?

R7 — IMPROVER_CHANGED
Did machinery for generating future improvements change, rather than only its stored data or ordering prior?

R8 — COMPOUNDED
Did an acquired improvement enable acquisition of a later improvement unavailable to the prior process?

R9 — RECURRED
Can the new improvement process itself participate in another such step?

A negative at R8 is uninterpretable if R0 or R7 was impossible.

Technical reruns

Technical failure is distinct from a scientific outcome.

A technically failed experiment may be rerun up to three times after the original attempt.

Examples include process death, corrupt checkpoint, malformed rows, evaluator disagreement, lease failure, missing artifact, resource termination, failed positive control or replay mismatch.

Normally an intervening DEV window diagnoses or repairs the defect.

If the repair changes a scientific variable, the repaired execution receives a new experiment version. The failed attempt remains part of the historical record.

After the third technical rerun, close the experiment BLOCKED_TECHNICAL unless separately reopened.

Scientific NULL/NO/INCONCLUSIVE outcomes do not automatically earn reruns.

Instrument doctrine

DEV diagnostic rule

After each TEST, the next DEV identifies the first failed rung:

REPRESENTATION → REACHABILITY → FINDABILITY → SELECTION → SOLUTION → REUSE → CAUSALITY → IMPROVER CHANGE → COMPOUNDING.

Fix the earliest failed rung before increasing compute downstream.

Examples:

If no target mechanisms exist in the supply, repair the world rather than the optimiser.

If they exist but search cannot reach them, repair the operators/search.

If candidates are found but validation does not prefer reusable ones, repair selection.

If selection succeeds but transfer does not, inspect representation/identity.

If transfer succeeds but the later improver is unchanged, do not call it recursive improvement.

If G2 exists but G3 is unrepresentable, record a representation ceiling before building bigger searches.
