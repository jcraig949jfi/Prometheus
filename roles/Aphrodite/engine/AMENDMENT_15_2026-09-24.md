5. SEMANTICALLY_NEW (operator C2)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

BODY MECHANISM KEY. A body b is a function of (acc, v, first, last). Its
signature is its value vector (FAIL included) over the frozen grid
    acc in {-10^6, -97, -30, -7, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 7, 30, 97, 10^6}
    v in {2, 3, 7, 30}, first in {2, 5, 30}, last in {1, 2, 7, 33, 97}.
Its negation conjugate is b*(acc, ...) = 0 - b(0 - acc, ...). The KEY of b is
the unordered pair {sig(b), sig(b*)}. Two bodies with equal keys are the same
mechanism, up to re-expression and whole-program negation conjugation (the
S1-certified conjugacy; a scale or offset is not an equivalence under the
ceiling, AMENDMENT 12 ADDENDUM 1).

G1 MECHANISMS = the keys of every in-space instantiation of (acc + {H}).
A candidate schema S is SEMANTICALLY_NEW iff at least one in-space
instantiation of S has a key OUTSIDE G1 MECHANISMS, and that body mentions
v. If every instantiation's key lies inside G1 MECHANISMS, then S is
equivalent to, a re-expression of, a specialisation of, or a conjugate of G1:
NOT new. "Useful" is established only at transfer (R3c).
