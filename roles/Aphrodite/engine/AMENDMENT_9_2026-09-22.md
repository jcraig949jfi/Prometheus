# LOCAL ENGINE AMENDMENT 9 -- TIER 3A: transplantable improver modification
# FROZEN BEFORE THE EXPRESSIVITY GATE, BEFORE META-DEVELOPMENT, BEFORE TRANSPLANT

Dated 2026-09-22, applying the operator's TIER 3A ruling. Campaign 1 remains
unchanged and unrun. Exactly ONE meta-generation is performed:

    improver_0  ->  frozen improver-modification artifact  ->  fresh improver_1

and the slice stops there. No Tier-3A recipient evolves a further generation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE MUTABLE OBJECT, AND EVERYTHING THAT IS NOT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

MUTABLE (exactly one component): the improver's SEARCH-COORDINATE / PROPOSAL
LIBRARY -- an ordered list of entries, each a structural macro with typed
holes, used to generate candidate worker artifacts.

    LIBRARY := [ ENTRY, ... ] ; ENTRY := (name, INIT_set, BODY_set, FINAL_set)
    The improver enumerates each entry's instantiations in order, then falls
    back to the COMPLETE base grammar G4.

IMMUTABLE, and outside the mutable artifact: the evaluator; the hostile
tribunal; the fitness/scoring rule; the selection rule; the escrow and its
accounting; the recipient constructor; the membrane; the task generators;
the entropy plumbing; the base grammar G4 and its semantics; and the
mutation machinery that generates candidate proposal libraries.

NO unrestricted source-code self-modification. A library entry is DATA --
three sets of expression strings -- and cannot express anything except a
selection over G4 programs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. EXPRESSIVITY-EQUIVALENCE GATE (certified before any Tier-3 run)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every entry in any proposal library must MECHANICALLY DESUGAR into unchanged
G4: each (init, body, final) triple it can emit must be a legal G4 fold
program, verified by membership in G4's declared component spaces and by
identical outputs on a shared input battery.

Both PRISTINE and MODIFIED improvers retain the COMPLETE base grammar as
fallback, so each contains G4 verbatim.

    CERTIFY: expressible(PRISTINE) == expressible(MODIFIED)

If the evolved improver can express worker programs the pristine improver
cannot, the slice STOPS and reports CAPABILITY_SUPPLY_NOT_IMPROVEMENT.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. META-DEVELOPMENT PHASE (the donor)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The donor improver receives a frozen set of meta-development problems drawn
mechanically from the predeclared META-DEV CATALOG (section 3a). It may:
  1. search for worker solutions on those problems;
  2. observe its OWN successful artifacts;
  3. generate candidate proposal libraries using the declared, immutable
     mutation operators (section 3b);
  4. evaluate candidate libraries ONLY on the meta-development problems;
  5. select among them under a fixed META-LEVEL ESCROW.

The donor never sees the tribunal, the meta-tribunal families, or any
evaluation outside its own development instances.

3a. FROZEN CATALOGS, declared here, disjoint by construction.
    Families are (BODY, FINAL) pairs over G4; names encode both.

    META-DEV CATALOG (proposal-library evolution only):
        md_sum_times_last     body acc+v            final acc*last
        md_prod_plus_first    body acc*v            final acc+first
        md_gcd_minus_first    body gcd(acc,v)       final acc-first
        md_sumsq_plus_last    body acc+(v*v)        final acc+last

    META-TRIBUNAL CATALOG (previously unseen; used ONLY after the artifact
    is frozen). Deliberately mixed, so over-specialisation is detectable:
        mt_sum_minus_last     body acc+v            final acc-last   [seen body]
        mt_gcd_plus_first     body gcd(acc,v)       final acc+first  [seen body]
        mt_summod_times_first body acc+(v%last)     final acc*first  [UNSEEN body]
        mt_prodshift_minus_first body acc*(v+first) final acc-first  [UNSEEN body]

    The meta-tribunal families participated in NO donor development, NO
    proposal-library evolution, NO macro extraction and NO family-selection
    decision. Two share a body kind with meta-dev (transfer is possible) and
    two do not (over-specialisation is exposed). Per-family results are
    reported separately and never aggregated favourably.

3b. MUTATION OPERATORS over libraries (immutable machinery):
    OP1 SPECIALISE_FROM_SUCCESSES -- build an entry whose INIT/BODY sets are
        the parts actually observed in the donor's own successful programs,
        keeping the FINAL set general; place it before the existing entries.
    OP2 ANTIUNIFY_PAIR -- anti-unify two successful programs by the same
        Plotkin LGG used in slice 3 and create an entry from the result.
    OP3 REORDER -- move an entry earlier.
    OP4 DROP -- remove an entry.
    Candidate libraries are generated by applying these operators; none can
    introduce a program outside G4.

3c. META-FITNESS (immutable): mean charges to a development-exact solution
    across the meta-dev problems, with failures censored at the meta-dev
    escrow. Lower is better; ties are broken by fewer entries, then by
    smaller total library size. The tribunal plays no part.

3d. EXTRACTION: the selected library is taken at a FIXED POSITIONAL BOUNDARY
    (the end of the declared meta-generation), canonicalised as sorted JSON,
    and SHA-256 hashed. After freeze no further adaptation is permitted.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. FRESH-IMPROVER TRANSPLANT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Fresh improvers are constructed with NO donor history: no cached solutions,
no development instances, no observed artifacts, no entropy derived from the
donor. Only the frozen library bytes cross.

    EVOLVED   fresh improver + the frozen evolved proposal library
    PRISTINE  fresh improver + the original proposal library L0
    SHAM      fresh improver + an equal-interface, equal-size library
              modification that failed to provide useful structure, produced
              by a DECLARED mechanical rewrite (the specialising entry built
              from the donor's FAILED bodies rather than its successful ones)
    POSITIVE  only if needed to establish assay sensitivity

All arms share the identical base grammar, evaluator, selection rule,
budgets and ultimate expressive power.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. ENDPOINTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

PRIMARY: restricted search effort across ALL recipients, with failures
charged at the fixed escrow boundary (censored mean). Paired recipient seeds
across arms.

ALSO REPORTED: fixed-budget probability of a tribunal-qualified artifact;
charges to first qualified artifact; tribunal score; extrapolation score;
search trajectories (which coordinate produced the solution); whether
PRISTINE independently reconstructs structures present in the evolved
library; and per-family effects WITHOUT favourable aggregation.

Conditional statistics among successful recipients are TELEMETRY ONLY and
cannot establish improvement by themselves. (Slice 4 is the cautionary
precedent: winners-only medians pointed the opposite way to the censored
means, purely through survivorship.)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. THE RECURSIVE-IMPROVEMENT CRITERION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Tier 3A qualifies ONLY if the frozen evolved artifact, transplanted into
fresh improvers facing previously unseen problems:
  C1 preserves expressivity equivalence with PRISTINE;
  C2 produces a reproducible improvement in discovery efficiency or
     fixed-budget discovery probability;
  C3 survives hostile tribunal evaluation of the worker artifacts it finds;
  C4 relies on no donor history, cached solutions, evaluator information or
     undeclared state;
  C5 outperforms SHAM;
  C6 shows a useful effect across MORE THAN ONE independently selected
     problem family.
One successful task is a fossil, not a recursive-improvement claim.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. COST ACCOUNTING (two efficiencies, reported separately)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

MARGINAL INHERITANCE VALUE: how much better is a fresh improver after
receiving the evolved library -- the per-recipient reduction in censored
search effort against PRISTINE.

AMORTIZED VALUE: does the downstream search saved repay the meta-search cost
required to evolve the library? Reported as the total meta-development
charges and the BREAK-EVEN NUMBER OF DESCENDANTS at which cumulative savings
equal that cost. An enormously expensive meta-search will not be hidden
behind cheap descendant searches. A mechanism may qualify scientifically
while remaining economically negative, and that will be said plainly.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. THE PERMITTED CLAIM, AND WHAT MAY NOT BE INFERRED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

If the gate passes:

  "The system produced a bounded, transferable modification to its own
   improvement machinery that caused fresh copies of that machinery to
   discover independently specified solutions more efficiently under equal
   expressive power and equal resources."

  LABEL: BOUNDED_RECURSIVE_SELF_IMPROVEMENT = YES

NOT to be inferred, and to be denied explicitly in the report: runaway
improvement; indefinite recursion; monotonic improvement across generations;
general intelligence improvement; autonomous goal modification. Each needs
its own experiment.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. NO SECOND RECURSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Tier-3A recipient does NOT evolve a further generation of improver
modifications. Exactly one meta-generation. A second requires a new ruling,
and this seat will not request one inside this slice.
