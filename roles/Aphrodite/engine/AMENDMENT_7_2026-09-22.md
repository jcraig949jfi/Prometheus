--------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. THE ORGAN-EXTRACTION PROCEDURE (declared before it is run)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The reusable fold is NOT defined by hand. It is computed by first-order
syntactic ANTI-UNIFICATION (Plotkin's least general generalisation) over
the two structures slice 2C discovered independently:

    list_sum       INIT = 0   E = (acc + v)   F = acc
    list_prod_mod  INIT = 1   E = (acc * v)   F = (acc % last)

Each discovered part is parsed into a term over the declared grammar:
variables {acc, v, last}, function symbols = the declared primitives,
integer literals as constants. The procedure:

  ANTIUNIFY(s, t):
    if s and t are identical terms            -> that term
    if s = f(s1..sn) and t = f(t1..tn), same
       symbol and arity                       -> f(ANTIUNIFY(si, ti)...)
    if s = f(s1..sn) and t = g(t1..tn) with
       f != g but n == arity matches          -> HOLE of arity n applied to
                                                 ANTIUNIFY(si, ti)...
    otherwise (leaf vs node, or arity clash)  -> HOLE covering the position
  Holes are numbered left-to-right, depth-first, and TYPED by the position
  they occupy (Int for value positions; an operator hole records its
  argument terms). Identical mismatched pairs map to the SAME hole
  (consistent generalisation).

Determinism: the traversal order is fixed, hole numbering is positional,
and the result is unique up to hole renaming. ADMISSIBILITY: the result is
admissible only if (i) it contains at least one hole, (ii) every hole has a
declared filling grammar, and (iii) instantiating the holes with the
original values recovers BOTH input structures exactly. If more than one
admissible result exists, or if any human choice is required to pick one,
the slice STOPS and reports ORGAN_NOT_IDENTIFIABLE.

WHAT IS NOT ANTI-UNIFIED. `trailing` (whether the final number is a query
parameter held outside the sequence) was never discovered by search: it was
fixed by each family's declared shape in slice 2C. It is therefore a
property of the family, not of the organ, and it is not a hole. The frozen
structural skeleton is exactly:

    acc = <HOLE>
    for v in vals:
        acc = <HOLE>(acc, v)
    return <HOLE>(acc, last)

HOLE FILLING GRAMMARS (unchanged from the slice-2C declared fold space):
    INIT hole : {0, 1}
    E hole    : expressions over {acc, v} of depth <= 2 in the declared
                primitives
    F hole    : `acc`, plus single primitive applications over {acc, last}
                for query-parameter families

The organ is serialised canonically and SHA-256 hashed BEFORE any third
family is selected or any recipient is executed.

B. THE FROZEN FAMILY CATALOG AND THE MECHANICAL SELECTION RULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The catalog is authored HERE, in advance of selection and in advance of any
transplant measurement. It is frozen on commit of this amendment.

    list_alt_sum   alternating sum of the sequence
    list_gcd       gcd of the whole sequence
    list_max       maximum of the sequence
    list_prod      product of the sequence
    list_sum_mod   sum of the sequence, modulo a trailing query parameter

SELECTION RULE, declared before any transplant performance is measured:
  Order the catalog LEXICOGRAPHICALLY by family name. Select the FIRST
  family that satisfies ALL admissibility conditions below, as established
  by the reachability certificate. Transplant performance plays no part; no
  family is chosen, re-ordered or discarded on the basis of how any arm
  performs on it.

ADMISSIBILITY (all five required):
  S1 unseen during slice-2C evolution;
  S2 basis-insufficient: no depth/size-bounded composition of the declared
     primitives solves it, including when given the trailing parameter;
  S3 reachable with the candidate organ plus admissible hole fillings;
  S4 independently falsifiable by a hostile tribunal (counterexample
     generators plus oracle-free metamorphic relations);
  S5 variable-length, with tribunal evaluation extrapolating beyond
     development lengths.

If no catalog family satisfies S1-S5, the slice stops and reports
NO_ADMISSIBLE_THIRD_FAMILY.

C. CLOSED TRANSPLANT ARM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

One complete discovered fold implementation is transplanted UNCHANGED into
a fresh recipient. No adaptation of INIT, E, F, control flow or constants
is permitted; the recipient may not search.

WHICH FOLD, declared mechanically: the fold belonging to the
lexicographically first slice-2C family, i.e. `list_prod_mod`
(list_prod_mod < list_sum).

This arm tests DIRECT COMPETENCE REUSE. A null is scientifically
acceptable and MUST NOT trigger modification of anything.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
D. OPEN-ORGAN TRANSPLANT ARM AND ITS CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Four arms, each with N = 16 independent recipients, independent development
entropy per recipient, identical total escrow, and one independently hidden
post-freeze tribunal:

  1 SCRATCH   fresh recipient, no transplanted organ, searching from the
              ORIGINAL grammar (composition + the full fold space)
  2 ORGAN     fresh recipient containing the extracted organ with the
              structural skeleton FROZEN, searching ONLY the declared holes
  3 SHAM      fresh recipient carrying an equal-size structural scaffold
              that FAILED the slice-2C load-bearing test (the decorative
              `h // h` loop fossil), searching the original grammar as
              scratch does. This tests whether merely carrying structure
              helps.
  4 POSITIVE  a hand-written correct solver, to establish that the third
              family is solvable under the declared budget at all.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
E. PRIMARY MEASUREMENT (not final score alone)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

    M1 candidate CHARGES to the first tribunal-qualified solution
    M2 probability of reaching a tribunal-qualified solution within the
       fixed escrow (qualified recipients / 16, per arm)
    M3 final tribunal performance
    M4 extrapolation performance (lengths beyond development)
    M5 artifact complexity (canonical bytes, and AST node count of the
       discovered parts)

Wall-clock time is NOT the primary efficiency measure and is reported only
as an aside. Search never sees the tribunal: qualification is determined
post-freeze, and M1 is the charge count recorded at the moment the
recipient adopted the solution that later qualified.

F. INTERPRETATION RULES (fixed before the numbers exist)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

  If the CLOSED transplant succeeds on the third family:
      DIRECT_COMPETENCE_REUSE = YES

  If the closed transplant fails, but the ORGAN arm reliably reaches
  qualified solutions with materially less search than SCRATCH:
      STRUCTURAL_SEARCH_LEVERAGE = YES
  read as: an evolved structural artifact improved a fresh recipient's
  capacity to ACQUIRE A NEW CAPABILITY.

  "Materially less search" is declared now, not chosen afterwards: the
  ORGAN arm's median M1 must be at most half of SCRATCH's median M1, AND
  the ORGAN arm's M2 must be at least as high as SCRATCH's. SHAM must not
  show the same advantage; if SHAM matches ORGAN, the effect is attributed
  to carrying scaffolding rather than to the organ.

  THIS IS NOT RECURSIVE SELF-IMPROVEMENT and will not be described as such.
  The mutation generator, evaluator, selection algorithm and evolve()
  remain unchanged, so nothing in this slice improves the improver.
