2. S3 -- THE DONOR'S DERIVATION PROCEDURE (mechanical, all declared here)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

D1 OBSERVE. On each qualified OBSERVE family, with the qualified development
   size and 3 replicate cells (S2 keyed order, cells independent of any
   library), the donor searches with the PRISTINE library and keeps the first
   development-consistent program per cell. It never sees a tribunal.
D2 CLASSES. Observed programs are grouped by S1 behavior_id.
D3 MEMBERS. For each observed class, the donor enumerates EVERY member within
   its own declared search space M = H1 x H2 x FINAL_SPACE (the PRISTINE
   entry, 151,920 programs): the programs whose behavior_id equals the class's.
   It first filters on the class's development examples, then confirms on the
   full B1 v2. The class, not a representative, is the unit.
D4 ANTI-UNIFICATION. For every pair of DISTINCT observed classes and every
   pair of their members, compute the standard first-order least general
   generalisation (LGG) of the two BODY terms (on the S1 structural form):
   identical subterms are kept, and each differing subterm pair becomes a
   hole, with the same pair mapped to the same hole. Keep a result as a
   CANDIDATE SCHEMA iff it has exactly ONE hole and the hole is not the root.
   Hole fillers range over the declared LEVEL1 space (the Tier-3C filler
   grammar), restricted to bodies inside BODY_SPACE. No operator holes, no
   hand-written schema, no schema from any sham or control.
D5 CANDIDATE LIBRARIES, all falling back to PRISTINE and G4 (equal expressive
   power):
     UNCHANGED   = PRISTINE;
     MEMORISE    = one entry holding the member bodies of the observed classes;
     SCHEMA_k    = one entry per candidate schema k (the derived schema's
                   instantiations; inits H1; finals FINAL_SPACE);
     SCHEMA_ALL  = one entry holding every candidate schema.
   Candidates are hashed before scoring.
D6 SELECTION. S2's paired rule (AMENDMENT 13 s3) over the qualified VALIDATE
   families, R = 8 cells each, escrow 250,000, cost censored at the escrow.

S3 OUTCOME:
  ENDOGENOUS_ABSTRACTION = YES  iff the selected library carries at least one
    hole-bearing schema produced by D4;
  ENDOGENOUS_ABSTRACTION = NO   iff D4 produced no schema, or the selected
    library is UNCHANGED or MEMORISE. This is a RESULT. S4 does NOT run, and
    the seat returns to the operator with it.
Reported either way: every candidate schema; whether any is semantically
equal (over its instantiation set) to `(acc + {H})`, reported and NEVER used
by the procedure; per-cell paired costs; the donor's meta-charges.

3. S4 -- TRANSPLANT OF THE DERIVED ABSTRACTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

ARMS (all fall back to PRISTINE + G4; all use emitter v2 and S2 keyed order):
  DERIVED            the library D6 selected, frozen and hashed before any
                     transfer family is touched -- THE TREATMENT;
  PRISTINE;
  SHAM_0..SHAM_7     the preregistered sham distribution: each carries 6 random
                     H2 behavior-class representatives and 1 random one-hole
                     schema, drawn from the LGG of random pairs of H2 members,
                     under seeds sha256("APHRODITE/T3D/SHAM/v1/<k>"), hashed
                     before the donor runs. (A sham may draw a useful schema by
                     chance, as Tier 3C's did; that only makes condition 3
                     harder.)
  POSITIVE_CONTROL   one entry with the schema `(acc + {H})` -- S5,
                     SENSITIVITY ONLY; it never enters the criterion;
  MEMORISE           the donor's memorise candidate -- sensitivity only.

S7 DIFFICULTY CONTROL. Before the treatment is evaluated, each transfer family
must be SOLVABLE: at least one of {POSITIVE_CONTROL, PRISTINE, SHAM_0..7}
qualifies (tribunal) in >= 8 of 16 PILOT recipients, whose seeds are disjoint
from the test recipients' (label "T3D-pilot"). DERIVED is excluded from
difficulty control, so families are never chosen by the treatment's success.
Every transfer family must also pass G2 generator qualification and the
positive-control-witness tribunal (the witness artifact qualifies). If fewer
than 2 unseen-body families survive, S4 STOPS before testing. That positive
control certifies the additive families is stated openly: it is an admission
bias that acts equally on every arm, never selection by the treatment.

RECIPIENTS: N = 16 per family per arm; escrow 250,000; up to 5 development-
consistent hits, each judged by the hostile MetaTribunal (Tier-3C
configuration) after freezing; recipient i on family f uses cell seed
("T3D-rx/<f>/<i>") in EVERY arm (S2 pairing).

THE S4 CRITERION (the eight Tier-3C conditions, treatment = DERIVED):
  1 expressive equivalence preserved (every arm desugars into G4);
  2 DERIVED improves qualified discovery vs PRISTINE: paired censored effort
    lower with the one-sided 95% bound excluding 0, on the admitted families
    pooled, AND at least as many qualified recipients in every admitted family;
  3 DERIVED beats the sham distribution: its censored effort is below every
    sham's on at least half the admitted families, and below the sham median
    on every family where it claims an effect;
  4 survives hostile evaluation (only tribunal-qualified solutions count);
  5 false-positive acceleration <= 25% of hits (the declared limit);
  6 useful effect on MULTIPLE (>= 2) admitted families;
  7 at least TWO of those are families whose SOLVING program's whole-program
    behavior class is ABSENT from the donor's observed classes (S1 identity);
  8 no donor state or evaluator information crosses (membrane receipts).
All eight: ABSTRACTION_TRANSPLANT = YES, and the operator's open clause is
answered positively for this engine: the improver discovered the abstraction
AND it transferred. Otherwise NO, with the failed conditions named.

ECONOMICS as AMENDMENT 11 s9 (horizon 64). Heterogeneity across families is
