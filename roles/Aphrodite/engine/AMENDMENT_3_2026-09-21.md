0. A CORRECTION OWED FROM SLICE 2 (pre-data, unprompted)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Slice 2 reported numtheory's `a*b + 1` as development-distribution
exploitation. That is true but INCOMPLETE, and the missing half favours
the apparatus less flatteringly than it favours the seat's narrative.

The v1 search performs two combination rounds, so the deepest expression
it can construct is op(depth2, depth2) -- depth 3. The true solution
    add( gcd(a,b), fdiv( mul(a,b), gcd(a,b) ) )
is op(depth2, op(depth2, depth2)) -- depth 4. IT WAS NEVER IN THE SEARCH
SPACE. The shortcut was therefore not only the most exploitative thing
available, it was close to the only thing available that fit.

Consequence for the fossil's label: numtheory remains an ADVERSARIAL
SHORTCUT FOSSIL, and its held-out 0.635 remains consistent with coprime
exploitation (6/pi^2 = 0.6079), but the causal story is joint --
saturating development signal AND an enumeration bound that excluded the
correct program. Both go in the record.

This also predicts something testable in this slice: a helper function
makes the true solution reachable at depth 3, because
add(h, fdiv(mul(n0,n1), h)) is op(terminal, op(depth2, terminal)). If a
structural template unlocks a program that pure composition could not
reach under an identical depth budget, that is the cleanest available
demonstration that STRUCTURE buys something COMPOSITION does not.

1. RULING 1 -- LINEAGE INDEPENDENCE. FROZEN DERIVATION RULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The ten v1 lineages are NOT independent experimental units: identical
development instances and an identical deterministic search made them
ten replays of one discovery trajectory. Replicated execution of an
identical trajectory is not lineage-level n>1, and will never again be
reported as such.

FROZEN DERIVATION RULE, declared before any run under it. Each launched
lineage receives independently derived development entropy from a
preregistered lineage identifier:

    dev_entropy(lineage_id, generation) =
        int(sha256("APHRODITE/ENGINE/DEV/v1/" + lineage_id + "/"
                   + str(generation)).hexdigest()[:16], 16)

    search_entropy(lineage_id) =
        int(sha256("APHRODITE/ENGINE/SEARCH/v1/" + lineage_id)
            .hexdigest()[:16], 16)

Development entropy MAY influence development instances and allowed
stochastic search decisions (search ordering, which candidates are
examined first under a bounded budget). It MUST NOT influence or reveal
tribunal entropy, tribunal instances, post-extraction evaluation, the
extraction generation, or recipient construction.

TRIBUNAL ENTROPY is drawn from a disjoint domain that contains no
lineage identifier:

    tribunal_entropy(capability_class, index) =
        int(sha256("APHRODITE/ENGINE/TRIBUNAL/v1/" + capability_class
                   + "/" + str(index)).hexdigest()[:16], 16)

ENFORCEMENT, not promise: the tribunal refuses to construct itself
without a frozen generation-8 artifact hash, and the evolving lineage is
never passed a tribunal object. Tested, not asserted.

Justification is EXPERIMENTAL-UNIT VALIDITY, not artifact diversity.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. RULING 2 -- DIVERSITY IS OBSERVED, NEVER ENGINEERED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Different artifact hashes are not a goal and not a pass condition.
Independent lineages may legitimately converge. Two quantities are
reported side by side, always:

    independent_launched_search_realizations
    distinct_extracted_artifact_hashes

Convergence under independent randomisation is evidence about the search
LANDSCAPE. Divergence into different shortcuts is evidence about the
search PROCESS. Neither is engineered in advance, and neither is a
success criterion.

4. RULING 4 -- HOSTILE TRIBUNAL (post-freeze only)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The development distribution is RETAINED unchanged: five classes,
DEV_PER_FAMILY = 2. Shortcuts are not cured by feeding the improver more
examples. Evaluation is strengthened after the freeze instead.

Each capability class gets, built from tribunal entropy only:
  - an ordinary held-out instance generator;
  - COUNTEREXAMPLE GENERATORS aimed at regions where known shortcuts
    fail. For numtheory these DELIBERATELY include non-coprime pairs:
    shared prime factors, one number dividing the other, equal numbers,
    and common-multiple constructions -- precisely where `a*b + 1`
    breaks. For modexp: m = 1, a divisible by m, a == m, and a > m with
    small exponents.
  - METAMORPHIC CHECKS, which need no oracle re-derivation:
      numtheory  f(a,b) == f(b,a)                     (symmetry)
                 f(k*a, k*b) == k * f(a,b)            (scaling)
      modexp     g(a,b,m) == g(a mod m, b, m)         (reduction)
                 g(a,b,m) == (g(a,b-1,m) * a) mod m   (recurrence)

NO TRIBUNAL INFORMATION FLOWS BACK to the lineage that produced the
artifact. There is no repair loop: a failing artifact is recorded as
failing.

5. RULING 5 -- PERMITTED SEARCH SPACE FOR THIS SLICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The mutation grammar may extend from program composition into BOUNDED
Tier-2 structural transformations. Implemented this slice:
  - composition of primitives (v1, retained unchanged);
  - HELPER-FUNCTION CONSTRUCTION with BOUNDED LOOPS: a helper of the
    form `while <guard> and steps < BOUND: (x, y) = (E1(x,y), E2(x,y))`,
    where E1 and E2 are searched over the expression grammar and are NOT
    supplied. Euclid's algorithm is expressible as E1 = y, E2 = x % y,
    and is therefore DISCOVERABLE, not handed;
  - BOUNDED CONDITIONAL control flow in the answer expression;
  - the helper as an INTERMEDIATE REPRESENTATION: once found, it becomes
    an additional terminal for the composition search.
Declared as permitted but NOT implemented this slice, so that the record
is not read as broader than the work: memoization/state mechanisms and
decomposition into multiple reusable subprocedures.

IMMUTABLE this slice, per the ruling: evolve(), the selection rule, the
evaluation protocol, the escrow and its accounting, and the
mutation-generation machinery. The ONLY changes are the entropy plumbing
required by RULING 1 and the grammar extension of RULING 5.

TIER 3 REMAINS FORBIDDEN and is not self-authorized. For the avoidance
of doubt, this seat treats as Tier 3: any lineage modification of
evolve(), of the selection rule, of the escrow or its accounting, of the
evaluator or tribunal, or of the improver's own mutation machinery;
unbounded loops or recursion; and any capability to write outside the
five module sources.

6. RULING 6 -- SUCCESS CRITERION AND YIELD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The slice succeeds as an ENGINE-CAPABILITY DEMONSTRATION (existence
only) if at least one independently launched lineage produces a non-base
generation-8 artifact that:
  C1 arose endogenously;
  C2 crosses the qualified membrane;
  C3 improves a fresh recipient on an independent hostile tribunal;
  C4 survives counterexample generation;
  C5 improves at least two independently generated/falsified capability
     classes;
  C6 contains a STRUCTURAL computational change -- not only tuned
     constants, answer memory, or direct single-expression dispatch.

C6 is checked on the artifact bytes: a qualifying artifact must contain
a helper definition with bounded control flow that the answer path
actually calls. A single-expression dispatch does not qualify however
accurate it is.

REPORTED SEPARATELY AND ALWAYS TOGETHER:
    discovery_yield = qualifying_artifacts / independent_launched_lineages
One success establishes EXISTENCE ONLY. Reliability is not inferred from
it, and the yield denominator is never quietly dropped.

PREDECLARED HONEST OUTCOME. There is a real possibility that the yield
is 0, for a reason that is itself the finding: selection is driven by
development accuracy, a shortcut can saturate it at 1.00, and the
tribunal is invisible before the freeze -- so nothing rewards structure
over a lucky shortcut. If that is what happens it will be reported as
the result, and the cure will NOT be to leak tribunal signal into
selection or to enlarge the development sample.
