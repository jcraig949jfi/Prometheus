1. THE QUESTION, AND WHY TIER 3B COULD NOT ANSWER IT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Can an improver form SEMANTIC ABSTRACTIONS from its own successful worker
artifacts, inherit them through a clean transplant, and thereby improve fresh
improvers across MULTIPLE previously unseen families?

Tier 3B's donor could only MEMORISE: its single operator collected the body
classes it had observed. A memorised body helps exactly on families that need
that body and costs elsewhere, which is precisely what the results showed --
47x where the class matched, worse where it did not, nothing on unseen bodies.

Tier 3C therefore adds an operator that GENERALISES. Anti-unification over
the donor's own successful bodies yields hole-bearing SCHEMAS:

    observed: add(acc, v), add(acc, mul(v, v)), mul(acc, v), gcd(acc, v)
    schemas:  add(acc, H)   -- H over the declared sub-expression space
              OP(acc, v)    -- OP over the declared primitives

A schema like add(acc, H) covers add(acc, mod(v, last)) -- a body the donor
NEVER SAW. That is the mechanism by which cross-family transfer can occur
without memorisation, and it is what condition 7 of the criterion tests.

2. LAYERED SEMANTIC IDENTITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

    source provenance  ->  structural canonical form  ->  semantic class

  - SOURCE is retained for ancestry and provenance and is NEVER mechanism
    identity;
  - STRUCTURAL CANONICAL FORM (cheap AST normalisation: commutative ordering,
    algebraic identities, canonical primitive names) is the high-volume path,
    used for deduplication and display;
  - SEMANTIC CLASS is formed by the FROZEN stronger procedure: the
    denotational signature over the declared exhaustive probe battery.

The procedure is fixed and tested BEFORE the donor artifact is produced. The
known Tier-3A/B aliases are REGRESSION FIXTURES, not special-case rewrites;
every rule is stated over variables and verified on expressions no donor
produced.

3. THE GENERATOR IS QUALIFIED, NOT ONE INSTANCE SET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Tier 3B qualified one greedy-selected sample to basin zero and then gave
lineages fresh draws; the guarantee did not transfer (4.688 false positives
per recipient on a family whose qualified basin was zero). That procedure is
retired.

For each family, BEFORE meta-development:
  1. freeze the family-specific instance generator;
  2. draw a preregistered CALIBRATION BATTERY across many independent seeds
     (declared: 200 independent development sets per candidate size);
  3. enumerate the reachable wrong semantic classes under a frozen rule (the
     PRISTINE entry space, deduplicated by semantic class);
  4. measure each wrong class's SURVIVAL RATE across those independent
     development sets -- the fraction of draws on which it is
     indistinguishable from the target;
  5. establish a confidence bound on residual false-positive discrimination:
     the declared statistic is the mean number of surviving wrong classes per
     draw, with a 95% upper bound by the normal approximation;
  6. REJECT the family or the generator if the preregistered threshold is not
     met. THRESHOLD, declared now: the 95% upper bound on surviving wrong
     classes per draw must be < 0.05 at the chosen development size, with a
     cap of 24 instances.

After qualification each lineage receives INDEPENDENT FRESH development
entropy from that unchanged generator. The guarantee is then a property of
the generator, which is what lineages actually sample.

4. THE DONOR ARTIFACT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The donor evolves ONLY its proposal / search-coordinate library. Its inputs
from successful worker artifacts are SEMANTIC CLASSES, never raw source
strings. It never receives tribunal results.

At the fixed positional boundary the library is frozen, canonicalised as
sorted JSON, hashed, and its semantic classes recorded. No post-hoc editing
or relabelling.

4a. THE DONOR SPLITS ITS OWN DEVELOPMENT PROBLEMS, declared in advance.

Tier 3B's donor scored candidate libraries on the SAME families it had
observed successes from. In-sample cost always favours MEMORISATION: a
library holding four concrete bodies beats a schema enumerating 258 fillers
on the very families those four bodies solve. The donor was therefore
structurally incapable of preferring an abstraction, and duly did not.

The meta-development catalog is split, by the frozen lexicographic rule,
into:
    OBSERVE families  -- the donor searches these and collects the SEMANTIC
                         CLASSES of its own successful bodies;
    VALIDATE families -- the donor never observes successes from these, and
                         candidate libraries are SCORED on them.

This is ordinary cross-validation carried out entirely inside the donor's own
development data. It uses no tribunal, no meta-tribunal family, and no
evaluator information. It gives a generalising abstraction the opportunity to
win on its merits, and a memorising library the opportunity to lose.

If the memorising library still wins, that is a RESULT -- evidence that
in-sample selection pressure favours memorisation even when cross-validated
-- and it will be reported as such rather than engineered away.
