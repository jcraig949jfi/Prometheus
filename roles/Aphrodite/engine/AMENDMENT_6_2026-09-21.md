after the basis-separation certificate passed in the shape the operator

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE SCIENTIFIC QUESTION, in the operator's narrower and better form
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Can evolution discover a genuinely load-bearing fold mechanism, and reuse
  that mechanism across more than one task family?

This is stated instead of pretending list_sum and list_prod_mod are two
independent kinds of algorithmic structure. They are two applications of the
same missing capability -- iteration with an accumulator -- which makes them
a REUSE test, not an independence test. The report will say so.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. FROZEN EXECUTION PARAMETERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

DISTRIBUTION (slice 2C): arith, sortkey, strops (solved by the base image)
+ list_sum, list_prod_mod (headroom, two independently scored classes, R5).
DEV_PER_FAMILY = 2, unchanged. Development instances are drawn ONLY at
SEARCH_LENGTHS = 4..9.

BASIS: unchanged arithmetic primitives (add, sub, mul, fdiv, mod, gcd,
powr). No primitive accumulates over variable-length input, iterates to
convergence, or branches.

MECHANISM SPACE available to the improver:
  composition  the v2 size-indexed symmetric enumerator (grammar hash
               713195e32926e538e2cebb9831359782854e20f29c227c3fbdd2f1f3f6849d16)
  M2 fold      acc = INIT; for v in vals: acc = E(acc, v); return F(acc[,last])
               INIT, E and F are SEARCHED, never supplied.
Which mode is attempted first is a stochastic search decision driven by the
lineage's own search entropy.

LINEAGES: 16, ids L2C-001..L2C-016, under the already-frozen derivation rule
(dev_entropy / search_entropy). Tribunal entropy remains a disjoint domain.
Extraction at generation 8, positional.

TRIBUNAL (constructed only after the generation-8 freeze, never shown to a
lineage, no feedback to search):
  held-out      instances at EXTRAPOLATION_LENGTHS = 20..60 -- deliberately
                outside every length the lineage ever saw
  stress        length 200
  counterexamples  list_sum: long lists, repeated values, minimum length;
                list_prod_mod: modulus 1, modulus dividing the product,
                modulus larger than the product, lists containing 1s
  metamorphic   list_sum: permutation invariance; f(xs + [k]) == f(xs) + k
                list_prod_mod: permutation invariance;
                f(xs + [k], m) == (f(xs, m) * k) mod m

4. SUCCESS CRITERION (engine capability, existence only)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

A qualifying artifact must:
  Q1 arise endogenously (non-base, no witness supplied);
  Q2 differ causally from the base image;
  Q3 cross the qualified clean-room membrane (hash at extraction == hash at
     load, crossed == {artifact_bytes}, donor_reads == [artifact sha256]);
  Q4 improve a fresh recipient on BOTH independently generated and falsified
     capability classes;
  Q5 survive hostile counterexample and metamorphic evaluation;
  Q6 contain a structural component that is LOAD-BEARING under the
     simpler-surrogate battery (constants, identity/passthrough, single
     primitive applications including over `last`, single-iteration control
     flow). Presence/invocation/looping/AST depth/helper count are telemetry
     only;
  Q7 EXTRAPOLATE: the same fixed artifact must hold at lengths 20..60 and at
     length 200, which no bounded unrolling can do;
  Q8 contain no donor state, evaluator information or undeclared payload.

REPORTED SEPARATELY, ALWAYS WITH DENOMINATORS:
  qualifying_artifacts / independent_lineages
  distinct_artifact_hashes / independent_lineages
  the disposition of EVERY failed artifact
  and, for the reuse question: how many qualifying artifacts carry a
  load-bearing fold for BOTH families versus only one.

ONE QUALIFYING LINEAGE ESTABLISHES EXISTENCE ONLY. Reliability is not
inferred from it.

THE CLAIM, IF IT SUCCEEDS, IS EXACTLY THIS AND NO MORE:
  "Evolution discovered a load-bearing variable-length accumulation
   mechanism that transfers across tasks and survives extrapolation beyond
   the lengths encountered during discovery."
Not RSI. Not reasoning. A computational organ.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. WHAT HAPPENS NEXT IS NOT AUTHORISED BY THIS AMENDMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

If the slice succeeds, the next rung -- excising the fold organ and
transplanting it into a fresh recipient facing a THIRD compatible
variable-length family it was never evolved against -- is RECOMMENDED BUT
NOT EXECUTED. That is the experiment that would test mechanism reuse rather
than task improvement, and it needs its own authorisation.
