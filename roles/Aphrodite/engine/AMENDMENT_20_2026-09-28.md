QUESTION. When reusable structure GENUINELY RECURS across tasks, does an inherited
abstraction become a reliable stepping stone to capability that PRISTINE cannot reach
efficiently? This is a MECHANISTIC assay: recurrence is guaranteed by construction.
  - A positive means the mechanism works when reusable structure recurs. It does NOT mean
    natural open-ended worlds supply such recurrence.
  - A clean negative under fair recurrence is strong stop evidence for the current
    compounding line.
