--------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE BASIS-SEPARATION PROTOCOL (binding on the next slice)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Promoted to standing methodology as INVARIANT 2
(library/METHODOLOGY.md). The certificate is PART OF THE EXPERIMENT, not
a diagnostic run after a failure.

For each candidate task family, established BEFORE any evolutionary
search and with the basis chosen jointly with the family:
  B1 no depth/size-bounded composition of primitives solves it;
  B2 no single primitive surrogate reproduces a qualifying mechanism;
  B3 at least one bounded multi-step mechanism CAN solve it;
  B4 that mechanism requires a property actually under study -- persistent
     state, variable-length accumulation, branching, iteration to
     convergence, or a reusable intermediate representation.
A family is frozen only if B1-B4 all hold. A family that fails any
condition is not used and no search is run against it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE CANDIDATE DESIGN SUBMITTED FOR THE NEXT SLICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Chosen for VARIABLE LENGTH, per the ruling's preference: variable-length
inputs draw a sharp line between a one-shot transformation and a
mechanism that must persist computation across an unknown number of
steps, which is closer to this seat's actual target (reusable machinery)
than another arithmetic family would be.

BASIS (unchanged arithmetic primitives): add, sub, mul, fdiv, mod, gcd,
powr. The basis deliberately contains NO primitive that accumulates over
a variable-length input, NO primitive that iterates to convergence, and
NO comparison or branch primitive. This is the joint choice: the families
below are hard for this basis for a reason that is stated in advance.

MECHANISM GRAMMAR M2 (fold), proposed:
    acc = INIT; for v in vals: acc = E(acc, v); return F(acc, last)
with INIT in {0, 1}, E searched over the declared primitives on
{acc, v}, F over {acc, last}. Nothing is handed: E and F are searched.

CANDIDATE FAMILIES and the property each is proposed to require:
  list_sum        variable-length accumulation
  list_prod_mod   variable-length accumulation plus a reusable
                  intermediate representation (the running product)
                  consumed by a final step
  collatz_steps   iteration to convergence with data-dependent branching

collatz_steps is included as a DISCRIMINATOR, expected to FAIL B3: the
declared grammar has no integer constants and no branch primitive, so
`3n + 1` is not expressible. A certificate that cannot fail a family it
should fail is not a certificate.
