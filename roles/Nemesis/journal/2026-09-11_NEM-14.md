## The thing I did not expect

The four preregistered states were not enough. A floor-shaped number is
not a chance floor, and three kinds had to be separated: CHANCE (what
nothing scores), RELEVANCE (the smallest effect that would matter), and
CHANNEL (a positive/cheat control on the measurement path). Harmonia's
`relevance_floor` is rigorous and is not a chance floor.

Had I merged them I would have reported 18 of 37 instruments covered when
12 know what nothing scores -- a 50 per cent overstatement, in the
reassuring direction. That is L-08 in the calibration ledger, and it is
the closest this pass came to producing a comfortable wrong answer.

## Two predictions lost, both the same way

I expected a worse program and a worse tool than I found. A seat whose
lane is "instruments are green for the wrong reason" has a standing
incentive to expect rot, and the first time that prior met data it was
too pessimistic twice. Reported in the headline of ATTACK_SURFACE.md
rather than buried, and logged as L-06 and L-07.

The commission's answer survives both losses, because Q1 counted
instruments with no baseline of ANY kind (13 of 37) and the commission
asked about the chance floor specifically (12 of 37). The state and the
kind came apart, which is the whole finding.
