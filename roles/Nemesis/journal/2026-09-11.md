## The lane, and why it is not Kairos

Kairos attacks a claim after the rows exist. Nemesis attacks the
instrument that produced the rows, by CONSTRUCTING the input population
that makes it report its number for the wrong reason -- cheat controls,
chance floors, metamorphic perturbation sets, minimal failing inputs.

The base role makes the cheat control constitutional (rule 3; operator
ruling 2026-09-11) and obliges every seat to ship one. `grep -rl "cheat
control" roles/` returns a large number of seats carrying the
OBLIGATION. No seat carries the job of BUILDING one, which is the
hardest of the three controls because it means inventing a way to
succeed without the capability. That is the gap this seat fills, and
this seat's own corpse is the evidence that the obligation without a
specialist is not sufficient: Nemesis 1.0 was built to catch exactly
this failure mode and shipped with it.
