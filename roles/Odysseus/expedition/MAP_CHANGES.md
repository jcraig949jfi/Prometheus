## I -- SCAFFOLD AND INTERNALISATION (proposed by Odysseus; strongest)

Evidence that forced it (not a metaphor search):
- Census A (census/CENSUS_A_z80.md): all three surviving acquisition cases
  in the Z80 family are a lineage REPLACING something the world or designer
  supplied with something it encodes itself -- Archaeon A1 (copiers stop
  reading their destination from the input), NPE N5 (copiers that relied on
  world-zeroed registers come to set their own), NPE N1 (descendants drop a
  self-location opcode the founder needed).
- [REMOVED 2026-09-28] Census B's one candidate (H8 delay-invariant reader)
  FAILED at matched compute (S7): a pristine search reaches it; it was speed,
  not internalisation. The Z80 cases stand alone as evidence for I.
- Natural induction (natural_induction/RESULT.md) retired as a "learning
  rule" but survives as a system internalising the minima it visited.
- Z80 threshold (z80_threshold/): Darwinian dynamics needs authored gifts
  (block copy worth ~3.7 decades of copier density; reset self-location in
  every successful soup).
Foreign mechanism: genetic assimilation (Waddington 1953: a trait first
induced by the environment becomes constitutive after selection) and
scaffolding theory (ecological scaffolding, Black et al. 2020; Bourrat 2022).
Internal prior art: the Baldwin effect in its learning-smooths-landscape
form is covered (frontier_campaign_69 dossier 89; Artemis PRIOR_ART_PRESSURE
C13 declined an arm); ecological scaffolding with a scaffold-removal test
for GROUP individuality is on Nestor's branch (npe-arc3, EXTERNAL_SCAFFOLDING.md,
via raid A EC-4). Not covered: assimilation of world-supplied REPRODUCTIVE
AFFORDANCES, and scaffold withdrawal as a way to lower the affordance
threshold.
Why A-G fail: A attributes a single event, not a function migrating from
world to organism over generations; C asks whether a capability is
reachable, not where its supply lives; D asks whether acquired objects are
built on -- internalisation adds no new capability, it moves an existing
one inside, making the world's gift removable. Prometheus vocabulary has
"self-location", "copy competence", "gift" as fixed properties; it has no
word for "supplied by the world, then absorbed".
Distinguishing experiment (scaffold withdrawal; executable in BEE or NPE):
  arms (>= 20 worlds each): NEVER (no gift), ALWAYS (gift throughout),
  ABRUPT (gift for T generations, then removed), GRADUAL (gift cost or
  availability ramped to zero over T); gift = one of {reset self-location
  registers, destination address in the input, block-copy op}.
  Assimilation predicts ABRUPT/GRADUAL lineages retain the function after
  removal at a higher rate than NEVER reaches it at matched generations,
  and GRADUAL > ABRUPT; the retained function is encoded in the genome
  (recertification harness: behaviour with the gift absent, mechanism by
  knockout).
Collapse condition (kill): withdrawn-gift lineages are no better than
NEVER at matched generations and random-walk budget -- the gift only masked
the need; internalisation is ordinary selection and I collapses into C.
Why it matters: if a transient gift lowers the affordance threshold,
Prometheus can author machinery that is later REMOVED, and Darwinian
dynamics becomes reachable with less permanent authorship -- a lever on the
operator's affordance-threshold question that no engine currently tests.

## H -- ANONYMITY AND ENDOGENOUS DISTINCTION (proposed by raid A)

What identical parts can compute, tell apart and keep, and what it costs
per unit in memory (population-protocol theorems: bounded-memory anonymous
agents compute only semilinear predicates; any unique leader is transient).
Why A-G fail: A asks who did it and presupposes identities exist; no
territory asks where identities come from when the substrate supplies none.
Collapse condition (stated by raid A): if every Prometheus substrate
secretly supplies identities (addresses, positions, clocks), H collapses
into A. Test first: an identity-supply audit of each engine's primitives.
Converges with Artemis ALIEN_CANDIDATES C3 (impossibility theorems as
rulers, n = 3f + 1) -- flagged, not merged.
