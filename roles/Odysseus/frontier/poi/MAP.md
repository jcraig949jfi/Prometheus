## 0. The organising question

"What must physically or computationally happen before something
resembling intelligence can ACCUMULATE?"

Accumulate is the operative word. Doing something clever once is a
configuration; intelligence in the north star's sense is capability that
grows and is carried forward. So the map is organised around the
conditions for accumulation, and each dimension is stated as a
MEASURABLE DISTINCTION -- a pair of things that look alike from outside
and must be told apart -- because Prometheus's forensic record shows its
headline results die exactly where such a pair was confused (location vs
material, copying vs self-replication, timing vs content, rediscovery vs
discovery).

This is NOT a ladder a system must climb (north star: no predetermined
ladder). It is a set of properties with DEPENDENCIES that are themselves
hypotheses. Alternative routes (s4) are part of the map.

## 1. Substrate affordances -- what the physics must allow

P1 PERSISTENCE: inert vs maintained.
   A state survives because nothing touches it, or because a process
   repairs it against noise. Only the second can carry information that
   the system itself keeps current.
   Measure: decay of a planted pattern under noise with dynamics on/off.
P2 PROPAGATION: influence vs content.
   A one-bit difference spreads (a light cone exists), or a STRUCTURED
   pattern arrives elsewhere with its structure. Aether's finding that a
   difference propagates "by activation timing rather than content" is
   the first half without the second.
   Measure: twin-difference light cone; mutual information between
   source and remote pattern beyond what arrival timing explains.
P3 TRANSFORMATION: transport vs modification.
   Information stored, moved, or MODIFIED (Lizier's storage / transfer /
   modification split). Computation is the third.
P4 RESOURCE FLOW: sustained vs starved.
   Maintenance and copying cost something; where does it come from, and
   does structure track the flux? (Aether: 89% of edge terminations are
   source starvation.)
P5 INTERACTION GEOMETRY: what can touch what.
   Locality, topology, addressing. PTE: routed relay works on one
   topology and gives exactly chance on a random graph; arrivals are
   summed, so source identity is lost -- a channel with no addresses.
P6 CHANNEL SEMANTICS (missing from the prompt list): what a message can
   distinguish. Summation vs addressing vs symbols; whether a receiver
   can know who spoke.

## 2. Individuation -- what counts as a "one" (missing from the prompt list)

I1 BOUNDARY: imposed vs maintained.
   Every Z80 world defines the organism (a tape); Aether defines none and
   has seen none. Whether a unit is drawn by the experimenter or held
   together by its own dynamics is a measurable difference (internal
   vs external information flow over time).
I2 IDENTITY THROUGH CHANGE: when a unit's material, location and executor
   separate (the causal lens's WHO / WHERE / WHAT), which one is "it"?
   Prometheus has measured this separation (E-001) and named its
   ill-posed cases (B1 symmetric crossover).

## 3. Heredity and selection -- the evolutionary route to accumulation

H1 COPY vs SELF-REPLICATE vs REPRODUCE: the substrate copies anything;
   a unit's own material governs its copying; a unit makes a VARIANT
   that is itself a unit. (Host-mediated reproduction: inert hosts gave
   births, all by foreign execution.)
H2 HEREDITY WITH CAUSAL CONTROL: inherited material vs inherited
   material that CAUSES the offspring's behaviour. Heredity without
   causal control carries nothing forward.
H3 HEREDITARY CAPACITY: how much information survives copying (error
   threshold); fidelity vs length vs mutation rate.
H4 SELECTION EXISTS: differential persistence/reproduction CORRELATED
   with heritable variation, not with position, luck or host.
H5 EVOLVABILITY: fraction of variation that is neutral or beneficial;
   cliffs (Proteus VM: 0 of 5,472 single edits improved a parent) vs
   neutral networks.
H6 OPEN-ENDEDNESS: novelty keeps arriving vs saturates.

## 4. Within-lifetime and non-reproductive routes (alternatives to s3)

L1 PLASTICITY vs LEARNING: experience changes state (plasticity) vs
   changes future performance on a criterion, generalising (learning).
L2 CREDIT PATH: how does information about an outcome reach the
   mechanism that produced it? (Selection = via reproduction; learning =
   via a signal; stigmergy = via the environment.) No credit path, no
   accumulation.
L3 MEMORY MEDIUM: state, structure (connectivity), environment
   (niche construction, stigmergy), population (culture).
L4 TIMESCALE SEPARATION: learning needs something slower than activity;
   evolution needs something slower than a lifetime.
A route that never reproduces -- one persistent, self-maintaining,
plastic system, or a population that accumulates in its shared
environment -- is a legitimate path to accumulation. Prometheus's
engines are mostly replicator-first; whether that is a choice or an
unexamined default is a question (C1 in s7).

## 5. Organisation -- what accumulation builds

O1 COMPOSITION: parts reused vs re-invented; stable interfaces between
   parts (missing: INTERFACE FORMATION).
O2 MAJOR TRANSITIONS: units combine into higher units with shared fate
   (and a new level of individuation, back to s2).
O3 COORDINATION: computation that depends causally on communication
   (PTE's COMM_DEPENDENT test), division of labour.
O4 COMPRESSION / ABSTRACTION: regularities represented compactly;
   evidenced by TRANSFER, not by fit. North-star "sagacity": a compact
   handle from which a receiver reconstructs a richer lesson.
O5 SELF-MODIFICATION: the machinery of variation, learning or
   inheritance itself changes (evolution of evolvability, meta-learning).
O6 RATCHET: gains persist and are built upon; measured as capability
   with inheritance vs with inheritance ablated.
O7 AGENCY (as a measurable): convergence to a target state from
   perturbed starts, across perturbations the system never met.

## 6. Cross-cutting dimensions

X1 TASK DEMAND / ENVIRONMENT STRUCTURE: capabilities appear only where
   the world pays for them; the environment may be part of the
   computation (PTE: the carrier was transport arriving on the readout
   tick; host-mediated reproduction).
X2 SEARCH vs CAPACITY: could the substrate express it, and could
   selection find it? Different questions; PTE's card says it cannot yet
   separate them.
X3 NOISE: destroyer (error threshold) vs resource (exploration) vs
   corrected (error correction).
X4 IRREVERSIBILITY / COMMITMENT: a ratchet needs something that cannot
   be undone cheaply (the Selective Irreversibility program).
X5 DYNAMICAL REGIME: ordered / critical / chaotic -- whether criticality
   is necessary is contested and should be tested, not assumed.
X6 COST OF COMPUTATION: energy or resource per bit stored, copied or
   erased; a sanity bound for any substrate.
X7 DETECTABILITY (missing, and specific to a program that grows rather
   than designs): can the property be measured without the experimenter
   smuggling it in? The recorded reversals (author-planted laws, labels
   read as properties, windows that miss the event) say this is a
   dimension of the science, not overhead.

## 7. Hypotheses about the map itself (to be attacked)

C1 Prometheus's engines cluster on the replicator route (s3); the
   non-reproductive routes (s4) have almost no lens.
C2 The gap that stops most engines is P2 (content propagation) or H2
   (causal control of heredity), not raw compute or scale.
C3 X7 (detectability) is the binding constraint of the whole program:
   more engines without better cross-engine instruments will add
   reversals faster than findings.
C4 Individuation (s2) is the least-instrumented dimension relative to
   its importance: every organism-level claim depends on it.
