1. BOUNDARY (I1) IS BUILT INTO EVERY ENGINE EXCEPT AETHER. Ten engines
   define the individual; the one that does not has seen no individual
   (raw/I2 T15). "What makes a one" is untested by construction.
2. SIX DIMENSIONS HAVE NO ENGINE: H6 open-endedness, O2 major transitions,
   O7 agency, X6 cost of computation, and (nearly) L4 timescale separation
   and P6 channel semantics (PTE only). These are new-lens candidates
   (NEWLENS.md), not gaps to fill by bending an existing engine.
3. THE REPLICATOR ROUTE (H1-H4) HAS THREE Z80 LENSES AND AN AUDITOR; the
   non-reproductive routes (L1-L4) have none designed for them (WTP builds
   the learner in). MAP C1 holds.
4. THE TRANSFORMATION/COMPOSITION DIMENSIONS (P3, O1) ARE WHERE AETHER IS
   STUCK: one sufficient parent per difference, joint causation 2 of 2,793
   (raw/I2 T12). Possibly the single gate between "physics that persists"
   and "physics that computes".
5. EVERY ACCUMULATION RESULT IS "A" (accidental) OR "B" (built in):
   nothing is designed to observe O6 directly. The north star's core
   dimension has no instrument of its own.
6. RESOURCE PHYSICS (P4, X6): where resource exists (Ares, BEE v3) it is a
   designed economy (payments, copy costs); no engine charges for
   information as physics (overwrite, erasure). External bounds exist
   (EXTERNAL s5) and nobody applies them.

## Where an engine would DISTORT a question (do not force it)

- Z80 worlds on "individuality": the tape IS the individual; they can
  only study identity through change (I2), never the emergence of a
  boundary.
- WTP/SI on "learning": the learner and its memory policy are the
  experimental factor, i.e. installed; it can compare policies, not ask
  where a policy comes from.
- CWE on "discovery": declared coordinates let the author smuggle the
  answer (raw/I3 T7); fit for killing laws, not for finding new ones
  outside the coordinates.
- PTE on symbolic communication: arrivals are summed, source identity is
  lost; it cannot express addressed messages (lens card).
- AGE on heredity/selection: none exists; demanding it there would
  install it.
