2. EXTERNAL FRONTIER (EXTERNAL.md)
-----
- A Google/Mila group now runs Prometheus's own experiment: Cicala et al.,
  arXiv:2607.09211 (v2 2026-09-02; VERIFIED): random 32-byte Z80 programs,
  emergent replication coevolving with a polynomial task; runtime cost
  yields conditional execution; niches yield a curriculum. Their gifts: a
  block-copy instruction and an installed task.
- The BFF group walked back its own emergence story: Knierim et al.,
  arXiv:2607.01483 (VERIFIED): mutation random walks find replicators at
  least as easily as soups; interaction governs takeover, not discovery.
- Universality does not imply replicators; copy-primitive density is a
  first-order substrate variable. Replicators from random code are 30+
  years old. Novelty is after replication.
- Nobody, anywhere, shows abstraction or evolvability accumulating over
  many levels without a human DSL, an LLM prior or a designed fitness.
  LLM library learning shows ~no reuse under audit; simple baselines match
  AlphaEvolve-style loops; self-modifying agents change scaffolds around
  frozen models and hack their evaluators.
- Learning without an installed rule has a known minimum (separated
  timescales, use-dependent change with decay, repeated perturbation,
  dissipation; contrast can come from timing). Almost every "physical
  learning" result installs the element rule.
- Prometheus already owns the strongest measurement: intervention with
  replay. Observational emergence measures only nominate candidates;
  compression signals track takeover, not capability.
- Heredity has a thermodynamic price; Prometheus's Z80 worlds copy for free.
- Gravity traps: LLM in the mutation step; "zero data" from a pretrained
  model; benchmark chasing; transformer-shaped tools; scale as explanation.
