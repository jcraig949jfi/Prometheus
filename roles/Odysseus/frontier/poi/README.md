## Thread IDs

POI-nnn. Candidate threads live here until an owner or the operator
promotes one to ops/threads/TH-nnn. An entry says where it came from
(harvested from a seat's record, raised by an external source, or new),
and its state:

    CANDIDATE   posed; not yet sharpened
    SHARP       question, unknowns and a cheapest discriminator are stated
    READY       a packet in ready/ lets a fresh worker start without us
    RUNNING     someone is on it (say who and where)
    ANSWERED    closed, with the evidence
    MERGED      folded into another thread (say which)
    WEAKENED    prior art answers most of it (say which)
    BLOCKED     needs an instrument or decision (say which)
