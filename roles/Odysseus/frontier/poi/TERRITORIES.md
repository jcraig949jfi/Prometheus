# Territories -- cross-thread structure and the north-star test (Blocks I, J)

Currency: 2026-09-27. Odysseus. The ~75 POI threads (BACKLOG.md) collapse
into seven territories. Each is stated as the ONE underlying question its
threads keep asking, the recurring failure it explains, and whether
resolving it would move Prometheus toward the north star. No numerical
ranking (directive). Pure ASCII.

## I. Deeper structure found (Block I)

1. MOST THREADS ARE ONE QUESTION ASKED AT DIFFERENT LEVELS: "IS THE PROPERTY
   WE NAMED THE PROPERTY THAT HAPPENED?" The program's most recurrent failure
   shape (label read as property: 21 rows, 15 seats, 7 engines; raw/I6) is
   the same object as territory A's science: who authored a birth, what is a
   replicator, what is an individual. Spike S1 found it again in a result
   that was never questioned (20 of 68 "competent self-replicators" cannot
   self-copy). Attribution is not bookkeeping; it is the physics of
   "a one" and "a doing", and it is under-instrumented.
2. "PERSISTENCE WITHOUT PROPAGATION", "PROPAGATION WITHOUT CONTENT", "FUNCTION
   WITHOUT CONTENT" AND "NO JOINT CAUSATION" ARE ONE GAP: the substrates move
   influence and timing but rarely move or COMBINE structured information.
   Seen in Aether (timing not content; 2/2,793 joint causes), PTE (a bit in
   flight that nothing decodes; SETRULE carrying no cue), Crius and Cosmos
   (operations without content). Territory B.
3. "EXISTENCE IS NOT ACCESSIBILITY", "BASIN WIDTH PICKS THE MECHANISM",
   "COPY-PRIMITIVE LENGTH GATES REPLICATION", THE "MUTATIONAL CLIFF" AND
   "SEARCH VS CAPACITY" ARE ONE GEOMETRY: what the encoding and the
   neutral structure make reachable. Four engines and the external record
   agree qualitatively; nobody has the shared metric. Territory C.
   Spike S3 sharpened it: the famous cliff is a 62%-neutral plateau with no
   uphill neighbour -- an accessibility fact, not a robustness fact.
4. THE ACCUMULATION QUESTION IS UNANSWERED EVERYWHERE, INSIDE AND OUT. No
   engine documents acquisition of an unseeded competence (raw/I3 T14);
   no external system shows multi-level accumulation without installed
   priors (raw/E6). The differentiating ground after Cicala 2026 is here.
   Territory D.
5. "AUTHOR SMUGGLES THE ANSWER", "GENERATOR DECIDES THE WINNER", "RANDOM WALK
   FINDS IT TOO" AND "VACUOUS CONTROL" ARE ONE EPISTEMIC PHYSICS: whether a
   growing (not designing) program can detect what grew. Territory E.
6. SPLIT, NOT MERGED: territory F (endogenous learning) looked like part of
   D; it is not. D asks what heredity accumulates; F asks whether anything
   accumulates WITHOUT reproduction (a learning rule nobody installed, memory
   in the world). Different lenses, different minimal conditions.
7. SPLIT: territory G (resource physics) was implicit in B and D; the
   external bounds (Landauer, Eigen, persistent-copy cost) make it a
   separately testable claim: free copying may be why replicators come easy
   and composition does not.

## II. The territories and the north-star test (Block J)

A. ATTRIBUTION AND INDIVIDUATION -- "who did it; what is a one"
   North star: MOVES IT, as an unlocking instrument. Every heredity,
   lineage and accumulation claim in D rests on it; its absence has already
   cost the program the largest share of reversals. Mundane-looking, high
   leverage. Threads POI-001..008.

B. CONTENT PROPAGATION AND COMPOSITION -- "can the substrate carry and
   combine structure"
   North star: MOVES IT for any non-organism substrate (Aether-class), where
   it is THE gate; lower leverage for Z80 worlds, which carry content by
   construction. Threads POI-010..019.

C. ACCESSIBILITY GEOMETRY -- "what makes a capability reachable"
   North star: MOVES IT STRONGLY IF A LAW EXISTS. A cross-engine law of
   reachability (encoding length, neutral connectivity, basin width) tells
   the program what to build into primitives -- exactly the north star's
   "we supply primitives". Cheap to test (stdlib, committed VMs).
   Threads POI-020..026.

D. ACCUMULATION AFTER REPLICATION -- "does anything build on anything"
   North star: THIS IS THE NORTH STAR. Also the most exposed to gravity:
   the tempting shortcuts (installed tasks, LLM mutation, designed reward
   ladders) are exactly what external work uses. The anti-gravity designs
   (no-gift soup, reproduction that requires computation without a task)
   are Prometheus's natural differentiation. Blocked in one place by
   doctrine (POI-041, readable fossils). Threads POI-030..042.

E. EXPERIMENTER-INDEPENDENCE -- "can we see it without planting it"
   North star: MOVES IT INDIRECTLY BUT UNIVERSALLY. A grown system is only
   useful if its growth can be detected honestly. The control-fires
   harness and matched random baselines are mundane and would have caught
   about a quarter of past reversals. Threads POI-050..057.

F. ENDOGENOUS LEARNING (NON-REPRODUCTIVE) -- "a rule nobody installed"
   North star: MOVES IT AND WIDENS IT. The north star names "mechanisms of
   reasoning ... co-evolve"; a learning rule that arises from physics
   (natural induction, temporal contrast) is a mechanism no one designed.
   No current engine is built for it: new-lens territory. Threads POI-060..066.

G. RESOURCE PHYSICS -- "what does a bit cost"
   North star: UNCERTAIN, WORTH A CHEAP TEST. If charging for information
   changes what emerges (POI-070), it is a primitive-level design fact;
   if not, it closes quickly. Threads POI-070..074.

## III. Intellectually rich but gravitationally dangerous

- The Selective Irreversibility law (POI-053): a real question framed in a
  memory-learner modality (bounded learners with eviction policies); its
  "elimination" half is conceded to be a theorem; risk of spending effort
  on a definitional result. Spike S2 tests how much empirical content is
  left.
- Criticality / edge of chaos (POI-080): contested externally; useful only
  as a covariate.
- LLM-in-the-loop evolution (POI-083): high short-term yield, converts
  results into measurements of a human prior. Keep only as a matched arm.
- Borrowed AI interpretability tools (SAEs, attention circuits): import the
  linear-representation assumption; Prometheus's replay-with-intervention
  is stronger and substrate-free.
