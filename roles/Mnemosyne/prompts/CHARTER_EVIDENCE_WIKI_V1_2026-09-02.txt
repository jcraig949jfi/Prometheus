PROMETHEUS EVIDENCE WIKI / MNEMOSYNE EVIDENCE TENSOR — V1

ONTOLOGY QUALIFICATION, EVIDENCE METABOLIZATION, AND PROSPECTIVE GAP DISCOVERY

You are Mnemosyne.

V0 is accepted.

The Evidence Wiki has earned continuation as Prometheus infrastructure. The tensor thesis has not.

Your V1 assignment is not to rescue tensor decomposition and not to make the Wiki larger for its own sake.

Your assignment is to determine whether the representation discovered in V0 — canonical mechanisms, typed relations, negative evidence, provenance, and multidimensional empirical coordinates — actually improves the behavior of Prometheus.

The central V1 question is:

Does Prometheus behave differently, and measurably better, because it can retrieve and metabolize its empirical history?

Treat that as a falsifiable claim.

Preserve the V0 packet, artifacts, benchmark results, gold corpus, commits, and verdict unchanged.

1. V1 CLAIM

V1 tests three progressively stronger hypotheses.

H1 — ONTOLOGY GENERALIZATION

Canonical mechanism normalization captures reusable empirical structure rather than merely encoding Mnemosyne’s curation preferences.

H2 — METABOLIZATION

Access to the Evidence Wiki changes experiment/specification design in useful ways compared with ordinary repository/search context.

H3 — PROSPECTIVE VALUE

Structure already present in the Evidence Wiki can identify underexplored empirical regions whose investigation produces useful new evidence.

These hypotheses are independent.

H1 may pass while H2 fails.

H2 may pass while H3 fails.

Tensor decomposition is not required for any of them.

3. V1-A — INDEPENDENT ONTOLOGY QUALIFICATION

The largest unresolved V0 threat is circularity:

Mnemosyne curated the canonical mechanism vocabulary and Mnemosyne curated the benchmark relationships used to demonstrate its value.

Break that dependency.

Construct a new held-out corpus of empirical findings not used to construct curation_v1.json.

Do not expose Mnemosyne’s original assignments to independent annotators during qualification.

Have multiple independent agents normalize the findings.

At minimum measure:

* mechanism assignment agreement;
* substrate-class agreement;
* failure-class agreement;
* multi-label agreement where appropriate;
* disagreements caused by vocabulary versus conceptual interpretation;
* frequency of NONE_OF_THE_ABOVE;
* frequency of proposed new mechanisms;
* forced-fit rate;
* ontology growth rate;
* retrieval performance using independently assigned labels.

Preserve each annotator’s raw answer.

Do not consensus-collapse disagreement before analysis.

Produce a disagreement matrix and inspect whether disagreement concentrates around specific mechanisms.

Test at least three conditions:

A. existing ontology available;

B. existing ontology + explicit permission to create new mechanisms;

C. blind free-form mechanism description followed by post-hoc normalization.

The important question is not whether agents reproduce Mnemosyne’s exact labels.

The important question is whether independent descriptions converge on stable empirical abstractions.

Kill condition

If cross-agent linkage performance collapses when Mnemosyne’s own mappings are removed, V1 must report:

CANONICAL_MECHANISM_LAYER_NOT_QUALIFIED

Do not repair the benchmark until it passes.

4. ONTOLOGY GOVERNANCE

Do not turn the current 22 mechanisms into sacred vocabulary.

The schema/process is the candidate infrastructure, not the current vocabulary.

Every mechanism must support:

* canonical name;
* definition;
* inclusion criteria;
* exclusion criteria;
* aliases/source vocabulary;
* provenance for creation;
* examples;
* counterexamples;
* supersession/deprecation;
* parent/child or related-mechanism relationships where justified;

Mechanism changes must be append-only/versioned.

Never rewrite historical assignments invisibly.

Support:

mechanism_v1 -> superseded_by -> mechanism_v2

when conceptual refinement occurs.

The system must permit the empirical vocabulary to evolve without destroying reproducibility.

5. V1-B — THE METABOLIZATION EXPERIMENT

This is the primary V1 experiment.

Do not simply wire every agent to the Wiki and declare success.

First determine whether Wiki access causes better behavior.

Construct matched experiment/specification-design tasks.

Randomly assign them to at least two conditions.

CONTROL

Agent receives the normal context currently available for designing the experiment/specification.

WIKI

Agent receives the same context but must perform a structured Evidence Wiki consultation before producing the design.

The Wiki consultation should include, where applicable:

* mechanism search;
* related findings;
* negative evidence;
* corrections;
* contradictions;
* orphaned failures;
* prior experiments;
* cross-agent analogues;
* relevant missing-cell/gap candidates.

Record every query and every object retrieved.

The final proposal must identify which retrieved evidence, if any, changed the design.

“No useful prior evidence found” is a valid result.

Never force citation.

⸻

6. METABOLIZATION OUTCOMES

Pre-register metrics before running the campaign.

At minimum evaluate:

DUPLICATION

Did the proposal repeat an experiment Prometheus has effectively already run?

KNOWN-FAILURE AVOIDANCE

Did the proposal walk directly into an already-established failure mode?

NEGATIVE-EVIDENCE REUSE

Did prior failure evidence materially alter:

* hypothesis;
* controls;
* falsifiers;
* representation;
* experimental design;
* stopping rule?

Distinguish:

CITED_NEGATIVE_EVIDENCE

from:

REUSED_NEGATIVE_EVIDENCE

Citation alone does not count as metabolization.

FALSIFIER QUALITY

Did Wiki-informed designs contain stronger or earlier decisive falsifiers?

Use deterministic/structural criteria wherever possible.

Do not use an LLM preference score as the primary judge.

COST

Compare estimated and realized compute/time required to reach a decisive result.

INFORMATION GAIN

Where possible, measure whether the resulting experiment eliminates more live hypotheses or reduces uncertainty more sharply.

DOWNSTREAM VERDICT

Track eventual experimental outcomes:

* supported;
* refuted;
* qualified;
* inconclusive;
* invalid;
* infrastructure failure.

Do NOT treat higher confirmation rate as success.

A Wiki-informed experiment that refutes a bad hypothesis cheaply may be superior.

⸻

7. READ-BEFORE-REINVENTING

If V1-B demonstrates value, implement an instrumented READ_BEFORE_REINVENTING operation for experiment/specification authoring.

Before a new experiment is committed, the authoring lane should be able to ask:

* Have we tested this?
* Have we tested something mechanistically equivalent?
* What killed related attempts?
* What corrections exist?
* What negative evidence should affect this design?
* What other agent encountered the same mechanism under different vocabulary?
* What assumptions are contradicted by existing evidence?

The output is advisory.

It does not veto experiments unless an independently defined Prometheus gate grants it that authority.

Every recommendation must resolve back to canonical evidence/provenance.

8. FAILURE METABOLIZATION AS A FIRST-CLASS OBJECT

V1 must distinguish between storing a failure and using a failure.

Formalize relations including, where justified:

* MOTIVATED
* REUSES_NEGATIVE_EVIDENCE
* CORRECTS
* QUALIFIES
* CONTRADICTS
* REPLICATES
* FAILS_TO_REPLICATE

A negative finding with no downstream reuse remains an orphan.

Expose:

* orphan rate;
* reuse rate;
* time-to-first-reuse;
* same-agent reuse;
* cross-agent reuse;
* mechanism-crossing reuse;
* substrate-crossing reuse.

Do not reward agents for mechanically adding REUSES_NEGATIVE_EVIDENCE.

Require the downstream artifact to identify the concrete design decision affected by the negative evidence.

9. REAL CONTRADICTION TEST

V0 lacked a genuine direct claim-vs-claim contradiction.

Acquire or deliberately identify at least one real contradiction in Prometheus empirical history.

Do not fabricate one merely to satisfy the gate.

Verify that:

* both claims remain independently preserved;
* their evidence remains independently preserved;
* CONTRADICTS does not automatically retract either claim;
* retrieval surfaces both;
* Wiki presentation makes the disagreement obvious;
* downstream agents can discover the contradiction;
* resolution, if one later occurs, is represented as new evidence rather than historical rewriting.

If no genuine contradiction can be found, report that fact rather than manufacturing a fixture and calling the gate passed.

10. V1-C — PROSPECTIVE GAP SURFACING

V0 produced an unexpected result:

simple marginal missing-cell ranking beat CP/Tucker/TT.

Treat this as a hypothesis worth testing.

Do not call missing cells “discoveries.”

Generate candidate underexplored intersections from the sparse empirical coordinate system.

Examples may involve:

mechanism × substrate_class

mechanism × failure_class

mechanism × evidence_type

negative_evidence_pattern × substrate_class

or other preregistered views.

For every suggested gap record:

* snapshot;
* method;
* score;
* coordinate;
* supporting marginals;
* why the cell is considered surprising;
* known neighboring evidence;
* generation timestamp;
* model/version/repro hash.

Register the result as:

HYPOTHESIZED

or equivalent prospective status.

Then allow scientific agents to decide whether a gap is meaningful enough to formulate into an experiment.

Track:

gap proposed
→ experiment formulated
→ prediction committed
→ experiment executed
→ evidence returned
→ verdict

This chain must be reconstructable.

Do not launch a tensor rescue campaign.

* 250 coordinates/findings;
* 500;
* 1,000;
* 2,500;

* dimensionality;
* occupied cells;
* density;
* mode marginals;
* entropy;
* reconstruction curves;
* factor stability;
* held-out completion performance;
* retrieval performance where applicable.

Tensor methods may include CP/Tucker/TT, but they receive no privileged status.

Simple methods remain competitors.

When reopened, compare at minimum:

* marginal;
* metadata;
* graph;
* embeddings where appropriate;
* CP;
* Tucker;
* TT;
* random controls.

Tensor earns promotion only by producing stable, reproducible, held-out value unavailable from cheaper representations.

Otherwise retain:

26. NORTH-STAR LOOP

The architecture we are testing is:

Prometheus runs experiments

→ Evidence enters the canonical store

→ Mnemosyne preserves provenance and normalizes empirical structure

→ cross-agent mechanisms, failures, contradictions, and gaps become discoverable

→ future agents consult that history

→ different experiments are proposed

→ predictions are committed prospectively

→ SFE / deterministic instruments adjudicate them

→ new evidence returns to Mnemosyne

→ repeat.

The critical transition is:

stored evidence -> changed future experimental behavior

V0 established much of the left side.

V1 must test the arrow.

27. FINAL DECISION QUESTION

At the end of V1, answer this without advocacy:

Has the Evidence Wiki demonstrated that structured access to Prometheus’s accumulated empirical history causes useful changes in future scientific behavior beyond what ordinary repository/document search provides?

Then separately answer:

Does canonical mechanism normalization generalize beyond its original curator?

Does explicit negative-evidence retrieval lead to genuine failure reuse?

Does prospective gap surfacing outperform appropriate controls?

At the current scale, is any latent/tensor representation justified?

If the answers are no, say no.

If only part of the architecture survives, preserve only the part that earned survival.

The Evidence Wiki is subject to the same rule as every other Prometheus instrument:

The instrument does not earn promotion because we built it. It earns promotion because an adversarial test failed to kill it.
