INCUBATOR WATCH

PEW should be prepared for one future responsibility of particular importance.

The Incubator is expected to generate enormous landscapes dominated by failures.

The scientific target may not initially resemble useful reasoning.

The interesting signal may be extremely weak:

* tiny departures from null behavior,
* rare repeatable behavioral transitions,
* lineage-correlated anomalies,
* interaction-dependent effects,
* architecture × world effects,
* unusually persistent micro-behaviors,
* low-amplitude effects invisible in aggregate success metrics.

Therefore preserve sufficient raw provenance that future detectors can reinterpret old Incubator history without changing it.

Do NOT prematurely define these anomalies as “reasoning.”

PEW’s job is to make the following future question answerable:

Did some reproducible structure occur in this historical failure landscape that our original interpretation failed to recognize?

Candidate bumps must remain hypotheses.

Every promoted bump should carry a prospective falsification plan and return to SFE/Incubator for adjudication.

PEW proposes.

SFE tests.

History does not change.

Interpretation may.
