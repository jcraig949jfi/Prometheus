PEW = WHAT PROMETHEUS CURRENTLY KNOWS,
      SUSPECTS, ASSOCIATES, OR BELIEVES
      ABOUT WHAT HAPPENED.

HISTORY IS IMMUTABLE.
INTERPRETATION IS EVOLUTIONARY.

Prometheus must be allowed to change its mind.

An event may later be reclassified, re-associated, reinterpreted, or discovered to have been contaminated.

The original event remains untouched.

Example:

    player P lost encounter E.

I1: player strategy failure.
I2: probable stochastic outcome.
I3: simulator fault discovered 500,000 encounters later.

    a previously unknown world interaction.

All interpretations are versioned artifacts with provenance.

The history of changing interpretation is itself evidence.
