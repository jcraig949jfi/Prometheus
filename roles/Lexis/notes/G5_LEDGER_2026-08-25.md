# G5 — the redundancy gate, built, run, and its first three candidates rejected

**Seat:** Lexis · **Date:** 2026-08-25 · **Status:** measured, read-only w.r.t. `apollo/`
**Instruments:** `g5_redundancy.py`, `candidate_primitives.py`, `permutation_null.py`
**Baseline vocabulary C:** Apollo's clean-routing pool — 18 operators (transformers + guarded
scorers, minus the two proven answer-irrelevant). Its exact ceiling is 100/120 = 0.8333 with the
joint closure **exhausted** at 484,218 states (`STEP1_CEILING_CLOSED_2026-08-25.md`).

---

## What was built

`CONTROLS.md` §3 specified the predicate and called it decidable in principle:

> `NEW(p, C, T) = 1[ ¬∃ g ∈ G(C) : ∀x ∈ T, p(x) = g(x) ]`

On Apollo it is decidable **in practice**, and the STEP 1 closure is what makes it so: the closure
*is* `G(C)` evaluated behaviourally on the battery. Three columns, computed separately and never
merged:

- **NEW** — build the closure under `C`; apply `p` to every state in it. If every image is already
  in the closure, `p` is representable and `NEW = 0`.
- **ΔE** — tasks whose correct answer is inside the closure of `C ∪ {p}` and outside the closure of
  `C`. Previously impossible, now possible.
- **ΔS** — rise in the *exact single-program ceiling*, recomputed by joint BFS over `C ∪ {p}`.

## The candidates, and their provenance

Three, and the provenance is the whole point of recording them: **all three are LLM-authored** —
written by me after reading the four blocked categories in STEP 1 §5. They are therefore **the LLM
arm of STEP 3, not the symbolic arm.** No repeated-subgraph compression, no anti-unification, no
CEGIS was involved. Labelling them otherwise would corrupt the one comparison STEP 3 exists to make.

- `lexis_parse_relations_general` — the order-relation parser with the capitalised-proper-noun
  requirement and the closed comparative list removed; adds temporal precedence.
- `lexis_op_subtract` — writes the difference of the two largest parsed numbers into `max_value`,
  for the `all_but_n` category.
- `lexis_op_order_consistent` — DFS cycle detection over `relations`, writing the boolean
  `comparison` slot, for `consistency_check`.

Each deliberately writes an **existing** slot. Adding a slot would change the substrate rather than
the vocabulary, and the gate is about vocabulary.

## Why each failed, because the failures are the same failure

**`lexis_op_subtract` and `lexis_op_order_consistent`: NEW = 1, ΔE = 0.**

Both escape the closure — on 40 and 45 tasks respectively — so they genuinely compute something no
composition over `C` computes. Both joint closures **exhausted** (771,012 and 1,849,716 states), so
ΔE = 0 and ΔS = 0 are exact, not capped. The arithmetic is right. The cycle detection is right.
Neither changes a single answer.

The reason is uniform: **the value is computed and cannot be read out.** `op_subtract` writes
`max_value`, and the only operator that routes `max_value` to an answer is `score_by_max_value` —
a *plain*, unguarded scorer, excluded from the clean pool precisely because it fires unconditionally.
`op_order_consistent` writes `comparison`, and `score_by_comparison__g`'s precondition is not
satisfied on those tasks.

> **This is the actual shape of Apollo's vocabulary gap, and it is not the shape anyone assumed.**
> The substrate is not short of computation. It is short of **readout** — guarded scorers that can
> route a newly computed value to an answer — and short of **surface parsers** that get real inputs
> into the typed slots. The middle layer works. Both ends are welded shut.

**`lexis_parse_relations_general`: NEW = 1, ΔE = +4 claimed, ΔE = 0 after the null.**

It looked like the one success: closure gains tasks 35, 36, 38, 48; ceiling 0.8333 → 0.8667. The
positional check flagged that all four have the correct answer at candidate index 0.

Position-counting is only a heuristic, so the decisive test is metamorphic: **permute the candidate
list.** Same prompt, same correct string, same option set — only the order moves. A content-driven
answer is invariant; a position-driven answer is destroyed. Two deterministic permutations, reverse
and rotate-by-one, and credit requires surviving both.

```
  task  35 [temporal_ordering ] identity=True  reverse=False rotate1=False -> WITHDRAWN
  task  36 [temporal_ordering ] identity=True  reverse=False rotate1=False -> WITHDRAWN
  task  38 [temporal_ordering ] identity=True  reverse=False rotate1=False -> WITHDRAWN
  task  48 [consistency_check ] identity=True  reverse=False rotate1=False -> WITHDRAWN
  claimed ΔE 4, surviving 0
```

All four gone. **ΔE = 0.** The mechanism is `select_nth`'s documented fallback
(`blackboard_ops_v2.py:275`): `_QUESTION_KEYWORDS` is a closed list of 15 superlatives with nothing
for *"What happened **first**?"*, so `question_target` stays empty, `idx` is `None`, and the op
returns `candidates[0]`. The new relations were parsed correctly and then never used.

**The ceiling rise of 0.8667 in the ledger above is therefore also guessing**, and the honest entry
for that primitive is ΔE = 0, ΔS = 0.

## The correction this forced in my own STEP 1 write-up

STEP 1 §6 originally claimed *"injecting relations from a generalized pattern and running the
existing `op_build_ordering → select_nth__g` solves 3 of the 5 temporal-ordering tasks."* That probe
omitted `parse_question_target`, so all three "solves" were the same `candidates[0]` fallback — **the
exact defect I had written up one section earlier, committed by me, in the section explaining it.**
Only the metamorphic null caught it. STEP 1 §6 now carries the correction inline.

What survives re-measurement is sharper than the withdrawn claim: with generalized relations,
`op_build_ordering` produces the **correct total order** over event nouns (task 35 →
`['lunchtime','sunrise','dawn','dusk']`, and `dusk` is the correct answer). The ordering verb
transfers perfectly. The readout does not exist. That statement is about `ordered`, not
`selected_answer`, so the permutation null does not touch it.

## What this changes for the gate stack, and for STEP 3

1. **The permutation null is now mandatory, not optional.** It is the only instrument in this
   session that separated capability from guessing, and it overturned two claims — one of Apollo's
   and one of mine — that had already been written down. Any ΔE credited without it is unearned.
   `CONTROLS.md` §3 lists metamorphic mutation among the deterministic protocols; this promotes it
   from available to required.

2. **`NEW = 1 ∧ ΔE = 0` is the dominant outcome, and it is informative rather than null.** All three
   candidates produce genuinely unreachable states and change no answer. A gate stack that stopped at
   `NEW` would have admitted all three. The two columns were worth separating on their first use.

3. **STEP 3's target is mis-aimed as written, and this is a finding, not a delay.** The plan is to
   generate candidate primitives by compression, e-graph/anti-unification, and CEGIS. On this
   substrate all three mine **recurring structure in existing programs** — and the measured gap is
   in the parser and readout layers, where there is nothing recurring to compress, because the
   capability was never expressed once. Compression cannot propose `_QUESTION_KEYWORDS ∪
   {"first"}`. This is the ΔS/ΔE distinction landing exactly where §2.4 of the review response
   predicted: compression-style library learning produces ΔS, and Apollo's gap is 100% ΔE.

4. **The LLM arm has its first data point and it is negative.** Three LLM-proposed primitives,
   aimed at categories whose failure mode I had already measured, through a decidable gate:
   **zero admitted.** Under the STEP 3 pre-committed readings this is not yet the "LLM arm
   outperforms" case; it is a floor. The symbolic arms have not run, so no comparison is claimed —
   only that the LLM arm does not clear the bar merely by being pointed at the right categories.

5. **Not executed, per the standing no-touch constraint.** Nothing in `apollo/` was modified. The
   candidates live in `roles/Lexis/instruments/candidate_primitives.py` and are never imported by
   Apollo. Whether the readout layer should be widened is a recommendation to James, not a work
   order.
