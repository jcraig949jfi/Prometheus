            Biological and artificial evolutionary systems exhibit varying degrees of evolvability and different rates of evolution. Such quantities
            can be affected by various factors. Here, we review some evolutionary mechanisms and discuss new developments in biology that
            can potentially improve evolvability or accelerate evolution in artificial systems. Biological notions are discussed to the degree
            they correspond to notions in Evolutionary Computation. We hope that the findings put forward here can be used to design
            computational models of evolution that produce significant gains in evolvability and evolutionary speed.
