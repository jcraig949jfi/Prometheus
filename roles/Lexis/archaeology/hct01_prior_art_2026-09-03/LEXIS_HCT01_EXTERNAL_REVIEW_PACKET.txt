1. ORIENTATION FOR A READER WHO HAS NEVER SEEN THIS PROGRAMME
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Prometheus is a research programme interested in whether evolutionary
systems change the machinery that generates their own future variation,
in a way that alters what they can subsequently acquire. Its "Historical
Collider" reconstructs old experiments to ask questions their authors
could not afford.

HC-T01 is one such experiment, built on Marc Toussaint's 2003 PhD thesis.
Toussaint evolved variable-length symbol strings with rewriting operators
toward a periodic target. A parameter beta controls "second-type"
mutations, which create and modify those operators. HC-T01 switched beta
on and off, and measured the distribution of phenotypes reachable in ONE
application of the variation operator from the current genotype -- what
this packet calls a LOCAL ACCESSIBILITY DETECTOR.

The claim under test, in the programme's own words: did the mechanism
change the distribution of future variation in a way that altered what
the population subsequently acquired?

This pass is literature adjudication and archaeology. NOTHING WAS RUN.

2. THE EXACT RESEARCH QUESTION PUT TO ME
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
I was handed a working position and told to attack it, not defend it. It
asserted that HC-T01 is not historically redundant but its original
justification is obsolete, that the surviving novelty is a
methodological-rigour composition of six elements, and that a narrow
reconstruction of a different paper (Kouvaris et al. 2017) might be
better value first.

The six elements, which I scored as A-F with sub-cells:

  A-LOCAL          detector samples the actual local mutational
                   neighbourhood from the lineage's current state
  A-REAL-OPERATOR  it uses the SAME operator used during evolution
  A-SAME-PROBE     the same probe draws are reused across time and arms
  A-EXHAUSTIVE     the neighbourhood is enumerated, not sampled
  B-LONGITUDINAL   measured at multiple evolutionary times
  B-WITHIN-RUN     those repeats are along the same run
  C-MECHANISM      the knob is a variation operator or representation,
                   not a selection pressure or goal schedule
  C-OPERATOR-CLEAN the intervention does not define the detector
  C-NEG-CONTROL    the detector is measured in the disabled arm too
  D-ACQUISITION    later adaptation is measured
  D-WITHIN-RUN     detector at t is related to acquisition at t+n in the
                   same run
  D-UNREACHABILITY "slow" is distinguishable from "not reachable"
  E-REPLICATION    independent evolutionary replicates
  E-UNCERTAINTY    SE, CI, bands or a formal test
  F-MECHANICAL-NULL the immediate effect of turning the knob is separated
                   from the evolved consequence of having had it
  F-COUPLING       are detector and outcome functionals of the same
                   frozen object, making agreement near-definitional

10. CAUSAL AND COUPLING ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Three hazards. Two are named here for the first time in this programme.

H-1 COUPLING -- CONFIRMED FAMILY-WIDE. Wherever the accessibility
    statistic and the acquisition outcome are both read off one frozen
    object, their agreement is near-definitional. Kouvaris 2017 is the
    extreme case: both readouts are deterministic functionals of the
    same frozen matrix. Ancel & Fontana is degenerate: the accessibility
    measure IS the robustness measure. The single clean exception found
    anywhere is Wagner 2023, and it is clean because the current-state
    term is subtracted in the DEFINITION.

H-2 CEILING-BOUNDED OUTCOME -- NEW, AND IT EXPLAINS K7. HC-T01's
    acquisition outcome is gain toward a bounded optimum, so gain is
    capped by (ceiling - current fitness) and current fitness is the
    SUFFICIENT STATISTIC for that cap. Any predictor correlated with
    fitness inherits the correlation. Measured (execution packet s.10),
    Spearman rho against subsequent gain:
      current best fitness  CHEAP        -1.000  -0.686  -0.987
      avgfit  positive control           -0.975  -0.683  -0.954
      modular degree  ACCESSIBILITY      -0.950  -0.624  -0.949
    The seat noticed one column was "partly degenerate". The defect is
    not confined to one column; it is a property of the outcome. K7 AS
    SPECIFIED IS CLOSE TO UNWINNABLE REGARDLESS OF WHETHER ACCESSIBILITY
    CARRIES INFORMATION. That cuts BOTH ways: it rescues the hypothesis
    from its strongest-sounding disconfirmation, and it convicts the
    design, because a kill condition that cannot be passed is not a
    test. Note also that HC-T01 reported MARGINAL Spearman correlations
    only, never PARTIAL ones.

H-3 MACHINERY PRESENCE -- CONFIRMED for HC-T01 and Kounios 2016. When an
    ablation REMOVES A CLASS OF MACHINERY rather than reorganising it,
    every downstream difference is explicable without any appeal to
    evolved reorganisation. THIS IS THE DIRECT ANSWER TO THE DIRECTIVE'S
    QUESTION 6: the operator ablation made causal identification WORSE
    than a selection-side intervention would have. Misevic's
    instruction-swap does not have this problem, which is precisely why
    it is the better design.

IS "LOCAL ACCESSIBILITY PREDICTS FUTURE ACQUISITION" SEPARABLE AT ALL?
In principle yes, and Wagner 2023 shows the construction: define the
statistic as a residual after the current-state contribution is removed,
at which point it is formally equivalent to average positive epistasis.
Whether it is separable in THIS substrate is unknown, because the one
design that could have told us was scored with the wrong statistic
against a hostile outcome. That is answerable from committed data.

11. THE KOUVARIS RECONSTRUCTION, ASSESSED AS AN INSTRUMENT TEST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The working position's second clause DOES NOT SURVIVE. Four reasons.

 1. IT CALIBRATES THE WRONG INSTRUMENT. HC-T01's detector is the
    one-step offspring cloud from the current genotype. Kouvaris's is
    5000 Sobol points drawn UNIFORMLY over the whole [-1,1]^16 cube and
    developed through the current matrix. A detector that ranks
    Kouvaris's arms has shown it can read a GLOBAL EXPRESSIBILITY
    distribution. It has shown nothing about local accessibility.
 2. THE EQUIVALENT CALIBRATION HAS ALREADY BEEN PASSED, IN THE RIGHT
    SUBSTRATE. HC-T01's V2 recovered Toussaint's published neutral
    degree: 0.504 -> 0.657 against a historical 0.45 -> 0.70, both
    within about 1.1 run-to-run SD of figure-read values. V1, V4 and V6
    also passed, and the reconstruction reproduced unprompted both
    misaligned modules the thesis names by hand.
 3. IT COULD PASS FOR THE WRONG REASON. The published effect is
    enormous -- the L1 arm reaches ZERO generalisation error while the
    control over-fits. Almost any competent distribution estimator will
    rank the arms. Low discriminating power, high over-reading risk.
 4. A FAILURE WOULD BE UNINTERPRETABLE. The Kouvaris record carries
    seven enumerated ambiguities, three of them direct code-versus-paper
    contradictions: P(matrix mutates) is 1/15 in Methods, 1/2 in
    Results and 1 in the code; the sigmoid gain is 0.5 in the appendix
    and 0.3 in the code; developmental steps are 10 versus 15; and the
    committed script CLAMPS the embryonic phenotype to the target with
    the gene-mutation line commented out, which is a different
    experiment from the one the Methods describe. A failure could not
    distinguish a bad detector from a wrong branch of a contradictory
    record.

WHAT TRANSFERS WITHOUT RECONSTRUCTING ANYTHING: the held-out target
class; the entropy statistic with a structurally derived target value (k
independent modules implies exactly k bits, computable on HC-T01's own
committed data); the frozen shared probe, which HC-T01 already adopted.

WHAT MUST NOT BE RECONSTRUCTED: the 1000-run x 2500-generation
acquisition assay, the 2^16 Hebbian enumeration (which replaced
evolution with a learning rule and so does not test the evolutionary
claim), and the parameter sweeps.

MINIMUM VERSION IF OVERRIDDEN: control arm and L1 arm only, entropy as
the single readout, validated against the two pre-derived values (16
bits untrained, 4 bits under L1), tolerances from the reconstruction's
own spread computed BEFORE any tolerance is chosen.

12. WHAT SHOULD BE STOLEN FROM HISTORICAL DESIGNS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Twelve entries in L_STEALABLE_METHODS.jsonl. The five that matter:

SM-01 SUBTRACT THE CURRENT-STATE CONTRIBUTION IN THE DEFINITION of the
      accessibility statistic (Wagner 2023, Eq.1/Eq.3). The direct fix
      for K7. Changes the claim: the result is CONDITIONAL
      accessibility, not accessibility.
SM-08 USE TIME-TO-RE-ADAPT AFTER AN ENVIRONMENT SWITCH, not gain toward
      a fixed optimum (Kumawat 2024's "lineage lag"). Defeats hazard
      H-2 and yields one measurement per switch per run. COST: HIGH for
      HC-T01, because it requires a CHANGING target, which Toussaint's
      Experiment 2 does not have. This is a substrate change.
SM-09 RECIPROCAL TRANSPLANT of the operator parameter between arms
      (Kumawat 2024). A stronger F control than a generation-zero
      baseline AND IT DOES NOT GO VACUOUS, because it is evaluated on
      evolved genomes where the statistic is non-degenerate. HC-T01's
