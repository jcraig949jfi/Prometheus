## 3. The three results that matter

**3.1 The type argument (strongest, and independent of any experiment).** If the decision is
`a* = argmax_a Q(x,a)`, a representation containing only `Z(x)` cannot rank actions — every candidate
inherits the same representation. Measured at exactly 0.5000. The minimum adequate object is
`Z(x,a)`, better `Z(x,a,x',o)`. **The kernel's unused `REWRITE(src, rule, tgt)` opcode is already
that object**, designed long before this thread rediscovered it empirically.

**3.2 The instrumentation divergence.** Prometheus specified the transition formulation twice in June
2026 and production did not instantiate it. `sigma.symbols` = **0 rows**. `step_trace` is degenerate:
332,883 of 332,886 steps are the same action; 66,746 of 66,747 traced records vary only an RNG seed.
But **~48.4M records carry parent links** and several generator families record genuine named actions
with deterministic oracles. The precise statement: *the dominant production representation is
autopsy-oriented, while fragments of a navigation-capable transition corpus already exist but were
never assembled and consumed as such.*

**3.3 Locality.** Cheap arithmetic recovers real conditional signal — but only inside its cell.
Across invariant pair, ~20% of the local advantage survives. Across relation type, transfer is
**below chance** (0.4885, interval excluding 0.500): a model fit on one relation type is *actively
anti-predictive* on the other within the same pair. Per-cell coefficient vectors are near-orthogonal.

## 5. A finding in its own right

Prometheus's corpus does not contain a second search process with a large, non-degenerate
action-choice structure. **Three of eight candidate action families log only successes.** That is the
recon's original point appearing one level down: the corpus was built to record outcomes, not
choices, and even where actions exist the negative class is frequently absent.

## 7. Scope, stated hard

Everything measured lives in cross-catalog invariant relations under two arithmetic predicates, plus
a six-operator synthetic algebra. Counterexample hunting over parity and bounded difference is the
easy case and should be expected to behave well. **This is an instrument proof. Nothing here
licenses a claim about mathematical solution search in general.** No novelty is claimed: the
`I(Z;F)` vs `I(Z;A*)` distinction is established in action-sufficiency work, and Local Optima
Networks, successor representations, empowerment and ATP next-tactic prediction each cover parts of
this.

## 8. Consequences for Prometheus

- The minimum adequate residue object is `Z(x,a)`; anything shaped `Z(x)` or `Z(a)` is *provably*
  unable to rank actions.
- **Any future residue schema storing a verdict against a state, without the action, is unusable for
  navigation by construction** — now a measurement, not an opinion.
- R2-5 has a concrete target: the conditional portion of the interval exists, verdict-shaped records
  reach none of it, cheap arithmetic reaches ~a fifth of it locally, and none of it transfers.
- The cheapest available fix may be **recovering and enforcing the kernel's abandoned edge
  semantics**, not building anything new.
