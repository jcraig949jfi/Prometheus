     cheat_planted              headroom 0.5   entropy 1.0000  qualifies=True
     cheat_absorbed_by_context  headroom 0.0   entropy 1.0000  qualifies=False

     vacuous_single_action      headroom None  entropy 0.0000  qualifies=False

   actions have marginal rate exactly 0.5 (ceiling = chance) while the
   oracle is exactly 1: the channel must read 0.5, and it does.
   Declaring parity as free context must absorb the whole signal to
   exactly 0.0, and it does. The vacuous case returns None rather than
   a number, as the enum discipline requires. Self-test: PASSED, all
   prior assertions unchanged.
