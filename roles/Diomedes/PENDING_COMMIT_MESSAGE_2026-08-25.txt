WHY THE ROWS ARE IN THE WRONG COMMITS, AND WHAT TO CHANGE
Staging with `git add` in one tool call and committing in another is a race whenever other seats are
live. Two seats ran pathspec-less `git commit` in those windows within twenty minutes, while a third
ran `git merge` and `git stash create`. My retry loop made it worse: it re-ran `git add` before each
of 120 attempts, so it generated the window rather than closing it.

Recommendation to the program: every seat should commit with an explicit pathspec,
`git commit <paths> -F msg`, so a commit can never carry files its message does not describe. Until
that is universal, verify with `git log -1 --format=%h -- <path>` and never infer success from a
clean `git status` - an empty status means committed, not committed by you.

Also recorded against myself: earlier I removed .git/index.lock judging it stale. That inference was
wrong - the lock belonged to a live seat whose commit landed minutes later. No damage, but unsound.
Waiting was correct; do not remove another seat's lock on a staleness argument.
