PROMETHEUS — SYNTHESIS CAPTURE DIRECTIVE
2026-09-21

PURPOSE

Capture the current conceptual synthesis of Prometheus after the Techne prior-art raid and Atlas experiment-queue build.

This is NOT an implementation directive.

This is NOT a decision to standardize any interface.

This is NOT permission to rename or restructure existing engines.

The goal is to preserve the current scientific model accurately enough that future agents do not rediscover it from fragments or accidentally collapse Prometheus back into a single familiar AI paradigm.

========================================================================
I. CENTRAL PROMETHEUS NORTH STAR

Prometheus is not ultimately searching for a particular architecture such as:

* neural networks;
* mixture-of-experts;
* skill libraries;
* planners;
* world models;
* language agents;
* reinforcement learners;
* artificial life organisms;
* program synthesis systems.

The desired outcome is broader:

systems that convert experience into increasing competence,
discover mechanisms that improve that conversion,
alter themselves or their environments in ways that expose new
learnable structure,
and eventually discover how to learn better.

Possible outcomes include:

* organisms that learn;
* organisms that learn how to learn;
* machinery from which learning systems can be constructed;
* environmental structures that accelerate learning;
* pressures that induce learning machinery;
* collectives that distribute cognition;
* memory systems;
* reusable computational organs;
* entirely unfamiliar mechanisms for converting experience into competence.

Do not assume in advance which of these is the “correct” route.

========================================================================
II. PROMETHEUS AS MULTIPLE PUDDLES

The current architecture should be understood as several differently biased
generators of scientific material.

Each engine is a differently shaped “puddle.”

We stir all of them.

What matters is what emerges.

Current conceptual roles:

AGE
Extremely low-prior origin-of-organization substrate.
Ask what replication, organization, heredity, metabolism-like structure,
or higher-order machinery can arise when very little is supplied.

NPE / NESTOR
Persistent computational organisms and mechanisms under evolutionary /
ecological pressure.
Especially relevant to discovering memory, state, representation,
machinery and computational organs.

BEE / BELLEROPHON
Rich composable worlds and ecologies supporting emergent behavior,
interactions, persistence, interventions and unusual organisms.

SFE
Open-ended foundry for generating, selecting, transferring and testing
worlds, pressures, mechanisms and stepping stones under strong provenance
and experimental control.

PROSPECTIVE ENGINE FIVE
A deliberately HIGH-PRIOR / HIGH-AFFORDANCE puddle emphasizing:
persistent acquired competence;
reusable executable structures;
cross-world adaptation;
generated or changing worlds;
self-generated curricula/tasks;
retrieval and composition;
possibly modular communication.

This fifth puddle may be highly anthropomorphic compared with AGE/NPE.

That is acceptable.

Its bias is part of the experiment.

It exists to generate a different distribution of mechanisms, successes and
failures for the larger Prometheus ecology to consume.

========================================================================
III. FIFTH ENGINE IS NOT “THE LEARNING ENGINE”

Important correction:

Learning is NOT unique to Engine Five.

The long-term hope is that every engine eventually discovers or supports:

learning;
learning-to-learn;
mechanisms that enable learning;
pressures that induce learning;
environments that accelerate learning.

Therefore Engine Five should not be defined by “learning.”

Its distinctive bias is closer to:

START WITH unusually powerful developmental / competence-building affordances
and ask what recursive structures emerge from them.

Likely ingredients include:

* persistent competence;
* reusable executable artifacts;
* retrieval;
* composition;
* changing worlds;
* task generation;
* cross-world interfaces;
* possibly mutable communication and modularity.

It remains scientifically useful even if it fails.

Failure data is part of the output distribution.

========================================================================
V. ENGINE FIVE CORE LOOP

The minimum scientific object is:

world
    ->
experience
    ->
acquired executable competence
    ->
persistent library
    ->
retrieval / composition
    ->
newly reachable world/task/state
    ->
new experience
    ->
further acquired competence

The key quantity is not benchmark reward.

A more important observable is:

expansion of reachable state / task / behavior space
attributable to retained competence.

Engine Five should earn its existence experimentally.

If the mechanism is merely a small BEE or SFE extension, it should remain an
experiment family rather than become a separate engine.

========================================================================
VI. ANTI-ANTHROPOMORPHIC RULE

Prometheus should aggressively steal enabling machinery from prior art while
remaining suspicious of imported cognitive decompositions.

Distinguish:

INGREDIENT

from
ANSWER.

Examples:

Communication
ingredient.

“Mixture-of-experts router”
answer.

Persistent state
ingredient.

“Episodic memory”
answer.

Addressability
ingredient.

“Skill library”
answer.

Composable executable structures
ingredient.

“Functions/tools”
answer.

Local signaling
ingredient.

“Attention”
answer.

Adaptive environmental pressure
ingredient.

“Curriculum learning”
answer.

The preferred Promethean strategy is:

provide fertile physics for organization,
not a pre-specified theory of cognition.

If distributed cognition is studied, prefer primitives such as:

* persistent addressable computational regions;
* mutable communication topology;
* bounded message channels;
* local state;
* communication/computation cost;
* module creation/destruction/copying/merging;
* heritable topology.

Do NOT hardcode concepts such as:

* expert;
* router;
* planner;
* memory module;
* executive;
* brain region.

If something MoE-like emerges, that is evidence.

If something stranger emerges, that is more interesting.

========================================================================
VII. PORTABLE-ORGAN HYPOTHESIS

A potentially important convergence has appeared independently:

Voyager skill objects,
Techne fossil/capsule machinery,
and Nyx mechanism-ledger direction

all approximate the shape:

{
    identity,
    semantic description,
    executable payload,
    retrieval key
}

Do NOT standardize this yet.

Treat it as a scientific hypothesis:

Can acquired machinery be represented as portable,
retrievable, executable organs that remain useful across
contexts, organisms or worlds?

Important experiments:

* remove semantic description;
* remove retrieval key;
* substitute irrelevant payload;
* transplant across agents;
* transplant across worlds;
* mutate payload while identity remains fixed;
* re-embed after mutation;
* compose multiple organs;
* compare against raw demonstrations;
* compare against episodic memory;
* compare against complete ancestor/fossil transfer.

If this survives causal testing, it may become foundational.

If not, discard it.

========================================================================
IX. CROSS-ENGINE EXPERIMENTAL ALGEBRA

The deeper scientific unit is increasingly:

organism
    ×
world
    ×
pressure
    ×
inherited/acquired machinery
    ×
observer

rather than simply:

engine run.

Atlas should ultimately index interactions at this level.

A surprising phenomenon should be testable under substitutions such as:

* same organism, different world;
* same world, different pressure;
* same pressure, different organism;
* same organism, organ removed;
* same organism, organ transplanted;
* same world/pressure, different engine lineage;
* same mechanism, independent rediscovery in multiple engines.

This creates a cross-engine causal science rather than five independent projects.

========================================================================
X. REPEATED STRUCTURAL CONVERGENCE

A major target for Atlas/Nyx/Harmonia is:

THE SAME CAUSAL MECHANISM APPEARING UNDER DIFFERENT FORMS.

Example:

An analogous mechanism might manifest as:

* communication subgraph in AGE;
* cooperating computational fragments in NPE;
* ecological specialization in BEE;
* preserved niche structure in SFE;
* routed executable competence in Engine Five.

Do NOT force these into one category prematurely.

Instead ask whether controlled intervention demonstrates that they solve a
similar pressure-response problem.

Repeated independent emergence across different substrates is potentially much
more important than high performance in any single engine.

========================================================================
XI. META-LEARNERS ABOVE THE ENGINES

The larger Prometheus organism should eventually crawl continuously across all
engines.

Its job is not to declare a winner.

Its job is to discover relationships such as:

which world causes which organism to change;
which pressure induces which mechanism;
which mechanisms transfer;
which mechanisms repeatedly reappear;
which failures predict future transitions;
which world/pressure combinations unlock previously unreachable behavior.

The meta-layer should continually test organisms, worlds, pressures and organs
across puddles.

This is the “lens” architecture:

multiple biased generators
    ->
shared observatory
    ->
cross-transplants
    ->
mechanism discovery
    ->
new pressures/worlds/tests
    ->
repeated stirring.

========================================================================
XII. FAILURE DATA IS FIRST-CLASS

A puddle does not have to produce intelligence to be valuable.

Examples of scientifically useful failure:

* skill libraries causing behavioral monoculture;
* generated worlds producing simulator exploitation;
* communication substrates never being adopted;
* persistent memory reducing exploration;
* mutable interpreters destroying heredity;
* curriculum systems discarding late-value worlds;
* cross-world controllers failing catastrophically under embodiment changes.

These outcomes constrain the search space.

Preserve them with the same rigor as successes.

========================================================================
XIII. RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN TECHNE, NYX, ATLAS AND HARMONIA

TECHNE
expands the available chemistry.
Finds, preserves and reproduces machinery from external lineages.

NYX
cuts mechanisms into reusable organs and recognizes recurring causal patterns.

ATLAS
maintains the scientific index and turns mechanisms into discriminating
experiments and cross-engine transplant questions.

HARMONIA
adjudicates claims, measurements and causal evidence.

Together they form the observatory/extraction layer above the engines.

Do not let any one of them silently become the scientific authority.

XV. CURRENT SCIENTIFIC PICTURE

Prometheus should NOT converge prematurely on one theory of intelligence.

Instead:

build multiple differently biased evolutionary / developmental puddles;
continuously stir them;
observe what mechanisms crawl out;
transplant those mechanisms into other puddles;
alter worlds and pressures;
look for repeated causal motifs;
preserve failures;
allow meta-learners to discover relationships across the whole system.

The desired discovery may initially look trivial:

a simple chemical reaction,
a tiny communication convention,
a state register,
a copier,
a local controller,
a primitive specialization.

Do not dismiss simple machinery merely because it does not look intelligent.

Complexity may arise from composition.

Prometheus is trying to discover the ingredients and pressures from which alien
competence can accumulate.
