the April cold data are duplicates of the backup root (no hashes compared).

9. DECISION / RECOMMENDATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Pan's lean: continue, narrowly.
  (1) v2 retrieval aimed at the measured shape: document-level vectors from
      a stronger embedding model (Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B over ~62k documents
      ~30 min GPU [E]) plus a duplicate-aware prior (earliest-committed copy
      of near-identical text ranks first); judged on a NEW held-out set
      written before any v2 run, ideally with queries supplied by other
      seats (removes Pan's conflict of interest).
  (2) Consolidation recipe for committed result rows (2.6 GB JSON/JSONL)
      into Parquet + Iceberg with source line counts as the oracle, before
      touching the 132 GB cold data -- which waits for a consumer.
  (3) A pan-search skill so other seats can actually use it; incremental
      refresh as a bounded loop.
Operator decisions open: Q-001 pgvector on M1 (hard gate); Q-003 lake
location; Q-007 disk budget (Pan self-caps at 10 GB on M1).
What should STOP: if v2 also misses 0.80 on fresh held-out queries written
by other seats, stop tuning retrieval and ship full-text + filters (lexical
0.80-0.92 is already useful), spending the effort on consolidation instead.
