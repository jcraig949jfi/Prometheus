4. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Establishes: all four models load and run 100 percent on the card at the
speeds shown; the harness sees passes and refuses cheats; truncation (not the
model) set most of gpt-oss's first-run failures. Does NOT establish: absolute
coding ability (HumanEval is likely in training data); anything about the
models with thinking enabled; performance on code unlike HumanEval (program
mutation inside Prometheus's own engines is the real target and is untested).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. RECOMMENDATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For a local code-mutation operator: gpt-oss:20b at ~4,096 tokens (fastest and
highest measured, 13 GB, apache-2.0); qwen2.5-coder:14b where outputs must
stay short. Next measurement worth its GPU: the same harness on mutations of
the program's own code (an engine's real edit tasks), not a public benchmark.
STOP candidate: more public-benchmark runs -- contamination caps their value.
