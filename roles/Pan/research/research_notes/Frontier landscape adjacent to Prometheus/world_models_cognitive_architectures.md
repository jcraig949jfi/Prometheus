# World Models and Beyond-LLM Cognitive Architectures: Frontier Landscape, 2025 to Oct 2026

Compiled 2026-10-09. Conventions: **[BG]** = pre-2025 background item. **UNVERIFIED** = seen only in secondary or low-quality sources, or sources conflict. GitHub stars, licenses and last-push dates come from the GitHub REST API, queried 2026-10-09 (`api.github.com/repos/<owner>/<repo>`). Out of scope (covered by other researchers): open-endedness/ALife/QD, LLM-guided program evolution, theorem provers, general local-LLM lists.

## Q1. What are the most important works from 2025 to Oct 2026, family by family?

### Takeaway
Across the field, the main 2025-2026 pattern is to iterate in a latent space and then verify or halt, rather than emit tokens. It shows up in JEPA planners (V-JEPA 2-AC, LeWorldModel), tiny recursive solvers (HRM, TRM), energy-based models (EBT, Kona), looped LMs (Huginn, Ouro) and Continuous Thought Machines. But ARC Prize ablations show the gains often come from the outer refinement loop, not from the advertised architecture. The strongest open and theory-backed line is LeCun/Balestriero JEPA work (LeJEPA, then LeWorldModel, then identifiability proofs), now carried by AMI Labs ($1.03B, March 2026). The flagship generative world models are closed (Genie 3) or large (Cosmos 2B/14B, Matrix-Game 5B to 28B). Active inference, Thousand Brains and the neurosymbolic stacks (Hyperon, NARS, Soar) all keep shipping open code, but none has posted a result competitive on shared benchmarks.

### Cited Findings

#### A. JEPA family, LeCun / AMI Labs direction
- **V-JEPA 2** (arXiv [2506.09985](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09985), submitted 2025-06-11; Assran et al., Meta FAIR, 30 authors incl. LeCun, Ballas). It is action-free JEPA pretraining on more than 1M hours of internet video and images, scoring 77.3 top-1 on Something-Something v2 and 39.7 R@5 on Epic-Kitchens-100. Aligned with an 8B LLM, it reaches 84.0 on PerceptionTest and 76.9 on TempCompass. **V-JEPA 2-AC**, an action-conditioned latent world model, was post-trained on less than 62 h of unlabeled DROID robot video and deployed zero-shot on Franka arms in two labs for pick-and-place by planning toward image goals — [arXiv/HF](https://huggingface.co/papers/2506.09985); [Meta publication page](https://ai.meta.com/research/publications/v-jepa-2-self-supervised-video-models-enable-understanding-prediction-and-planning/)
  - Code: [facebookresearch/vjepa2](https://github.com/facebookresearch/vjepa2), MIT (a few files Apache-2.0), 4,740 stars, last push 2026-03-23. The README gives the release date as 2025-06-25. Checkpoints: ViT-L 300M, ViT-H 600M, ViT-g 1B (256 px), ViT-g 1B (384 px), plus a V-JEPA 2-AC ViT-g. They are on HF as `facebook/vjepa2-*` and in PyTorch Hub. The README states no separate license for the weights — [README](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/facebookresearch/vjepa2@main/README.md)
- **V-JEPA 2.1: Unlocking Dense Features in Video SSL** (arXiv [2603.14482](https://alphaxiv.org/audio/2603.14482v2), March 2026, Meta FAIR incl. LeCun; code released 2026-03-16). It adds a dense predictive loss, deep self-supervision across intermediate layers and multi-modal image/video tokenizers, and scales to 2B parameters. Checkpoints are ViT-B 80M, ViT-L 300M, ViT-g 1B and ViT-G 2B at 384 px — [vjepa2 README](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/facebookresearch/vjepa2@main/README.md). One downstream figure is UNVERIFIED (third-party wiki only): a "20% improvement in real-robot grasping over V-JEPA 2-AC" — [search summary of roboticscenter/emergentmind](https://www.emergentmind.com/videos/v-jepa-2-1-dense-features-in-video-ssl-a3c9e9e3)
- **VL-JEPA** (arXiv [2512.10942](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.10942v1), Dec 2025, v2 Feb 2026; Meta FAIR; ICLR 2026 poster). A vision-language model that predicts continuous text embeddings instead of tokens. The authors report it beats a token-space VLM with the same encoder and data while using about half the trainable parameters. Its X-encoder is V-JEPA 2 — [ICLR 2026](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2026/poster/10006956); [BD TechTalks 2026-01-03](https://bdtechtalks.com/2026/01/03/meta-vl-jepa-vision-language-model/)
- **LeJEPA: Provable and Scalable SSL Without the Heuristics** (arXiv [2511.08544](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08544), 2025-11-11; Balestriero (Brown/FAIR) and LeCun (NYU/FAIR)). The paper proves the isotropic Gaussian is the optimal embedding distribution and introduces SIGReg (Sketched Isotropic Gaussian Regularization). Training needs one hyperparameter and no stop-gradient or teacher-student setup, in about 50 lines of code. It reaches 79% ImageNet-1k linear probe with ViT-H/14 — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08544)
  - Code: [galilai-group/lejepa](https://github.com/galilai-group/lejepa), moved from rbalestr-lab. License NOASSERTION, 1,382 stars, created 2025-11-11 (GitHub API)
- **What Drives Success in Physical Planning with JEPA World Models?** (arXiv [2512.24497](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.24497v1), late Dec 2025; Terver, Yang, Ponce, Bardes, LeCun; Meta FAIR/INRIA/NYU). A systematic ablation over architecture, objective and planner. The resulting "JEPA-WM" beats DINO-WM and V-JEPA-2-AC on navigation and manipulation — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.24497v2). Code, data and weights: [facebookresearch/jepa-wms](https://github.com/facebookresearch/jepa-wms), license NOASSERTION, 496 stars (GitHub API)
- **LeWorldModel (LeWM): Stable End-to-End JEPA from Pixels** (arXiv [2603.19312](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.19312), 2026-03-24; Maes, Le Lidec, Scieur, LeCun, Balestriero; Mila/NYU/Samsung SAIL/Brown). The authors call it the first JEPA trained stably end-to-end from raw pixels. It uses two loss terms (next-embedding prediction plus a Gaussian regularizer, i.e. SIGReg) and has 1 loss hyperparameter instead of 6. It has about 15M parameters, trains on a single GPU in a few hours, and plans up to 48x faster than foundation-model-based world models on 2D/3D control — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.19312)
  - Code: [lucas-maes/le-wm](https://github.com/lucas-maes/le-wm), MIT, 4,502 stars, created 2026-03-13. It builds on [galilai-group/stable-worldmodel](https://github.com/galilai-group/stable-worldmodel) (MIT, 2,242 stars, a platform for reproducible world-model research) and [galilai-group/stable-pretraining](https://github.com/galilai-group/stable-pretraining) (MIT) (GitHub API)
- **When Does LeJEPA Learn a World Model?** (arXiv [2605.26379](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.26379), 2026-05-25; Klindt, LeCun, Balestriero). It proves linear identifiability of world latents under stationary additive-noise transitions, with the Gaussian as the unique latent distribution for which the guarantee holds. Linear-orthogonal identifiability supports optimal latent planning — [Emergent Mind](https://www.emergentmind.com/papers/2605.26379); [CSHL record](https://repository.cshl.edu/id/eprint/42215/)
- **AMI Labs (Advanced Machine Intelligence Labs), Paris.** LeCun confirmed the startup in Dec 2025 — [TechCrunch 2025-12-19](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/19/yann-lecun-confirms-his-new-world-model-startup-reportedly-seeks-5b-valuation). It raised $1.03B (about EUR 890M) at a $3.5B pre-money valuation, announced 2026-03-09/10. The round was co-led by Cathay Innovation, Greycroft, Hiro Capital, HV Capital and Bezos Expeditions. LeCun is Executive Chairman and Alex LeBrun (ex-Nabla) is CEO. The CEO says the first usable product is about a year away, with targets in healthcare, robotics, wearables and industrial automation — [TechCrunch 2026-03-09](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/09/yann-lecuns-ami-labs-raises-1-03-billion-to-build-world-models/); [HPCwire 2026-03-11](https://www.hpcwire.com/aiwire/2026/03/11/yann-lecuns-ami-secures-1b-seed-to-develop-ai-world-models/). AMI's own open-source and licensing policy is UNVERIFIED. Code so far appears through collaborators' repos (le-wm, galilai-group) — [search synthesis, low-quality sources](https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/ai-figures/2026/figure-yann-lecun-jepa-formal-proof-world-models-2026-08-04)
- [BG] The V-JEPA (2024) code, [facebookresearch/jepa](https://github.com/facebookresearch/jepa), has had no pushes since 2025-02-27 (GitHub API).

#### B. Generative / interactive world models (Genie, Cosmos, open alternatives)
- **Genie 3** (Google DeepMind, announced 2025-08-05, limited research preview, no paper or weights found). It builds explorable environments from text at 720p and 24 fps that stay consistent for several minutes; Genie 2 [BG, Dec 2024] managed about 10-20 s. It adds "promptable world events" and spatial memory for off-screen objects. DeepMind lists limited action space and multi-agent interaction as limitations — [TechCrunch 2025-08-05](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/05/deepmind-reveals-genie-3-a-world-model-that-could-be-the-key-to-reaching-agi); [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/ai-platforms-assistants/watch-this-google-genie-3-can-create-a-3d-world-let-you-explore-it-and-interact-with-it-in-real-time)
  - **Project Genie**, a consumer prototype on Genie 3, Nano Banana Pro and Gemini, opened to US Google AI Ultra subscribers on 2026-01-29 with 60 s sessions — [TechCrunch 2026-01-29](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/29/i-built-marshmallow-castles-in-googles-new-ai-world-generator-project-genie). One source gives a 2026-06-23 start date, so the date is UNVERIFIED — [macaron blog](https://macaron.im/es/blog/genie-3-release-timeline). No "Genie 4" announcement was found as of 2026-10-09.
- **SIMA 2: A Generalist Embodied Agent for Virtual Worlds** (arXiv [2512.04797](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.04797), announced Nov 2025; Google DeepMind). A Gemini-based agent with a self-improvement loop: Gemini proposes tasks and estimated rewards, and the agent trains on the resulting self-generated experience. The paper calls it the first working example of an agent learning inside Genie 3 worlds, trained on urban environments and tested on held-out natural ones. Access is restricted — [InfoQ 2025-12](https://www.infoq.com/news/2025/12/sima-2-gemini-agent); [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.04797v1)
- **Cosmos-Predict2.5 / Transfer2.5: World Simulation with Video Foundation Models for Physical AI** (arXiv [2511.00062](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.00062v1), about 2025-10-28; NVIDIA, Ali + 87 authors; models released 2025-10-06). A flow-based model that unifies Text2World, Image2World and Video2World. It was trained on 200M curated clips with RL post-training, comes in 2B and 14B sizes, and runs up to 30 s at 720p/16 fps — [project page](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/cosmos-lab/cosmos-predict2.5); [HF blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/nvidia/cosmos-predict-and-transfer2-5)
  - Weights use the NVIDIA Open Model License, which is not OSI, and are "ready for commercial use" — [HF model card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Cosmos-Predict2.5-14B/blob/main/README.md). Code: [nvidia-cosmos/cosmos-predict2.5](https://github.com/nvidia-cosmos/cosmos-predict2.5), Apache-2.0, 1,378 stars, last push 2026-06-08. Physical-common-sense VLM: [nvidia-cosmos/cosmos-reason1](https://github.com/nvidia-cosmos/cosmos-reason1), Apache-2.0 (GitHub API). The changelog adds Robot/Action-Cond distillation and policy models in Feb 2026 — [GitHub](https://github.com/nvidia-cosmos/cosmos-predict2.5)
- **Matrix-Game 2.0** (arXiv [2508.13009](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.13009), released 2025-08-11/12; Skywork AI). An open real-time interactive world model: few-step autoregressive diffusion, keyboard and mouse control, 25 FPS, minute-long videos, about 1,200 h of Unreal Engine and game data. Weights are `Skywork/Matrix-Game-2.0` on HF — [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/matrix-game-2-0-provides-an-open-source-option-to-deepminds-genie-3/); [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/08/11/3131160/0/en/Matrix-Game-2-0-Released-The-First-Open-Source-Interactive-World-Model-for-Real-Time-Long-Sequence-Generation.html)
  - **Matrix-Game 3.0** (arXiv [2604.08995](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.08995), v1 2026-04-10; GitHub lists the release as 2026-03-27). Adds camera-aware long-horizon memory and runs at 720p up to 40 FPS with a 5B model; a 2x14B scale-up is also reported — [hyper.ai summary](https://hyper.ai/en/papers/2604.08995); [GitHub](https://github.com/SkyworkAI/Matrix-Game). Code is MIT, 2,346 stars, last push 2026-09-29 (GitHub API)
- **Navigation World Models** (CVPR 2025, Meta): [facebookresearch/nwm](https://github.com/facebookresearch/nwm), license NOASSERTION, 695 stars (GitHub API)
- [BG] **DIAMOND**, a diffusion world-model RL agent from 2024: [eloialonso/diamond](https://github.com/eloialonso/diamond), MIT, 2,113 stars, inactive since 2024-12 (GitHub API)

#### C. Model-based RL (Dreamer line)
- **DreamerV3, Nature version: "Mastering diverse control tasks through world models"** (Hafner, Pasukonis, Ba, Lillicrap; Nature, DOI 10.1038/s41586-025-08744-2, published 2025-04-17; preprint [BG] arXiv 2301.04104). One configuration covers more than 150 tasks, and it was the first agent to collect Minecraft diamonds from scratch — [project page](https://danijar.com/project/dreamerv3/); [SUFE listing](https://academicnewsletter.sufe.edu.cn/info/246634). Code: [danijar/dreamerv3](https://github.com/danijar/dreamerv3), MIT, 3,853 stars, last push 2026-10-04 (GitHub API)
- **Dreamer 4: Training Agents Inside of Scalable World Models** (arXiv [2509.24527](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.24527v1), 2025-09-29; Hafner, Wilson Yan, Lillicrap). The first agent to get Minecraft diamonds purely from offline data, learning behaviour in imagination. A "shortcut forcing" objective plus an efficient transformer gives real-time interactive inference on a single GPU. Action conditioning is learned from a small labelled subset of mostly unlabeled video — [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.24527). UNVERIFIED secondary figures: 2B parameters, 0.7% diamond success, about 100 of 2,500 video hours action-labelled — [arxiviq](https://arxiviq.substack.com/p/dreamer-4-training-agents-inside); [implicator](https://www.implicator.ai/dreamer-4-mines-diamonds-in-an-imagined-minecraft/)
  - No official code (`danijar/dreamer4` returns 404 on the GitHub API). Unofficial ports: [nicklashansen/dreamer4](https://gittrend.io/repo/nicklashansen/dreamer4) (PyTorch), [lucidrains/dreamer4](https://gittrend.io/repo/lucidrains/dreamer4) (PyTorch), and next-state/open-dreamer (JAX, a 1.6B Minecraft dynamics model, no BC/RL training loops) — [edgex/MarkTechPost-derived](https://pro.edgex.exchange/it-IT/news/article/open-dreamer-open-source-dreamer-4-world-model)
- **Dyn-O** (arXiv [2507.03298](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.03298v1), Jul 2025). An object-centric world model learned from pixels that beats DreamerV3 on rollout prediction accuracy in Procgen — [arXiv](https://web3.arxiv.org/pdf/2507.03298)

#### D. Small recursive and latent-reasoning models
- **Hierarchical Reasoning Model (HRM)** (arXiv [2506.21734](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.21734), June 2025; Sapient Intelligence, Singapore). Two coupled recurrent transformer modules, slow/high-level and fast/low-level, with 27M parameters. It is trained on about 1k examples per task: ARC-1 960, ARC-2 1,120, Sudoku-Extreme 1k, Maze 30x30 Hard 1k — [HRM README](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/sapientinc/HRM@main/README.md). Code: [sapientinc/HRM](https://github.com/sapientinc/HRM), Apache-2.0, 12,650 stars, last push 2026-03-31 (GitHub API)
  - **ARC Prize independent analysis, "The Hidden Drivers of HRM's Performance on ARC-AGI" (2025-08-15).** It approximately reproduced HRM at 32% on ARC-AGI-1 Semi-Private and 2% on ARC-AGI-2. The hierarchical architecture had minimal impact compared with a similarly sized transformer; the under-documented outer refinement loop drove most of the performance — [ARC Prize blog](https://newhotness.arcprize.org/blog/hrm-analysis); [Tim Kellogg thread](https://timkellogg.me/threads/2025-08-15-hrm-confirmed-by-arc-agi-team-but-also-dismissed). A small-scale replication also found the fast/slow hierarchy added nothing over a wider flat loop at matched compute — [SOTAVerified](https://sotaverified.org/blog/hierarchy-flat-loop)
  - **HRM-Text-1B** (Sapient, 2026; arXiv 2605.20613 "HRM-Text: Efficient Pretraining Beyond Scaling" per a 36kr link). About 1B parameters with HRM's slow and fast transformer stacks, trained on 40B tokens with a PrefixLM objective and no code data. It is a pre-alignment checkpoint, not a chat model, and requires `token_type_ids`. Apache-2.0 — [HF model card](https://huggingface.co/sapientinc/HRM-Text-1B). The release date (GitHub about May 18, 2026) and cost (about $1,472 on 16 H100s for 46 h) are UNVERIFIED — [pasqualepillitteri.it](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/news/9655/hrm-text-sapient-costi-specifiche-ai-act); [36kr](https://www.36kr.com/p/3845426151835906)
  - Sapient funding: a $22M seed (Dec 2024) at a $200M valuation and an $11.1M "Series A" (May 2025, PitchBook). These figures are irreconciled and UNVERIFIED — [getcoai](https://getcoai.com/news/singapore-startup-raises-big-bucks-to-develop-cutting-edge-ai-model-architectures); [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/673728-76)
- **Tiny Recursive Model (TRM): "Less is More: Recursive Reasoning with Tiny Networks"** (arXiv [2510.04871](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04871), 2025-10-06; Alexia Jolicoeur-Martineau, Samsung SAIL Montreal). A single 2-layer network with 7M parameters that recursively updates a latent z and an answer y. Results: 44.6% ARC-AGI-1 and 7.8% ARC-AGI-2 (HRM: 40.3/5.0), Sudoku-Extreme 87.4% (HRM 55.0), Maze-Hard 85.3% (HRM 74.5) — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04871); [gonzoml](https://gonzoml.substack.com/p/tiny-recursive-model). It won 1st place ($50k) in the ARC Prize 2025 Paper Awards — [ARC Prize 2025 results, 2025-12-05](https://arcprize.org/blog/arc-prize-2025-results-analysis)
  - Code: [SamsungSAILMontreal/TinyRecursiveModels](https://github.com/SamsungSAILMontreal/TinyRecursiveModels), MIT, 6,556 stars, **archived (read-only)**, last push 2026-04-01 (GitHub API). The README cites limited maintenance capacity — [README](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/samsungsailmontreal/tinyrecursivemodels@main/README.md)
- **2026 HRM/TRM follow-ups**: — [search synthesis](https://openreview.net/pdf/cb0d33e74ecb64051d93d47865ebd10e7b5fafb6.pdf)
  - **GRAM** (ICLR 2026 Workshop on AI with Recursive Self-Improvement) injects stochastic guidance at each recursion step to get diverse solution trajectories and parallel test-time scaling — [OpenReview PDF](https://openreview.net/pdf/cb0d33e74ecb64051d93d47865ebd10e7b5fafb6.pdf)
  - **Probabilistic TRM** (arXiv [2605.19943](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.19943v1)) adds Gaussian latent noise with K parallel rollouts, selected by the Q head.
  - **Tiny Recursive Reasoning with Mamba-2 Attention Hybrid** (arXiv [2602.12078](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.12078)) reports +2.0% pass@2 on ARC-AGI-1 at matched 6.8M parameters.
  - **Steering Recurrent Reasoners at Inference Time with Readout Feedback** (arXiv [2608.24136](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.24136)).
  - **"Loop the Loopies!"** (arXiv [2607.16051](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.16051)) is a related-work catalogue covering CosmicFish-HRM, Recursive Inference Machines, Fixed-Point Reasoners and Tiny Autoregressive Recursive Models.
- **CompressARC: ARC-AGI Without Pretraining** (arXiv [2512.06104](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.06104v1), 2025-12-05; Isaac Liao and Albert Gu, CMU). About 76K parameters, no pretraining and no training set. It minimizes description length (MDL) on the single target puzzle at inference time and solves 20% of ARC-AGI-1 eval; ARC Prize lists 20-34% on ARC-AGI-1 and 4% on ARC-AGI-2. It won 3rd place in the ARC Prize 2025 Paper Awards — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.06104v1); [ARC Prize 2025 results](https://arcprize.org/blog/arc-prize-2025-results-analysis). Ars Technica's March 2025 figures (43.9% / 56.5%) conflict and are UNVERIFIED — [via tagteam](https://tagteam.harvard.edu/hub_feeds/3382/feed_items/13262477)
- **Continuous Thought Machines (CTM)** (arXiv [2505.05522](https://huggingface.co/SakanaAI/ctm-imagenet), May 2025; Darlow, Regan, Risi, Seely, Llion Jones; Sakana AI). Neuron-level temporal models with neural synchronization as the representation, so internal "thought" ticks are decoupled from input. Tasks: ImageNet, 2D mazes, parity, Q&A MNIST, RL — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/SakanaAI/ctm-imagenet). Code: [SakanaAI/continuous-thought-machines](https://github.com/SakanaAI/continuous-thought-machines), Apache-2.0, 2,038 stars, last push 2025-12-29 (GitHub API)
- **Recurrent-depth / looped LMs** (LLM-adjacent, but the reasoning happens in latent space):
  - **Huginn**: "Scaling up Test-Time Compute with Latent Reasoning: A Recurrent Depth Approach" (arXiv [2502.05171](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.05171), Feb 2025; Geiping et al., UMD/LLNL/ELLIS). A 3.5B model trained on 800B tokens that unrolls to arbitrary depth at test time, reaching compute equivalent to 50B parameters. Code: seal-rg/recurrent-pretraining; weights: `tomg-group-umd/huginn-0125` — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.05171v1)
  - **Ouro**: "Scaling Latent Reasoning via Looped Language Models" (arXiv [2510.25741](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.25741), 2025-10-29, v5 2026-07-01; ByteDance + Bengio, Eshraghian). LoopLMs pretrained on 7.7T tokens with entropy-regularized learned depth. The 1.4B and 2.6B models match results of SOTA LLMs up to 12B. Weights `ByteDance/Ouro-1.4B` and `ByteDance/Ouro-2.6B` are research-only — [HF](https://huggingface.co/ByteDance/Ouro-1.4B)
  - [BG] **Coconut** (arXiv [2412.06769](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.06769v1), Dec 2024, Meta) feeds hidden states back as "continuous thoughts" instead of tokens. Code: [facebookresearch/coconut](https://github.com/facebookresearch/coconut), MIT, 1,719 stars, last push 2026-07-02 (GitHub API). A critique, "Do Latent Tokens Think?" (arXiv [2512.21711](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.21711v1), Dec 2025), finds Coconut tokens insensitive to steering and exploiting dataset shortcuts ("pseudo-reasoning") — [search summary](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.21711v1)
  - Surveys: "A Survey on Latent Reasoning" (arXiv [2507.06203](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.06203v1), Jul 2025) and "Reasoning Beyond Language: Latent CoT survey" (arXiv [2505.16782](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.16782v2), rev. Nov 2025; it keeps a GitHub paper list)

#### E. Energy-based models (EBMs)
- **Energy-Based Transformers are Scalable Learners and Thinkers** (arXiv [2507.02092](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.02092), 2025-07-02; Gladstone et al.; UVA/UIUC/Amazon/Stanford/Harvard; ICLR 2026 oral). The transformer scores each (input, candidate) pair with an energy, and inference minimizes that energy by gradient descent, so the energy acts as a verifier. Reported results: up to 35% higher scaling rate than Transformer++; +29% more gain from extra inference compute on language; beats DiT on denoising with fewer forward passes — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.02092v1); [ICLR oral](https://iclr.cc/virtual/2026/oral/10008833). Code: [alexiglad/EBT](https://github.com/alexiglad/EBT), Apache-2.0, 661 stars, last push 2026-04-21 (GitHub API)
- **Kona 1.0** (Logical Intelligence, San Francisco; announced 2026-01-20). Billed as an "energy-based reasoning model". ### Inferences
- **Mechanism versus architecture is unresolved.** HRM's architectural claim was largely deflated by ARC Prize's ablation, which credits the outer refinement loop. TRM then won by simplifying further, and ARC Prize names the "refinement loop" the defining 2025 trend. For Prometheus, the evolvable unit is probably the loop (state, update, halt/verify) rather than a named architecture. Any claimed architectural win should face matched-compute flat-loop controls.
- **The JEPA line is the most "Prometheus-shaped" world-model substrate.** It is small (LeWM about 15M parameters), trains on one GPU in hours, has one loss hyperparameter and permissive MIT code, and now carries identifiability theory (2605.26379) telling you when latents are recoverable. That makes it a cheap, well-posed genome for evolutionary variation.
- **Generative world models are split.** Frontier ones (Genie 3) are closed services. Open ones (Cosmos 2.5, Matrix-Game 2/3) are large video diffusion models more useful as environment generators than as evolvable cognitive cores.
- **Commercial health of alternative-architecture companies is weak, while their open tooling is healthier.** The AXIOM repo has been frozen since 2025-06 and VERSES is retrenching. ONA has been quiet since 2025-05, Intel Lava is archived, and the TRM repo is archived. Monty, pymdp, RxInfer, MORK, Soar and SpikingJelly are actively pushed.
- **Several "beyond-LLM" entries are LLM-shaped underneath.** LFM2.5 is a conv/attention hybrid LM, Ouro and Huginn are looped LMs, SpikingBrain is a linear-attention LLM with spike coding, and VL-JEPA's predictor is initialized from Llama layers. Watch-lists should tag "architecturally novel" separately from "efficiency-novel".

## Q2. Which are runnable on one workstation (16 GB VRAM GPU, 32 GB RAM)?

### Takeaway
Runnable locally for both training and inference: TRM and HRM on Sudoku/Maze, CompressARC, LeWorldModel, small CTM/EBT experiments, DreamerV3-class agents, LFM2/2.5 small models, and every CPU-native cognitive architecture (pymdp, RxInfer, Monty, ONA, Hyperon/PeTTa, Soar). ### Inferences
- **Laptop-class (8-16 GB) evolutionary inner loops are realistic** for TRM/HRM-style recursive solvers on Sudoku/Maze, CompressARC per-puzzle runs, and LeWM-style JEPA world models. All three are cheap enough to run many variants per day, which is what evolutionary pressure needs.
- **V-JEPA 2 ViT-L/H inference** should fit in 16 GB at fp16/bf16, and ViT-g 1B probably does with small batches. This is an estimate from parameter counts, not a measured figure, so treat 2B ViT-G as borderline.
- **SpikingBrain-7B** at bf16 (about 14 GB of weights) would be tight on 16 GB and likely needs quantization. Also an estimate.
- **Every CPU-native cognitive architecture** (Monty, ONA, Hyperon/PeTTa/MORK, Soar, pymdp-legacy, RxInfer) runs within 32 GB RAM; their bottleneck is research maturity, not compute. Monty's simulator dependencies (habitat-sim, now MuJoCo) were not checked.

## Q4. Which benchmarks compare these architectures with LLMs?

### Takeaway
The ARC-AGI family is the shared arena:
- ARC-AGI-2 (2025) is where tiny recursive models are compared with frontier LLMs.
- ARC-AGI-3 (launched 2026-03-25) is interactive. Frontier LLMs started at 0.51% against 100% for humans, and harness/scaffold effects dominate scores.
- Sudoku-Extreme and Maze-Hard (30x30) are the HRM/TRM home turf.

Physical world models are scored on IntPhys 2, CausalVQA, MVPBench and Physics-IQ (plus a 2026 audit), and on robotics planning success against V-JEPA 2-AC and DINO-WM baselines. Active inference uses VERSES' own Gameworld 10k.

  - ARC Prize 2025 drew 1,455 teams and 15,154 entries. The top private-eval score was 24%, and 90 papers were submitted (47 in 2024) — [ARC Prize 2025 Technical Report, arXiv 2601.10904, 2026-01-15](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10904)

### Inferences
- **ARC-AGI-3 is the most relevant yardstick for Prometheus**, because it rewards exploration, goal discovery and memory, which is what world-model and active-inference agents claim. Two cautions apply. The only non-LLM preview leader collapsed from 12.58% to 0.25% on the full set. And all Milestone #1 winners were local-LLM agents. So currently the "beyond-LLM" signal there is weak, and harness effects swamp architecture effects.
- **Sudoku-Extreme and Maze-Hard are saturating, narrow testbeds.** They suit fast fitness evaluation inside an evolutionary loop, but generalization claims need ARC-AGI-2/3 or COGITAO-style held-out compositions.
- **Physical-world-model benchmarks are themselves unstable.** Physics-IQ rankings shifted under the 2026 audit, so the corpus should store benchmark versions and audit papers alongside scores.
