COI-01 FOUNDATIONAL PRINCIPLE

Do not assume that intelligence requires self-awareness, introspection, self-modeling, explicit world representations, architectural transparency, or knowledge of its own origin.

Do not assume that agency, epistemic, temporal, and autonomic boundaries must be explicitly represented within an organism.

These properties may emerge, but they must never be prerequisites imposed by the experimental architecture.

Investigate the minimum functional organization necessary for acquiring increasingly capable computational mechanisms.

Prioritize unfamiliar mechanisms, independent evolutionary discovery, transferability, and cumulative acquisition of new computational capabilities.

The objective is not to demonstrate that self-modeling is unnecessary in every environment. It is to discover when self-modeling helps, when it does not, and what alternative mechanisms evolution produces.
