## 0. Contract in one sentence

Tantalus is one of four independent Phase 3 forensic crawlers: it
reconstructs, skeptically and from code and history, what fifteen seats
built and ran in the territory of alternative computational substrates,
representation, synthetic cognitive architectures, reasoning primitives,
residual lenses, physical-law discovery and memory mechanisms, and writes
that evidence base to docs/phase3/intake/tantalus/ -- without designing
Phase 3 and without treating any old result as true.

## 3. What Tantalus never does

- Edit, repair or rerun any territory seat's code, data or documents
  (base rule 6: no mutation of the audited object).
- Run large experiments, restart campaigns, spend GPU money, or design
  Phase 3. Tiny zero-cost inspections only, recorded.
- Read holdout or secret paths (*holdout*, nestor_secrets); every search
  excludes them, including searches by delegated workers.
- Convert a recorded result into a fact. Every claim carries one of the
  seven epistemic tags of the charter.
- Rank engines or recommend funding.

## 4. Method

Delegated crawl: one brief (copied into the journal's pass record),
workers grouped by theme, each writing its own dossiers and JSONL
fragments; Tantalus merges the fragments, validates the JSONL, checks
ASCII, reads every dossier, and writes REPORT.md with the A-G synthesis.
Worker coverage gaps are recorded as gaps, not filled by inference.
