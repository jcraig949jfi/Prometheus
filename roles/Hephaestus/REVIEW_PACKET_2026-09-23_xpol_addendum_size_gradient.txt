  fourth (XP-059, 0.3226) is at chance. Agreement with NCD picks 0.45

3. THE SIZE GRADIENT, RESTATED
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
  The remark is half right. Everything sits within 0.15 of the floor,
  so a scoreboard makes the tiers look alike. But the FLOOR each tier
  sits on is different, and that is the finding:
    14B local           0.32-0.34   constant-first: no mechanism at all
    120B (nemotron 03,  0.35-0.41   NCD echo: agreement with NCD picks
     gpt-oss 09)                    0.84-0.89; one parser at most fires
    Fable 5.1           0.42-0.52   several parsers fire (R1 .60-.83,
                                    R3 .67-.78); R2 and R4 still ~floor
  The gradient is not "better reasoning"; it is whether ANY structural
  parser exists and fires. Model size buys the existence of the
  mechanism, not its quality. The prompt architecture (regex + NCD
  tiebreak) caps everyone; the ruler's R2/R4 tiers are unmoved by any
  model at any size.
