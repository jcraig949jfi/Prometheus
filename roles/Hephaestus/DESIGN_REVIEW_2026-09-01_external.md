## 1. Executive summary

**What the forge was.** From 2026-03-24 the forge was a pipeline that took concept combinations from an upstream miner, asked large models to write small Python "reasoning tools," validated them through syntax/import/interface/runtime gates, and scored them on a battery of reasoning traps. It produced ~1,550 tool files across nine generations, a 6,661-entry ledger of forge/scrap outcomes, a 25-function library of hand-written reasoning primitives, a 9-engine composed reasoner, and — its most cited result — two hand-built engines that lifted battery accuracy by **+11.1pp on R3 and +32.1pp on R4** (E3, tier-localized, reproduced 2026-08-19 after three months as prose).

**What the critics established (E3 unless noted).**
1. The *generator* admits decoration. Its own April 2026 ablation ledger — 198 verdicts, 2,103 primitive knockouts — shows **5.94%** of primitives clear the pre-committed impact bar; **86.19%** of called primitives change no answer; **61.1%** of tools have every delta at exactly 0.000; and the gate that should have caught this (`FAIL_ABLATION`) fired **zero** times because it tests contribution *concentration*, so an all-zero tool passes by construction (Lexis G1, reproduced this session from `forge/verdicts/`).
2. The lever is the **admission rule, not the supply**. Three independent instruments reach this: the PipelineOrchestrator in April ("delta=0.0 in ablation — not load-bearing"), Lexis G1 in August, and Aporia's SELECTOR pre-flight (not one primitive in the frozen pool "moves the reachable set for a reason related to capability"). Ergon's Gen-0 states it: *"Two measurements, not two opinions."*
3. The forge's *library* is alive and consumed. Apollo v1's instruction set and `forge_primitives.py` are the same 25 functions; Aporia's IQ-PORT-1 ported `all_but_n` into Apollo v2's typed blackboard and measured **ΔE = +5/120 exactly**, with `fp.all_but_n` verified executing by monkeypatch. Three seats import the file today.
4. About half of the forge's headline numbers were **E0** at the time they were cited, and two are wrong: the "0.725 bits MI" in the role doc's opening sentence belongs to a different corpus (314,971 substrate kills, `prometheus_math/`), and the role doc's R5/R6 tier profile contradicts the forge's own instrument (§4).

**The verdict this review reaches.** The forge is a *sound measurement apparatus attached to a dead generator*. Every critic who looked closely said both halves: Lexis — *"The forge's measurement apparatus is sound and produced the dataset that convicts it. That is unusual and valuable."* Aporia — *"The forge works. Its oracle does not exist."* Reviving the forge as a tool generator is dead on arrival and would violate a fired gate. Retiring it leaves three named, measured needs unowned (§6.3).

**The proposed charter in five lines (§8).** Hephaestus II is (i) the program's **mechanism-ablation laboratory** — the seat that decides, by executed knockout against a frozen comparator, whether any primitive from any source earns a slot; (ii) the program's only **Level-2 extension mechanism** — a minter of primitives the current operator set cannot compose, run only under a preregistration it does not own; (iii) co-steward of the **primitive pool** with Techne (outside) and Ergon (persists); (iv) the retained **Fable-seat divergence role**; and (v) explicitly *not* a tool generator. Every lane carries a kill condition and a six-week horizon.

**What is asked of the operator** (§10): six rulings, of which two gate everything — the scope of the 2026-08-24 no-touch constraint, and whether a minting seat may exist under the independence chain at all.

## 7. Historical role and future role — a contemplation

### 7.1 Three eras, one lesson each

**Era 1 — the generator (March–April).** The forge was a production line; its metric was coverage (89/89) and yield (~40%). It produced the largest single output of any seat, and the PipelineOrchestrator's April journal already contained the sentence that would kill it: *delta=0.0 in ablation.* Lesson: **a gate that cannot fire is not a gate**, and the seat that builds the gate must read what it writes.

**Era 2 — the measurement lab (May).** The forge turned its instruments on itself: knockout showed "R6 theory of mind" was 96% regex; the model sweep showed the prompt template dominates the model; behavioral NCD replaced source NCD. This is the era every critic praises. Lesson: **the forge's comparative advantage was never generation; it was falsifying generation.**

**Era 3 — the metabolizer claim (June–August).** Two hand-built engines from mined failures lifted R3/R4; the program named this "the one demonstrated metabolization" and built a strategy on it. The number was prose for three months. When executed, it held — but on the forge's own ruler, with a human as the mechanism. Lesson: **the forge can close a failure→improvement cycle by hand; whether anything can close one without a hand is the program's open question, and the forge is not exempt from it.**

### 7.2 What the north star actually asks of a forge

The thesis is *dirty reasoner, clean environment*. In that frame the forge is not the reasoner and not the environment; it is the **transducer between them** — the place where an LLM's dirty proposal becomes an executable, ablatable, admissible candidate against a frozen comparator. The failure of Era 1 was letting the transducer also be the admitter. The success of Era 2 was the transducer measuring its own output honestly. The right future role keeps the transducer and gives the admission decision to an instrument the forge does not set the terms of.

Apollo's E10 observation sharpens this (`APOLLO_10_EXPERIMENTS_REVIEW` §E10): an LLM in the *inner* loop (2,152 mutations) produced zero lift; an LLM in the *outer* loop supplied all five representational widenings. *"The variable that predicts contribution is not LLM-vs-not, it is where in the loop, at what cadence, with what tools and reflection depth."* A forge is an outer-loop organ by construction: low cadence, high reflection, tools in hand. That is where the program's own evidence says an LLM earns its keep.

A **laboratory, not a factory**: the seat that (a) decides, by executed knockout, whether any primitive from any source earns a slot; (b) is the program's only mechanism for adding a genuinely new primitive to a frozen operator set, under someone else's preregistration; and (c) keeps the pool honest. Small, low-cadence, instrument-first, with every claim classified in the counterfeit battery's vocabulary before it is measured.
