5. RECOMMENDED FIRST ARCHAEOLOGICAL EXPERIMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SPECIMEN: EvCA -- GA-evolved one-dimensional cellular automata for density
classification (Santa Fe Institute, 1993-1999; Mitchell, Crutchfield, Das,
Hraber, Hordijk).

Deliberately NOT the famous choice. Tierra and Avida are more cited; EvCA is
chosen on the anti-fame criteria the directive specifies.

Why it wins
  small physics ............ 128-bit rule table, 149-cell ring, ~300 steps
  artifact to check against  best rules published as hex tables
  known lineage phenomenon   epochal innovation; rare transition to
                             particle-based strategies
  affordable replication ... EST 1e4-1e5 runs per CPU-day
  meaningful blindness ..... 6 of 8 blind-spot rows blind
  counterfactual available . mass replay from pre-transition checkpoints

Runners-up: Lindgren IPD (cheaper, cleaner mechanism, but no artifact to
validate a rebuild) and RNA relay series (kept precisely BECAUSE its original
detector was good -- it is the best place to discover that we add nothing).

THE THREE STAGES
  1  Faithful physics, observation only, pinned seeds, 1e4 runs.
     Question: does the transition recur, at what rate, with what CI?
  2  Add resolution without touching physics: full population per generation,
     lineage DAG, per-initial-condition failure classes, neutral occupancy,
     content-addressed rule tables, fork divergence points.
  3  The question 1996 could not afford: fork 1e3 times from the generation
     preceding each transition, and 1e3 times from a fitness-matched control
     generation with no subsequent transition. Compare conditional transition
     probability. Then ablate rule-table bits of the pre-transition elite.

  In one sentence: was the pre-transition rule table ALREADY SPECIAL in a way
  that changed what was reachable, or was the transition a coin flip that
  happened to land?

PRE-REGISTERED OUTCOMES (fixed before any code exists)
  HISTORICAL_PHENOMENON_RECURS .. rate within CI of the reported rate
  RECONSTRUCTION_FAILS .......... it is not; name the responsible parameter
  CONTINGENT .................... replay from precursor > matched control
  NOT_CONTINGENT ................ indistinguishable; the candidate dies
  NO_PARTICLE_STRATEGIES ........ transition never occurs in 1e4 runs

The CONTINGENT gate line will be computed from the SE of the replay-set
difference BEFORE the replay runs, and its attainable range checked so the gate
can actually fire. That is a standing Prometheus rule after two prior defects
(a gate closer to the observed value than its own SE; a gate above the maximum
attainable value).

6. THE ONE SUBSTANTIVE LEAD RECORDED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Across the three families whose detectors were profiled (EvCA, Lindgren IPD,
Avida EQU 2003), the same four rows are blind in all three:

  1  change in the descendant mutation-effect distribution
  2  base rate of a precursor that led nowhere
  3  reachable-region change, as opposed to fitness change
  4  acquisition cost of the NEXT capability

Stated as a hypothesis: historical instruments measured STATE (fitness,
complexity, diversity) and almost never measured CHANGE IN WHAT IS REACHABLE
FROM HERE. If that holds across 20+ families it would explain why forty years
of work reads as "no open-endedness" while saying almost nothing about whether
microscopic accessibility changes occurred.

STATUS: three hand-filled families written from model recall. This is a LEAD,
not a finding. It is recorded because it is falsifiable and because it is the
seat's opening thesis, which a reviewer should attack early.

Sharpest instance: the Avida 2003 detector was GOOD on the successful lineage
(full line of descent, reward ablation, mutation reversion) and BLIND on the
population. It could not measure how often the same deleterious intermediate
arose and led nowhere. Without that base rate, "deleterious stepping stone" is
a survivorship description. That base rate costs roughly one CPU-day now.
