1. WHAT THIS SEAT IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Herakles is the RETROSPECTIVE instrument of a research programme. It excavates
roughly forty years of artificial life, evolutionary computation, genetic
programming, artificial chemistry and cellular automata.

It does NOT ask whether any historical system produced intelligence. It asks
whether any contained microscopic changes in computational organization that
altered what subsequent search could reach.

Its counterpart, Daedalus, owns the prospective instrument (a durable
multi-world research runtime). Herakles is chartered in part to prove Daedalus
wrong.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE CLAIM UNDER REVIEW, AND THE ONE THAT DIED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OPENING THESIS, now DEAD:
  "Historical instruments measured STATE (fitness, complexity, diversity) and
   almost never measured CHANGE IN WHAT IS REACHABLE."

Killed by two counterexamples, both unanimous under adversarial verification,
and BOTH WERE ON THE SEAT'S OWN LIST OF PLACES TO LOOK:

  Altenberg 1994 (Advances in Genetic Programming, ch.3) defines evolvability
  as a property of the OFFSPRING FITNESS DISTRIBUTION and, in Theorem 4, gives
  a RATE LAW FOR ITS CHANGE via Price's theorem. His own words: "Fisher's
  fundamental theorem of Natural Selection, but applied not to the fitness of
  the evolving programs, but to their evolvability." That is a
  reachability-change formalism from 32 years ago.

  Mengistu, Lehman and Clune 2016 (GECCO pp.141-148) make the offspring-effect
  distribution the FITNESS FUNCTION ITSELF, then transfer the most evolvable
  organism into an unseen environment and measure how much of it the offspring
  cloud reaches. The measure is INHERITED, not invented: the anchor date is
  Lehman and Stanley 2011 or earlier.

REPLACEMENT THESIS (narrower, sharper, and stated so it can die cleanly):
  "Across historically important adaptive-computation experiments, how often
   was the local offspring / reachable distribution measured REPEATEDLY ACROSS
   POPULATION LINEAGES, in a way that permits changes in future accessibility
   to be reconstructed over evolutionary time?"

THE FINDING THAT MATTERS MOST -- A DETECTOR COMPOSITION NOBODY MADE.

  1. a formal definition of the phenotypic exploration distribution
  2. a working Monte Carlo estimator (2000 samples per individual per
     generation)

HE NEVER RAN THE ESTIMATOR INSIDE THE ABLATION. Where he measured

8. THE FIRST EXPERIMENT, AND WHY IT IS NOW DIAGNOSABLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SPECIMEN: EvCA, PPSN III configuration. Chosen against fame; Tierra and Avida
are far more cited.

VERIFIED PHYSICS (all confirmed in print): k=2, r=3, periodic boundaries;
128-bit rule table over a 2^128 space; N=149; population 100; elite 20;
single-point crossover between elite parents chosen WITH replacement; exactly
2 point mutations per offspring; 100 initial conditions per evaluation,
REGENERATED EVERY GENERATION INCLUDING FOR THE ELITE; relaxation time drawn
from a Poisson with mean 320.

TWO CONFOUNDS, BOTH SELF-REPORTED BY THE ORIGINAL AUTHORS:
  - initial conditions sampled uniformly over density, NOT unbiased binomial;
    the authors state this "turns out to impede the GA in later generations"
  - the fitness measure has a noise floor of about 0.02, which the authors
    identify as what prevented them resolving GKL-quality rules
  Both push AGAINST finding late-run innovation.

CIRCULARITY BROKEN -- and this only became visible after recovery. If the sole
validation target for the genetic algorithm were the 7/300 transition rate,
Stage 1 would be simultaneously validation and measurement, and a failure would
be uninterpretable. The recovered artifacts supply four INDEPENDENT targets:
the four-epoch signature (46/50), the 4/50 never above 0.5, the 37/50 stuck at
0.5 with crossover disabled (a differential signal on ONE operator), and the
fitness of the recovered rules under the biased measure. RECONSTRUCTION_FAILS
is now diagnosable rather than merely declarable.

PRE-REGISTERED OUTCOMES. Original rate 7/300 = 0.0233,
Wilson 95% interval [0.0113, 0.0474], normal-approximation SE 0.0087.
