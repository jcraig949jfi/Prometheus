2. WHY HC-T01 IS NOT ENOUGH

HC-T01 demonstrated:

identical phenotype
identical fitness
identical probe
different reachable variation

But the treatments differed in whether a representational machinery class could arise at all.

That leaves a trivial structural explanation:

versus

The next specimen must weaken or eliminate this.

Prefer experiments where:

1. both arms possess the same primitive machinery;

5. THE FIVE-LEVEL EVIDENCE SCALE

Classify candidate specimens:

H0 — STATE DIFFERENCE ONLY

History changes current phenotype/fitness.

Not relevant.

H1 — HIDDEN ORGANIZATION DIFFERENCE

Current phenotype/fitness can match, but internal representation differs.

Interesting but no accessibility measurement.

H2 — SAME-PROBE ACCESSIBILITY DIFFERENCE

Matched present state + identical probe produces different future distribution.

This independently reproduces the HC-T01 particle shape.

H3 — ACQUISITION ASSOCIATION

The accessibility difference predicts subsequent acquisition better than matched current-state variables.

This is what HC-T01 failed to establish.

H4 — CAUSAL ACCESSIBILITY EFFECT

Perturbing/removing the historical organization changes both:

future distribution

and
subsequent acquisition.

This is the strongest target.

Do not collapse these levels.

8. SEPARATE HISTORY FROM LOCATION

Be careful.

A genotype may have a different future because it is currently in a different location, irrespective of how it arrived there.

That is still scientifically interesting, but it is not necessarily historical dependence.

Distinguish:

STATE-LOCATION ACCESSIBILITY

Current hidden state determines future reachability.

HISTORY-CONDITIONED ACCESSIBILITY

Different histories produce different hidden states not captured by coarse phenotype/fitness.

PATH-DEPENDENT ACCESSIBILITY

The same apparent current representation responds differently because information from history persists outside the measured state.

Do not overclaim path dependence when ordinary hidden state suffices.

FINAL DIRECTIVE

HC-T01 found something real but incomplete.

Two populations could arrive at the same phenotype and the same fitness while possessing radically different reachable futures under the same probe.

But the experiment did not show that this difference predicted future acquisition better than current performance, and one arm alone was allowed to evolve the relevant representational machinery.

So do not celebrate the particle.

Try to reproduce it somewhere it has fewer excuses.

Find systems with:

the same present,
the same machinery,
the same perturbation,
but different futures.

Then determine whether those different futures actually matter for what gets acquired next.

If they do, we have moved beyond Toussaint.

If they do not, HC-T01 becomes a beautiful example of hidden representational state — but not yet a component of compounding intelligence.

And while you search, look for the missing third part:

a machine that can detect the shape of its own future and use that information to change how it searches.

That may already exist under another name.

Find it.
