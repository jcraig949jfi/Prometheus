figure to reproduce, and none of the four missions of this seat

search) has an experiment that would take a function-body corpus as
input. The one family that is archaeological in shape, the 17,141
May-forge rows, is Hephaestus's own record and Talos already lists
Hephaestus as the seat to ask.

No transformation of the rows changes this answer, so no contract is
proposed and no production is requested.
