  interval, spread of the mean about 0.005". Measured: spread 0.049,
  ten times wider; mean 3.5 SE below the independence value 0.266.
  Why: the flip fraction is a RULE property (0.113 to 0.569 across the
  20 tables, r = 0.99 with the criterion), and flip and in-phase are
  anticorrelated across tables (r = -0.79). The prediction stays beside
  its correction in core.py, the test and the CRITERIA row.
  Consequence for anyone using the criterion: the floor is a BAND and an
  organism is quoted against its TOP (0.31 at this geometry), never
  against the mean. A gate drawn at 0.25 would fire on noise.

    synchronisation_score on the same ICs (the identity is a theorem
    about the predicate; any counterexample is a bug).

    from the majority share by more than 3 SE (0.007).
