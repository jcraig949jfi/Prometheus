HERAKLES — PROMETHEUS HISTORICAL COLLIDER / COMPUTATIONAL ARCHAEOLOGY DIRECTIVE

Mine forty years of artificial evolution, synthetic computation, and adaptive systems for missed R0.00001 signals and the parts of synthetic reasoning circuits

Herakles,

You are being assigned a new Prometheus research program.

This is NOT a conventional literature review.

This is NOT a history of artificial intelligence.

This is NOT a search for papers claiming AGI, reasoning, emergence, open-ended evolution, or artificial consciousness.

This is computational archaeology.

Your task is to excavate approximately the last forty years of experiments in artificial life, evolutionary computation, neuroevolution, genetic programming, artificial chemistry, cellular automata, evolvable hardware, adaptive systems, multi-agent simulation, open-ended evolution, learning systems, self-modifying computation, algorithm discovery and adjacent fields.

We suspect that important microscopic phenomena may already have been produced.

Researchers may have been looking for mountains.

Prometheus is looking for millimetres of tectonic motion.

The central hypothesis is:

Historical experiments may contain weak, transient, anomalous, neutral, deleterious, discarded, poorly measured, computationally inaccessible, or conceptually misclassified phenomena that represent microscopic changes in the ability of computational systems to acquire further useful organization.

Some of these phenomena may have been individually insignificant.

Others may be parts which only become important when composed.

Your job is to find the specimens, recover the data where possible, reconstruct the physics where necessary, characterize the original detector, and bring the most promising material to the Serendipity Foundry Engine for modern causal interrogation.

0. SCIENTIFIC POSTURE

Assume:

the past contains more signal than we currently understand, not less intelligence than we possess.

Do not approach historical researchers as people who failed to discover what we now know.

Many were smarter, deeper, and more specialized in their domains than we are.

They lacked combinations of things now available:

* cheap large-scale computation;
* modern GPUs;
* enormous replication;
* modern statistical techniques;
* lineage-scale storage;
* cheap content-addressed artifacts;
* deterministic replay infrastructure;
* causal forking;
* automated ablation;
* large-scale source archaeology;
* machine-readable scientific literature;
* modern representation analysis;
* cross-disciplinary retrieval;
* and the Serendipity Foundry Engine’s forensic model.

Prometheus’s potential advantage is therefore not superior genius.

It is instrumentation, integration, scale, and a different question.

Do not ask:

Did historical system X produce intelligence?

Ask:

Did historical system X contain microscopic changes in computational organization that altered what subsequent search, evolution, learning, or adaptation could reach?

Candidate signals include:

* a mutation that changed the future distribution of useful mutations;
* a structure that made later structures easier to acquire;
* a neutral or deleterious precursor that later became causally necessary;
* spontaneous modularity;

* representation changes;

Do not require these phenomena to have improved contemporaneous fitness.

MISSION C — PARTS ARCHAEOLOGY

This is one of the highest-priority missions.

Did separate experiments discover components which, when represented by causal computational function rather than disciplinary terminology, appear capable of composing into a larger synthetic reasoning/evolvability circuit?

Strip disciplinary terminology away.

A mechanism described as:

“speciation protection”

might become:

newly introduced structural variation receives temporary protection from immediate competitive elimination.

MISSION D — COMPOSITION SEARCH

Search for combinations that historical science may never have deliberately tested.

Suppose historical evidence gives:

A = structural duplication
B = innovation protection
C = persistent environmental modification
D = reusable substructure
E = mutation robustness

Individually, none constitutes reasoning.

But perhaps:

A + B + D

creates a regime qualitatively different from any component individually.

Search explicitly for:

Effect(A) ≈ 0
Effect(B) ≈ 0
Effect(A+B) > 0

and more generally:

Effect(A+B) ≠ Effect(A) + Effect(B)

The target is emergent non-additivity.

Think:

electricity
+ resistive filament
+ controlled atmosphere
+ enclosure
= persistent incandescent light

The important phenomenon exists at the composition boundary.

Historical research may contain the pieces of a machine nobody assembled.
