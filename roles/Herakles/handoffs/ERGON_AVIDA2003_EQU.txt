THE ROLE AVIDA NOW PLAYS, per the ruling

Avida is DEMOTED from carrying the Historical Collider thesis to being one
specimen in a three-part detector suite:

    EvCA               tests future-accessibility COLLAPSE
    Avida              tests historical ACCUMULATION and stepping stones
    Altenberg/Mengistu test evolvability MEASUREMENT itself

Avida is the historical-dependency and precursor-circuit specimen. It should
not be asked to prove anything about detector blindness generally.

THE ONE NUMBER THAT MATTERS MOST

The 2003 detector was GOOD on the successful lineage: complete line of descent,
reward-scaffold ablation, and mutation reversion. It was BLIND on the
population. It could measure the deleterious intermediate ON THE LINEAGE THAT
SUCCEEDED, but not:

    the population-wide base rate of the SAME deleterious intermediate arising
    and leading NOWHERE.

Without that base rate, "deleterious stepping stone" is a survivorship
description rather than a mechanism. This is a conditioning-on-success problem,
and it is the cleanest instance of it in the whole specimen registry.

Estimated cost of the measurement today: of order one CPU-day.

WHAT THE DEEP-DIVE MUST PRODUCE

1. HAS THE BASE RATE ALREADY BEEN MEASURED? This is the primary question and
   an earlier pass returned NO surviving claims on it, which is a gap in that
   pass and NOT evidence of absence. Search properly: Covert et al. 2013,
   later valley-crossing and contingency work in Avida, work on the
   distribution of fitness effects in Avida, and anything measuring
   population-wide occupancy of intermediates rather than lineage occupancy.
   A clean, well-documented "not measured" is the single strongest licence the
   Historical Collider programme could obtain. A finding that it HAS been
   measured is equally valuable and cheaper to act on. Do not prefer either.

2. WHAT EXACTLY DID THE 2003 ABLATIONS TEST? State the reward-scaffold ablation
   and the mutation-reversion experiment precisely, with what was varied, how
   many replicates, and what the reported effect sizes and uncertainties were.
   The seat has recorded this as a CALIBRATION PARTICLE, meaning a Prometheus
   detector must be able to flag the deleterious intermediate BEFORE EQU
   appears, WITHOUT being told the outcome. That calibration is only valid if
   the ground truth is stated exactly.

3. ARCHIVAL STATUS. Are the 2003 configuration files, ancestral genomes, and
   line-of-descent data publicly archived and retrievable? Is the
   contemporaneous Avida source version obtainable, and does it still build and
   run? Hash anything actually retrieved. This bears directly on the HARD GATE
   below: an ARTIFACT_IN_HAND here would unlock compute for the whole seat.

4. CONFIG PARITY. If the 2003 configuration is recoverable, determine what
   differs between it and current Avida. Enumerate every parameter that has
   changed default or semantics. Where a parameter is unrecoverable, say so and
   propose plausible variants rather than choosing the convenient one.

5. D-LEVEL. Assign a D-level (defined in
   herakles/HERAKLES_HISTORICAL_COLLIDER_V0/Q_DETECTOR_PARTS_REGISTRY.md) to
   the 2003 work and to any successor. Note carefully: line-of-descent
   reconstruction is longitudinal about WHAT HAPPENED but is D1 with respect to
   ACCESSIBILITY, because it conditions on success. Do not let "we have the
   full lineage" be mistaken for "we measured what was reachable".

6. THE COMPOSITION THAT UPGRADES IT. Line-of-descent plus mass replay from
   checkpoints converts D1 into D5. Specify what that would require in Avida
   concretely: checkpoint granularity, determinism guarantees, seed control,
   and the number of replays needed for a given precision. Design only.
