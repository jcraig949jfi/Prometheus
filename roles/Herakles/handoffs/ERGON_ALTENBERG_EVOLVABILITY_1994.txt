WHY THIS MATTERS TO PROMETHEUS

This chapter killed the strong form of the Herakles opening hypothesis. It is
also, per the ruling, potentially a PART rather than merely a citation:

    structure X appears
      -> X changes the offspring distribution
        -> descendants have altered probability of useful variation
          -> X is selected BECAUSE of that second-order effect

That is candidate machinery for the evolution of evolvability. Not reasoning,
not intelligence, but plausibly one of the parts below the threshold this
programme hunts. It therefore belongs in BOTH the mechanism-parts registry
(E_PARTS_REGISTRY.jsonl) and the detector-parts registry
(Q_DETECTOR_PARTS_REGISTRY.md, entry det-part-01).

WHAT IS ALREADY ESTABLISHED (do not re-derive; verify and extend)

Confirmed 3-0 by an earlier adversarial verification pass, from the full
29-page extracted text:

  - Four numbered theorems exist: Thm 1 (Covariance and Selection, Price 1970),
    Thm 2 (Evolution of the Fitness Distribution), Thm 3 (Evolvability),
    Thm 4 (Evolution of Evolvability), plus Lemma 1 (Exploration Rate Limit).
  - Thm 3 DECOMPOSES (it is not a bare equality; there is a sign condition)
    the probability of producing individuals fitter than any existing, into a
    search-bias term plus a parent-offspring regression term scaled by fitness
    variance. Altenberg proposes it as a replacement foundation for the Schema
    Theorem.
  - Thm 4 gives a RATE LAW: evolvability, defined as the probability that a
    duplicated random block of code increases fitness, increases at a rate
    proportional to the variance in the constructional fitnesses of its blocks.
    Altenberg's own words: "Fisher's fundamental theorem of Natural Selection,
    but applied not to the fitness of the evolving programs, but to their
    evolvability."
  - The chapter is THEORY plus a deliberately simplified analytic model. It
    performs no measurement campaign. Altenberg writes the quantity "should be
    possible to measure in GP runs" and that tests "are currently under way".
  - Stated simplifying assumptions: blocks can be added but not deleted or
    disrupted; GP hill-climbing; constructional fitness e_i assumed constant,
    which Altenberg himself flags as unrealistic.
  - The Thm 4 proportionality constant nu_0/rho_t DECAYS as the program grows.
  - Thm 4 addresses only the DUPLICATION half of the robustness-versus-
    duplication question; it says nothing about robustness selection.

Three precision corrections already logged, which this deep-dive must respect:
  (i)   say "decomposes into", never "equals", for Thm 3;
  (ii)  "computable" is Prometheus's editorial word, not Altenberg's -- the
        search-bias term is defined against a random-search reference and is
        NOT directly computable for a real GP system;
  (iii) Thm 3 measures the LEVEL of reachability at one generation and Thm 4
        the CHANGE; conflating them is an off-by-one-derivative error.

WHAT THE DEEP-DIVE MUST PRODUCE

1. THE EXACT QUANTITY. State the evolvability quantity in full, with its
   definition, its units, its reference distribution, and every symbol
   defined. Write the equations out. Distinguish the quantity Thm 3 bounds
   from the quantity Thm 4 differentiates.

2. WHAT THE PRICE DERIVATION ACTUALLY IMPLIES. Price's theorem is an identity,
   so it constrains rather than predicts. State precisely what additional
   assumptions convert the identity into the reported rate law, and what
   happens to the law when each assumption is relaxed one at a time.
   Specifically: what survives when blocks CAN be deleted or disrupted, and
   what survives when constructional fitness is allowed to depend on context?

3. CONSTRUCTIONAL FITNESS, PRECISELY. Define e_i as Altenberg does. Then
   answer: is it measurable in a real system, and if so by what procedure and
   at what cost? This is the crux -- if it is measurable, Prometheus should
   measure it rather than invent a substitute; if it is not, say exactly which
   term blocks measurement.

4. THE THEORY / EMPIRICISM SPLIT. For every claim in the chapter, mark it as
   MATHEMATICAL ARGUMENT or EXPERIMENTAL MEASUREMENT. The expectation from the
   earlier pass is that essentially all of it is the former. Confirm or refute.

5. THE FORWARD TRACE, WHICH IS THE REAL DELIVERABLE. Altenberg said tests were
   under way. FIND OUT WHAT HAPPENED. Trace forward from 1994: Altenberg's own
   later work; anyone who implemented the upward-mobility selection conjecture;
   anyone who measured constructional fitness empirically; anyone who tested
   Thm 4's rate law against data. Cite what you find, and state plainly where
   the trail goes cold. A documented "this was never followed up" is a
   first-class result and is exactly what the seat needs.

6. THE LONGITUDINAL GAP. Assign a D-level (defined in
   Q_DETECTOR_PARTS_REGISTRY.md) to Altenberg 1994 and to every follow-up you
   find. Identify which portions of the mechanism have NEVER been instrumented
   longitudinally. This directly answers the ruling's governing question.

7. THE BOXED CONJECTURE. The chapter contains a boxed conjecture on
   upward-mobility selection, brood selection and evolvability. State it
   exactly. Then design the smallest experiment that would test it, with its
   cost. If it was never tested, it is a candidate first experiment in its own
   right and would be a genuine result rather than a methodology proof.
