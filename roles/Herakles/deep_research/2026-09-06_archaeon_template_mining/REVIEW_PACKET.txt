2. METHOD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The 69 fields were grouped into 14 clusters of about five. Every prompt carried
an IDENTICAL description of the bench and an identical output contract; only
the field list changed. That was deliberate. The answers have to be comparable
across fields, and the bench description is the part most likely to drift if
written fourteen times.

The bench description was taken from source, never from recall:

    vivarium/viv/kinds.py          the executor kinds and their exact params
    vivarium/viv/executors.py      what each executor actually returns
    vivarium/tests/conftest.py     the spec v3 shape, outcome_rule, repeat
    archaeon/docs/ROADMAP.md:94    the template schema

Returned blocks are validated against Vivarium's LIVE kind registry, imported
rather than transcribed. A template whose param_space does not match its kind
is rejected, because it would be admitted and then fail at execution. A
template naming a kind that does not exist is NOT rejected: per the roadmap
that template IS an expansion request, so it is kept and flagged.

3. THE BENCH AS IT ACTUALLY IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the ground truth every prompt carried, and it is the reason the
headline came out as it did.

Three executor kinds exist. Each declares an EXACT parameter set: a missing
parameter is a rejected spec, and so is an extra one. No executor is permitted
a default, because a default would mean the bench silently choosing a
scientific value on the experimenter's behalf.

    kind                 payload params        returns
    -----------------    ------------------    ------------------------------
    noop_v0              none                  executed
    evaluate_bitstring   bits, length          bits, score, solved
    random_walk_v0       steps, step_scale     position, start_position,
                                               displacement, steps, step_scale

random_walk_v0 is the only STATEFUL kind, so it is the only one that may
declare repeat.state = persist. A fourth kind, archaeon.probe.v0, was retired
on 2026-09-06 and cannot be admitted again.

THE OUTCOME RULE IS A SINGLE SCALAR COMPARISON. One field, one operator, one
value, mapped onto SURVIVED / FALSIFIED / INCONCLUSIVE. No conjunction, no
disjunction, no expression over two fields, no aggregation across repeats, no
ordering or trend test.

ONE WORLD PER SPEC. The world is a single integer seed root. There is no
declared population, generation, archive, niche grid, tournament, opponent, or
second world to compare against.

6. A QUALIFICATION ON GAP 1 THAT CHANGES ITS PRIORITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
I want to argue against my own headline finding here, because I think the
outcome-rule gap is smaller than the count suggests.

The single-scalar outcome rule constrains the VERDICT a spec can pronounce on
itself. It does not constrain what is MEASURED. A spec with repeat.count = N
produces N observations, all fossilized with their inputs and seeds, and
Archaeon's detectors already read across fossils. An experiment that needs a
mean, a trend, or a two-arm contrast can therefore be RUN today; what it cannot
do is state its own verdict.

If that is right, then several of the nine fields in gap 1 are not blocked from
running, only from self-adjudicating, and the expansion is cheaper and less
urgent than a raw count of blocked fields implies. It also means the real
question for those fields is which seat owns the verdict, which is a Harmonia
question about nulls and controls, not a Vivarium question about executors.

I have NOT verified this against how a family or arm reaches the fossil record,
and Archaeon's own notes flag that path as unresolved. Treat this section as a
hypothesis with a named test, not as a finding.

7. WHAT THIS COST, AND A TOOL DEFECT WORTH KNOWING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Three runs, twenty reports, exactly one day's allocation.

    run   prompts   template blocks usable      outcome
    ---   -------   ----------------------      -------------------------
    1           3   1 of 15                     stopped mid-flight
    2          14   2 of 69                     salvaged, see section 8
    3           3   15 of 15                    clean, encoding proven

ROOT CAUSE. The research service rewrites a report AFTER the model writes it,
replacing bracketed spans with source markers. It cannot distinguish a citation
bracket from a JSON array, so every array of numbers was overwritten and its
contents destroyed. A choice list of three integers came back as a source
marker with the integers gone.

Instructing the model not to do it DOES NOT WORK. Run 2 was fired with a prompt
that described the failure explicitly and forbade it, and lost 67 of 69 blocks.
The substitution happens after generation. This is a property of the tool.

THE FIX is to remove brackets from the format. Ranges and choice lists are now
quoted strings, decoded on arrival:

    "length":    {"choices": "16, 24, 32"}
    "seed_root": {"int_range": "100000 to 999999"}

Run 3 parsed 15 of 15 with no repair. The finding is recorded in
roles/Herakles/CAPABILITY_DEEP_RESEARCH.md and in memory, with the standing
rule to probe with ONE prompt before spending a batch.

9. DEFECTS FOUND IN MY OWN INSTRUMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Four, all found and fixed during this pass. Recording them because three of the
four are the same shape: a rule of mine narrower than the legitimate data.

    a. param_space validated against payload parameters alone, which would have
       REJECTED the roadmap's own canonical template, because that template
       varies world.seed_root and the seed is not a payload parameter. A gate
       that cannot pass its founding case is not a gate. Now a fixture.

    b. The template id rule allowed underscores only after the first dot, so it
       rejected version_space_search.v0 and eleven siblings. Underscores are
       normal; the kinds themselves are named random_walk_v0.

    c. The INCOMPLETE flag scanned for surviving source markers, but the repair
       pass had already replaced them with null. It reported ZERO damaged
       templates while fifty were damaged. A report claiming nothing is broken
       while fifty rows are broken is worse than no report.

    d. The deck instructed exactly-payload-parameters, which is narrower than
       the roadmap's schema. See section 4. Disclosed, not silently patched.

10. ADDITIONAL RESEARCH ROUNDS WORTH RUNNING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Yes, and the most valuable one is cheap. The question asked this round was
FIELD-FIRST: what would your field characteristically do? That reliably
produces field-characteristic experiments, and those need new executors. The
inverse question has not been asked.

R1. THE BENCH-FIRST INVERSION.  HIGHEST VALUE, RUN THIS NEXT.
    Ask: given EXACTLY these result fields (bits, score, solved; position,
    displacement) and a single-scalar outcome rule, name experiments from your
    field that are expressible NOW, without any new executor.
    Expected yield: templates that are admissible today rather than expansion
    requests. This round produced 1; a bench-first round should produce many.
    Cost: 5 to 8 prompts.

R2. FITNESS-LANDSCAPE AND QUERY-COMPLEXITY DEPTH ON evaluate_bitstring.
    The evaluator returns a CONTINUOUS score, not just a solved flag, and the
    executor accepts any positive length (the 16/24/32 restriction is
    Archaeon's declared space, not the executor's limit). That makes a real
    literature reachable today: black-box and query complexity, Hamming-oracle
    search, needle-in-haystack landscapes, random-search baselines, and the
    length-scaling of hit probability. None of it needs a new kind.
    Cost: 3 to 4 prompts.

R3. STOCHASTIC-PROCESS DEPTH ON random_walk_v0.
    The walk is stateful and repeat.state may be reset or persist. The
    executor's own docstring says that contrast is what a lag-1 within-world
    autocorrelation reads. That is a genuine two-condition experiment the bench
    can express TODAY. Reachable: diffusion scaling of displacement against
    steps, variance scaling in step_scale, first-passage, time-average versus
    ensemble-average.
    Cost: 3 to 4 prompts.

R4. THE ONE-CAPABILITY-CONDITIONAL ROUND.
    Ask: if the outcome rule gained two-arm comparison and nothing else, what
    becomes expressible? Repeat for persistent state, and for output-to-input
    chaining. This PRICES each expansion in templates-unlocked before anyone
    builds it, which is the measurement Challenge 3 wants and does not yet
    have.
    Cost: 3 prompts, one per candidate capability.

R5. NULLS AND CONTROLS.  Delegated to Harmonia by the roadmap, but a research
    round would seed it: for each admitted template, what null and what control
    make its outcome interpretable? Without this a template produces fossils
    nobody can read.

R6. REFERENCE VERIFICATION.  Not a Deep Research round. A pass that checks
    origin.reference against primary sources before admission. Cheap, and it is
    the difference between a citation and a decoration.

Not a research round but worth naming: the CHAOS mutation operator in the
roadmap can generate new PROPOSED templates from admitted ones with no
literature at all. It should not wait on mining.
