# Eos Season II: interruption economics and the restart recommendation

Currency: 2026-09-11. Season law, operator: "EOS MAY PROVE THAT AN ITEM
DOES NOT DESERVE AN INTERRUPTION. EOS MAY PROVE THAT AN INTERRUPTION IS
STRUCTURALLY POSSIBLE. EOS MAY NOT PROVE THAT AN EXTERNAL IDEA IS
IMPORTANT."

## Mission 7 answer, first, because the rest is why

    RECOMMENDATION: KEEP_DARK

Not because the machinery failed to get built. It got built, it works,
and it is better than what it replaced. KEEP_DARK because the measured
numbers do not support running anything, and because Eos's own
preregistered consequence fires.

## The preregistered consequence, fired by Eos on Eos

Written into the EOS-30 commission before any attacker saw it:

    "If 2 OR MORE of the six come back as 'wrong referent' or
     'irrelevant to my file', then the current PENDING rate is INFLATED,
     that number goes into CALIBRATION.md as a row against Eos, and
     COLLECTION DOES NOT RESTART."

Nemesis reported 200 of 200 constructed wrong-referent ANCHOR claims
CROSSED the gate, and declined to fire the consequence on Eos's behalf,
noting: "the threshold was 2 and nothing I built was refused."

Eos fires it. The precommitment was written about the six because the six
were what Eos had; its SUBJECT was whether the gate admits wrong
referents. That question is answered at a rate of 1.00 on 200 constructed
claims and at n=1 on Eos's own Test 4. Reading the wording narrowly to
escape the consequence would make it a prediction that could not be lost,
which is the one thing a precommitment may not be.

    CONSEQUENCE: PENDING rate inflated. No collection restart.

### The economic finding that matters more than the ratio

THE GATE DOES NOT CONTROL THE INTERRUPTION RATE. THE PROPOSER DOES.

Nemesis's 200/200 shows the gate cannot distinguish a right referent
from a wrong one; it removes only claims that name NOTHING. So the
fraction of interruptions that are useful is a property of whoever writes
the claims, not of Eos's machinery. Adding collection volume multiplies
the proposer's honesty by a bigger number; it does not improve the ratio.

That is the argument against RESTART_BOUNDED in one line, and it does not
depend on any of Eos's labels.

## Mission 1: what the independent attacker found

Nemesis (roles/Nemesis/attacks/2026-09-11_eos_intake_gate/, 271 verdicts)

Cheapest crossing ANCHOR claim after shrinking to a fixpoint: 22

REFUSAL SIDE -- 49 of the first season's 51 refusals carried no
information about the item. Every non-candidate claim's referent was

Eos's Season I headline said of those refusals: "none of the refusals is

whole difference between a boundary and a constant, and Eos's own

A third finding, not about fraud: `capability_absent` grepped the WORKING

Kairos, the model-independent attacker, has not answered. Nemesis runs the

## Repairs made in response, with their controls

    1  RESOURCE IS NO LONGER A TERMINAL STATE. The carve-out is removed
       rather than patched. The gate now settles NOTHING but REFUSED,
       which is exactly the season law. A RESOURCE claim must carry
       `observation_ref` naming a COMMITTED PROBE ARTIFACT and a record
       index; the gate opens that file itself and requires
       response-derived evidence (latency and byte count) that a status
       code alone cannot supply. A forger must now commit a file with a
       diff and an author instead of typing a string.
       Control: test_cheat_forged_observer_label_is_refused. Nemesis's
       forgery is replayed in the season harness every run and is
       REFUSED.

    2  NOT_EXAMINED, a state that is not a refusal. A claim whose
       referent was auto-generated and names no real place is recorded
       as never having been judged. Re-running the season: 45
       NOT_EXAMINED, 7 REFUSED, 7 PENDING. The refusal corpus is 7 rows,
       not 51.
       Controls: test_auto_generated_referent_is_not_examined_not_refused
       and test_a_hand_written_bad_referent_is_still_a_refusal, so the
       new state cannot swallow real refusals.

    3  `git grep --cached`. Searches the index, not the working tree.
       Control: test_capability_search_reads_the_index_not_the_working_tree.

    All 34 controls pass; 3 xfail markers hold work that is NOT done.

## Mission 3: contact witness -- a negative, with one by-product

Full argument: roles/Eos/EOS31_CONTACT_WITNESS.md.

NO deterministic contact witness exists, and the reason is structural,
not a gap in effort. Witnesses are either referent-side (a function of
the internal referent alone) or pair-side. Referent-side witnesses are
provably useless here: Eos's Test 4 bait and live item 5 NAME THE SAME
REFERENT, so every referent-side quantity is identical for them by
construction. The pair-side family is nearly empty because the repository
holds no edges to external objects except citations of things it has
already metabolised. The hole is preserved; the human admission boundary
stays.

The by-product is real: that one available pair-side check -- does the
repository already cite this object -- caught ATLAS, which Aporia had
already written into a frontier hypothesis. A 1-in-6 false interruption
that a cheap non-semantic check would have prevented.

Three referent-side quantities are worth REPORTING to owners without
gating on them, and they convict Eos: the six claims named tokens
occurring in 6, 14, 118, 481 and 1198 tracked files. A token in 1,198
files is a word, not a referent.

## Why not the other three options

    BOUNDED_MANUAL     is what Season II already was, and it produced 6
                       interruptions of which 1 was a known duplicate and
                       0 have been confirmed useful. Repeating it before
                       the repairs are attacked would buy another
                       unmeasured sample at the same cost.
    RESTART_BOUNDED    presumes an acceptance rate nobody has measured,
                       and the proposer-not-gate finding says volume
                       cannot improve it.
    RETIRE_COLLECTION  overshoots. Three specific, cheap repairs are
                       identified and two are already built; the boundary
                       is not shown to be unbuildable, only unmeasured.
                       Retiring now would discard a working refusal
                       mechanism on the strength of a bad season.
