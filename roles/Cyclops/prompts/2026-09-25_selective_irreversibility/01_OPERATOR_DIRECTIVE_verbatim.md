⸻

1. Candidate Law

Prometheus will provisionally organize its next experimental cycle around the following falsifiable hypothesis:

SELECTIVE IRREVERSIBILITY HYPOTHESIS

In a bounded, reusable system that must predict, control, and generalize across changing environments, intelligence requires the selective elimination of distinctions from its accessible causal state while preferentially preserving distinctions relevant to future prediction and control.

The hypothesis is deliberately broader than the Information Bottleneck and narrower than the vague statement that “intelligence compresses.”

We are interested in whether successful adaptive systems must eventually perform some form of relevance-selective contraction of accessible causal state.

We are not assuming:

* neural networks;
* explicit representations;
* explicit memory modules;
* organisms;
* symbolic concepts;
* Information Bottleneck optimization;
* biological evolution;
* conventional compression;
* thermodynamic erasure occurring in any particular component.

The phenomenon may be implemented in ways we have not anticipated.

⸻

2. Important Correction and Scope

Do not reduce the hypothesis to:

“Intelligence requires erasing bits.”

Landauer’s principle concerns logically irreversible information processing and its thermodynamic consequences. Reversible computation can in principle preserve computational history rather than erase it.

Therefore the interesting question is not simply whether bits disappear.

The stronger question is:

Can a bounded, indefinitely reusable, generalizing adaptive system avoid relevance-selective loss of accessible distinctions anywhere in the complete agent/environment/resource cycle?

A purported reversible counterexample does not count if it merely exports an ever-growing history tape, accumulates unpriced memory, hides resets elsewhere, or pushes irreversibility outside the accounting boundary.

Similarly, an irreversible system does not support the hypothesis merely because it forgets things.

The selectivity of the lost information is essential.

⸻

3. Primary Scientific Objective

The fleet is now primarily tasked with trying to falsify this hypothesis.

Do not treat selective irreversibility as something to confirm.

The strongest outcome is a clean counterexample.

The second-strongest outcome is repeated independent emergence of relevance-selective contraction across radically different substrates despite active attempts to avoid it.

The program should distinguish:

1. information that remains accessible;
2. information that becomes inaccessible;
3. information preserved elsewhere or externalized;
4. information recoverable in principle but inaccessible to the acting system;
5. information genuinely destroyed by the relevant physical/computational process;
6. whether retained information predicts or controls future outcomes;
7. whether lost information was irrelevant to those outcomes;
8. total resource costs required by each strategy.

⸻

4. Principal Countermodels

The portfolio must aggressively seek at least these countermodels.

A. Reversible Intelligence

Seek a system that achieves sustained prediction, control, transfer, and generalization while:

* its relevant state transitions remain bijective/reversible;
* distinctions are not erased;
* history is not hidden in an unbounded external store;
* memory remains bounded;
* operation can continue cyclically;
* resets or entropy-producing operations are not moved outside the accounting boundary.

A successful instance would seriously damage the hypothesis.

B. Lossless Memorizer

Seek systems that preserve exact episodic information rather than coarse-graining it.

Give them a fair implementation.

Ask whether exact retention plus retrieval can outperform abstraction-forming systems under honest accounting of:

* storage;
* memory traffic;
* retrieval computation;
* latency;
* communication;
* lifetime;
* external state;
* reset cost.

If lossless retention remains competitive under increasing environmental complexity without silently performing an equivalent coarse-graining operation, that damages the hypothesis.

C. Indiscriminate Irreversibility

Match the amount of information loss while destroying distinctions randomly or according to a relevance-blind rule.

If relevance-blind destruction performs as well as selective destruction, then the interesting mechanism may be finite memory, regularization, noise, computational savings, or another effect rather than selective irreversibility.

D. Unknown Countermodels

Do not restrict the search to the three categories above.

Existing search engines should remain capable of inventing mechanisms that neither the operator nor you anticipated.

⸻

5. Experimental Contamination Rule

This is critical.

Do not tell every engine to search for selective irreversibility.

We need both theory-aware and theory-blind evidence.

Maintain at least three portfolio classes:

DIRECT FALSIFICATION

Engines may know the hypothesis and deliberately construct attacks against it.

Expected major participants include:

* Aether / AGE;
* Cosmos / CWE;
* Nestor / NPE;
* Ananke / PTE;
* Ensorain / WTP.

BLIND DISCOVERY

At least one substantial search lane must continue without being told that selective irreversibility is the target.

It should continue searching for unusual competence, organization, reproduction, reasoning, transfer, abstraction, or other phenomena under its own prior charter.

Only afterward may its artifacts be tested for the proposed relationship.

Do not contaminate blind runs by inserting hypothesis-derived objectives, features, classifiers, reward terms, or promotion criteria.

OBSERVATION / ADJUDICATION

Use measurement-oriented systems to determine retrospectively:

* what information survived;
* what became inaccessible;
* lineage and causal ownership;
* whether losses aligned with future relevance;
* whether apparent compression was genuine;
* whether the same relationship appears across independent implementations.

Archaeon’s emerging environmental/heredity/attribution lens is particularly relevant here.

Atlas and Harmonia should also play major roles.

7. Engine Development Moratorium

Until the operator changes this ruling:

FREEZE

Pause:

* creation of new general-purpose engines;
* large architecture rewrites without a direct experimental need;
* feature accumulation merely because an engine can support more features;
* premature unification of the existing substrates;
* building an “abstraction engine” whose construction assumes the hypothesis is true.

CONTINUE

Allow:

* repairs required for reproducibility;
* instrumentation required for falsification;
* adapters needed for cross-engine experiments;
* narrow mechanisms required by a preregistered experiment;
* new substrates only when they supply a genuinely missing countermodel that cannot be tested honestly with existing machinery.

The default question for new engineering work is:

Does this materially increase our ability to falsify, discriminate, or independently test the candidate law?

If not, park it.

9. Resource Accounting

A counterexample is invalid if it wins by moving its costs out of frame.

Experiments should account for relevant quantities such as:

* active state size;
* retained history;
* externalized history;
* memory capacity;
* memory traffic;
* computational operations;
* communication;
* wall time;
* latency;
* reset operations;
* writes/overwrites;
* persistent storage;
* reusable-cycle cost.

Do not immediately reduce everything to joules or (k_BT\ln2).

Logical-information accounting and physical thermodynamic accounting should remain distinguishable.

Where physical implementations justify thermodynamic analysis, add it explicitly.

16. Scientific Standard

The purpose of this program is not to show that selective irreversibility is profound.

The purpose is to determine whether it is false.

If a clean bounded reversible intelligence exists, find it.

If exact memory wins, show it.

If random forgetting works just as well, show it.

If intelligence routinely avoids state-space contraction, show it.

If the hypothesis survives, it should survive because heterogeneous systems repeatedly failed to escape it under fair conditions—not because we designed the fleet to instantiate it.

Preserve alien mechanisms.

Preserve negative results.

Preserve disagreement.

Prefer adversarial experiments over persuasive narratives.

The North Star is not confirmation.

The North Star is a law that remains standing after Prometheus has learned how to attack it.
