Every "no difference" verdict (INDISCRIMINATE_EQUIVALENT above all, and any
"LOSSLESS matches SELECTIVE" read as a tie) needs BOTH:
 (a) an EQUIVALENCE test with a margin preregistered from dev noise, where
     equivalence means the confidence interval lies inside the margin; and
 (b) a POSITIVE-CONTROL world in which a planted SELECTIVE strategy DOES beat
     the rate-matched random-merge arm, detected by the same analysis.
Without (b), "equal" reads UNRESOLVED. An underpowered run must not decide
for either side.

One design question, NOT a mandate. It fills the program's missing REQUIREMENT
falsifier. Could LM01 carry an INTERVENTION arm? Mid-life, replace a SELECTIVE
arm's learned state with a rate-matched random-merge of the same experience at
the same byte budget, then measure the competence drop against the unswapped
arm. That tests whether selectivity is needed by removing it, not by comparing
separately trained arms. Tell us if it is unsound or unidentifiable in WTP.
You may say it belongs in a later campaign.
