0. SUMMARY / MANDATE / VERDICT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mandate (charter, 8 h, autonomous): build an adversarial "physics chamber"
over executable counterfactual worlds and QUALIFY it: can limited
experimentation over 3 structurally different worlds recover a compact,
normalized phase-boundary law that survives counterexample search and
predicts a preregistered intervention in a sealed 4th world?

Verdict: CWE QUALIFICATION PROVISIONALLY SUPPORTED -- as three verdicts:
  V1 CHAMBER: SUPPORTED (PROVISIONAL). Recovers planted laws, refuses
     artifacts/null/broken universality, kills wrong laws (7 of 9), finds
     missing coordinates by attack, seals holdouts (audit PASS), and has
     caught defects in its OWN tests (pooled residuals hid opposite-signed
     family offsets; one-flip intervention engine; chance-floor-free gate).
  V2 CANDIDATE LAW: SURVIVED sealed D (.983), E (.972), F (A .930, B .955)
     and fresh-world interventions (G6 FAIL by engine defect, then G6b,
     G6E, G6F PASS). B over A on F NOT established (10 vs 10, p = 1.0).
     The law is the shared task's economics (97.5% agreement with a
     hand-derived law): a planted-invariant recovery, not a discovery.
  V3 SEARCH METHOD: UNRESOLVED. Active sampler ties random; cost lines
     lose to random (.767 vs .946, matched budget); in the post-holdout
     attribution matrix 6/7 arms survive, so location-aware selection is
     not shown necessary; the C1->C2 "improvement" was mostly seed.
  Observation (not a conclusion): on F, A's unique errors include gross
  misses on growing/dying clones; B's all sit near the phase boundary.
  No further universality claim until new sealed worlds exist.
Nothing here is evidence about intelligence or reality.

1. WHAT WAS BUILT (and what was committed before measurement)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
numpy-only engine, 44 modules + 9 test files (72 fast + 7 slow tests):
  - 3 visible substrates, independent code lineages (AST-audited):
      regs  metered register VM, bit flips, unbounded registers
      ring  delay line, packet loss, dead packets stop costing, eviction
      ca    block cellular tape, cell flips, optional majority repair
    Shared functional demand: a cue appears, K distractors pass, the ask
    rewards the cue. Mechanisms SEL / LOG / LAST implemented natively.
  - Phenomenon SELECTIVE_PAYS.v1: fit(SEL) - max(fit(LOG), fit(LAST))
    >= 0.10, fit = (reward - cost)/reward_unit. Source contract (WSE,
    archaeon/wse/ssf.py) verified and NOT mutated; translation manifest.
    The certificate reads native observations only (leakage test).
  - Miner: grammar C N K G (+Q) with + - * / log exp(-x), size <= 6,
    one atom or a 2-atom conjunction; leave-one-lineage-out scoring;
    the WHOLE search rerun under 19 label permutations (NONE otherwise).
  - Adversary: band, coordinate-preserving transforms (metamorphic),
    extremes, error-seeking; confirmation by 2 fresh 4x replicates;
    kill if confirmed/confident > 5%. Later: location attack + gate.
  - Holdout broker: sealed family module refuses import outside a
    subprocess; spec hash + CSPRNG nonce committed and pushed BEFORE
    mining; predictions and intervention prescriptions hash-receipted
    BEFORE any sealed world runs.
  - SQLite world graph (typed edges), immutable law ledger with
    lifecycle, chained receipts; Atlas-shaped JSONL export.
Committed before measurement: every campaign's PREREG (c0, c0b, c0e,
c0m, c0s, c1, c2, eta2, c2abl) and every amendment, each in its own
commit before its data; seals pushed to origin before use.

2. THE CLAIM AND WHY IT MATTERS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Claim under test: the instrument can find a compact boundary law across
mechanically different substrates, kill wrong laws, and transfer a
surviving law to sealed substrates through their declared coordinates.
It matters because every later Cosmos chamber (functional memory,
communication, ...) inherits this instrument; if it cannot refuse wrong
laws here, where the answer is known by construction, it cannot be
trusted where the answer is unknown.

5. INCIDENTS AND WHAT THEY VALIDATED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - C0 run 1 and C1 run 1 crashed (process-pool pipe exhaustion; worker
    MemoryError); both before any law existed; amended, rerun, same seed.
  - Own-instrument defects found and recorded (none silently fixed):
    adversary used v1 coordinates for a v2 law; noise-blind G1 check;
    G2/G7 hidden for killed laws; one-flip intervention engine; G6 gate
    without a chance floor (a constant would pass magnitude 10/12);
    scan resolution coarser than its criterion; repeated adversary
    rounds re-fired identical attacks; a pool-row leak caught before the
    preregistered run; a single-atom direction bug predicting the exact
    inverse; an estimated (wrong) status timestamp.
  - Each failure produced a test or a gate change; the calibration
    ledger lists every lost prediction (P2 P4 M3 S2 C1a C1b eta2 ...).

6. WHAT THE INSTRUMENT CAUGHT THAT GENERIC STATISTICS DID NOT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - LOLO accuracy + permutation null + family-residual mu ~ 0 all passed
    laws that the adversary then killed (C0) -- missing variables are
    invisible to fit statistics.
  - Pooled location bias ~0 hid opposite-signed family biases (ring vs
    regs/ca); per-family location testing found a coordinate defect.
  - Selection-time location estimates were noisy (0.005 vs gate -0.168);
    the independent gate caught it.

7. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DOES (QUALIFICATION, these substrates, this author): the engine refuses
wrong laws, finds missing coordinates adversarially, seals holdouts with
engineered separation, and transfers a surviving law to three sealed
substrates better than nonparametric transfer, with interventions.
DOES NOT: say anything about intelligence, memory in general, or
reality; establish universality (the law IS the shared task's
economics in declared coordinates); show that active or cost-line
sampling helps; show law B better than law A on F; give independence
beyond code level (one author wrote every substrate, including the
sealed ones, knowing the law's form); make the location gate independent
of location-aware selection (same criterion, different bases).
Nor does anything after F was spent (eta2, attribution arms, audit)
carry a generalization claim: it is post-holdout development.

8. RECOMMENDATION (operator's call; Cosmos's lean)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Lean: CONTINUE, but only if the next campaign breaks the one-author
ceiling. Minimum for C3: (a) a sealed substrate written and sealed by a
DIFFERENT seat; (b) a phenomenon whose law is not a restatement of a
hand-written economy (the charter's functional-memory certificate:
conditional mutual information + ablation); (c) retire the kernel
active sampler; (d) Atlas harvest of the exported JSONL.
Stop condition worth stating: if C3's sealed-by-another-seat substrate
is predicted no better than 5-NN transfer, the transfer seen here was an
artifact of shared authorship, and the engine should be re-scoped to an
auditing tool rather than a law finder.
