6. C4 DESIGN v0.1 (key choices)
-----
- Question moved upstream: what physical properties of a world make history causally accessible?
- S1 machinery firewall:
  - coordinates come ONLY from task-free, single, unperturbed trajectories;
  - unconditional statistics at any lag; input-conditioned statistics only at lags 0-1;
  - no twins, swaps, ablations, resampling or task-trained readouts;
  - enforced by an import audit, a mutation test (the coordinates must be bitwise unchanged when the
    certificate code or seeds change), a runner that never stores inputs older than 1 step, and a
    re-encoding invariance suite.
- S2: family predictability reported as kappa. WARNING above .30 triggers three dependence tests.
  LOFO is primary.
- S3: Certificate B shares no machinery with A: a different estimator class, and an unpaired
  marginal-resampling intervention instead of the paired swap.
- S4: the target intervention arm is "perturbation still registered, upstream property changed ->
  predicted FUNCTIONAL->NONE", where the certificate rule predicts no change. It has a matched
  opposite arm.
- Planted calibration includes a PURE-CHAOS control: T3-DOWN calls it FUNCTIONAL, and it is NONE by
  construction.
- World sample: >= 240 determinate worlds, >= 4 families (>= 2 new mechanisms), and a label-blind
  sampler putting >= 50% in high-noise / near-critical strata. The C3 visible worlds are burned for
  scoring.
- D2: its compatibility is determined separately, after the visible gates, by a seat other than
  Cosmos, against the operator's 4 conditions (including the I2 ruling).
