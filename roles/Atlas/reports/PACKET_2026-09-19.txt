--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SUBJECT UNDER REVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A two-tier relational history of the experiments the Prometheus engines
ran, built only by rerunnable read-only collectors. Tier 1 is the
manifest (engine, host, engine instance, campaign, experiment, attempt).
Tier 2 holds segments, facts, verbatim conclusions, typed edges, ideas,
defects and first-class source pointers. WHAT RAN, WHAT WAS OBSERVED and
WHAT SOMEONE CONCLUDED are separate tables. Atlas's own readings are
labelled ATLAS_DERIVED, with the rule and version that produced them.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. METHOD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Eight versioned collectors, all idempotent. Each run records the host,
harvester and version that wrote it in a harvest_run row.

  commits/2             all remote + local refs since 2026-08-25
  archaeon_campaigns/1  archaeon/campaign1..6 (SFE)
  frontier/3            archaeon/frontier (DEEP FRONTIER scheduler)
  npe/4                 M1-LOCAL branch nestor/sidequest-graphworld
                        (rounds r1..r8, CW01 + loop)
  vivarium/2            viv.research_experiment_queue, execution_attempt
  pew/1                 ew.campaign_observations (pointers only)
  local_files/2         M1 SFE ledgers (idle, read-only, immutable),
                        logs, pm-data, CW01 run logs
  comb/2                13 surfacing rules -> atlas.signal

Identity is built from native ids, never from filenames. A native id
seen under two keys is recorded as a collision, never merged. The merge
rule never erases data: scalars coalesce, arrays union, and a watched
field that disagrees is written to field_conflict. Pruning removes only
derived rows that the same harvester wrote on the same host.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. KEY DEFINITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
engine > campaign > experiment > attempt > segment.
A new attempt means the same spec was re-executed; a changed spec means a
descendant experiment. Edges have a basis of DECLARED, INFERRED or
ATLAS_DERIVED, and a reason drawn from the vocab (BUG_FIX ... UNKNOWN).
Source visibility is one of GIT_REMOTE, GIT_LOCAL:M1, FS:M1, PG:M1 or
EXPECTED:<host>. EXPECTED means "referenced but not seen from here"; it
never means "did not happen".

5. WHAT NO SINGLE ENGINE OR CAMPAIGN CAN SEE BY ITSELF
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
a. The SFE and the NPE share one scientific lineage. NPE ideas T-ARCH4
   and T-ARCH5 originate in Archaeon Campaigns 4/5. Campaign 5
   experiments C5-01/02/03/05 are declared as superseding NPE loop
   perturbations P-C01/C02/C09/C11. P-E03 continues C4-08. C4-REH-1 and
   Campaign 5 are cited as evidence for NPE trajectories. Neither
   engine's files show the whole chain.
b. Campaign 4's damage census (C4-01) has descendants in three
   programs: C4 and C5 on the SFE, frontier transformations C4-cliff.T1/
   T3 (declared ORGANISM_DEFORMATION onto graph organisms), and a
   frontier lineage idea. Query: atlas.descendants('experiment',
   'archaeon.campaign/cmp4:C4-01').
c. The dormant M1 SFE ledger (engine instance eng_8a37..., schema 8,
   2026-09-01..11) holds 66,958 engine experiments: vivarium 63,403,
   harmonia-m2 3,068. Membership shows that 610 Vivarium attempts ran
   against that M1 instance. No git receipt says so.
d. C4-07's mechanism reading ("robustness = dead code") is marked as a
   SUPERSEDED_INTERPRETATION, superseded by C5-08. C4-07's execution and
   numbers stay valid and are kept.
e. 17 SFE/NPE experiments changed disposition across attempts. For
   example, several C2 experiments went from POSITIVE_CONTROL_FAILED to
   WEAK_POSITIVE or CAPABLE_NEGATIVE after repairs. Only the attempt
   history shows that.
f. Defect classes recur across campaigns: RECOVERY 28 (3 campaigns),
   LANDSCAPE 21 (3), BUG 20 (5), MISSING_TELEMETRY 13 (4),
   ASSAY_STATE 9 (4).
g. Two frontier interpretations are still PROVISIONAL:
   - Campaign 5's "flat elite" may be a compute/population artefact.
   - The graph firing-rate mismatch.
h. A live, previously uncatalogued service is running on M1:
   F:/SerendipityD foundry_service.py (pids 7988 and 8176). It is
   registered as engine serendipity.foundry.d and covered stat-only.
