## 0 Stance and verdict vocabulary

A regularity found "in N engines" is evidence about substrates only to the extent that the N sightings do not
share a cause upstream of the substrates. This program has an unusually dense set of such shared causes:
- one operator;
- one model family, which writes the worlds, the rulers, the plants, the searches, the audits, the digests,
  and both synthesists;
- one comms bus;
- one governance code;
- one brief;
- a two-week window.

For Stage 2 I will give each candidate regularity one of four verdicts:

| verdict | meaning |
|---|---|
| **SUBSTRATE** | holds across substrates whose designs do not share the causal factor, measured by rulers that do not share the failure mode |
| **DESIGN-FAMILY** | true, but only of worlds built with this program's design grammar (supplied ops, zero init, discrete deterministic integer state, authored task economies). This is still informative, but it is a fact about what we build, not about "substrates". |
| **PROGRAM-CULTURE** | the pattern is produced by the rulers, audits, vocabulary, brief or governance. It may be true of the program and still say nothing about the worlds. |
| **UNDETERMINED** | the corpus cannot separate the above |

**Headline (ATLAS_DERIVED).** All four regularities the brief names as examples are at serious risk.
- Two of them are nearly verbatim phrases of the READER_BRIEF that every digest author worked from:
  - item 3 asks for "search/reachability limits";
  - item 4 asks for "any case where the ruler could not see the target".
- The other two come from a single ISA lineage and from authored worlds.

## D Shared governance and incentives

1. **"Absence counts only where a plant fired" (D1).**
   - Codified in PTE C1b A3, in Harmonia STANDING_RULES F1-F8 (all dated 09-30), and in R-11's advice "treat every NULL ... as UNRESOLVED unless a same-substrate positive fired" (HR s9.1).
   - Consequence: the corpus can almost never assert "physics forbids X". Every absence routes to one of three bins: (a) ruler blind, (b) design unreachable, or (c) search did not reach.
   - The plant must be author-written, so the rule licenses absence only for things the author can already build.
   - "Search reachability, not physics, bounds what we see" is close to the only conclusion this rule allows an absence to reach. It is a rule-shaped regularity.
   - The single durable physics-negative, Aether's "lacks propagation" (CA s9.3), survived only because its twin assay is exact. Even it holds for "ONE energy regime".
2. **Frozen bars before attainable range (D2).**
   - AH s9 F1 counts at least 20 targets or gates not shown attainable before freezing.
   - Outcomes: CW01 e07/e08/e09 "DESIGN UNREACHABLE"; Tyche H1 "UNREACHABLE_BY_DESIGN"; REPL-02 positive arm extinct; LM01 never launched.
   - The regime mass-produces reachability verdicts. Their frequency measures bar-setting, not worlds.
3. **Defect base rate (D3).**
   - Artemis's self-test found that a random harvested question with about 1-2 agent-hours "produced a consequential finding ~9 times in 10" (HR s7.11).
   - R-11 audited 94 absence gates; about 40% never fired (HR s4a).
   - So "rulers that cannot observe their target recur everywhere" is what you find when you audit instruments built in hours by one author under time caps. It is an ATLAS_DERIVED fact about the build process.
   - Discriminator: audit an EXTERNAL, mature instrument with equal effort (e.g. a published Avida/Tierra analysis tool, or Harmonia's own POET novelty.py ruling, HR s1 row 4). If the defect rate is similar, the regularity is about auditing, not about this program.
4. **Asymmetric bars (D4).**
   - Positive claims face frozen p < 0.001 plus a plant (e.g. C-SWAP-ACQUIRE 9/240 vs 0/240, p 0.0018 = NULL).
   - Deflationary kills often rest on a NON-significant difference: Cosmos C0 laws RESTRICTED because they did not beat the zero-parameter rung (McNemar p .688/.688/.125; CA s3). A tie read as "restatement" is an absence claim with no plant of its own, which breaks the program's own D1 rule.
   - Cheapest check: list the 10 most-cited deflations and record for each (i) a frozen bar, (ii) a planted positive that the deflating attack could have FAILED on. My expectation is that few have (ii).
5. **Challenge routing and dormant seats (D5).**
   - BEE is a "blind lane for Artemis routing"; FR-091 is "unowned at the program level" (EB s8).
   - Proteus (last commit 09-18) and SFE (orphaned) never answered R-01..R-34 or D002-03q (AR s9.5).
   - Deflationary challenges against silent seats stand by default. The "cliff = reachability" reading of PROTEUS-46 is one example.
6. **Operator directives.**
   - One operator issued the Z80 directive, the 72 h campaign, the harvest directive ("Prepare REL4 as if its eventual result could be the last PTE observation", AP s8), the ENVGATE R1 adjudication and the C5 acceptance.
   - The operator also asked Atlas for cross-engine structure. A synthesis step commissioned to find regularities has a demand-side bias toward finding some.
   - The memory index has `feedback_ludus_bench_not_kill_claims`, i.e. the operator pushing against kill-claim culture, so operator taste is not uniformly deflationary.
7. **Lessons propagate as priors.** There are 153 `feedback_*` memory files (AH s0), and Nestor keeps a lessons ledger. A lesson learned on one engine (e.g. "count rulers manufacture length") is then looked for, and found, on the next engine, as in the ATLAS readings of C4-02 (AR s8). This is prime-then-detect.
