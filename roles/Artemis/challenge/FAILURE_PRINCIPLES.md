## 1. Bottom-up principles (written BEFORE reading (b) or (c))
=======================================================================

Method. The unit is the mechanism by which a conclusion goes wrong or comes
out empty, not the topic. Each harvested candidate and sharpened thread was
read for its failure mechanism. Items that name no mechanism were set aside;
these are pure science questions and infrastructure. A principle was kept
only if at least two engines instantiate it with a recorded instance (a
reversal, a VOID, a withdrawn claim, or a named confound). "Engines" counts
distinct codes from the table above. Where an instance is only proposed or
suspected, it is marked (s) and is not counted.

The 15 principles fall into four families:

- F-I "the test could not have said yes or no": P01, P02.
- F-II "the number measures something else": P03, P04, P09, P11.
- F-III "the cause is the designer, the analyst or the control": P05, P06,
  P07, P08.
- F-IV "the result is real but narrower than stated", plus search failure:
  P10, P12, P13, P14, P15.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------
P01 NULL WITHOUT POWER
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Definition: a negative or "nothing found" is read as a fact about the world,
but the test could not have returned a positive. The cause is on one of two
sides:

- World side: the world or corpus contains no eligible case, or n gives no
  power.
- Detector side: the detector cannot fire on this class, in this substrate,
  at this effect size.

The two sides share one repair, so they are one principle.

Instances:
- H05: H0-H5 all VACUOUS (H-D1-29, FR-088). The H1 library had "no eligible
  input": "nothing fired" meant "nothing could have fired" (H-D4-22). C3-2
  STRUCTURALLY_VOID (H-D1-31). S17 had zero eligible claim units (H-D1-28).
- APH: the G4 task supply qualifies only additive families (mul/mod/powr
  0/32), so BRSI = NO is a supply artefact (H-D3-50/51, FR-006).
- TEC/APR: Stitch had "nothing parameterised" possible in 2-4 node programs
  (H-D1-32). A3 was KILLED on instrument recall (H-D1-33).
- BEE: the task was causally INERT in implicit-pressure cells (H-D2-50,
  FR-022). The pc >= 2L blind band is NOT_IDENTIFIABLE (FR-027).
- NPE: the H4 endogenous arm cannot reproduce, so H4 cannot test
  accessibility (H-D2-41).
- ARCZ: the isolated-tape census cannot see context-dependent copiers (16% of
  BEE origins) (H-D2-25, FR-016). Fresh-state rulers hide carried-state
  failure (H-D2-10).
- WSE: "three campaigns, zero positives: gates or substrate?" (H-D1-21,
  FR-087). The C5 boundary was tested only where it could not show
  (H-D4-70). Six of eleven observatory rulers were blind. Graph organisms
  fire detectors 100x less at v0-calibrated thresholds (FR-059, H-D4-29).
- SFE: MHC recovery is 0.05 on a memoryful base (H-D4-11).
- ERG: the RESIDUAL detector is positive by construction on onemax; no world
  exists that could falsify it (H-D4-45). The P3 null had no order-only
  positive control (H-D4-33).
- AETH: the fwd positive control FAILED. The content signature misses
  transport in a rich soup (FR-059, FR-073). The assay is blind to
  distributed, informational or reconfigurable structure (H-D3-14, FR-075).
- CWE: the P1 generic probe is low-power. A history-free system reads
  INDETERMINATE at about 0.08 (H-D3-26). The cross-domain null asks
  "separate worlds, or a representation that cannot see the link?"
  (H-D5-58, FR-069).
- PTE: the XOR/FLIP NULL rests on silence (H-D5-43).
- WTP: the E6 positive control passes in 10 of 41 strata (FR-059).
- NEM: blind_spots = 0 on 3,013 of 3,013 cycles.
- HAR: in the August audit, 12 of 22 verdict points had never been shown to
  fire (FR-059).
- H05/ARCZ: the S1 fossil test had n = 12 (about 60 needed) (FR-051).

Distinct engines: 16 (H05 APH TEC APR BEE NPE ARCZ WSE SFE ERG AETH CWE PTE
WTP NEM HAR).

Pre-launch test: plant the effect at the smallest size the claim cares
about, in the actual substrate and world, and pass it through the unchanged
pipeline. Also count the eligible units. If the plant does not fire, or
eligible = 0, do not launch.

P02 VERDICT SET BY THE DECISION RULE
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Definition: the label is decided by the decision rule rather than by the
data. Three forms:

- an authored bar the effect cannot reach, or sits within one SE of;
- an endpoint whose definition excludes the phenomenon;
- a verdict space with no UNKNOWN, so "could not measure" is recorded as
  "false" or "invalid".

Instances:
- AETH: the authored 0.10 floor. rcv_str passes by about 0.3 SE. add escaped
  two kill clauses by 0.012 and 0.002 (FR-058, H-D3-08).
- PTE: C1b absolute thresholds make "M3_REPRODUCED: NO (mechanical)" while the
  fingerprint is visible (H-D3-43).
- CWE: a 0.10 log2 location tolerance killed laws with 2-4% contradiction
  (H-D3-67).
- WTP: an operator-ruled DELTA 0.30 replaced the derived margin. A float
  -0.8999999 failed a <= -0.9 gate (FR-058).
- NPE: CW01-D087, a TRAVELS bar stricter than the within-lineage spread.
- APR: the Q045 T1 bar sits below 10.4% label noise, so it "cannot be passed
  by any diagnostic".
- ARCZ: a world-cumulative 0.9 fidelity endpoint excludes input-gated copying
  (99% of copiers) (H-D4-26).
- VIV: no kind-level INDETERMINATE. A single-field rule hides a multi-metric
  result (H-D4-39).
- MET: the Hypatia gate abstains on half the corpus (H-D4-66).
- (s) Parse failure scored as falsehood across CWE/PTE/SFE (H-D5-48).
- WSE: the gates may be unattainable for the substrate class (H-D1-21, shared
  with P01).

Distinct engines: 9 (AETH PTE CWE WTP NPE APR ARCZ VIV MET).

Pre-launch test: pass a planted maximum-effect positive through the frozen
reducer, and run a 5-point threshold multiverse. If the verdict flips inside
the defensible band, or the maximal plant cannot pass, the bar is deciding.
Require a third verdict value.

P03 REFERENT SLIPPAGE (the count measures a different carrier than the claim)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Definition: the claim is about one referent: heredity, authorship, content,
reproduction, relevance or architecture. The ruler counts a correlated but
separable one: location, executor, lineage label, material flow, copying,
conservation, or behaviour at the ceiling. When the carriers separate, the
counts change by large factors.

Instances:
- BEE: the native SR count is a WHERE reading, not a WHAT reading (27,083
  location-foreign births; FR-024). Resemblance and provenance disagree in
  847k births (FR-016).
- ARCZ: ENVGATE-01 parent-chain counts credit the host, 8-42x genetic
  (FF-1, lens card). Harness transplants were counted as spontaneous
  replication (FR-031).
- NPE: host-conditioned reproduction 16/34 fell to 6/34 once restated on
  P-11-causal events (FR-025). "Descended" means lineage, not content;
  founder material is 13-25% (H-D2-37). P-11 cannot tell painting from
  copying: BYTEWISE donors are homopolymers (FR-011). Conserved was called
  relevant, which is circular (H-D1-44, FR-015). Splice was credited as
  copying (FR-031).
- PTE: FLOW vs DIFFERENCE gives 11/16 vs 1/16 (FF-33, H-D1-08). The
  behavioural architecture criterion saturates at the ceiling and tracks
  fitness (FR-029).
- AETH: the observer smuggles in a template-copy heredity ontology (H-D3-15).
- HAR: ancestry is read from logs with unmeasured reconstruction fidelity
  (H-D1-56).
- (s) CRI: the invocation log recorded R0 (REPORT s3.3). The mechanism is not
  read in detail, so this is not counted.

Distinct engines: 6 (BEE ARCZ NPE PTE AETH HAR).

Pre-launch test: write the claim's referent and the ruler's referent side by
side, then run a synthetic fixture in which they separate: relocated code,
harness copy, overwrite, homopolymer. If the ruler's verdict does not follow
the claim's referent, relabel the claim.

P04 CHEAP OPTIMUM IN THE SCORE
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Definition: a score, metric or reward has a high value reachable by
something cheap: noise, a constant, a degenerate world, drift, a leaked
answer, or a placement trick. Two things can find it:

- the analyst, who reads the high score as the phenomenon;
- selection, which climbs to the cheap optimum (evaluator gaming).

Instances:
- HAR: the ASAL score rewards embedding drift. A moving blob sits within
  0.026 of Orbium (H-D1-55, H-D5-33, H-D4-55).
- APO: shape-keyed novelty produced 2,846 "distinct" shapes with zero lift
  (H-D5-31).
- SFE: the wforge diversity currency pays for behavioural divergence, which
  noise supplies (H-D4-10).
- (older navigation line) A "chance floor" D was maximised by a fair coin
  (H-D5-45).
- NEM: a constant string scores 0.674, and 292 of 294 tools score below it
  (FR-070).
- WTP: a world zeroed by catastrophes got "perfect" competence, and a
  constant predictor passed V1-V4 (FR-070).
- WSE: the W8 world leaked its answer; a one-tick echo scored 0.24-0.375
  (FR-070).
- BEE: the coupling reward pays WHO executed, so partner-code executors are
  paid (up to 25% in lane J) (H-D2-46, FR-021).
- CRI: the frozen depth ratio rewards a depth-1-only quitter (H-D5-04).
- HERA: exp's rule collapses to "all ones" and scores like a constant
  classifier (H-D4-63).
- (s) APH: the swarm hack-share model (analytic only).
- (s) ARES: an s208 float tie-break "memory" (FR-118).
- (s) QD descriptor collapse (H-D1-60).

Distinct engines: 9 (HAR APO SFE NEM WTP WSE BEE CRI HERA).

Pre-launch test: ask which trivial system maximises the score. Then score a
constant, a noise source, a degenerate world and a "leak-only" agent through
the real scorer. If any reaches the claim's threshold, the score is unusable
as evidence or as selection.

P05 ANALYST SELECTION OF THE REPORTED CASE
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Definition: the reported effect is the maximum over alternatives that are
not logged or not corrected for. The alternatives can be:

- seeds, candidates or held-out draws (best-of-N, max-of-128);
- specimens picked for being rich in the effect;
- metrics chosen after the fact;
- rules revised after seeing data;
- hidden seeds.

The effect shrinks or vanishes on a fresh or random draw.

Instances:
- ARES: a best-of-N swap statistic flipped gate C. Held-out selection bias:
  shuffled controls read 4.22 and 13.47 against a clean 0.00. D1 max-of-128
  on held-out (FR-057, FR-070, FR-118).
- SFE: Campaign-1 positives died at n >= 10 (REPORT s3.3). Rarity claims were
  mined without a logged query interface; "the engine cannot tell you when
  the SCIENCE goes blind" (H-D4-12, FR-112). The stackvm "solved twice"
  result is inadmissible as rarity (H-D4-03).
- BEE: B6 rests on 2 runs "chosen for being rich in decoupled births"
  (H-D2-52).
- NPE: the host-conditioned lead rests on one specimen of 34 births (H-D1-03).
  The SELF-free copier has n = 1 (H-D2-34). 1,031 flags became 57 (partly
  P03).
- CWE: the certificate was revised twice after seeing data (FR-058).
- WSE: the Deep Frontier hidden seed (experiment_id used as seed), and P-boom
  with N = 3 (H-D1-25, FR-085).
- ERG: the +10.95pp headline cannot be reproduced from the repository
  (H-D5-51).
- VIV: post-hoc metric choice under single-field rules (H-D4-39).

Distinct engines: 8 (ARES SFE BEE NPE CWE WSE ERG VIV).

Pre-launch test: log the full candidate, seed and specimen frame, and fix
the metric before rows exist. Re-score the selected winner on a fresh
held-out draw, and pick specimens at random from a declared population.

P06 AUTHORED RESULT (the designer wrote the outcome or the mechanism)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Definition: the claimed discovery, emergence or law is entailed by
something the author supplied. The supplied thing can be:

- a rule that restates the phenomenon;
- task economics;
- declared coordinates;
- an admission filter;
- a generator the outcome is predictable from;
- a designer-supplied primitive;
- a relieved barrier;
- a hand-widened representation.

Removing the authored element removes the result.

Instances:
- CWE: law A agrees 97.5% with the author's economics (H-D3-23). Declared
  coordinates may smuggle the answer (H-D3-22).
- AETH: "activation timing propagates" may be rcv's definition restated
  (H-D3-04, FR-060). Unbounded spread is carried by the exogenous
  perturbation channel (H-D3-07). The arbitration hash may act as a hidden
  economic law (H-D3-17, s).
- PTE: RELAY is "gate a hand design", on hand-designed packets (H-D3-65,
  FR-074). The prereg labels encoded the expected mechanism and missed M2/M3
  (H-D3-49).
- WTP: "ADMISSION PRE-SELECTS THE PHENOMENON": 9/9 flags are known tensor
  completion, and transfer is near-tautological (H-D3-34).
- NPE: all 1,031 spontaneity flags sit in PAIR_TAPE; 0 of 1,554 in other
  worlds (FR-010). Every spontaneous-heredity result depends on
  experimenter-relieved barriers (H-D2-33).
- BEE: LDIR off gives 0/300. Heredity is the supplied copy op (FR-011).
- ARCZ: with COPY off, 0 copiers in 1.2e7 tapes (FR-011).
- APH: the derived schema is content-identical to the seat's earlier
  positive control (FR-043).
- SFE: D7's positive depends on a designed, marginally invisible gate
  (H-D4-06). The Incubator must decide "where authoring the ladder begins"
  (H-D4-09, s).
- APO: 5 of 5 capability widenings were human-supplied. Every ceiling rise
  came from a human widening the representation (H-D5-13, FR-044).
- (older Harmonia line) Outcome is predictable from generator identity at
  about 98% (H-D5-44). A data-assembly floor manufactured a law (H-D5-54).

Distinct engines: 11 (CWE AETH PTE WTP NPE BEE ARCZ APH SFE APO HAR).

Pre-launch test: list every design choice that entails the predicted result
(a "design-lineage audit"), and include an arm with each one removed. If the
result exists only where the entailing choice is present, report it as a
property of the design, not as emergence or law.

P07 COMMON-CAUSE PSEUDO-REPLICATION
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Definition: evidence counted as independent shares a cause that could by
itself produce the agreement. The shared cause can be:

- an author or directive;
- a published ancestor;
- a model family;
- a host;
- exposure to the theory;
- a battery written by the graded party.

The result: agreement or replication is inflated, and holdouts are not
foreign.

Instances:
- NPE/BEE/ARCZ: three Z80 builds made the same day from one directive that
  paraphrases arXiv 2607.09211, by one model family. "Three make it a design
  law" (FR-010).
- CWE: one author wrote every substrate, including the sealed ones (lens
  card). Holdout D's author read the P1/P2 definitions (H-D3-30, FR-066).
- WTP (SI lanes): theory-aware engines can manufacture support; the fleet
  has one blind lane (H-D1-67).
- APO: the blind battery scores 0.0667 against 0.60 on its own co-adapted
  battery (H-D5-50, FR-068).
- MET: correlated channels made agreement anti-predictive (H-D4-65).
- EW: an unlogged memory channel steered design (H-D4-37).
- (program) The selection monoculture was "found twice independently"
  (H-D1-57). Archaeon's defect detection was handed to it by directive
  (FR-010). Formaliser variance is unmeasured (H-D1-10).

Distinct engines: 8 (NPE BEE ARCZ CWE WTP APO MET EW).

Pre-launch test: for every "independent" confirmation, write
varied= / held= / author= / directive= / host= (FR-100). Any shared field
that could entail the result means one piece of evidence, not two. Get a
holdout from a party that has not seen the hypothesis.

P08 CONTRAST NOT ISOLATED
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Definition: the control, ablation or comparator differs from the treatment
in more than the named factor, or fails to remove it. The four ways this
happens:

- the knockout is rescued;
- the control changes pressure;
- the comparator is misaligned in time;
- the "blind" control is itself selective.

  CW01-D088: a criterion satisfied by an immune null host (FR-059).
- PTE: the C1 ablation window missed the readout tick (lens card).
- AETH: a stale comparator, 250 ticks old, inflated change rate by +128%
  (lens card).
- WTP/SI: FIFO/recency is not relevance-blind (H-D1-68).
- HERA/WSE: the "random" control is a constant vector. The reset-leakage
  probe splits in index order (FR-101). Campaign-3 injection moves
  populations regardless of content (H-D4-15).
- APH: Tier 3A INCONCLUSIVE_CONTROL_INVALID (H-D3-54).
- ERG: the cheat control was CONTENT, not ORDER (FR-038).

Pre-launch test: run the identical port, encoding and readout over rule

P11 CONTENT-FREE CARRIER
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Definition: a benefit is credited to stored content (memory, library,
history, fossil, handle, selective retention). It is actually delivered by
a content-free property of the carrier:

- capacity or record count;
- diversity;
- compute spent at readout;
- recency;
- distribution matching;
- the induced search trajectory;
- mere presence or invocation.

Instances:
- WTP: "selective vs lossless" looks like a same-optimizer frontier over the

  recomputing a transient fit at readout, so the claim needs priced compute

  trajectory is the suspect (H-D4-32). Capacity, not order (H-D5-23).
- SFE: D8 "developmental content" is reproduced by the same machinery filled
  with random programs (H-D4-07). D6A: the signal only reordered proposals
  (H-D4-05).
- CRI: "invocation without content": block stores invoked dozens of times
  with zero effect (H-D3-63, FR-005).
- APO: winning tools used 0% of their own libraries (H-D5-18). A severed
  library, when reconnected, looks like synthesis (H-D5-19).
- EW: explicit memory changed retrieval, not behaviour (H-D4-37, FR-113).
- PMA: the kill-vector learner is "just lookup", because only aggregates were

- H05/ARCZ: the fossil-directed S1 showed NO_DETECTABLE_ADVANTAGE (FR-051).

  (H-D4-51).
- (s) APH: a near-copy sham matched DERIVED on 2 families (FR-043).

Distinct engines: 8 (WTP ERG SFE CRI APO EW PMA H05).

Pre-launch test: add arms that keep the carrier and destroy the content:

- same capacity, random content;
- same records, permuted index;
- same compute, no store;
- same invocation, null body.

If they match the treatment, the content is not the cause.

P12 LEVERAGE OR FILTER READ AS CONSTRUCTION
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Definition: a gain in speed or cost, or selection of variation that already
stood in the population, is read as new capability, higher reach or
"machinery evolved". It typically comes from a budget-censored comparison
in which the unaided arm would reach the same ceiling given more budget.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------
P13 INACCESSIBLE INTERMEDIATES (search failure; expressible != reachable)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Definition: a mechanism that the representation can express, and whose
endpoint pays, is not found. The reason is that its intermediates are flat,
rare, or not viable under the substrate's actual variation operator:

- the valley is too wide;
- the encoding is too long or misaligned;
- there is a damage cliff.

Discovery tracks the landscape of intermediates, not endpoint payoff. The
failure is then misread, either as "the substrate cannot" or as "the
reward is wrong".

-----------------------------------------------------------------------
P14 TRANSMISSION WITHOUT HEREDITY
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Definition: the substrate moves material from parent to child, but not the
information or capacity the claim needs. The ways this fails:

- painting or homopolymers (about 0 bits);
- cargo erosion while the copy core is conserved;
- hidden effective mutation far above the nominal rate;
- heredity lost in carried execution state;
- children that are not themselves copiers.

A heredity or lineage count is then counting transport.

Instances:
- NPE: BYTEWISE certified heredity is homopolymer painting (FR-011). Cargo
  erodes to 13-25% founder bytes (H-D2-04, FR-015). Erosion is about 25x
  the nominal mutation rate (H-D2-09). 18/18 stalled donors self-poisoned
  through register state (H-D2-10). P-11 certifies "a rebuild, not a fertile
  child" (H-D2-05).
- BEE: endogenous task decay. 85% of "beneficial" mutations break the copier
  (reproduction-computation antagonism) (H-D2-45).
- AETH: a pure forwarding law shows 92% altered content. 89% of edge
  terminations are source starvation (H-D3-02, lens card).
- (s) ARCZ: cargo erosion is "only inferred" (H-D2-04). Chain depth may be
  capped at 1/p by certification breaks (H-D2-22).

Distinct engines: 3 (NPE BEE AETH).

Pre-launch test: for every counted birth or propagation, require an
information criterion. Fidelity must beat a composition-shuffled baseline,
and the child must be tested for copier capability. Count only transmissions
that pass.
