1. MOST CONSEQUENTIAL (an answer could redirect the program)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FR-011  Must the program author its unit of inheritance?  [MATURE]
  Every heredity result in the three Z80 worlds depends on a designer-
  supplied copy op. NEW this pass, from committed NPE data (Artemis
  re-verified): certified copying occurs in 47/531 runs WITH block copy
  and 10/500 WITHOUT -- and all 10 copy-op-free "replicators" are near-
  homopolymers (one byte = 91-96% of the genome; 5 of 10 are 0x36, which
  reproduces itself by painting). So without the op, NPE's certified
  heredity carries ~0 bits, and NPE's P-11 certificate cannot tell
  painting from copying (an instrument finding for Nestor). Prior art: in
  published Z80 soups, stack (PUSH) and byte-loop replicators DO arise
  without block copy; none of our three VMs has a stack route. Chop:
  zero-CPU re-tally with an information criterion, then seeded replicators.
  Bears on the open operator decision APO-28 / D-A02 ("author the
  replicator and say so, or hold that it condenses").

FR-001  The construction landscape, not the payoff, decides discovery.
  Five engines (Crius, Ares, Aether D-15, Ananke XOR/FLIP, Aphrodite
  recursion) report compatible findings that no thread connected. Prior
  art names it ("arrival of the frequent", Schaper & Louis 2014; "survival
  of the flattest", Wilke 2001) and adds a cheap basin-volume estimator
  (Mingard et al. 2021). Caution found: Ares's headline confounds basin
  and payoff (recurrence has the wider basin AND the higher peak, 40 vs
  33.75), and its own falsifier was demoted. "Payoff" must mean the
  finished mechanism's payoff (Lenski 2003: intermediates are the lever).

FR-010  Are the three Z80 builds independent evidence?  [MATURE]
  Partly answered: no. The 09-19 directive calls the published Z80 soup
  (Cicala et al. 2026, arXiv 2607.09211) "a donor of machinery" and asks
  for "analogues of the paper's phenomena"; Atlas catalogued the paper the
  same day. Same directive, model family, operator and host. The open part:
  which cross-engine "recurrences" are entailed by shared design choices
  (chop: factor matrix + tallies over arms that break each factor). This is
  the operator's convergence concern made measurable.

FR-043 / FR-044  Sagacity, operationally: does a compact handle make more
  reachable for a FRESH receiver, and does anything minted inside the
  ecology move a ceiling rather than just speed?
  Prior art: sagacity is a transmission property (Kirby 2008; Chaabouni
  2020) -- donor-side scores do not test it; handle format matters (LILO).
  Aphrodite's S4 used fresh recipients but only measured speed on a capped
  budget. Cheapest: PRISTINE alone at 10x budget on S4's five families --
  ~16/16 means S4 was speed only; 0-1/16 means a ceiling moved.

FR-067  Preregister the detector of surprise, not the surprise.
  The only proposal on record that squares preregistration with finding
  mechanisms nobody conceived (Aporia APO-06/APO-11; never started).
  Cheapest: a capped closure-growth spike on Aporia's existing world3 code.
