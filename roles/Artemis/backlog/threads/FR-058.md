# FR-058 -- Threshold provenance: verdicts decided by the bar, not the physics
State: SHARPENED. Cluster: G instrument validity. Sources: H-D3-67 H-D3-08 H-D3-43
Relations: narrower-than FR-057; related FR-059 FR-064; prior art PA_instruments_and_gaming.md s6 U6, s7 T1/T2, s4 P4/P5
Last reviewed: 2026-09-27 (first sharpening; per-engine provenance of the latest verdicts read from the frozen preregs; Ensorain
LM01 found to have RETIRED its replicate-derived margin, which complicates the proposed standard)

QUESTION -- For each engine's latest verdict, was the deciding threshold derived (from replicate or null uncertainty, or from a
planted-positive completeness curve) or authored (a round number or an operator ruling)? How many verdicts sit close enough to an
authored bar that a defensible alternative would flip them?

WHY IT MATTERS -- A negative decided by an arbitrary bar carries no information about the world (Aether Q3: "Is that the physics,
or the bar?"). If bar-decided verdicts are common, the program should adopt Cosmos C3's rule ("tolerances from uncertainty,
preregistered as rules not numbers") everywhere. If they are rare, the concern is anecdotal and can be closed.

Proposed: one standard, derived tolerances plus planted positives (H-D3-67). PA T1: census, SD of existing seeds, and a 5-point
threshold multiverse. PA T2: pass a planted maximum-effect champion through the unchanged reducer to test attainability.
External: HEP blind analysis fixes cuts while blind (PA W9). SESOI and TOST make negatives informative (PA S7). Kepler
completeness curves set a bar where recall crosses a stated level (PA W11). U6: no computational-evolution program was found
that audits threshold provenance per gate.

UNCERTAINTY -- (1) How many current verdicts are within about 1 SE of an authored bar. (2) Whether "derived" is actually safer.
Ensorain D10 shows a noise-derived margin rewarding unstable arms, and Cosmos re-derived its rule twice after seeing data. The
real issue may be "fixed blind and checked for attainability" rather than "derived versus authored". (3) Whether Aether's and
Ananke's bars are attainable at all for the effect sizes their designs allow.

CHEAPEST DISCRIMINATOR -- A repo-only provenance table plus a threshold multiverse on committed rows.
Inputs: the frozen prereg and reducer of each engine's LATEST verdict: Aether AETH-03 Block D (branch; E-006 REDUCTION.json is
  committed there), Ananke C1b (rows committed), Cosmos C3 v3 and the CWE location gate, Ensorain LM01 (frozen, no rows),
  Ares cycle 2 gates A-C, Nestor P-11, and the Archaeon causal-lens verdicts.
Procedure: (1) For every deciding threshold, record: gate id; number; stated source (replicate/null quantile | planted
  completeness | SESOI | authored | operator ruling); n; when it was fixed relative to the data (before any data / after a
  smoke run / after results); and the margin of the observed statistic to the bar in SE units (binomial or bootstrap from
  committed per-seed values). (2) Flag every verdict with |margin| < 1 SE, or with an authored bar and no attainability check.
  (3) For flagged verdicts that have committed rows, recompute the label at the bar x0.5, x1 and x2 (or +-1 SE) and report the
  flip fraction. For Ananke C1b, also recompute with a relative bar (a drop from the champion's own normal accuracy).
Belief moves: if >= 1/3 of latest verdicts are flagged or flip inside the range, the problem is systemic; recommend blind-fixed,
  attainability-checked bars (PA T2) as a fleet rule, and mark the affected verdicts UNRESOLVED. If < 10% are flagged, close
  H-D3-67 as anecdotal, with the Ananke C1b and Aether rcv_str cases carried as named exceptions.
Cost: about 0.5-1 agent-day, no engine runs (label recomputation over committed JSON only).
