# FR-001 -- Construction landscape, not payoff, decides discovery (five engines)
State: SHARPENED. Cluster: A accessibility and discovery. Sources: H-D3-64 H-D3-60 H-D1-45 H-D1-23 H-D5-40
Relations: umbrella of FR-002..009, FR-132, FR-133; related FR-074 FR-118; prior art PA_accessibility_landscape.md

  as the bar any accessibility ruler must clear)

QUESTION -- Across Crius, Ares, Aether D-15, Ananke and Aphrodite, is whether a mechanism is discovered predicted by the
landscape of its intermediates (their payoff, frequency and robustness under the substrate's variation operators) rather
than by the ENDPOINT payoff of the finished mechanism -- and does any accessibility measure predict discovery better than
current fitness does?

WHY IT MATTERS -- If yes, the program's lever is primitive and operator design (what the substrate makes cheap and
graded), not reward design, and every "evolution did not find X" becomes a map of intermediates. The essay's closing claim
("None of these has been tested anywhere", docs/essays/2026-09-24-accessibility-frontier.md:222@391395aac) still holds.

EXISTING EVIDENCE
Definitional (PA note item 8): Lenski 2003's lever was the payoff of INTERMEDIATES, so "landscape vs payoff" is only
well posed if payoff means the finished mechanism's payoff. Adopted here.
Measured, per engine:
- Crius: complete mechanism +15.974 on 10/10 streams; recorder alone -0.001, invoker alone -0.002, pair +1.138 (7/10),
  planner alone -0.057; 47 edits; 0 of 36 runs assembled it (essay s5 table and :25/:61@391395aac). High endpoint
  payoff, flat intermediates, not found. Consistent.
- Ares: every carrier reaches the W4 cap alone (endpoint tied); recurrence wins on speed; first-carrier payoff and basin
  width are collinear (see FR-002). Consistent, but cannot yet say which intermediate property matters.
- Ananke: XOR and FLIP "0 signal in 165 evolve cells"; superposition sums packets, so XOR needs a |sum| threshold and
  exact co-arrival (roles/Ananke/pte/C1_REPORT.md:119@e35fb9704). PA item 10: NEAT evolves XOR routinely, so this
  reads as substrate-specific encoding, i.e. a landscape property. Consistent, weakly.
- Aphrodite: E1 a valid negative, but only additive families qualify (mul, mod, powr accepted 0/32 in both catalogs)
  (APHRODITE_AMENDMENT16_CAMPAIGN_REPORT_2026-09-26.md:79,96@4f937e88f, branch aphrodite/a16-campaign). A task-supply
  frequency effect (PA U6), not a mechanism test.
- Herakles HC-T01: an accessibility effect "PRESENT AND LARGE", yet current best fitness predicted later acquisition as
  well or better (Spearman -1.000 over gens 100-500) -- kill condition K7; Elenchus diagnosis: breadth statistics are
  target-blind, a detector must be content-addressed
  (herakles/HERAKLES_HISTORICAL_COLLIDER_V0/ACCESSIBILITY_WITHOUT_ACQUISITION_NEGATIVES.jsonl rows 1,11@2a5ac0117).
Proposed, never built: Aether D-15 transplant (Aether/AETHER_DECISIONS.md:93@abe63ef42; still CANDIDATE, deferred on
origin/aether/research-block-2026-09-27); Crius controlled pair and rulers; Ananke v2 dials; Aphrodite non-degenerate
sampler.

UNCERTAINTY -- No engine has varied intermediate landscape while holding endpoint payoff fixed and paired (PA U1). Every
case is post hoc and consistent with the thesis; none could have falsified it. Whether "frequency", "intermediate payoff"
and "robustness" are one axis or three. Whether any ruler beats current fitness (K7).

CHEAPEST DISCRIMINATOR -- A read-only cross-engine table first, then one existing-data test.
(1) 3-4 h, no runs: one row per engine and mechanism with four columns taken only from committed receipts -- endpoint
payoff (measured?), intermediate payoff profile (measured?), arrival rate or prior frequency (measured?), outcome -- plus
the Aphrodite slice-2C positive (48e17a7d1, a discovered accumulation mechanism) and the W16 carriers of FR-118 as
positives. The thesis predicts every found mechanism had a graded or frequent first intermediate and every unfound one a
flat or rare one, whatever the endpoint payoff. One found mechanism with flat intermediates, or one unfound mechanism with
a graded, frequent first step, moves belief against it. A table of empty cells shows where the thesis is untestable.
(2) Ares is the one engine where endpoint payoff is already tied and arrival was manipulated (c3): run FR-002's
read-only step. It is the cheapest place where "intermediate payoff" and "frequency" can be pulled apart on committed
data.
Any ruler proposed from the table must first beat current fitness on the Ares or Crius receipts (K7) before FR-003 uses it.

(PA s6), but the underlying principle is established (arrival of the frequent).
