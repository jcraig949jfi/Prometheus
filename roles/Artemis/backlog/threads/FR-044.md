QUESTION -- Has any primitive, abstraction or compression minted by an engine (not supplied by a human) ever raised the best
score reachable in a fixed task battery at a budget where the unaided system was already saturated? Or do in-ecology minted
things only speed convergence to a ceiling that only human widening of the representation has moved?

WHY IT MATTERS -- The North Star claims mechanisms and representations CO-EVOLVE. Speed gains are compatible with a fixed
representation and a better searcher; only a ceiling that moves from inside would show representational evolution. If the
answer is "only speed", every current positive is search leverage (PA P12), and the program's metric should switch from
efficiency to ceiling movement, as Aporia recommended in August.
