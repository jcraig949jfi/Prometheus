QUESTION -- At a matched proposal budget, is P(useful proposal | failure record) > P(useful proposal) for a blind proposer, in
an engine whose failure corpus is large and per-candidate? Which engine has such a corpus today?

WHY IT MATTERS -- "Failure is evidence and metabolic material" is in the North Star and in charter commitment 2
(CHARTER_AND_CONTINUITY.md:38 @d06e0130e). SFE, Archaeon and Aphrodite assume it; the external review says the opposite may
hold (records may be exhaust). A clean positive licenses investing in failure structure; a clean null says stop enlarging
corpora (Lexis decision 2, below).

UNCERTAINTY
- Whether S1's null is the rule (one bit uses little of the fossil) or the budget (n = 12); S1 itself names both.
- Whether any positive in a Hamming world transfers to engines where a failure is not exact information about the target.
- Whether failure-conditioning helps SELECTION among candidates (cheap to test offline) but not GENERATION of new ones.

CHEAPEST DISCRIMINATOR -- offline, on the z80atlas index (no new runs).
  Inputs: INDEX.jsonl.gz @3b407946e (23,471 runs) and its ADJUDICATION.json.
  Procedure: split runs by seed into train/test halves. Define "useful" in advance (e.g. an admissible adjudicated flag, or
  non-extinction with endogenous births). F-proposer: rank test-half CELLS by a model fitted ONLY on train-half FAILURES
  (which cells died, how); B-proposer: uniform over the same cells; S-proposer: fitted on train-half successes only. Compare
  precision@k at matched k with a permutation null over cell labels.
  Reading: F > B with CI excluding zero and F adds over S -> failures carry selection information beyond successes; take the
  test to a generative engine (Aphrodite: schemas derived from near-miss programs vs S4 shams at matched charges). F ~ B ->
  consistent with H-D5-10 at the configuration level; together with S1-S3, Lexis's corpus freeze stands.
  Limits: this tests selection among cells the campaign already drew, not generation; say so in any readout.
  Cost: one read-only script; minutes of CPU.
  Second, generative step (only if the offline test is positive): Archaeon S1 with the two-fossil disagreement rule at n ~ 60
  pairs, as its own readout proposed; needs Vivarium execution and Archaeon's prereg.
