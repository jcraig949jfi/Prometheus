recoverability HR2, a deferred selectivity intervention) and Ananke PTE-SI01 (recoverability of Relevant / Expired / Never-relevant
distinctions) each certify "history kept and causally used". Applied to the SAME specimen, do they return compatible verdicts, and
which committed specimens could all three be applied to?

WHY IT MATTERS -- The Selective-Irreversibility candidate law is being tested in three lanes with incommensurable rulers. If the
rulers disagree on a shared specimen, a verdict in one lane is a fact about its instrument, not about memory. One agreed ruler
would let the program compare substrates (tensor stores, packet ecologies, planted systems) on one axis.
