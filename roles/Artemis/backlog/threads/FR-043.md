QUESTION -- Handed a compact handle (a schema, primitive, key or name) minted from experience, does a FRESH receiver reach
tasks it could not reach without it, beyond what a random handle of the same shape, the same compute, or a verbatim memo of
the handle's source witnesses would give? This is the North-Star sagacity anchor stated operationally.

WHY IT MATTERS -- The anchor (roles/base-role/NORTH_STAR.md:41-44 @eb733cdfe; CHARTER_AND_CONTINUITY.md:13,25,37 @d06e0130e)
says a compact handle lets a receiver reconstruct a richer lesson, and that compression's downstream utility must be measured
separately from size, with a named consumer. If handles only accelerate what the receiver can already reach, the program is
building search leverage, not inheritance of understanding; the design of every carrier (Ananke packets, Ensorain keys,
Aphrodite libraries) depends on which.

UNCERTAINTY
- S4 is transmission to a same-architecture receiver and a SPEED readout: every unseen-body family was expressible to PRISTINE,
  which was censored at 250,000; whether PRISTINE solves them at 10x escrow is unmeasured.
- Whether (acc + {H}) helps a receiver whose search order differs (different enumerator or proposer).
- Whether the shams are the right random-handle null: one of seven (SHAM_7) was a near-copy and matched DERIVED on 2 families.
- For LLM-substrate carriers the handle is also a NAME; random-name and scrambled-body-same-name controls separate the name's
  prior from the content (PA P10). In the integer DSL there are no names, so only the body controls apply.

CHEAPEST DISCRIMINATOR -- PA T1 cut down to the arms the Aphrodite engine already has.
  Inputs: frozen S3 library (sha 4fed9489d956...), S4 shams (hashed before the donor ran), S4 families, and as a ceiling
  candidate set R1 the A17 fdiv/gcd OBSERVE families no donor solved (branch-only).
  Procedure: partition held-out families BEFORE running into R0 (PRISTINE solves at escrow B) and R1 (PRISTINE fails at 10B).
  Arms, same recipients and keyed seeds: A0 PRISTINE at B; A0' PRISTINE at B + the 6,916,141-charge meta-cost (compute match);
  A1 DERIVED; A2 the 8 shams; A3 a same-shape scrambled schema ((acc - {H}) excluded as a known near-copy, labelled);
  A4 MEMORISE (verbatim witnesses); A5 DERIVED given to a receiver with a different enumeration order.
  Readouts: |R1 solved|; cost on R0; usage census and leave-one-out mask on every A1 win (PA P9).
  Reading: A1 solves R1 families that A0', A2, A3, A4 do not, masking the handle removes the wins, and A5 also gains ->
  first evidence that a minted handle raises reach (sagacity, not leverage). A1 wins only on R0 -> LEVERAGE, never labelled
  sagacity. A5 gains nothing -> the handle is a local search bias of its lineage (PA T1 reinjection extension). R1 empty after
  the 10B pass -> the DSL cannot speak to reach; say so and stop.
  Cost: step R1 (10x PRISTINE on the candidate families) is the dominant term; the rest is S4-sized (the A17 campaign ran in
  48 min on 8 workers). Rough total: hours of CPU, not days (UNVERIFIED estimate).
