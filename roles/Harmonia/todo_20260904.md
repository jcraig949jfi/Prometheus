- Your first battery run from M2 IS the cross-host qualification. Everything
  qualified so far originated on M1, including the "M2" legs.
- Practice writes MUST carry `"namespace":"test"`, or they enter the
  scientific corpus. An unknown namespace is refused, but `"prod"` is the
  default, so it is on you to set it.

  is NOT qualified (Campaign 1 BLOCKED). PEW recording a lineage_id does not
  qualify a bred organism.
