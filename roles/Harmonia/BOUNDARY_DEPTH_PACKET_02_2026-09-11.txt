Three things changed since packet 01, and two of them are corrections to
packet 01 rather than new discoveries.

  (1) PACKET 01's HEADLINE NUMBER WAS WRONG, BY MY OWN ERROR.

1. KNOWN-TRUTH CONTROLS  (the keystone -- everything else rests on this)
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  Built from the ISA, read out of the interpreter rather than the docs.
  An instruction is four words [op, a, bw, cw], op = word % 25;
  op 21 IN, 23 OUT, 3 LOADI, 4 MOV, 0 NOP, 1 HALT. Registers start at 0.

  COUPLING CONTROLS                              self-test 5/5
    WC_POS         IN r0 ; OUT r0 ; HALT          coupled     -> recovered
    WC_NEG         LOADI r0=42 ; OUT r0 ; HALT    blind       -> recovered
    WC_STATE_ONLY  IN r0 ; HALT                   coupled     -> recovered,
                   and ONLY through the image, confirming with known truth
                   what packet 01 inferred from the menagerie
    EMIT_ONLY      LOADI r0=7 ; OUT r0 ; HALT     blind       -> recovered
    INERT          NOP ; HALT                     blind       -> recovered

  PARTICIPATION CONTROLS -- the pairs that kill the old criteria
    DEP_DATA    A: IN r0        B: OUT r0    TRUE dependence
    DEP_CONST   A: LOADI r0=42  B: OUT r0    TRUE dependence, world-blind
    INDEP_REG   A: LOADI r5     B: OUT r0    NO dependence
    INDEP_LONG  A: 4x LOADI r5  B: OUT r0    NO dependence, A longer
    POSITION    A: NOP NOP      B: OUT r0    NO dependence, pure padding
    RESOURCE    A: 8x NOP       B: OUT loop  budget competition

  SCORES (A's contribution; the A-segments of DEP_DATA/DEP_CONST/INDEP_REG
  are all ONE instruction, so layout, offsets and op count are identical
  across them)

    criterion              DEP_DATA  INDEP_REG   verdict
    ---------------------  --------  ----------  --------------------------
    activation_evidence    ACTIVATED ACTIVATED   FAILS -- cannot separate
    P5 moves_execution     True      True        FAILS -- cannot separate
    order-sensitivity      True      True        FAILS -- cannot separate
    moves OUTPUT           True      False       SEPARATES
    cross-contribution     True      False       SEPARATES
      at constant cost

  ops(A+B) == ops(A+B\A) == 3 for both DEP_DATA and INDEP_REG, so the
  separation is not a cost artifact.

  THE ANSWER TO "WHAT WOULD CONVINCE US A CAUSALLY CHANGES B":
    Ablating A changes what B produces, while A's bytes are replaced
    in place by NOPs so that length, offsets and instruction count are
    unchanged. Proteus's ablate() rewrites ONLY the opcode word, and a
    NOP costs exactly one op, so NOP-ablation is RESOURCE-NEUTRAL BY
    CONSTRUCTION. Measured contrast on the identical pair:
        NOP-ablation   ops 16, out_writes 4, genome 40 words
        deletion       ops 16, out_writes 8, genome  8 words
    Deletion changes what B can do; neutralisation does not. The
    criterion is valid ONLY under NOP-ablation, and that is now written
    into the instrument.

  Scored 6/6 on the known cases. This is the minimum bar -- one known
  positive and one known negative -- not a proof of generality.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE P2 COLLAPSE: MECHANISM, NOT OBSERVATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  Re-measured with one consistent definition: of 240 ordered pairs of
  world-coupled parents, 146 (60.8%) of the COMPOSITIONS are still
  world-coupled. Packet 01's 15/240 was an artifact of dropping the
  image surface on composed objects only.

  MECHANISM ATTRIBUTION for the 94 that do lose coupling, by measurement
  rather than by reading genomes:

    M1 STARVATION          30  (31.9%)  second component never executes
    M5 RAN BUT UNCOUPLED   18  (19.1%)  both ran; coupling still absent
    M2 FIRST-HALT          18  (19.1%)  first component halts
    M3 CURSOR/PATH LOSS    14  (14.9%)  composition reads LESS input than
                                        the first component does alone
    M4 BUDGET TRUNCATION   14  (14.9%)  budget exhausted in component one

  No single mechanism dominates. "Composition destroys coupling" was
  never one thing.

  M3 is specific to this ISA and would be invisible to anyone reasoning
  about composition abstractly: input read CURSORS ARE TICK-SCOPED and
  the IN opcode advances them, so an earlier component CONSUMES the
  world and a later one reads different values or zeros. 14 pairs read
  strictly less input composed than solo. Zero pairs went all the way to
  zero reads, so cursor exhaustion attenuates coupling rather than
  eliminating it.

3. GATE NECESSITY TABLE
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  For each gate: which claim needs it, and can the phenomenon exist
  without it (counterexample by construction, not by argument)?

  G_activated  both components ran
    necessary for  any claim about the PAIR rather than about one part
    fails-but-real no -- if a component never runs it cannot contribute
    passes-but-absent YES -- INDEP_REG passes and has no dependence
    CLASS: logically necessary, grossly insufficient

  G_coupled  the composition responds to the world
    necessary for  "interacts WITH THE WORLD" only
    fails-but-real YES -- DEP_CONST has a genuine cross-component
                   dependence and is world-blind BY CONSTRUCTION
    CLASS: NOT necessary for a dependence claim. Packet 01 used it as a
           core gate and thereby excluded an entire valid class.

  G_emits  the composition writes an output
    necessary for  nothing
    fails-but-real YES -- SILENT_DEP_TRUE (A: LOADI r3=5; B: MOV r4<-r3)
                   has a real dependence and emits NOTHING; recovered
                   correctly by the restricted-register surface (sec. 4)
    CLASS: INSTRUMENTATION CONVENIENCE. Retired as a gate; replacement
           built and tested.

  G_unsaturated  execution size is free to vary
    necessary for  nothing; it guards a confound
    CLASS: empirically useful. Keep as a reporting condition, not a gate.

  G_causal (cross-contribution at constant cost)
    CLASS: the candidate necessary condition. 6/6 on known truth.

  GATE CORRELATION (Jaccard, over 42 composed pairs of coupled parents)
    G_activated and G_moves_execution are IDENTICAL sets (J = 1.00).
    P5 added no information over activation at any point -- it was a
    redundant gate wearing a different name. G_unsaturated and G_coupled
    are near-collinear (J = 0.86). G_causal is nearly orthogonal to
    everything (J <= 0.11 against every other gate).

  So of packet 01's five conjunctive gates, two were the same gate, two
  more were 86% the same, and the only near-independent one was the one
  I have since had to replace.

4. STRUCTURAL CONTAMINATION MAP  (reusable, now enforced in code)
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  The runtime copies the genome onto the front of the tape. Therefore:

  observable        contaminated by             safe for
  ----------------  --------------------------  ----------------------
  tape image        genome bytes, length        SAME object across
                                                different worlds
  final state       tape (above) + regs         same-object comparison
  meter ops_by_cat  ablation itself (opcode ->  nothing involving
                    NOP moves category counts)  ablation
  execution hash    inherits the above          same-object comparison
  whole-register    the ablated component's     nothing involving
    digest          OWN writes                  ablation
  OUTPUT            nothing measured            cross-object comparison
  registers the     nothing measured            cross-object comparison
    OTHER component                             INCLUDING non-emitting
    reads                                       compositions

  MEASURED, not asserted. Ablating A in the six known cases:
    d_output      fires 3/3 positives, 0/3 negatives   SEPARATES
    d_regs_all    fires 3/3 positives, 2/3 negatives   CONTAMINATED
    d_tape        fires 3/3 positives, 2/3 negatives   CONTAMINATED
    d_execution   fires 3/3 positives, 2/3 negatives   CONTAMINATED
    d_regs_restricted-to-what-B-reads
                  6/6 correct, including 2 positives that emit NOTHING

  The restricted-register surface is the packet's main instrumentation
  gain: it lifts the emission requirement entirely, and it is derived
  from a static over-approximation of which registers the other
  component consumes.

5. THE CORRECTED FUNNEL, AND WHY IT IS WORSE THAN PACKET 01 SAID
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  Under one consistent coupling definition with the 3-world ladder used
  by the instrument, 7 of 64 specimens are solo-coupled -- not 16. The
  16 in packet 01 included a fourth "empty world" input set. Coupling is
  therefore NOT a property of an organism; it is a property of the
  (organism, world-set) pair, and the count moves by more than 2x on the
  choice of world ladder. That is the single most important
  conditionality in either packet.

  Funnels over the 42 composed ordered pairs of coupled parents:

    as packet 01 ran it            42 -> 15 -> 0 ...
      (G_coupled_execution_only passes 0/42 -- the buggy gate was not
       merely strict, on this population it was empty)
    corrected, packet 01's shape   42 -> 15 -> 13 -> 10 -> 0
    for the dependence claim       42 -> 15 -> 1
      (G_activated then G_causal_any)

  G_causal_any: 1 of 42.  G_causal_both: 1 of 42.
  Near-misses: 2 pairs fail ONLY G_causal_both; 40 fail two or more.

  SO: packet 01 reported seven usable pairs. Under a criterion that can
  tell a known interaction from a known non-interaction, the number is
  ONE, and it is not established that even that one is an interaction --
  only that it satisfies a necessary condition.

  Packet 01's seven were selected by a conjunction in which two gates
  were identical, two more were 86% collinear, and the discriminating
  gate was wrong. The seven are WITHDRAWN as a pre-registered population.

    magnitude.

  EXTRAPOLATION AT THE MEASURED n=50 RATE (32.7/min), and the reasons
  not to trust it:
    1e3 encounters      ~31 minutes
    1e5 encounters      ~51 hours
    1e6 encounters      ~21 days
  ASSUMPTIONS ATTACKED: the rate is already degrading with N over a
  range of 10x, so extrapolating it 20,000x further is unsound in the
  direction of optimism; worlds accumulate and are never collected
  (Daedalus's I-WORLD-GC is deferred and the growth rate is still
  unmeasured); and every encounter adds one outbound SFE verify-anchor
  call whose behaviour under concurrency is untested because BD-1 killed
  the concurrency probe.

  NOT MEASURED, and now explicitly backlog: DB/ledger/disk growth per
  encounter, orphan accumulation, service restart mid-run, client
  restart mid-run, PEW-unavailable, network interruption, ordering under
  concurrent writers, resume-without-changing-the-run.

11. RECOMMENDATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  READY ONLY FOR MECHANISTIC CASE STUDIES
  + LONG-RUN INFRASTRUCTURE NOT YET SAFE
  + COMPOSITION SEMANTICS NEED REDESIGN

  Nothing should be recorded to PEW until BD-1 is cleared; the evidence
  store currently cannot accept an encounter.

  The scientific blocker is BD-3. Under a criterion that demonstrably
  separates a known interaction from a known non-interaction, ONE pair
  out of 42 shows any causal contribution at all. concat.v0 places
  components in sequence in one address space with one budget and one
  instruction pointer, and what that mostly produces is the first
  component running and the second not mattering. Before a campaign is
  designed, someone should answer whether that is the composition
  semantics the scientific claim actually needs. That is a Proteus
  question and I have not pre-judged it.

  What I would NOT do is enlarge the funnel until seven pairs come back.
  Packet 01's seven were an artifact of a broken gate, and the honest
  count under a validated criterion is one.

  "This menagerie plus this glue cannot support the claim" remains a
  first-class answer and is now better supported than it was in packet 01.
