This is not a scientific conclusion. It is a statement about the
instrument.

TRANSCRIPT: OBVIOUSLY DEGENERATE, and on this exercise worse than
degenerate -- CONSTANT. One class among five conditions, including a full
exact ablation of a demonstrably ACTIVATED component. Transcript-only
science is refused for this configuration. Note this is stronger than
Proteus's finding (3 classes / 87.5% largest among 56 players) because
these five differ by construction and it still cannot see them.

METER: USABLE, WITH A NAMED LIMIT.
  The triviality attacks the directive asks for:
    genome instruction count   does NOT explain the split
    component count            does NOT explain the split
    total executed ops         does NOT explain the split
    ticks / budget exposure    does NOT explain the split
    output opportunity         does NOT explain the split
    state footprint            does NOT explain the split

  The dissociation is real in BOTH directions, which is what makes it
  worth anything: A (4 instr, 1 component) has a meter IDENTICAL to
  A+B\B (8 instr, 2 components), while A and B (both 4 instr, 1
  component) differ. Length and component count are decoupled from the
  meter both ways.

  THE LIMIT, stated because it would be easy to overclaim past it: four
  of those six covariates could not have explained anything, because the
  shared envelope and a fully saturated budget pinned them CONSTANT
  across all five conditions. ops was 2048 everywhere -- tick_budget 256
  x 8 ticks -- i.e. every condition burned its entire budget. This
  exercise never entered a regime where execution size VARIES. The honest
  reading is: "no confound was detectable here", not "the meter is
  confound-free". The real experiment must include conditions that halt
  early and conditions that emit, or the ops covariate will be untested
  rather than cleared.

  UNRESOLVED, and a design question rather than a bug:
  proxy_search_expenditure equalled branches_taken exactly in all five
  conditions. It is a derived alias, not a second independent signal, and
  should not be counted twice.
