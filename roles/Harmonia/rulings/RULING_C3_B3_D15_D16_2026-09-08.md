# C3 -- PAIRED / CLUSTERED ANALYSIS AND THE INDEPENDENT UNIT
==========================================================================

RULING. C3 does not have ONE independent unit. It carries THREE claims over
three different populations, and each needs its own declared unit. A single
corpus-wide unit is wrong whichever value it takes, and X1's
`unit_of_analysis` is a per-ANALYSIS field for exactly this reason. C3
therefore needs three registered analyses, not one.

The corpus: four distinct IC samples of 100, the SAME four shared across every
rule. So each rule is measured on 400 ICs, and all rules see the same 400.
The four "repeats" vary the IC sample, not a seed -- which is correct, and
worth stating, because for a deterministic CA four repeats on the SAME ICs
would be bit-identical and the control could never fire.

## C3-U1 -- the accuracy of ONE rule. UNIT = THE IC, n = 400.

For a qualification number (reproducing a published accuracy, or reporting a
genome's accuracy under its own source protocol) the ICs are i.i.d. draws from
the declared ensemble. The sample boundary is an artifact of batching and
carries no nuisance.

    unit = IC, n = 400      binomial SE 0.0250   95% CI +/- 0.049
    unit = IC SAMPLE, n = 4 t(3) on 4 means      95% CI +/- 0.0796

I RULE AGAINST the sample as the unit here, and this amends the order's
formulation. Two reasons, both decisive. The CI is 1.6x wider for no gain,
which would push borderline qualification comparisons to INDETERMINATE on a
choice of bookkeeping. And at four samples a paired permutation test has
2^4 = 16 sign patterns, so its minimum attainable two-sided p is 0.125 --
INELIGIBLE at 0.05 whatever the data (HA-1.6). C1-e's own precedent is the IC:
it used 16,000 ICs and 10k samples as its unit.

## C3-U2 -- rule A versus rule B. UNIT = THE IC, PAIRED, n = 400.

Because the ICs are shared, a rule-vs-rule contrast is PAIRED and must be
analysed that way -- McNemar over the discordant pairs, not two independent
proportions. Measured, 400 ICs, shared IC difficulty:

    null, no true difference    paired 0.047   unpaired 0.039
    true difference 0.05        paired 0.315   unpaired 0.298

Both are calibrated under the null; the paired gain at this effect size is
real but MODEST, about two points. I am reporting that honestly rather than
claiming pairing rescues the design: the reason to analyse paired is that it
matches how the corpus was actually run, not that it buys power here.

WHAT THIS SIZES. At 400 ICs a 0.05 accuracy difference is detected about 30% of
the time. The comparisons C3 is built for are much larger than that -- the
historical genomes near 0.75 against random rules near 0.5 -- and those are
effectively certain. But a comparison BETWEEN two historical genomes differing
by a couple of points is not supported at 400 ICs, and must not be reported as
a null. State the detectable difference beside any negative result.

## C3-U3 -- a claim about the POPULATION OF RULES. UNIT = THE RULE.

H1 ("most random rules are far below 0.5"), H2 (region structure predicts
accuracy), and every D3 statement are claims about a population of rules. The
unit is the RULE: n = 120 for C3-acq, n = 6 for C3-hist. The 400 ICs inside a
rule sharpen that rule's estimate and add NO independent units to this claim.
D3 accordingly sees one row per rule, which at 120 rules over 10 regions gives
about 12 per region -- the F-3 discrimination optimum, as adopted.

## MULTIPLICITY, and it is not optional at 120 rules

Any per-rule testing across C3-acq is 120 tests. H1 as written is a statement
about the DISTRIBUTION and needs no correction. But "these three random rules
beat 0.5 significantly" is selection, and selection at small promotion
probability exaggerates: measured in S6, P(promote) below 0.2 gives a Type M
ratio of 3.66. Report the distribution, or declare the correction in advance.
Extremes drawn from 120 draws are not findings about those rules.

# B3 -- PROTOCOL CHOICE
==========================================================================

## B3-FROZEN. May proceed on M-SIGNAL's requirements, WITH A RESIZE.

The frozen route is the right first route and its requirements are already
written: frozen corpus and frozen universe both content-hashed before the first
outcome, both orders committed in advance, a fixed budget declared absolutely
and as a fraction of the universe, spend-the-whole-budget stopping with no
interim look, and declared void conditions.

BLOCKING AMENDMENT. B3 as specified is 3 seeds per arm. Its minimum attainable
two-sided p is 0.10 unpaired -- and 0.25 if the arms are paired by seed, which
is the natural design here and is WORSE. The gate cannot fire at 0.05 either
way. Six seeds per arm gives 0.0022 unpaired and 0.031 paired. Resize to six,
or declare B3 descriptive and run no test. This is the third design in flight
with this defect, which is why HA-1.6 is now a standing rule rather than a
third amendment.

## B3-ADAPTIVE. Needs its own protocol; the operator's list is accepted, plus four.

Accepted as listed: precommitted policy, allowed evidence, seeds, budgets,
tie-breaks, stopping and censoring, success-within-budget as the primary
endpoint, and every assigned problem in the denominator. That last one is the
important one -- it is what stops a policy looking good by quietly not
attempting the hard problems.

ADDED, and each has cost us something before:

  (i)   the random control must be drawn over the universe the adaptive policy
        ACTUALLY explored. An adaptive policy widens its own universe as it
        runs; a directed order on a wide universe against a control drawn on a
        narrow one is a confound, not a comparison. Re-draw and version it.
  (ii)  censoring is reported AS censoring. A run that exhausts its budget is
        neither a success nor a failure; success-within-budget already handles
        this, and the censored count is reported beside the endpoint.
  (iii) tie-breaks must be deterministic and declared in advance. An adaptive
        policy's tie-break is a free parameter and is tunable after the fact.
  (iv)  no interim look that could change the order, and the policy's own
        state must be reconstructible from the record at every decision point,
        or the "precommitted policy" is unverifiable.

# D-16 -- SCOPE OF THE OPCODE-BIJECTION NULL
==========================================================================

RULING. Option (a) ACCEPTED: a data-channel disjointness precondition detected
from the EXECUTION trace, Class I guaranteed, Class II reported and counted out.
Option (c) REFUSED. Option (b) refused as insufficient.

The reasoning is right and I want it recorded: the exposure is DYNAMIC -- a
load from a genome address on the probes actually run -- so a static exclusion
under-counts real exposure and a whole-program assertion over-claims. Only
opcode slots are relabelled, and offsetting an operand is an edit rather than a
relabelling, which the B1-d bug already demonstrated.

THREE CONDITIONS.

1. THE EXCLUSION COUNT IS PART OF THE NULL'S DENOMINATOR AND IS REPORTED WITH
   IT, ALWAYS. Class I count, Class II count, and the fraction. A null whose
   denominator is silently reduced by dropping the hard cases reports a rate on
   the easy subset. Declare in advance the Class II share above which the null
   is not informative about the family.

2. CLASS II IS UNINFORMATIVE IN BOTH DIRECTIONS AND IS COUNTED, NEVER SCORED.
   A Class II run that happens to be identical is not evidence the invariant
   holds there; a Class II run that differs is not a defect. Neither may be
   aggregated into any rate.

3. CLASS I'S GUARANTEE IS SCOPED TO THE PROBES RUN. Proteus's own caveat is
   load-bearing: the check detects a data dependence only when it reaches the
   observable on the probes executed, so `genome_read` is a LOWER BOUND on
   exposure. Class I means "no genome read observed on these probes", not "no
   genome read possible". Written the second way it over-claims exactly as (c)
   does. This is the standing asymmetry of an invariance null -- failure proves
   the defect, success proves only equivariance over what was tested.

The negative fixture -- a program that loads its own genome and therefore
changes under relabelling -- is the right shape and is consistent with packet
v2's refusal of symmetric fixtures.

# D-15 -- APPEND-ONLY WITNESS-AVAILABILITY EVENTS. ACCEPT (a), FOUR AMENDMENTS.
==========================================================================

(a) is the right choice and (b) is correctly rejected by PEW's never-overwrite
rule. Keeping availability outside the content address so one witness identity
survives an availability change is right. Four holes.

## A-1. "CURRENT STATE = LATEST EVENT" RE-MERGES THE TWO STATES THE ENUM
##      EXISTS TO SEPARATE. This is the real one.

s1.3's whole purpose is that "there is none" (ABSENT) and "we could not look"
(UNAVAILABLE) must stay distinguishable, because NULL cannot separate them. At
a point in time the enum does that. ACROSS TIME, "current state = the latest
event" destroys it: a transient storage outage writes UNAVAILABLE over a
verified PRESENT, and a consumer reading current state sees a witness that is,
as far as it can tell, unobtainable -- with no way to know it was verified
present with a matching digest yesterday. That is absence of ACCESS read as
absence of the THING, which is the prohibition already written into
`epistemic_bounds.json`.

FIX: derive TWO fields, because one field cannot serve both -- one is monotone
and the other is not.

    reachability_now          the latest event. NOT monotone. what a replay
                              consumer needs.
    verification_high_water   the strongest state ever verified against a
                              MATCHING digest. MONOTONE, never decreases.
                              what a scientific consumer asking "did this
                              witness ever exist" needs.

## A-2. A PRESENT EVENT WITH NO OBSERVED DIGEST IS A CLAIM, NOT A VERIFICATION.

Every availability event must carry the digest ACTUALLY OBSERVED at that
moment (NULL when unreachable) and what did the observing. Without it the log
records assertions about content it never checked, and A-1's high-water mark is
not computable -- "verified" requires that a digest was observed AND matched.
This is the same shape as the executor attestation finding: echoing the
expected value back proves nothing, so record what was actually seen.

## A-3. TRUNCATED IS A PROPERTY OF CONTENT, NOT OF AVAILABILITY, AND THE
##      DESIGN USES ONE NAME FOR TWO THINGS.

`witness_refs` is content-addressed over a tuple INCLUDING the digest. So
TRUNCATED at write time is a CONTENT fact: the identity already covers the
truncated bytes, and it can never change. But TRUNCATED appearing later in an
availability timeline is an ACCESS fact about a DIFFERENT byte sequence than
the one the identity names. Those must not share a state name in a timeline.
Split them, or require every TRUNCATED event to carry the observed digest and
the declared bound so a reader can tell which it is. The design's own rule --
a truncated witness is not a witness for replay -- must survive into the log
form, and today it lives only in the column.

## A-4. "LATEST" NEEDS A TOTAL ORDER THAT IS NOT A WALL CLOCK.

Two writers, a retry, or clock skew make "the latest event" ambiguous, and then
the current state is not a function of the log. Every other append-only
structure in PEW orders by a monotonic sequence; this one must too, with a
deterministic tie-break. Additionally, any transition AWAY from PRESENT should
carry a reason code and its probe evidence, or a witness can be demoted by an
unfalsifiable event.

VERDICT: (a) with A-1 through A-4. A-1 and A-2 are the ones I would not ship
without; A-3 and A-4 are correctness of the record. The operator decides.

# WHAT THIS LICENSES / DOES NOT / OPEN
==========================================================================

LICENSES. C3 may be issued and analysed under three registered analyses with
their three declared units. B3-FROZEN may proceed at six seeds per arm. WP-B2's
null may be built on option (a) with its three conditions. D-15 may be built on
(a) if the operator accepts A-1 through A-4.

DOES NOT LICENSE. Any C3 analysis at the four-IC-sample unit -- it is both
wider and ineligible. Any single corpus-wide unit for C3. B3 at three seeds per
arm. Any Class II result aggregated into a rate. Any Class I guarantee stated
as a property of the program rather than of the probes run. Any D-15 consumer
reading current availability as evidence about existence.
