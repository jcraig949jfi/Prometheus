HEADLINE
  The stack is sound; the POPULATION is not. Of 4,032 ordered pairs in the
  frozen menagerie, SEVEN survive the preconditions a first interaction
  experiment requires. The binding constraint is not composition and not
  the instrument -- it is that 75% of the specimens cannot see the world at
  all, and that composition destroys world-coupling in 94% of the cases
  where both parts had it.

  A long campaign over this menagerie would spend hours measuring organisms
  for which the world is decorative. That is the failure this pass exists
  to find, and it would not have been visible from any single run.

SCIENTIFIC VALIDITY BLOCKERS
 SV-1  75% of the menagerie is WORLD-BLIND. A campaign that samples the
       registry uniformly spends 3 runs in 4 on organisms for which the
       world cannot matter. SILENT: nothing errors; the runs look fine.
       RANK 1 (highest likelihood x highest late cost).
 SV-2  Composition destroys world-coupling (only 6.2% survive). Any
       "A+B is not coupled" result would be a property of the glue, not
       of the pair. RANK 2.
 SV-3  Usable population is SEVEN ordered pairs. This cannot be a
       campaign; it is at most 7 case studies. Any power calculation
       written against "64 specimens" is wrong by three orders of
       magnitude. RANK 3.
 SV-4  Distinctness-by-construction. Both L1 (64/64 meters) and L3 (7/7
       profiles) produced perfect separation for reasons unrelated to
       behaviour. Any future "the surface discriminates" claim needs a
       shuffled/structure-only null before it is quoted. RANK 4.

MEASUREMENT / INSTRUMENTATION GAPS
 MI-1  The arena records transcript+meter and NOT final state. State is
       the most sensitive surface (14/16 vs 11/16 vs 2/16) and 4 of 16
       coupled specimens are invisible without it. Small, mine, cheap.
 MI-2  EXECUTION and IMAGE observables must be reported separately.
       Merging them makes every structurally-distinct object look
       behaviourally distinct (this is what produced L3's false headline).
 MI-3  proxy_search_expenditure aliases branches_taken exactly (carried
       from the closure packet, re-confirmed). Not a second signal.
