0. WHAT THIS PACKET IS

Two instruments were preregistered, built and run in one session. (1) The
ASAL fast lane: an instrument that asks whether a search restricted to
legitimate Lenia parameters can push ASAL's "open-endedness" score below
the score that meaningless garbage images achieve through the same CLIP
observer. Nyx froze a prediction packet on that question; this seat ran
the search sealed, then unsealed it against the freeze and ruled. (2) The
ancestry ground-truth ruler: a calibration instrument for "how much
evolutionary information is lost when history is compressed", built and
controlled on synthetic ground truth before any real fossil is measured.
CONFLICT OF INTEREST: the author wrote both instruments, their plans,
their amendments, the class thresholds, and the rulings. The author also
made the coverage defect in section 7 and found it only after reading
the results.

1. CONTEXT

ASAL (Sakana AI, 2024) scores a simulation's "open-endedness" as: embed
8 frames with CLIP, and for each frame take its maximum similarity to any
earlier frame; average; lower is "more open-ended". Techne had shown on
M3 that eight unrelated ellipse scenes ("GARBAGE") score 0.8167, below a
living Lenia glider ("Orbium", 0.8472); the operator asked whether
LEGITIMATE Lenia, searched within its own parameter space, could cross
below garbage, and whether the crosser would be a metric exploit or real
dynamics. Lenia is a continuous cellular automaton; a "lifeform" is a
parameter set plus an initial pattern from a public catalogue of 548.
The three rules adopted this session (plan amendment with a hash on any
criterion change; no verdict on a stochastic comparison without a power
statement; extension seeds disjoint) are now in roles/Harmonia/
STANDING_RULES.md, merged as a union with a sibling instance's
executable version.

2. THE QUESTIONS

(a) Packet MECH-ASAL-LEGIT-SEARCH-001, four exact rows: I0 the raw
    catalogue's best ALIVE score stays in [0.8167, 0.875] (no crossing
    without search); I1 the search's best ALIVE score falls below 0.8167;
    I2 below 0.7999 (garbage mean minus two sd); I3 the best crosser is a
    metric or observer exploit, not genuine dynamical novelty.
(b) Instrument-level: does the ancestry ruler pass its own cheat, positive,
    negative and order controls, and what do six degradations of a known
    history cost, measured?

6. WHAT THIS DID NOT ESTABLISH

- Anything about ASAL's own JAX Lenia or Flax CLIP: the instrument is a
  descendant port; the fossil's lines were never executed.
- The full preregistered domain: 650 of 1,045 draws were refused by the
  port (s7). Directions of I0-I2 are robust to that (a subset minimum
  bounds the full minimum from above); I3's object is the best crosser
  of the executed budget only.
- Whether the class thresholds, frozen from a single Orbium rollout,
  generalise; 47 crossers were UNCLASSIFIED.
- Any Avida result: the ancestry ruler has measured synthetic truth
  only; the .spop from Techne does not yet exist.
- No POET ruler exists yet; the bodies landed on M3 during this session.

7. AUTHOR'S OWN ERRORS AND WHAT THEY COST

- The ASAL search domain was written from the catalogue and never
  validated against the port that would execute it: fractional kernel
  rings ("1/2,1") and kernel/growth cores 3-4 raise in the port. Cost: a
  1,045-rollout budget executed 395; the catalogue scan covered 154 of
  545 lifeforms; a full-domain run needs an extended port and a new
  preregistration. A domain-acceptance check now belongs in the fixture.
- Two controls were built with a fresh random generator per frame,
  making every frame identical; a float32 tolerance of 1e-9 could never
  pass. Cost: one aborted fixture run and Amendment A.
- The ancestry ruler's first tie-break read organism ids, which its own
  order control forbids. Cost: Amendment B and a re-run.
- Two ancestry predictions named a measure (parent-edge recall) that is
  undefined for the degradations they were about, and a third confused
  extinct ancestors with extinct branches. Cost: a crash, Amendment C,
  and the honest label VACUOUS on two of four preregistered predictions.
- The first ancestry control run used an unwindowed pure-Python
  Levenshtein and would have taken hours; killed after 10 minutes.
- 18 MB of trajectories and frames were committed to the repository;
  a reviewer may prefer an artifact store.

9. QUESTIONS FOR THE EXTERNAL REVIEWER

 1. The ASAL verdicts were issued on 395 of 1,045 budgeted rollouts with
    the argument that a subset minimum bounds the full minimum from
    above. Is that sufficient for CUT_SUPPORTED on I1/I2, or should the
    packet have been returned INDETERMINATE pending a full-domain run?
 2. What would falsify RETURN 1? We name: the same 395 rollouts through
    ASAL's own Flax CLIP giving a minimum ALIVE score above 0.8167. Is a
    seven-arm fixture agreement at 1e-6 enough to waive that test?
 3. I0 failed by 0.009, which is 1.1 sd of the garbage arm's 5-seed mean.
    The packet chose the mean as an exact threshold. Should a threshold
    derived from a 5-seed mean ever be treated as exact?
 4. The sealed-then-frozen ordering (results fixed before the prediction,
    prediction fixed before the results were read) is stronger than
    prereg-then-run. Should it become the standard when the instrument
    exists before the packet?
 5. What should we stop? Candidates: freezing a domain the port has not
    accepted; preregistering a measure without checking it is defined on
    every arm; committing multi-megabyte trajectories to git.
 6. The class rule split the crossers into turbulence (49), unclassified
    (47) and genuine (9). Is a rule frozen from one Orbium rollout a
    defensible instrument, or does the 47 say the thresholds need their
    own calibration set?
