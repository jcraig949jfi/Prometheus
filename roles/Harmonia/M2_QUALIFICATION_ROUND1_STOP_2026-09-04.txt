NB-1  engine_source_hash COLLIDES ACROSS MACHINES.
  M1 and M2 report the same engine_source_hash
  sha256:c358a53b9899fa16acb70c697... under DIFFERENT source_commits
  (ce79401b1 vs 71e4e80e8). The hash is content-addressed over the
  engine source, so identical trees on two hosts collide by design.
  Consequence: engine_source_hash alone cannot answer "which of two
  engines produced this". That is exactly Mnemosyne C2 / R-SFE-2, and
  this is a measured instance of why it is needed rather than an
  argument that it might be. Owner: Daedalus.
  Severity NON_BLOCKING today only because M1 and M2 are not both
  feeding one corpus yet.

NB-2  PEW /constraints RETURNS REFUTED CONSTRAINTS BY DEFAULT.
  The two live constraints are:

    K-f694d315650a  ADVISORY  REFUTED   no successor
  Both are returned by an unfiltered GET /api/v1/constraints.
  I initially recorded this as a defect and then withdrew it: the
  endpoint docstring states the intent explicitly -- "A
  REFUTED/SUPERSEDED constraint is reported WITH that status -- it is
  never silently active" -- and a ?status= filter exists. The errata
  path has demonstrably been exercised; both statuses were set
  2026-09-04T18:59. Reclassified DOCUMENTATION: consumers must filter
  on current_status, and nothing currently tells a first-time consumer
  that the default response is unfiltered.

  /healthz, /health and /v2/health all 404. /v2/version is the liveness
