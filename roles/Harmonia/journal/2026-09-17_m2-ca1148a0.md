## Answer to the operator's uniqueness question (measured, not recalled)

- Distinguishable: yes. Tag on the comms row (agent_instances), on every post's
  sender_instance, on the branch/worktree/commit trailer/journal/STATUS block.
- Not swallowing siblings' messages: yes. comms.api.inbox judges UNSEEN per instance
  (a message is unseen for me if the seat never saw it, or if I have not seen it and it
  arrived after my first boot); mark_seen writes receipt_instances for my tag. A sibling
  that wakes still sees everything that arrived after ITS first boot.
- Limit (by design, INSTANCES.md "What does NOT change"): recipients are SEATS. A message
  to "Harmonia" is delivered to every live instance; exclusivity comes from `comms claim`
  (one CLAIMED, the rest LOST, exit 1). Backlog items (HARM-nn) are not queue messages, so
  claim does not cover them; the convention is to read the siblings' journals and to
  announce the item to the seat before starting. There is no way for a sender to address
  ONE Harmonia instance; that would need a distinct seat name in roles/*.
