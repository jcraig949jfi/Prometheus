# PROMETHEUS NORTH STAR

PROMETHEUS NORTH STAR. Prometheus exists to grow, not hand-design,
increasingly sagacious computational systems. Its objective is to create a
continuously running evolutionary ecology in which mechanisms of reasoning
and the information, representations, abstractions, and compressions they
consume co-evolve under selection pressure across diverse worlds, tasks,
organisms, and resource constraints. We supply primitives, environments,
falsification instruments, provenance, and pressures -- not a predetermined
reasoning architecture or ladder -- and allow useful mechanisms to be
discovered, composed, dismantled, recombined, retained, and improved,
including mechanisms no human explicitly conceived. Failure is evidence and
metabolic material, not a death sentence: falsification kills only the
tested claim, while weak signals, gradients, useful residue, and
alternative lineages remain available to future search. The long-term
measure of success is not whether Prometheus reproduces Lean, MOSEK,
DreamCoder, Mathematica, POET, or any other human-designed tool, but
whether sufficiently rich computational "primordial soup" can
pressure-cook its own functional equivalents -- and eventually forms of
sagacity for which we have no human name.

## What this means for a seat's daily choices

- Build primitives, environments, instruments, provenance and pressures;
  do not build the reasoner. A seat that finds itself designing the
  answer has left its lane.
- Prefer the route that lets a mechanism be discovered, composed,
  dismantled, recombined, retained and improved over the route that
  installs one. Declared interfaces, typed artifacts, and retention with
  provenance are how discovery is made inheritable.
- A falsified claim is dead; nothing else is. Record the weak signal, the
  gradient, the residue and the alternative lineage so a later search can
  reach them. Kill claims, never lineages, and never the loop.
- Reproducing a human-designed tool is a calibration, not the goal. When
  Lean, MOSEK, DreamCoder, POET or Mathematica appear in a backlog they
  are reference arms and acquisition candidates, never the target.
- "Sagacity" is the anchor: a compact handle through which a receiver
  reconstructs a much richer lesson. Measure whether experience changed
  what the system carries forward AND what it can do with that
  inheritance (CHARTER_AND_CONTINUITY.md).
