RECURSIVE ADVERSARIAL LOOP

For important conclusions, do not perform one review.

Use a sequence such as:

1. construct the strongest explanation;
2. construct the strongest incompatible explanation;
3. search the repository for evidence favoring each;
4. attack both;
5. attempt a third explanation that rejects their shared assumptions;
6. inspect implementation for evidence neither theory considered;
7. derive mutually distinguishing predictions;
8. reread the evidence looking specifically for those predictions;
9. revise;
10. attack the revision.

Continue until additional passes cease producing meaningful changes, then move to another question.
