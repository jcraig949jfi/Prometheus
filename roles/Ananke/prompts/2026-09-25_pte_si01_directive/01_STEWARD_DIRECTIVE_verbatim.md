PART III — PTE-SI01

5. Core scientific question

The experiment is:

When a bounded communication-based system repeatedly solves changing tasks, do distinctions cease to remain accessible specifically when they cease to matter for future prediction/control?

The hypothesis predicts a relationship between:

1. a distinction’s future relevance;
2. its continued recoverability from endogenous causal state;
3. its causal utility;
4. bounded-resource competence.

We are trying to break that relationship.

Do not assume it exists.

⸻

6. Three distinction classes

PTE-SI01 must distinguish at least three categories.

R — CURRENTLY RELEVANT

A distinction that changes the oracle-correct future answer.

Example:

the current HOLD cue before its query.

E — EXPIRED

A distinction that was relevant earlier, but whose associated query has already occurred and which cannot affect any future oracle-correct answer.

This class is particularly important.

If intelligence selectively destroys distinctions according to future relevance, E should eventually behave differently from R.

N — NEVER RELEVANT NUISANCE

A matched distinction introduced into the episode that never affects the oracle-correct future answer.

Its injection timing, encoding magnitude, and opportunity to enter the machinery should be matched as closely as practical to R.

Do not define relevance from what the evolved agent happens to use.

Relevance comes from the task oracle.

11. Four possible states of a distinction

Your reporting should be able to identify:

RECOVERABLE + CAUSAL

RECOVERABLE + NONCAUSAL

UNRECOVERABLE + COMPETENCE PRESERVED

The distinction has disappeared without hurting current competence.

UNRECOVERABLE + COMPETENCE LOST

Destruction removed something still needed.
