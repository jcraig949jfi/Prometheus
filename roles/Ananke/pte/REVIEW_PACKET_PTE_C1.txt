--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CLAIM UNDER REVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Can organised, communication-dependent information processing emerge in a
mutable integer tensor substrate from asynchronous, lossy, delayed,
superposing packets with no imported neural machinery -- and where in
communication-physics space? Preregistered falsifiers: every SIGNAL must
beat 0.55 (99% lower bound over mirror pairs) on held-out worlds; COMM
claims must beat the same law with communication removed; CAUSAL claims
must die under zero-comm and packet ablation; 8 losable predictions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. METHOD / SUBSTRATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B worlds x N sites (torus/ring/random/smallworld/global). int32 state
saturated to +-32767; counter-hash randomness per named stream; packets =
(channel, payload[P]) that SUM on arrival; per-copy loss, latency, jitter,
duplication, noise; receiver cap with none/aloha/saturate; sync/async;
decay S -= S>>k. Update law = straight-line program, 16 integer ops
(NOP MOV ADD SUB MULQ ADDI CONST GT SEL MAX SHR XOR MOD RAND SETRULE
WIMM). Adaptation: writable routing, writable immediates, rule switching,
in-lifetime mutation. 31 physics dials + 4 env dials. Outer search: a
declared GA (pop 96, 36 gens), selection shaped by a twin-contrast bonus
(max +0.12); champion chosen on training accuracy only.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. GROUND TRUTH / KEY DEFINITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Engine = independent CPU oracle, bit-identical (oracle written from the
spec by a separate agent that never read the engine; 145 tests).
Targets iid, worlds in MIRROR PAIRS (negated targets, shared physics
randomness): every constant policy scores exactly 0.500. Positions redrawn
per world, so a homogeneous law cannot memorise its actuator.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
