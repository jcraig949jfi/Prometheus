 6 MAX T0 = max(SENSE,IN0_0) 7 GT T0 = T0>0 (+ evidence)  8 GT T1 = IN0_1>SENSE (- evidence)
 9 GT PAY0 = T0>S1          10 GT PAY1 = T1>S2       11 ADD EMIT = PAY0+PAY1 (forward each newly learned flag once)

7. DISAGREEMENTS (all with the frozen plan's premises; I read no principal synthesis)
(1) The plan says no plant exists for multi-hop RELAY at any physics. relay_flood is already a multi-hop plant: it scores .984 at d9cc d5, and C1's own A0 plant rows reach .97-.99 at multi-hop envs (e.g. torus r1 d2, torus r3 d5). What was missing was a measurement at d9cc, not a plant.
(2) The plan's one-hop XOR design is not possible under envs.build. The XOR actuator is placed uniformly at distance >= max(1, d//2) from both sensors, so it is not a neighbour of both in every world. I replaced it with a flood plus a tick clock (ADDENDUM A1).
(3) "C1 NULLs are search, not physics" does not hold for d9cc XOR. The light-cone bound caps it at .574.
(4) XOR loophole: the mirror negates only x1, so a readout built from a single sign flag keeps the x1*x2 term. Measured: "+ iff P" scores .237 at X0. Its negation, "+ iff no P", is a plain OR-type rule, and since the readout is never zero (no ties) it scores 1 - .237 = .763 there. So C1's SIGNAL rule for XOR (lo99 > .55) can be met without computing XOR. XOR SIGNAL claims should be checked against one-flag readouts.
(5) Strictness of "a physics point C1 sampled": with genome-space overrides (the C1b A2.2 convention; C1's plant_viability itself reset prog_len) a point is not a full C1 dial vector. I report XOR in both senses; FLIP and MULTIHOP needed no override.
