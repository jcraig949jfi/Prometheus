Sampling scheme (S_late): uniform random 64-byte genomes (Python random, seed 20261020 + 1000*worker), ACCEPTED for the
exact S3 COMPETENT screen (cell 7ae3, dense) iff the first copy-op site is at position >= 35, where a copy-op site is
byte E5 or E7, or ED followed by B0/B8 (same set as W2-13's filter). I.e. no copy-op site in [0,35), at least one in [35,64).
This is rejection sampling from the SAME uniform distribution, conditioned on a static, outcome-blind event.
Validation: every 20th REJECTED genome (first copy site < 35, or no copy site) is also fully screened; hits there are
logged but NOT added to the analysis set (they are not from S_late).
Inclusion: EVERY S_late hit enters RAND, whatever its traced L_pre (no post-hoc selection on L_pre or on outcome).
Stop: per worker 6 hits or 270 CPU-s, whichever first (6 workers).

and lengths (instrumented dense VM, 5 victim seeds, median L_pre; pre_set from seed 12345). analysis.py and robustness.py
copied verbatim and run on lengths.json = W2-13 rows + new rows.
