-----
0. SUMMARY
-----

Starting point (XTG-v2):
  A planted copier carrying a task routine (CT_UA) spreads by causal (P-11)
  copying. Its descendants are competent for about 60-90 epochs. Then the
  task vanishes while the copier stays fixed.

Question:
  Under what endogenous conditions does a useful function become a
  MAINTAINED part of heredity rather than transient cargo?

Answer (pair-tape worlds ffa6 and 7ae3):
  The function persists iff competence biases the DIRECTION of the copy
  exchange (who overwrites whom). A bias in the RATE of encounters is not
  enough.

Confirmed on fresh seeds:
  - DIR (an external ruler decides who moves first): 12/12 vs 0/12.
  - ONTAPE (priority EARNED from the organism's own answers in the very
    execution that copies; no offline scorer): 12/12 vs 0/12.

Compute:
  560 runs, 66.0 CPU-h, BUCKKEEP, 4 workers.
  The window was paused about 36 h for an operator demo.

-----
1. WHY THE FUNCTION WAS TRANSIENT (measured, exact per-interaction ledger)
-----

Transmission is fine:
  - P(competent child | competent P-11 donor) = 0.89-0.92 per generation.
  - The tape copy itself is 0.94 competent; post-copy mutation costs about
    4% more.

But the copy race ignores function:
  - Side 0 (the first mover) wins 93-98% of conversions.
  - After the peak, competent-over-non-competent wins / the reverse = 0.90-0.92,
    which equals the transmission fidelity. Competence is a NEUTRAL passenger.

The one-way leak:
  - About 3.5% of competent halves become non-competent copiers per
    interaction; the reverse is about 0.
  - A mutated routine byte is destructive 0.63-0.67 of the time, against
    0.08-0.12 for the copier.
  - 81-87% of individual losses are overwrites by those copiers.

XTG-v2's symmetric rate gate was HARMFUL:
  Competent halves interacted 6.6x more, with the same hazard per
  interaction. Median survival: TG 70 epochs < SHUF 220 < blind floor 400.

-----
2. HOW PERSISTENCE WAS ACHIEVED
-----

  DIR (offense):
    competent goes first; 12/12 + 12/12 vs 0/21; exchange 60:1;
    CS about 0.92 at mutation-selection balance.
  VETO (defense):
    a less competent first mover is skipped; 12/12; ledger identical to
    DIR (offense == defense).
  ONTAPE (endogenous):
    the episode is fed into the copying execution; priority is the EWMA of
    the organism's own answers; exchange only about 2:1, yet 10/10 and the
    confirm 12/12.

  Boundary (X-DIR-SURFACE):
    monotone in directional strength q x mutation k.
    q about 0.1 suffices at k = 1; q >= 0.5 is needed at k = 4.
    At k = 16 the COPIER itself fails first (two error thresholds, ordered).

  Generalizes:
    7ae3 world: DIR 12/12 vs 0/9; ONTAPE 6/6 vs 0/7. Higher equilibria,
    as the leak model predicts (opcode-sparing mutation).

-----
3. ARCHITECTURE UNDER SELECTION (what changed, what did not)
-----

  - The conditional core is conserved.
  - Code that is dead OFFLINE but executed on every input-less TAPE
    interaction (the answer-before-read branch) is rewritten in every
    maintained run.
  - PAIR-DISTRIBUTED function (ONTAPE). Some genomes answer by jumping into
    the PARTNER's code (byte-29 JRZ +66 lands on the partner's OUT):
      - competent on the tape (homotypic use 1.0), incompetent alone (u = 0);
      - recurs in 8/12 confirm runs;
      - NEUTRAL against a marker, from rare and from common;
      - becomes the majority more often when reputation is heritable
        (deeper genealogies).
  - A robust architecture (CT_W) displaced CT_UA 11/12 (k = 1) and 12/12
    (k = 4), but the CONFIRM failed (7/9 against a bar of 8): a
    single-founder establishment lottery.
  - Over 8000 epochs: routine robustness +0.05 to +0.08 at k = 4 only.
    No task-copier fusion.

-----
4. RE-DISCOVERY AND EMERGENCE
-----

  - Lost function is re-found only 1 mutational step away, and only EARLY.
  - At about 7x the mutational supply there was no gain (falsified).
  - Replay: the share of the population one step from use is 0.023 at
    epoch 25 and 0 from epoch 50 on, while the copier persists.
    => The unselected routine ERODES the target.
  - From random populations: no copier regime at all (blocked at rung 1).

-----
6. WHAT THIS DOES / DOES NOT ESTABLISH
-----

DOES:
  - Direction-coupled exchange is sufficient for persistence, in two
    pair-tape worlds, with an external or an earned priority.
  - Rate coupling is not sufficient, and is harmful.

DOES NOT:
  - Selection on function is DESIGNED into the world rule (DIR/VETO read a
    ruler; ONTAPE reads on-tape answers). It does not arise from physics.
  - No task-copier integration.
  - No de novo function.
  - One task (ADD37); pair tapes only.
