  Periodic and immune are robust to tick budget and tape; ask-time and schedule are budget-entangled

- **P-H08 NEUTRALITY_PRESERVES.** Unfiltered mutants lose the shape (keep .02-.45 after 16
  operations) and reward (0-.02); neutral walkers keep both (.62-1.0). Shape is inherited with function.

  every depth, start-anchored .81 (vs .94), schedule .44 (vs .62). T-ARCH5 reactivated.
