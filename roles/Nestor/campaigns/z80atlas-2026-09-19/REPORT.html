Key findings

Spontaneous replication is robust and common. 1,031 randomly-initialised populations produced evidence-backed self-replicators (fidelity 0.906–0.990) with zero seeding. These are the cleanest admissible results in the campaign — no flags on seeding, no ambiguity about the initial condition. The earliest arose at epoch 5; the latest at epoch 2,899, suggesting no sharp accessibility cliff.
Explicit fitness selection is the strongest single-factor signal. Removing explicit fitness (EXPLICIT_FITNESS → NONE_IMPLICIT) raised held-out scores by a mean of +0.30 across 513 matched pairs, with 85 crossings only in the explicit-fitness arm vs 11 in the implicit arm. This is the largest and best-supported effect in the table.
Pair-execution (endogenous) reproduction hurts task performance. PAIR_EXECUTION → EXTERNAL shows mean Δ = −0.12 across 2,258 pairs (the largest pair count), with 218 crossings only under pair-execution vs 343 under external. Endogenous reproduction carries a performance cost in this substrate; the question of whether it unlocks qualitatively different accessibility remains open (one ADMISSIBLE instance at final state, but marginal — see defects).
Forced read order is the cycle-8 accessibility finding in bytes. ANSWER_BEFORE_READ → FORCED_READ: mean Δ = +0.10, 37 crossings only in the forced-read arm vs 13 in the answer-before-read arm. Consistent with the CW01 cycle-8 topological obstruction: the plateau organism answers before reading, and every partial-read edit is deleterious. Enforcing the read order in the world rather than the genome moves the population.
Reservoir stepping-stone structure is unanswered. All 124 RESERVOIR_CROSSED_A_MOAT flags are inadmissible: 22 of 124 are seeded instruments and the remainder are exogenous controls or carry D01 final-state mismatches. The question requires tracing each crossing lineage's ancestry into the easy niche from per-run lineage records. Records are preserved; this is answerable post-campaign.

Defects recorded in flight

Three defects found during execution. None patched in flight — the campaign froze its flag rules before launch; patching mid-run would make the two halves of the record incomparable. All three adjudicated post-hoc by adjudicate.py.

Z80A-D01 · Medium
Flags mix a historical crossing event with a final-state measurement

crossed is true if any organism ever crossed the threshold at any validation pass; held_max is the best score at the end. REACHED_ONLY_UNDER_ENDOGENOUS_REPRODUCTION fires on the historical flag and reports final-state numbers as evidence, with no margin requirement. Of 65 instances, 4 had a negative margin — the exogenous control finished better than the flagged run.

Z80A-D02 · Medium
Reservoir flag does not require the reservoir to have contributed

RESERVOIR_CROSSED_A_MOAT fires on structure + moat + any historical crossing, without checking that the easy niche supplied the crossing lineage. 22 of 124 instances are seeded instruments; niche occupancy in inspected instances is roughly uniform, consistent with no stepping-stone structure. Answerable from per-run lineage records.

Z80A-D03 · Low (adjudicator only)
Index whitelist omits replication evidence; adjudicator read it there

The index carries replicated and replication_rate but not replication_events or births_similar_no_write. The first adjudicator read from the index, found those fields absent, and returned INADMISSIBLE for all 36 spontaneous-replicator flags in flight at the time. Per-run records always held both. Index schema left unchanged; adjudicator now reads per-run records.
