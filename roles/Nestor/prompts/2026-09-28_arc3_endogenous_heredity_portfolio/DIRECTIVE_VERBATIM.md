CURRENT SCIENTIFIC BASELINE

Treat the following as evidence to attack, not conclusions to defend.

ACQUISITION

W1’s original “encoding length” interpretation has been narrowed.

Current best evidence:

* block-write density alone produces no donors;
* planting the ordinary 2-byte copy instruction produces donors;
* the 1-byte alias produces more;
* all observed dense-VM donors use the alias.

Current interpretation:

acquisition is strongly affected by how available usable copying material is in the variation/search process.

Do not equate this automatically with opcode length.

ESTABLISHMENT

“Persistent state poisons donors” is withdrawn.

Current stronger interpretation:

many donors specialize on a zero-register environment that supplies values they use as implicit self-location; their own execution destroys that useful condition.

Fresh-panel confirmation supports this strongly.

But there is circularity:

* competence is currently certified from zero state;
* therefore the experiment may preferentially discover zero-state specialists.

That must be attacked.

CELL SPECIFICITY

The ffa6-versus-7ae3 distinction did not survive.

The earlier 7ae3 null appears to have been low-power.

Do not continue treating cell identity as the primary explanation.

DONOR ARCHITECTURE

Approximately 96% of observed competent donors do not explicitly locate themselves.

They exploit:

* zero register state;
* wrapped tape geometry;
* fixed execution context.

This makes the environment part of the reproductive mechanism.

ENDOGENOUS TRANSITION

n = 1.
