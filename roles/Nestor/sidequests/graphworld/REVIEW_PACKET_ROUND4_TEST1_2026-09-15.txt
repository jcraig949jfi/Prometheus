Floor per cell (operator 12 + ruling 13 "gate IN"):
  floor = max(abstain,
              best constant action (exhaustive 8^W, chosen on TRAIN),
              uniform random (median of 8 policy seeds),
              input-invariant learner (open-loop genome, obs never read),
              best 2-action gate (act iff one obs feature crosses a
              threshold))

Baseline: float linear MAP-Elites (M2), >= 8 run seeds at the round 1
budget, elites saved, bootstrap 95% CI of the median (10,000 resamples,
PCG64 seed 20260914).

Verdict, active variant gate_in|HOLD:
- SURVIVED iff baseline ci95 low > floor.
- HELD iff gate > four-policy floor but baseline ci95 low <= full floor.
  The world rewards reaction and the search missed it. The cell is filed
  as an anomaly for D.
- PENDING (conductor scheduling): ci95 low <= a bound made of the
  pressure's own parts, so the cell cannot survive, but HELD vs CULLED
  needs the expensive train128 learner (~7.8 h). Treated as INELIGIBLE;
  run after the test launch.
- CULLED otherwise.
All four variants (four_policy/gate_in x CULL/HOLD) are recorded per cell.

Compute honesty: the literal screen (train128 learner on every candidate)
was estimated at ~74 h. It ran the learner only where it could change a
verdict under some variant. A bound uses only that pressure's own
components; the author caught and fixed a draft that let the train8
learner bound the train128 floor.
