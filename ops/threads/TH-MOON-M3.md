## Question / capability
Make "a null means something" provable. Define and certify three reachability levels and bind
them to the UNDERPOWERED/KILL semantics (design doc R-RC, R10):
- RC0 expressible: a hand-written organism in the palette exhibits the mechanism.
- RC1 locally traversable: fitness-improving / mortality-reducing intermediary mutations exist
  between randomized starts and the mechanism (the landscape is not a needle).
- RC2 search-reachable: a preregistered search crosses the floor at a known rate from
  appropriately randomized starts.
A null is KILL only once the level relevant to the claim is certified; otherwise UNDERPOWERED.
This may be Moonshot's most reusable contribution to the whole program.
