## How it serves the phase epics
The refinery takes material from any epic (an anomaly from a Phase 2-B replay, a counterexample from the RSO,
external prior art) and returns refined evidence, organs, pressures, rulings and index entries to whichever epic
can use them, by the cross-epic convention (roles/base-role/DISTRIBUTED_WORK.md s11). It never takes over
another epic's claims: evidence rolls upward; interpretive authority does not.
