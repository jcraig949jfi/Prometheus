# TH-009 -- Reproduction vs copying: is the capacity to reproduce transmitted?

Status: OPEN. Type: SCIENCE / partly NEW-LENS. Origin: deep block 2026-09-27 (G_PRIOR_ART.md, Griesemer).

Question: which recorded "reproductions" transmit the CAPACITY to reproduce, and which only transmit material? Does the answer differ by
mechanism (self-copy vs hosted vs origination) and by engine?

reproduce. NPE predecessor births and BEE copy events often move material without it.
