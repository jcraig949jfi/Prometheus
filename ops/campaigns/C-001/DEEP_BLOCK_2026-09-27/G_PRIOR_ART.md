## Where Prometheus looks somewhere genuinely different
1. **Referent separation for executing code (WHERE vs WHAT) measured in evolved populations**, with quantified misattribution
   (BEE 27,083/28,163). Nothing found treats code location as a misleading referent of authorship.
2. **The same conceptual question answered by three independently written substrates**, with recurring structural hazards (harness
   copying credited to organisms; label-as-material). The ALife literature mostly reports single systems.
3. **Named-intervention necessity/sufficiency attached to individual reproduction events** (NPE P-11, the Archaeon sham arms).
   Standard ALife tracking records genealogy, not event-level counterfactuals.

## Where prior art CHALLENGES our conclusions
- **Griesemer:** the lens has no "capacity-transmission" requirement, so "reproduction" in our records often means copying.
- **ARG:** C-OP'-style singular lineage recovery is not how the mature field treats recombination; graded per-locus ancestry is.
- **GP introns:** "inert" units may matter evolutionarily even when they carry no attribution information.
