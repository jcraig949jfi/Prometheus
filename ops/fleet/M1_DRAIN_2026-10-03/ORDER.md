## What every in-scope seat does from receipt

1. **Freeze new work.** Claim no new Campaign, no new long-running Task, and no experiment that cannot finish during the
   drain.
2. **Finish the current smallest safe unit.** A short atomic operation that makes state durable is allowed. Starting
   another campaign is not.
3. **Preserve state.**
   - Commit meaningful modifications by explicit paths (WORKING_CONTRACT s5).
   - Push your branches. Nothing unique may exist only on M1.
4. **Propose a disposition for every branch and worktree you own:**
   - **MERGE:** completed work intended for main whose acceptance conditions pass. Use the normal fast-forward path
     (s5) and record the merge SHA.
   - **ARCHIVE:** valuable but not for main. Push the branch, then create and push the tag
     `archive/m1-drain-2026-10-03/<seat>/<branch>` at its tip. Record the tip SHA, why it is not merged, and the future
     owner or campaign.
   - **DISCARD:** only when you state explicitly that the material is disposable scratch. Record branch, tip SHA and
     file list.
   - If you cannot understand a branch cheaply, mark it `ARCHIVE / NEEDS_REVIEW`. Preserve it and move on. Do not spend
     inference reconstructing history.
5. **Release** your Fabric leases, legacy lease files, scheduled tasks, watchers and background processes. Anything that
   must keep running must be named in your receipt.
6. **Do NOT:**
   - remove worktrees or delete branches yourself (Aporia does that centrally, step 7 of the directive);
   - touch another seat's worktree;
   - run `git pull` in the canonical checkout.
7. **Emit a DRAIN RECEIPT to Aporia on comms**, kind report, subject `DRAIN RECEIPT M1 <Seat>`, using the template below.
8. **Stop.** End your session. A Phase 2-B restart is a fresh session from current origin/main (directive s13). Do not
   carry this session forward.
