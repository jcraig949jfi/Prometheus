## TL;DR

`bottled_serendipity` answers *how a single hallucinated claim becomes truth* (filter it). It does
**not** answer two things that decide whether scaling the soup produces a heartbeat or a graveyard:

1. **What "lightning" is.** Not emergence we wait for. It is the **ruthless ablation of the
   decorative** — the mechanism-knockout that burns away an LLM's sophistry and leaves the
   load-bearing R3 structural morpheme that actually shifts the gradient. The heartbeat is the
   *survivor of ablation*, available now, not a future spark.

2. **How the population stays alive.** Ablation is a *convergent* pressure. Unchecked, it manufactures
   exactly the monoculture it's supposed to avoid (Apollo Gen-3551: the whole population converged on
   one `fencepost_count` Goodhart trick because that trick survived the gate). Selection and diversity

Both collapse onto one object. **The KillVector basis is simultaneously the niche descriptor (which
forbids monoculture) and the coordinate system of the dark-matter map (whose resolution is kill-mode
diversity, not corpse count).** Get the basis right — orthogonal, directional, real — and the corpses
*tile* kill-space: real niches, monoculture structurally forbidden, a map with resolution. Get it
wrong — correlated, count-shaped, proxy — and every death points the same way: a million identical
corpses, a monoculture wearing a map costume, and an ablation engine that converges on **certified
mirages**. Same engine, opposite fates, and the fork is entirely the basis.

## 1. The thesis, stripped to substrate mechanics

### 1.1 LLM = radiation; battery = environment

The LLM is a high-dimensional probability distribution over human syntax, not a reasoner. That makes
it the right *mutation engine* (chaotic, prior-shaped variance) and the wrong *oracle*. Operating rule,
sharper than "use it as a mutation operator":

> **LLM on the mutation side only. Never on the selection side.**

The bias James observes — "the models keep steering us toward writing papers and existing failure
patterns" — does not come from the hallucinations. It comes from **every seat where an LLM judges,
ranks, or decides.** The moment a model evaluates fitness, the human prior re-enters through the back
door: it scores "looks like a known-good approach" as fit. Anywhere the engine steers toward polished
successes, audit for an LLM in a selection seat. (Icarus is the in-house instance: its Generator,
Skeptic, *and* Integrator are LLMs; only its Contract Lens and TDD are clean — and its single Goodhart
hole sits at the one LLM-judged gate.)

### 1.2 Lightning = the mechanism-knockout protocol

The poetic reading ("wait for the primordial soup to wake up") is deprioritized — not anticipated soon,
and not the operational mechanism. The operational lightning is **ablation of the decorative**: when
the LLM mutates a monstrous hallucinated claim, the falsification battery and mechanism-knockout test
strip the sophistry and leave only the load-bearing residue. The surviving structural morpheme — the
piece that actually shifts the gradient — is the heartbeat. This converts an act of faith into a
mechanism the substrate already owns.

### 1.3 Navigating the soup

Two standing pressures, both required: **anti-monoculture** (don't let the population collapse onto one
surviving trick) and **push against the void** (every failure, type-mismatch, compile error emits a
*directional* KillVector). The slogan that compresses it: *the failure is the residue; the residue is
the map.* §2 and §3 are the conditions under which that slogan is true rather than aspirational.

## 2. The knockout, run on the engine itself

Three places the thesis-as-stated hides a hole. Each is an invariant a new machine must honor.

### 2.1 Ablation certifies "load-bearing *for an effect*" — not "real vs. artifact"

Mechanism-knockout tells you which morpheme carries a measured effect. It does **not** tell you whether
the effect is real or a selection-frame artifact. **F044 is the in-house proof:** the "rank-4 theorem"
(100% prime-conductor concentration) had a perfectly load-bearing morpheme — knockout would confirm
"conductor is load-bearing." But the effect itself was a sampling-frame artifact of LMFDB sourcing
(identical concentration appears at ranks 2–5 once you condition on conductor ≥ 10⁸). Mechanism-knockout
would have **certified the corpse as a heartbeat.**

> **Invariant A — frame before mechanism.** Run the frame/null check (Pattern 4, block-shuffle,
> `null_protocol_v1`) *before* the mechanism-knockout. Without it, the engine ablates its way to
> certified mirages, and the "residue" is a fossil of an instrument artifact.

### 2.2 The lightning eats the diversity

Ruthless ablation is a convergent pressure: it concentrates the population on whatever survives the
battery. That is the exact process that produced the **Apollo Gen-3551 monoculture** — everyone learned
the one trick that beat the gate. So "avoid monoculture" and "ruthless ablation" are not allies; the
lightning *manufactures* the monoculture unless diversity is protected explicitly.

> **Invariant B — quality-diversity, not pure selection.** Select ruthlessly *within* niches; protect
> the niches. The engine's coherence reduces entirely to: *what defines a niche?* Define niches on a
> proxy and you breed **diverse Goodhart**.

### 2.3 A million corpses map only at high kill-diversity

A million corpses from a monoculture is one corpse photographed a million times. The resolution of the
dark-matter map is the **diversity of kill-modes**, not the volume of kills.

> **Invariant C — coverage, not count.** The map metric is coverage of kill-space (KillVector
> diversity), never the number of kills. A void is informative only if probed densely; a sparsely
> sampled void is indistinguishable from an exhausted one and means the opposite. (Ergon's shadow-archive
> "confidence saturates on narrow exploration" is the canonical false-exhaustion instance.)

## 3. The load-bearing decision: the KillVector basis *is* the niche descriptor

Invariants B and C collapse onto a single object. The KillVector basis is *both*:

- the **niche / behavior descriptor** for quality-diversity (what makes two deaths "different"), and
- the **coordinate system of the dark-matter map** (whose resolution is its diversity).

So the basis is the single decision the whole engine balances on:

| KillVector basis | Population fate | Map fate | Ablation fate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **orthogonal · directional · real** | corpses tile kill-space; niches real; monoculture structurally forbidden | map has resolution; coverage measurable | survivors are heartbeats |
| **correlated · count-shaped · proxy** | every death points the same way; collinear; monoculture inevitable | one corpse × N; map is a costume | converges on certified mirages |

Same engine, opposite fates. **Building more machinery into the soup is only safe if every machine
emits into a *shared, real* KillVector basis** — that is what turns isolated smashing events into
*reactions* (units eating each other's residue) rather than a thousand private lottery tickets. More
nodes ≠ more reactions; reactions need a shared currency, and the KillVector is it.

## 4. Implications for new machinery (what reactions to dump into the soup)

The goal is "more chemical reactions," not "more machines." A reaction is one unit consuming another's
residue. The design moves that make a new machine a reaction rather than an isolated event:

1. **Emit into the shared KillVector basis** — not a private failure format. A private kill-log is an
   isolated event; a kill in the shared basis is a reaction another unit can metabolize.
2. **LLM on mutation only; selection deterministic** — battery + frame-check (§1.1, §2.1). No LLM in a
   judge/rank/decide seat.
3. **Quality-diversity over the KillVector basis** — protect niches; forbid the monoculture the
   ablation pressure would otherwise breed (§2.2).
4. **Frame before mechanism** — null/frame check upstream of knockout (§2.1).
5. **Retain both halves** — survivors become rungs (R3 morphemes for the ladder); kills become
   void-geometry. Both bias where the *next* mutation lands. This retention is the autocatalysis: it is
   the difference between an evolving population and random search re-rolled each generation.

Net: the engine grows by adding reactions in a *shared* kill-space, not by adding isolated machines.

## 5. Design invariants (the contract a new machine must satisfy)

- [ ] Mutation is LLM-driven; **no LLM occupies a selection/judge/rank seat.**
- [ ] Selection is deterministic, and the **frame/null check runs before the mechanism-knockout.**
- [ ] Every failure emits a **directional KillVector into the shared basis** (not a private log).
- [ ] Diversity is protected by **quality-diversity over the KillVector basis** (niches, not a global elite).
- [ ] Map progress is reported as **kill-space coverage, never kill count.**
- [ ] Survivors and kills are **retained and fed back** as bias on the next mutation (autocatalysis).

A machine that violates the first two breeds mirages; one that violates the next two breeds a
monoculture; one that violates the last two is a lottery ticket, not a reaction.
