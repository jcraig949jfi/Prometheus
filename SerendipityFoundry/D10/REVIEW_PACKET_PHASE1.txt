1. CLAIM / SUBJECT UNDER REVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This packet asks you to review a PROPOSED EXPERIMENT, not a result. No
preregistration is frozen, no main run has occurred, and no scientific
verdict exists. What is under review is: an operational definition of
"machine-created memory organization", an information boundary, a control
architecture, a set of preflight measurements that fix the operating point,
and the adversarial review that has already been run against all of it.

The question: can a computational system construct its own useful
organization of accumulated computational experience, rather than having
that organization specified by humans?

The MAXIMUM claim the proposed design could ever support:

  In one substrate (stackvm-v1), on one distribution of substrate-generated
  exact program-synthesis tasks, a 64-bit retrieval key function produced by
  substrate search under selection on downstream acquisition consequences
  causes a higher held-out exact-solve rate than (a) no memory, (b)
  unorganized memory, (c) a shipped human-designed organization, (d) a
  machine-fitted human-designed organization, (e) a compute-matched
  organizer whose selection signal was decoupled from the query, and (f) a
  compute-matched cold search given the same TOTAL budget -- and survives
  ablation, stratified scrambling, query-shuffling and transplantation.

Deliberately out of scope: intelligence, cognition, concepts, language,
abstraction, understanding, "alien reasoning". A maximal positive result
reads as: a machine-produced hash function beat every control we built.
That is intended to sound disappointingly narrow.

PRE-REGISTERED FALSIFIERS (what would kill the claim)
  - E does not beat R2 (the within-organizer, query-shuffled null) by the
    pre-declared minimum effect size at the lineage level.
  - The effect survives statistics-preserving scrambling of the key
    assignment (would mean the specific organization is not doing the work).
  - N+ (cold search given E's construction compute) matches or beats E
    (mechanism claim may survive; utility claim dies).
  - A planted negative is admitted, or a planted positive is not detected.
  - Any information-boundary detector fires.

2. METHOD / SETUP / SUBSTRATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUBSTRATE. stackvm-v1, the Foundry's in-house metered stack/register VM.
33 opcodes; EVERY byte string is a legal program (no invalid genotypes);
bit-deterministic in (code, inputs, max_steps); exact step meter.
EngineLimits(max_steps=200, timeout_s=5.0).

THE OBJECT UNDER STUDY. An "organization" is a pair of total functions

    KA : genotype bytes  ->  64-bit key      (artifact key)
    KQ : train examples  ->  64-bit key      (query key)

plus a SUPPLIED retrieval rule: return the k corpus artifacts minimising
Hamming distance between their KA key and the query's KQ key, ties broken
by a deterministic hash. The organization IS the induced structure: which
artifacts share a key, which are Hamming-near, which of the 64 bits are
live, what those bits separate, and what is discarded.

An organizer genome is ONE byte string decoded totally into (KA, KQ), so it
is an ordinary substrate artifact with ordinary Foundry lineage.

WHY THIS SHAPE. The charter forbids assuming the answer's shape. We did not
assume it; we chose it and we declare it. The consequence is stated in
section 3 as E4-prime and is the biggest concession in this packet.

WHAT WE DID NOT USE, AND WHY. The Foundry ships six competing index
builders (Q0..Q5). We judge them INADEQUATE to this question and did not
build the experiment on them:
  - All six consume ONE human feature space (foundry/representations/
    features.py: 256-bin byte histogram, Shannon entropy, log1p length,
    failure-kind one-hots, mean behaviour vector, output-hash diversity)
    through Euclidean kNN. That is a human-specified organization with
    fitted parameters at best -- charter category 4, not 5 or 6.
  - Q1/Q3/Q4/Q5 embed the QUERY side through an explicitly random
    projection. The instrument documents this honestly in query_path_doc
    ("pure seeded noise -- carries no learned structure"). There is
    therefore NO learned task coupling on the query side at all.
Q3 is retained only as arm H-fixed, the "off-the-shelf human organization"
reference point, with the above caveat attached to any result it produces.

3. KEY DEFINITIONS AND THE INFORMATION BOUNDARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
An organization is ENDOGENOUS iff all four hold:

E1  SUBSTRATE PROVENANCE. KA and KQ are substrate programs with complete
    Foundry lineage terminating in create_random events with recorded
    seeds. No human- or LLM-authored byte. No "import" creation-op anywhere
    in an organizer lineage.

E2  CONSEQUENCE SELECTION. Every survival event's only input was measured
    downstream acquisition outcomes on FITTING tasks. Verified by replaying
    the organizer GA byte-identically from its seeds.

E3  NO PRIVILEGED INFORMATION. Nothing outside the declared encoded input
    reaches KA/KQ. In particular not task_id (a content hash of train AND
    test cases, i.e. a perfect task fingerprint), family identity,
    generator parameters, difficulty, or chronology.

E4-prime  UNRESTRICTED FUNCTION CLASS, SUPPLIED GEOMETRY. The key
    functions are unrestricted substrate programs, BUT the retrieval
    geometry (a shared 64-bit space, Hamming distance, top-k) is SUPPLIED
    and is itself a human-designed organizational schema.

E4-prime replaces a draft E4 which asserted that no distance function, no
dimensionality and no similarity notion were supplied -- while the very
same table supplied all three. Reviewer A caught the self-contradiction.
This is the most important correction of the phase and it lowers the
headline claim: we can test whether the KEY ASSIGNMENT is machine-created;
we cannot test whether the NOTION OF NEARNESS is.

THE BOUNDARY (exhaustive; hashed in SUPPLIED_MANIFEST.json before the run
and re-verified after; anything outside it that influenced an organizer's
survival invalidates the run):

acquisition procedure (GA shape)          which artifacts collide into one key
endpoint predicate (exact, held out)      partition granularity

key width = 64 bits                       what the live bits separate
Hamming; top-k; tiebreak                  which artifacts are near / far

The boundary is TESTED, not asserted: d10/tests/test_boundary.py, 9/9
passing, including 200/200 identical query keys across tasks with identical

4. ENVIRONMENT, CORPUS, AND ARMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ENVIRONMENT (d10/lib/progtasks.py). A task is defined by a REFERENCE
PROGRAM in the same substrate the learner searches. Rationale: exact
solvability is guaranteed by construction (so a null is a hypothesis
failure, not an unreachable target), difficulty has a mechanical dial,
family members share reusable substructure BY DESCENT rather than by a
human notion of similarity, and no human semantic category enters at all.

  - family      = a random reference program (root, length L_ref) plus its
                  mutational neighbours (N_mut substrate mutations)
  - task        = a family member + 6 train cases + 20 disjoint test cases,
                  inputs uniform on [0,255]^2
  - filters     = reference must halt within 64 steps, produce outputs in
                  [0,255], and give >= 16 distinct outputs over 40 probes
  - SHORTCUT SCREEN = task rejected if ANY member of a frozen trivial set
                  (all <=2-instruction programs + 2000 fixed random
                  programs) reproduces train+test exactly

"Same family" is a fact about substrate descent. No learner, index or
organizer can read it; it lives oracle-side only.

CORPUS RULE (supplied, arm-neutral, identical for all arms), per lineage:
    C = union of the FINAL POPULATION of every history search
      + every history search's solver genotype
      + a uniform random subsample of all history artifacts
This is declared rather than disguised as "raw experience": a purely
uniform subsample of a large history contains almost no useful material
(preflight p7: 0 solvers in 36 history trials at B=800).

ARMS. Every arm runs the IDENTICAL acquisition procedure -- a fixed-size
population of which exactly k members are injected genotypes, identical
budget, identical trial seeds. Arms differ ONLY in which k. This
neutralises "more material" and "more compute" structurally rather than
statistically.

Arm       Injected k                              Isolates
-------   -------------------------------------   ------------------------
N         fresh random genotypes                  no memory
N+        fresh random, at B + C_org/(T*S)        COMPUTE-MATCHED SEARCH
U         uniform corpus sample                   unorganized memory
U-len     random, length/diversity matched to E   material, not relevance
H-fixed   Foundry Q3 (AURORA-like) top-k          human-designed organization
H-fit     sign(features . W), W_A 278x64 and      machine-fitted human
          W_Q 83x64 evolved on the same signal    organization
R         E machinery, family-block-disjoint      compute-matched, non-
          derangement of fitting evidence         task-conditional
R2        E's OWN winner, query key taken from    task-conditionality, with
          another eval task's evidence            nothing else varying
E         evolved KA / KQ                         the hypothesis

5. PROCEDURE / METRICS / STATISTICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ENDPOINT. Test-split exact solve, checked oracle-side, never visible to the
search. Train-exact success is the SEARCH signal only. This separation is
load-bearing: preflight found a case at 0.450 train-exact / 0.000
test-exact, i.e. train-exact is a fake endpoint.

UNIT OF ANALYSIS: THE LINEAGE. Every trial inside a lineage-arm cell is
driven by ONE draw of an organizer, so L is the sample size, not L*T*S.
Reviewer C simulated the naive pooled two-proportion test under a true
null and obtained 12.8% Type-I error at zero between-lineage variance
(family clustering alone), rising to 48.8% at sigma_org = 0.04.

PRIMARY CONTRAST: E - R2. Adopted from reviewer C over the draft's E - R.
R2 is within-organizer, exactly compute-matched, costs zero extra
construction compute, and isolates task-conditionality with nothing else
varying. R is retained as the compute-matched secondary null.

PRIMARY TEST. Per-lineage paired differences d_l under common random
numbers; exact one-sided sign-flip permutation test over {d_l}, exhaustive
to L ~ 22. CI by two-way cluster bootstrap over lineages AND eval families.
GLMM as sensitivity only, with non-convergence pre-declared as "not a
result". The invalid pooled analysis is reported and LABELLED INVALID.

MULTIPLICITY. Single primary, one-sided, alpha 0.05, uncorrected.
Secondaries in a fixed-sequence gatekeeping family (E-H_fit, R-U,
H_fixed-U, H_fit-H_fixed, U-N), stopping at first non-rejection, costing
zero alpha. Counterfactuals become GATES with pre-declared thresholds, not
tests. Everything else is BH-FDR q=0.10 and labelled exploratory. The
operating-point cell is frozen BEFORE any eval task is generated -- note
that preflight has already swept 72 cells, which is a partly-walked garden
of forking paths and must be declared as such.

DESIGN CONSTRAINTS DERIVED, NOT CHOSEN
  L >= 8            minimum attainable one-sided p = 2^-L
  F_eval >= 24      effective df is min(L, F) - 1; family members are
                    near-duplicate programs, so tasks are not independent
  >= 8 expected successes per lineage-arm cell in the weakest organized arm
  L cannot be set until sigma_org is measured

SECONDARY ENDPOINT. Number of the 20 held-out test cases passed by the
final best-by-train individual (ordinal 0..20). The draft's
"evaluations-to-test-solve" is NOT MEASURABLE: the harness halts on the
first TRAIN-exact success, so no time-to-test-solve exists.

COST MODEL (four separately metered ledgers, pre-declared)
  C_hist    identical across arms, charged to none
  C_org     zero for N/U/U-len/N+; index-build for H-fixed; equal for
            E / H-fit / R
  C_eval    CAP-PARITY: identical B, realised <= B, reported descriptively
  ret_steps retrieval key computation, in VM STEPS, never silently
            converted to evaluations
Pre-declared: a total-cost loss does NOT invalidate the mechanism claim
(settled by the equal-marginal-budget primary contrast) but DOES invalidate
every efficiency claim. If N+ >= E, the organization is dominated by simply
searching longer: mechanism survives, utility dies. That wording is fixed
NOW, not improvised after seeing numbers.

8. CONTROLS, PLANTED INSTRUMENTS, AND CAUSAL INTERVENTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PLANTED INSTRUMENTS (must pass before any main run)
  PP1  assay sensitivity, PRIVILEGED. Oracle-ranked retrieval. Exercises
       the full pipeline and sets the ceiling. MEASURED: 0.344 vs U 0.031.
       Never admitted as evidence.
  PP2  mechanism-faithful positive. A HAND-WRITTEN KA/KQ pair using no
       privileged information. PROMOTED TO A HARD PRE-FREEZE GATE: if no
       hand-written pair can beat the null under the frozen interface,
       evolution will not find one, and a null would measure the interface
       rather than the hypothesis. NOT YET CONSTRUCTED.
  PN1  planted negative. KA = sha256(artifact_id), constant KQ. Must not
       beat U.
  PN2  planted negative. Scrambled winner. Must not beat U.

CAUSAL INTERVENTIONS on each lineage's shipped E organizer, all at
cap-parity budget: bit ablation on live bits; STRATIFIED scramble (permute
within length-decile x first-byte-octile x multiplicity strata);
QUERY-SHUFFLE (artifact side entirely fixed); bag control; transplantation
across lineages; held-out families; matched-random organizer at matched
granularity.

WHY THE NAIVE SCRAMBLE WAS REPLACED. It preserves key multiset,
granularity, entropy and bit marginals -- exactly and only what our own
test checked -- while destroying key-to-length, key-to-first-byte,
key-to-multiplicity and key-to-quality joints. So "E beats scramble"
licenses only "the key assignment carries information", never
"task-conditional relevance". R2, not scramble, is the task-conditionality
null.

9. INDEPENDENT ADVERSARIAL VERIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Three independent hostile reviewers, disjoint mandates, given the design
brief AND the code, and told to kill it. 38 findings; 15 judged blocking.
We reproduced the 6 most consequential ourselves rather than taking them on
trust, and failed to reproduce one reviewer's specific magnitude figure
using a different statistic (recorded as such).

FOUR CONFIRMED LIVE DEFECTS IN OUR OWN CODE

D1  A SUPPLIED LENGTH CHANNEL STRAIGHT INTO THE KEY FUNCTION.
    artifact_words passes len(g) as word 0, which the VM mirrors into
    register R0, so the 2-byte program LDR R0 returns genotype length
    EXACTLY. Reproduced:
        len   7 -> key   7      len  96 -> key  96
        len  16 -> key  16      len 250 -> key 250
        len  40 -> key  40
    The function's own docstring claimed "no length bucket". And length is
    a strong channel to exactly what E3 forbids:
        source history task means:   22.1 / 85.5 / 15.1   (5.7x spread)
        within-run first vs second half:  30.6 -> 13.7
                                          26.7 ->  3.5    (up to 7.6x)

D2  A CONTENT-FREE BYTE DECIDES WHETHER RETRIEVED MEMORY SURVIVES.
    The acquisition GA sorts by (-fitness, genotype_bytes), and at cold
    start essentially all fitness is tied, so the early GA is a
    lexicographic sort on bytes. Reproduced over 400 replicates with
    injected genotypes differing ONLY in their leading byte:
        leading byte   mean rank (of 24)   share of the 2 elite slots
        ------------   -----------------   --------------------------
        0x00                        1.55                        0.979
        0xFF                       21.47                        0.000

D3  THE FITNESS SHAPING TERM IS THE PLANTED POSITIVE'S OWN CRITERION.
    The draft organizer fitness added 0.01 * mean best_train_fitness,
    described as a tiebreak. Two defects. Arithmetically it is a strict
    tiebreak only while 1/(N_fit*trials) > 0.01:
        n_fit_units    min increment    tiebreak valid
        -----------    -------------    --------------
                 16          0.06250    yes
                 54          0.01852    yes
                100          0.01000    NO
                160          0.00625    NO
    Ontologically it asserts "an experience is relevant to a query if it
    passes more of that query's train cases" -- which is LITERALLY the PP1
    oracle ranking criterion. Since the unshaped landscape is near-flat, it
    is plausibly the only usable gradient, and it is query-conditional so
    it does NOT difference out of the primary contrast. This is the
    charter's central failure mode occurring inside the design meant to
    prevent it.

D4  STEPS PER EVALUATION IS NOT CONSTANT (found via a crashed probe).
        Budget     mean len    max len    VM steps / evaluation
        -------    --------    -------    ---------------------
          1,600        23.2        110                      247
          6,400       118.9        415                    1,130
         25,600       157.9        561                    1,481
    max_genotype_bytes defaults to 1,000,000 and the adapter only
    PENALISES oversize genotypes after evaluation; nothing bounds growth.
    So a 6x swing in VM steps sits inside a budget the cost model treated
    as matched, and an arm could gain by retrieving LONGER genotypes with
    no task-conditional relevance at all.

TWO STRUCTURAL DEFECTS IN THE INSTRUMENT'S COURT (foundry/court/)

D5  THE ADJUDICATION GATES ARE SIMULTANEOUSLY UNREACHABLE AND UNFAILABLE.
        DEFAULT_MARGINS: exact_effect 0.25, ablation 0.25, transfer 0.25,
                         control 0.10, null 0.10, cost 0.0
        measured maximum attainable delta: 0.313
      - exact_effect / transfer at 0.25 are passable only by a near-oracle
        organizer  -> impossible positive controls
      - control at 0.10 passes iff |delta| <= 0.10, and realistic deltas
        are below that  -> the controls CANNOT FAIL
      - cost at 0.0 passes on any positive delta at ANY cost  -> fail-open
      - selection_penalty defaults to 0.0  -> the multiple-comparison
        mechanism is INERT by default
      - CourtHarness.n_trials defaults to 8, and the predicates apply no
        statistical test at all (point estimate vs fixed margin)

D6  A REAL EFFECT FAILS THE GATE BECAUSE IT IS REAL.
    _two_arm_effect requires passed = (budget_equal AND delta > margin),
    with budget_tolerance defaulting to 0, while the acquisition harness
    early-stops on success -- so a MORE effective arm consumes FEWER
    evaluations, budget equality fails, and the gate rejects it.

ONE MORE, AFFECTING REPRODUCIBILITY OF THE OBJECT OF STUDY

D7  run_key passes timeout_s=5.0 to the VM, so a key program running near
    the wall-clock backstop yields a DIFFERENT KEY on a loaded machine than
    on an idle one. The organization itself is not reproducible, and total
    wall clock is unbounded.

WHAT THE REVIEWERS FOUND SOUND
  - The query-side firewall holds STRUCTURALLY, not by convention.
    evidence_words touches only train_examples; TaskEvidence/TaskView are
    extra="forbid"; task_id provably hashes train AND test and is never
    read. Verified 200/200 identical query keys.
  - The held-out test split is genuinely unreconstructible from train
    evidence within the step cap (Mersenne Twister inversion needs 624
    outputs; 12 are available).
  - Degenerate key collapse floors cleanly at arm U rather than at
    something pathological.
  - Unplanned determinism cross-check PASSED: two probes ran the identical
    history phase in separate processes and produced byte-identical
    summaries (96 trials, 11 solves, 25 solver genotypes, 1,011,559
    artifacts, corpus 3,962).

Reviewer B's summary is worth quoting in substance: the leaks that exist
are on the ARTIFACT side and in the HARNESS -- the opposite of where our
defences were concentrated.

10. COMMENTARY AND ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(a) The design's defences were pointed the wrong way. We spent the
    engineering effort on the query side -- task_id, oracle metadata,
    family labels -- and that side held perfectly. Every confirmed leak is
    on the artifact side (a supplied length statistic) or in the harness
    (a lexicographic tie-break, an unbounded genotype length, a shaping
    term). The general lesson: the firewall you design is the firewall you
    test, and the firewall you test is the one that holds. The dangerous
    surface is the one you did not think of as a surface.

(b) D3 is the most instructive failure. We wrote a preregistration whose
    section 3 lists "the experimenter supplying the important ontology
    indirectly" as threat T-zero, and then supplied it -- as a term we
    labelled a tiebreak, whose content is identical to the oracle criterion
    we planted as the positive control. It survived our own review and two
    of three hostile reviewers.

(c) The assay's viability turned on a parameter we nearly did not sweep.
    n_train 6 -> 10 moved the oracle ceiling 0.042 -> 0.188, and proper
    history provisioning moved it to 0.344. Nothing about the hypothesis
    changed. Had we frozen at the first configuration we would have run a
    correctly-designed experiment in a regime with no measurable dynamic
    range and reported a null.

(d) We are NOT confident the hypothesis is testable at this interface.
    Random key programs execute a median of ~20 VM steps. KA sees genotype
    BYTES and cannot execute them; KQ sees I/O NUMBERS and cannot execute
    anything. For E to beat R2, some pair must correlate syntax with
    semantics -- which is the program-synthesis problem the acquisition GA
    exists to solve -- inside a few hundred steps. Hence the PP2 gate.

(e) E4-prime materially reduces the claim, and we think that is correct.
    Reviewer A measured that swapping only the supplied comparison changes
    the retrieved top-4 on roughly half of queries.

(f) The Court is not the safety net we assumed. It adjudicates effect and
    provenance integrity. It cannot see the encoders, the corpus, or the
    derangement; it cannot verify that a shuffle is a shuffle; it cannot
    check creation_op, so it cannot adjudicate E1 at all; and its defaults
    are fail-open in four places.

(g) An honest note on our own process. Three of the four code defects were
    found by hostile reviewers, not by us, and we had already written and
    passed a boundary test suite that cannot fail. Self-review was
    insufficient here by a wide margin.
