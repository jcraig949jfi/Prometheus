0. ORIENTATION (self-contained)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Microstructure Observatory (MHC) hunts tiny reproducible structure in
deterministic digital-organism worlds. Its statistics admit a candidate when
an anytime-valid wealth process of conditional ranks crosses a purchased
threshold K. The governing questions of this mission:

  Q-A  Can the admission boundary be a TRUE CONSERVED-RISK SYSTEM -- no
       sequence of adaptive registrations, crashes, retries, concurrency,
       replays, expirations, beacon manipulations, or malformed histories
       can spend more false-admission risk than was preregistered?
  Q-B  At the moment an admission test purchases risk, is its future
       admission evidence GENUINELY UNDERIVABLE from everything available
       before commitment?

Neither is answered philosophically below. The system was built, frozen,
attacked with hostile histories, broken, repaired with every failure
preserved, and re-attacked. Adjudicated amendments honored: ALPHA_LIFE=0.1
permanent; scientific K_MIN=1000; K>=100 calibration-only and structurally
incapable of scientific admission; expected-count guarantee with Markov
corollary; guarantee scoped to candidate-local constructed-null violations;
sham-arm halts admissions not diagnostics; founder-lineage and held-out
grammar-family separations load-bearing; scientific exposure forbidden.

FINAL RULE, enforced in code rather than prose: in a deterministic
substrate, "not yet observed" is NOT a seal. Evidence is sealed only when
some indispensable information required to derive it did not exist, and
could not be known, until after the hypothesis paid for the right to look.

3. THE EXACT CONSERVED-RISK INVARIANT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Per namespace ns in {SCIENTIFIC, CALIBRATION}, at EVERY valid history
prefix, in exact rational arithmetic:

    unreserved(ns) + SUM open-family remaining reservations(ns)
                   + purchased(ns)  ==  BUDGET(ns)

with BUDGET(SCIENTIFIC) = 1/10 permanently, and:
  * purchased(ns) is MONOTONE NON-DECREASING: no code path reduces it.
    A purchase is never refunded because a test lost, expired, was
    abandoned, failed to reach K, or was inconvenient.
  * reservations are an allocation device: releasing UNSPENT remainder
    (close or TTL) is provably safe -- the guarantee counts purchases only.
  * the guarantee: E[# constructed-null false admissions over the
    instrument lifetime] <= SUM 1/K_c <= 1/10, by per-candidate Ville +
    linearity of expectation; Markov gives P(any, ever) <= 1/10. Valid
    under arbitrary dependence and unbounded adaptive registration,
    GIVEN the Validation Clause (section 8).

4. STATE-MACHINE / ACCOUNTING MODEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Event-sourced: the ledger IS an append-only hash-chained log; state is a
pure fold. Consequences that kill whole attack classes by construction:
  * no debit/record gap -- the accepted PURCHASE event IS the debit; a
    crash either durably persisted it (state has it; caller was told) or
    did not (state lacks it; caller was never told). H10 truncated the
    final record at EVERY byte boundary: zero conservation breaks.
  * logical time: caller-supplied monotone integer, step-bounded
    (MAX_LT_STEP) so a forged clock cannot detonate TTLs (H3); refusals
    log at the ledger's own lt and never advance the watermark; expiry is
    lazy, deterministic (family-id order), applied BEFORE each event's
    validation, boundary pinned as lt > expires_lt.
  * families: OPEN -> {CLOSED | EXPIRED}; close of non-open refuses;
    double-release impossible (remainder zeroed at transition).
  * aggregate reservation cap: un-co-signed OPEN reservations may total at
    most 25% of the namespace BUDGET (the per-open cap fell to a geometric
    grab, H4 -- 99% capture in 17 compliant opens; preserved, fixed).
  * K: floor per namespace (1000 scientific / 100 calibration);
    reachability ceiling (13/4)^block_budget with early-exit arithmetic;
    block budget capped at 128; single purchase <= half the family's
    original reservation.
  * evidence blocks: ids are DERIVED H(candidate, index) -- structurally
    unclaimable by others (H6 burn-griefing preserved, fixed); one-time-
    use globally; refused events consume nothing.
  * rule commitments are PROTOCOL-ENUMERATED (section 8) -- free-form
    strings were accepted by v1.0 (H5, preserved) and are now refused.

5. INDEPENDENT RECONSTRUCTION VERIFIER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
verify.py reads ONLY the raw log. It re-derives: initial ALPHA_LIFE;
reservation, purchase, and release histories; current unreserved wealth;
reserved-but-unspent wealth; permanently spent wealth; remaining maximum
scientific liability -- and asserts the accounting identity AT EVERY PREFIX
in exact arithmetic (no float ever touches money). It rejects: chain breaks,
hash mismatches, time regressions and bombs, impossible reservations,
unfundable or duplicate purchases, block reuse and non-derived block ids,
K-floor and cap violations, non-canonical rule commitments, admissions
without purchases or below K, record-type forgeries, scientific admissions
carrying mock attestations, purchases/admissions during halt, and
illegitimate halt lifts. SERVICE/VERIFIER DRIFT (red-team FATAL: the
verifier checking a weaker rule set than the service is itself the
vulnerability) was closed by porting every refusal rule into the verifier
and differentially checking fuzz histories: every service-accepted history
verifies; every tampered variant is rejected (H9, H12).

7. EXTERNAL-ANCHOR AND BEACON PROTOCOL (G14)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ordering, implemented and enforced by the fold:
  1. registration artifact exists (canonical candidate, all commitments);
  2. hash-committed: CANDIDATE_REGISTER carries the irrevocable 1/K
     purchase IN THE SAME EVENT -- purchase strictly precedes anchoring;
  3. the registration is anchored (ANCHOR_ATTEST must cite the commit
     hash; anchoring something else is refused);
  4. eligible beacon round FIXED BY RULE: first valid pulse STRICTLY after
     the anchor; the fold refuses round_time <= anchor_time and any round
     that is not the rule-determined round; no nearest-pulse, no
     designation, no retry-until-favorable, ONE beacon attestation per
     candidate (immutable -- no round shopping);
  5. beacon value arrives (a mock round's value is UNDEFINED before its
     pulse time -- no early peeks even offline);
  6. seeds, reference draws, role permutations, tie-breaks derive as pure
     functions of (commit_hash, beacon_value, purpose, index);
  7. evidence blocks become computable; every EVIDENCE_BLOCK must cite the
     candidate's attested beacon value or it is refused;
  8. admission verifies the entire chain.
FAIL CLOSED everywhere: no anchor -> no beacon; no beacon -> no evidence;
no local randomness fallback exists in the codebase. Risk events are
anchored SYNCHRONOUSLY before acknowledgement, so no acknowledged purchase,
admission, halt, or reservation can be erased by truncation (H1) or
replaced within an anchor window (H2): restart refuses any log that fails
to reach EVERY externally anchored head.

  V3 the role permutation Pi is protocol-derived, uniform via domain-
     separated hash draws with exact rejection sampling;
  V4 tie handling is BEACON_UNIFORM or STRICT_TOP_ONLY, nothing else;
  V5 the betting rule is from the enumerated fairness-certified family
     (sum_r payout(r)/(m+1) <= 1), predictable, and CONTEXT-BLIND -- it
     may read past ranks and wealth, never the current block's contexts
     (a context-peeking rule defeats Ville while marginal uniformity
     holds: the deepest lemma-level attack found);
  V6 the 1/K purchase is irrevocable at registration, chain-ordered
     before the anchor (pay-on-admission = free lottery tickets,
     E[false] -> K x ALPHA_LIFE).

H0_LOCAL: under the committed beacon measure, the candidate's tie-broken
rank is uniform on {0..m} conditional on F and the prior blocks.
  LEMMA 1: the sharp null (delta-equivalent effects) implies exact
  uniformity for ANY evaluation function -- structure cancels through the
  role permutation and uniform ties.
  LEMMA 2: NO F-measurable adaptive selection can make a null-true
  candidate cross with probability > 1/K. Selection's only power is to
  make H0_LOCAL FALSE -- a TRUE constructed-null rejection whose
  scientific worth is artifact-arm jurisdiction, outside ALPHA_LIFE by
  adjudicated amendment 5. Stated, not hidden.
  LEMMA 3 (aggregation): lifetime bound by linearity under ARBITRARY
  cross-candidate dependence; shared beacon rounds concentrate failures
  into bursts; Markov survives; variance-based tail claims are forfeit.
  BLOCK-FILTRATION HONESTY: all blocks expand one pulse via a domain-
  separated XOF; per-block conditional uniformity is exact under ideal
  expansion and computational against the enumerated (context-blind)
  betting class otherwise; a per-block-round variant restores
  unconditional exactness at operational cost. Stated, not discovered

   Precondition: file access or crash timing after an acked purchase, no

   "torn tail"; verifier accepts shortened history. Invalid state: an
   acknowledged 1/K debit ceases to exist while its evidence escaped. Why

   co-signs) rewritable. Fix as X1 + every anchor (not merely the latest)

   lt=2^60 mass-expired every family and bricked future TTLs. Fix:
   MAX_LT_STEP bound; refusals never advance the clock. Regression: H3.
   Implementation integrity, not the expectation bound.
