--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0. THE VALIDATION CLAUSE (checkable by the refold verifier; claims below are
   asserted ONLY for histories satisfying all six)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  V1  R is the CANONICAL reference sampler (uniform PRF-index into the
      frozen operator ensemble), pinned at GENESIS. It is NEVER registrant-
      supplied: a registrant-committed R with correct support but a doctored
      law (reject-and-resample toward weak references) is bit-reproducible
      and empties the guarantee -- the ledger refuses any registration whose
      ref_rule differs from the canonical id.
  V2  W comes from the GENESIS-pinned canonical context-family whitelist;
      the admitted claim is scoped to W's pushforward context law. Bespoke
      context rules are refused (W-gerrymandering: cherry-picked contexts
      produce true-but-artifact rejections).
  V3  Pi is PROTOCOL-DERIVED: uniform on the (m+1)! permutations via
      domain-separated hash draws with exact rejection sampling. Never
      registrant-supplied. (The v1.0 implementation used LCG low bits and
      was measurably non-uniform -- caught by exact-enumeration
      qualification, preserved as a defect record, repaired.)
  V4  Tie handling is one of exactly two protocol values: BEACON_UNIFORM
      (exact) or STRICT_TOP_ONLY (conservative). "Candidate wins ties" plus
      a coarse evaluation function is a guaranteed-admission machine.
  V5  The betting rule comes from the protocol-ENUMERATED fairness-
      certified family: for every reachable state, sum over ranks r of
      payout(r, state)/(m+1) <= 1, bets nonnegative and bounded by current
      wealth, and the bet for block t is measurable w.r.t. G_{t-1} =
      (F, blocks 1..t-1's ranks and wealth) -- CONTEXT-BLIND: the bet may
      never read block t's context, references, roles, or values. A hash of
      arbitrary code is not a commitment to fairness; a context-peeking
      rule defeats Ville while the marginal rank law is perfectly uniform.
  V6  The 1/K purchase is IRREVOCABLE AT REGISTRATION, chain-ordered
      strictly before the anchor that fixes the beacon round. Pay-on-
      admission would make registrations free lottery tickets
      (E[false admissions] -> K x ALPHA_LIFE).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHY THIS DOCUMENT EXISTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The ledger theorem
    E[# false admissions] <= sum 1/K_c <= ALPHA_LIFE
is only as good as its per-candidate ingredient
    P( sup_t E_t >= K | F ) <= 1/K   under the candidate-local null,
which in turn requires the per-block rank law
    P( rank_t = r | F, G_{t-1} ) = 1/(m+1)   for r in {0..m}.
This document states exactly what is randomized, exactly what symmetry makes
the rank uniform, exactly what an adaptive adversary can and cannot do, and
exactly where the statement's power ends. "Exchangeability" is not invoked as
a vibe anywhere below.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE PRE-BEACON FILTRATION F
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F is the sigma-algebra (informally: the complete information set) available
to the registrant at the moment the registration commit is appended to the
ledger. F INCLUDES, at minimum:

  * the full frozen population snapshot available to any miner;
  * all preservation-stage data and every mu vector ever computed;
  * all organism metadata, lineage information, and world/seed history;
  * all candidate-generation and family-generation outputs;
  * all earlier candidate histories, including failed and successful
    calibration tests and their complete evidence;
  * every PREVIOUSLY revealed beacon value and every previously unsealed
    evidence block;
  * all matching/similarity covariates;
  * every adaptive decision made by any process (human, script, LLM) before
    the current registration -- including the decision to register THIS
    candidate, made for ANY reason, including having peeked at anything
    above.

The registrant is assumed to be F-omniscient and computationally unbounded
over F. In a deterministic substrate this is not paranoia; it is the literal
situation (every function of F is precomputable).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHAT IS COMMITTED AT REGISTRATION (all F-measurable, all immutable)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  delta*         the candidate perturbation (the hypothesis).
  R              the reference-generation rule: a deterministic function
                 (U, t) -> (delta_1..delta_m), drawing m references from the
                 FROZEN OPERATOR DISTRIBUTION. R is not free-form: it must
                 be an instance of a LEDGER-VALIDATED rule template whose
                 sampling measure is the operator distribution registered
                 for the family (rule-template hash checked at
                 registration). A registrant cannot commit a doctored R.
  W              the context-generation rule: (U, t) -> evidence context
                 (world, seeds, clone assignment), drawing from the
                 preregistered context space. Same template validation.
  Pi             the role-assignment rule: (U, t) -> a permutation of the
                 m+1 slots, uniform by construction of the template.
  tau            the tie-break rule: (U, t, slot) -> tie-break key, uniform
                 by construction of the template.
  V              the evaluation function (the sealed detector artifact
                 hash).
  K, m, block budget B, betting rule beta (predictable: the bet for block
                 t+1 may depend on blocks 1..t and on F, never on block
                 t+1's own data).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. THE BEACON ASSUMPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  U is the public beacon value of the round determined BY RULE from the
  external anchor of the registration commit (first valid pulse strictly
  after the anchor time).

  ASSUMPTION (cryptographic, not mathematical): U is independent of F --
  no F-measurable computation predicts U better than chance.

  This assumption is NOT proved here; it is enforced operationally by the
  seal protocol (external anchoring before the eligible round, rule-fixed
  round selection, no fallback randomness) and its failure modes are
  attacked in the G15 campaign. Every claim below is conditional on it.
  The empirical counterpart: the beacon-precomputation attack harness gives
  an F-omniscient attacker unlimited offline compute and measures whether
  they can identify future crossers better than the guessing rate.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. THE BLOCK CONSTRUCTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For block t = 1..B:
  context   C_t = W(U, t)                       (fresh, beacon-derived)
  refs      (delta_1..delta_m) = R(U, t)        (fresh operator draws)
  roles     pi_t = Pi(U, t)                     (uniform permutation)
  the m+1 deltas {delta*, delta_1..delta_m} are assigned to the m+1
  physical evaluation slots (seed streams, clone instances) of C_t by pi_t;
  values    y_j = V(C_t, slot j, assigned delta), j = 0..m
  rank      r_t = rank of the delta*-carrying value among all m+1 values,
            ordered by (value, tau(U, t, slot)) -- the beacon tie-break
            makes the order total, so r_t is always well-defined.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. THE NULL HYPOTHESIS, STATED WITHOUT HAND-WAVING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  H0_LOCAL(delta*, F):
    Under the committed beacon measure -- the push-forward of the uniform
    distribution of U through (W, R, Pi, tau) -- the conditional law of
    r_t given F and G_{t-1} (the sigma-algebra of blocks 1..t-1) is
    uniform on {0..m}, for every t.

  This is a DEFINITION of the null, and that is deliberate. It is the exact
  randomization-inference analogue of "the treatment label is arbitrary":
  the null asserts that the candidate perturbation is not distinguishable
  from fresh operator draws BY THE COMMITTED EVIDENCE-GENERATION PROCESS.
  What saves it from vacuity is the pair of theorems below: one gives a
  concrete, mechanism-level sufficient condition (the sharp null); the
  other shows the definition cannot be gamed from F.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. LEMMA 1 (SHARP NULL => EXACT UNIFORMITY, ANY EVALUATION FUNCTION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  SHARP NULL: on context C_t, replacing the delta assigned to any slot by
  any other member of {delta*, delta_1..delta_m} leaves that slot's value
  unchanged:   V(C_t, j, delta_a) = V(C_t, j, delta_b)  for all j, a, b.
  (The deltas are effect-equivalent on this context; slot values depend
  only on the slot.)

  CLAIM: under the sharp null, r_t is EXACTLY uniform on {0..m}
  conditional on F and G_{t-1}.

  PROOF: under the sharp null, y_j = g(j) for a fixed (F-measurable)
  function g. The delta*-carrying value is y at slot pi_t^{-1}(index of
  delta*), i.e. g evaluated at a slot chosen uniformly among the m+1 slots
  by pi_t (uniform, independent of F by the beacon assumption; independent
  of G_{t-1} because Pi(U, t) uses a domain-separated derivation per t).
  The total order on slots induced by (g, tau) is F x U-measurable but the
  POSITION of the candidate slot within that order is a uniform draw of
  one slot out of m+1 without replacement -- so each rank has probability
  exactly 1/(m+1). Ties in g are broken by tau, which is uniform and
  slot-symmetric, preserving exactness. QED.

  REMARK: the sharp null is what "capacity without use" and "inert dP"
  look like; Lemma 1 is why the dead-code calibration (exact ties, wealth
  1) behaves exactly. Lemma 1 makes NO assumption about V, the organism,
  its structure, or its history: pathological structure enters g and
  cancels.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. LEMMA 2 (NO F-MEASURABLE SELECTION BREAKS THE BOUND)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  CLAIM: for ANY F-measurable registration strategy (any adaptive choice
  of delta*, family, K, m, B, and validated rules R, W, Pi, tau, beta --
  including choices made after unlimited computation over F), IF
  H0_LOCAL(delta*, F) holds, THEN
      P( sup_{t<=B} E_t >= K | F ) <= 1/K,
  where E_t is the wealth process with the committed predictable betting
  rule beta on the tie-broken ranks.

  PROOF SHAPE: conditional on F, the rank sequence r_1..r_B is, under
  H0_LOCAL, a sequence whose conditional laws given the past are uniform
  (by the definition of H0_LOCAL, which conditions on G_{t-1}). A
  predictable, CONTEXT-BLIND (V5) betting rule on uniform ranks with
  fairness-certified payoffs makes E_t a nonnegative supermartingale with
  E_0 = 1 conditional on F. Ville's inequality applies conditionally:
  P(sup E >= K | F) <= 1/K. The F-measurability of every committed object
  means conditioning on F does not disturb the argument -- the ONLY
  randomness used is U, which is independent of F by assumption. QED.

  BLOCK-FILTRATION HONESTY (repaired after review): all per-block objects
  derive from ONE beacon pulse U via a domain-separated XOF, so blocks are
  statistically dependent given F in principle -- a committed rule could
  in theory infer the U-region from early ranks. The per-block conditional
  uniformity is therefore EXACT under ideal XOF expansion and
  COMPUTATIONAL against the enumerated betting-rule class otherwise. The
  enumerated family (V5) reads only ranks and wealth, closing the
  practical channel; a deployment wanting unconditional exactness may fix
  one fresh beacon round per block (round t = first pulse after block
  t-1's evidence is ledgered) at higher operational cost. Stated so
  nobody discovers it later.

  AGGREGATION CLAUSE (Lemma 3 restated for implementers): the lifetime
  guarantee is a SUM of per-candidate conditional bounds via linearity of
  expectation -- valid under ARBITRARY cross-candidate dependence,
  including many candidates sharing one beacon round. Shared rounds
  concentrate failures into bursts: Markov's P(any false admission) <=
  ALPHA_LIFE survives, but variance-based tail claims are forfeit and
  must never be made. The only independence used anywhere is each
  candidate's U from that candidate's own F.

  WHAT THIS MEANS OPERATIONALLY: adaptive selection cannot make a
  null-true candidate cross more often than 1/K. Selection has exactly one
  power: to pick candidates for which H0_LOCAL is FALSE. And if H0_LOCAL
  is false, the admission is a TRUE detection of the constructed null's
  complement: delta* genuinely behaves atypically, relative to fresh
  operator draws, on beacon-fresh contexts, reproducibly. Whether that
  atypicality is scientifically interesting or a boring consequence of how
  delta* was chosen is EXPLICITLY OUTSIDE this lemma and outside
  ALPHA_LIFE's scope (adjudicated amendment 5); it is the jurisdiction of
  the artifact-control arm.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. LEMMA 3 (MULTI-CANDIDATE ACCOUNTING UNDER SHARED BEACONS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Candidates registered before the same beacon round share U; their wealth
  processes may be arbitrarily DEPENDENT. The lifetime guarantee needs
  only:
      E[# false admissions] = sum_c P(E_c crosses K_c | H0_c) <= sum 1/K_c
  which uses LINEARITY OF EXPECTATION and the per-candidate bound of
  Lemma 2 -- no independence anywhere. Dependence can make false
  admissions arrive together; it cannot make them arrive more often in
  expectation. The Markov corollary P(any false admission) <= ALPHA_LIFE
  likewise survives arbitrary dependence.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. TIES, EXACTLY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Primary rule: beacon-uniform tie-break tau (section 5) -- ranks remain
  EXACTLY uniform under H0_LOCAL, including the all-tied (sharp-null,
  inert-delta) case.
  Conservative fallback (for any deployment where tau is unavailable):
  bet only on STRICT top rank; a tie is scored as a loss. This can only
  reduce wealth, so the crossing bound is preserved a fortiori.
  FORBIDDEN: any data-dependent tie handling chosen after seeing values.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. WHAT THE LEMMA DOES NOT SAY (the honest boundary)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  N1  It does not certify the BEACON. U independent of F is an assumption
      discharged operationally (seal protocol) and attacked empirically
      (G15 precomputation harness). If the beacon is predictable, every
      bound above is void.
  N2  It does not certify R, W, Pi, tau AS IMPLEMENTED. The lemma requires
      them to be instances of validated templates whose measures are what
      the template says (uniform permutation, operator-distribution
      draws). A doctored R (references drawn weak) makes H0_LOCAL false
      for boring reasons and its admissions artifact-class; template
      validation at registration is the ledger's job and is attacked in
      G15.
  N3  It does not bound artifact admissions. A registrant who selects
      delta* to be beacon-average-atypical (e.g. an operator-distribution
      outlier chosen by peeking at preservation data) earns a TRUE
      constructed-null rejection. The ledger charges it, the record is
      real, and its scientific worth is adjudicated elsewhere.
  N4  It does not apply to any evidence not generated through the
      committed (W, R, Pi, tau) path. Preservation-stage statistics,
      calibration runs, and diagnostic probes have NO rank-law protection.
  N5  The betting rule must be PREDICTABLE. A bet that reads block t's
      data before betting on block t voids Lemma 2. The registration
      commit pins beta; the evaluation service refuses deviations.
