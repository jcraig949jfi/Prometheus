1. THE PREREGISTERED GUARANTEE (restated with its true scope)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  PRIMARY GUARANTEE. Under the CANDIDATE-LOCAL CONSTRUCTED NULL -- the
  candidate contrast exchangeable with its matched references GIVEN the full
  frozen snapshot and all pre-registration data -- the expected number of
  false ADMITTED_MICROSTRUCTURE records over the instrument lifetime is
  <= ALPHA_LIFE (proposed 0.1; adjudicable).

  COROLLARY (stated to preempt the strongest external objection): since
  ALPHA_LIFE < 1, Markov gives P(ANY false admission over the lifetime)
  <= ALPHA_LIFE -- the expected-count guarantee IS a lifetime familywise
  guarantee at the same level, under arbitrary dependence and unbounded
  adaptive registration. FDR was considered and REJECTED: with a
  one-particle deliverable the denominator is ~1, FDR degenerates to
  per-comparison error, and its denominator is gameable by admitting easy
  true positives.

  SCOPE LINE (red-team MAJOR, accepted): this guarantee bounds
  CONSTRUCTED-NULL VIOLATIONS ONLY. Admissions of structure that is real
  under the constructed null but is an ARTIFACT OF THE PIPELINE (cloning
  side-effects, snapshot transients, perturbation-ensemble asymmetries) are
  NOT bounded by any ledger. Therefore a mandatory ARTIFACT-CONTROL ARM
  runs sham perturbations and pipeline-only interventions through the
  identical clone/measure path on the scientific population, with a
  preregistered tripwire: any admission-strength signal in the sham arm
  HALTS ADMISSIONS pending review.

  WHY EXCHANGEABILITY HOLDS BY CONSTRUCTION, NOT BY MODELING (red-team
  FATAL, resolved): on evolved organisms the "complete Observatory null"
  never holds, and selection on preservation data searches for exactly the
  (organism, contrast) pairs where background structure leaks
  asymmetrically. The fix is randomization inference: AFTER the
  registration commit, post-commit beacon randomness assigns WHICH of the
  matched intervention slots plays the candidate role on each block. Under
  the local null, rank uniformity then holds for ANY background structure,
  because uniformity comes from experimenter-controlled randomness rather
  than from assumptions about the organism. Verified against structured-
  base and basis-cancelling plants before detector freeze; a detector that
  cannot pass this on structured-base plants is not exposed.

2. THE ADMISSION-RIGHTS LEDGER (G9a + G9b; implemented; demonstrated)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  MECHANICS (mhc/ledger.py; demonstration mhc/LEDGER_DEMO_RESULTS.txt):
    * global wealth = ALPHA_LIFE, in admission-risk units;
    * G9a: a family RESERVES at instantiation (any origin, LLM mining
      included). Reservations are an ALLOCATION DEVICE -- the guarantee
      counts only purchases, so releasing UNSPENT reservation on closure or
      TTL expiry is provably safe under arbitrary adaptivity. Enforced:
      reservation cap (<= 25% of remaining without adjudicator
      co-signature), mandatory TTL, release-on-close.
    * G9b: a candidate PURCHASES threshold K at price 1/K at REGISTRATION,
      jointly committing (K, block budget, m references, betting rule).
      Enforced: K floor (K_MIN = 100 -- one careless cheap-threshold
      purchase must not drain the lifetime budget), K reachability
      (K <= max-multiplier^block-budget -- an unwinnable test is not a
      test), single-purchase cap (<= half the family's original
      reservation), K immutability.
    * ADMISSION iff anytime wealth E_c >= K_c. NO RECREDITING of purchases
      ever (refunding losers = infinite retry at pay-only-if-you-win, which
      unbounds the expectation). Releasing reservations != refunding
      purchases; the two are separated in the accounting identity.
    BOUND: E[#false] = sum P(E_c >= K_c | local H0) <= sum 1/K_c <=
    ALPHA_LIFE. Linearity of expectation: survives arbitrary dependence,
    unbounded counts, adaptive family generation. Perfectly correlated
    variant spam gains nothing (all-or-none crossing at P <= 1/K);
    candidate definitions are canonicalized at registration and duplicates
    merged, purely for audit hygiene.

  THE TWO PRECONDITIONS, AND HOW THEY ARE ENFORCED:
    P1 EVIDENCE UNDERIVABLE AT REGISTRATION. Procedural ordering is NOT
       sealing in a deterministic substrate: an adversary precomputes every
       E_c offline before registering, then registers only observed
       crossers (red-team FATAL; the ~200-fold violation is demonstrated
       numerically in the ledger demo: 626 false admissions for 3.1 units
       of would-be spend across 100,000 simulated null candidates).
       THEREFORE SEALING IS CRYPTOGRAPHIC: admission-block seeds, reference
       assignments, and candidate/reference role permutations derive from
       PUBLIC BEACON randomness occurring AFTER the registration commit;
       admission is invalid unless seed provenance verifies. The ledger
       refuses evidence lacking a post-registration beacon attestation.
    P2 ONE-TIME-USE BLOCKS. Cross-candidate evidence reuse launders peeking
       through blocks unsealed for earlier candidates (red-team FATAL).
       A block is bound to exactly one registration; anything ever unsealed
       is permanently contaminated for later registrations. Free once
       beacon-derived per-registration seeds exist; enforced as a ledger
       invariant regardless.

  CAPACITY, STATED PLAINLY: ALPHA_LIFE = 0.1 at K = 200 funds at most 20
  admission attempts over the instrument lifetime (demo D1 spends the
  budget exactly). Attempts are precious BY DESIGN. If the adjudicator
  wants more capacity, raise ALPHA_LIFE explicitly -- never quietly.

3. M1 -- THE B1 PROGRAM (structured-base power)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THE ESTIMAND MOVES, per adjudication, from absolute D(k) to CHANGES IN A
  STRUCTURED ORGANISM'S CAUSAL FINGERPRINT. The red team then found the
  natural null for fingerprint CHANGE re-imports the wrong-null problem one
  level up (any structured organism's fingerprint drifts under any
  perturbation -- fragility of structure). RESOLUTION, now normative:

    THE FINGERPRINT-CHANGE NULL IS PERTURBATION-EXCHANGEABILITY ON CLONES
    OF THE SAME ORGANISM. Preregister an ensemble {P0..Pm} of perturbations
    exchangeable with the candidate perturbation under H0; apply each to an
    independent clone of the SAME organism; rank the candidate
    perturbation's fingerprint change within the ensemble's.
    Organism-vs-organism comparisons are BANNED as null material.

  REFERENCE MECHANISMS must be CONSTRUCTION-EXCHANGEABLE (permutation /
  relabel / re-clone symmetries provable a priori), each with a written
  exchangeability proof in the sealed detector record. Similarity-based
  "matching" is demoted to a variance-reduction covariate applied
  identically to candidate and references (exchangeability then survives
  arbitrary misestimation). Empirical rank uniformity on the calibration
  beam is a smoke test, NEVER the justification.

  THE (a)/(b)/(c) DECOMPOSITION (background / directional / interaction) is
  preregistered strictly as a VARIANCE-REDUCTION DEVICE: the interaction
  term is not identifiable without a homogeneity assumption the program
  rejects, so ADMITTED records are forbidden from asserting which variance
  component carried an effect. All scientific claims are stated only in
  terms of the exact rank/wealth statistic.

4. CALIBRATION-BEAM DISCIPLINE (the collider rule, hardened)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Calibration beam first, unknown beam second. Plus red-team hardening:

  * FOUNDER-LINEAGE TUNING SPLIT (was FATAL: clone-level splits are the
    tuning set with different noise). Any lineage touched by ANY tuning
    iteration is permanently tuning-only. Power is reported solely on
    lineages first opened AFTER the detector-hash commit, with the count of
    tuning iterations logged to the ledger before reporting lineages
    unseal.
  * LOAD-BEARING DISJOINTNESS ONLY (was MAJOR: seed/world/time disjointness
    is theater). Mandatory: no shared founder lineages between calibration
    and scientific populations (otherwise a calibration anomaly is
    trivially "re-discovered" and the quarantine rule is void). Mandatory
    for power claims: at least one grammar family held out of ALL
    calibration and tuning, with frozen-detector transfer power measured on
    it before scientific exposure. Calibration-grammar power is quoted only
    as an UPPER BOUND for the scientific population. Seed/world/time
    disjointness may be listed but is labeled non-load-bearing.
  * NO CALIBRATION ANOMALY BECOMES A SCIENTIFIC CANDIDATE. Ever.
  * ONE CLONING/SNAPSHOT PROCEDURE, hash-committed in the seal, used
    verbatim in calibration and science; any divergence forces
    recalibration on a sacrificial disjoint lineage before admissions
    resume.
  * THE FREEZE COVERS THE COMPLETE INFERENCE ARTIFACT: code + weights +
    templates + resolved configuration + m, as one sealed object; the
    admission pipeline must be a pure function of (sealed artifact,
    registered candidate record, block data); data-dependent normalization
    is computed within-block and applied identically to candidate and
    references.

5. M3 -- ADVERSARIAL PLANT PROGRAM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  CLASSES: structured-base; sign-reversing; late-onset (the class v0's
  sealed holdout proved blind to); basis-cancelling (B10); sandbox-evolved;
  plus at least one class NEVER INSTANTIATED during calibration or tuning,
  whose detection rate is reported separately as the out-of-class
  robustness figure.

  THE INDEPENDENCE PARADOX, RESOLVED MECHANICALLY (was MAJOR: a
  basis-cancelling plant author must know the basis; a blind author cancels
  a guessed basis and the certification is theater):
    SPLIT KNOWLEDGE. The plant author commits a PARAMETRIC GENERATOR with
    the cancellation/base direction as a free input. The adjudication seat
    commits a NEUTRAL COMPOSITION SCRIPT. After both commits, the script
    computes the direction from the sealed family definition and
    instantiates plants with post-commit public randomness. No human holds
    both halves; the ledger holds the composition. Sign-reversing and
    late-onset plants, which attack timing/sign conventions, remain fully
    blind-authored.
  SANDBOX-EVOLVED PLANTS (was MAJOR: surrogate fitness = strawman, raw
  detector access = collusion): fitness queries go to an ISOLATED
  EVALUATION SERVICE running the committed detector artifact; the author
  sees scalar scores only; a preregistered query budget is enforced and
  every query hash is ledgered before science exposure -- residual leakage
  bounded and auditable rather than denied.

6. M4 -- EXTERNAL SEAL PROTOCOL v1.1 (four FATALs closed)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  S1 COMMIT THE EXECUTION CLOSURE, NOT A SCRIPT HASH: container/image
     digest covering code + dependencies + pinned platform. INDEPENDENT
     BIT-EXACT RE-DERIVATION IS A PROTOCOL GATE: the adjudication seat (or
     any third party) recomputes plant parameters from (bundle, beacon) and
     must match exactly BEFORE any calibration verdict is recorded;
     mismatch voids the run on the ledger.
  S2 THE BEACON ROUND IS FIXED BY RULE, NOT DESIGNATION: "first beacon
     pulse strictly after the commit's external anchor time." One active
     commit per campaign; every commit is binding; an abandoned commit is a
     publicly recorded failed attempt, never a silently dropped draft.
     (Grinding via commit spam or round selection is thereby closed.)
  S3 THE LEDGER HEAD IS ANCHORED EXTERNALLY before the eligible round
     (independent timestamping / public chain / the beacon operator), so an
     insider cannot backdate a commit after seeing the beacon. "Everyone
     can reconstruct later" verifies the derivation; the anchor verifies
     the ORDERING.
  S4 NO UNCOMMITTED DEGREES OF FREEDOM: plant_params = f(committed bundle,
     beacon value) and NOTHING else. The seat re-executes in an empty
     environment; any read of an uncommitted input (file, env, network,
     clock) detected during re-derivation voids the run. All knobs are
     literals in the bundle or beacon-derived.
  S5 ABORTS ARE ADJUDICATED by the independent seat BEFORE parameters
     unseal to the detector side; burned parameters are never reused; a
     preregistered attempt budget per campaign; any detector modification
     after any attempt requires fresh commits and a fresh round, with the
     diff against failed-plant regions disclosed to the adjudicator.

7. M5 -- STERILITY AND MULTI-HOST (observe live, intervene on clones)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * PRE-PARTITIONED SEED SPACE (was FATAL: a shared RNG allocator means
    every clone spawn shifts the live world's future draws -- data-driven
    cloning becomes an intervention on the scientific target). Each live
    world receives its reserved seed block at creation; clones draw from a
    disjoint reserved namespace. VERIFICATION: replay a live world with and
    without concurrent clone activity; demand bit-identical state hashes.
  * RESOURCE ISOLATION beyond state hashes (was MAJOR: censored telemetry
    correlates with clone activity while state checksums pass): dedicated
    clone hosts or hard CPU/IO reservations for the live recording path;
    separate artifact store for clone output; scheduled NO-CLONE CONTROL
    WINDOWS comparing trace-completeness, drop counters, and checkpoint
    latencies against clone-active windows.
  * READS GO TO REPLICAS (was MAJOR: "passive" observation consumes I/O on
    evolution hosts, and interest-driven read scheduling censors exactly
    the episodes under study): live traces are write-once and shipped;
    analysis reads hit replicas only; anything touching live hosts is
    fixed-rate and preregistered; drop/backpressure counters are
    first-class instrument-health channels.
  * CLONE FROM THE CHECKPOINT STREAM ONLY, never the live process image
    (snapshotting live is itself an intervention at exactly the interesting
    moments).
  * SEPARATE STORES for admission-ledger writes and world event logs; all
    world-side ordering uses engine-issued logical timestamps, never ledger
    arrival order.

8. B10 RETENTION (retroactive-family replayability, hardened)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Derive-on-demand silently assumes indefinite bit-exact replay; engine
  upgrades and platform drift break it, and the moment they do, every
  archived encounter becomes unevaluable for any future observable family
  -- basis-cancelling blindness becomes PERMANENT for the historical
  population while the retention policy reports itself satisfied (red-team
  FATAL). THEREFORE:
    * RETENTION BUNDLE per encounter: all seeds (candidate AND every
      matched reference), the complete external input/event stream, the
      PINNED ENGINE BUILD ARTIFACT (image digest), and the null-
      construction metadata (matching, block boundaries, sealed/unsealed
      partition) -- without which a future family's per-block ranks are not
      uniform and its wealth process is invalid from record one.
    * REPLAY CANARY: on a schedule, replay sampled archived encounters and
      verify state hashes. ON FIRST DRIFT: immediately materialize full raw
      traces for the archive using the last-good engine, before the
      capability decays further.
