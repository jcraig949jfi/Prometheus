MICROSTRUCTURE OBSERVATORY -- V0 OBSERVABLE FAMILY (FROZEN)
============================================================
Freeze discipline (directive section 18): this family is frozen BEFORE any
input from the external historical survey. The content hash of THIS FILE is
the observable_family_hash carried by every weak-signal record. Changing this
file is a NEW FAMILY VERSION; records across versions are never pooled.

Sources permitted in constructing this family: the directive's own candidate
list (section 6), vetted on measurement/statistical grounds only; the
substrate's mechanical properties (deterministic replay, integer traces,
paired twins). Sources excluded: the historical ALife/OEE survey (assigned to
another seat), cognitive-science taxonomies, any semantic notion of reasoning.

V0 COORDINATES OF THE MICROSTRUCTURE VECTOR mu
------------------------------------------------------------
mu1  PAIRED_OUTCOME_DELTA
     Per-twin difference in raw outcome channels: final charge, ticks alive,
     yield events, actions used, abstention ticks. Block-aggregated.

mu2  INTERVENTIONAL_CAUSAL_DEPTH_PROFILE  D(k), k in {1,2,4,6,8,12}
     Replay-based intervention: flip one seeded-chosen observation element at
     tick t-k, replay the remainder deterministically, record normalized
     action-stream divergence over the window [t, t+W). Marginalized over
     seeded t-samples. Measures USE of history, not capacity: an unused
     buffer contributes exactly zero. Summary coordinates: D_max_lag (largest
     k with D(k) above the divergence floor), D_area (sum over profile).

mu3  RELEVANT_IRRELEVANT_SEPARATION  S = D_rel - D_irr
     Same intervention, restricted to observation channels that causally feed
     the world's yield predicate (relevant) vs channels that do not
     (irrelevant), determined WORLD-SIDE from the mechanics DAG and never
     visible to players. A pure history-echo shows S ~ 0; differential
     response shows S != 0. Serves double duty as the falsifier for
     "causal depth fooled by recurrence".

mu4  TRAJECTORY_STRUCTURE_CONTRAST
     Held-out hit-rate of a frozen order-2 integer context model on the
     action stream MINUS the same statistic on a seeded within-player
     shuffled/rotated control stream. Integer-exact (hit counts). Constants
     and pure noise both give contrast ~ 0 (constants: control predicts
     equally well; noise: neither predicts).

mu5  TWIN_BEHAVIORAL_DIVERGENCE
     Normalized Hamming distance between twin action streams, and its onset
     tick. Scheme versioned. (Coherence of this divergence across blocks --
     not its magnitude -- is what the null adjudicates.)

mu6  RESOURCE_ALLOCATION_DELTA
     Per-twin difference in steps-used, action-magnitude distribution
     (bucketed), abstention structure. Separates "does something different"
     from "spends differently".

DELIBERATELY EXCLUDED FROM V0 (recorded for the packet's rejected list):
  - raw trajectory entropy (rewards noise; replaced by mu4 contrast)
  - observational mutual-information history estimators (confounded by
    recurrence; replaced by interventional mu2)
  - any aggregate/composite score over mu (forbidden by design)
  - adaptation savings (requires a learning-budget protocol; deferred to the
    family version that accompanies checkpointed players)
  - predictive-information / compressibility via general-purpose compressors
    (compressor-version dependence; non-reproducible across hosts)

NULL FAMILY (frozen with the observables)
------------------------------------------------------------
N-A  BLOCK SIGN-FLIP: exchangeable unit = (world, seed-block) twin aggregate;
     signs flipped at block level; preserves all within-block dependence.
N-B  ROBUST AGGREGATION: block aggregates enter as signs / ranks, never raw
     magnitudes (chaotic blocks must not dominate stable ones).
N-C  LINEAGE-BLOCK PERMUTATION for genealogical trend tests: generation
     labels permuted within lineage; trend statistic = directional
     consistency of per-generation mu deltas.
N-D  E-PROCESS LUMINOSITY: investigation-stage evidence accumulates by
     betting on block-aggregate direction (binomial e-process on signs with
     a preregistered success-probability grid); anytime-valid; optional
     stopping safe.
N-E  MATCHED CONTROL TWIN: every plant/candidate statistic is computed
     against a sibling control twin-set constructed identically except for
     the hypothesized delta.

THRESHOLDS (frozen)
------------------------------------------------------------
PRESERVATION   storage act; everything preregistered and computed is kept.
FLAG           permissive: uncorrected block sign-test p < 0.10 on any single
               mu coordinate, or >= 70% directional consistency across >= 10
               blocks. Marks UNRESOLVED_WEAK_SIGNAL. Zero consequences.
INVESTIGATION  lottery/FIFO admission within a fixed budget slice; hypothesis
               frozen at entry; luminosity-only response to weakness.
ADMISSION      preregistered direction reproduced on held-out blocks with
               e-value >= 20 under N-D; invariance profile STABLE on >= 3
               preregistered axes; pseudo-signal battery cleared;
               alpha-investing ledger charged for the admission family.
