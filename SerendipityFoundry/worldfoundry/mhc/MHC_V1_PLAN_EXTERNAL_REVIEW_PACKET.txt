3. THE ADMISSION-RIGHTS LEDGER (G9a + G9b -- implemented)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * The global ledger opens with wealth ALPHA_LIFE, in admission-risk units.
  * G9a (GLOBAL FAMILY MULTIPLICITY). A hypothesis family RESERVES wealth
    at instantiation, whatever its origin -- human, miner, or LLM. The
    family PROCESS is priced at creation: unbounded family generation is
    structurally impossible because wealth is finite and debited up front.
    Enforced: reservation cap (<= 25% of remaining global wealth without
    adjudicator co-signature -- no land grabs), mandatory TTL, and
    release-of-unspent-reservation on closure/expiry. The release is
    PROVABLY SAFE under arbitrary adaptivity because the guarantee counts
    only PURCHASES (below), never reservations.
  * G9b (WITHIN-FAMILY CANDIDATE MULTIPLICITY). A candidate admission test
    PURCHASES a threshold K at price 1/K at REGISTRATION, jointly and
    immutably committing (K, block budget, m references, betting rule).
    No candidate holds an independent alpha entitlement because its family
    paid an entrance fee: every test is individually priced. Enforced:
    K floor (K_MIN = 100 -- a single cheap-threshold purchase cannot drain
    the budget), K reachability ceiling (K <= max-multiplier^block-budget:
    an unwinnable test is not a test), single-purchase cap (<= half the
    family's original reservation), K immutability.
  * ADMISSION iff the candidate's anytime wealth ever reaches K on its
    admission evidence. PURCHASES ARE NEVER REFUNDED: refunding losers
    reintroduces infinite retry at pay-only-if-you-win, which unbounds the
    expected false-admission count. (Releasing unspent RESERVATIONS is a
    different accounting identity, and is safe.)

  THE BOUND AND ITS PROOF SHAPE:
    E[#false admissions] = sum_c P(E_c >= K_c | local H0_c)
                        <= sum_c 1/K_c          (Ville, per candidate)
                        <= ALPHA_LIFE            (ledger conservation)
  Linearity of expectation makes this valid under ARBITRARY dependence
  between candidates (shared worlds, shared founders, correlated
  evidence), unbounded candidate and family counts, and adaptive
  data-driven family generation. Perfectly correlated variant spam gains
  nothing: all-or-none crossing at P <= 1/K leaves both the expected count
  and P(any) unchanged. Candidate definitions are canonicalized at
  registration and duplicates merged, purely for audit hygiene.

  CAPACITY, STATED PLAINLY: ALPHA_LIFE = 0.1 at K = 200 funds AT MOST 20
  admission attempts over the instrument lifetime. Attempts are precious
  BY DESIGN: preservation is free storage, flagging is free annotation,
  investigation is rationed compute -- and admission tests are rare,
  priced events. A program whose deliverable is one particle should feel
  this. More capacity requires raising ALPHA_LIFE explicitly, never
  quietly.

4. WHY PROCEDURAL SEALING FAILS -- THE DEMONSTRATED EXPLOIT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The draft's precondition was procedural: "K is fixed at registration,
before admission evidence is unsealed." The red team's deepest finding:

  IN A DETERMINISTIC SUBSTRATE, NOTHING IS SEALED BY ORDERING. The
  evidence is a computable function, not a random variable that comes
  into existence at unsealing. An adversary (or an LLM miner, or an
  honest team with a script) privately re-derives the admission blocks
  for a million candidate definitions BEFORE registering anything,
  computes every wealth trajectory offline, then registers ONLY the
  observed crossers with K set just below the observed supremum. The
  ledger sees a perfectly compliant order of operations.

DEMONSTRATED NUMERICALLY (mhc/ledger_demo.py, deterministic, reproducible):
  100,000 simulated null candidates, 64 blocks each, exact field
  arithmetic (fair conditional ranks, m=3, mixture betting):
    - honest use: 20 candidates registered at K=200 BEFORE evidence
      generation -> ledger bound 0.100 spent exactly -> realized false
      admissions: 0.
    - the exploit: 626 null candidates crossed wealth 100 somewhere in
      their trajectory; buying each post hoc at K = observed peak costs
      3.12 units -> 626 FALSE ADMISSIONS FOR 3.12 UNITS OF SPEND: THE
      EXPECTATION BOUND IS VIOLATED ~200-FOLD.

THE FIX IS CRYPTOGRAPHIC, NOT PROCEDURAL (and it unifies with the plant-
sealing protocol the adjudicator already imposed):
  * Registration commits (candidate definition, family, K, block budget,
    m, betting rule) to the immutable ledger.
  * The admission blocks' SEEDS, REFERENCE ASSIGNMENTS, AND CANDIDATE/
    REFERENCE ROLE PERMUTATIONS are then derived deterministically from
    PUBLIC BEACON RANDOMNESS occurring AFTER the commit. No one can
    precompute a wealth trajectory at registration time; anyone can verify
    the derivation later. The ledger refuses evidence lacking a
    post-registration beacon attestation.
  * ONE-TIME-USE BLOCKS: a block is bound to exactly one registration;
    anything ever unsealed is permanently contaminated for later
    registrations. This closes the second FATAL -- laundering the peek
    through blocks unsealed for EARLIER candidates.
  * THE ROLE PERMUTATION also closes the third FATAL: on evolved organisms
    the "complete null" never holds, and selection on preservation data
    searches for exactly the (organism, contrast) pairs where background
    structure leaks asymmetrically into candidate-vs-reference. Beacon-
    randomized role assignment makes rank uniformity hold under the LOCAL
    null BY RANDOMIZATION -- experimenter-controlled randomness, no
    assumptions about the organism -- for any background structure.

MECHANICAL REFUSALS, ALL DEMONSTRATED (LEDGER_DEMO_RESULTS.txt):
  [OK] family land-grab (>25% remaining, no co-sign) refused

  [OK] K below preregistered floor refused

5. M1 -- THE B1 PROGRAM (structured-base power)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
V0's headline blindness: near-zero power for micro-effects on organisms with
genuine intrinsic structure. The adjudicated estimand moves from absolute
causal depth to CHANGES IN A STRUCTURED ORGANISM'S CAUSAL FINGERPRINT. The
red team then showed the naive null for fingerprint CHANGE re-imports the
wrong-null problem one level up: any structured organism's fingerprint
drifts under ANY perturbation, so organism-vs-organism comparison detects
"is structured", not microstructure -- fragility of structure. RESOLUTION,
now normative:

  THE FINGERPRINT-CHANGE NULL IS PERTURBATION-EXCHANGEABILITY ON CLONES OF
  THE SAME ORGANISM. Preregister an ensemble {P0..Pm} of perturbations
  exchangeable with the candidate perturbation under H0; apply each to an
  independent clone of the SAME organism; rank the candidate
  perturbation's fingerprint change within the ensemble's changes.
  Structure-level differences between organisms cancel by construction.
  ORGANISM-VS-ORGANISM COMPARISONS ARE BANNED AS NULL MATERIAL.

Further hardening:
  * Reference mechanisms must be CONSTRUCTION-EXCHANGEABLE (permutation /
    relabel / re-clone symmetries provable a priori), each with a written
    exchangeability proof in the sealed detector record. Similarity-based
    "matching" is demoted to a variance-reduction covariate applied
    identically to candidate and references, so exchangeability survives
    arbitrary misestimation. Empirical uniformity on the calibration beam
    is a smoke test, never the justification.
  * The background/directional/interaction decomposition is preregistered
    strictly as a VARIANCE-REDUCTION DEVICE (the interaction term is not
    identifiable without a homogeneity assumption the program rejects);
    ADMITTED records are forbidden from asserting which variance component
    carried an effect.

6. CALIBRATION-BEAM DISCIPLINE (collider rule, hardened)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Calibration beam first, unknown beam second. V1 runs on CLONED, QUARANTINED
EVOLVED ORGANISMS as calibration substrates; NO anomaly discovered during
calibration is ever eligible to become a scientific candidate. Hardening:

  * FOUNDER-LINEAGE TUNING SPLIT (was FATAL): clones of one organism are
    the tuning set with different noise, so clone/seed/world-level splits
    are void. Any lineage touched by ANY tuning iteration is permanently
    tuning-only; power is reported solely on lineages first opened AFTER
    the detector-hash commit, with the tuning-iteration count ledgered
    before reporting lineages unseal.
  * ONLY TWO DISJOINTNESSES ARE LOAD-BEARING (was MAJOR): (a) no shared
    founder lineages between calibration and scientific populations --
    otherwise a calibration anomaly is trivially "re-discovered" and the
    quarantine rule is void; (b) at least one grammar family held out of
    ALL calibration and tuning, with frozen-detector transfer power
    measured on it before scientific exposure. Calibration-grammar power
    is quoted only as an UPPER BOUND. Seed/world/time disjointness is
    labeled non-load-bearing (theater) rather than counted.
  * THE FREEZE COVERS THE COMPLETE INFERENCE ARTIFACT: code + weights +
    templates + resolved configuration + m, one sealed object; the
    admission pipeline is a pure function of (sealed artifact, registered
    candidate record, block data).
  * ONE CLONING/SNAPSHOT PROCEDURE, hash-committed, used verbatim in both
    phases; divergence forces recalibration on a sacrificial disjoint
    lineage before admissions resume.

9. M5 -- STERILITY AND MULTI-HOST (observe live, intervene on clones)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * PRE-PARTITIONED SEED SPACE (was FATAL): with a shared RNG allocator,
    every clone spawn advances the allocator and shifts every subsequent
    live-world draw -- data-driven cloning becomes an intervention on the
    scientific target that no per-clone audit can see. Each live world
    receives its reserved seed block at creation; clones draw from a
    disjoint namespace. VERIFICATION: replay a live world with and without
    concurrent clone activity; demand bit-identical state hashes.
  * RESOURCE ISOLATION BEYOND STATE HASHES (was MAJOR): clone I/O load can
    censor live telemetry while state checksums pass, and the censoring
    correlates with candidate interest. Dedicated clone hosts or hard
    CPU/IO reservations for the live recording path; separate artifact
    store; scheduled NO-CLONE CONTROL WINDOWS comparing trace-completeness
    statistics, drop counters, and checkpoint latencies.
  * READS GO TO REPLICAS (was MAJOR): "passive" observation consumes I/O
    on evolution hosts, and interest-driven read scheduling censors
    exactly the interesting episodes. Live traces are write-once and
    shipped; analysis reads hit replicas only; anything touching live
    hosts is fixed-rate and preregistered.
  * CLONE FROM THE CHECKPOINT STREAM ONLY, never the live process image.
  * SEPARATE STORES for admission-ledger and world event logs; world-side
    ordering uses engine-issued logical timestamps, never ledger arrival
    order.

10. B10 RETENTION (retroactive replayability, hardened)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B10 (basis-cancelling microstructure -- real effects whose components cancel
inside today's coordinates) makes retroactive re-analysis by FUTURE
observable families a core requirement. Derive-on-demand storage silently
assumes indefinite bit-exact replay; engine upgrades and platform drift
break it, at which point basis-cancelling blindness becomes PERMANENT for
the historical population while the retention policy reports itself
satisfied (was FATAL). THEREFORE:
  * RETENTION BUNDLE per encounter: all seeds (candidate AND every matched
    reference), the complete external input/event stream, the PINNED
    ENGINE BUILD ARTIFACT (image digest), and the null-construction
    metadata (matching, block boundaries, sealed/unsealed partition) --
    without which a future family's per-block ranks are not uniform and
    its wealth process is invalid from record one.
  * REPLAY CANARY: scheduled replay of sampled archives with state-hash
    verification. ON FIRST DRIFT: immediately materialize full raw traces
    using the last-good engine, before the capability decays further.
