1. THE PERMANENT DETECTOR CONTRACT (estimand, fixed)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  MHC DETECTS CANDIDATE-SPECIFIC DEPARTURES FROM THE RESPONSE DISTRIBUTION
  INDUCED BY THE FROZEN PERTURBATION OPERATOR.

  It intentionally CANNOT detect structure generic to the perturbation
  operator itself. That is the design, not a defect: conditioning away
  operator-generic effects is what prevents the instrument from perpetually
  rediscovering that mutating functional systems tends to hurt them.
  COROLLARY (binding on all future claims): the MHC must never be described
  as detecting "all weak structure". If the operator-generic question ever
  matters, BUILD ANOTHER INSTRUMENT; do not contaminate this null.

3. BLINDNESS REGISTER AMENDMENT (v0 packet unmodified)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  B10 BASIS-CANCELLING MICROSTRUCTURE (added). A real effect may produce
      almost no marginal change in any frozen coordinate because positive
      and negative components cancel INSIDE the observable -- within one
      encounter or one block, distinct from world-family sign reversal
      (B4). Example: half of an organism's state pathways acquire deeper
      dependence while the other half become shallower; total D(k) barely
      moves; the architecture changed. CONSEQUENCE: raw/reconstructable
      microstructure must survive even when today's coordinate vector reads
      zero -- a future observable family must be replayable over old
      encounters. This is a core PEW justification.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. B1 REINTERPRETED (guidance binding on C1)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Do not simply raise the reference count until B1 goes away. On a flat
  organism the question is "did this perturbation cause structured
  behavior?"; on a structured organism it is "did this perturbation cause
  NEW structure distinguishable from existing endogenous structure?" --
  a different statistical problem. C1 investigates the GEOMETRY OF THE
  NOISE and explicitly decomposes:
      background intrinsic variation
    | perturbation-induced directional variation
    | candidate x perturbation interaction
  The long-run shape is less "detect absolute D(k)" and more "detect
  changes in a structured organism's CAUSAL FINGERPRINT".

5. THE AUTHORIZED WORKFLOW LOOP (recorded for the program charter)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Proteus  -> enormous genotype variation without knowing what succeeds
  SFE      -> collisions and immutable phenotype exhaust
  MHC      -> deviations from calibrated nulls, usefulness not asked
  PEW      -> retains unresolved deviations; future reinterpretation
  new detector family -> "did this pattern already happen 400,000 times in
                         organisms everyone thought were dead?"
  SFE      -> prospectively recreates the claimed phenomenon
  The last step is what keeps retrospective mining from becoming mythology.
  The search target is not an organism that suddenly reasons; it is
  machinery capable of discovering that something extremely boring happened
  slightly too often.
