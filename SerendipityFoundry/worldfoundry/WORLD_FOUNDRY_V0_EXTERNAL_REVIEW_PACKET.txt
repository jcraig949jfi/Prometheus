0. ORIENTATION AND ROLE SEPARATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Incubator is a distributed evolutionary experiment with strict role
separation, which this design never collapses:

    PROTEUS    player foundry      -> frozen player manifests
    DAEDALUS   world foundry       -> frozen world manifests
    HARMONIA   matchmaker/operator -> schedules frozen encounters on M1-M4
    SFE        engine              -> authoritative immutable experiment record
    MNEMOSYNE  PEW/Tensor          -> qualified, evolving knowledge about it

SFE is a qualified research runtime: hash-chained per-world ledgers,
content-addressed artifacts, idempotency keys, lease-fenced work claims,
lineage-scoped budgets, and an irreversible experiment-commit boundary that
closes a prospective-prediction window BEFORE execution. The fabric below reuses
that machinery rather than reinventing it.

SCIENTIFIC OBJECTIVE. Not player fitness. The question is whether the joint
PLAYER x WORLD x ECOLOGY landscape contains REPRODUCIBLE NON-FLAT REGIONS.
Expected workload: millions of cheap failures. Target: ONE bump that survives a
prospective falsification crucible. Build a seismograph, not a leaderboard.

THE THREE MOST IMPORTANT THINGS AN EXTERNAL REVIEWER SHOULD SCRUTINIZE:

  (1) R13 -- WHAT A BUMP IS. The draft's bump ("flagged, then confirmed") was
      not well-posed: the crucible tests internal validity only, so a
      grammar-baked tautology (a world family whose cost primitives punish
      constants) would flag, replay, transfer, and confirm -- and the program
      would declare success having learned only its own design taste. The
      revision: BUMPHOOD IS A COMPARATIVE CLAIM -- exceedance over the

      positive) by a preregistered margin, with a mandatory crucible arm that
      runs the full reference battery on the flagged world itself.

  (2) R4/R5 -- THE NULL. A null calibrated on unevolved reference players under
      uniform sampling is the WRONG null for tier-selected survivors under
      adaptive scheduling. Five qualification tiers passing the top-20% leave
      the top 3.2e-4 of the null: the detector would flag a large fraction of
      ALL survivors and the seismograph would read a permanent earthquake.

  (3) R14 -- EXTERNAL CONVINCIBILITY. Hash chains prove nobody tampered AFTER
      commit; they prove nothing about what we chose to commit. The one
      artifact a hostile reviewer cannot dismiss is detector power certified
      against SEALED planted positives authored by an external party.

2. WORLD FOUNDRY ARCHITECTURE                                    [v0 item 1]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Package `wforge`, to live INSIDE the engine source tree so that grammar,
expansion code, and cost tables all fall within the build-derived release
identity from day one (the WOW-0 forensic lesson: an environment outside the
release allowlist can change without the pin moving).

    genome  --expand-->  mechanics DAG  --instantiate-->  encounter runtime
    (data)   (pure fn)   (typed nodes)     (integer VM)    (deterministic)

  * A world is DATA: (grammar_version, generation_seed, parents, mutations).
  * Expansion is a PURE FUNCTION; re-expansion must reproduce the mechanics
    manifest bit-for-bit or the world is invalid.
  * Primitive families: STATE (register banks, ring tapes, graphs), TRANSITION
    (linear maps mod M, regime switches, seeded stochastic kernels,
    delayed-effect queues), OBSERVATION (projection, permutation, quantization,
    seeded corruption, delay), ACTION_COUPLING (costed writes), RESOURCE
    (conserved charge, metabolic costs, contested yield), INTERACTION
    (shared-state contention, transfers, signal channels, and -- R11 -- at
    least one POSITIVE-SUM joint-production primitive), TERMINATION, FAULT.
  * ALL INTEGER ARITHMETIC. Floats are banned from world physics: cross-host
    bit-exactness by construction, not by hope.
  * This is a WORLD SPACE, not a catalog: composable primitives produce
    structures we did not hand-author individually. Demonstrated: 300 genomes
    from grammar v0 yield 108 distinct behavioral fingerprints and 23 distinct
    probe orderings (section 19).

R12 (EARNED ADMISSION -- red team FATAL-adjacent). The draft shipped the entire
primitive catalog under one aggregate kill test that could essentially never
fire. Revision: PER-PRIMITIVE-FAMILY EARNED ADMISSION. Before a family enters
the campaign sampling distribution, the null battery runs on ABLATION GRAMMARS
(with vs without the family); the family is admitted only if it WIDENS the
discriminative band or changes the reference-player ordering in some region
beyond a preregistered margin. Admissions and rejections are recorded in the
grammar phylogeny -- this is what makes the phylogeny discipline real rather
than cosmetic bookkeeping, and it is the operative descendant of the previous
program's earned-worlds principle.

4. WORLD MUTATION GRAMMAR                                        [v0 item 3]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Operators over the mechanics DAG, each typed and seeded, each producing a
DESCENDANT genome (never an in-place edit):

  PARAM_PERTURB      seeded jitter of one primitive's integer parameters
  PRIMITIVE_INSERT   add a typed node (type-checked composition)
  PRIMITIVE_DELETE   remove a node (arity-preserving)
  REWIRE             change an action target / input edge
  SUBGRAPH_SPLICE    recombination: graft a subgraph from a second parent
  BUDGET_MUTATE      change horizons/budgets within grammar bounds
  INTERFACE_MUTATE   change observation corruption/delay/mixing level

The mutation history is interpreted at EXPANSION time, so a descendant genome
alone reproduces the mutated world exactly -- no diff artifacts to lose.
Demonstrated: 4 mutation classes on one parent produced 3 behaviorally distinct
children and 1 neutral variant, with the grandchild's provenance chain intact
(section 19).

5. SEMANTIC QUARANTINE SPECIFICATION                             [v0 item 4]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE BOUNDARY. Player-visible traffic is opaque integer vectors. Forbidden
across the boundary: natural language, semantic labels, human corpora,
pretrained embeddings, LLM output, human-named concepts, privileged simulator
state, PEW interpretations. Channel identity is permuted per-world by seed.
Human-readable instrumentation exists ONLY outside the player boundary.
Removing English strings is necessary and NOT sufficient; the audit targets
ontology leakage disguised as numeric identifiers or convenient decompositions.

R1 (RED TEAM FATAL -- the draft's kill criterion killed the informative worlds).
The draft said: train a decoder adversary to reconstruct designer-side labels
from player-visible traffic; above-chance reconstruction = breach = kill. The
red team showed above-chance decoding of PRIMITIVE FAMILY identity is a generic
property of any structured dynamics -- exactly the worlds in the discriminative
band -- so the criterion as stated kills the band and leaves only worlds mixed
into statistical whiteness (the flat region). REVISION:
  * Two label classes, treated differently:
      DESIGNER-SEMANTIC labels (channel role: which channel is another player's
      action, which is reward-adjacent, designer messages) -- GATED. These are
      anthropic leakage.
      PRIMITIVE-FAMILY identity (mathematical structure) -- MEASURED AND
      REPORTED, never a kill criterion. Structure is what makes a world
      learnable at all.
  * The gate is a PREREGISTERED LEAKAGE BUDGET, not a binary: fixed decoder
    hypothesis class, fixed training budget, leakage measured as effect size
    (balanced-accuracy margin over chance / MI bound), acceptance envelope
    calibrated JOINTLY with the discriminative band during the null campaign.
  * The mixing layer is retained as an EXPERIMENTAL ARM only (it is a placebo
    against a competent decoder while taxing learnability -- red team MAJOR);
    how much of any result depends on our channel decomposition is a measured
    quantity, not a hidden constant.

R3 (RED TEAM MAJOR -- ontology leaks through the measurement instrument).
The reference battery, planted positives, and band definition are themselves
human-shaped, and they sit designer-side of the boundary where no decoder
looks. REVISION: the production battery MUST include programs sampled from
Proteus's own genotype distribution (random organisms, not human scripts); the
discriminative band is computed under BOTH batteries and grammar kill decisions
require emptiness under both; planted positives are drawn from the same
generative family as real players (mutated organisms with induced effects).

6. MULTIPLAYER MECHANICS CONTRACT                                [v0 item 5]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Worlds declare n_slots. Players interact ONLY through world state; the
    interface never distinguishes "other player" from "world mechanism" --
    W* = W + other players is a fact of physics, not a labeled feature.
  * Within-tick: simultaneous action intake, deterministic two-phase
    settlement with an explicit total order (seed, slot index) -- never
    arrival order, never unordered iteration.
  * POPULATION DYNAMICS HAPPEN ACROSS MATCHES, NEVER INSIDE ONE: every match
    is fully frozen; a "dynamically changing opponent population" is a series
    of frozen manifests whose composition drifts, each individually replayable.
  * Supported experiment shapes: solitary; homogeneous and heterogeneous
    populations; symmetric and asymmetric roles; cooperation opportunities
    (R11 positive-sum primitive); contention; signaling (world-mediated);
    interference; persistent modification (within-episode state; persistent
    cross-episode world state deferred to grammar v1 -- see item 24).

R11 (RED TEAM MAJOR -- zero-sum collapse and ill-typed bumps). With only
zero-sum interaction primitives, theft is the fastest legible win and
multi-slot worlds converge to hawk-dove over transfers; worse, any multiplayer
bump is frequency-dependent. REVISIONS: (a) at least one positive-sum
joint-production primitive (super-additive return on coordinated costed
writes) so cooperation is representable; (b) a preregistered COLLAPSE METRIC
-- fraction of score variance attributable to zero-sum transfer flux per world
region -- with a rebalance criterion; (c) multiplayer bump claims are TYPED
HONESTLY as (player, world, opponent-distribution) with crucible-measured
sensitivity to opponent mix reported as part of the finding.

7. IMMUTABLE WORLD LINEAGE                                       [v0 item 6]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Never mutate a historical world in place. Worlds are content-addressed,
versioned, immutable; evolution produces descendants recorded with full
provenance (parents, ops, seeds). Historical evidence binds to the exact
content hash of the world that generated it, forever. FOSSIL RULE: no genome

a bump becomes visible. The world phylogeny schema from the prior program
(incubator/world_phylogeny.schema.v0.json) records the GRAMMAR-VERSION lineage
with the same discipline: prereg sealed before runs, scale-as-intervention
declared, abandoned versions retained.

8. PLAYER / WORLD INTERFACE                                      [v0 item 7]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  observe(slot) -> integer vector (width obs_width, values in [0, 2^value_bits))
  act(slot)     <- integer vector (width act_width)
  no other channel exists

  * Per-world channel permutation; optional mixing arm (R1).
  * Malformed actions are typed faults, never partial credit.
  * A slot that cannot afford its action is FORCED-ABSTAIN, and abstention is
    counted (an uninstrumented non-attempt would deform the landscape
    invisibly -- the dog that did not bark, carried over from World-0).
  * Players may carry bounded persistent state between ticks (and, as
    checkpoints, between encounters); the checkpoint is content-addressed and
    referenced by later manifests only after its producing encounter is
    QUALIFIED (R8 admission rule).

R2 (RED TEAM FATAL -- transfer under permutation). Zero-shot transfer probes
under fresh per-world permutation pass ONLY channel-permutation-invariant
players (constants, aggregate responders): staged qualification would
systematically enrich for channel-blind organisms and filter out the entire
space of structured strategies -- while any covert cross-world channel
alignment to "fix" this would itself be an ontology leak invisible to a
per-world decoder. REVISION: TRANSFER IS PREREGISTERED AS ADAPTATION SAVINGS,
never zero-shot performance -- transplant under a fresh permutation with a
fixed small re-adaptation budget, scored as advantage over ancestor/fresh
controls given the identical budget. If any cross-world coordinate stability
is ever introduced, it must be declared in the grammar version and a
CROSS-WORLD decoder task (predict world-A labels from world-B traffic) is
added to the quarantine battery.

10. DISTRIBUTED M1-M4 EXECUTION PLAN                             [v0 item 9]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Reuses the engine's existing fabric: lease-fenced claims (claim_id fencing,
heartbeats, expiry reassignment), idempotency keys, content-addressed
artifacts, client-scoped isolation. Scientific correctness never depends on
all four machines being alive. Revisions from the distributed red team:

R8a WALL-CLOCK KILLS ARE NEVER OUTCOMES (FATAL). A wall-clock kill is host-
    load-dependent and fires preferentially on expensive worlds: as an outcome
    it would be load-correlated censoring concentrated exactly where compute-
    coupled bumps live. Revision: typed EXECUTION_FAULT ledger record; voids
    the claim; bounded requeue; after N faults the manifest is INFEASIBLE.
    Null calibration and the detector ingest per-region fault-rate series as
    first-class data; a region whose fault rate departs from calibration is
    frozen for detection.

R8b DUPLICATE EXECUTION IS A FREE DETERMINISM AUDIT (FATAL). First-write-wins
    dedupe would discard the second trace unexamined -- converting every real
    determinism bug into an undetected coin flip. Revision: on duplicate
    commit for one match_id, compare canonical trace digests. Equal: dedupe.
    UNEQUAL: DETERMINISM_FAULT -- quarantine both traces, freeze the (world,
    execution_version) pair, halt promotions touching that region. Plus a
    DELIBERATE 1-2% redundancy rate (same manifest, two hosts) as a continuous
    determinism canary rather than relying on crash luck.

R8c EXECUTION_VERSION = SEALED RUNTIME IMAGE DIGEST (MAJOR). Integer-only
    removes float divergence; hash-order iteration, interpreter drift, and
    mixed int backends remain. Revision: execution_version is the content
    digest of a sealed runtime image (interpreter, dependencies, engine code,
    fixed hash seed); a worker whose local image digest differs MUST refuse
    the claim. Engine-core lint bans unordered iteration in any settlement
    path; the settlement total order is an explicit tested function of
    (seed, slot).

R8d CHECKPOINT ADMISSION RULE (MAJOR). A manifest is claimable only if every
    referenced checkpoint resolves to a COMMITTED, QUALIFIED, non-voided
    producing encounter at a strictly earlier ledger position (recorded in the
    manifest). Voiding an encounter marks all ledgered descendants TAINTED and
    excludes them from detection statistics and promotion. No lineage may
    climb tiers on a voided ancestor.

R8e TWO-PHASE BUDGET (MAJOR). Atomic RESERVE(max_steps) at claim (fails if the
    lineage budget cannot cover it -- closing the concurrent-overspend race),
    SETTLE(actual) at evidence commit, RELEASE by compensation record on lease
    expiry. All three are ledger entries, so the crucible's compute-
    explanation test reasons over replayable accounting.

R8f CONSERVATION INVARIANT (MAJOR). Continuously checked:
    claims(ledger) == outcomes + faults + budget-exhaustions (ledger). Machine-
    correlated fault classes (OOM on big-state worlds, disk-full on long
    traces) cannot silently eat evidence: fault dispositions carry manifest id
    and fault class in the SCIENTIFIC ledger; diagnostic detail stays in
    segregated telemetry.

R8g FLEET MEMBERSHIP IS LEDGERED (MINOR). Host-set requirements (e.g. the
    crucible's independent-hosts arms) are explicit preconditions in the
    prospective test: a confirmation that cannot meet them BLOCKS VISIBLY
    rather than degrading silently. Cache reads verify artifact digests before
    execution; derived-artifact cache keys include grammar and execution
    versions; a digest mismatch is an EXECUTION_FAULT, never a silent refetch.

12. PEW INGESTION / METABOLIZATION CONTRACT                      [v0 item 11]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BOUNDARY: SFE = what happened. PEW = evolving knowledge about what happened.
Qualified evidence flows into PEW as references (world hash, ledger seq,
match_id) -- never as copies that could drift. PEW may relate player lineages,
world lineages, ecologies, mutations, behaviors, failures, resource profiles,
transfer outcomes, candidate bumps, falsification attempts, reinterpretations.

PEW MAY PROPOSE WHERE TO LOOK. PEW MAY NOT MANUFACTURE THE EVIDENCE THAT
CONFIRMS ITS OWN PROPOSAL. Mechanism: every PEW proposal becomes a frozen
prospective_test_id committed through the engine's irreversible boundary;
confirming evidence must postdate the commit; the scheduling policy that
executes it is versioned and replayable.

R14a (RED TEAM FATAL -- the loop the firewall missed). The prospective_test_id
mechanism quarantines CONFIRMATION evidence but not the detector's OPERATING
data: PEW -> scheduler -> sampling distribution -> empirical null -> detector
thresholds -> what PEW sees next is a contamination loop that survives the
draft firewall. REVISION: a PERMANENT, VERSIONED, IMMUTABLE REFERENCE STREAM
-- a fixed fraction of every machine's budget forever runs (reference battery
+ continuing planted positives) x a FROZEN grammar-uniform world sampler.
Detector thresholds, false-positive monitoring, and power certification are
computed ONLY from that stream. PEW-shaped scheduling directs the remainder
and can never touch threshold-setting data. Every threshold update ledgered
next to the reference-stream policy hash.

R14b (RED TEAM MAJOR -- cold-region quota). Every batch of flag-guided world
proposals carries a preregistered matched quota of COLD-REGION (flag-naive)
proposals, so grammar drift toward flag-rich scoring geometry is measurable
against a control stream.

13. BUDGET MODEL                                                 [v0 item 12]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Authoritative, engine-enforced, lineage-scoped (a fork cannot mint budget).
Tracked and limited per encounter: player compute (VM steps), memory,
persistent storage, action count, environment steps; wall-clock as safety kill
only. Two-phase RESERVE/SETTLE/RELEASE per R8e. Unlimited brute force destroys
the selection pressure; the habitat makes cognition expensive enough that
efficient representation can matter.

R10 (RED TEAM FATAL -- the true fitness function). Compute is the reproductive
resource, so under a single outcome-gated ladder the quantity actually under
selection is EARLY LEGIBLE SCORE PER UNIT COMPUTE -- a monoculture of fast
shallow exploiters that then poisons every multiplayer statistic and
re-anchors every baseline. REVISION: the campaign budget splits into TWO
PREREGISTERED CURRENCIES:
  * an OUTCOME-GATED ladder (staged qualification as designed), and
  * a DIVERSITY-GATED allocation funding lineages by behavioral divergence
    from the current survivor population (the statistic already exists for
    detection; reused for allocation).
Plus a STRATEGY-CLUSTER CAP: no behavioral cluster may hold more than a
preregistered fraction of expensive-tier budget. Both are ecology-shaping
policies: versioned, ledgered, replayable, like the scheduler.

14. STAGED EARLY-DEATH QUALIFICATION                             [v0 item 13]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  T0_MICRO -> T1_DIFFERENTIATION -> T2_TRANSFER -> T3_NOVEL_WORLD
                       -> T4_FALSIFICATION -> T5_PERSISTENT

Most encounters die at T0 for almost nothing. Promotion statistics are
preregistered per (tier x horizon-class) stratum. Transfer at T2 is ADAPTATION
SAVINGS (R2). Failure evidence is always preserved (fossil rules).

R9 (RED TEAM FATAL -- the slow-developer mitigations relabeled the bias).
Horizon stratification, a blind lottery, and horizon normalization all move
the bias without shrinking it: micro tiers cannot express delayed payoffs, so
promotion from the long-horizon stratum is a coin flip dressed as
qualification; lottery survivors die one tier later against baselines set by
sprinters. REVISIONS:
  * SEPARATE "INSUFFICIENT POWER" FROM "FLAT". A lineage is killed only on
    AFFIRMATIVE flatness: a sequential test concluding indistinguishability
    from the reference battery AT ADEQUATE POWER for its horizon stratum.
    Anything else is DEFERRED, not killed: parked as a frozen manifest (which
    costs nothing) in a bounded re-audition queue.
  * The long-horizon stratum gets its OWN ladder whose T0 is BUDGET-matched
    (longer horizon, fewer replicates), not duration-matched.
  * The lottery is CONDITIONED, not blind: reserved for lineages whose cheap
    NON-outcome signals (interaction with delayed-effect nodes, behavioral
    divergence from the battery) are anomalous despite flat outcomes.

16. BUMP DETECTOR                                                [v0 item 15]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE DETECTOR IS A PRIORITIZER, NOT A JUDGE. It triages candidates into a
capacity-bounded crucible; it confirms nothing. Statistic family
(preregistered, small): survival, adaptation-savings transfer,
resource-normalized outcome, behavioral divergence, repeated survival across
contexts. Candidate detection is conservative; a candidate triggers FREEZE
(player, world, opponents, evidence-at-detection), never exploitation.

The red team found the draft detector statistically unsound on four
independent grounds. Revisions:

R4  TIER-CONDITIONAL, SELF-REFRESHING NULL (FATAL). Thresholds are calibrated
    on the distribution of SURVIVORS of each tier under outcome-irrelevant
    randomization (pseudo-lineages pushed through the identical selection
    funnel), not on the raw reference battery. A STANDING NEGATIVE-CONTROL
    STREAM (reference players + permuted-outcome evolved players injected into
    the live match distribution) recalibrates on a preregistered cadence;
    every recalibration is ledgered. EMPIRICAL GATE: the full detector runs
    against the negative-control stream under the REAL adaptive scheduler; a
    detector version whose realized flag rate exceeds nominal is killed.

R5  EXCHANGEABLE UNITS AND FILTRATION DISCIPLINE (FATAL). One e-value per
    (world x seed-block) aggregate -- never per encounter -- under a
    hierarchical null carrying world- and opponent-level random effects
    estimated in calibration (shared-world encounters are correlated; at
    intra-class rho=0.1 and n=100, effective sample size is ~9, so an
    independence-assuming e-process overstates evidence ~11x). Scheduler
    routing must be predictable w.r.t. the e-process filtration (R5/section 9).

R7  REGIONS ARE PREREGISTERED, MULTI-GRANULAR, AND OWNED (MAJOR). The
    (world-region x architecture-region x opponent-cluster) partition is
    computed per grammar version from PRE-DATA coordinates (grammar features +
    behavioral embeddings measured only on the reference battery during
    calibration), validated for within-region exchangeability, and frozen by
    hash. Detection runs at 2-3 preregistered granularities with multiplicity
    priced in (a bump filling 1% of a coarse region needs ~1e4x the evidence
    -- narrow bumps must not be structurally invisible). Any repartition is a
    new hypothesis family with a fresh evidence budget; old data demotes to
    pilot.

R11 CONTEMPORANEOUS SENTINEL BASELINE (MAJOR). Detector statistics compare
    against a continuing trickle of reference-battery sentinels ON THE SAME
    WORLDS in the evolved era -- never against the epoch-zero null alone -- so
    ecology drift does not read as landscape structure.

R13a TWO-STATISTIC FLAG RULE AND DENOMINATOR FLOOR (MAJOR). Resource-
    normalized outcome is gameable by denominator starvation (noop + one cheap
    exploit = enormous ratio, and it replays perfectly). The denominator floor
    is preregistered from the null campaign's empirical resource distribution,
    and a FREEZE requires flags under at least TWO independent members of the
    statistic family.

R6a CAPACITY-DERIVED THRESHOLD (MAJOR). The detector threshold is set so that
    expected flags/week ~= crucible slots/week: a preregistered quantity, not
    an aspiration. Admission from the candidate queue is by e-value-
    proportional lottery or FIFO -- NEVER top-k by observed effect size, which
    would preferentially admit the most winner's-curse-inflated candidates.

R3b PLANT TAXONOMY WITH HOLDOUT (MAJOR). Planted positives span the axes real
    bumps would vary on (opponent-conditional, delayed-onset, region-
    localized, transfer-only, normalized-only), are drawn from the organism
    family, are injected CONTINUOUSLY into the live stream blinded by manifest
    hashing, and at least one family is held out from all detector tuning.
    Holdout-family power is the only number allowed to inform go/no-go.

17. BUMP FALSIFICATION CRUCIBLE                                  [v0 item 16]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
On candidate: freeze player, world, opponents, evidence-at-detection; commit
prospective tests through the engine's irreversible boundary; then:

  ENGINE-INTEGRITY (necessary, carries ZERO confirmatory weight -- R6c):
    1 exact replay (deterministic by construction: it confirms the fabric,
      never the finding)
  CONFIRMATION (each arm preregistered with alpha and power):
    2 fresh seeds                    3 independent hosts
    4 nearby world-parameter perturbation
    5 nearby player-architecture perturbation
    6 component ablation             7 player transplant
    8 opponent transplant (sampling frame preregistered across strategy
      clusters INCLUDING the reference battery -- R11)
    9 ancestor comparison           10 fresh controls
   11 compute-explanation test (does budget explain the effect?)
   12 REFERENCE-FRONTIER TEST (R13 -- mandatory): the full reference battery
      and largest planted positive run on the flagged world itself. A
      candidate that null-grade players reproduce is a WORLD-GEOMETRY
      ARTIFACT, classified and fed back as a grammar diagnostic -- not a bump.
   13 MINIMAL-SCRIPT ABLATION (R13a): extract the minimal action skeleton
      from the flagged trace; if a scripted replay of the skeleton reproduces
      the statistic, classify as statistic exploit.

  ERROR CONTROL ACROSS THE QUEUE (R6b -- FATAL in draft): effects are shrunk
  empirically (empirical-Bayes against the survivor null) BEFORE powering the
  arms; alpha-investing / merged e-values across candidates bound the
  confirmed-set FDR at a stated level (target: 10%). The draft, uncorrected,
  yields confirmed-set FDR ~87% under realistic numbers (200 candidates/yr,
  5 real, power 0.3, per-arm alpha 0.05).
  ECOLOGY SNAPSHOT (R6a): the transplant/control opponent pool is frozen AT
  FLAG TIME so late-run confirmations remain exchangeable with early ones.

ONLY after surviving all of this does a candidate become an ADMITTED BUMP.
We do not call it intelligence. We call it evidence of landscape structure.

AFTER A REAL BUMP (R14c -- the governance gap the red team called the
highest-pressure moment of the program): DO NOT HAND-OPTIMIZE. Neighborhood
mapping is a PREREGISTERED CAMPAIGN: the world-mutation sampling distribution
around the confirmed point is committed BEFORE any mapping encounter runs;
mapping worlds are drawn only from that distribution, never accepted or
rejected on the focal player's performance; the map reports ALL draws, no
discards. Then descendants explore the neighborhood under selection -- the
question is whether EVOLUTION can amplify the bump, not whether we can.

23. ASSUMPTIONS WE BELIEVE ARE WEAK (READ FIRST)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A1  THE REFERENCE FRONTIER DEFINES BUMPHOOD (R13) -- so bumphood is relative
    to a battery we chose. The organism-distribution battery (R3) softens but
    does not eliminate this: some genuinely novel capability could sit below
    the frontier on every preregistered statistic. The seismograph is blind to
    what its statistic family cannot express. Mitigated only by keeping the
    family small, preregistered, and versioned -- and by admitting the
    blindness out loud.
A2  QUARANTINE IS A BUDGET, NOT A PROOF (R1). We gate designer-semantic
    leakage at a preregistered effect size; below-threshold leakage exists by
    construction. A reviewer must judge whether the budget concept is
    scientifically honest or a licensed hole.
A3  THE EXCHANGEABLE-UNIT NULL (R5) depends on estimating world- and
    opponent-level random effects from calibration data that predates
    evolution. If evolved play changes the correlation structure itself, the
    hierarchical null drifts and only the negative-control stream (R4) would
    notice. The whole detector guarantee therefore rests on the negative
    controls being truly outcome-irrelevant.
A4  DEFER-NOT-KILL (R9) creates an ever-growing parked population. The
    re-audition queue is bounded, so genuine slow developers can still starve
    in the queue -- the bias is reduced and measured, not removed.
A5  CRUCIBLE CAPACITY (R6a) is the program's real rate limiter: at realistic
    flag rates the crucible, not the fleet, decides how fast the program can
    learn. Four machines may be too few to run discovery and confirmation
    concurrently at any interesting scale.
A6  THE DIVERSITY CURRENCY (R10) pays for behavioral divergence, which is
    gameable in principle (cheap noise is divergent). The cluster cap and the
    two-statistic rule guard the obvious exploit; the red team of the NEXT
    revision should attack this specifically.
A7  WORLD-GEOMETRY ARTIFACTS (R13, arm 12) are classified by whether
    null-grade players reproduce the signature -- but a geometry artifact
    exploitable only ABOVE reference-grade play would pass arm 12 and be
    admitted. The distinction between "landscape structure" and "grammar
    property" is graded, not binary, and the packet says so.
A8  EXTERNAL ESCROW (R14) requires an external party willing to author sealed
    plants and hold hashes. If none exists, the program's results remain
    internally consistent and externally unverifiable -- a stated limitation,
    not a hidden one.

  FINAL PRINCIPLE
  We are not building a world in which intelligence succeeds. We are building
  an ecology in which different ways of processing experience encounter
  different consequences. Provenance, physics, budgets, replay and
  falsification are frozen; representations, algorithms, architectures and
  eventually world ecologies vary.

  The goal is extraordinarily modest: FIND ONE REAL BUMP. Millions of dead
  organisms and dead worlds are acceptable -- the cemetery is the product,
  because the surrounding failures define the landscape against which a bump
  becomes visible.

  Build the machine capable of finding it. Then let it be wrong in public.
