--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

26 opcodes + literal tokens; max 12 tokens post-expansion; stack cap 12 with
lossy bottom-discard on overflow; pop-on-empty yields 0; one scratch register.

LEGAL PHYSICS, never sanitized. Cost = tokens executed. All randomness flows
through sha256-keyed named RNGs; every run exactly replayable.
