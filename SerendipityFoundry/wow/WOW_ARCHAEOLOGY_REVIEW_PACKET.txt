0. WHAT THIS IS, AND WHAT IT IS NOT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The MHC admission-rights ledger and cryptographic seal changed the epistemic
status of historical mining: because admission is now structurally protected
from whatever a search does, the search may be maximally aggressive. This
packet is the product of that licence.

  IT ESTABLISHES: things about the historical corpus that are directly
  readable from the record, and a queue of nominations.

  IT ESTABLISHES NOTHING ABOUT ANY PHENOMENON. Every candidate here was
  selected by unlimited hindsight over a corpus the archaeologist read in
  full. No p-value, rarity estimate, cross-substrate coincidence, or
  retrospective replication appearing anywhere below is confirmatory.

  THE STRONGEST PERMITTED LABEL is SEALED_TEST_WORTHY. In fact not even
  that is yet earned: see section 6.

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY -- FOUR RESULTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
R1  THE CORPUS IS FAR LARGER AND STRANGER THAN THE PRIOR SURVEY RECORDED.
    The 2026-08-30 forensic survey documented one engine, ~14k executions
    and two candidate successes. The corpus actually holds THREE registered
    engines (stackvm-v1, treegp-deap, push-pyshgp), 183,856 ledger records,
    71,683 executions, 30,121 artifacts, 40,796 lineage edges, TWO driver
    eras (objective_ga, then map_elites), and SEVEN successes -- five of
    which the prior survey never saw.

R2  THE HISTORICAL "EVOLUTION" WAS LARGELY DRIFT, AND THE HISTORICAL
    "FAILURE LANDSCAPE" WAS LARGELY AN INSTRUMENT SETTING. 97.7% of all
    selection events (85 of 87) were FULLY TIED -- every candidate
    identical, resolved by seeded tie-break alone. And 89.3% of the 6,945
    failures occurred under step ceilings of 100-400, a regime with roughly
    32x the failure rate of the dominant 100,000-step regime. Both facts
    are audit-readable and neither needs a sealed test.

R3  THE POPULATION WAS TWO NON-INTERBREEDING POPULATIONS THAT EVERY POOLED
    STATISTIC SILENTLY MIXED. Genotype length is bimodal: 28,392 artifacts
    at 1-150 bytes, 1,393 above 700, and 335 in between. In 38,163
    length-changing mutations, ZERO cross from <=150 to >700. The long mode
    was seeded independently by create_random and self-sustained. The
    401-700 band (327 artifacts) was used as a mutation parent EXACTLY ZERO
    TIMES.

R4  THE MOST IMPORTANT FINDING IS A BLOCKER, NOT A SIGNAL. Every candidate
    worth testing is a claim about D13/stackvm-v1 operators. The MHC
    admission protocol's canonical reference sampler and context family are
    defined for the World Foundry grammar. THE ADMISSION PROTOCOL CANNOT
    CURRENTLY STATE THESE CLAIMS. Section 6.

2. THE BOUNDARY, REBUILT AFTER ADVERSARIAL REVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The draft design went to an independent red team BEFORE any candidate was
nominated. All four dimensions returned UNSOUND_AS_STATED. The three
findings that changed the design:

F-A  THE DRAFT INDEPENDENCE TEST MEASURED INPUT NOVELTY, NOT VERDICT
     ENTROPY -- the same error as G14's procedural seal, one level up. The
     draft asked "can the corpus compute this prediction?" But the ledger's
     own ENGINE_REGISTERED record declares the engine bit-deterministic and
     the interpreter is shipped, so an archaeologist runs 10^6 self-chosen
     seeds offline, observes P(pass)=0.9993, and registers a prediction
     whose INPUTS are beacon-fresh and whose VERDICT is already known. The
     beacon randomizes WHICH draw, not WHETHER.
     REPAIR: the criterion is now VERDICT ENTROPY. Registration must ship an
     executable verdict function V(beacon, evidence) and evidence sampler
     S(beacon), both hashed into the commit. The HARNESS -- never the
     nominator -- draws 10,000 decoy beacons and measures the empirical
     level p0 (must be <= 1/K) and the admit rate p1 (must satisfy
     H(p1) >= 0.5 bits). The attack above scores H(0.9993) = 0.0083 bits and
     is refused. A beacon-sensitivity assertion (two decoys must produce
     different evidence hashes) additionally kills constant-fibre
     predictions entailed by statically-readable substrate invariants.

F-B  HINDSIGHT IN THE HYPOTHESIS IS FREE; HINDSIGHT IN THE NULL IS FATAL.
     This is the deepest result of the mission. Selecting a candidate by
     unlimited hindsight costs nothing: the candidate is corpus-measurable,
     and for a null whose distribution GIVEN the candidate does not depend
     on the corpus, P(reject | H0) <= 1/K holds for ANY corpus-measurable
     selection rule -- no multiplicity is owed for the search, however many
     millions of hypotheses it examined. That premise dies the moment the
     reference sampler, context rule, tie rule, matching covariates, or
     exclusions are FITTED to the corpus (e.g. "match on genotype length"
     chosen because that is where the artifact looks most extreme).
     REPAIR: the null path is TYPED. Every element of {ref_rule,
     context_rule, tie_rule, betting_rule, matching_covariates,
     lineage_exclusions, grammar_family_exclusions, stopping_rule} is
     declared PreCorpusConstant | BeaconDerived | CorpusDerived, and a
     CorpusDerived value in the null path is a TYPE ERROR. Schema
     separation of record FILES was theater; this is where it had to go.

F-C  THE RANKING WAS GAMEABLE IN SIX WAYS AT ONCE. Nominator-supplied
     priors maximize information at p=0.5, so every entry converges to
     "honestly 50/50" and the ranking degenerates to ranking by K. Without
     a power model the score is monotone in K, so the dominant strategy is
     K=10^9 and the queue fills with tests a real effect cannot pass. The
     denominator was wrong: alpha is not the binding resource -- RIGHTS
     are (~100 ever; a death costs ~0 alpha but 1% of the program).
     Independence was scored on hypothesis text, so n_eff collapses toward
     1. And no calibration tranche existed, so the corpus->sealed transfer
     function would never be estimated.
     REPAIR: priors are structural intervals scored at their pessimistic
     end; a power model at a declared minimum effect size is mandatory;
     both bits-per-alpha and bits-per-RIGHT are computed and the binding
     constraint named; deaths are discounted by q_boring (nondeterminism,
     resource truncation, mis-specified observable, extraction bug);
     independence is a shared-nuisance loading vector yielding a queue
     n_eff; and the queue OPENS with a calibration tranche.

3. DISCOVERY LEDGER -- AUDIT FACTS (no sealed test needed or wanted)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
These are directly readable from the record. Spending a scarce admission
right on any of them would be absurd; they are reported so that every
future claim on this corpus is interpreted correctly.

A1  SELECTION WAS DRIFT. 85 of 87 SELECTION events (97.7%) were fully tied
    -- n_tied == n_candidates -- across both tournament (80) and
    elite_truncation (7) operators, resolved by seeded_uniform tie-break.
    In the objective_ga era the fitness signal was flat and "selection"
    was a random draw.

A2  THE FAILURE LANDSCAPE WAS SHAPED BY A STEP CEILING. All 6,945 era-A
    failures are a single kind (resource_exhausted, detail
    "exhausted_cases=12/12"). By step regime: 43.0% at max_steps=300,
    41.5% at 100, 4.8% at 400, 0.2% at 200 -- 89.3% under tight ceilings --
    versus 10.4% in the 100,000-step regime that carried 56,967 of the
    71,683 executions. Tight-ceiling failure rate ~42%; 100k-regime rate
    ~1.3%. The 722 failures at 100,000 steps are the only ones in the
    corpus not plausibly explained by the ceiling.

A3  BIMODAL LENGTH WITH A STERILE VALLEY (R3 above). Bands: 1-50 (12,766
    artifacts, used as mutation parent 19,242 times), 51-150 (15,626 /
    16,925), 151-400 (8 / 3), 401-700 (327 / ZERO), >700 (1,393 / 2,336).
    Mutation length change: 45.1% same length, 42.9% +-1..8, 11.4%
    +-9..100, 0.7% big jumps; max child/parent ratio 29.0. Crossings from
    <=150 to >700: ZERO.

A4  NEUTRAL SELF-LOOPS ARE CONCENTRATED AND U-SHAPED IN LENGTH. 344 of
    40,796 lineage edges (0.8%) are self-loops -- mutation returning a
    byte-identical genotype -- and they fall on only 25 artifacts (top:
    150, 70, 60, 14, 11 occurrences). Rate by parent length: 1.497%
    (1-50), 0.106% (51-150), 1.541% (>700). Not monotone, so not a simple
    resample collision.

A5  ERA-B INSTRUMENTATION VOID. In the separate engine substrate: all
    3,077 observations have NULL info_kind; all 2,411 failures have NULL
    kind; all 138 hypotheses have NULL status. Outcomes: 654 SURVIVED, 19
    FALSIFIED, 2,404 INCONCLUSIVE (78%). Evidence classes were populated
    (2,935 ENGINE_WORK_RESULT, 142 CLIENT_ASSERTED). Three schema fields
    the engine provides were never written, so era-B failure structure is
    unrecoverable from this record.

A6  CROSS-ENGINE STERILITY. treegp-deap (3,008 artifacts) and push-pyshgp
    (2,404) produced ZERO successes; all seven belong to stackvm-v1.

A7  SEVEN SUCCESSES, TWO ERAS. objective_ga: 26ab2cb1 (12/12 and 8/8 on
    two tasks), 4065e488 (12/12 and 8/8). map_elites: eadcc0bd, 8aa32ef8,
    9bc2b861. Task a768fad8 was solved TWICE, by eadcc0bd (37 bytes,
    mutate, 3 ancestors) and 9bc2b861 (707 bytes, create_random) --
    artifacts with ZERO shared ancestry, from OPPOSITE length modes.

A8  PROVENANCE ASYMMETRY, STATED AGAINST INTEREST. The two objective_ga
    successes have linked execution records (exec_seq refs). The three
    map_elites successes have NONE that the extractor could link -- their
    SUCCESS events carry artifact and task ids but no execution join. The
    strongest-looking candidates therefore rest on the weaker provenance.

4. WHY MOST FINDINGS ARE NOT CANDIDATES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A distinction the mission implies and this packet makes explicit: an
archaeological result is only a CANDIDATE if it asserts something about
FUTURE behaviour that the corpus cannot settle. A1-A8 are audit facts about
what was recorded. "Was selection tied?" is answered by reading the ledger,
not by purchasing 1% of the program's lifetime error budget. Nominating
them would convert a scarce irreplaceable resource into a re-derivation of
something already known.

6. THE BLOCKER: NO ENTRY IS CERTIFIED, AND NONE CAN BE YET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every queue entry carries entropy_validation = PENDING_ENTROPY_VALIDATION
and the label SEALED_TEST_WORTHY_PENDING_VALIDATION. Two independent
reasons, both stated against interest:

 B1 THE VERDICT-ENTROPY HARNESS CANNOT RUN. It requires an executable
    verdict function and evidence sampler evaluated against 10,000 decoy
    beacons under a canonical reference sampler. That machinery does not
    exist for this substrate. Until it runs, "SEALED_TEST_WORTHY" is a
    claim about a check nobody performed -- exactly the kind of assertion
    this program has spent its whole methodology refusing.

 B2 SUBSTRATE MISMATCH (the mission's most valuable finding). Every
    candidate is a claim about D13/stackvm-v1 operators -- mutation from a
    length band, crossing between modes, task solvability under a specific
    interpreter. The MHC admission protocol's canonical reference sampler
    (CANON-R-V1: uniform PRF-index into the frozen operator ensemble) and
    canonical context family (CANON-W-V1) are GENESIS-pinned for the World
    Foundry grammar, and registrant-supplied samplers are refused at fold
    time precisely because a corpus-fitted null voids the bound. THE
    INTERESTING HISTORICAL CLAIMS ARE EXACTLY THE ONES THE ADMISSION
    PROTOCOL CANNOT CURRENTLY STATE.
    The two exits, both requiring adjudication:
      (i) give stackvm-v1 its own GENESIS-pinned canonical sampler and
          context family, authored WITHOUT reference to this corpus (or the
          null becomes corpus-fitted and F-B applies); or
      (ii) re-pose each claim in the World Foundry grammar -- which changes
          the claim, and the re-posed version is a NEW hypothesis whose
          historical support does not transfer.
