# Preserved failed approaches (mission section 25.19)

* `T2_source_of_randomness_dichotomy.md` — my original criterion: "selection is
  free when the null's randomness is created after the candidate is fixed, and
  fatal when the null is a measure over the space the candidate was selected
  from." REFUTED by independent review: freshness of U is necessary, never
  sufficient. The support, statistic, threshold and conditioning event are all
  D-measurable, so a registrant can truthfully claim fresh randomness and still
  have a level of 1. Replaced by the sigma-field criterion over the whole test
  object theta.
* `T1_sup_over_c_premise.md` — my original premise required the level bound
  uniformly over c. Simultaneously too strong (sup_c = 1 for most useful tests)
  and, as practised, too weak (registrants verify on average). Replaced by the
  Registered Conditional Level Certificate at the realized c.
* `T3_single_selection_replicating_null.md` — my original single "selection-
  replicating null" whose unit is the pipeline's nominee. Shown to be a
  MARGINAL null that never conditions on the candidate: p ~ Uniform(0,1)
  exactly for every artifact and every statistic. Split into D1 (pipeline
  misspecification), D2 (conditional/selective), D3 (provably vacuous).
* `T4_unstateable_non_sequitur.md` — my original conclusion that an adaptive
  nomination process makes rarity claims unstateable. NON SEQUITUR: selective
  inference conditions on the selection EVENT, not the rule; a G-conditional
  null is valid for every rule in G. The real question is whether the consulted
  sigma-field is BOUNDED. Status corrected to INADMISSIBLE FOR LACK OF A
  BOUNDED SELECTION SIGMA-FIELD -- factual and remediable.
