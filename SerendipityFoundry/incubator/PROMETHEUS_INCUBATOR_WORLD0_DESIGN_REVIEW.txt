1. EXECUTIVE VERDICT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

                          BUILD_WITH_REVISIONS

PROVENANCE OF THIS VERDICT: the design below was submitted to an independent
adversarial review before this packet was written. That review found THREE FATAL
ECONOMIC DEFECTS and FOUR FATAL METHODOLOGICAL DEFECTS in the first draft. All are
incorporated. The reader should assume the first draft would have produced a
degenerate world and an unfalsifiable headline claim, and that this revision is
the result of trying hard to kill the design rather than to defend it.

WHY NOT "BUILD": six revisions are load-bearing and BLOCKING. R1-R3 are economic
and must be settled in the physics before a line of world code is written; R4-R6
are methodological and must be settled before Campaign 1.

  R1. CHARGE MUST ACTUALLY BE CONSERVED. The first draft claimed "one conserved
      resource" and then paid organisms for accuracy -- from nowhere. That
      unspecified mint makes DEPOSIT WASH TRADING dominant and, provably, NO
      choice of cost parameters separates it from real prediction: a depositor
      controls the entropy of its own channel, so it can always make itself
      perfectly predictable, and any deposit cost high enough to forbid the
      exploit also makes Layer 1 unusable. FIX: WORLD channels are the ONLY
      exogenous source of charge (the "sun"); DEPOSIT channels pay predictors
      solely out of an escrow the depositor funds. Wash trading then becomes
      strictly negative-sum and self-eliminates with no detector, no anti-
      collusion rule, and no semantic judgement. This single rule also closes
      self-prediction, kin collusion, and the steganographic exploit (R2).

  R2. PAY THE RESIDUAL, NOT THE ACCURACY. Any branching generator over an
      instruction set puts most mass on near-trivial programs, and SEMANTIC
      triviality exceeds syntactic triviality (deep programs collapse to
      constants via dead code). Under raw-accuracy payout, "predict last value"
      earns a larger share of the same payoff at lower step cost than any real
      predictor -- so K0 fires on day one and "generator depth" is decoration.
      FIX: payout is scaled by the residual against a FROZEN, MINIMAL,
      engine-owned null battery (constant, last-value, first-difference,
      period-p repeat); a channel the battery already predicts is worth ~0. Plus
      behavioural reject-sampling: a candidate generator enters the channel
      population only if the battery fails on it. This defines ZERO, not a
      direction. The battery must stay MINIMAL or it becomes the authored ladder
      of K2 in a new hiding place.

  R3. DELETE THE FREE COMPUTE QUANTUM. The first draft granted a per-tick compute
      quantum AND charged per VM step -- two resources, one priced. That produces
      r-selection collapse, and worse, makes in-lifetime search free, which makes
      the evolution arm and the random-search control PHYSICALLY IDENTICAL and
      renders K6 unreachable by measurement. FIX: VM steps are BOUGHT from the
      organism's own charge. A hard per-tick step ceiling remains as a
      determinism bound -- a ceiling is not an allowance. This revision is a
      deletion, not an addition.

  R4. THE TECTONIC HYPOTHESIS IS PARTLY TAUTOLOGICAL AS FIRST STATED, and is not
      falsifiable without four sealed pre-commitments and a coordinate partition.
      Capability is defined by prediction residual; several proposed failure
      coordinates (first-divergence index, residual structure) are monotone
      functions of that same residual, so a finer-resolution readout of the
      outcome would "precede" the outcome by construction. FIX: partition the
      coordinates into CAPABILITY-COUPLED and CAPABILITY-DISJOINT, freeze the
      partition by hash, and run the confirmatory test ONLY on the disjoint set.
      See section 8. (Kill criterion K12.)

  R5. THE HUMAN-PRIOR ARMS (H and X, charter section 9) ARE DEFERRED OUT of
      World-0 on scientific, not scheduling, grounds: an LLM-seeded arm cannot be
      compute-matched against a bytecode organism (K6) and imports exactly the
      semantics the design excludes (K9). World-0 runs the P arm only, preserving
      the interface. I am not convinced the H-vs-P comparison is well-posed at
      all (section 18, A4).

  R6. THE PROPOSED FIRST CAMPAIGN COULD NOT FIRE ITS OWN KILL CRITERIA. A
      shakedown of fixed, non-evolving organisms cannot exhibit wash trading, kin
      collusion, or r-selection -- those are things a POPULATION discovers -- so a
      broken economy would have passed. FIX: Campaign 0 ships SCRIPTED ADVERSARY
      ORGANISMS AS PLANTED POSITIVES, one per named exploit, and its success
      condition is that the criteria DO fire on them. See section 17.

A SEVENTH, NON-BLOCKING BUT IMPORTANT REVISION: "prediction pays" is itself an
ontological choice we make on the organisms' behalf. Defensible, but not neutral,
and section 18 says so out loud rather than hiding it.

WHY NOT "NOT_READY": the substrate is unusually far along. The qualified GEN-2.1
engine already supplies, as shipped capabilities, the machinery that this program
would otherwise have to invent: hash-chained per-world ledgers and bit-exact
reconstruction; fork-by-reference from checkpoints (the natural ablation
primitive); lineage-scoped budgets that a fork cannot inflate (the compute-confound
defence); first-class failures with explicit metabolization edges; an
information-availability frontier query; policy-gated cross-world inheritance with
registered consent; and evidence provenance classes that prevent a client
assertion from being recorded as an engine-attested fact. Section 14 maps these
precisely. The genuinely missing pieces are few and cheap (section 15).

ONE-LINE SUMMARY: the instrument is closer to ready than the hypothesis is.

2. WORLD-0 PRIMITIVE PHYSICS (LAYER 0, ENGINE-OWNED, IMMUTABLE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

DESIGN STANCE: a synthetic microbial ecology, not a game. Ten primitive rules
whose interaction generates surprise, rather than a thousand authored mechanics.

P1. SUBSTRATE. A deterministic bytecode VM, descended from the already-qualified
    stackvm-v1 lineage: fixed instruction set, no I/O, no syscalls, bounded steps
    and memory, genotypes are INTERPRETED DATA and never host code. Determinism
    is a promise the engine keeps, and it is the precondition for replay.

P2. MEDIUM. The world contains CHANNELS. A channel emits a deterministic value
    sequence. There are exactly two channel classes:
      WORLD channels   -- the sequence is produced by a hidden generator program
                          drawn from a compositional distribution over the same
                          instruction set, at unbounded composition depth.
                          INSTANCES are resampled; the DISTRIBUTION is stable.
                          Therefore structural adaptation pays and instance
                          memorization does not (this is the K1 defence, built
                          into the physics rather than bolted on).
      DEPOSIT channels -- emitted by organisms at a cost (see P7). Yesterday's
                          successful strategy thereby becomes part of today's
                          environment, which is the endogenous curriculum
                          mechanism of charter section 7.

P3. TIME. Discrete ticks, two-phase (see P11). No wall-clock dependence anywhere.
    THERE IS NO COMPUTE ALLOWANCE. VM steps are BOUGHT from the organism's own
    charge at a fixed rate. A hard per-tick step ceiling exists purely as a
    determinism/termination bound; a ceiling is not an allowance (revision R3).
    Consequence: "thinking harder" is directly costly, in-lifetime search is not
    free, and the evolution arm is not physically identical to random search.

P4. RESOURCE. ONE genuinely conserved quantity: CHARGE. No second currency, no
    scalar "intelligence score." Conservation is enforced, not asserted (P9).

P5. THE CORE INTERACTION. An organism ATTACHES to a channel and emits exactly k
    predicted values. Extra or missing output is a typed fault, never partial
    credit. Payout is a graded distance function MULTIPLIED BY the channel's
    residual weight (P10) -- graded so that search has a gradient to climb,
    residual-weighted so that trivial channels are worth approximately nothing.

P6. COSTS. Charge is consumed by: VM steps; per-tick persistence; attaching;
    funding a deposit's escrow; and reproducing. Viability is a charge-balance
    property, never a score the engine awards.

P7. CONSTRUCTION. An organism may deposit a reusable arrangement of primitives
    that others may invoke at a cost. The engine adjudicates EFFECT, never
    meaning: there is no TOOL, LIBRARY, ALGORITHM, LANGUAGE or STRATEGY concept
    anywhere in the engine. Invocation fees are PURE TRANSFERS under conservation
    (P9), so collusive invocation is zero-sum minus fees and self-eliminates.

P8. LOCALITY (deferred). World-0 v1 has no spatial locality. Not needed to test
    the hypothesis; it multiplies the state space. Deferred unless review objects.

P9. CONSERVATION -- THE LOAD-BEARING RULE (revision R1).
    (a) WORLD channels are the ONLY exogenous source of charge. Each injects a
        FIXED budget per tick -- "the sun." Nothing else creates charge.
    (b) That budget is RIVALROUS: it is split among the organisms attached to
        that channel in proportion to their residual-weighted accuracy share. So
        being the BEST predictor of a channel matters, not merely an adequate one.
    (c) DEPOSIT channels create NO charge. They pay their predictors solely from
        an ESCROW that the depositing organism funds out of its own charge, and
        they carry per-tick upkeep drawn from that same escrow. Escrow empty =
        the deposit stops paying and is evicted.
    Consequences, obtained without a single anti-cheating rule: mutual-prediction
    wash trading is strictly negative-sum; self-prediction is pure loss; kin
    collusion between clones cannot mint; a steganographic channel (two clones
    sharing a PRNG seed, maximally "surprising" to the null battery) extracts at
    most what its depositor funded, minus fees; and channel spam is bounded
    because persistence must be paid for. Persistence of a deposit therefore IS
    evidence of ongoing funding, which is exactly the property that makes
    cross-generational epistemic inheritance meaningful rather than free.

P10. RESIDUAL WEIGHTING AND THE NULL BATTERY (revision R2).
    A channel's payout is scaled by how badly a FROZEN, MINIMAL, engine-owned
    null battery predicts it. The battery is deliberately tiny -- constant,
    last-value, first-difference, and period-p repeat for small p -- and is
    frozen, hashed and preregistered. A channel the battery already predicts is
    worth ~0, which prices the generator's trivial head out of the economy and
    removes anything worth "attach-shopping" for.
    Additionally, a candidate generator is admitted to the channel population
    only if the battery FAILS on its output (behavioural reject-sampling). The
    preregistered claim then becomes "the CONDITIONED generator distribution is
    stable," and the rejection rate is logged.
    *** THIS IS THE MOST DANGEROUS COMPONENT IN THE DESIGN. A rich battery would
    mean the world pays for "beat these specific human-chosen model classes,"
    relocating the authored ladder (K2) into the reward function where it is
    harder to see. The battery must stay minimal, frozen, and adversarially
    reviewed. ***

P11. TWO-PHASE TICK WITH SIMULTANEOUS SETTLEMENT.
    Because payout is rivalrous, sequential settlement would make an organism's
    charge depend on iteration order, silently destroying the bit-exact replay
    that K8 exists to guarantee. Phase 1 evaluates every organism's predictions
    against the tick's channel values, moving no charge. Phase 2 settles all
    transfers from that snapshot under a canonical ordering. Replay determinism
    is a physics property, not an implementation detail.

P12. ABSTENTION IS A FIRST-CLASS TYPED INTERACTION (see section 7).
    Every fix above makes acting expensive, which selects for organisms that fail
    less by attempting less -- and a non-attempt would emit no failure vector at
    all. That would deform the PRIMARY OBSERVABLE for purely economic reasons.
    Therefore non-attachment and attach-then-no-output emit coordinate records of
    their own. We instrument the dog that did not bark.

P10. NO PRIVILEGED FORM. The engine never inspects an organism's internals, never
     rewards legibility, and never prefers one representation to another.

WHY THIS HAS DEPTH RATHER THAN DIFFICULTY (charter section 6 / question 10):
difficulty would be a bigger number; depth is a change in what is exploitable.
Here, mastering shallow channel structure raises charge surplus, which funds
attachment to deeper channels and deposit emission, which creates new
regularities for others to exploit, which changes the payoff of modelling
machinery. The ladder is not encoded; it is a consequence of a compositional
generator meeting a charge economy.

*** THE CENTRAL DESIGN RISK, STATED PLAINLY: this depth claim is an ASSUMPTION,
not a result. If the generator distribution has a thin tail of exploitable
structure -- if deep compositions look statistically like noise -- then there is
no ladder to climb and World-0 is a difficulty world wearing a depth costume.
Testing this is the primary job of Campaign 0. See sections 17 and 18. ***

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. ORGANISM / CLIENT INTERFACE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT AN ORGANISM IS (question 2): a genotype (VM program, content-addressed), a
charge balance, a lineage identity, a generation index, and whatever internal
state it maintains between ticks within its own memory bounds. It is nothing else.
The engine has no model of its beliefs, goals, or strategy.

WHAT IT CAN OBSERVE (question 3), all costed:
  * the value history of channels it is attached to, up to a bounded window
  * a channel's public descriptor: id, class, age, current attach count
    (NOT its generator, NOT its depth -- depth is an analysis coordinate, never
    an observation, or we would be handing out the difficulty label)
  * its own charge and remaining quanta
  * the identifiers of deposits legally available to it
Nothing else. In particular: no aggregate leaderboard, no other organism's
internals, no engine telemetry, and no analysis products. Telemetry is for the
scientist; it must never become advice to the organism.

WHAT IT CAN DO (question 4): ATTACH, PREDICT, DEPOSIT, INVOKE (a deposit),
REPRODUCE, and idle. That is the entire action set. Six verbs.

BOUNDARY DISCIPLINE (charter section 1): the engine answers "what happened";
the organism answers "what does it mean and what next." Any feature that would
have the engine suggest, rank, score, or explain is rejected by construction.

A NOTE ON A CATEGORY ERROR WE MUST NOT MAKE: SFE already has an epistemic
protocol in which the RESEARCHER registers prospective predictions before an
experiment commits. World-0 organisms also "predict." THESE ARE DIFFERENT LAYERS
AND MUST NEVER BE CONFLATED. Organism prediction is world physics. Researcher
prediction is the scientific protocol governing our claims about the world. The
packet keeps them lexically distinct throughout.

4. RESOURCE AND SELECTION MODEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SELECTION IS MINIMAL-CRITERION, NOT FITNESS-PROPORTIONAL. An organism that holds
charge above a viability floor for a required interval may reproduce; it is not
ranked against a global best. Rationale: fitness-proportional selection in a small
coevolving world reliably converges to one narrow strategy (kill criterion K3),
and Minimal Criterion Coevolution exists in the literature precisely because of
this failure mode. A minimal criterion preserves ecological breadth, which is the
substrate on which failure geometry is even definable.

FITNESS IS NOT A FIELD. There is no fitness column. Viability is derived: did the
organism maintain positive charge balance, and did it reproduce? Everything else
is analysis.

RIVALROUS PAYOUT. The per-tick world charge budget is finite and shared across
attached organisms, so being the best predictor of a channel matters more than
being an adequate predictor of everything.

COMPUTE ACCOUNTING IS THE SAME CURRENCY AS SURVIVAL. Because charge is spent per
VM step, "thinking harder" is directly costly. This makes the compute-confound
control (K6) native rather than retrofitted: capability comparisons are reported
at equal total steps and equal quanta, and the engine's lineage-scoped budgets
already guarantee a fork cannot mint fresh compute.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. REPRODUCTION / MUTATION / INHERITANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RECOMMENDED FOR v1: yes, but minimal.

  REPRODUCTION  asexual, costed, produces a child genotype from the parent under
                a mutation operator. Parentage is recorded as a lineage edge.
  MUTATION      point substitution, insertion, deletion, and duplication of
                instruction subsequences. Duplication matters: gene duplication
                is one of the few mechanisms with real evidence for creating
                material for new function without an authored ladder.
  RECOMBINATION deferred to v2 unless review objects. It adds a second population
                structure and its own confounds.

TWO INHERITANCE CHANNELS (charter section 8), kept mechanically separate so that
either can be ablated independently:
  A. ORGANISMIC  the genotype, transmitted at reproduction.
  B. EPISTEMIC   deposits that persist beyond the depositor's lifetime and remain
                 legally available to descendants and (subject to policy) others.

THE SEPARATION IS THE POINT. The no-epistemic-inheritance control (section 11)
disables B while leaving A intact. If capability is unchanged, kill criterion K7
(inheritance theater) fires and the cumulative-culture story dies. That is a
result worth having.

WE DO NOT CALL DEPOSITS THEOREMS, TOOLS, OR KNOWLEDGE. They are arrangements of
primitives with measurable effects. Naming them would be the first step toward
scoring them.

6. CONSTRUCTIBLE-WORLD MODEL (LAYER 1)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A deposit is: a content-addressed program fragment, an invocation cost, an
emitting lineage, and an availability record. The engine enforces invocation
bounds (including a recursion/expansion limit, which is both a physics rule and a
containment rule) and nothing else.

DEPOSITS ARE SIMULTANEOUSLY: an epistemic-inheritance vehicle (section 5B); an
ecological modification, because a deposit channel changes what others face; and
the primary object of causal ablation (section 9).

DEPOSIT VISIBILITY IS POLICY-GATED, reusing the engine's existing sharing
topology: within a lineage by default; across lineages only through an explicit,
registered, bilateral group. This is what makes "no epistemic inheritance,"
"lineage-only inheritance," and "open inheritance" three configurable treatment
arms rather than three code branches.

7. FAILURE-COORDINATE SCHEMA (THE PRIMARY SCIENTIFIC PRODUCT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Normative definition: incubator/failure_coordinate_schema.v0.json (machine
readable, versioned, shipped with this packet).

PRINCIPLE: failure is never "success = false." Every externally observable
unsuccessful interaction emits a COORDINATE VECTOR carrying: world/tick/ledger
anchor; organism, lineage, generation, ancestor-path hash; ENGINE identity and
WORLD-PHYSICS identity; the interaction context (channel class, generator depth,
generator hash, age, contention, and for deposits the ORIGIN LINEAGE); the
resource state at failure; a coarse outcome class; and then the actual geometry --
VM fault code with program counter and composition DEPTH, or prediction miss with
correct-prefix length, first divergence index, and residual structure.

FRONTIER MARKERS. Each record carries a discretized region_id plus
lineage-first-encounter and population-first-encounter flags. This is the
instrument for the charter's key insight that a MORE capable organism may look
worse because it has reached states where new things can fail. Novel failure is
preserved as candidate evidence of frontier expansion and is never suppressed,
downsampled, or aggregated away.

DISAPPEARANCE VS METABOLIZATION (charter section 12). The seven causes are
separated as follows:
  solved                  -> same region still reachable, now succeeded
  avoided                 -> region no longer entered though still reachable
                             (requires the reachability probe, section 9)
  environment changed     -> generator/deposit population shifted; detectable
                             from channel provenance
  niche abandoned         -> lineage attachment distribution moved
  pressure removed        -> competitor lineage extinct
  representation changed  -> coordinate no longer applies; SEE SECTION 18, THIS
                             IS THE HARDEST CASE AND WE DO NOT FULLY SOLVE IT
  measurement broke       -> replay audit fails (K8)
The engine already models the "metabolized" case explicitly: a failure can be
recorded as CONSUMED_BY a downstream object, as a CLAIMED reference that does not
by itself assert usefulness. World-0 inherits that discipline unchanged.

8. FAILURE-TOPOLOGY ANALYSIS PROPOSAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE HYPOTHESIS UNDER TEST: changes in failure topology PRECEDE measurable
capability transitions.

THIS IS THE MOST DANGEROUS ANALYSIS IN THE PROGRAM, because the failure landscape
is high-dimensional, we will have many candidate statistics and many windows, and
we very much want the answer to be yes. Left uncontrolled, something will always
appear to precede something. Discipline is therefore mandatory, and is enforced
as kill criterion K12.

ADVERSARIAL REVIEW FOUND FOUR FATAL DEFECTS IN THE FIRST VERSION OF THIS ANALYSIS.
Each would have GUARANTEED A POSITIVE RESULT UNDER THE NULL. They are listed
first because they constrain everything after.

  D1. DEFINITIONAL COUPLING (the hypothesis was partly tautological).
      Capability is defined by prediction residual. But "first-divergence index"
      and "residual structure" are monotone functions of THAT SAME RESIDUAL. A
      finer-resolution readout of a quantity necessarily moves before a coarse
      threshold on the same quantity is crossed. We would have "discovered" that
      a thermometer precedes a fever.
      MANDATORY FIX: partition every failure coordinate, BEFORE any analysis,
      into CAPABILITY-COUPLED (any function of prediction residual on channels
      the capability metric reads) and CAPABILITY-DISJOINT (VM fault class,
      fault depth, attachment/abstention structure, resource state, channel
      provenance). Freeze the partition by content hash. THE CONFIRMATORY TEST
      RUNS ONLY ON THE DISJOINT SET. Coupled coordinates are exploratory only.

  D2. SAMPLING-INTENSITY CONFOUND. Failure vectors are emitted per interaction,
      and interaction count is itself under selection (more charge -> more
      attachments). A lineage approaching a transition is therefore SAMPLED MORE
      DENSELY by construction, and every topology statistic named in the charter
      -- clustering, density, boundary movement, basin counts -- is sample-size
      sensitive. We would measure our own sampling rate.
      MANDATORY FIX: rarefaction. Every topology statistic is computed on exactly
      n0 interactions subsampled uniformly per lineage-window, n0 fixed in the
      prereg; windows with fewer than n0 interactions are dropped, not padded.

  D3. UNIT-OF-ANALYSIS INFLATION. One long run is n=1 for any population-level
      claim. Organisms under minimal-criterion selection are near-clonal and
      strongly correlated; lineages share ancestry; generations are serially
      dependent. Treating organisms or generations as replicates would inflate
      significance by orders of magnitude.
      MANDATORY FIX: the independent RUN (independent world seed) is the unit of
      analysis for population-level claims; the transition EVENT is the unit for
      the precursor claim. A pilot establishes the transition base rate and is
      DISCARDED from the confirmatory analysis. If the affordable number of
      independent runs is too small to support the claim, we say so and do not
      make the claim.

  D4. "PRECEDES" HAD NO PRE-COMMITTED STATISTIC, WINDOW, OR CAPABILITY VARIABLE,
      and "failure topology" is undefined without a supplied embedding, metric,
      clustering algorithm and resolution -- each a free parameter. Three free
      parameters make the claim satisfiable by construction.
      MANDATORY FIX: pre-commit exactly one of each, by content hash.

REQUIRED PRE-COMMITMENTS, SEALED VIA THE ENGINE'S IRREVERSIBLE EXPERIMENT-COMMIT
BOUNDARY BEFORE CAMPAIGN 1 (this is precisely what that boundary is for):
  (a) EMBEDDING E: fixed and NOT learned -- one-hot VM fault class, rarefied
      fault-depth histogram over fixed bins, binned channel-generator depth,
      binned charge decile, and abstention rate. All CAPABILITY-DISJOINT by
      construction (D1).
  (b) PRIMARY STATISTIC: occupancy-boundary flux on E -- the rate at which the
      occupied region set gains cells adjacent to previously occupied cells while
      losing interior cells. Exactly one statistic. Computed under rarefaction.
  (c) CAPABILITY-TRANSITION DETECTOR: a sustained step change in
      interactions-to-criterion on HELD-OUT channel families, compute-matched.
  (d) LEAD-TIME WINDOW W: named before data is seen.
  (e) THRESHOLD and DECISION RULE, stated as a single test.

NULL MODELS (all six required; each breaks exactly one dependency and preserves
the rest): circular BLOCK permutation within lineage, with block length at least
twice the measured integrated autocorrelation time; lineage-label shuffle;
time-offset shuffle; a NEUTRAL-DRIFT arm using the same mutation operator and
channel population but no selection; a compute-matched random-search arm; and a
rarefaction-only arm that holds sampling constant while removing selection.
RATIONALE: evolutionary series are strongly autocorrelated. For two independent
AR(1) series at autocorrelation 0.9, effective sample size for cross-correlation
falls to roughly n/10 -- so naive lead-lag testing inflates type-I error about
tenfold. Bonferroni is the WRONG correction here; the correct null is a
MAX-STATISTIC null over the whole search space actually searched.

CONFIRMATORY VS EXPLORATORY, SPLIT IN THE PREREG WITH DIFFERENT RULES:
  CONFIRMATORY -- exactly one statistic, one capability variable, one window, one
    threshold: a single test on capability-disjoint coordinates.
  EXPLORATORY -- clustering, density, basins, fractures, bottlenecks, ridges, and
    all capability-coupled coordinates. Reported as exploratory, evaluated
    against a max-statistic null over the full enumerated search space, and
    permitted to generate World-1 hypotheses ONLY. They may never support a
    World-0 claim. Enumerating honestly, this space is tens of thousands of
    tests; pretending otherwise is how programs like this fool themselves.

ONE FURTHER TRAP, RECORDED: the NOVEL-REGION flag is a first-passage functional
of a lineage's own path. Any exploring process -- including a pure random walk
under no selection -- shows elevated first-passage rates before a subsequent
path record, as a near-mathematical identity. The raw novel-region rate must
NEVER be used as a precursor. Only its RESIDUAL against the matched
neutral-drift arm may be used.

COMPARATORS (charter section 20): the primary statistic must be evaluated against
aggregate fitness, success rate, resource efficiency, behavioural novelty, and
lineage diversity, plus Bedau-style evolutionary activity. The question is not
"does failure topology move" -- it will. The question is whether it moves EARLIER
or MORE INFORMATIVELY than cheap conventional metrics. If it does not, we revise
or kill the hypothesis and publish that.

9. REPLAY AND CAUSAL-ABLATION DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

REPLAY. Every episode is addressed by (genotype_addr, world_seed, episode_seed,
deposit_closure_hash, world_physics_hash, engine_source_hash). Re-execution must
reproduce the recorded result hash and the recorded failure coordinate bit-exactly.
Divergence tolerance on the deterministic sample is ZERO (K8).

ABLATION uses machinery the engine already has, which is the single strongest
argument that this program is buildable:
  * CHECKPOINT + FORK-BY-REFERENCE gives counterfactual branching in which the
    child shares the parent's immutable event prefix and cannot mutate it. The
    "with X" and "without X" arms are two forks of one checkpoint, so they share
    an identical, verifiable past.
  * THE INFORMATION-AVAILABILITY FRONTIER QUERY gives, for any world and any
    ledger sequence, the set of information identities that were LEGALLY
    AVAILABLE at that point -- explicitly not what was read, used, or causally
    responsible. That distinction is exactly the one this program needs, and it
    is already implemented and tested.
  * RESTRICTED-CLOSURE FORK (the one genuinely new ablation primitive, P0 item 3
    in section 15): fork a world at a checkpoint with the deposit closure
    restricted to exclude a nominated structure X, holding genotype and seeds
    fixed.

THE TARGET EVIDENCE SHAPE:
    replay descendant D with X            -> capability C
    replay descendant D without X         -> capability C absent or degraded
    remove X1 upstream                    -> X2, X3 fail to arise
                                          -> C disappears
That is an epistemic dependency chain, and it is categorically stronger than a
correlation between generation number and performance.

MEASURED ON ABLATION: capability loss, failure-landscape reversion (does the
occupied region set revert to an ancestral configuration?), search-cost increase,
lost reachable states, and adaptation-rate change.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. L0-L3 OPERATIONAL DEFINITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

L0 ADAPTATION. Within a fixed channel family and fixed world configuration, a
   lineage's charge balance and predictive accuracy improve across generations.
   EVIDENCE: improvement significant against a compute-matched random-search
   control. NOT SUFFICIENT FOR ANYTHING. A spectacular L0 organism is a
   spectacular game player.

L1 TRANSFER. Machinery acquired under channel family A improves performance on a
   distinct but structurally related family B, relative to a lineage of matched
   compute and matched generation that never saw A.
   EVIDENCE: held-out family performance, matched controls.

L2 EVOLVABILITY. Acquired machinery REDUCES THE COST OF ACQUIRING FUTURE
   CAPABILITY: interactions-to-criterion on novel families falls for descendants
   carrying X and does not fall for matched descendants without X.
   EVIDENCE: requires the ablation of section 9, compute-matched. This is the
   beginning of the phenomenon Prometheus cares about.

L3 EPISTEMIC TRANSITION. A new structure makes a previously INACCESSIBLE class of
   capability reachable -- not merely cheaper. Operationally: a region of the
   channel-depth/structure space with zero successful occupancy across all
   lineages and all compute budgets before X, and non-trivial occupancy after,
   with the dependency established by removal.
   EVIDENCE: reachability, not rate. Requires the strongest ablation.

CLAIM DISCIPLINE: L2 and L3 may NEVER be claimed from behavioural impression,
from a suggestive plot, or from a narrative. Counterfactual evidence or no claim.

                          | deposit-RICH medium | deposit-STRIPPED medium
    evolved descendant    |         A           |          B

13. CONTAINMENT MODEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Organisms are INTERPRETED DATA inside a bounded VM. They have no access to
networking, filesystem, shells, subprocesses, host processes, credentials, host
executable formats, persistence outside the engine, or any host API. No
instruction can generate, load, or evaluate host code; there is no JIT and no
escape hatch to native execution. Steps, memory, deposit invocation depth, and
output size are all bounded, and exceeding a bound is an ordinary, recorded
physics fault rather than an error condition.

Their capabilities are meaningful inside their universe and useless outside it.
This is treated as foundational: K10 is zero-tolerance and triggers immediate
program halt and disclosure, not a bug ticket.

A NOTE ON THE LITERATURE: this work draws conceptual lessons from digital
evolution and host-parasite coevolution research. It does not and must not
produce anything resembling self-replicating code with host-level effect. The
self-replication in World-0 is replication of interpreted data within an
engine-owned population structure -- categorically different from Tierra-style
replication in a shared machine-code address space, and deliberately so.

    1. Does a trivial policy dominate? (K0 -- the most likely failure)

18. ASSUMPTIONS I BELIEVE ARE WRONG OR WEAK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This section is the reason to read this packet. I am the instrument maintainer,
not the advocate, and these are the places where I think the charter is betting
more than it has.

A1. THE COORDINATE-STABILITY PARADOX -- MY STRONGEST OBJECTION.
    Measuring "deformation of the failure landscape" presupposes a coordinate
    system stable enough to compare generation 100 against generation 800. But
    the charter ALSO anticipates (section 12, cause 6) that a representation may
    change so radically that the old failure coordinate no longer applies -- and
    that is precisely the epistemic transition we most want to catch. These pull
    in opposite directions. A coordinate system rigid enough to track deformation
    may be exactly the one that cannot register the transitions that matter; a
    coordinate system that adapts is no longer a fixed frame and its "movement"
    is partly our own construction.
    World-0 mitigates this by anchoring coordinates in PHYSICS-INTRINSIC events
    (VM fault codes, composition depth, divergence index) rather than in learned
    or semantic categories -- these remain meaningful regardless of how the
    organism's internals change. I believe that is the best available answer. I
    do NOT believe it is a complete answer, and reviewers should treat any
    claimed L3 transition as suspect until this is addressed properly.

A2. "PREDICTION PAYS" IS ITSELF A HUMAN ONTOLOGY.
    The charter forbids selecting for "become alien" on the grounds that it
    injects a human ontology. Choosing to pay charge for predictive accuracy does
    the same thing more quietly: we have decided that MODELLING is what the world
    rewards. A world could instead pay for persistence, replication, control, or
    construction, and would select for different machinery. This is defensible --
    prediction is the least anthropomorphic operationalization I can find, and it
    does not privilege any representational FORM -- but it is not neutral and the
    packet should not pretend otherwise.

A3. THE GENERATOR'S TAIL MAY BE NOISE, NOT DEPTH.
    The depth claim requires that deeper compositions be exploitably regular. For
    many program distributions, deep random composition is statistically
    indistinguishable from noise, in which case there is no ladder and organisms
    plateau at shallow structure. The fix -- biasing the generator toward
    compositional REUSE -- edges toward authoring the ladder we claim not to
    author. This is a genuine, unresolved tension between charter section 6
    (depth) and kill criterion K2 (no authored ladder), and I flag it as needing
    reviewer adjudication (section 19, Q2).

A4. THE HUMAN-PRIOR EXPERIMENT IS PROBABLY NOT RUNNABLE HERE, MAYBE NOT ANYWHERE.
    Comparing an H arm (frontier-model / human-derived primitives) against a P arm
    under "identical physics" is close to incoherent: you cannot compute-match a
    language model against a bytecode organism (K6), the H arm imports exactly the
    semantics the design excludes (K9), and any result is dominated by the
    arbitrary choice of how the human corpus is encoded into primitives. I
    recommend World-0 preserve the INTERFACE and run P only. I would go further
    for review: I am not convinced the H-vs-P comparison is a well-posed
    experiment at all, and I would rather we said so than ran a version of it that
    produces a publishable-looking number.

A5. SCALE MAY PUT L2 OUT OF REACH.
    Published digital-evolution results of comparable ambition consumed very large
    numbers of updates. If World-0 can afford only thousands of generations, we may
    be instrumented to detect a phenomenon we cannot afford to grow. Campaign 0
    must report throughput precisely, and if the implied generation budget is far
    below what L2 detection needs, that is a reason to revise scope BEFORE
    Campaign 1 rather than to run a hopeful campaign.

A6. COEVOLUTION IS AS LIKELY TO PRODUCE A CLOSED ECONOMY AS A CURRICULUM.
    The most probable degenerate attractor is organisms paying each other for
    mutual predictability -- deposit-to-deposit loops that yield charge without
    reducing uncertainty about the world. Added as K11.

A7. WE WILL FIND STRUCTURE IN THE FAILURE LANDSCAPE WHETHER OR NOT IT IS THERE.
    High-dimensional data, many statistics, motivated observers. The section 8
    pre-commitment protocol is not bureaucratic caution; without it this program's
    headline result is unfalsifiable by construction.

A9. THE FIXES INTRODUCE THEIR OWN AUTHORING PROBLEM (found by review, and I do
    not consider it closed). Residual-weighted payout requires an engine-owned
    null battery, and the battery IS a supplied model class. A minimal battery
    (constant, last-value, first-difference, period-p) defines "trivial" and
    seems defensible. A rich battery would mean the world pays for beating
    human-chosen model classes, which is kill criterion K2 relocated into the
    reward function where it is much harder to see. There is no bright line
    between the two, only a judgement about minimality. This is now the second
    place (with A3) where the design's integrity rests on a judgement call about
    how much structure we are allowed to supply.

A10. THE PAYOFF SHAPE AND THE VIABILITY THRESHOLD ARE THE STRONGEST AUTHORED
    GRADIENTS IN THE WORLD, and the first draft hid them inside the phrase "paid
    as a function of accuracy." A graded partial-credit payoff authors a smooth
    ladder; an all-or-nothing payoff authors a cliff and may make the world
    inert. Neither is neutral. These must be declared as SUPPLIED choices in a
    supplied-versus-measured table, and the payoff shape should be a
    preregistered ARM (graded / thresholded / prefix-exact), not a silent
    constant chosen by the designer.

A11. THE STARVATION--FREE-LUNCH BRACKET IS NARROW AND ADJACENT. Above a cost
    line, naive organisms free-ride forever (K0). Below it, the dominant strategy
    is to do nothing at all -- never attach, never run steps -- and the world is
    inert. These two failure modes sit next to each other, and the first draft
    had no calibration protocol at all. Campaign 0 must set costs empirically so
    that a null-battery-equivalent organism nets approximately zero over a long
    run, and must report how wide the viable corridor actually is. If the
    corridor is very narrow, that is itself evidence the world is fragile.

A12. WE MAY HAVE MADE FAILURE TOO EXPENSIVE TO OBSERVE. Every economic fix makes
    acting costly, which selects for organisms that fail less by attempting less.
    Abstention is now instrumented as a first-class typed interaction (P12), but
    this is a mitigation, not a solution: a world in which the optimal policy is
    caution produces a thin failure landscape, and the thinness is caused by our
    own cost structure rather than by anything about intelligence.

A8. "SMALLEST DEFENSIBLE" AND "GENUINELY OPEN-ENDED" MAY BE INCOMPATIBLE.
    The charter asks for a world small enough for exhaustive counterfactual replay
    AND rich enough for unbounded epistemic compounding. Those may simply trade
    off. My judgement is that World-0 should buy REPLAYABILITY at the cost of
    open-endedness, and treat open-endedness as a World-1 question -- but that is a
    judgement call a reviewer may reasonably overturn.

19. QUESTIONS REQUIRING EXTERNAL REVIEWER ADJUDICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q1. Is "the world pays for predictive accuracy" an acceptable operationalization
    of fitness, or does it prejudge the epistemic ontology (A2)? If unacceptable,
    what alternative payout is less anthropomorphic without becoming a mere
    survival game?

Q2. WHERE IS THE LINE between "biasing the channel generator toward compositional
    reuse so that depth is exploitable" and "authoring the ladder" (K2)? This is
    the single most consequential unresolved design question (A3).

Q3. Do you accept the coordinate-stability mitigation of A1 -- anchoring failure
    coordinates in physics-intrinsic faults -- or does the paradox invalidate
    claims of landscape deformation across a representational transition?

Q4. Do you accept deferring the H and X arms out of World-0 (R2, A4), and do you
    consider the H-vs-P comparison well-posed in principle?

Q5. What total compute budget is authorized? This determines whether L2 is
    reachable at all (A5) and should be settled before Campaign 1, not discovered
    during it.

Q6. Should organism-organism interaction (deposit channels) be present in v1 at
    all, or deferred to v2 to keep Campaign 0 interpretable? Including it makes
    the world interesting and also makes K11 immediately live.

Q7. Is minimal-criterion selection the right choice over fitness-proportional
    selection, given that it slows convergence and therefore raises the compute
    cost of any result?

Q8. Do you accept the pre-commitment protocol of section 8 as SUFFICIENT to make
    the tectonic hypothesis falsifiable, and is the proposed primary statistic
    (occupancy-boundary flux) the right primary?
