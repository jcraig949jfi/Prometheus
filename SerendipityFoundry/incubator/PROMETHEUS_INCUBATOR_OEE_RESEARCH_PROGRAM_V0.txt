PROGRAM THESIS. The target is not novelty, not difficulty, not proficiency inside
a fixed game. The target is EPISTEMIC COMPOUNDING: acquired machinery causally
increasing the ability to acquire further useful machinery, repeatedly expanding
the reachable capability space. The signature is a causal chain
R1 -> R2 -> R3 -> capability region C, with counterfactual evidence that removing
upstream machinery breaks downstream acquisition.

THE REFRAME. We previously treated the artificial-life / open-ended-evolution
literature as a parts bin. That was too conservative. The field has spent decades
producing extraordinarily valuable partial results and extraordinarily informative
FAILURES. The failures are the opportunity. This document converts them into
experiments.

THE FOUNDATIONAL DISTINCTION, stated once and never violated:
  We must NOT author a reasoning ladder for the ORGANISMS.
  We MAY build a scientific ladder of falsification experiments for OURSELVES.
Every design decision below is checked against that line.

1. REVISED WORLD-0 SCOPE (AFTER REMOVING DEPOSITS AND ECOLOGICAL CONSTRUCTION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RETAINED (the irreducible core):
  * deterministic interpreted bytecode VM; genotypes are DATA, never host code
  * primitive genome; mutation (substitution, insertion, deletion, DUPLICATION)
  * reproduction, costed
  * ONE conserved resource: CHARGE
  * COSTED COMPUTE: VM steps are BOUGHT from the organism's own charge; a per-tick
    step ceiling exists only as a determinism bound (a ceiling is not an allowance)
  * WORLD channels only: value sequences from hidden generator programs drawn from
    a compositional distribution over the same instruction set, unbounded depth;
    INSTANCES resampled, DISTRIBUTION stable
  * residual weighting against a FROZEN, MINIMAL null battery (constant,
    last-value, first-difference, period-p), plus behavioural reject-sampling of
    candidate generators
  * lineage identity, generation, ancestry
  * bit-exact replay
  * typed failure coordinates, including ABSTENTION as a first-class class
  * strict containment

REMOVED:
  deposits; organism-created channels; cultural / epistemic inheritance;
  host-parasite ecology; communication; spatial locality; LLM anything; Evidence
  Wiki integration; human-prior (H/X) comparison; multi-level selection.

THE ONE ECOLOGICAL FORCE THAT REMAINS, and it comes free with conservation:
each WORLD channel injects a FIXED charge budget per tick, split among attached
organisms in proportion to residual-weighted accuracy share. That makes payout
RIVALROUS and therefore FREQUENCY-DEPENDENT: if everyone crowds the same channel,
per-capita return falls, creating pressure toward partitioning across channels.
This is the minimal ecology, it requires no new machinery, and it is the only
organism-organism interaction World-0 has.

FOUR CONSEQUENCES OF THE STRIPPING -- THREE OF THEM SCIENTIFIC GAINS:

  (a) THE WASH-TRADING EXPLOIT DISAPPEARS. The previous review's most serious
      economic defect was that deposit channels let organisms mint charge by
      predicting each other. With no deposits there is nothing to mint from, and
      the escrow machinery designed to fix it is UNNECESSARY. "WORLD channels are
      the only exogenous source" becomes trivially true rather than a rule.

  (b) THE L2 IDENTIFIABILITY CONFOUND DISAPPEARS. Adversarial review found that
      evolvability was unidentifiable from environmental accumulation: a
      descendant might acquire faster because the MEDIUM accumulated useful
      deposits, not because the LINEAGE acquired machinery -- and the
      environmental explanation was the more likely one. With no deposits there
      is no medium accumulation, so the environmental component is STRUCTURALLY
      ZERO and the organismic component is measured directly. The 2x2 transplant
      factorial collapses to a single clean comparison. This is a real gain.

  (c) K11 (economic degeneracy) becomes moot for World-0. Retained in the
      criteria set for later worlds that reintroduce deposits.

  (d) THE COST: World-0 has NO coevolution, NO Red Queen, NO endogenous
      curriculum, and NO endogenous opportunity creation. Accepted deliberately.

*** THE MOST IMPORTANT CONSEQUENCE, AND IT IS A RISK, NOT A GAIN:
WITH DEPOSITS REMOVED, THE COMPOSITIONAL GENERATOR IS THE SOLE SOURCE OF
EXPLOITABLE DEPTH IN WORLD-0. There is no longer any second source of structure.
If the generator's tail is statistically noise rather than exploitable regularity,
World-0 contains NO DEPTH AT ALL and every downstream question is unanswerable.
That makes the structure probe (section 21) the single load-bearing measurement
of the entire program, and it is why it is now the recommended next action. ***

2. EXACT HYPOTHESIS WORLD-0 TESTS (three nested, in strict order)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

H0-1  STRUCTURE EXISTS.
      The channel generator distribution contains EXPLOITABLE STRUCTURAL DEPTH:
      there exist channels on which the frozen null battery fails AND which some
      reachable VM program predicts; and predictability degrades with generator
      composition depth in a graded way rather than collapsing to noise past a
      shallow horizon.
      NOTE: this is a property of the WORLD ALONE. It requires no organisms, no
      population, no economy, and no evolution. It is testable first and cheapest.

H0-2  EVOLUTION EXPLOITS IT.
      Under conserved charge and costed compute, an evolving population discovers
      machinery that exploits DEEPER structure than compute-matched random search
      and compute-matched neutral drift.
      (This is E1/L0 -- adaptation. It is necessary and deeply insufficient.)

H0-3  ACQUIRED MACHINERY REDUCES LATER ACQUISITION COST.
      Evolved internal structure X reduces the acquisition cost of capability on
      HELD-OUT channel families relative to matched lineages lacking X, with the
      dependency established by REMOVAL ABLATION, under matched compute, mutation
      opportunity, exposure, and population.
      (This is the E3 evolvability precursor -- the first rung Prometheus
      actually cares about.)

World-0 succeeds SCIENTIFICALLY if it answers these three in order, WHATEVER the
answers are, provided the answers are reconstructable.

3. WHAT WORLD-0 EXPLICITLY DOES NOT TEST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stated so that no result from World-0 is ever over-claimed:

  * epistemic / cultural inheritance and cumulative culture (no deposits)
  * coevolution, arms races, Red Queen dynamics (no organism-created environment)
  * endogenous opportunity creation (organisms cannot change the world)
  * major evolutionary transitions (no multi-level structure or reproduction)
  * environmental construction / niche construction
  * communication, signalling, division of labour
  * the human-prior hypothesis (no H/X arms -- see section 12 for the synthetic
    substitute that IS runnable)
  * escape from the ontology ceiling (World-0 can PILOT a measure, section 12,
    but cannot answer the question)
  * E4, E5, E6, E7 of the hierarchy in section 9 -- ALL structurally unreachable
    in World-0. Any apparent E4+ signal in World-0 is a measurement error and
    should be treated as such until proven otherwise.

4. OEE FAILURE ATLAS v0
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
UNIT OF ANALYSIS: the FAILED OR LIMITED DYNAMICAL REGIME, not the paper.

The provisional taxonomy F1-F17 (policy saturation; novelty without accumulation;
complexity without functional expansion; coevolutionary cycling; ontology ceiling;
representational lock-in; premature convergence; resource trap; exploration
without retention; retention without metabolization; metabolization without
transfer; environmental exhaustion; difficulty masquerading as depth;
scalar-fitness collapse; major-transition failure; evolvability plateau;
measurement illusion) is ADOPTED AS A WORKING TAXONOMY AND EXPLICITLY NOT
ASSUMED CORRECT.

STRUCTURAL CRITIQUE OF THE TAXONOMY (before any evidence):

  (i) IT MIXES CAUSES WITH SYMPTOMS. F1 (policy saturation), F3 (complexity
      without functional expansion) and F13 (difficulty masquerading as depth)
      are largely SYMPTOMS. F5 (ontology ceiling), F8 (resource trap), F6
      (representational lock-in) and F12 (environmental exhaustion) are candidate
      CAUSES. A useful atlas must separate the observable regime from the
      mechanism producing it, because the intervention attaches to the mechanism.

  (ii) SEVERAL CLASSES ARE NOT DYNAMICALLY DISTINGUISHABLE IN DATA without an
      ablation. F9/F10/F11 (exploration without retention / retention without
      metabolization / metabolization without transfer) form a single pipeline,
      and telling them apart REQUIRES the removal experiments -- they are not
      separable by observation. Same for F1 vs F16: policy saturation and
      evolvability plateau look identical on a fitness curve and are separated
      only by an acquisition-cost measurement on held-out families.

  (iii) F17 (measurement illusion) IS NOT A SIBLING OF THE OTHERS. It is a
      META-CLASS that gates our ability to detect ANY of the others. It should be
      treated as a standing methodological threat applied to every claim, not as
      one regime among seventeen.

  (iv) A CLASS IS MISSING: SELECTION-LEVEL MISMATCH -- where the level at which
      selection acts is not the level at which the interesting organization would
      have to be stabilized. This is arguably the true mechanism behind F15
      (major-transition failure) rather than a separate phenomenon.

  (v) A SECOND CLASS IS ARGUABLY MISSING: VARIATION-OPERATOR CEILING -- where the
      mutation/recombination operators cannot construct or preserve the class of
      structures that would be required, independently of selection or ontology.
      This is distinct from F5 (what the ontology permits) and from F8 (what
      selection tolerates), and it is directly actionable.

CRITIQUE FROM THE EVIDENCE SURVEY (these are corrections to the taxonomy that the
literature forces, and several change our own design):

  (vi) F5 AND F12 DO NOT SEPARATE in any system where THE REWARD CHANNEL IS THE
      ENVIRONMENT -- which is most of the corpus. They separate only when a world
      contains exploitable structure the reward channel does not price. The
      taxonomy is missing the term that makes the distinction operational:
      *** REWARD-CHANNEL BANDWIDTH *** -- distinct both from designer-supplied
      categories (F5) and from world richness (F12). Avida is the demonstration:
      its world contains real unpriced structure (spatial position, neighbour
      identity, timing) that no organism can ever be PAID for exploiting.
      DIRECT CONSEQUENCE FOR US: World-0's residual-weighted payout IS a reward
      channel of limited bandwidth. Structure that our null battery cannot see, or
      that our payoff cannot price, is invisible to selection no matter how much
      of it the generator produces. THIS IS NOW A FIRST-CLASS DESIGN CONCERN AND A
      MEASUREMENT TARGET IN THE STRUCTURE PROBE.

  (vii) F13 IS A PROPERTY OF THE OBSERVER'S METRIC, not of a system, and is
      therefore a special case of F17 -- as is much of F3, where "complexity" is
      always a chosen measure. F17 is promoted to a META-CLASS with two sub-cases:
        F17a METRIC ARTIFACT      -- the measure counts the wrong thing;
        F17b DEFINITION CIRCULARITY -- the measure's categories are the designer's
             own vocabulary and therefore CANNOT REGISTER VOCABULARY GROWTH.
      F17b is the deepest: it is the ontology ceiling applied to MEASUREMENT, and
      it is exactly the trap our own failure-coordinate schema could fall into.

  (viii) F1 AND F16 ARE DIFFERENT DERIVATIVES OF THE SAME CURVE (capability rate
      vs acquisition-cost rate) and the taxonomy does not pin the axis. Avida
      after task exhaustion is F1-positive and F16-negative SIMULTANEOUSLY:
      repertoire frozen while mutational architecture keeps improving. Unless the
      capability axis is named, these classes will be assigned inconsistently.

  (ix) F2 AND F9 ARE NOT DISTINGUISHABLE in asexual digital systems, where
      retention is automatic via lineage inheritance. They separate only where a
      population-level store exists SEPARATE from the lineage. IN WORLD-0
      (asexual, no archive) THEY COLLAPSE INTO ONE CLASS. Recorded so we do not
      claim a distinction our substrate cannot express.

  (x) F10 IS CURRENTLY UNDIAGNOSABLE, not merely rare: in every classic system the
      only available measure of "descendant capability" IS the selection currency,
      so retention-without-metabolization cannot be separated from scalar-fitness
      collapse. *** WORLD-0 HAS THE MISSING INGREDIENT: held-out channel families
      give a capability measure that is NOT the selection currency. This is a
      genuine methodological advantage of our design and should be exploited. ***

  MISSING CLASSES THE EVIDENCE DEMANDS:
    F18 REGRESSIVE EROSION -- acquired machinery is ACTIVELY DELETED once its
        reward is withdrawn, after long successful retention. Not F9 (never
        retained), not F8 (never acquired), not F10 (retained but inert).
        COMPOUNDING REQUIRES MACHINERY TO SURVIVE PERIODS OF NON-PAYMENT. The
        engineering response is near-zero carriage cost for unexpressed structure
        -- which is exactly the parameter our resource-trap sweep already varies.
    F19 UNREGISTERED CONSTRUCTION (non-writable substrate) -- inhabitants can
        build, but the world's physics never reads their construction back, so
        niche construction has no causal consequence. Not applicable to World-0
        (no construction) but CRITICAL for World-2+.
    F20 EXOGENOUSLY DRIVEN ACTIVITY MISTAKEN FOR ENDOGENOUS -- systems requiring a
        continuous external perturbation pump, whose novelty rate is a monotone
        function of that pump. *** LIVE RISK IN OUR OWN DESIGN: channel RESAMPLING
        is an external perturbation. A "continuously resampled" arm could
        manufacture apparent open-endedness that is really just our resampling
        rate. Any such arm must be paired with a perturbation-rate sweep. ***
    F21 DEMOGRAPHIC / DRIFT-LIMITED PLATEAU -- small-population, short-run
        artifacts misread as structural ceilings. Identified by the survey as
        THE SINGLE MOST LIKELY SOURCE OF SYSTEMATIC MISDIAGNOSIS when applying
        this taxonomy to historical results, precisely because the taxonomy has no
        demographic class and so the cheap control (a scale sweep) never gets run.
        A SCALE SWEEP IS THEREFORE MANDATORY BEFORE ANY CEILING CLAIM.

  RE-SCOPINGS:
    F4 IS MIS-STATED AS A FAILURE. Digital host-parasite results indicate that
       coevolutionary cycling WITH MAINTAINED DIVERSITY is the mechanism with the
       best evidence for defeating F8, F14 and F16 at once. CYCLING PER SE IS NOT
       A FAILURE; CYCLING IN A BOUNDED TRAIT SPACE IS. Renamed accordingly, or the
       taxonomy discourages the most promising mechanism in the literature.
    F15 NAMES AN ABSENT EVENT, not a dynamical regime, and every system in the
       corpus exhibits it -- near-zero discriminating power. Demote to an outcome
       flag and break out the actual causes: no enforced superadditive return to
       aggregation; no stable group-level inheritance; no defection control.
    F6 IS ASSERTED THROUGHOUT THE LITERATURE AND, AS FAR AS THE SURVEY COULD
       DETERMINE, NEVER MEASURED -- no classic system has run the counterfactual of
       restarting from an alternative early abstraction and comparing reachable
       capability. It must be marked as a HYPOTHESIS WITH A REQUIRED EXPERIMENT,
       not a class diagnosable by observation. *** THIS MAKES OUR EXPERIMENT O2
       (section 12) POTENTIALLY THE FIRST DIRECT MEASUREMENT OF REPRESENTATIONAL
       LOCK-IN, AND RAISES ITS PRIORITY ACCORDINGLY. ***

PROPOSED REFINEMENT (adopted for sections 5-8): separate SYMPTOMS from MECHANISMS.
Four symptom classes -- S1 no new machinery; S2 no accumulation across
generations; S3 no expansion of the frontier; S4 no improvement in acquisition
cost -- with the F-classes retained as MECHANISMS that produce them, each owning a
distinguishing intervention. F17 sits above both as the meta-class gating whether
any of it is measurable. F21 (demographic) must be excluded before any mechanism
is claimed.

6. STRONGEST EVIDENCE FOR EACH CLASS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The strongest single body of evidence in the field is the EVOLUTIONARY ACTIVITY
STATISTICS programme (Bedau, Packard and colleagues), which classified long-term
evolutionary dynamics into qualitative classes and found that the biosphere
exhibits unbounded adaptive activity while ARTIFICIAL systems characteristically
DO NOT reach that class. CONFIDENCE: HIGH on the qualitative claim; MEDIUM on the
precise classification of individual systems.

WHY THIS MATTERS MORE THAN ANY OTHER RESULT FOR PROMETHEUS: it is a NEGATIVE
result obtained with a purpose-built MEASURE. It simultaneously (a) supports F1/F2
as the field's characteristic failure, and (b) demonstrates the F17 discipline --
the claim is only as good as the statistic, so the statistic must be qualified
first. We adopt evolutionary-activity-style statistics as a COMPARATOR against
which our failure-topology statistic must prove its worth (section 16). If the
cheap established statistic predicts as well, we use the cheap statistic.

POSITIVE RESULTS ON INDUCING (rather than merely observing) THE PHENOMENA -- these
are the most actionable literature for us:
  * MODULARLY VARYING GOALS: environments whose objectives vary in a modular
    fashion have been reported to produce modular organization AND faster
    subsequent adaptation. CONFIDENCE MEDIUM-HIGH. This is one of the few
    published levers that plausibly acts on EVOLVABILITY ITSELF rather than on
    fitness, and it is directly portable to a channel-family design.
  * CONNECTION / WIRING COST: adding a cost term for structural connectivity has
    been reported to produce modularity. CONFIDENCE MEDIUM. Portable as a
    genome-size or structure cost.
  * ROBUSTNESS AND NEUTRAL NETWORKS: the line of work arguing that mutational
    robustness and neutral genotype networks ENABLE innovation. CONFIDENCE
    MEDIUM-HIGH. Directly relevant to F8 (resource trap): if carrying currently
    useless structure is expensive, neutral exploration is suppressed.
  * HISTORICAL CONTINGENCY AND REPLAY: long-term microbial evolution work
    established potentiation experimentally BY REPLAYING FROZEN ANCESTORS. The
    specific trait matters less to us than THE METHOD: replay-from-ancestor is
    the empirical template for our removal ablations, and it is exactly what
    checkpoint+fork gives us cheaply.
  * EXPERIMENTAL INDUCTION OF MULTICELLULARITY: a major-transition-like
    reorganization has been induced under a selection regime favouring settling
    of clusters. CONFIDENCE MEDIUM-HIGH. Evidence that transitions can be INDUCED
    by changing the selective environment, not only described after the fact.

7. COMPETING EXPLANATIONS (applied to every atlas entry)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For any observed plateau, the following must be excluded before a mechanism is
claimed. This list is deliberately the same one as section 18 of the directive,
because it is the discipline that separates a research programme from a habit:

  insufficient time * insufficient population * insufficient compute * wrong
  physics * wrong mutation operators * insufficient environmental opportunity *
  selection too harsh * selection too weak * measurement insensitive * genuine
  absence of the phenomenon.

STANDING RULE: SCALE IS AN INTERVENTION, NOT A REFLEX. "Run it longer / make it
bigger / add agents" must be a declared, preregistered fork with a prediction --
never the automatic response to a plateau. The world-phylogeny schema carries an
explicit boolean, scale_is_the_intervention, so that a scaling fork cannot be
disguised as a mechanistic one.

9. PROMETHEUS E0-E7 OPERATIONAL HIERARCHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
An OBSERVATIONAL TAXONOMY OF DYNAMICAL REGIMES. Never a task list given to
organisms. Never a badge. Regression, branching and temporary attainment are all
expected and must be recordable.

  E0 VARIATION            novel genotypes/behaviours keep appearing.
                          EVIDENCE: novelty rate above the neutral-drift arm.
                          Weakest possible condition.
  E1 ADAPTIVE ACCUM.      historically acquired changes improve descendants'
                          viability. EVIDENCE: descendant beats reconstructed
                          ancestor under matched compute.
  E2 REUSABLE INNOVATION  acquired machinery transfers across structurally
                          DISTINCT situations. EVIDENCE: held-out family
                          performance, with the structure ablated as control.
  E3 EVOLVABILITY         acquired machinery reduces the COST of acquiring
                          future machinery. EVIDENCE: acquisition-cost
                          differential under removal ablation (section 10).
                          *** THE FIRST RUNG PROMETHEUS ACTUALLY CARES ABOUT.
                          World-0's ceiling. ***
  E4 OPPORTUNITY CREATION innovations make previously-useless innovations
                          valuable. EVIDENCE: an opportunity-dependency edge
                          (section 15). UNREACHABLE IN WORLD-0.
  E5 REPRESENTATIONAL     new internal organization makes a previously
     TRANSITION           unreachable functional region accessible. EVIDENCE:
                          reachability, not rate (zero occupancy before at ANY
                          compute; non-trivial after; dependency by removal).
  E6 META-EVOLVABILITY    the mechanisms controlling evolvability themselves
                          improve cumulatively.
  E7 SUSTAINED COMPOUNDING repeated E3-E6 chains with no human-authored target
                          sequence.

CLAIM DISCIPLINE: E2 and above require COUNTERFACTUAL evidence. A trajectory, a
plot, or a behavioural impression is never sufficient. E4-E7 are structurally
unreachable in World-0 and any apparent signal there is presumed artifact.

10. EVOLVABILITY MEASUREMENT PROTOCOL (E3)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For a candidate structure X in lineage L:

  MEASURE   acquisition cost of capability family B, defined as
            interactions-to-criterion (and total VM steps to criterion) on a
            HELD-OUT family B, for:
              (i)  descendants carrying X
              (ii) descendants with X ablated (removal by restricted replay,
                   holding genotype length and seeds fixed; the ablation must
                   replace X with matched-length inert or randomized code, never
                   simply delete it -- otherwise we measure genome length)
  MATCHED   compute (total VM steps AND charge), mutation opportunity,
            environmental exposure, population size, and lineage history depth.
  REPEAT    across MULTIPLE structurally distinct held-out families. A structure
            that improves ONE family is SPECIALIZED MACHINERY, not evolvability.
            Evolvability is a claim about the DISTRIBUTION of future tasks.
  ESTIMATOR survival model (hazard of next acquisition) with lineage frailty and
            EXPLICIT CENSORING. NEVER a mean over surviving lineages: survivorship
            biases acquisition cost downward, and the bias GROWS WITH SELECTION
            STRENGTH -- i.e. it is largest exactly where E3 would be claimed.
  GUARD     exclude the acquisition event used to SELECT the lineage from the
            measurement window (otherwise regression to the mean manufactures the
            effect).
  NULL      compute-matched random search and neutral drift, both run to the same
            total steps.

THEN, AND ONLY THEN, THE SECOND-ORDER QUESTION: can mechanisms that improve
evolvability themselves evolve? That is E6, and it is not a World-0 question.

ABSOLUTE PROHIBITION (section 11 of the directive, restated because it is the
easiest rule in the programme to violate by accident): NEVER place evolvability,
generality, novelty, abstraction, modularity, or "learning to learn" into
selection. Those are SCIENTIST-SIDE MEASUREMENTS. The organism receives
consequences from the world. If evolvability emerges because it has long-run
resource consequences, that is evidence. If we reward it, we have authored the
answer and the result is worthless.

11. CYCLING-VERSUS-ESCALATION PROTOCOL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mostly a World-2+ protocol (World-0 has no coevolution), specified now so the
architecture does not preclude it. The ENVIRONMENT-transplant half IS runnable in
World-0 against ancestral channel families.

  TRANSPLANT MATRIX
    restore ancestral opponents / ancestral environments
    transplant current organisms BACKWARD into ancestral conditions
    transplant ancestors FORWARD into current conditions
  INTRANSITIVITY TEST
    if A beats B, B beats C, and C beats A -> CYCLING, not escalation.
    Cycling is a DIRECTED-GRAPH property and must be tested as one, over many
    pairs, not inferred from a rising fitness curve.
  DIRECTIONAL-EXPANSION TEST
    generation-N organisms reliably exploit conditions that generations 1..N-k
    could not, AND the responsible machinery TRANSFERS to independent contexts.
    Specialized counters do not count: the transfer requirement is what separates
    "better adapted to you" from "more generally capable."
  DISTINGUISH FIVE OUTCOMES, not two: cyclic adaptation / arms-race escalation /
  ecological diversification / niche displacement / increasing generality. Only
  the last two, with transfer evidence, bear on epistemic compounding.

12. ONTOLOGY-CEILING EXPERIMENTAL PROGRAM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE DEEPEST QUESTION IN THE PROGRAMME. Any synthetic world contains
designer-supplied physics; we cannot demand metaphysical independence from it. We
CAN demand increasing DISTANCE between supplied primitives and selected functional
organization -- and we must be ruthless about what would count.

CANDIDATE DISTANCE MEASURES (all suspect until proven to have downstream utility):
  * description length of the evolved solution relative to the primitive set
  * reusable substructure not explicitly represented in the instruction set
  * emergent equivalence classes over genotypes with stable causal roles
  * structures whose utility survives a change of SUPERFICIAL ENCODING
  * intermediate representations that are REQUIRED for a later capability

THE ONLY CRITERION THAT MATTERS: CAUSAL DOWNSTREAM UTILITY. A metric that merely
rewards complexity is worthless. A structure counts only if removing it degrades
the acquisition of something else.

EXPERIMENT O1 -- DUAL-ONTOLOGY (the core test).
  Two instruction sets A and B with MATCHED expressiveness and matched step costs
  but DIFFERENT primitives. Same channel families, same everything else.
  IF evolved functional organization converges across A and B -> the WORLD's
     structure dominates and the ceiling is loose.
  IF organization stays "A-shaped" in A and "B-shaped" in B, and the solution that
     is short in B is never found in A even at large compute -> THE CEILING BINDS,
     and we have measured it rather than asserted it.

EXPERIMENT O2 -- REPRESENTATIONAL LOCK-IN, AND THE HUMAN-PRIOR QUESTION MADE
RUNNABLE. Seed populations with (i) random genotypes, (ii) genotypes pre-adapted
to a SHALLOW family. Then measure long-run reachability of a DEEP family under
matched total compute.
  IF the pre-adapted seed is PERMANENTLY worse on the deep family despite equal
  or greater total compute -> representational lock-in (F6) is real and
  measurable.
  *** WHY THIS MATTERS BEYOND WORLD-0: this is the scientific core of the
  human-prior hypothesis -- "an early successful representation may become an
  attractor that forecloses better ones" -- tested WITHOUT an LLM, WITHOUT a
  human corpus, and WITHOUT the incoherent compute-matching problem that made the
  H-vs-P comparison ill-posed. If lock-in is demonstrable between two SYNTHETIC
  seeds, the mechanism underlying the north-star hypothesis is established on a
  substrate where we can actually run the counterfactual. ***

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
13. MAJOR-TRANSITION EXPERIMENTAL PROGRAM (World-4+)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NO SEMANTIC TARGETS. We do not insert multicellularity, society, or language.
The abstract question: WHEN DO INDEPENDENTLY SELECTED COMPONENTS BECOME PART OF A
HIGHER-LEVEL PERSISTENT ADAPTIVE UNIT?

Mechanisms later worlds may test: shared inherited structure; dependency between
organisms; persistent composite structures; reproduction at more than one level;
alignment or conflict between selection levels.

THE OPERATIONAL CRITERION, and it is strict: a collection is NOT a higher-order
unit merely because it behaves cooperatively or persists. It qualifies only if it
exhibits persistent adaptive organization WHOSE LOSS DESTROYS CAPABILITY -- i.e.
the causal ablation must be run at the level of the composite.
LEADING SUSPECTED MECHANISM (from the taxonomy critique, section 4.iv):
SELECTION-LEVEL MISMATCH. If selection acts only on individuals, higher-level
organization has no stabilizing force, and its absence is unsurprising rather
than mysterious. The first intervention should therefore be on WHERE SELECTION
ACTS, not on adding cooperative affordances.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
14. CUMULATIVE-CULTURE EXPERIMENTAL PROGRAM (World-3+)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When epistemic inheritance is reintroduced, DISTINGUISH SIX THINGS that are
routinely conflated: persistence / reuse / recombination / improvement / causal
dependence / cumulative depth.

AN ARCHIVE FULL OF ARTIFACTS IS NOT CULTURE. A wiki full of entries is not
metabolization. The question is strictly: DO LATER ORGANISMS ACQUIRE CAPABILITIES
THAT WOULD HAVE BEEN INACCESSIBLE OR SIGNIFICANTLY MORE EXPENSIVE WITHOUT THE
INHERITED HISTORICAL SEQUENCE?

REQUIRED INTERVENTIONS: shuffle history; remove critical ancestors; randomize
artifact order; replace artifacts with matched irrelevant ones; restart
populations with and without the accumulated structures. Note that the engine's
information-availability frontier query already computes what was LEGALLY
AVAILABLE at any point -- explicitly not what was read or used -- which is exactly
the quantity these ablations need.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
15. ENDOGENOUS-OPPORTUNITY EXPERIMENTAL PROGRAM (World-2+)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The decisive property of natural evolution may be that organisms change the world
in ways that create NEW NICHES. The test is stronger than "generate harder
environments":

  CAN ONE INNOVATION MAKE ANOTHER, PREVIOUSLY USELESS, INNOVATION VALUABLE?

Instrument the OPPORTUNITY-DEPENDENCY GRAPH: an edge A -> B exists when B's
selective value is measurably positive only in populations/worlds where A is
present, established by removal. Do NOT semantically classify the nodes. If that
graph GROWS over time, we are observing an endogenous ladder -- which is the only
kind we are allowed to have.

16. FAILURE-TOPOLOGY QUALIFICATION PLAN (EARN THE TECTONIC HYPOTHESIS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The hypothesis: EVOLUTIONARY REGIME TRANSITIONS DEFORM THE FAILURE LANDSCAPE
BEFORE OR WHILE CONVENTIONAL CAPABILITY MEASURES CHANGE.

FAILURE GEOMETRY IS NOT EXEMPT FROM FALSIFICATION. Five qualification gates must
be passed BEFORE the statistic is used to support any claim:

  Q1 PLANTED TRANSITIONS. Construct runs with KNOWN, INJECTED transitions (e.g.
     switch the channel family distribution at a known generation, or transplant
     a known-superior genotype). Does the statistic detect them? Establishes
     SENSITIVITY. An instrument never validated on a known positive is not an
     instrument.
  Q2 FRONTIER EXPANSION vs SAMPLING INCREASE. Interaction count is under
     selection, so denser sampling alone moves every topology statistic. The
     statistic must be computed under RAREFACTION to a fixed interaction count
     and must still detect Q1 transitions.
  Q3 NON-RESTATEMENT. The statistic must be computed ONLY on CAPABILITY-DISJOINT
     coordinates (VM fault class, fault depth, abstention structure, resource
     state), with the partition frozen by hash. Coordinates that are monotone
     functions of the prediction residual restate the capability metric and would
     "predict" it tautologically.
  Q4 COMPARISON AGAINST CHEAP METRICS. Evaluate against aggregate fitness,
     success rate, efficiency, diversity, and evolutionary-activity statistics.
     *** IF A CHEAP METRIC PREDICTS TRANSITIONS EQUALLY WELL, WE USE THE CHEAP
     METRIC AND SAY SO. *** Failure geometry earns its place only by telling us
     WHERE AND WHY the process is stuck -- not because the metaphor is attractive.
  Q5 FALSE-POSITIVE RATE under neutral drift and compute-matched random search,
     with block-permutation nulls sized to the measured autocorrelation time.

ONLY AFTER Q1-Q5: the confirmatory test, with one pre-committed statistic
(occupancy-boundary flux), one capability-transition detector, one lead-time
window, one threshold, sealed by the engine's irreversible commit boundary.
Everything else is exploratory and reported as such.

17. WORLD-FORKING METHODOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
World N+1 EXISTS BECAUSE WORLD N EXPOSED A SPECIFIC, CHARACTERIZED CEILING.

    build minimal world -> observe regime -> characterize failure landscape ->
    identify limiting mechanism -> formulate intervention -> FORK ->
    test whether the regime changes -> retain or kill

  * Each ceiling spawns SIBLING ARMS that differ by ONE named change, plus a
    control that changes nothing causal.
  * Predictions are SEALED before the run via the engine's commit boundary; a
    fork whose prediction was written after its result is not evidence.
  * FAILED WORLDS ARE FOSSILS. Never overwritten, never deleted. A world that
    cleanly exposes a ceiling is often worth more than one that looks successful.
  * "Run it longer / bigger" is a DECLARED intervention with its own prediction,
    never the default response to a plateau.
  * The purpose is NOT to find one perfect world. It is to learn WHICH CHANGES TO
    SYNTHETIC PHYSICS PRODUCE WHICH CHANGES IN EVOLUTIONARY REGIME.

The engine already supplies the mechanics: checkpoint + fork-by-reference gives
counterfactual branches sharing a verifiable immutable past; lineage-scoped
budgets prevent a fork from minting compute; hash-chained ledgers make every
branch reconstructable.

19. THE FIVE OPEN PROBLEMS MOST LIKELY TO BLOCK EPISTEMIC COMPOUNDING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ranked by expected blocking power, with reasoning.

P0 (PRECONDITION, NOT A BLOCKER) EXCLUDE THE DEMOGRAPHIC PLATEAU (F21) FIRST.
   The survey identifies this as THE SINGLE MOST LIKELY SOURCE OF SYSTEMATIC
   MISDIAGNOSIS in the whole field: small-population, short-run artifacts get
   labelled as structural ceilings because the taxonomy has no demographic class,
   so the cheap control is never run. A SCALE SWEEP IS MANDATORY BEFORE ANY
   CEILING CLAIM IN THIS PROGRAMME. Note this is NOT a licence to answer plateaus
   with "run it longer" -- it is the opposite: scale must be RULED OUT explicitly,
   as a declared control, before a mechanism is asserted.

P1 THE ONTOLOGY CEILING (F5) -- REFRAMED AS TWO SEPARABLE CONSTRAINTS.
   If evolution can only recombine functional categories implicit in the supplied
   primitives, compounding is bounded a priori and everything else is decoration.
   The strongest published evidence is the intermediate-reward dependence in
   digital logic-task evolution: remove the authored intermediates and compounding
   stops dead, even though genuine intra-ontology machinery reuse does occur.
   THE SURVEY FORCES A SPLIT, and it applies directly to us:
     P1a SUPPLIED-CATEGORY CEILING -- what the primitives can express.
     P1b REWARD-CHANNEL BANDWIDTH -- what the payoff can PRICE. A world may
         contain abundant unpriced exploitable structure that is invisible to
         selection because nothing pays for it. Avida's spatial position,
         neighbour identity and timing are exactly this.
   *** WORLD-0 IS EXPOSED ON P1b: our residual-weighted payout against a MINIMAL
   null battery is a deliberately NARROW reward channel. Structure the battery
   cannot see, or the payoff cannot price, is invisible to selection however much
   the generator produces. Narrowing the battery to avoid authoring a ladder (K2)
   DIRECTLY NARROWS THE REWARD CHANNEL. That trade-off is now explicit and must be
   measured, not assumed away. ***
   RANKED FIRST because it bounds the ceiling of the entire programme.

P2 EVOLVABILITY IS SECOND-ORDER AND WEAKLY SELECTED -- THE RESOURCE TRAP (F16+F8).
   Machinery that improves FUTURE acquisition pays off later, diffusely, and often
   to descendants rather than the bearer. Under tight resource selection, carrying
   currently-useless exploratory structure is simply a cost. If selection cannot
   see the benefit, evolvability does not arise -- not because it is impossible but
   because it is unaffordable. RANKED SECOND because it is the most likely
   mechanism to stop us in a SMALL world specifically, and because it is directly
   tunable.

P3 THE METABOLIZATION / TRANSFER GAP (F10+F11).
   Innovations may persist without making descendants more capable, or help in one
   niche without ever becoming a reusable primitive. This is precisely the
   distinction between E1 and E2/E3, and it is invisible without ablation. RANKED
   THIRD because it is where a programme most easily fools itself: retention looks
   like accumulation on every conventional plot.

P4 OPPORTUNITY EXHAUSTION (F12, with F4 as its coevolutionary form).
   A fixed world eventually stops exposing new exploitable structure; adding
   organisms without endogenous opportunity creation converts the ceiling into
   cycling rather than removing it. RANKED FOURTH because it is a hard limit on
   sustained compounding but is comparatively easy to detect and to intervene on.

P5 REPRESENTATIONAL LOCK-IN (F6).
   Early successful abstractions may make superior alternatives increasingly
   inaccessible -- a path-dependence that could permanently cap the reachable
   region. RANKED FIFTH by expected frequency but FIRST by strategic importance to
   Prometheus, because it is the mechanistic core of the north-star hypothesis
   that the human corpus may be an attractor rather than merely a bootstrap.
   *** RAISED IN PRIORITY BY THE SURVEY: F6 is ASSERTED THROUGHOUT THE LITERATURE
   AND APPARENTLY NEVER MEASURED -- no classic system has run the counterfactual of
   restarting from an alternative early abstraction and comparing reachable
   capability. It must be treated as a HYPOTHESIS WITH A REQUIRED EXPERIMENT
   rather than a diagnosable class. That makes experiment O2 (section 12)
   potentially the FIRST DIRECT MEASUREMENT of representational lock-in, and it is
   cheap. It is arguably the highest value-per-unit-cost experiment available to
   this programme, and it is runnable in World-0 as specified. ***

P6 REGRESSIVE EROSION (F18, added by the survey).
   Acquired machinery is ACTIVELY DELETED once its reward is withdrawn, after long
   periods of successful retention -- distinct from never acquiring it, never
   retaining it, or retaining it inertly. COMPOUNDING REQUIRES MACHINERY TO
   SURVIVE PERIODS OF NON-PAYMENT, and a world that charges for carrying
   unexpressed structure actively destroys the substrate of compounding. Not in
   the original five, but it shares its intervention with P2 (the carriage-cost
   sweep), so it is measured for free alongside it.

CROSS-CUTTING META-BLOCKER, NOT RANKED WITH THE OTHERS:
M0 MEASUREMENT ILLUSION (F17). It does not block the phenomenon; it blocks our
   ability to KNOW. It gates P1-P5 entirely and is why section 16 qualifies the
   instrument before using it.

20. FOR EACH BLOCKER: THE SMALLEST BELIEF-CHANGING EXPERIMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All five are runnable in a tiny deterministic VM world with mutation,
reproduction, conserved charge, and hidden generative structure. NONE requires
deposits, culture, coevolution, or an LLM.

P1 ONTOLOGY CEILING -> EXPERIMENT O1 (DUAL-ONTOLOGY), section 12.
   ARMS: instruction set A vs B, matched expressiveness and cost, identical
   channel families and economy.
   MEASURED: whether the functional organization of evolved solutions converges;
   and whether a solution that is SHORT in B is ever found in A at large compute.
   FALSIFIES: "the ceiling binds" is falsified by convergence; "the ceiling is
   loose" is falsified by persistent ontology-shaped divergence plus
   non-discovery.

P2 RESOURCE TRAP -> THE NEUTRAL-STRUCTURE COST SWEEP.
   ARMS: sweep the charge cost of carrying NON-EXECUTED genome structure (and,
   separately, the cost of executed-but-unrewarded exploration) across several
   levels, including zero.
   MEASURED: acquisition cost on held-out families (E3 protocol, section 10) as a
   function of that cost.
   FALSIFIES: if evolvability is flat across the sweep, the resource trap is NOT
   the binding constraint in this world and P2 drops in rank. If evolvability
   rises sharply as the cost falls, we have identified a tunable lever -- and,
   importantly, one we did not author as a reward.
   THIS IS THE CHEAPEST HIGH-INFORMATION EXPERIMENT IN THE PROGRAMME.

P3 METABOLIZATION / TRANSFER -> MOTIF ABLATION.
   METHOD: identify recurring subprograms (motifs) in evolved genotypes by
   structural recurrence, NOT by semantic interpretation; ablate each by
   replacing it with matched-length inert or randomized code; measure acquisition
   cost on multiple held-out families with and without.
   FALSIFIES: if no motif's removal changes acquisition cost on ANY held-out
   family, then at this scale innovations are specialized and E2/E3 do not obtain
   -- a clean, publishable negative.

P4 OPPORTUNITY EXHAUSTION -> THE SATURATION PROBE.
   ARMS: fixed finite channel population vs continuously resampled population,
   with rivalrous payout in both.
   MEASURED: the residual-weighted quantity of UNEXPLOITED structure remaining
   over time, and whether acquisition rate declines as it is consumed.
   FALSIFIES: if unexploited structure does not deplete, exhaustion is not the
   binding constraint at this scale.
   *** MANDATORY GUARD (F20, added by the survey): CHANNEL RESAMPLING IS AN
   EXTERNAL PERTURBATION PUMP. A resampled arm can manufacture apparent
   open-endedness that is nothing but our own resampling rate -- the failure that
   afflicted externally-driven ALife systems. The resampled arm MUST therefore be
   run as a PERTURBATION-RATE SWEEP, and any novelty or activity statistic that is
   a monotone function of the resampling rate is thereby DISQUALIFIED as evidence
   of endogenous open-endedness. ***

P5 REPRESENTATIONAL LOCK-IN -> EXPERIMENT O2 (SEEDED LOCK-IN), section 12.
   ARMS: random seed vs shallow-family-pre-adapted seed, matched TOTAL compute
   (not matched generations).
   MEASURED: long-run reachability of a deep family.
   FALSIFIES: if the pre-adapted seed eventually matches or beats the random seed
   on the deep family, lock-in is not demonstrated at this scale -- which would
   also be a meaningful negative result for the north-star hypothesis.

21. RECOMMENDED WORLD-0 CAMPAIGN 0
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THREE STAGES, IN STRICT ORDER. STAGE A REQUIRES NO ORGANISMS, NO POPULATION, NO
ECONOMY AND NO EVOLUTION, AND IT CAN KILL THE WHOLE WORLD BY ITSELF.

  C0-A  THE STRUCTURE PROBE  (tests H0-1; the load-bearing measurement)
        Sample many generator programs from the compositional distribution.
        MEASURE: (1) the fraction on which the frozen minimal null battery fails
        (behavioural non-triviality, NOT syntactic depth); (2) whether bounded
        search -- random and enumerative -- can find predictors for them; (3) how
        predictability degrades with composition depth; (4) whether "depth"
        correlates with anything exploitable at all, or whether the tail is
        simply noise; and (5) REWARD-CHANNEL BANDWIDTH (added after the evidence
        survey): how much of the structure that bounded search CAN exploit is
        actually PRICED by the residual-weighted payoff against the minimal null
        battery. Structure that exists, and is reachable, but that the payoff
        cannot pay for, is invisible to selection -- the Avida
        unpriced-structure failure reproduced in our own economy. If a large
        fraction of exploitable structure is unpriced, the binding constraint is
        our reward channel rather than the world, and the fork targets the payoff
        rather than the generator.
        DECISION: if there is no band of channels that are BOTH non-trivial to the
        null battery AND reachable by bounded search, WORLD-0 HAS NO EXPLOITABLE
        DEPTH and the design is dead as specified. Kill criterion K15.
        *** THIS IS THE CHEAPEST POSSIBLE FALSIFICATION OF THE ENTIRE PROGRAMME
        AND IT MUST BE RUN FIRST. ***

  C0-B  ECONOMY CALIBRATION AND DETECTOR VALIDATION (only if C0-A passes)
        Frozen trivial-predictor reference set (constant, last-value,
        first-difference, matched-length random program) PLUS scripted adversary
        organisms as PLANTED POSITIVES. With deposits removed the adversary set
        shrinks to: predict-last-value, predict-constant, do-nothing/hoarder,
        attach-shopper, and step-waster. (Wash traders, clone colluders and
        steganographic pairs are no longer expressible -- a direct benefit of the
        stripped scope.)
        CALIBRATE costs so a null-battery-equivalent organism nets approximately
        zero over a long run, and REPORT HOW WIDE THE VIABLE CORRIDOR IS between
        free-riding and total inertia. A very narrow corridor is itself evidence
        of fragility.
        SUCCESS CONDITION, INVERTED: the kill criteria DO fire on the planted
        positives and DO NOT fire on the honest reference set. We validate the
        detector before trusting its silence.

  C0-C  REPLAY AND THROUGHPUT AUDIT
        Bit-exact replay on a random sample (zero divergence tolerance), and a
        precise throughput measurement that determines HOW MANY GENERATIONS
        CAMPAIGN 1 CAN ACTUALLY AFFORD. If the affordable budget is far below what
        an E3 effect plausibly needs, that is a reason to revise scope BEFORE
        Campaign 1, not to run a hopeful campaign.

HONEST EXPECTED OUTCOME: a kill criterion fires and the physics is revised. That
is a SUCCESSFUL Campaign 0. A world that survives its own shakedown unmodified
should be viewed with suspicion.

25. ONE RECOMMENDED NEXT IMPLEMENTATION ACTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BUILD ONLY THE GENERATOR AND THE NULL BATTERY, AND RUN C0-A (THE STRUCTURE PROBE).

  IN SCOPE: the compositional channel generator; the frozen minimal null battery;
  a bounded search harness (random and enumerative); and the measurement of
  behavioural non-triviality versus composition depth. Placed inside the engine
  source so it falls within the build-derived release identity from day one.
  OUT OF SCOPE, DELIBERATELY: no organisms, no population, no charge economy, no
  mutation, no reproduction, no evolution loop, no service, no port, no UI.

RATIONALE. The previous packet recommended building the whole physics core. That
is still too much. Stripping deposits made the generator THE SOLE SOURCE OF DEPTH
in World-0, and whether that depth exists is a property of the generator ALONE --
measurable with no organisms at all. If C0-A fails, the VM economy, the mutation
operators, the selection model and the failure-coordinate machinery would all
have been built to explore a world that contains nothing to find.

This is the smallest artifact in the entire programme that can falsify it, and it
is a few hundred lines. Build that, run it, and report the answer honestly --
including, and especially, if the answer is that the tail is noise.

================================================================================
  FINAL DIRECTIVE, RESTATED
  The question is no longer "how do we build a world in which intelligence can
  evolve." It is: WHAT PREVENTS INTELLIGENCE FROM CONTINUING TO INVENT BETTER WAYS
  OF BECOMING INTELLIGENT, AND WHAT CHANGES TO THE CONDITIONS OF EVOLUTION REMOVE
  THOSE BARRIERS?

  Worlds are experiments. Evolutionary plateaus are evidence. Failure landscapes
  locate the pressure. World forks test the cause. The prize is not an excellent
  synthetic organism -- it is the conditions under which epistemic compounding
  becomes an open-ended dynamical process.

  Do not merely borrow open-ended evolution. Try to finish some of its unfinished
  business.
================================================================================
